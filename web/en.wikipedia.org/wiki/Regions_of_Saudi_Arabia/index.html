Regions of Saudi Arabia

The regions of Saudi Arabia, also called provinces or emirates (Arabic: مناطق, manatiq), are the first-level administrative divisions of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. There are 13 regions, each governed by an emir (prince) appointed by the King, assisted by a deputy emir.

The regions are subdivided into governorates (muhafazat), of which there are 136, and then into sub-governorates and centres. The system was established by the Law of the Regions promulgated in 1992.
