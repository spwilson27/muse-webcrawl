Sister city

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A sister city, or twin town, relationship is a form of legal or social agreement between two geographically and politically distinct localities for the purpose of promoting cultural and commercial ties. The modern concept developed after the Second World War as a way to foster reconciliation and understanding between former enemies — one of the earliest pairings linked Coventry, heavily bombed in the Blitz, with Stalingrad and later with Dresden — and it spread worldwide during the Cold War. In the United States the movement was formalised in 1956 when President Dwight D. Eisenhower launched the people-to-people programme that became Sister Cities International.

Terminology

"Twin town" and "twinning" are the usual terms in Britain and much of Europe; "sister city" predominates in North America; other languages use terms such as jumelage (French), Städtepartnerschaft (German) and "friendship city" — the latter sometimes denoting a looser, less formal relationship, for example in Chinese practice, where a hierarchy from friendship cities to sister cities is observed.

Activities

Formal twinning agreements are typically signed by mayors and ratified by councils, and are maintained by volunteer committees. Typical activities include:

  • student and youth exchanges and school links;
  • cultural delegations, exhibitions and festival visits;
  • trade missions and municipal best-practice exchanges;
  • humanitarian aid to a twin affected by disaster or war;
  • symbolic gestures such as twinning plaques, welcome signs and street names.

Large cities may have dozens of partners — Chicago, Shanghai and Istanbul each maintain extensive networks — while some pairings reflect shared names, industries or history, such as mining towns or ports. Critics occasionally question the cost and value of civic delegations ("junkets"), and some relationships lapse into purely nominal status; others are suspended for political reasons, as happened to several Western–Russian pairings after 2022.
