The Tampa Bay Rays are an American professional baseball team based in St. Petersburg, Florida, competing in Major League Baseball as a member of the American League East Division. The team began play in 1998 as the Tampa Bay Devil Rays and shortened its name to Rays for the 2008 season.

History

After a decade of losing seasons, the rebranded Rays of 2008, managed by Joe Maddon, won the American League pennant before losing the World Series to the Philadelphia Phillies. The club won a second pennant in 2020, losing the World Series to the Los Angeles Dodgers. Despite consistently low payrolls, the Rays became noted for innovative strategies, analytics, and player development, including popularizing the "opener" pitching strategy.

Stadium

The Rays have played at Tropicana Field, the only fixed-domed stadium in Major League Baseball, since their inception. Long-running efforts to secure a new ballpark in the Tampa Bay area have been a recurring theme of the franchise's history.
