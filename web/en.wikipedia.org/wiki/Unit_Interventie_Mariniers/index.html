Unit Interventie Mariniers

The Unit Interventie Mariniers (UIM), formerly known as the Bijzondere Bijstandseenheid (BBE), is a special operations force of the Netherlands Marine Corps, part of the Netherlands Maritime Special Operations Forces (NLMARSOF). It is the principal domestic counter-terrorism unit of the Netherlands, tasked with interventions against especially large or complex terrorist incidents.

History

The unit was founded in 1973 in response to the Munich massacre of 1972 and the threat of hijackings and hostage-takings. It became known through operations during the 1970s, including the simultaneous resolution of the 1977 Dutch train hostage crisis at De Punt and a school hostage crisis at Bovensmilde.

Role and organisation

The UIM operates under the National Coordinator for Counterterrorism and Security for domestic deployments and trains for hostage rescue, arrests of high-risk suspects, and maritime and large-scale interventions. Its operators are drawn from the Marine Corps and undergo an extended selection and training pipeline.
