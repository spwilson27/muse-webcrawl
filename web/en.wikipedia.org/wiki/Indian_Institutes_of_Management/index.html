Indian Institutes of Management

The Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs) are a group of public business schools in India, designated as institutes of national importance. The first IIMs were established in Calcutta (Kolkata) and Ahmedabad in 1961, in the wake of recommendations for management education to support India's industrial development; IIM Calcutta was set up in collaboration with the MIT Sloan School of Management, and IIM Ahmedabad with Harvard Business School.

Expansion and governance

The network grew with institutes at Bangalore (1973), Lucknow (1984), Kozhikode and Indore (1996–97), and a further wave from 2007 onward, bringing the total to 21 IIMs. The Indian Institutes of Management Act, 2017 granted the institutes greater autonomy and empowered them to award degrees (the MBA) rather than only postgraduate diplomas.

Programmes and admissions

The flagship programme is the two-year MBA (formerly the PGDM/PGP), admission to which is through the highly competitive Common Admission Test (CAT). The IIMs also run executive programmes, doctoral (PhD/FPM) programmes and integrated five-year programmes at some campuses. Their graduates are prominent in Indian and global business, and the older IIMs — especially Ahmedabad, Bangalore and Calcutta ("ABC") — are consistently ranked among Asia's top business schools.
