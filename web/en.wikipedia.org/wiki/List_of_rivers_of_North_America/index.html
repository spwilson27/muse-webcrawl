List of rivers of North America

This is a list of the principal rivers of North America, including those of Canada, the United States, Mexico and Central America. The continent's drainage is organised by the Continental Divide: rivers east of the Rockies flow to the Atlantic, the Arctic or the Gulf of Mexico, while those to the west reach the Pacific.
