EFL League One

EFL League One
Country: England (and Welsh clubs)
Level: 3rd tier
Teams: 24
Founded: 2004 (as League One)

EFL League One, commonly known simply as League One, is the third tier of the English football league system and the second division of the English Football League (EFL). It contains 24 clubs.

Competition format

Each club plays the others twice over a season running from August to May. The top two clubs are promoted automatically to the EFL Championship; the next four enter play-offs for a third promotion place. The bottom four clubs are relegated to EFL League Two. Clubs also compete in the FA Cup, EFL Cup and EFL Trophy.

History

The tier was known as the Football League Third Division from 1958, became the Second Division after the Premier League breakaway in 1992, and was rebranded League One in 2004. Sponsorship names have included Sky Bet League One.
