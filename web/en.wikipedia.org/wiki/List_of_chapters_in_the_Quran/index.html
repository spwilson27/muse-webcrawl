List of chapters in the Quran

The Quran is divided into 114 chapters, called surahs (Arabic: سورة). Each surah is divided into verses called ayahs. Surahs are roughly ordered by decreasing length rather than by chronology of revelation; each is traditionally classified as Meccan or Medinan according to where it was revealed. Together the Quran contains about 6,236 verses (numbering varies slightly by tradition).
