The National (TV program)

The National is a Canadian national television newscast broadcast by CBC Television and CBC News Network. It has been the CBC's flagship nightly news program since 1954.

History

The program began as The National News and took its current name by 1970. Peter Mansbridge anchored from 1988 until 2017, becoming one of Canada's most familiar journalists. In 2017 the broadcast relaunched with a four-host, multi-city format; it later settled into a pattern with anchors in Toronto and Vancouver.

Format

Airing at 10 p.m. local time, The National combines national and international news, investigative segments from programs such as Marketplace and The Fifth Estate's contributors, and the panel segment "At Issue" on political stories.
