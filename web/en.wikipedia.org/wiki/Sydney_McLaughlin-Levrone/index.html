Sydney McLaughlin-Levrone (born August 7, 1999) is an American hurdler and sprinter who competes in the 400 metres hurdles and 400 metres. She is the world record holder in the 400 m hurdles and a four-time Olympic gold medallist, having won the individual 400 m hurdles and the 4 × 400 m relay at both the Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024 Games.

Career

McLaughlin emerged as a prodigy in New Jersey high school athletics, qualifying for the 2016 Rio Olympics at the age of 16. After one record-breaking season at the University of Kentucky, she turned professional in 2018. At the Tokyo Olympics she broke the world record with 51.46 seconds, and at the 2022 World Championships in Eugene she lowered it dramatically to 50.68. She has since improved the record several more times, including 50.65 at the 2024 U.S. Olympic trials and 50.37 in winning the Paris Olympic final. Coached by Bob Kersee, she has also excelled in the flat 400 metres.

Personal life

McLaughlin married former NFL player Andre Levrone Jr. in 2022 and adopted the surname McLaughlin-Levrone. She has written about the role of her Christian faith in her career in her memoir Far Beyond Gold (2024).
