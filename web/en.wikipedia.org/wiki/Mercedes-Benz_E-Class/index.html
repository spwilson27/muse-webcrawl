The Mercedes-Benz E-Class is a range of executive cars manufactured by Mercedes-Benz in various engine and body configurations. Produced since 1953, the E-Class falls mid-range in the Mercedes line-up and has been marketed worldwide across five generations.

Before 1993, the E in Mercedes-Benz nomenclature was a suffix following a model's model designation (e.g. 300E), denoting Einspritzmotor (fuel injection engine). The E-Class name first appeared with the facelifted W124 in 1993, when all Mercedes models began using the alphabetic class system.

Generations

W124 (1984–1995) — the first to carry the E-Class name after its 1993 facelift; renowned for durability, including the 500 E built with Porsche. W210 (1995–2002) — introduced the four-headlamp face. W211 (2002–2009) — reintroduced some of the qualities of earlier generations. W212 (2009–2016) — return to a squarer design. W213 (2016–2023) — a technology flagship sharing semi-autonomous driving features with the S-Class. W214 (2023–present) — saloon and estate with extensive digitalisation and plug-in hybrids.

Use as taxis

E-Class diesels have long been a mainstay of taxi fleets in Germany and many other countries, valued for longevity and comfort.
