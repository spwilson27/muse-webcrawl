iMac

DeveloperApple Inc.
TypeAll-in-one desktop
Release dateAugust 15, 1998

The iMac is a series of all-in-one desktop computers designed, manufactured, and sold by Apple Inc. It has been the primary part of Apple's consumer desktop offerings since its debut in August 1998, and has evolved through seven distinct forms.

History

The original iMac G3, designed under Jony Ive, was credited with reviving Apple financially and popularizing translucent colored industrial design and the USB standard. Later generations included the sunflower-like iMac G4, the plastic and aluminum Intel models, the slim unibody designs, the Retina 5K models, and the colorful Apple silicon iMac with the M1 chip introduced in 2021.

Design

All iMacs integrate the display and computer in a single enclosure. Current models use Apple silicon processors and come in a range of colors with a 24-inch 4.5K Retina display.
