Canadian Hockey League

The Canadian Hockey League (CHL; French: Ligue canadienne de hockey) is an umbrella organization that represents the three Canada-based major junior ice hockey leagues: the Western Hockey League (WHL), the Ontario Hockey League (OHL) and the Quebec Maritimes Junior Hockey League (QMJHL). Based in Toronto, the CHL has 60 teams in nine Canadian provinces and four American states.

Overview

The CHL was founded in 1975 as the Canadian Major Junior Hockey League. Its champion teams from each league, plus a host team, contest the Memorial Cup, first awarded in 1919 and one of the most prestigious trophies in Canadian sport. CHL players are typically 16 to 20 years old, and the league is the foremost development pipeline for the National Hockey League, supplying the majority of players selected in the NHL entry draft.

Events and education

The CHL stages the CHL/NHL Top Prospects Game and interlocking events such as the CHL Canada/Russia Series (in various formats). Its scholarship program funds post-secondary education for alumni who do not turn professional.
