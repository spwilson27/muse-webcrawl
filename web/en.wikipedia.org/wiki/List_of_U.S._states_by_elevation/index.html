List of U.S. states by elevation

The elevation of the U.S. states varies enormously, from Denali in Alaska (20,310 feet / 6,190.5 m), the highest point in North America, to the below-sea-level basin of Badwater in California's Death Valley (−282 feet / −86 m). This article ranks the states by their highest point; the complete article also gives lowest points and mean elevations.

Lowest points

The lowest natural point in the United States is Badwater Basin in Death Valley, California, at −282 feet (−86 m). Most coastal states reach sea level; landlocked states' lowest points are typically river surfaces, such as the Mississippi River in Missouri or the Red River in Oklahoma.

Colorado has the highest mean elevation of any state (about 6,800 ft), followed by Wyoming and Utah, while Delaware has the lowest mean elevation (about 60 ft).
