Buzz marketing

Buzz marketing is a viral marketing technique focused on maximising the word-of-mouth potential of a campaign or product. Such strategies encourage individuals to pass along a marketing message to others, creating the potential for exponential growth in the message's exposure and influence.

Methods

Common buzz marketing methods include generating controversy or surprise, using influencers and early adopters as "buzz agents", seeding products with trendsetters, and designing campaigns that consumers want to share on social media. The term "buzz" refers to the informal chatter a successful campaign generates.

Criticism

Buzz marketing has been criticised when agents fail to disclose that they are being compensated, blurring the line between genuine recommendation and advertising. Regulators in several countries require disclosure of paid endorsements.
