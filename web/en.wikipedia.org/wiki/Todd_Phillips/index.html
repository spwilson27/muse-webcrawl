Todd Phillips
Born: Todd Philip Bunzl, 20 December 1970, Brooklyn, New York, U.S.
Occupation: Film director, producer, screenwriter
Years active: 1993–present

Todd Phillips (born 20 December 1970) is an American filmmaker. He first gained notice with documentary films and then became known for directing hit comedies including Road Trip (2000), Old School (2003) and The Hangover trilogy (2009–2013), one of the most commercially successful comedy franchises of its era.

Later work

Phillips shifted toward drama with War Dogs (2016) and especially Joker (2019), a dark psychological origin story for the DC Comics character, which he directed and co-wrote. The film won the Golden Lion at the Venice Film Festival and earned over one billion dollars at the box office; Phillips received Academy Award nominations for Best Director and Best Picture. A sequel, Joker: Folie à Deux, followed in 2024.
