Peter Douglas Molyneux OBE (born 5 May 1959) is an English video game designer and programmer. He co-founded Bullfrog Productions and designed games including Populous (1989), which popularised the "god game" genre, Dungeon Keeper and Theme Park. After Bullfrog's acquisition by Electronic Arts, he founded Lionhead Studios, creating Black & White and the Fable series.

Career

Molyneux began in the early 1980s selling games and business software before co-founding Bullfrog in 1987. Populous sold millions of copies and established his reputation for ambitious simulation games. At Lionhead, Black & White (2001) and Fable (2004) were major commercial successes. He later became creative director of Microsoft Game Studios Europe before leaving in 2012 to found the independent studio 22cans, whose projects included the experimental Curiosity: What's Inside the Cube? and Godus.

Reputation

Molyneux is renowned both for visionary design and for over-promising features that did not appear in finished games, a pattern he has publicly acknowledged. He has received numerous honours, including an OBE, a BAFTA Fellowship and induction into the Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences Hall of Fame.
