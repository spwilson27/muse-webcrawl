The Hu

The Hu is a Mongolian folk metal band formed in 2016 in Ulaanbaatar. The group performs in Mongolian, using traditional instruments such as the morin khuur (horsehead fiddle) and tovshuur (lute) together with throat singing, a style they call "Hunnu rock."

History

The band was formed by producer Dashdondog "Dashka" Bayarmagnai, who brought together musicians trained at the Mongolian State Conservatory. Their videos for "Yuve Yuve Yu" and "Wolf Totem," released in late 2018, went viral on YouTube, drawing tens of millions of views and international attention.

Their debut album, The Gereg (2019), reached number one on Billboard's World Albums chart, and the band became the first Mongolian act to top a Billboard chart. A deluxe edition featured guest vocalists including Jacoby Shaddix of Papa Roach and Lzzy Hale of Halestorm. Their song "Black Thunder" was written for the video game Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order. The second album, Rumble of Thunder, followed in 2022.

Style

The Hu's "Hunnu rock" — named for the ancient Hunnu (Xiongnu) empire — fuses heavy metal riffing with Mongolian throat singing (khöömii), horsehead fiddles and lyrics drawn from ancient poetry, war cries and praise of nature and Mongolian heritage. In 2019 the band members received the Order of Genghis Khan, Mongolia's highest state award, and in 2022 UNESCO named the band an Artist for Peace.
