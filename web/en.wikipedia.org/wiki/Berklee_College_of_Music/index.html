Berklee College of Music

Berklee's campus is woven into Boston's Back Bay and Fenway neighbourhoods.

Berklee College of Music is a private music college in Boston, Massachusetts, and the largest independent college of contemporary music in the world. Known for the study of jazz and modern American music, it offers degrees across performance, composition, production, music business, music therapy and other fields. Alumni have won hundreds of Grammy Awards.

History

The school was founded in 1945 by pianist and arranger Lawrence Berk as Schillinger House, teaching the Schillinger System of musical composition — a radical departure at a time when American music schools concentrated on classical repertoire. Berk renamed it Berklee School of Music in 1954, after his son Lee; the name is also an anagram of "Lee Berk". Unusually for its era, the school welcomed jazz, dance-band and popular music as serious studies, and it became the first American college to grant degrees in jazz-influenced fields. It received accreditation as a college in 1973.

Academics and campuses

Berklee's undergraduate majors include performance, jazz composition, contemporary writing and production, electronic production and design, film scoring, songwriting, music business/management, music education and music therapy. In 2016 Berklee merged with the Boston Conservatory, a performing-arts institution strong in dance, theatre and classical music, forming a single parent institution, Berklee, while the two schools retained their names. Berklee also operates a campus in Valencia, Spain, offering graduate programmes, and Berklee Online, one of the largest online music education providers.

People

Notable alumni include Quincy Jones, Diana Krall, John Mayer, Esperanza Spalding, Melissa Etheridge, Gary Burton, and members of bands such as Aerosmith and Imagine Dragons; faculty and alumni together account for more than 300 Grammy Awards. The college's performance venues, including the Berklee Performance Center and the Red Room at Cafe 939, are fixtures of Boston's music scene.
