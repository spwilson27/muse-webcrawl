List of trans-Neptunian objects

A trans-Neptunian object (TNO) is any minor planet in the Solar System that orbits the Sun at a greater average distance than Neptune (30.1 AU). The region beyond Neptune includes the Kuiper belt, the scattered disc and the detached objects, and thousands of TNOs have been catalogued since the discovery of Albion (1992 QB1) — Pluto, found in 1930, was the first known example.

Classification

TNOs are grouped by their orbits: classical Kuiper belt objects (cubewanos), resonant objects such as the plutinos in 3:2 resonance with Neptune, scattered-disc objects, and detached objects like Sedna whose perihelia lie far beyond Neptune's influence.
