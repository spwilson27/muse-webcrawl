Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry is a fictional school of magic in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter novels. A vast castle in the Scottish Highlands, hidden from non-magical people (Muggles), it educates young witches and wizards from Britain and Ireland from the ages of eleven to eighteen.

Hogwarts castle stands above the Black Lake near the village of Hogsmeade.

History in the fiction

Hogwarts was founded around 990 AD by four of the greatest witches and wizards of the age: Godric Gryffindor, Helga Hufflepuff, Rowena Ravenclaw and Salazar Slytherin. Each founded a house bearing their name and selected students according to the qualities they prized — bravery, loyalty, intelligence and ambition respectively. Slytherin later quarrelled with the others over the admission of Muggle-born students and left the school, leaving behind the hidden Chamber of Secrets. The school is supported by magic that makes it appear as dangerous ruins to Muggles, and is governed by a headmaster or headmistress — most famously Albus Dumbledore — under the oversight of school governors and the Ministry of Magic.

The school

Students arrive by the Hogwarts Express from Platform 9¾ at King's Cross station and are sorted into houses by the Sorting Hat. The seven-year curriculum includes Transfiguration, Potions, Charms, Defence Against the Dark Arts, Herbology, Astronomy and History of Magic, with examinations (O.W.L.s and N.E.W.T.s) in the fifth and seventh years. The castle features moving staircases, secret passages, talking portraits and the Great Hall, beneath whose enchanted ceiling school feasts are held. The grounds include the Quidditch pitch, the Forbidden Forest and the Black Lake. House points and the annual Quidditch Cup structure school rivalry.

In the story

Hogwarts is the principal setting of the series: Harry Potter attends from 1991, and the school becomes a battleground in the war against Lord Voldemort, culminating in the Battle of Hogwarts in the final novel, Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows (2007). In the films the castle's exterior was composited from locations including Alnwick Castle and Durham Cathedral, with a large model at Leavesden Studios; a full-scale version anchors The Wizarding World of Harry Potter theme-park lands.
