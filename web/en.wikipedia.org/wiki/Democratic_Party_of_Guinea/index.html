Democratic Party of Guinea

The Democratic Party of Guinea (French: Parti démocratique de Guinée, PDG) was the ruling and for a time only legal political party in Guinea. Founded in 1947 as a territorial branch of the African Democratic Rally, it came under the leadership of Ahmed Sékou Touré.

History

The PDG led Guinea to independence from France in 1958 and governed as a one-party socialist state under Touré until his death in 1984, after which it was dissolved following a military coup. A revived party, the PDG-RDA, later contested elections with limited success.
