Dragon (zodiac)

The Dragon is the fifth of the 12-year cycle of animals that appear in the Chinese zodiac related to the Chinese calendar. The Year of the Dragon is associated with the Earthly Branch symbol 辰 (chén).

Dragon
Position: 5th of 12
Earthly Branch: 辰 (chen)
Recent years: 2012, 2024; next 2036
Element cycle: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water

Years and traits

Recent Dragon years include 2000 (Metal), 2012 (Water) and 2024 (Wood). In Chinese astrology Dragons are considered powerful, lucky, confident, charismatic and ambitious — the only mythical creature among the zodiac animals — and births often rise in Dragon years in parts of East and Southeast Asia as parents hope for auspicious children.

Compatibility and culture

The Dragon is traditionally most compatible with the Rat and Monkey (forming the trine of power) and least compatible with the Dog. Dragon imagery permeates Lunar New Year celebrations, dragon dances and boat races, and the sign anchors the zodiac as used in China, Vietnam (where the Cat replaces the Rabbit, but the Dragon remains), Korea and Japan.
