Morbid Angel

OriginTampa, Florida, U.S.
GenresDeath metal
Years active1983–present
Key membersTrey Azagthoth, Steve Tucker

Morbid Angel is an American death metal band based in Tampa, Florida, formed in 1983 by guitarist, primary composer and sole remaining original member Trey Azagthoth, together with bassist and vocalist Dallas Ward and drummer Mike Browning. The band is considered one of the founding acts of death metal and helped develop the Florida death metal scene alongside Death and Deicide.

History

The group's debut album Altars of Madness (1989), with bassist/vocalist David Vincent and drummer Pete Sandoval, is regarded as a landmark of the genre. Follow-up albums Blessed Are the Sick (1991) and Covenant (1993) — the latter the first death metal album released by a major label in the United States — cemented their reputation. Vincent left in 1996 and was replaced by Steve Tucker; Vincent returned for the industrial-influenced Illud Divinum Insanus (2011), which divided fans, before Tucker rejoined for Kingdoms Disdained (2017).

Style and themes

Morbid Angel's music features rapid, downtuned guitar riffing, double-bass drumming, and solos influenced by Azagthoth's interest in classical music and "lava" imagery; lyrical themes draw on ancient Sumerian religion, the occult, and anti-religious philosophy.

Discography

Studio albums include Altars of Madness (1989), Blessed Are the Sick (1991), Covenant (1993), Domination (1995), Formulas Fatal to the Flesh (1998), Gateways to Annihilation (2000), Heretic (2003), Illud Divinum Insanus (2011), and Kingdoms Disdained (2017). Notably, the first letters of their album titles proceed alphabetically.
