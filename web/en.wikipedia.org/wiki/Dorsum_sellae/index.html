Dorsum sellae

The dorsum sellae is a square plate of bone forming the posterior boundary of the sella turcica in the sphenoid bone of the human skull. Its upper corners project as the posterior clinoid processes.

Anatomy

Together with the tuberculum sellae it cradles the hypophyseal fossa containing the pituitary gland. It separates the sella from the clivus sloping down behind it.

Clinical notes

Erosion or enlargement of the dorsum sellae on imaging can indicate raised intracranial pressure or a pituitary tumour expanding the sella.
