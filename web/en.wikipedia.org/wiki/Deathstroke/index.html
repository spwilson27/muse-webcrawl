Deathstroke (Slade Wilson) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Marv Wolfman and artist George Pérez, he debuted in The New Teen Titans #2 (December 1980) as a mercenary and assassin who became the arch-enemy of the Teen Titans and one of DC's most prominent anti-heroes and villains.

Character biography

Slade Wilson was a decorated soldier who volunteered for an experimental procedure that enhanced his strength, reflexes and intellect — he is said to use 90% of his brain capacity — but was left unstable. Working as the world's greatest mercenary under a strict personal code, he clashed with the Titans after his son Grant died trying to fulfil a contract against them. His complicated family — his ex-wife Adeline, and children Joseph (Jericho) and Rose (Ravager) — features heavily in his stories. Deathstroke is recognisable by his two-tone mask, with one side blacked out over his blinded right eye, and his arsenal of swords, staffs and firearms.

In other media

Deathstroke has appeared extensively in animation (Teen Titans, where he is called simply "Slade"), the Arkham video games, the TV series Arrow (played by Manu Bennett), Titans (Esai Morales), and the DC Extended Universe films, played by Joe Manganiello.
