Mining in Australia

Mining in Australia has long been a significant primary sector industry and contributor to the Australian economy by providing export income, royalty payments, and employment. Historically, mining booms have attracted large numbers of immigrants seeking work, and mining has been central to the economic development of several states and territories, particularly Western Australia, Queensland, and New South Wales.

History

Indigenous Australians mined ochre, flint, and other materials for tens of thousands of years. European mining began soon after colonisation, with coal mined near Newcastle from the 1790s. The discovery of gold near Bathurst in 1851 triggered the Australian gold rushes, transforming the colonies' population and economy; major rushes followed in Victoria at Ballarat and Bendigo, and later in Western Australia at Kalgoorlie and Coolgardie in the 1890s. Base-metal mining developed at Broken Hill from 1883, which became one of the world's largest lead-zinc-silver deposits.

Major commodities

Australia is among the world's leading producers of iron ore, coal (black coal, both thermal and metallurgical), gold, aluminium (bauxite and alumina), copper, nickel, uranium, lithium, zinc, lead, natural gas, and mineral sands. The Pilbara region of Western Australia hosts some of the world's largest iron ore mines, and Australia is consistently the world's largest exporter of iron ore and among the largest exporters of coal and liquefied natural gas.

Economic significance

The resources sector accounts for a substantial share of Australia's exports — in recent years minerals and fuels have made up well over half of merchandise export earnings — and the mining boom of the 2000s, driven by demand from China, drove large investment in mines, railways, and ports. Major companies include BHP, Rio Tinto, Fortescue, Hancock Prospecting, Glencore, and Newmont.

Issues

Mining in Australia raises ongoing debates over royalties and taxation (including the former Minerals Resource Rent Tax), native title and Aboriginal land rights under the Native Title Act 1993, environmental impacts, water use, mine safety, rehabilitation, and the effect of commodity cycles on regional communities.
