Guilty Gear

Genre: Fighting game

Developer: Arc System Works

Creator: Daisuke Ishiwatari

First release: Guilty Gear (1998)

Guilty Gear is a series of fighting video games developed by Arc System Works, created by Daisuke Ishiwatari. The first game appeared on the PlayStation in 1998. Set in a post-apocalyptic future shaped by a war against living magical weapons called Gears, the series follows bounty hunter Sol Badguy and rivals such as Ky Kiske.

Gameplay and style

The games are known for fast, combo-heavy gameplay, mechanics such as the Tension gauge, Roman Cancels and instant-kill moves, anime-style art, and a hard rock and heavy metal soundtrack composed largely by Ishiwatari, with characters named after rock musicians and songs.

Major entries

Key titles include Guilty Gear X (2000), Guilty Gear XX (2002, repeatedly revised), the Xrd series (from 2014), and Guilty Gear Strive (2021), which brought the series to a much larger audience.
