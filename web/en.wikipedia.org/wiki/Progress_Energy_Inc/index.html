Progress Energy Inc

Industry: Electric utility
Founded: 2000 (as Progress Energy)
Defunct: 2012
Headquarters: Raleigh, North Carolina, United States

Progress Energy Inc was a United States electric utility holding company headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina. It was formed in 2000 following the merger involving Carolina Power & Light, and its regulated utility subsidiaries served customers in North Carolina, South Carolina, and Florida. In July 2012, Progress Energy merged with Duke Energy, creating the largest electric utility in the United States at the time.

Operations

The company's principal subsidiaries were Progress Energy Carolinas (the former Carolina Power & Light) and Progress Energy Florida (the former Florida Power Corporation). Together they served about 3.1 million customers. Its generating fleet included coal, natural gas, hydroelectric, and nuclear plants, including the Crystal River, Brunswick, Harris, and H.B. Robinson nuclear stations.

Merger with Duke Energy

The merger with Duke Energy was announced in January 2011 and completed on July 2, 2012. The combined company retained the Duke Energy name and Charlotte headquarters, while Raleigh remained a significant operations center.
