Swedish Ardennes

The Swedish Ardennes is a medium-sized draft horse breed developed in Sweden from imported Ardennes horses crossed with the native North Swedish Horse. It is a compact, powerful horse bred for agricultural and forestry work.

History

In the 1870s Swedish farmers imported heavy Ardennes stallions from Belgium and France and crossed them with local mares to produce a stronger agricultural horse. A studbook society was formed in the early 20th century to fix the type.

Characteristics

The breed has a heavy, short head, a muscular arched neck, a deep compact body and strong legs with moderate feathering. Common colours are bay, black, brown and chestnut. Swedish Ardennes are noted for their longevity, soundness and calm temperament, and remain popular for logging and small-scale farming.
