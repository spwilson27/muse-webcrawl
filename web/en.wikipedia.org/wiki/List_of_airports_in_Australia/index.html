List of airports in Australia

This is a list of airports in Australia, grouped by state and territory. Australia has hundreds of licensed aerodromes; its busiest international gateways are Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Perth. Airservices Australia and the Civil Aviation Safety Authority oversee air navigation and safety regulation.

By state

New South Wales airports include Newcastle, Coffs Harbour, and regional fields such as Dubbo and Wagga Wagga. Victoria has Avalon as a secondary Melbourne gateway. Queensland's large network serves mining and tourist centres including Townsville, Mackay, Rockhampton, and Mount Isa. Western Australia's remote aerodromes, such as Broome and Karratha, support the resources industry. Tasmania's second airport is Launceston.
