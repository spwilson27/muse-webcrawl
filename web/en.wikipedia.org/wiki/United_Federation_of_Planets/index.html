The United Federation of Planets (abbreviated as UFP and commonly referred to as "the Federation") is a fictional interstellar federal republic in the Star Trek science fiction franchise. Composed of numerous member worlds spread across thousands of light years, it is depicted as a post-scarcity, technologically advanced society governed by democratic principles and committed to peaceful exploration and the rights of sentient beings.

Depiction

Within the fictional chronology, the Federation was founded in 2161 by United Earth, Vulcan, the Andorian Empire, and Tellar Prime, following the Earth–Romulan War and the earlier formation of a Coalition of Planets. By the 24th century it encompasses more than 150 member worlds and thousands of colonies. Its guiding law includes the Federation Charter and the Prime Directive (Starfleet General Order 1), which prohibits interference with the internal development of less advanced civilizations.

Government

The Federation is governed by the Federation Council, a legislature of representatives from member worlds, and headed by a President whose office is located in Paris on Earth. Starfleet, headquartered in San Francisco, serves as the Federation's primary exploratory, scientific, diplomatic, and defensive service. Member worlds retain substantial local self-government while accepting Federation law on matters such as sentient rights and the prohibition of caste-based discrimination.

Reception and influence

Commentators have frequently interpreted the Federation as an idealized projection of liberal democratic values and of the United Nations, reflecting creator Gene Roddenberry's vision of a future in which humanity had overcome war, poverty, and prejudice. Economists and futurists have cited its moneyless, post-scarcity economy—enabled by replicator technology—as a touchstone in discussions of automation and abundance. The Federation and its emblem have become some of the most recognizable elements of the Star Trek franchise.
