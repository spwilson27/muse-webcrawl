Goat milk

Goat milk is the milk of domestic goats. Goats produce about 2% of the world's annual milk supply, and goat milk is widely consumed fresh and as cheese, yogurt and butter in many regions.

Goat milk
Source: Domestic goat
Uses: Drinking, cheese, yogurt
Composition: ~3.5% fat, ~3% protein, lactose present

Composition and nutrition

Compared with cow milk, goat milk has smaller fat globules, somewhat more medium-chain fatty acids and calcium, and forms a softer curd. It still contains lactose and its proteins cross-react in most cow-milk allergies, so it is not a safe substitute for allergic infants.

Products

Famous goat cheeses include French chèvre, feta-style cheeses, and Norwegian brunost-type products. Goat milk is also used in soaps and skincare. Top producers include India, Bangladesh and countries around the Mediterranean.
