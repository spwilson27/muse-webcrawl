Krypto

Krypto, also known as Krypto the Superdog, is a fictional superhero dog appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, commonly in association with Superman. Created by writer Otto Binder and artist Curt Swan, the character first appeared in Adventure Comics #210 (March 1955). Krypto is a white dog from the planet Krypton who possesses the same superpowers as Superman, including flight, super-strength, invulnerability, and heat vision, and wears a cape and a collar bearing a shield-shaped tag.

Fictional history

In the original Silver Age stories, Krypto was the El family dog on Krypton. Jor-El, testing the rockets that would eventually carry his son Kal-El to Earth, sent the puppy into space as a test subject; the rocket drifted off course and Krypto arrived on Earth years after Superman, reuniting with the now-teenaged Superboy. Krypto was a regular supporting character in Superboy and Superman stories of the 1950s and 1960s and a member of the Legion of Super-Pets.

The character was removed from continuity after the 1985–86 Crisis on Infinite Earths reboot, but versions of Krypto returned in later decades, including a modern reintroduction in the early 2000s Superman comics as a real Kryptonian dog rescued from a phantom duplicate of Krypton. Krypto has since become a fixture of Superman adaptations, starring in his own animated series Krypto the Superdog (2005–2006), appearing in DC League of Super-Pets (2022), where he was voiced by Dwayne Johnson, and in the 2025 live-action film Superman.
