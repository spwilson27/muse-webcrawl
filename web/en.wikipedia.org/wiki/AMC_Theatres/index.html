AMC Theatres (legally American Multi-Cinema, Inc.) is an American movie theater chain headquartered in Leawood, Kansas. It is the largest movie theater chain in the world, operating roughly 900 theatres and more than 10,000 screens across the United States and Europe. Its European and international operations include the Odeon Cinemas Group and UCI Cinemas brands.

History

The company traces its origins to 1920, when the Dubinsky brothers began operating a traveling theater troupe and later purchased theaters in Missouri. Edward Durwood, one of the brothers, expanded the business as Durwood Theatres. His son Stanley Durwood is credited with pioneering the multiplex concept, opening the first multi-screen theater in Kansas City in 1963 and founding American Multi-Cinema in 1968. The company grew through acquisitions and new construction, popularizing features such as cupholder armrests and stadium seating.

Growth and ownership

AMC was acquired by China's Dalian Wanda Group in 2012, which made AMC the largest theater operator in North America. Acquisitions of Carmike Cinemas (2016), Odeon & UCI Cinemas (2016), and Nordic Cinema Group (2017) extended its lead globally. In 2021 AMC became a prominent "meme stock" during a retail trading frenzy associated with the GameStop short squeeze era, which allowed the heavily indebted company to raise substantial equity capital.

Operations

AMC offers premium formats including Dolby Cinema, IMAX, and its own Prime brand, along with recliner seating, expanded food and beverage menus, and the AMC Stubs loyalty program, whose A-List tier provides a subscription model for frequent moviegoers.
