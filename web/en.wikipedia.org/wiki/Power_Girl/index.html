Power Girl

Power Girl, also known as Kara Zor-L (and Karen Starr), is a superheroine appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Gerry Conway and artist Ric Estrada, she first appeared in All Star Comics #58 (January/February 1976) as the Earth-Two counterpart of Supergirl.

Fictional character biography

Kara Zor-L is the cousin of Earth-Two's Superman, Kal-L. Like him, she was rocketed from the dying planet Krypton as an infant, but her ship took much longer to reach Earth, arriving after Superman was already an adult hero. She joined the Justice Society of America and revealed her identity publicly, adopting the civilian identity of computer programmer and later businesswoman Karen Starr, founder of Starrware Industries.

Publishing history

After Crisis on Infinite Earths (1985) merged DC's parallel earths, Power Girl's Kryptonian origin was retconned to an Atlantean one, before a Kryptonian origin tied to an alternate timeline was restored in the 2000s. She has headlined her own series, notably a well-received 2009–2011 run by Jimmy Palmiotti, Justin Gray and Amanda Conner, and has been a prominent member of the Justice Society and Birds of Prey.

Powers and abilities

As a Kryptonian under a yellow sun, Power Girl possesses superhuman strength, speed, flight, invulnerability, heat vision, X-ray vision and super-hearing comparable to Superman's, balanced by vulnerability to kryptonite and magic. She is also depicted as a skilled businesswoman and technologist.
