Kenneth Lonergan

Kenneth Lonergan (born October 16, 1962) is an American playwright, screenwriter, and film director. He first gained notice as a playwright with works including This Is Our Youth (1996) and Lobby Hero (2001), and he made his film-directing debut with You Can Count on Me (2000), which was widely praised.

He won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay for Manchester by the Sea (2016), which he also directed. The film starred Casey Affleck as a grieving handyman. His film Margaret (2011) had a famously troubled production and editing history, but the extended cut released later was acclaimed by critics.

Early life and education

Lonergan was born in the Bronx, New York City, to a psychiatrist father and a physician mother. He attended Walden School and Wesleyan University, where he studied drama, and later NYU's film school.

Career

Lonergan began as a speechwriter for the Environmental Protection Agency before turning to playwriting. As a screenwriter he contributed to Analyze This (1999) and Gangs of New York (2002), the latter earning him an Academy Award nomination.
