Retrieval practice

Retrieval practice (also called practice testing or the testing effect) is a learning strategy in which bringing information to mind enhances and boosts learning. Actively retrieving information from memory strengthens retention more than re-reading or re-studying.

Testing effect

Experiments by Henry Roediger, Jeffrey Karpicke and others show that students who take practice tests remember far more after a delay than those who restudy. Retrieval is thought to elaborate memory traces and improve transfer.

Applications

Techniques include flashcards (e.g. Anki), self-quizzing, low-stakes classroom quizzes, and practice questions. It combines powerfully with spaced repetition. Feedback enhances the effect and corrects errors.
