List of pen types, brands and companies

This article lists the principal types of pen, major pen brands, and notable manufacturing companies.

Types of pen

  • Ballpoint pen — oil-based ink dispensed by a rolling ball; patented in modern form by László Bíró.
  • Fountain pen — water-based ink fed from a reservoir to a nib by capillary action.
  • Rollerball pen — water-based or gel ink with a ball tip.
  • Gel pen — pigmented gel ink.
  • Felt-tip / marker pen — porous fibre tip; includes whiteboard and permanent markers.
  • Dip pen, quill, reed pen — historical instruments dipped in ink.
  • Technical pen — tubular nib for drafting (e.g. Rotring).
  • Stylus and digital pens — for touchscreens and graphics tablets.
