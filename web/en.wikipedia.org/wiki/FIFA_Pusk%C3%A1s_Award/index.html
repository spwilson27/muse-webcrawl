The FIFA Puskás Award is an award established on 20 October 2009 by the Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) and presented to the player judged to have scored the most aesthetically significant, or "most beautiful", goal of the calendar year. It is named in honour of Hungarian striker Ferenc Puskás, captain of the celebrated Hungarian national team of the 1950s and a star of Real Madrid.

Selection

FIFA compiles a shortlist of goals scored in top-level competitions, and the winner is chosen by a combination of fan votes and a panel of experts; the format has varied over the years. Since 2024 the women's equivalent has been awarded separately as the FIFA Marta Award, named after Brazil's Marta, with the Puskás Award applying to men's football.

Notable winners

Early winners include Cristiano Ronaldo (2009, for Manchester United against Porto), Neymar (2011), Zlatan Ibrahimović (2013, for his overhead kick against England), James Rodríguez (2014, for Colombia at the World Cup), Mohamed Salah (2018), Son Heung-min (2020), and Alejandro Garnacho (2024). In 2017 Mohd Faiz Subri of Malaysia became the first Asian winner.
