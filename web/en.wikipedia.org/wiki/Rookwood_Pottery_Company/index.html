Rookwood Pottery Company
Founded1880, Cincinnati, Ohio
FounderMaria Longworth Nichols
IndustryArt pottery, architectural faience

The Rookwood Pottery Company is an American ceramics company founded in Cincinnati, Ohio, in 1880 by Maria Longworth Nichols Storer. It became the first large American art pottery and won international acclaim, including a gold medal at the 1889 Paris Exposition.

Products

Rookwood is renowned for its Standard Glaze wares — rich brown-toned pieces decorated with flowers and portraits under the glaze — and for later lines such as Iris, Sea Green, and matte glazes. Its architectural faience department supplied decorative tile and fixtures to hotels, stations, and homes across the United States.

Later history

The company faltered in the Great Depression, changed hands several times, and ceased original production in the 1960s. The brand and its historic moulds and glaze formulas were revived in Cincinnati in the 21st century.
