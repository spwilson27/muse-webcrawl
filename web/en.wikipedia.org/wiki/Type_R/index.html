Type R

Type R is a badge used by the Japanese manufacturer Honda for special high-performance versions of its cars. The "R" stands for "Racing", and Type R models feature a red Honda badge, uprated engines, stiffer suspension, reduced weight and aerodynamic additions, following a philosophy of maximising performance with minimal comfort-oriented equipment.

Models

The badge debuted on the NSX Type R of 1992 and the Integra Type R of 1995, the latter frequently praised as one of the finest front-wheel-drive cars ever made. The Civic Type R, launched in 1997, became the best-known bearer of the name, progressing through generations including the turbocharged FK2, FK8 and FL5, the last of which set front-wheel-drive lap records at the Nürburgring. An Accord Type R was also sold in Europe.
