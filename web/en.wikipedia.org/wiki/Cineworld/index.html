Cineworld Group plc is a British cinema company and one of the largest cinema chains in the world. Founded in 1995, it operates in the United Kingdom, Ireland, Poland, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, Bulgaria, Romania, Israel and the United States.

History

Cineworld opened its first multiplex in Stevenage in 1996 and expanded across the UK, acquiring the British operations of UGC in 2004. In 2014 it merged with Cinema City International, creating a major pan-European operator. In December 2017 Cineworld agreed to acquire the American chain Regal Entertainment Group for about $3.6 billion, making it the world's second-largest cinema operator by number of screens.

Hit hard by cinema closures during the COVID-19 pandemic, Cineworld filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in the United States in September 2022 and completed a restructuring in 2023, which significantly reduced its debt and handed control to its lenders.

Operations

In the UK the group trades under the Cineworld and Picturehouse brands, and offers IMAX, 4DX and ScreenX formats at selected sites. Its Unlimited membership card provides subscribers with unlimited screenings for a monthly fee.
