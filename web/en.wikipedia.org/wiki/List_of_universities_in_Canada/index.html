List of universities in Canada

Canada has over 100 public universities, most of them members of Universities Canada. Universities are a provincial responsibility, and degrees are granted under provincial charters. The oldest university in Canada is Université Laval (1663, as the Séminaire de Québec); the oldest English-language university is the University of New Brunswick (1785).
