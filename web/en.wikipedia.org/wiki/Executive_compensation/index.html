Executive compensation

Executive compensation is the combination of salary, bonuses, stock, options, pensions and other benefits paid to senior executives of a company. It is a major topic in corporate governance, especially in the United States, where CEO pay relative to average workers has risen sharply since the 1970s.

Executive compensation
Components: Salary, bonus, equity, perks
Issues: Pay ratio, golden parachute, say on pay

Components

Packages typically combine base salary, annual incentives tied to performance, long-term incentives such as restricted stock and stock options, retirement benefits, and perquisites. "Golden parachutes" provide large payments on loss of job after a takeover.

Controversy and reform

Critics argue pay is set by captured boards and compensation consultants, bears little relation to performance, and drives inequality and short-termism. Defenders cite competition for talent and alignment via equity. Reforms include SEC disclosure, say-on-pay votes under the Dodd-Frank Act, and clawback rules.
