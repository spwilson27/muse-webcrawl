Local government in the United Kingdom

Local government in the United Kingdom is organised through a system of local authorities responsible for a range of public services including education, transport, housing, planning, social services, libraries, and waste management. The structure differs between England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland, reflecting devolution.

England

In England, local government is a mix of two-tier structures (county councils and district councils) and single-tier unitary authorities, alongside combined authorities and the Greater London Authority.

Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland

Scotland has 32 council areas and Wales has 22 principal areas, both single-tier. Northern Ireland has 11 local government districts.

Funding

Local authorities are funded through a combination of central government grants, council tax, and business rates.
