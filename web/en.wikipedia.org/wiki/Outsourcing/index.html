Outsourcing

Outsourcing is a business practice in which companies use external providers to carry out business processes that would otherwise be performed internally. Commonly outsourced functions include manufacturing, customer support, information technology services, payroll and human resources.

When outsourcing involves providers in another country it is often called offshoring; when the provider is in a nearby country, nearshoring. Motivations include reducing costs, accessing specialised skills, and focusing on core competencies, while risks include loss of control, quality problems and job losses in the home country.

Outsourcing grew rapidly from the 1980s with improvements in telecommunications and computing, and remains a debated topic in economics and politics because of its effects on employment and supply-chain resilience.
