Museum Campus

The Museum Campus is a 57-acre park area in Chicago, Illinois, on the shore of Lake Michigan, created in the 1990s to consolidate three of the city's major natural-science museums in a landscaped, pedestrian-friendly setting south of Grant Park. It forms the southern portion of the Burnham Plan's vision for Chicago's lakefront.

Institutions

The campus brings together the Field Museum of natural history (opened 1921 at its present site), the Shedd Aquarium (1930) and the Adler Planetarium (1930) on Northerly Island, the former Meigs Field peninsula. Soldier Field, home of the Chicago Bears, stands at the campus's southern end.

Creation

The campus was made possible by rerouting the southbound lanes of Lake Shore Drive westward, removing the highway that had previously separated the museums. The project, completed in 1998, added lawns, walkways and a lakeside promenade linking the institutions, and it is regarded as one of Mayor Richard M. Daley's signature lakefront improvements.
