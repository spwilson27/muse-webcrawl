While there is no single definitive list of the greatest films ever made, many films are frequently cited among the best by critics, polls, and institutions. This article surveys the films most often ranked at the top of major polls.

Sight and Sound poll

The British Film Institute's magazine Sight and Sound has conducted a decennial critics' poll since 1952. Winners by year:

AFI's 100 Years... 100 Movies

The American Film Institute's 2007 (10th anniversary) list is headed by Citizen Kane, followed by The Godfather, Casablanca (1942), Raging Bull (1980) and Singin' in the Rain (1952).
