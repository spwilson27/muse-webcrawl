Who Goes There?
Author: John W. Campbell
Published: 1938 (Astounding Science-Fiction)
Genre: Science fiction / horror novella

Who Goes There? is a science fiction horror novella by American author John W. Campbell Jr., published under the pen name Don A. Stuart in the August 1938 issue of Astounding Science-Fiction. It is considered one of the classic stories of the genre.

Plot

A team of researchers in Antarctica discovers a spacecraft buried in the ice for millions of years and recovers the frozen body of an alien. When the creature thaws, it proves to be a shape-shifting organism that can imitate any living being perfectly. Paranoia mounts as the men try to determine which of them are still human, eventually devising a blood test to expose the imitations.

Adaptations

The novella was adapted as The Thing from Another World (1951) and more faithfully as John Carpenter's The Thing (1982), with a prequel in 2011.
