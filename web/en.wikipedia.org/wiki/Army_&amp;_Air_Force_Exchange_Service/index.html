The Army & Air Force Exchange Service (AAFES), branded as the Exchange, is the retailer on U.S. Army and Air Force installations worldwide. Founded in 1895, it operates department stores, convenience stores, restaurants, movie theatres and other services for service members and their families.

History

The Exchange traces its origins to an 1895 order establishing post exchanges at Army installations. It took its present name in 1948, shortly after the creation of the independent U.S. Air Force. AAFES has deployed civilian employees alongside troops in every major conflict since the Second World War, including Korea, Vietnam, Iraq and Afghanistan.

Operations

AAFES operates thousands of facilities in dozens of countries and all 50 U.S. states. Its earnings support Morale, Welfare and Recreation (MWR) programs: a majority of profits are returned to the services as dividends funding quality-of-life programs, with the remainder reinvested in facilities.
