Hogwarts staff

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The Hogwarts staff are the fictional teachers and employees of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series. The school is led by a headmaster or headmistress, supported by a deputy, four heads of house, subject teachers, a librarian, a matron, a groundskeeper, and a caretaker.

Leadership

Albus Dumbledore serves as headmaster for most of the series, with Minerva McGonagall as his deputy and later headmistress. Dolores Umbridge briefly seizes the headship in Order of the Phoenix, and Severus Snape holds the post in Deathly Hallows. Earlier headmasters include Phineas Nigellus Black, remembered as the school's least popular head.

Teachers

Notable staff include Severus Snape (Potions, later Defence Against the Dark Arts), Minerva McGonagall (Transfiguration), Filius Flitwick (Charms), Pomona Sprout (Herbology), Rubeus Hagrid (Care of Magical Creatures and Keeper of Keys and Grounds), Sybill Trelawney (Divination), Cuthbert Binns, the ghost who teaches History of Magic, and Madam Rolanda Hooch (Flying). The Defence Against the Dark Arts post is widely believed cursed, changing hands every year of the series — Quirinus Quirrell, Gilderoy Lockhart, Remus Lupin, Barty Crouch Jr. (disguised as Alastor Moody), Umbridge, Snape, and Amycus Carrow each hold it in turn.

Other staff

Argus Filch is the caretaker, aided by his cat Mrs Norris; Madam Poppy Pomfrey runs the hospital wing; Madam Irma Pince is the librarian; and an army of house-elves works unseen in the kitchens. In the films the staff are portrayed by actors including Michael Gambon, Maggie Smith, Alan Rickman, and Robbie Coltrane.
