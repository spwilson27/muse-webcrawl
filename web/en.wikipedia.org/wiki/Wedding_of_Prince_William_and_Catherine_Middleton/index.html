Wedding of Prince William and Catherine Middleton

The wedding of Prince William and Catherine Middleton took place on Friday, 29 April 2011, at Westminster Abbey in London, England. Prince William, second in the line of succession to the British throne, first met Catherine Middleton in 2001 at the University of St Andrews. Their engagement, announced on 16 November 2010, followed a proposal in Kenya with the sapphire engagement ring that had belonged to William's mother, Diana, Princess of Wales.

The ceremony

The service was conducted by the Dean of Westminster, with the Archbishop of Canterbury, Rowan Williams, officiating the marriage. Catherine's dress, designed by Sarah Burton for Alexander McQueen, combined lace appliqué with a satin bodice and drew wide praise. Her sister Pippa Middleton served as maid of honour and Prince Harry as best man. Following the service, the couple appeared on the Buckingham Palace balcony, where they famously kissed twice.

Public holiday and celebrations

The wedding day was declared a public holiday in the United Kingdom. Around a million people lined the procession route in London, and street parties were held across the country. On the morning of the wedding, Queen Elizabeth II created William Duke of Cambridge, Earl of Strathearn, and Baron Carrickfergus, making Catherine Duchess of Cambridge upon marriage.

Aftermath

The couple delayed their honeymoon, later travelling to the Seychelles. They have three children: George (born 2013), Charlotte (born 2015), and Louis (born 2018). Following the accession of Charles III in 2022, William and Catherine became Prince and Princess of Wales.
