Nobita Nobi

Nobita Nobi (野比 のび太, Nobi Nobita) is the main protagonist of the Japanese manga and anime series Doraemon, created by Fujiko F. Fujio. He is a kind-hearted but lazy and unlucky elementary school boy in Tokyo whose life is transformed by the arrival of Doraemon, a robotic cat sent from the 22nd century by Nobita's future descendant to improve his fortunes.

Character

Nobita is notoriously poor at studying and sports, frequently scores zero on tests, and is bullied by Gian and teased by Suneo. He is, however, exceptionally talented at string figures (cat's cradle), marksmanship, and falling asleep quickly, and he is compassionate toward animals and people in trouble. His love interest and eventual wife in the future timeline is Shizuka Minamoto.

Role in the series

Most stories follow a pattern: Nobita faces a problem, begs Doraemon for a futuristic gadget, misuses it, and learns a lesson — though episodes often highlight his courage and kindness when friends are in genuine danger, especially in the feature films.
