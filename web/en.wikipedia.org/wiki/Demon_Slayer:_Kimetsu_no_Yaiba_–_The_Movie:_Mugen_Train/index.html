Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – The Movie: Mugen Train is a 2020 Japanese animated dark fantasy action film based on the "Mugen Train" arc of the Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba manga series by Koyoharu Gotouge. It is a direct sequel to the first season of the anime television series. The film was directed by Haruo Sotozaki and produced by Ufotable.

Tanjiro and his companions board the Mugen Train to assist the Flame Hashira, Kyojuro Rengoku.

Plot

Tanjiro Kamado, his sister Nezuko, and their companions Zenitsu Agatsuma and Inosuke Hashibira board the Mugen Train to assist the Flame Hashira, Kyojuro Rengoku, who is investigating the disappearance of over 40 passengers. Aboard the train they fall under the spell of Enmu, a demon of the Twelve Kizuki who forces them into deep dreams. Breaking free, they fight Enmu, who has fused with the train itself. After Enmu's defeat, the Upper Rank demon Akaza appears and battles Rengoku, mortally wounding him. Rengoku dies with a smile, urging Tanjiro to set his heart ablaze.

Release and box office

The film was released in Japan on 16 October 2020 by Aniplex and Toho. Despite the COVID-19 pandemic, it became a phenomenal success: it surpassed Spirited Away to become the highest-grossing Japanese film of all time and the highest-grossing film of 2020 worldwide, earning over US$500 million. It was also among the highest-grossing anime and R-rated animated films of all time at its release.

Reception

The film received acclaim for its animation, action sequences, and emotional climax. The second season of the television series re-adapted the Mugen Train arc in expanded episodic form before moving on to the Entertainment District arc.
