Day of the Tiles

The Day of the Tiles (Journée des Tuiles) was an uprising on 7 June 1788 in Grenoble, France, in which residents protesting royal reforms hurled roof tiles at troops — one of the first violent events of the pre-Revolution.


Date: 7 June 1788
Location: Grenoble, France

Background

Justice minister Lamoignon's reforms curtailing the parlements provoked resistance. When royal troops tried to exile Grenoble's magistrates, crowds rioted, climbed onto roofs, and bombarded soldiers with tiles, forcing the troops to withdraw.

Consequences

The revolt led to the Assembly of Vizille (21 July 1788), where representatives of the three estates demanded a meeting of the Estates-General and doubled Third Estate representation — a key step toward the French Revolution of 1789.
