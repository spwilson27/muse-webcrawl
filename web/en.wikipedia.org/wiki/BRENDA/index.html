BRENDA

BRENDA (BRaunschweig ENzyme DAtabase) is an information system and database of enzymes and enzyme-related data, maintained at the Technical University of Braunschweig in Germany. It is one of the most comprehensive repositories of functional enzyme data, covering all classified enzymes described by the Enzyme Commission (EC) numbering system.

Contents

BRENDA stores data extracted manually from the primary literature, including reaction kinetics (Km and turnover values), substrates and products, inhibitors and activators, optimal pH and temperature, stability, and the organisms and tissues in which enzymes occur. Entries are linked to protein sequences, structures and disease associations.

Access

The database is freely available to academic users via a web interface and search tools such as AMENDA and FRENDA for tissue- and disease-specific information. It integrates with other resources including KEGG and UniProt.
