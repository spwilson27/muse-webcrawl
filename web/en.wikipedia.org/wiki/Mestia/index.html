Mestia (Georgian: მესტია) is a highland townlet in northwest Georgia, at an elevation of about 1,500 metres (4,900 ft) in the Caucasus Mountains. It is the administrative centre of Mestia Municipality and the main settlement of the historic province of Svaneti.

History and landmarks

Mestia is famous for its medieval Svan defensive tower houses, built between the 9th and 12th centuries to protect families in times of feud and invasion. Together with the villages of Upper Svaneti, they form a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The town's Svaneti Museum of History and Ethnography, founded in 1936, holds medieval icons, manuscripts and weapons. The alpinist Mikheil Khergiani, a native of Mestia, is commemorated by a house-museum.

Tourism and access

Mestia is a centre for mountain tourism, serving as a base for hikes to the Chaladi Glacier and treks to Ushguli, and for skiing at the Hatsvali and Tetnuldi resorts. Queen Tamar Airport, opened in 2010, connects the town with Tbilisi.
