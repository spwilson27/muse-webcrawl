Prince George's County is a county located in the U.S. state of Maryland, bordering the eastern side of Washington, D.C. It is part of the Washington metropolitan area and is one of the most affluent majority-African-American counties in the United States.

History

The county was created in 1696 and named for Prince George of Denmark, consort of Queen Anne. It was long an agricultural region dominated by tobacco plantations worked by enslaved people, and suburbanised rapidly after the Second World War.

Geography and communities

The county covers about 499 square miles. Major communities include Bowie, College Park (home of the University of Maryland), Greenbelt, Hyattsville, Laurel, and National Harbor on the Potomac River. The county seat is Upper Marlboro.

Economy and transport

Federal facilities, the University of Maryland, and Andrews Air Force Base are major employers. The county is served by Metrorail lines, MARC commuter rail, and Interstates 95 and 495 (the Capital Beltway).
