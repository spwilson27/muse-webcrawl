The W. S. Cox Plate is a Group 1 Thoroughbred horse race held annually at Moonee Valley Racecourse in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. Run over 2,040 metres under weight-for-age conditions, it is widely regarded as Australia's premier weight-for-age championship and one of the most prestigious races in the country.

The race is named after William Samuel Cox, founder of the Moonee Valley Racing Club, and was first run in 1922. It is traditionally held on the Saturday before the Melbourne Cup, in late October.

Notable winners

Famous winners include Phar Lap, who won in 1930 and 1931, Kingston Town, the only horse to win three consecutive Cox Plates (1980–1982), and Winx, who won four consecutive editions from 2015 to 2018 — a record. Other celebrated winners include Tulloch, Gunsynd, Octagonal, Sunline, Makybe Diva, and So You Think.

Records

The tight, turning Moonee Valley circuit and its short home straight make the Cox Plate a test of tactical speed and acceleration, contributing to its reputation as "the race where legends are made".
