Serie B

Serie B (Italian pronunciation: [ˈsɛːrje ˈbi]), currently named Serie BKT for sponsorship reasons, is the second-highest division in the Italian football league system after Serie A. It has been operating for over ninety years since the 1929–30 season.

Format

Since 2004–05, Serie B has had 20 teams. At the end of each season, the top two teams are promoted to Serie A, while a third promotion place is decided by play-offs contested by the teams finishing third to eighth, provided the third-placed team does not finish more than 14 points ahead of the fourth-placed team. The bottom three teams are relegated to Serie C, with a fourth relegation place decided by a play-out between the sixteenth and seventeenth-placed teams unless separated by more than four points.

History

The championship was created in 1929 as a national second division. Over the decades it has served as the proving ground for many clubs that later won national titles, including Atalanta, Genoa and Napoli. The league is organised by the Lega Nazionale Professionisti Serie B.

Notable clubs

Genoa, founded in 1893, is among the most historically successful clubs to have played in Serie B. Other frequent participants include Brescia, Bari, Palermo, Empoli and Frosinone.
