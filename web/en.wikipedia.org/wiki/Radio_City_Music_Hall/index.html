Radio City Music Hall is an entertainment venue and theater at 1260 Avenue of the Americas, within Rockefeller Center, in the Midtown Manhattan neighborhood of New York City. Nicknamed "the Showplace of the Nation", it is the headquarters for the Rockettes dance company.

The 12,000-square-foot (1,100 m²) stage is considered one of the largest performance stages in the world. Its auditorium was the largest in the world when it opened in 1932, seating nearly 6,000 people. The theater is particularly known as the home of the annual Radio City Christmas Spectacular, which has been performed since 1933, and for its precision dance company, the Rockettes.

History

Radio City Music Hall was built as part of the construction of Rockefeller Center during the Great Depression. John D. Rockefeller Jr. partnered with the Radio Corporation of America (RCA) to develop the complex, and the theater was designed as a palace for the people — a place of high-quality entertainment at prices ordinary people could afford. It opened on December 27, 1932, with an elaborate stage show that received mixed reviews, and within weeks the format was shifted toward the presentation of feature films accompanied by stage spectacles.

For decades, the Music Hall presented first-run Hollywood films together with lavish stage productions, drawing enormous crowds. By the late 1970s the theater was losing money and was threatened with closure or conversion to office space, but public outcry and its designation as a New York City landmark in 1978 helped save it. It was restored extensively in 1999.

Architecture and design

The exterior was designed by Edward Durell Stone, while the interiors, in the Art Deco style, were designed by Donald Deskey. The auditorium is famous for its sweeping arches, often compared to a setting sun, which frame the proscenium and incorporate lighting that can simulate sunrise and sunset. The grand foyer features a large mural, and the theater contains numerous lounges, murals, and decorative works by prominent artists of the era.

Performances and events

In addition to the Christmas Spectacular, Radio City Music Hall hosts concerts, awards shows, movie premieres, and special events. It has hosted the Tony Awards, the Grammy Awards, and the NFL Draft, among many other ceremonies. The Rockettes, founded in St. Louis in 1925 as the Missouri Rockets, have performed at the theater since its opening season.
