List of indoor arenas in Canada

The following is a list of indoor arenas in Canada with a capacity of at least 5,000 for sporting events. Ice hockey dominates the list: Canada has seven National Hockey League arenas, and dozens of major junior and minor league rinks.

Historical arenas

Famous demolished or closed arenas include the Montreal Forum (1924–1996), Maple Leaf Gardens in Toronto (opened 1931, still standing as a multi-use facility), and the Halifax Forum, among the oldest arenas still in use in Canada.
