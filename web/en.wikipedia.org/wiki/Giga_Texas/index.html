Gigafactory Texas (also known as Giga Texas or Giga Austin) is a Tesla automotive and battery manufacturing plant in Travis County, Texas, southeast of Austin. Announced in July 2020 and opened in April 2022, it is one of the largest factory buildings in the world by volume.

Production

The plant began producing the Model Y crossover and later the Cybertruck pickup, which entered production in 2023. It also manufactures 4680 battery cells and serves as Tesla's corporate headquarters, relocated from California in 2021.

Site

The factory sits on roughly 2,500 acres along the Colorado River near Texas State Highway 130. Its main building is over half a mile long. Tesla has continued expanding the site with additional production and logistics facilities.
