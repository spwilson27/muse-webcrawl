Lent term

Lent term is the name of the second academic term at the University of Cambridge and several other British educational institutions. It runs from mid-January to mid-March and takes its name from Lent, the period of the Christian calendar with which it largely coincides. The corresponding term at the University of Oxford is called Hilary term.

Cambridge

At Cambridge the academic year is divided into Michaelmas term (autumn), Lent term (spring), and Easter term (summer). Lent term's full term — the period during which undergraduates are required to be in residence and lectures are held — typically begins in mid-January and ends in mid-March, spanning eight weeks. The term includes the university's Lent Bumps rowing races on the River Cam, held in late February or early March.

Other institutions

The name is also used by the London School of Economics, where Lent term is the second of three terms, and by a number of British independent and state schools whose spring term begins after Christmas, such as Epsom College and Lancaster Royal Grammar School. Some schools instead use names such as "spring term" or "Hilary term" for the same period.
