List of lost films

A lost film is a feature or short film for which no copy is known to survive in any archive or private collection. The great majority of silent-era films are lost: the Library of Congress has estimated that roughly 75 per cent of American silent features no longer exist, destroyed by nitrate decomposition, fires, neglect, or deliberate junking after the coming of sound. This list collects notable examples.

Causes of loss

Early films were printed on highly flammable cellulose nitrate stock, and studios often saw no commercial value in old titles. Vault fires — at Fox in 1937 and MGM in 1965 among them — destroyed entire catalogues. Silver reclamation during the Second World War and the routine junking of silent prints after 1930 accounted for many more. Occasionally, lost films resurface in foreign archives or mislabelled cans, as happened with the rediscovery of footage from Metropolis in Buenos Aires in 2008.
