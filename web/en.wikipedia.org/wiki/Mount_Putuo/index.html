Mount Putuo

Mount Putuo
Location: Zhoushan Islands, Zhejiang, China
Elevation: 286 m (938 ft)
Area: approx. 12.5 km²
Sacred to: Guanyin (Avalokiteśvara)

Mount Putuo (Chinese: 普陀山; pinyin: Pǔtuóshān) is an island in the East China Sea, in the Zhoushan Archipelago off the coast of Zhejiang Province, China. It is one of the Four Sacred Mountains of Chinese Buddhism and is regarded as the earthly abode and bodhimaṇḍa of Guanyin, the bodhisattva of compassion (Avalokiteśvara).

Temple complex on Mount Putuo, sacred to the bodhisattva Guanyin

Geography

The island covers about 12.5 square kilometres, with roughly 6.5 kilometres from east to west and 4.5 kilometres from north to south. Its highest point, Foding Peak (HuiJi Peak), rises about 286 metres above sea level. The island's name derives from Mount Potalaka, the mythical mountain abode of Avalokiteśvara described in Buddhist scriptures.

History

According to legend, in 863 a Japanese monk, Hui'e, was carrying a statue of Guanyin from Mount Wutai to Japan when a storm forced his ship aground near the island; taking this as a sign, he left the statue there, and a temple was established. Mount Putuo subsequently developed into a major pilgrimage centre, flourishing particularly during the Song, Ming, and Qing dynasties, when emperors patronized its temples. At its height the island held hundreds of temples, monasteries, and hermitages, and thousands of monks.

Temples

The three principal temples are Puji Temple, Fayu Temple, and Huiji Temple, which together form the heart of worship on the island. Other notable sites include the colossal 33-metre statue of Guanyin (Nanhai Guanyin) erected in 1997, the Refusing-to-Leave Guanyin Temple, and the Purple Bamboo Grove. Pilgrims traditionally ascend the 1,000-plus stone steps to Huiji Temple on Foding Peak.

Access

Mount Putuo is reached by ferry from Zhoushan and from ports serving Ningbo and Shanghai. The island restricts motor vehicles, and its temples, beaches, and wooded paths draw both pilgrims and tourists throughout the year.
