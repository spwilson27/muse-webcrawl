Casspir

The Casspir is a South African mine-resistant ambush protected vehicle (MRAP) developed in the 1970s by the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) for the South African Police during the Border War in Namibia. Its V-shaped monocoque hull deflects mine blasts away from occupants.

Design and service

The 4×4 Casspir carries a crew of two plus twelve passengers, with steel armour protecting against small arms and mines. Widely used by the police unit Koevoet, it became a symbol of apartheid-era internal security, but its blast-survival design also pioneered principles later adopted in American MRAPs in Iraq and Afghanistan, and Casspirs serve in demining and peacekeeping roles worldwide.
