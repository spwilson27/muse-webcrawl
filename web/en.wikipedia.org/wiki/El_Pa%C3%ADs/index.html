El País is a Spanish-language daily newspaper based in Madrid, founded on 4 May 1976, months after Franco's death, which became the flagship paper of Spain's democratic transition and the best-selling newspaper in Spain. Published by PRISA, it runs editions for the Americas and the digital elpais.com is among the most-read Spanish-language news sites.

History and reach

Founded by José Ortega Spottorno and edited first by Juan Luis Cebrián, El País defined the new democratic Spain; its defence of democracy during the 23-F coup attempt of 1981 is famous. It launched a Portuguese-language Brazilian edition (2013) and a substantial English-language edition of translated stories.
