Mark Fisher

Mark Fisher (1968 – 2017) was a British writer, cultural theorist, and teacher. He was associated with the Cybernetic Culture Research Unit at the University of Warwick and wrote the influential blog k-punk.

Work

Fisher's book Capitalist Realism: Is There No Alternative? (2009) argued that capitalist ideology presents itself as the only viable political and economic system, a condition he linked to widespread depression and political paralysis. His later works include Ghosts of My Life (2014) on hauntology in music and culture, and the posthumous The Weird and the Eerie (2016). He lectured at Goldsmiths, University of London. Fisher died by suicide in January 2017; his unfinished project Acid Communism was published posthumously in excerpts.
