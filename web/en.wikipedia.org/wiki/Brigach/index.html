The Brigach is a river in Baden-Württemberg, Germany. Together with the Breg, it is one of the two headstreams of the Danube: the two rivers join at Donaueschingen, and from that confluence the combined river is first called the Danube.

Course

The Brigach rises near St. Georgen im Schwarzwald in the Black Forest, at an elevation of about 925 metres. It flows south-east through Villingen before turning east to Donaueschingen, where it meets the longer Breg coming from the north. The traditional saying "Brigach und Breg bringen die Donau zu Weg" ("Brigach and Breg bring the Danube on its way") records the local claim that the true source of the Danube lies here rather than at the spring in the Donaueschingen palace park.
