Pueblo Grande Museum and Archaeological Park

Pueblo Grande Museum and Archaeological Park, now known as S'edav Va'aki Museum, in Phoenix, Arizona, preserves the ruins of a large Hohokam village and platform mound occupied roughly between 450 and 1450 CE, alongside an extensive canal system that irrigated the Salt River Valley.

Site

The park covers about 102 acres and includes a partially excavated platform mound, ballcourt, irrigation canals and replicas of Hohokam houses. A trail of about two-thirds of a mile interprets the site, and the museum displays pottery, tools and exhibits on Hohokam culture and desert archaeology.

The site was designated a National Historic Landmark and is operated by the City of Phoenix. In 2023 the museum was renamed S'edav Va'aki, an O'odham name referring to the central platform mound, following consultation with tribal communities.
