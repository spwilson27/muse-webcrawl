F-35 Lightning II

The Lockheed Martin F-35 Lightning II is a family of American single-seat, single-engine, stealth multirole combat aircraft developed under the Joint Strike Fighter programme. It is intended to perform ground attack, air defence and reconnaissance missions.

F-35 Lightning II
Manufacturer: Lockheed Martin
First flight: 15 December 2006
Variants: F-35A (CTOL), F-35B (STOVL), F-35C (carrier)

Development

Descended from the X-35 that beat Boeing's X-32 in 2001, the F-35 is the most expensive weapons programme in history, developed with eight international partners. Its sensor fusion, helmet display, and stealth shaping are hallmarks; costs, delays and software complexity drew sustained criticism.

Service

The US Marine Corps declared initial operating capability in 2015, followed by the US Air Force and Navy and many allies including the UK, Israel, Japan and Australia; Israel first used it in combat in 2018.
