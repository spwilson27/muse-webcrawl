Ijon Tichy

Ijon Tichy
Creator: Stanisław Lem
First appearance: The Star Diaries (1957)
Occupation: space explorer
Associate: Professor Tarantoga

Ijon Tichy is a fictional character and the narrator-hero of many works by the Polish science-fiction writer Stanisław Lem. A wide-eyed, accident-prone space traveller, Tichy recounts absurd and philosophically pointed voyages in the tradition of Baron Munchausen and Gulliver.

Appearances

He stars in The Star Diaries (Dzienniki gwiazdowe, 1957, expanded later), Memoirs of a Space Traveler, The Futurological Congress (1971), Inspection at the Scene of the Crime and Peace on Earth (1987). His friend Professor Tarantoga frames several tales. In the Seventh Voyage, relativistic time effects multiply Tichy into a crowd of his past and future selves quarrelling aboard his own rocket — a classic time-travel comedy.

Character and themes

Tichy's deadpan reports send up bureaucracy, theology, artificial intelligence (the rebellious computer in Voyage Eleven), evolution (the Eighth Voyage's application for Earth's membership in a galactic federation) and the limits of human reason. A German television series, Ijon Tichy: Raumpilot (2007), adapted the diaries.
