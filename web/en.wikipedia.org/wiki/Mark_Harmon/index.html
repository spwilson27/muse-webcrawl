Mark Harmon

Thomas Mark Harmon (born September 2, 1951) is an American actor. He is best known for playing Special Agent Leroy Jethro Gibbs in the television series NCIS, a role he held from 2003 to 2021, and for earlier leading roles in St. Elsewhere and Chicago Hope.

Early life and sports

Harmon was born in Burbank, California, the son of Heisman Trophy winner and sportscaster Tom Harmon and actress Elyse Knox. He played college football as a starting quarterback for the UCLA Bruins in 1972–73 and won the National Football Foundation Award as the nation’s top scholar-athlete. People magazine named him its Sexiest Man Alive in 1986.

Acting career

Harmon’s television breakthrough came as Dr. Robert Caldwell on St. Elsewhere (1983–1986); his character’s contracting of AIDS was an early treatment of the epidemic on American TV. In 2003 he began playing Gibbs on NCIS, becoming the anchor of one of the most-watched drama series in the world and serving as an executive producer. He has narrated young-Gibbs prequel material for NCIS: Origins. He is married to actress Pam Dawber.
