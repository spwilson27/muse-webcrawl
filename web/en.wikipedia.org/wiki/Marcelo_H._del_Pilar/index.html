Marcelo Hilario del Pilar y Gatmaitán (1850–1896) was a Filipino writer, lawyer, journalist and freemason, and one of the leading figures of the Philippine Propaganda Movement in Spain.

Early life

Born in Bulakan, Bulacan, he studied law at the University of Santo Tomas. His criticism of the abuses of Spanish friars made him a target of the colonial authorities, and he fled to Spain in 1888.

Propaganda work

In Madrid, del Pilar became editor of La Solidaridad, the newspaper of Filipino reformists, writing under the pen name Plaridel. He advocated assimilation of the Philippines as a province of Spain with equal rights, freedom of the press and representation in the Cortes.

Death and legacy

Impoverished, he died of tuberculosis in Barcelona in 1896, weeks before the Philippine Revolution began. He is honoured as a national hero of the Philippines; the town of his birth and many streets bear his name.
