Dragonfly

A dragonfly is a flying insect belonging to the infraorder Anisoptera under the order Odonata. Dragonflies are agile predators with two pairs of large membranous wings, elongated bodies and huge compound eyes.

Biology

Nymphs live in water for months or years, breathing through rectal gills and hunting other invertebrates and small fish; adults catch prey in flight with basket-like legs and can fly in any direction, including backwards.

Evolution

Their giant Carboniferous relatives, griffinflies such as Meganeura, had wingspans near 70 cm. About 3,000 dragonfly species are known worldwide.

Ecology

Dragonflies indicate healthy wetlands and consume large numbers of mosquitoes; many species are threatened by habitat loss.
