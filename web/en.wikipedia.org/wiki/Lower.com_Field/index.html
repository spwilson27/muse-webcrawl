Lower.com Field is a soccer-specific stadium in Columbus, Ohio, United States. It serves as the home of the Columbus Crew of Major League Soccer (MLS) and replaced the club's previous home, Historic Crew Stadium. The stadium opened on July 3, 2021, with a match against the New England Revolution that finished 2–2.

History

Plans for a new downtown stadium were announced in December 2018 as part of the effort to keep the Crew in Columbus after a proposed relocation to Austin, Texas. The new ownership group, led by the Haslam and Edwards families, committed to building a stadium in the Arena District's Astor Park neighborhood, near the confluence of the Scioto and Olentangy rivers. Construction began in October 2019.

In June 2021, the club announced a naming rights agreement with Lower.com, a Columbus-based home finance company, and the venue was named Lower.com Field.

Design

The stadium has a capacity of 20,371 spectators, including 3,356 seats in a safe-standing supporters' section known as the Nordecke. A continuous canopy roof covers all seating areas, and the venue includes 30 suites, loge boxes, and several premium clubs. The playing surface is natural grass kept in condition by a SubAir system that can heat, cool, and aerate the pitch.

Events

Beyond Crew matches, the stadium has hosted United States men's and women's national team fixtures, including World Cup qualifying matches, as well as concerts and community events. It was also the venue for the 2023 MLS Cup final, in which the Crew defeated Los Angeles FC 2–1.
