Duke of Westminster is a title in the Peerage of the United Kingdom, created in 1874 for Hugh Grosvenor, 3rd Marquess of Westminster. The Grosvenor family is among the richest in Britain, its fortune founded on land in London — Mayfair and Belgravia were developed on Grosvenor land acquired by marriage in the 17th century — and managed today through the Grosvenor Group. The family seat is Eaton Hall in Cheshire.

History

The Grosvenors descend from a Norman ancestor who came to England with William the Conqueror. The 2nd Duke, Hugh "Bendor" Grosvenor, was a famous sportsman and landowner. The 6th Duke, Gerald Grosvenor, developed the family's property empire and served in the Territorial Army, rising to major-general.

The current holder is Hugh Grosvenor, 7th Duke of Westminster, who inherited in 2016 at the age of 25 and is regularly listed among the wealthiest people in Britain.
