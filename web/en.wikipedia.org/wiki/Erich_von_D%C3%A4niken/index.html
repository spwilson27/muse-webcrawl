Erich Anton Paul von Däniken (born 14 April 1935) is a Swiss author of several pseudoscientific books that claim extraterrestrial beings influenced early human culture, including Chariots of the Gods?, published in 1968. He is regarded as a leading figure in the field of paleocontact, or "ancient astronaut" theories.

Life and career

Von Däniken was born in Zofingen, Switzerland, and educated at the Collège St-Michel in Fribourg. He worked in the hotel business before becoming a full-time author. His first book, Chariots of the Gods?, argued that structures such as the Egyptian pyramids, Stonehenge and the Nazca Lines could only have been built with help from extraterrestrial visitors. The book became an international bestseller and was adapted into a successful documentary film.

Criticism

Von Däniken's ideas have been widely rejected by scientists and historians, who regard them as pseudoscience and pseudohistory. Critics note that many of his claims misrepresent archaeological evidence and that ancient monuments are well within the technical capabilities of the cultures that built them. Despite this criticism, he has remained a popular author, selling tens of millions of books, and co-founded a short-lived theme park, Mystery Park, in Interlaken, Switzerland.

Selected works

Chariots of the Gods? (1968); Return to the Stars (1970); The Gold of the Gods (1973); The Eyes of the Sphinx (1996); History Is Wrong (2009).
