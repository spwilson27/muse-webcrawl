The Tomb of Dracula is a horror comic book series published by Marvel Comics from 1972 to 1979. It starred Count Dracula as the villain-protagonist, pursued across the world by a band of vampire hunters.

Publication history

The series found its classic form under writer Marv Wolfman and artist Gene Colan, whose long run is regarded as one of the finest horror comics of the Bronze Age. Colan drew all 70 issues of the main run.

Characters

Dracula's opponents included Quincy Harker, Rachel van Helsing, Frank Drake, Hannibal King and the vampire hunter Blade, who made his first appearance in issue #10 (1973). Other figures included Dracula's daughter Lilith and the detective inspector Chelm.

Legacy

The series has been repeatedly reprinted in collected editions and its version of Dracula remains Marvel's principal incarnation of the character.
