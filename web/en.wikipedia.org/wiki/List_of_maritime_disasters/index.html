List of maritime disasters

The following is a list of maritime disasters — shipwrecks and sinkings with heavy loss of life. The deadliest maritime disasters in history occurred in wartime: the sinking of the German evacuation ships Wilhelm Gustloff, Goya and Steuben by Soviet submarines in 1945 each killed many thousands. The deadliest peacetime disaster was the collision of the Philippine ferry Doña Paz with the tanker Vector in 1987, which killed an estimated 4,386 people. The most famous shipwreck, the Titanic (1912), killed about 1,500 people and transformed maritime safety regulation, leading to the first SOLAS convention in 1914.
