International Chamber of Shipping

ICS
Formation: 1921
Headquarters: London, United Kingdom
Type: Trade association
Members: National shipowners' associations

The International Chamber of Shipping (ICS) is the principal international trade association for merchant shipowners and operators, representing national shipowners' associations in Asia, Europe and the Americas. Its members operate over 80 per cent of the world's merchant tonnage.

Role

The ICS represents the shipping industry at international bodies, most importantly the International Maritime Organization, where it has consultative status. It works on technical, legal, employment and environmental issues, including maritime safety, seafarer welfare and the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions from shipping.

Publications

Together with industry partners, the ICS publishes the widely used Bridge Procedures Guide and other guidance on safe navigation, pollution prevention and shipboard operations.
