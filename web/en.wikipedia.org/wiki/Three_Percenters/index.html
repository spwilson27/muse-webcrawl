The Three Percenters (also rendered III%ers) are an American and Canadian far-right anti-government militia movement. The name derives from the claim — regarded by historians as inaccurate — that only three percent of American colonists fought against the British during the American Revolutionary War.

History and beliefs

The movement formed in 2008, during the presidency of Barack Obama, around opposition to perceived federal government overreach and support for unrestricted gun ownership. Its adherents typically organize as local militia and "patriot" groups rather than under a single national command.

Legal and governmental responses

Members have been involved in armed standoffs and plots, including the 2016 occupation of the Malheur National Wildlife Refuge and the 2020 plot to kidnap Michigan governor Gretchen Whitmer; some participants were present at the January 6 United States Capitol attack. In 2021 Canada designated the Three Percenters a terrorist entity.
