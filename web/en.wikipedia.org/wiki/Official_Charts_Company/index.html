Official Charts Company

Formed1990 (as CIN)
HeadquartersLondon, United Kingdom
ProductsUK Singles Chart, UK Albums Chart

The Official Charts Company (OCC) is a British organisation that compiles various official music record charts in the United Kingdom, including the UK Singles Chart, the UK Albums Chart, and numerous genre and independent charts. Its charts are broadcast by BBC Radio 1 and published widely in the media.

History

Officially sanctioned UK charts began in 1969. The Official Charts Company was formed in 1990 as Chart Information Network (CIN), a joint venture between the British Phonographic Industry (BPI) and the Entertainment Retailers Association, and took its present name in 2001. Chart data is compiled from sales and streaming information supplied by retailers, download stores, and streaming services across the UK.

Methodology

The singles chart combined physical and digital sales for decades; streaming was incorporated in 2014, initially at a rate of 100 streams to one sale, later adjusted to 150:1 for established tracks, with a chart-eligibility system (Accelerated Chart Ratio) applying higher conversion rates to older, declining songs. The company also administers the Official Charts Number 1 Award presented to chart-topping artists.

Other charts

Beyond the flagship Top 100 singles and albums charts, the OCC compiles charts for downloads, vinyl, compilations, classical, dance, rock, and many other categories, as well as the Scottish and Welsh regional charts.
