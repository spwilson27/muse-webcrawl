Wo Shing Wo

Wo Shing Wo (Chinese: 和勝和) is the oldest existing triad society in Hong Kong. It was established in 1930 and is part of the Wo group of triad societies. According to the Hong Kong police, the triad is involved in extortion, drug trafficking, gambling and prostitution.

History

Wo Shing Wo was founded in Sham Shui Po in 1930. While all other Wo group triads were founded in the nineteenth century, Wo Shing Wo was not the oldest, but at the time of writing it remains the oldest still-existing Wo group triad because its predecessors no longer exist. In 1933, when the founder died, the group changed its name slightly but retained its structure.

Structure and activities

Like other Hong Kong triads, Wo Shing Wo is organised with a chairman ("dragon head"), and members swear loyalty through traditional initiation rituals. Its activities have been linked to control of minibus routes, street markets, and entertainment venues, as well as involvement in the smuggling of counterfeit goods and human trafficking.
