Sports-related curses

A sports-related curse is a superstitious belief that a sports team, athlete, or organization is afflicted by a curse that causes a prolonged period of failure, bad luck, or near-misses. Such curses are usually said to originate from some act — a controversial trade, an insult to a rival, a broken promise, or an offended spirit — and to end only after many years, if at all. Sports curses are a popular subject in sports media and folklore, and several famous examples have entered the broader culture.

Explanations

Sceptics attribute sports curses to ordinary statistical variation, confirmation bias, and the media's appetite for narrative. Long championship droughts are not unusual in leagues with many teams, and memorable failures near the finish line tend to be remembered while ordinary seasons are forgotten. Nevertheless, curses remain a fixture of fan culture, generating rituals, merchandise, and journalistic shorthand for a franchise's misfortunes.
