Taragarh Fort, Bundi

Taragarh Fort is a hill fortress overlooking the town of Bundi in Rajasthan, India. Built in 1354 by Rao Raja Bar Singh, founder of Bundi, it is one of the oldest hill forts in Rajasthan and should not be confused with the Taragarh Fort at Ajmer.

Description

The fort crowns a steep hillside above Bundi's famous stepwells and palaces. Within its walls stand three great reservoirs cut from solid rock, which supplied the garrison in sieges, together with palaces, temples, and the massive Bhim Burj bastion, once mounting a huge cannon. Tunnels were said to run beneath the fort to the palace below.

History and setting

Taragarh guarded the Hara Chauhan rulers of Bundi for centuries. Today its crumbling walls are overgrown and inhabited by monkeys, but it offers sweeping views over Bundi and its blue-washed old town, and remains a landmark of the Hadoti region.
