BBC Studios is the main commercial subsidiary of the BBC, responsible for producing programmes and distributing BBC and independent content worldwide. It was formed by merging the BBC's in-house production arm with BBC Worldwide in April 2018, combining production, sales and distribution in one company.

Activities

BBC Studios produces or distributes global brands such as Doctor Who, Strictly Come Dancing (Dancing with the Stars), Top Gear, Planet Earth and Bluey (distribution). It operates international channels, the BritBox streaming venture with ITV, and returns profits to the BBC to supplement licence-fee income.

History

Production was separated into BBC Studios in 2016 so that it could compete to make programmes for other broadcasters. The 2018 merger with BBC Worldwide created a single commercial operation headquartered in London with offices worldwide.
