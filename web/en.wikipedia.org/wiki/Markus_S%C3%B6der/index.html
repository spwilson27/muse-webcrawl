Markus Thomas Theodor Söder (born 5 January 1967) is a German politician who has served as Minister-President of Bavaria since 2018 and as leader of the Christian Social Union (CSU) since 2019.

Career

A lawyer from Nuremberg, Söder entered the Bavarian Landtag in 1994 and held state portfolios including environment and health, then finance (2011–2018), where he built a profile as a fiscal conservative. In March 2018 he succeeded Horst Seehofer as Minister-President.

Leadership

Söder gained national prominence during the COVID-19 pandemic for advocating strict measures. He repositioned the CSU toward environmental themes, calling climate protection a central task, while maintaining hard lines on migration. He was widely discussed as a possible conservative candidate for Chancellor in 2021 but yielded to Armin Laschet; he publicly floated ambitions again ahead of the 2025 election before Friedrich Merz was chosen.
