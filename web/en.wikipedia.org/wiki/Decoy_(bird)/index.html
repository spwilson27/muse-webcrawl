Decoy (bird)

A decoy is a model of a bird, traditionally carved from wood, used by hunters to attract wildfowl, or a live bird used for the same purpose. The term also denotes a bird kept to lure others into traps.

Hunting decoys

Floating decoys imitating ducks and geese are placed on water to draw birds within range. Modern decoys are often plastic and highly detailed.

Historical use

In Britain and the Netherlands, decoys were also structures—ponds with netted tunnels—where tame ducks lured wild ducks for capture.

Collecting

Antique hand-carved decoys are prized folk art and collectibles.
