Der Klassiker

Der Klassiker ("The Classic") is the name given to football matches between Bayern Munich and Borussia Dortmund, the two most successful German clubs of the modern era. The rivalry grew in prominence from the 1990s as Dortmund emerged as Bayern's principal challenger in the Bundesliga.

History

Dortmund won back-to-back Bundesliga titles in 1995 and 1996 and the UEFA Champions League in 1997, beating Juventus in a final played in Munich. Under Jürgen Klopp, Dortmund again dominated in 2011 and 2012, winning the league twice and thrashing Bayern 5–2 in the 2012 DFB-Pokal final. The rivalry peaked with the 2013 UEFA Champions League Final at Wembley, the first all-German final, which Bayern won 2–1 through a late Arjen Robben goal.

Significance

The fixture regularly decides the Bundesliga title race and draws Germany's largest television audiences. Bayern's recruitment of Dortmund stars such as Mario Götze, Robert Lewandowski, and Mats Hummels added extra bitterness to the meetings for Dortmund supporters.
