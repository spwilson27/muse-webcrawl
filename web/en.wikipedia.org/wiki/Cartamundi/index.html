Cartamundi

Cartamundi Group is a Belgian company based in Turnhout that manufactures, produces, and sells board games, card games, collectible card games, playing cards, and game boards worldwide. The name of the company in Latin means "cards for the world." It is one of the world's largest manufacturers of playing cards.

History

Cartamundi was founded in 1970 as a joint venture between Brepols, Van Genechten, and Biermans, three Belgian printing companies with playing-card traditions in Turnhout reaching back to the 19th century. The company grew through international acquisitions of card and game manufacturers.

Acquisitions and operations

In 2015 Cartamundi acquired Hasbro's board game factories in Ireland and Massachusetts. In 2019 it acquired the United States Playing Card Company (USPC), maker of the Bicycle, Bee, Tally-Ho, and KEM brands, from Newell Brands. The group operates manufacturing and sales sites across Europe, the Americas, Asia, and Australia, producing billions of card decks per year.

Products

Cartamundi manufactures casino cards, collectible card games, board games for publishers, promotional cards, and digital-physical hybrid games.
