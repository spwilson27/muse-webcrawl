Romsdalen

Romsdalen is a valley in Møre og Romsdal county, Norway, running about 60 kilometres from the Romsdalsfjord near Åndalsnes southeast to the border with Innlandet county. The Rauma River flows through the valley, which is a traditional district within the larger Romsdal region.

Geography

The valley is flanked by some of Norway's most dramatic mountains, including Romsdalshornet (1,550 m) and the Trolltindene range, whose Trollveggen (Troll Wall) is the tallest vertical rock face in Europe, rising about 1,100 metres from the valley floor. The European route E136 highway and the Rauma railway line run through the valley, serving the town of Åndalsnes at its mouth.

Tourism

Romsdalen is a centre for mountaineering, base jumping (now restricted), fishing on the Rauma, and sightseeing; the nearby Trollstigen mountain road is one of Norway's most visited tourist routes.
