John O'Neill (8 March 1834 – 7 January 1878) was an Irish-born American soldier and a leader of the Fenian Brotherhood. He led several of the Fenian raids into Canada in the years after the American Civil War.

Life

O'Neill emigrated from Ireland to the United States as a boy and served with distinction in the Union Army during the Civil War. After the war he joined the Fenian Brotherhood, which sought to pressure Britain into granting Irish independence by attacking its North American colonies.

Fenian raids

In 1866 O'Neill led Fenian forces across the Niagara River and won the Battle of Ridgeway against Canadian militia, though the raid ultimately failed. He headed further expeditions into Canada in 1870 and 1871, the last aimed at Manitoba, all of which were broken up by American authorities. He later settled in Nebraska, founding the Irish colony of O'Neill, which bears his name.
