Domino, Marvel Comics mercenary and mutant

Domino (character)

Domino (Neena Thurman) is a character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Fabian Nicieza and artist Rob Liefeld, she first appeared in The New Mutants #98 (February 1991) — though that appearance was later retconned as the shapeshifter Copycat impersonating her, with the real Domino debuting in X-Force #8 (1992). She is a skilled mercenary and a mutant with the power to subconsciously manipulate probability in her favour.

Powers and character

Domino's mutant ability generates a "probability field" that makes unlikely positive outcomes happen around her — opponents' weapons jam, structures collapse conveniently — effectively giving her supernaturally good luck, balanced by the requirement that she actively participate in the situation. Combined with expert marksmanship, hand-to-hand combat and acrobatics, this makes her a formidable operative. She is a longtime member of X-Force and a frequent partner and romantic interest of Cable.

In other media

Domino appears in the film Deadpool 2 (2018), portrayed by Zazie Beetz, where her luck power is played for comedy and action. She has also appeared in animated series and video games in the X-Men franchise.
