Woburn, Bedfordshire

Woburn (pronounced /ˈwoʊbərn/) is a village and civil parish in Bedfordshire, England, about 5 miles (8 km) south-east of Milton Keynes and 3 miles (5 km) south of the M1 motorway junction 13. The population of the civil parish at the 2011 census was 933. The village lies on the Greensand Ridge and is best known as the location of Woburn Abbey, the seat of the Dukes of Bedford, and of the adjoining Woburn Safari Park.

History

Woburn is an ancient settlement recorded as a market town by the 10th century. A Cistercian abbey was founded here in 1145 by Hugh de Bolebec; after the Dissolution of the Monasteries the estate was granted in 1547 to John Russell, 1st Earl of Bedford, whose descendants, the Dukes of Bedford, rebuilt the abbey house in the 18th century to designs associated with Henry Flitcroft and Henry Holland. The village was rebuilt in a fashionable Georgian style after fires in the 18th century, notably the fire of 1724, giving its market place a distinctive character.

The 11th Duke's son, Herbrand Russell, and especially Henry Russell, 12th Duke, and his wife Mary ("the Flying Duchess") shaped the modern estate. In 1955 the 13th Duke, Ian Russell, opened Woburn Abbey to the public to meet death duties, pioneering the commercial stately-home business; the safari park opened in 1970 in the 3,000-acre deer park, which is famous for its herd of Père David's deer — a species saved from extinction by animals collected at Woburn.

Places of interest

  • Woburn Abbey — the ducal seat, containing art collections including works by Canaletto, Van Dyck and Reynolds, and the vault where the "Woburn" state bed is displayed.
  • Woburn Safari Park — a drive-through safari park opened in 1970.
  • Woburn Golf Club — three championship courses in the deer park, host to many professional tournaments.
  • St Mary's Church — the parish church, substantially rebuilt in the 19th century.

Transport

The village is served by Woburn Sands railway station, about 2 miles away on the Marston Vale line between Bletchley and Bedford. The A4012 and A5130 pass nearby, and the village lies just south of the M1.
