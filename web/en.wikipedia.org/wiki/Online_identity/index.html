Online identity

Related conceptsDigital footprint, anonymity, pseudonymity
ComponentsAccounts, profiles, reputation, identifiers

An online identity, also called an internet identity or internet persona, is a social identity that an Internet user establishes in online communities and websites. It may be tied to a person's real-world identity or deliberately constructed as a pseudonym or anonymous persona.

Presentation and behaviour

Users disclose identity through usernames, avatars, profiles, posts, and activity histories. Research in social psychology notes that anonymity and reduced social cues can produce online disinhibition — greater openness and self-disclosure, but also flaming and trolling. People may maintain different identities on different platforms, a practice that platforms' "real name" policies have periodically tried to restrict.

Identity and data

Beyond self-presentation, an online identity includes the data trail a person leaves — cookies, device identifiers, browsing history — which platforms and advertisers use to build profiles. This raises questions of privacy, identity theft, and control over one's digital footprint, addressed partly by data-protection law and right-to-erasure provisions.

Authentication

Verifying that an online identity corresponds to a real person underlies account security, federated login systems, and digital identity frameworks such as national e-ID schemes.
