Kentucky Three-Day Event
Location: Kentucky Horse Park, Lexington
First held: 1978
Level: CCI5*-L

The Kentucky Three-Day Event, currently sponsored as the Defender Kentucky Three-Day Event, is an eventing competition held each spring at the Kentucky Horse Park in Lexington, Kentucky. It is one of only seven CCI5*-L events worldwide, the highest level in the sport.

History

The event began in 1978 when the World Three-Day Event Championships were held at the newly opened Kentucky Horse Park. It returned annually from 1979 and for many years was sponsored by Rolex, becoming known as the Rolex Kentucky Three-Day Event.

Format

Horse and rider combinations compete in dressage, cross-country over a course designed in recent years by Derek di Grazia, and show jumping. The event draws large crowds and a major trade fair, and serves as a selection trial for U.S. Olympic and world championship teams.
