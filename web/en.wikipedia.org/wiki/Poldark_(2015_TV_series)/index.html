Poldark (2015 TV series)

Poldark is a British historical drama television series based on the novels of the same title by Winston Graham and starring Aidan Turner in the lead role. The series was written and adapted by Debbie Horsfield for the BBC, and directed by several directors throughout its run. It ran for five series, broadcast on BBC One from 2015 to 2019.

Plot

Set between 1781 and 1801 in Cornwall, the story follows Captain Ross Poldark, who returns home from the American War of Independence to find his father dead, his estate in ruins and his sweetheart Elizabeth betrothed to his cousin Francis. Ross rebuilds his fortunes by reopening his family's derelict copper mine, marries his kitchen maid Demelza Carne, and clashes repeatedly with the grasping banker George Warleggan. Later series follow the fortunes of Ross and Demelza, their children, and their friends and enemies, against a backdrop of smuggling, mining, elections and the threat of French invasion.

Reception

The series was a major ratings and critical success, credited with boosting tourism to Cornwall, and Aidan Turner's portrayal of Ross Poldark became a cultural phenomenon in the UK. The fifth and final series, written to bridge the gap between Graham's novels, concluded in August 2019.
