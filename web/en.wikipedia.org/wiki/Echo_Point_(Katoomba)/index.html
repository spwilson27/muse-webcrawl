Echo Point (Katoomba)

Part ofBlue Mountains National Park / Greater Blue Mountains Area World Heritage Site

Echo Point is a lookout in Katoomba, New South Wales, Australia, in the Blue Mountains. It is the principal viewing point for the Three Sisters rock formation and offers sweeping views over the Jamison Valley.

Description

Echo Point is located near the edge of the cliff at Katoomba and is one of the most visited sites in the Blue Mountains, attracting millions of visitors each year. The lookout provides views of the Three Sisters, Mount Solitary, and the Ruined Castle rock formation. On clear days, visitors can see the distinctive blue haze produced by eucalyptus oil droplets that gives the mountains their name.

Visitor facilities

The Echo Point visitor information centre provides displays on the geology, Aboriginal heritage, flora and fauna of the area. Walking tracks lead from Echo Point down into the valley, including the Giant Stairway near the Three Sisters, and along the cliff tops toward Katoomba Falls and the Scenic World attractions. The area is within the traditional lands of the Gundungurra and Darug peoples.
