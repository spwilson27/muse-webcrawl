Defense Logistics Agency

Founded: 1961 (as Defense Supply Agency)

Headquarters: Fort Belvoir, Virginia

Parent: U.S. Department of Defense

The Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) is a combat support agency of the United States Department of Defense. Headquartered at Fort Belvoir, Virginia, it provides logistics, acquisition, and technical services to the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and other federal agencies and allied forces.

History

The agency traces its origins to the Second World War and the post-war drive to consolidate the military services' overlapping supply systems. It was formally established in 1961 as the Defense Supply Agency and renamed the Defense Logistics Agency in 1977.

Mission

DLA manages the global supply chains for major classes of supply including food, fuel and energy, medical supplies, clothing and textiles, construction material, and industrial and aviation spare parts. Through its distribution organisation it operates a network of distribution centres, and through DLA Disposition Services it handles the reuse and disposal of excess military property. The agency employs tens of thousands of civilian and military personnel at sites in nearly every U.S. state and more than two dozen countries, and supports humanitarian assistance and disaster relief operations as well as combat logistics.
