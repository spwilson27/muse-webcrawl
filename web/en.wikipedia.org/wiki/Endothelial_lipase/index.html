Endothelial lipase

Endothelial lipase is a form of lipase secreted by vascular endothelial cells in tissues with high metabolic rates and vascularization, such as the liver, lung, kidney, and thyroid gland. It is encoded by the LIPG gene in humans and belongs to the triglyceride lipase gene family, which also includes hepatic lipase and lipoprotein lipase.

Function

Unlike other family members, endothelial lipase acts primarily as a phospholipase, hydrolyzing phospholipids in high-density lipoprotein (HDL) particles. It plays a significant role in HDL metabolism and plasma HDL cholesterol levels; loss-of-function variants in LIPG are associated with elevated HDL cholesterol, making the enzyme a target of research into cardiovascular disease.
