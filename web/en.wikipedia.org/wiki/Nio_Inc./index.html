Nio Inc. (Chinese: 蔚来; pinyin: Wèilái, stylized as NIO) is a Chinese multinational automobile manufacturer headquartered in Shanghai, specialising in premium electric vehicles. Founded in November 2014 by William Li, the company is known for its battery-swap technology, its customer-community "NIO House" showrooms, and its in-car voice assistant NOMI. Its name means "Blue Sky Coming" in Chinese, reflecting a vision of cleaner air.

History

Nio launched with the EP9 electric supercar in 2016, which set lap records at the Nürburgring and other circuits and showcased the company's engineering ambitions. Its first mass-market model, the ES8 seven-seat SUV, debuted in December 2017, followed by the ES6, EC6 coupe-SUV, and the ET7 flagship sedan. The company listed on the New York Stock Exchange in September 2018. In 2020, facing a cash crisis, Nio secured a major investment led by entities of the Hefei municipal government, after which its valuation surged. It later obtained secondary listings in Hong Kong and Singapore (2022) and launched sub-brands: the mass-market Onvo (2024) and the small-car Firefly brand (2025).

Battery swapping and services

Nio's signature feature is its network of Power Swap stations, concentrated in China with a smaller footprint in Europe, where a depleted battery can be exchanged for a charged one in a few minutes. The company sells cars under a "Battery as a Service" (BaaS) model, letting buyers purchase the vehicle without the battery and pay a monthly subscription. Nio has also built extensive charging infrastructure, destination chargers, and a network of NIO Houses and Spaces that function as clubs and co-working spaces for owners.

Motorsport

The Nio Formula E team (descended from the China Racing/NextEV squads) won the inaugural FIA Formula E drivers' championship in 2014–15 with Nelson Piquet Jr. The company departed the series after selling the team, which continued under other ownership as Envision and later ERT.
