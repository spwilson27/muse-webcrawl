Ray Holman

Ray Holman (born 1944) is a Trinidadian steelpan arranger, pannist and composer. He is regarded as one of the most innovative arrangers in the history of the steelband movement, credited with transforming the arranging style of Panorama, Trinidad and Tobago's premier steelband competition.

Career

Holman began arranging as a teenager for Invaders Steel Orchestra. In the late 1960s and early 1970s, while arranging for Starlift Steel Orchestra, he broke with convention by composing original music for Panorama rather than arranging calypsos by others; his 1972 piece "Pan on the Move" was among the first original compositions to succeed in the competition, opening a new era in Panorama music.

He went on to arrange for bands including Phase II Pan Groove, Renegades and Exodus, and has worked extensively as an educator and ambassador for pan in the United States and elsewhere. His compositions, including "Pan Woman" and "Heroes of the Nation", remain staples of the steelband repertoire.
