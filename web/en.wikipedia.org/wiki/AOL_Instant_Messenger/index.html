AOL Instant Messenger

AOL Instant Messenger (AIM)
Developer: AOL
Initial release: May 1997
Discontinued: December 15, 2017
Type: Instant messaging client

AOL Instant Messenger (AIM) was an instant messaging and presence computer program created by AOL, using the proprietary OSCAR instant messaging protocol and the TOC protocol. Launched in 1997, it was once the dominant instant messenger in North America.

History

AIM introduced the Buddy List, away messages, profiles and Buddy Icons that became cultural hallmarks of early social internet use. Its popularity declined with mobile messaging and social networks, and the service was shut down on December 15, 2017 after 20 years.

Features

Features included text chat, voice and video, file transfer, chat rooms, and games. The running man mascot became widely recognized.
