War Horse

War Horse is a 1982 children's novel by Michael Morpurgo.

Author: Michael Morpurgo
Published: 1982
Genre: Historical fiction

War Horse is a British war novel by Michael Morpurgo, published in 1982. Set during the First World War, it is narrated by Joey, a farm horse sold to the British Army and sent to the Western Front, and follows his bond with the Devon farm boy Albert Narracott, who enlists to find him.

Plot

Joey, a thoroughbred-cross foal bought at auction by a drunken farmer, is raised and trained by the farmer's son Albert. When war breaks out in 1914, Joey is sold to the cavalry and shipped to France, where he serves under officers and soldiers on both sides of the conflict, enduring charges, artillery fire and work hauling guns and ambulances. Albert, too young at first, eventually enlists and searches for Joey amid the trenches; the two are reunited shortly before the Armistice, and Joey is brought home to Devon after Albert buys him back at auction.

Adaptations

The novel was adapted into a celebrated stage play by Nick Stafford for the National Theatre in 2007, famous for its life-sized horse puppets created by the Handspring Puppet Company; the production transferred to the West End and Broadway, winning multiple Tony Awards. Steven Spielberg directed a film adaptation, War Horse, released in 2011.
