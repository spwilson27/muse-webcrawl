Red ensign

The Red Ensign or "Red Duster" is the civil ensign of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland. It is one of the British ensigns and consists of a red field with the Union Flag in the canton. It is flown by British merchant vessels and private craft.

History

The plain red ensign dates to the early 17th century, when it was one of the squadronal colors of the Royal Navy; from 1864, when naval ensigns were reorganized, the red ensign was assigned to the merchant fleet, the white ensign to the Royal Navy, and the blue ensign to government vessels. It thus became the principal flag seen on British merchant ships worldwide.

Defaced versions

Many British colonies, and later Commonwealth realms and territories, used red ensigns defaced with a local badge in the fly. The Canadian Red Ensign served as Canada's de facto national flag until the adoption of the maple leaf flag in 1965. Defaced red ensigns remain in use today as the civil ensigns of British Overseas Territories such as Bermuda and the Isle of Man, and as the basis of the flags of several Canadian provinces and Australian states in their blue-ensign forms.
