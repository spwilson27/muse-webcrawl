South Dakota Highway 87

South Dakota Highway 87 (SD 87) is a state highway in the southwestern part of South Dakota, running through the Black Hills. It is famous as the Needles Highway for much of its length through Custer State Park, passing through narrow one-lane tunnels blasted through granite spires ("needles").

Route description

SD 87 runs north–south through Custer State Park. Its southern portion passes near Wind Cave National Park, and the route shares the park's scenic drives network, joining the Peter Norbeck Scenic Byway system. The Needles Highway segment, completed in 1922, features sharp switchbacks, pigtail bridges, and single-lane tunnels including the Needles Eye Tunnel, with widths of only about 8 feet that restrict vehicle size. The road offers views of the Cathedral Spires and Sylvan Lake.

History

The Needles Highway was championed by Governor Peter Norbeck, who scouted the route on foot and horseback; engineer Scovel Johnson designed the alignment. Its construction through seemingly impassable granite was a celebrated engineering feat of its time.
