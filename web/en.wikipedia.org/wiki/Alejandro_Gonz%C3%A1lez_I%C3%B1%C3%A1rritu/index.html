Alejandro González Iñárritu

Born15 August 1963, Mexico City, Mexico
OccupationFilm director, producer, screenwriter
Notable worksAmores perros, Babel, Birdman, The Revenant

Alejandro González Iñárritu (born 1963) is a Mexican filmmaker. He is primarily known for making modern psychological drama films about the human condition. He is the first Mexican director to win the Academy Award for Best Director, which he won in consecutive years for Birdman (2014) and The Revenant (2015).

Career

Iñárritu began in radio and advertising in Mexico before his feature debut, Amores perros (2000), won international acclaim. With screenwriter Guillermo Arriaga he made 21 Grams (2003) and Babel (2006), a trilogy built on interlocking narratives. Later works include Biutiful (2010), Birdman, The Revenant and Bardo (2022).

Recognition

His films have won numerous awards at Cannes, Venice and the Academy Awards; Birdman also won Best Picture. He is, with Alfonso Cuarón and Guillermo del Toro, one of the "Three Amigos" of contemporary Mexican cinema.
