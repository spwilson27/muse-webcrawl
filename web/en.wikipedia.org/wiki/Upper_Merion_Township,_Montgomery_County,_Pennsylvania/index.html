Upper Merion Township is a township in Montgomery County, Pennsylvania, United States. The population was 33,613 at the 2020 census. Located roughly 16 miles northwest of Philadelphia along the Schuylkill River, the township is a major suburban commercial centre, home to the borough-encompassing community of King of Prussia.

History and points of interest

The township, first settled by Welsh and Swedish colonists in the 17th and 18th centuries, includes a portion of Valley Forge National Historical Park, site of the Continental Army's famous winter encampment of 1777–78. The King of Prussia Mall, one of the largest shopping malls in the United States, is located in the township, along with a major concentration of office parks at the junction of the Pennsylvania Turnpike and the Schuylkill Expressway (I-76).

Communities

Unincorporated communities within the township include King of Prussia, Bridgeport-adjacent sections, Gulph Mills, Swedeland, Swedesburg, and West Conshohocken-adjacent areas.
