Docker, Inc.

Docker, Inc. is an American technology company that develops developer tools and a platform for building, sharing, and running applications in containers. It is the company behind the open-source Docker container technology, Docker Engine, Docker Hub, and Docker Desktop.

The Docker logo depicts a whale carrying a stack of containers.

History

The company was founded in 2008 by Solomon Hykes in Paris, France, as dotCloud, a platform-as-a-service company that participated in the Y Combinator accelerator. In 2013 dotCloud released its internal container tool as the open-source Docker project, which rapidly became popular; the company renamed itself Docker, Inc. in late 2013. In 2019 the company sold its enterprise business to Mirantis and refocused on developer tools under CEO Scott Johnston.

Products

Docker, Inc.'s offerings include Docker Desktop, the Docker Hub registry, Docker Build Cloud, Docker Scout, and subscription tiers for teams and businesses.
