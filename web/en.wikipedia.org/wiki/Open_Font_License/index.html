The SIL Open Font License (OFL) is a free and open-source licence designed for fonts by SIL International. First published in 2005 and revised as version 1.1 in 2007, it is the most widely used licence for open typefaces, including families such as DejaVu, Noto, and many Google Fonts.

Terms

The licence permits use, study, modification, and redistribution of the font, including embedding in documents and bundling with software. The font itself may not be sold on its own, and modified versions must remain under the OFL. If the original font's reserved name is specified, derivatives may not use that name without permission. Documents created with the font are not encumbered.

Reception

The Free Software Foundation considers the OFL a free licence, though incompatible with the GPL unless a font exception is used for embedding. Its terms balance the interests of type designers — credit and protection of the font's name — with broad freedom for users.
