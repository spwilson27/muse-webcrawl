The XM2010 Enhanced Sniper Rifle (ESR) is a bolt-action sniper rifle developed by Remington Arms for the United States Army. It is a rechambered and extensively modernised derivative of the long-serving M24 Sniper Weapon System, converted from 7.62×51mm NATO to the longer-ranged .300 Winchester Magnum cartridge.

Design

The XM2010 features a fully adjustable folding stock, a free-floating handguard with MIL-STD-1913 accessory rails, a five-round detachable box magazine, a muzzle brake and quick-detach suppressor, and a Leupold Mark 4 telescopic sight. The conversion reuses M24 receivers while replacing the barrel, stock and other components, roughly doubling the system's effective range to about 1,200 metres.

Service

The Army awarded Remington the contract in 2010 and fielded the rifles to snipers in Afghanistan beginning in 2011, designating the type-classified system the M2010. It served alongside, and in many units replaced, the M24 for long-range engagements in mountainous terrain.
