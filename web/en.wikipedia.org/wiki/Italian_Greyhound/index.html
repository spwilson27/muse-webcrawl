Italian Greyhound

Other names: Piccolo Levriero Italiano
Origin: Italy
Height: 32–38 cm
Weight: up to about 5 kg
Group: Sighthound / toy

The Italian Greyhound (Italian: Piccolo Levriero Italiano) is an Italian breed of small sighthound, the smallest of the sighthound breeds, bred as a companion dog.

History

Small greyhound-type dogs appear in Mediterranean art from antiquity, including Roman artefacts and Renaissance paintings. The breed was a favourite at European courts; owners are said to have included Frederick the Great and Queen Victoria. It was formally developed in Italy, from which its modern name derives.

Characteristics

The Italian Greyhound is slender and elegant, with a fine short coat in various solid colours. Temperament is typically affectionate, playful and somewhat reserved with strangers. The breed is delicate-boned and can be prone to leg fractures, and like other toy breeds may be slow to house-train.
