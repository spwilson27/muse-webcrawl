Infamous (2006 film)

Infamous

Director: Douglas McGrath

Based on: Capote: In Which Various Friends, Enemies, Acquaintances and Detractors Recall His Turbulent Career by George Plimpton

Starring: Toby Jones, Sandra Bullock, Daniel Craig

Released: 2006

Infamous is a 2006 American drama film written and directed by Douglas McGrath. It depicts writer Truman Capote's research in Kansas for his true-crime book In Cold Blood, following the 1959 murders of the Clutter family.

Cast and reception

Toby Jones plays Capote, with Sandra Bullock as his friend Harper Lee and Daniel Craig as murderer Perry Smith. Critics praised Jones's performance, though the film was overshadowed by the previous year's similar film Capote, starring Philip Seymour Hoffman.
