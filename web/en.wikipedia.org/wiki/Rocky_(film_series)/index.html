Rocky is an American sports drama multimedia franchise created by Sylvester Stallone, centred on the boxing career and personal life of Rocky Balboa, a working-class Italian-American boxer from Philadelphia. Beginning with the Academy Award-winning Rocky in 1976, the series grew to six films following Rocky, followed by the Creed spin-off series focusing on Adonis Creed, the son of Rocky's rival-turned-friend Apollo Creed.

Films

The original series comprises Rocky (1976), Rocky II (1979), Rocky III (1982), Rocky IV (1985), Rocky V (1990), and Rocky Balboa (2006). The spin-off series comprises Creed (2015), Creed II (2018), and Creed III (2023). Recurring opponents and figures include Apollo Creed (Carl Weathers), Clubber Lang (Mr. T), Ivan Drago (Dolph Lundgren), and Adrian Balboa (Talia Shire).

Reception and legacy

The franchise has grossed more than $1.9 billion worldwide. The first film won the Academy Award for Best Picture. The character of Rocky and the steps of the Philadelphia Museum of Art — which Rocky famously runs up in training montages to the theme "Gonna Fly Now" — have become enduring icons of American popular culture, symbolising the underdog spirit.
