DS Automobiles is a French premium automotive marque founded in 2009 as a premium sub-brand of Citroën and established as a standalone brand in 2014–2015. It is part of Stellantis, formed from the former PSA Group. The name and logo pay homage to the classic Citroën DS of 1955–1975.

History

The DS line debuted with the DS 3 hatchback in 2010, followed by the DS 4 and DS 5. In China, DS was launched as a separate brand from the outset through a joint venture. After the creation of the PSA Group's premium division, DS became independent of Citroën badging in Europe in 2015, launching models such as the DS 7 Crossback SUV (2017), the DS 3 Crossback and E-Tense electric variants, the DS 9 saloon, and the DS 4 hatchback. DS has competed in Formula E through the DS Techeetah and later DS Penske teams, winning multiple drivers' and teams' championships. The brand has announced that its new models will be fully electric.
