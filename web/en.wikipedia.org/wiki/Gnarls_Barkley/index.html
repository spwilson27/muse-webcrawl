Gnarls Barkley

Gnarls Barkley is an American musical duo composed of singer-songwriter CeeLo Green and producer Danger Mouse (Brian Burton). The pair achieved worldwide fame in 2006 with the single "Crazy", which became the first song to reach number one in the United Kingdom purely on digital downloads.

History

Green and Burton first met while Burton was DJing at a radio station in Athens, Georgia, where Green was performing, and began collaborating years later. Their name is a playful pun on the basketball star Charles Barkley. Their debut album, St. Elsewhere (2006), blended soul, hip-hop production and psychedelic pop, and included the singles "Crazy", "Smiley Faces" and "Gone Daddy Gone" (a Violent Femmes cover).

"Crazy", built around a sample from the 1968 spaghetti western score "Nel cimitero di Tucson" by Gianfranco and Gian Piero Reverberi, topped charts around the world and won the Grammy Award for Best Urban/Alternative Performance; St. Elsewhere won Best Alternative Music Album. The duo were known for dressing in themed costumes — film characters, tennis players, Star Wars figures — in performances and photo shoots.

The second album, The Odd Couple (2008), was a darker, more introspective record, led by the single "Run (I'm a Natural Disaster)". After 2010 the duo went on an extended hiatus as both pursued other projects, though Burton and Green repeatedly stated that a third album was planned.
