Emery–Dreifuss muscular dystrophy

Emery–Dreifuss muscular dystrophy (EDMD) is a rare genetic muscular dystrophy characterised by a triad of early joint contractures, slowly progressive muscle weakness and wasting beginning in the upper arms and lower legs, and cardiac conduction defects that can be life-threatening.

Genetics

The condition is most often caused by mutations in the EMD gene encoding emerin (X-linked) or the LMNA gene encoding lamins A and C (autosomal dominant or recessive) — proteins of the nuclear envelope.

Management

There is no cure; management focuses on physiotherapy for contractures and vigilant cardiac monitoring, with pacemakers or implantable defibrillators often required to prevent sudden cardiac death.
