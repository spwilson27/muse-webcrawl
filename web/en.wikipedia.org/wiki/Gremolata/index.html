Gremolata

Gremolata (Italian: [ɡremoˈlaːta]) is a green sauce made of chopped parsley, lemon zest, and garlic. It is the usual accompaniment to the Lombard braised veal shank dish ossobuco alla milanese.

Ingredients and use

The condiment is prepared fresh and served raw so that its bright, pungent flavours contrast with rich, slow-cooked meats. A classic gremolata contains finely chopped flat-leaf parsley, grated lemon zest, and minced garlic, sometimes with a little salt. It is sprinkled over the dish at the very end of cooking or at the table.

Variations

Regional and modern variants substitute orange or lime zest for lemon, add anchovy, or use other herbs such as mint, cilantro, rosemary, or sage. Similar chopped-herb condiments include the French persillade and the Argentine chimichurri, though the latter is dressed with oil and vinegar.
