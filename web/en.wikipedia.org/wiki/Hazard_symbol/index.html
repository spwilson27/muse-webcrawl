Hazard symbols or warning symbols are recognisable symbols designed to warn about dangerous materials, locations or objects, including electrical hazards, poisons and radioactivity. International standards include the ISO 7010 safety signs, the European CLP/GHS pictograms (white diamonds with red borders, standardised under the Globally Harmonized System), and older national systems such as the EU's orange squares and the US NFPA 704 diamond.

Well-known symbols: the radiation trefoil, the biohazard symbol, the skull and crossbones (poison), the flame (flammable), corrosion (test tubes dripping on skin/metal), the exploding bomb, the exclamation mark, and Mr Yuk (a regional poison symbol for children). Design aims for instant, language-free comprehension; studies test symbols on children and across cultures, since misunderstanding a hazard symbol can be fatal.
