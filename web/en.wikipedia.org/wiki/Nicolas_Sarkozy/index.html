Nicolas Sarkozy

Nicolas Paul Stéphane Sarkozy de Nagy-Bocsa (born 28 January 1955) is a French politician who served as President of France from 2007 to 2012. A leading figure of the French centre-right, he previously served as Minister of the Interior and Minister of Finance.

Nicolas Sarkozy

Born: 28 January 1955, Paris, France

Office: President of France (2007–2012)

Party: UMP / The Republicans

Spouse: Carla Bruni (m. 2008)

Early life and career

Sarkozy was born in Paris, the son of a Hungarian immigrant father and a French mother of Greek Jewish descent. He trained as a lawyer and entered politics in Neuilly-sur-Seine, becoming its mayor at age 28. He served as budget minister under Prime Minister Édouard Balladur and supported Balladur's unsuccessful 1995 presidential bid.

Presidency

Elected president in 2007 after serving as interior minister under Jacques Chirac, Sarkozy pursued economic reforms, raised the retirement age, and took an activist role in foreign affairs, including during the 2008 Russo-Georgian War and the 2011 intervention in Libya. His presidency coincided with the 2008 financial crisis and the eurozone debt crisis. He lost re-election to François Hollande in 2012.

Later life

Sarkozy returned to lead the opposition UMP (later The Republicans) in 2014 but failed to win the party's 2016 presidential primary. After leaving office he faced several legal proceedings relating to campaign financing and influence-peddling, resulting in convictions in the 2020s.
