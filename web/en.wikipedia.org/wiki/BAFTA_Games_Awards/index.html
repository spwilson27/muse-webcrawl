British Academy Games Awards

Awarded forExcellence in video games
Presented byBritish Academy of Film and Television Arts (BAFTA)
First awarded2004
CountryUnited Kingdom

The British Academy Games Awards are an annual awards ceremony honouring outstanding achievement in the video game industry, presented by BAFTA alongside its better-known film and television awards. The first ceremony took place in 2004, honouring games released in 2003, after BAFTA elevated interactive entertainment to an equal status with film and television within its remit.

Categories

The principal award is Best Game. Other categories have included British Game, Debut Game, Evolving Game, Family, Game Beyond Entertainment, Game Design, Multiplayer, Music, Narrative, Original Property, and performance awards for Performer in a Leading Role and Performer in a Supporting Role. BAFTA also presents a Fellowship and occasional Special Awards for lifetime contribution to games.

Best Game winners

Winners of Best Game have included Call of Duty (2003), Half-Life 2, BioShock, Super Mario Galaxy, Batman: Arkham Asylum, Mass Effect 2, Portal 2, Dishonored, The Last of Us, Fallout 4, Inside, What Remains of Edith Finch, God of War (2018), Outer Wilds, Hades, Returnal, Vampire Survivors, and Baldur's Gate 3.

Ceremony

The ceremony is held in London each spring and has been hosted by comedians and broadcasters including Dara Ó Briain and, in 2024 and 2025, Phil Wang. It is streamed live and is regarded, with The Game Awards and the Golden Joystick Awards, as one of the most prominent honours in the medium.
