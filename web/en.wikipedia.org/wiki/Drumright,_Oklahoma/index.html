Drumright, Oklahoma

Drumright is a city in Creek and Payne counties in the US state of Oklahoma. The population was 2,560 at the 2020 census. Founded as an oil boomtown in 1912 after the discovery of oil in the Cushing-Drumright Oil Field, it was named for Aaron Drumright, a local landowner and oilman.

History

The town grew almost overnight with the oil boom, at one point claiming thousands of residents and extensive oilfield development. A tornado in 1956 and later earthquakes linked in studies to wastewater injection have affected the area. The Drumright Derrick and local museum preserve its oil heritage.

Geography

Drumright is located in north-eastern Oklahoma, west of Sapulpa and south-west of Tulsa, along State Highway 33 and State Highway 99.
