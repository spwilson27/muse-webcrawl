University of Hargeisa

The University of Hargeisa (Somali: Jaamacadda Hargeysa; Arabic: جامعة هرجيسا) is a public university in Hargeisa, the capital of Somaliland. Founded in 2000, it is the largest institution of higher education in Somaliland, enrolling more than 10,000 students.

History

The university was established after the Somali Civil War destroyed the country's higher education infrastructure; the Somali National University in Mogadishu had been the country's main university before the state collapsed in 1991. The University of Hargeisa was created to provide university education to students in Somaliland, with support from the Somaliland government and the Somali diaspora.

Academics

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs through colleges including medicine and health sciences, business and public administration, law, education, natural and applied sciences, information and communication technology, and Islamic studies. Its Institute for Peace and Conflict Studies was among the first graduate programs of its kind in the region. Instruction is primarily in English.
