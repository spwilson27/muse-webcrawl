Sir Patrick Michael Leigh Fermor (11 February 1915 – 10 June 2011) was a British author, scholar, and soldier, widely regarded as one of the finest travel writers of the twentieth century.

Life and work

At eighteen, Leigh Fermor set out to walk across Europe from the Hook of Holland to Constantinople, a journey he began in 1933. Decades later he recounted it in A Time of Gifts (1977) and Between the Woods and the Water (1986); a final volume, The Broken Road, was published posthumously in 2013.

During the Second World War he served with the Special Operations Executive in Crete, where he took part in the 1944 abduction of German general Heinrich Kreipe, an episode later dramatized in the book and film Ill Met by Moonlight. After the war he settled in Greece, building a house at Kardamyli in the Mani Peninsula, and wrote celebrated books on Greece including Mani and Roumeli.
