Jonathan Hickman

Jonathan Hickman (born 3 September 1972) is an American comic book writer and artist, known for creator-owned series such as The Nightly News, East of West, The Manhattan Projects and Black Monday Murders, and for major runs at Marvel Comics.

For Marvel he wrote Fantastic Four, The Avengers and New Avengers, culminating in the 2015 crossover Secret Wars. In 2019 he relaunched the X-Men line with House of X and Powers of X, beginning the Krakoa era of X-Men comics.
