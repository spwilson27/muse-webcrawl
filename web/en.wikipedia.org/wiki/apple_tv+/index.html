Apple TV+ is an American subscription streaming service owned and operated by Apple Inc. Launched on November 1, 2019, it offers a selection of original production film and television series, marketed as Apple Originals, in contrast to rival services that license large back catalogues. It is distributed through the Apple TV app, which also aggregates content from other providers.

History

Apple announced the service at a special event in March 2019, enlisting prominent creators and performers. It launched in over 100 countries with a modest slate of originals, including The Morning Show, For All Mankind, See, and Dickinson. Apple offered a free year of the service with purchases of new Apple hardware, a promotion later scaled back.

Programming

The service built critical acclaim with series such as the comedy Ted Lasso, which won multiple Emmy Awards, the thriller Severance, and the espionage drama Slow Horses. The 2021 film CODA won the Academy Award for Best Picture, making Apple TV+ the first streaming service to win the top Oscar. Other notable titles include Killers of the Flower Moon, The Tragedy of Macbeth, and the documentary Prehistoric Planet. In 2022 the service began streaming live sports, including Major League Baseball's Friday Night Baseball and, from 2023, Major League Soccer through the MLS Season Pass.

Availability and pricing

Apple TV+ is available via the Apple TV app on Apple devices, many smart televisions, streaming players, and game consoles, and on the web. Its monthly price rose over time from an introductory US$4.99, and it is also bundled in the Apple One subscription packages.
