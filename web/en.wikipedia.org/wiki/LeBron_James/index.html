LeBron James

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

LeBron James
Born: 30 December 1984, Akron, Ohio
Teams: Cleveland Cavaliers, Miami Heat, Los Angeles Lakers
Honours: Four NBA championships; four league MVPs; NBA all-time scoring leader

LeBron James is an American professional basketball player for the Los Angeles Lakers, widely considered one of the greatest players in NBA history and the central figure of the "greatest of all time" debate alongside Michael Jordan.

Drafted first overall by Cleveland straight from high school in 2003, James won two MVP awards before his controversial 2010 free-agency "Decision" took him to Miami, where he won two championships (2012, 2013). He returned to Cleveland and delivered the city's first major title in 52 years in 2016, coming back from 3–1 down against the 73-win Golden State Warriors. Joining the Lakers in 2018, he won a fourth title in 2020; in February 2023 he passed Kareem Abdul-Jabbar as the NBA's all-time leading scorer, and in 2024 he began playing alongside his son Bronny — a first for the league.

Off the court James has built a media and business empire (SpringHill Company), opened the I PROMISE public school in Akron, and been an outspoken voice on social and political issues. His combination of scoring, passing, longevity and availability across three decades of play is without precedent in the sport.
