Castellers de Vilafranca

Castellers de Vilafranca is a cultural and sporting association of castellers (human tower builders), founded in 1948 in Vilafranca del Penedès, Catalonia, Spain. It is widely regarded as one of the most successful and influential colles (teams) in the world of castells.

History and achievements

The team wears green shirts and is known as els verds (the greens). Castellers de Vilafranca were the first team to complete several of the most difficult structures in castelling history, and they have repeatedly won the Concurs de Castells, the biennial human tower competition held in Tarragona. Among their landmark achievements are the first completed 3 de 10 amb folre i manilles (a ten-level tower) in 1998, and the first 4 de 9 sense folre of the modern era.

Castells were inscribed in 2010 on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
