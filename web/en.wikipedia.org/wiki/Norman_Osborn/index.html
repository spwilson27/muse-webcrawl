Norman Osborn

Norman Virgil Osborn is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, most commonly as the Green Goblin and the archenemy of Spider-Man. Created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, he first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #14 (1964), though his identity was not revealed until issue #37.

Norman Osborn
Alter ego: Green Goblin
Creators: Stan Lee, Steve Ditko
First appearance: The Amazing Spider-Man #14 (1964)
Portrayed by: Willem Dafoe (film)

Character

Osborn is the ruthless industrialist founder of Oscorp. Exposure to an experimental strength-enhancing formula increased his strength and intellect but drove him insane; adopting the Green Goblin persona, he became Spider-Man's most personal foe. He is responsible for the death of Gwen Stacy, one of the most famous storylines in comics history, and is the father of Peter Parker's best friend Harry Osborn.

Later storylines

Presumed dead for decades, Osborn returned in the 1990s, later seizing power during the "Dark Reign" storyline as head of H.A.M.M.E.R. and leader of the Dark Avengers in Iron Patriot armour. Willem Dafoe portrayed him in Sam Raimi's Spider-Man (2002) and reprised the role in Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021).
