Westfield Garden State Plaza is a large luxury shopping mall located in Paramus and Maywood in Bergen County, New Jersey, United States, at the intersection of Route 4 and Route 17 near the Garden State Parkway. Owned by Unibail-Rodamco-Westfield, it is one of the largest malls in New Jersey and among the busiest in the United States, with roughly 2.1 million square feet (195,000 m²) of leasable space and around 300 stores.

History

The mall opened on 25 May 1957 as Garden State Plaza, an open-air centre developed by Macy's New York division, with a large Bamberger's department store as its anchor. It was one of the first suburban shopping centres in the New York metropolitan area. The centre was enclosed in the 1980s and repeatedly expanded, adding Nordstrom, JCPenney (later closed), Neiman Marcus and a 16-screen AMC cinema among other tenants. Westfield acquired the property in the 1980s and added its name to the mall in the 1990s.

Blue laws

Because Bergen County enforces strict Sunday "blue laws" prohibiting most retail sales, Garden State Plaza is closed on Sundays except for restaurants, the cinema and certain entertainment uses — a notable exception among major American malls. Traffic congestion on the surrounding highways, especially on Saturdays and during the Christmas season, is a longstanding local issue.

Tenancy

The mall is positioned upmarket, with luxury brands such as Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Tiffany & Co. and Burberry alongside department stores Nordstrom, Macy's and Neiman Marcus, and a large dining and entertainment offer.
