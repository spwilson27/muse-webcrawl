Arturo Erasmo Vidal Pardo (born 22 May 1987) is a Chilean professional footballer who plays as a midfielder. Nicknamed El Guerrero ("The Warrior") and "King Arturo," he is regarded as one of the finest Chilean players of his generation, known for his aggressive, all-action style: tough tackling, stamina, pressing and a significant goal threat from midfield, including from the penalty spot.

Club career

Vidal began his career with Colo-Colo in Santiago, winning three Chilean league titles, before moving to Bayer Leverkusen in 2007. After four seasons in the Bundesliga he joined Juventus in 2011, where he formed a celebrated midfield with Andrea Pirlo, Claudio Marchisio and Paul Pogba and won four consecutive Serie A titles and reached the 2015 Champions League final. He then spent three seasons at Bayern Munich (three Bundesliga titles), two at Barcelona (one La Liga title), and two at Inter Milan, winning Serie A again in 2021. Returning to South America, he won the Copa Libertadores with Flamengo in 2022, played for Athletico Paranaense, and rejoined Colo-Colo in 2024.

International career

Vidal debuted for Chile in 2007 and became a central figure of the country's "golden generation." He helped Chile win the Copa América in 2015 — Chile's first major trophy, won on home soil — and the Copa América Centenario in 2016, both times defeating Argentina in a final decided by penalties. He also played at the 2010 and 2014 World Cups. With more than 140 caps, he is among Chile's most-capped players and its most-capped midfielder.

Style of play

A versatile box-to-box midfielder, Vidal has been deployed as a defensive midfielder, central midfielder, attacking midfielder and occasionally in defence. His game combines combative tackling, aerial ability, work rate and late runs into the penalty area; his intensity and distinctive mohawk hairstyle made him one of the most recognisable midfielders of the 2010s.
