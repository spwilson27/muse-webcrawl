Birds of Prey (comics)

The original core team: Oracle, Black Canary and Huntress.

The Birds of Prey are a superhero team featured in comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Chuck Dixon, the team originally paired Barbara Gordon — operating as the information broker Oracle after her paralysis as Batgirl — with the martial artist and sonic-powered hero Black Canary (Dinah Lance), who carried out field missions under Oracle's direction. The Huntress (Helena Bertinelli) soon became a core member, and the roster has expanded and rotated many times since.

Publication history

The concept debuted in the one-shot Black Canary/Oracle: Birds of Prey (1996), followed by miniseries and an ongoing series launched in 1999. The title is particularly associated with writer Gail Simone, whose run beginning in 2003 is widely credited with deepening the characters and making the book a critical success. The team has been relaunched several times, including in DC's New 52 (2011) and Rebirth (2016) initiatives, with line-ups variously including Batgirl, Lady Blackhawk, Hawk and Dove, Poison Ivy, Katana, Harley Quinn and Cassandra Cain.

In other media

A short-lived television series, Birds of Prey, aired on The WB in 2002–2003. The 2020 film Birds of Prey (and the Fantabulous Emancipation of One Harley Quinn), starring Margot Robbie, brought a version of the team to cinema, and the characters have appeared in animated series and video games.
