Detective Pikachu (video game)

DeveloperCreatures Inc.
PublisherThe Pokémon Company; Nintendo
Release2016 (Japan, episodic); March 2018 (worldwide, full version)
PlatformNintendo 3DS
GenreAdventure

Detective Pikachu is an adventure video game in the Pokémon franchise, developed by Creatures Inc. and published for the Nintendo 3DS. Players control Tim Goodman, who teams up with a talking, coffee-loving Detective Pikachu to solve mysteries in Ryme City while searching for Tim's missing father.

Gameplay and release

Gameplay involves exploring environments, gathering testimony from people and Pokémon, and solving deduction puzzles. An initial episode, Birth of a New Duo, was released in Japan in February 2016; the complete game followed in March 2018 alongside a large Detective Pikachu amiibo. A sequel, Detective Pikachu Returns, was released for Nintendo Switch in 2023.

Adaptation

The game was adapted into the 2019 live-action film Pokémon Detective Pikachu, starring Justice Smith and Ryan Reynolds as the voice of Pikachu, which became one of the highest-grossing video game film adaptations.
