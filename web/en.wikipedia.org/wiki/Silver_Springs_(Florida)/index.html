Silver Springs (Florida)

FeatureFirst-magnitude artesian spring

Silver Springs is a group of artesian springs west of the Ocala National Forest in Marion County, Florida, forming the headwaters of the Silver River, a tributary of the Ocklawaha River. It is one of the largest first-magnitude springs in the United States and was one of Florida's earliest tourist attractions.

Tourism history

From the late nineteenth century, visitors came to see the remarkably clear water; glass-bottom boat tours, begun around 1878, became the signature attraction. The privately operated Silver Springs attraction featured jungle cruises, animal exhibits and lush gardens, and the springs served as a filming location for movies and television, including Tarzan films and Sea Hunt.

State park

In 2013 the former attraction merged with the adjacent state park to form Silver Springs State Park, managed publicly with an emphasis on conservation. Declining spring flow and nitrate pollution from the surrounding springshed are ongoing concerns.
