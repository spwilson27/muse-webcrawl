Matra

Matra
Industry: Aerospace, defence, automobiles
Founded: 1964 (as Matra)
Headquarters: France

Matra (Mécanique Aviation Traction) was a French industrial conglomerate active in aerospace, weapons, and automobiles. Its racing team won the Formula One World Championship in 1969 with Jackie Stewart driving the Matra MS80 (using a Ford Cosworth engine), and Matra sports cars won the 24 Hours of Le Mans three times (1972–1974).

Automobiles

Road cars included the Djet, Matra 530, Bagheera, Murena, and the pioneering Espace minivan, developed with Simca and produced by Matra for Renault. Matra also built the Rancho.

Aerospace and legacy

Matra produced missiles such as the R530 and Magic air-to-air missiles and was involved in space systems; its defence activities became part of MBDA and its aerospace interests fed into EADS/Airbus. The automobile division closed in 2003.
