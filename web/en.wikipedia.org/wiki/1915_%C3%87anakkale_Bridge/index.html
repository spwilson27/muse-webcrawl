The 1915 Çanakkale Bridge (Turkish: 1915 Çanakkale Köprüsü) is a suspension bridge across the Dardanelles strait in northwestern Turkey, connecting Gelibolu on the European side with Lapseki on the Asian side. On opening in 2022 it became the longest suspension bridge in the world.

Design

The bridge has a main span of 2,023 metres — a figure chosen to mark the centenary year of the Republic of Turkey in 2023 — surpassing Japan's Akashi Kaikyo Bridge. The total length is about 4.6 km including approach viaducts, and the towers rise 318 metres. The name "1915" commemorates the Ottoman naval victory in the Dardanelles campaign of the First World War.

Construction and opening

Built by a Turkish–South Korean consortium under a build-operate-transfer model, construction began in 2017. President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan opened the bridge on 18 March 2022, the anniversary of the 1915 naval battle. It forms part of the Kınalı–Tekirdağ–Çanakkale–Balıkesir motorway and greatly shortens road journeys between Europe and western Anatolia.
