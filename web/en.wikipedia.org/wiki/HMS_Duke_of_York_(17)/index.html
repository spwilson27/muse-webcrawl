HMS Duke of York (17)

HMS Duke of York (pennant number 17) was a King George V-class battleship of the Royal Navy, launched in February 1940 and commissioned in November 1941. She is best remembered as the flagship of Admiral Sir Bruce Fraser at the Battle of North Cape on 26 December 1943, where her 14-inch guns played the decisive role in sinking the German battleship Scharnhorst.

Launched28 February 1940
Commissioned4 November 1941
Displacement~42,000 tons (full load)
Armament10 × 14-inch guns
FateScrapped 1958

Service history

Duke of York escorted Arctic and Atlantic convoys and covered the North African landings of Operation Torch. At North Cape, in darkness and heavy seas on Boxing Day 1943, she engaged Scharnhorst at close range, scoring the hits that disabled the German ship's speed and allowed British destroyers and cruisers to finish her with torpedoes. In 1945 she joined the British Pacific Fleet as flagship and was present in Tokyo Bay for the Japanese surrender. Placed in reserve in 1951, she was broken up at Faslane in 1958.
