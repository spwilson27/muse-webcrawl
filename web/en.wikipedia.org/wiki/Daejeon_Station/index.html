Daejeon Station

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Daejeon Station (Korean: 대전역) is a railway station in Dong District, Daejeon, South Korea. It is one of the principal stations of the Korean national rail network, operated by Korail, and a key interchange between the historic Gyeongbu Line (connecting Seoul and Busan) and the Honam Line toward Gwangju and Mokpo.

History

The station opened on 1 January 1905 with the completion of the Gyeongbu Line. Its position made Daejeon a natural railway junction and contributed greatly to the growth of the city. During the Korean War the station area was heavily contested; the defence of Daejeon in July 1950 is commemorated at the station. The present station building, a large modern structure incorporating retail space, was completed in the early 2000s with the arrival of high-speed services.

Services

Daejeon is served by:

  • KTX high-speed trains on the Gyeongbu KTX line to Seoul and Busan, and on the Honam KTX line.
  • SRT high-speed services from Suseo Station in Seoul.
  • ITX-Saemaeul, Mugunghwa and Nuriro conventional trains on the Gyeongbu and Honam lines.

The station connects with Daejeon Metro Line 1 at Daejeon Station metro station, and travel time to Seoul by KTX is under one hour.

Layout

The station has multiple island platforms serving through tracks for high-speed and conventional services, with a concourse above track level. Its location in the original city centre makes it a gateway to the old downtown area of Daejeon, including the nearby Daejeon Jungang Market.
