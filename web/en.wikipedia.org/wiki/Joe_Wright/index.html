Joe Wright

Born: Joseph Wright, 25 August 1972, London, England

Occupation: Film director

Years active: 1997–present

Joe Wright (born 25 August 1972) is an English film director. He is best known for a series of literary adaptations, including Pride & Prejudice (2005), Atonement (2007), Anna Karenina (2012), and for the Winston Churchill drama Darkest Hour (2017).

Career

Wright grew up around the Little Angel Theatre, the puppet theatre founded by his parents in Islington, London. He began his career in television, winning a BAFTA for the serial Charles II: The Power and the Passion (2003). His feature debut, Pride & Prejudice, was a critical and commercial success, and Atonement won the BAFTA for Best Film; both are noted for elaborate long takes, including the Dunkirk evacuation sequence in Atonement. Later films include Hanna (2011), Pan (2015), The Woman in the Window (2021) and Cyrano (2021). Darkest Hour earned Gary Oldman the Academy Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of Churchill.

Wright has frequently collaborated with actors Keira Knightley and Saoirse Ronan and with composer Dario Marianelli.
