List of cat breeds

The domestic cat has been selectively bred into dozens of recognized breeds, differing in size, coat length and texture, color, body type, and temperament. Registries such as the Cat Fanciers' Association (CFA), The International Cat Association (TICA), and the Fédération Internationale Féline (FIFe) recognize between roughly 40 and 70 breeds each. This list gives breeds recognized by at least one major registry.
