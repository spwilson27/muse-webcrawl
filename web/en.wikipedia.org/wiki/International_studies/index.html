International studies

International studies (also international education or global studies) is an interdisciplinary academic field devoted to the study of international affairs, global issues and the relationships among societies, cultures, states and economies across national boundaries. It draws on disciplines including political science, economics, history, geography, sociology, anthropology and law.

Scope

International studies overlaps with international relations but is broader in scope, addressing topics such as globalization, human rights, international development, global health, environmental sustainability, migration and cultural exchange, rather than focusing primarily on state-to-state politics and diplomacy.

Education

Degree programmes in international studies are offered at undergraduate and graduate levels worldwide and often include foreign-language study, study abroad and area studies components. Graduates commonly work in diplomacy, international organizations, non-governmental organizations, journalism, development and international business.
