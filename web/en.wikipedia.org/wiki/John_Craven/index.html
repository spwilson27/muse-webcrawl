John Craven

John Craven
Born: John Raymond Craven, 16 August 1940, Leeds, England
Occupation: Television presenter, journalist
Known for: Newsround; Countryfile

John Raymond Craven OBE (born 16 August 1940) is an English journalist and television presenter, best known for presenting the BBC children's news programme Newsround and the rural affairs programme Countryfile.

Career

Craven was born in Leeds and began his career in print journalism in Yorkshire before moving into local radio and television with the BBC in Newcastle and Bristol. In 1972 he became the first presenter of Newsround — originally titled John Craven's Newsround — the BBC's pioneering news bulletin for children, which he fronted for some seventeen years and more than 3,000 editions. The programme established a model for presenting serious news — wars, disasters, politics — in language accessible to young audiences.

In 1989 Craven moved to Countryfile, the BBC's long-running programme about the British countryside, farming, and environmental issues, which he has presented for decades, becoming one of the most familiar faces of British rural television. He has also appeared on other programmes and written books connected with the countryside and broadcasting.

Honours

Craven was appointed an Officer of the Order of the British Empire (OBE) in 2000 for services to broadcasting. He received the Royal Television Society's Baird Medal and was elected to the Royal Television Society Hall of Fame. A train has been named in his honour.
