Peñarol

Club Atlético Peñarol is a Uruguayan professional sports club based in Montevideo, best known for its football team. Founded in 1891 as the Central Uruguay Railway Cricket Club, it is one of the most successful clubs in South American football.

Founded28 September 1891
GroundEstadio Campeón del Siglo
LeagueUruguayan Primera División
ColoursYellow and black

History

Peñarol has won the Uruguayan championship more than 50 times and the Copa Libertadores five times (1960, 1961, 1966, 1982, 1987), along with three Intercontinental Cups. It won the first-ever Copa Libertadores in 1960.

Rivalry

Its rivalry with Nacional, the Clásico Uruguayo, is one of the oldest and fiercest in world football, dating to 1900.
