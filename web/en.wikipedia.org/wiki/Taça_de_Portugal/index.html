The Taça de Portugal (Portuguese Cup) is the premier knockout football cup competition in Portugal, organised by the Portuguese Football Federation (FPF) and contested annually by professional and amateur clubs from across the country.

History

The competition began in 1938, succeeding the earlier Campeonato de Portugal. The final is traditionally held at the Estádio Nacional in Oeiras. Winners qualify for the UEFA Europa League (or its predecessors) and contest the Supertaça against the league champions.

Records

Benfica are the most successful club with 26 titles, followed by Porto and Sporting CP. The cup is noted for occasional giant-killings by lower-division sides, and its final is one of the highlights of the Portuguese football calendar.
