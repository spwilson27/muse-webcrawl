New York Racing Association

The New York Racing Association, Inc. (NYRA) is a not-for-profit corporation that operates the three largest Thoroughbred horse racing tracks in New York State: Aqueduct Racetrack in Queens, Belmont Park in Elmont, and Saratoga Race Course in Saratoga Springs.

History

NYRA was founded in 1955 as the Greater New York Association, acquiring the assets of the racing associations that previously operated the tracks; it took its present name in 1958. It holds a franchise from the State of New York to conduct racing at the three tracks, a franchise most recently extended to 2033. Belmont Park hosts the Belmont Stakes, the third leg of the American Triple Crown, while Saratoga's summer meet — highlighted by the Travers Stakes — is among the most prestigious in North American racing.

Operations

NYRA conducts more than 200 days of racing a year and handles wagering through its NYRA Bets platform. In 2023 New York State approved a loan for a major renovation of Belmont Park, with racing temporarily consolidated at Saratoga and Aqueduct during construction.
