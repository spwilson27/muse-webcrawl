Kevin Costner

Kevin Michael Costner (born January 18, 1955) is an American actor and filmmaker. He has received various accolades, including two Academy Awards, three Golden Globe Awards, and a Primetime Emmy Award. He rose to fame in the late 1980s with films such as The Untouchables (1987), Bull Durham (1988), and Field of Dreams (1989).

Career

Costner directed and starred in the epic Western Dances with Wolves (1990), which won seven Academy Awards including Best Picture and Best Director. He consolidated his stardom with Robin Hood: Prince of Thieves (1991), JFK (1991), and The Bodyguard (1992), before the expensive flop Waterworld (1995).

Later work

Costner earned renewed acclaim for the miniseries Hatfields & McCoys (2012), winning an Emmy Award, and for starring as rancher John Dutton in the television series Yellowstone (2018–2024). He also directed and starred in the multi-part Western epic Horizon: An American Saga (2024).
