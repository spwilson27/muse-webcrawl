Kaymaklı Underground City

Kaymaklı Underground City is an ancient multi-level underground settlement in the Cappadocia region of Turkey, in the town of Kaymaklı in Nevşehir Province. Carved from soft volcanic tuff, it is one of the largest of Cappadocia's underground cities.

Description

The city extends over several levels — four are open to visitors — connected by narrow tunnels, with stables, cellars, kitchens, churches and ventilation shafts. Large round stone doors could be rolled across corridors to seal off sections.

History

The earliest tunnels may date to Phrygian times, and the complex was expanded in the Byzantine era, when inhabitants used it as a refuge from Arab invasions and later raiders.
