Bender (Futurama)

Bender Bending Rodríguez (designated Bending Unit 22) is a fictional robot character in the animated television series Futurama, voiced by John DiMaggio. A wisecracking, self-centred, cigar-smoking, heavy-drinking bending unit, Bender is the comic anti-hero of the Planet Express crew and the best friend of the series' protagonist, Philip J. Fry. His catchphrases include "Bite my shiny metal ass!" and "Kill all humans".

Character

Bender was manufactured by Mom's Friendly Robot Company to bend girders for suicide booths. After meeting Fry in 2999 he quit his job and joined Planet Express, where he serves variously as ship's cook, salesman and general labour. Unlike most robots in the show, Bender runs on alcohol, which he consumes constantly to recharge his fuel cells; when deprived of it he becomes sluggish and develops a 5 o'clock rust.

Greedy, vain and kleptomaniac, Bender nonetheless repeatedly demonstrates loyalty to Fry and the crew. His chest compartment stores an implausible variety of stolen objects, and his antenna and extendable arms are frequent plot devices. Episodes centred on Bender explore his fear of mortality, his desire for fame and his occasional heroism.

Reception

Bender is consistently ranked among the most popular animated characters of all time, and DiMaggio's performance — originally conceived with a drunken, slurring quality — is considered a signature element of the series. The character's name honours John Bender, the delinquent played by Judd Nelson in The Breakfast Club.
