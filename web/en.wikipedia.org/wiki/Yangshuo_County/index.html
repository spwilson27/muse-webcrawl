Yangshuo County (simplified Chinese: 阳朔县; traditional Chinese: 陽朔縣) is a county in the northeast of Guangxi, China, administered by the prefecture-level city of Guilin. It is world-renowned for its dramatic karst scenery along the Li River and is one of China's most popular destinations for both domestic and international tourists.

Geography

Yangshuo lies about 65 kilometres south of Guilin. The Li River winds through the county between sheer limestone pinnacles, rice paddies and bamboo groves — a landscape celebrated in Chinese painting and poetry and pictured on the back of the 20-yuan banknote (a scene near Xingping). The Yulong River, a quieter tributary, is famous for bamboo rafting.

Tourism

The county seat's West Street (Xi Jie), lined with cafés, shops and language schools, has drawn backpackers since the 1980s and is sometimes called "Foreigners' Street." The area is a major centre for sport climbing, with hundreds of bolted routes on the karst towers, and for cycling through the surrounding villages. The open-air evening show Impression Liu Sanjie, directed by Zhang Yimou and staged on the Li River itself, premiered in 2004.

Administration

The county administers six towns and three townships, including Baisha, Fuli, Xingping, Putao, Gaotian and Jinbao. Its economy rests on tourism and agriculture, notably citrus fruit and rice.
