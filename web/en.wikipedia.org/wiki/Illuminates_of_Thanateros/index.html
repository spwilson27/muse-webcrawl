Illuminates of Thanateros

The Illuminates of Thanateros (IOT) is a magical organisation founded in 1978, dedicated to chaos magic. Its name combines Thanatos (death) and Eros (love/sex), representing two driving forces the order's founders saw behind magic.

History

The IOT grew out of work by Peter J. Carroll and Ray Sherwin in England. Carroll's Liber Null (1978) became a foundational text of chaos magic, and the order was organised through a "pact" of semi-autonomous temples and sections in several countries.

Practices

Chaos magic emphasises belief as a tool, sigil work, gnosis, and pragmatic, results-based experimentation rather than fixed dogma. The IOT has used a graded structure with rituals and training drawn from its published corpus.
