Otl Aicher

Otl Aicher (13 May 1922 – 1 September 1991) was a German graphic designer and typographer. He is best known for having designed the pictograms for the 1972 Olympic Games in Munich and for being one of the founders of the Ulm School of Design (Hochschule für Gestaltung Ulm).

Aicher co-founded the Ulm School in 1953 with Inge Scholl and Max Bill, and it became a leading centre of design education modelled on the Bauhaus. His pictogram system for the Munich Olympics, developed with his team, became a landmark in wayfinding and corporate identity design, using simple stick figures to communicate across languages.

He also designed the Rotis typeface family, released in 1988, and worked on corporate identities for Lufthansa, ERCO and Braun. Aicher was married to Inge Scholl, sister of Hans and Sophie Scholl of the White Rose resistance movement.
