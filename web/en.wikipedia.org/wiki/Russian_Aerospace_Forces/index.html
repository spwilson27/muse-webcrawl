The Russian Aerospace Forces (Russian: Воздушно-космические силы, VKS) is the branch of the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation responsible for air and space operations. It was formed on 1 August 2015 by merging the Russian Air Force with the Russian Aerospace Defence Forces, under a plan approved by President Vladimir Putin.

Structure

The Aerospace Forces comprise three arms: the Air Force, the Air and Missile Defence Forces (responsible for air defence and ballistic missile defence, including the Moscow A-135 anti-ballistic missile system), and the Space Forces, which operate Russia's space launch facilities, satellites, and the GLONASS navigation constellation from sites such as Plesetsk Cosmodrome.

Operations

Since their formation, the Aerospace Forces have conducted Russia's air campaign in Syria beginning in 2015 and have been heavily involved in the Russian invasion of Ukraine from 2022, conducting strike, air defence, and space-support missions.
