Home cinema

Home cinema, also called home theatre, is a home entertainment system that seeks to reproduce a movie theatre experience using video and audio equipment in a private home. A typical setup combines a large display or projector and screen with surround sound speakers and a media source.

Components

Core elements are a display (television or projector), an AV receiver, multiple loudspeakers in configurations such as 5.1 or 7.1 surround sound, a subwoofer, and sources such as Blu-ray players or streaming devices.

History

Home cinema grew in the 1980s with VHS and LaserDisc, expanded with DVD and Dolby Digital in the 1990s, and matured with high-definition and object-based audio such as Dolby Atmos in the 2010s.
