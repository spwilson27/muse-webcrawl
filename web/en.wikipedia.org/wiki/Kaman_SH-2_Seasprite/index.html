The Kaman SH-2 Seasprite is a ship-based anti-submarine warfare helicopter developed and manufactured by the American company Kaman Aircraft. It entered service with the United States Navy in 1962 as the HU2K utility helicopter and was later converted to the SH-2 anti-submarine and search-and-rescue role.

Development and variants

Designed to operate from frigates and destroyers, the Seasprite was valued for its compact size and all-weather capability. The substantially upgraded SH-2G Super Seasprite, with new engines, composite rotor blades, and modern avionics, was produced from the late 1980s and exported to Egypt, New Zealand, and Poland.

Service

The type served the U.S. Navy until 2001. The Royal New Zealand Navy operated the SH-2G(I) Super Seasprite from ANZAC-class frigates into the 2020s.
