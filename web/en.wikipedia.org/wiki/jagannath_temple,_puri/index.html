Jagannath Temple, Puri

Jagannath Temple
Location: Puri, Odisha, India
Deity: Jagannath
Festival: Ratha Yatra

The Jagannath Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to the deity Jagannath, a form of Vishnu, located in Puri in the state of Odisha on the eastern coast of India. The present temple was rebuilt from the tenth century onwards, on the site of a pre-existing temple.

Significance

The temple is one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites and is famous for its annual Ratha Yatra, or chariot festival, in which the three principal deities are hauled on huge decorated temple cars. The deities Jagannath, Balabhadra and Subhadra are worshipped in the form of large wooden idols.

Architecture

The temple complex covers an area of over 37,000 square metres and is surrounded by a high fortified wall. The main spire (shikhara) rises to about 65 metres above the inner sanctum.
