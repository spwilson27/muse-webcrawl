Frederik X

Frederik X (Frederik André Henrik Christian; born 26 May 1968) is King of Denmark. He acceded to the throne on 14 January 2024, following the abdication of his mother, Margrethe II, after a reign of exactly 52 years.

Reign: 14 January 2024 – present
Predecessor: Margrethe II
Born: 26 May 1968, Copenhagen, Denmark
Spouse: Mary Donaldson (Queen Mary)
Heir: Crown Prince Christian

Early life and education

Frederik was born at Rigshospitalet in Copenhagen, the elder son of Princess Margrethe (later Queen Margrethe II) and Prince Henrik. He studied at Krebs' Skole and the École des Roches in France, read political science at Aarhus University — with a year at Harvard under the name Frederik Henriksen — and earned a master's degree in 1995, the first Danish royal to complete a university education. He served a diplomatic posting at the Danish mission to the United Nations and at the embassy in Paris.

Military service and sports

Frederik trained in all three services, most notably as a frogman in the Royal Danish Navy's elite Frømandskorpset, where he earned the nickname "Pingo". In 2000 he took part in the Sirius Expedition, a 2,795 km dog-sledge journey across northern Greenland. An accomplished marathon runner and sailor, he founded the Royal Run mass-participation race in 2018 and was a member of the International Olympic Committee from 2009 to 2021.

Marriage and family

Frederik met Tasmanian marketing consultant Mary Donaldson in Sydney during the 2000 Olympic Games. They married on 14 May 2004 in Copenhagen Cathedral. The couple have four children: Crown Prince Christian (born 2005), Princess Isabella (2007), and twins Prince Vincent and Princess Josephine (2011).

Reign

Margrethe II announced her abdication in her New Year's address of 31 December 2023. On 14 January 2024 Frederik was proclaimed king from the balcony of Christiansborg Palace by Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen, taking the motto "Forbundet, forpligtet, for Kongeriget Danmark" ("United, committed, for the Kingdom of Denmark"). His reign has emphasised the unity of the Danish realm, including Greenland and the Faroe Islands, which he visited early in his reign.
