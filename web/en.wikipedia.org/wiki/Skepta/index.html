Skepta

Skepta
Birth name: Joseph Olaitan Adenuga Jr.
Born: 19 September 1982, Tottenham, London, England
Genres: Grime, British hip hop
Occupations: Rapper, songwriter, record producer

Joseph Olaitan Adenuga Jr. (born 19 September 1982), known professionally as Skepta, is a British grime MC, rapper, and record producer. A founding figure of the grime scene in North London, he co-founded the crew Meridian Crew and later the record label Boy Better Know (BBK) with his younger brother Jme.

Skepta first gained attention as a DJ and producer on pirate radio before becoming an MC. After early albums Greatest Hits (2007) and Microphone Champion (2009), he achieved mainstream breakthrough with the single "Shutdown" (2015) and his fourth studio album Konnichiwa (2016), which won the Mercury Prize and is credited with bringing grime to a global audience. The album reached number two on the UK Albums Chart.

Later work

His subsequent albums include Ignorance Is Bliss (2019) and the collaborative project Insomnia (2020) with Chip and Young Adz. In 2018 he was installed as a chief in his parents' home region of Nigeria, receiving the chieftaincy title Amuludun of Odo-Aje. Skepta has also collaborated with artists including Drake, A$AP Rocky, and Adele, and founded the fashion line MAINS.
