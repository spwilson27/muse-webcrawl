Just Stop Oil

Organisation
Formation: 2022
Type: Activist coalition
Purpose: Opposition to new fossil fuel licensing
Region: United Kingdom

Just Stop Oil is a British environmental activist coalition launched in February 2022. Its central demand is that the United Kingdom government halt all new licences and consents for the exploration, development and production of fossil fuels. The group uses nonviolent civil resistance and direct action, and is funded in part by the Climate Emergency Fund.

Actions

Just Stop Oil supporters have blocked oil terminals and motorways, interrupted sporting and cultural events, and staged high-profile protests at artworks and landmarks — including throwing soup at Van Gogh's Sunflowers at the National Gallery in 2022 and spraying paint at buildings in London. In June 2024 two supporters sprayed orange paint over stones at Stonehenge on the eve of the summer solstice. The protests have drawn widespread media coverage, public controversy and numerous arrests and prison sentences, and prompted new public-order legislation in the United Kingdom. In 2025 the group announced it would end its campaign of direct action after the government committed to no new oil and gas licences, holding a final march in London.
