Queen's Royal Lancers

The Queen's Royal Lancers (The Queen's Own and Royal Irish) was a cavalry regiment of the British Army, formed in 1993 by the amalgamation of the 16th/5th The Queen's Royal Lancers and the 17th/21st Lancers.

Role and service

The regiment served as a formation reconnaissance regiment of the Royal Armoured Corps, operating Scimitar and other armoured reconnaissance vehicles. It undertook tours in Bosnia, Kosovo, Iraq (including Operation Telic), and Afghanistan, and was based for much of its existence at Catterick Garrison.

Amalgamation

On 2 May 2015 the Queen's Royal Lancers amalgamated with the 9th/12th Royal Lancers to form the Royal Lancers (Queen Elizabeths' Own). The regiment perpetuated the "Death or Glory" skull-and-crossbones motto inherited from the 17th Lancers.
