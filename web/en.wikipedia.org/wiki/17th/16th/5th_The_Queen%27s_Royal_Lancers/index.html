16th/5th The Queen's Royal Lancers

The 16th/5th The Queen's Royal Lancers was a cavalry regiment of the British Army, formed in 1922 by the amalgamation of the 16th The Queen's Lancers and the 5th Royal Irish Lancers. It served as an armoured reconnaissance regiment through the Second World War and the Cold War.

History

The regiment fought in North Africa and Italy during the Second World War, notably at the Battle of Kasserine Pass and in the advance up the Italian peninsula. Post-war it served in Germany with the British Army of the Rhine and on internal security duties in Northern Ireland.

Amalgamation

In 1993, under the Options for Change reductions, the regiment was amalgamated with the 17th/21st Lancers to form the Queen's Royal Lancers, which itself merged in 2015 into the Royal Lancers. Its motto combined traditions of both predecessors, and its nickname was "the Scarlet Lancers" from the 16th's scarlet tunic.
