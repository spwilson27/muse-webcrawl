Lupin the Third

Lupin the Third (Japanese: ルパン三世, Rupan Sansei), also written as Lupin III, is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Monkey Punch. It follows the escapades of master thief Arsène Lupin III, the grandson of Arsène Lupin, the gentleman thief of Maurice Leblanc's series of novels.

Story and characters

Lupin III is joined by his partners: the ex-hitman marksman Daisuke Jigen, the swordsman Goemon Ishikawa XIII, who wields the near-indestructible blade Zantetsuken, and the femme fatale Fujiko Mine, who alternately allies with and betrays the gang. They are relentlessly pursued across the globe by Inspector Koichi Zenigata of Interpol, whose life's mission is to arrest Lupin. The tone blends caper comedy, action and occasional darker drama.

Media

The manga debuted in Manga Action in 1967 and has spawned a long-running media franchise: multiple anime television series (beginning in 1971), animated films including Hayao Miyazaki's feature directorial debut The Castle of Cagliostro (1979), television specials, OVAs, live-action films, video games and musicals. It is regarded as one of the most influential manga and anime franchises, popularising the "gentleman thief" caper in Japanese pop culture.
