Roads in Germany

Road network of Germany
Autobahns: approx. 13,000 km
Federal roads (Bundesstraßen): approx. 38,000 km
Total public roads: approx. 625,000 km
Road classification: Autobahn, Bundesstraße, Landesstraße, Kreisstraße, Gemeindestraße

Roads in Germany form a dense, hierarchically classified network serving the most populous country in the European Union. The network is best known internationally for the Autobahn system of controlled-access motorways, parts of which have no general speed limit for cars, though an advisory speed (Richtgeschwindigkeit) of 130 km/h applies and many sections are limited.

Classification

German roads are classified by the level of government responsible for them. Bundesautobahnen (federal motorways, designated "A" followed by a number) are administered by the federal government through the Autobahn GmbH. Bundesstraßen (federal highways, "B" numbers) are major interurban roads. Below these are Landesstraßen (state roads, "L"), Kreisstraßen (district roads, "K"), and Gemeindestraßen (municipal roads). Numbering of Autobahns broadly follows a pattern in which north–south routes have odd numbers and east–west routes even numbers.

History

The first motorway-like road, the AVUS in Berlin, opened in 1921. The first true intercity autobahn, between Cologne and Bonn, opened in 1932. The network was expanded greatly in the 1930s under the Reichsautobahn programme, and after the Second World War both West and East Germany developed their systems separately; following reunification in 1990, the eastern network was extensively modernised. Germany has no general toll for private cars on autobahns, though trucks pay a distance-based toll (LKW-Maut).

Regulations

Driving is on the right. Within built-up areas the default speed limit is 50 km/h, and 100 km/h outside built-up areas on ordinary roads. Winter tyres are required in wintry conditions, and many city centres are designated low-emission zones (Umweltzonen) requiring an emissions sticker. Germany has an extensive system of driving schools and a demanding driving test, and the blood alcohol limit for drivers is 0.05%.
