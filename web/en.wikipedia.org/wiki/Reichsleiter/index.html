Reichsleiter

Reichsleiter (German for "Reich leader") was the second-highest political rank in the Nazi Party (NSDAP), subordinate only to the Führer, Adolf Hitler, and above the rank of Gauleiter. Reichsleiters headed the major directorates (Reichsleitungen) of the party's national organisation.

Organisation

Each Reichsleiter was responsible for a specific sphere of party activity, such as propaganda (Joseph Goebbels), organisation (Robert Ley), the press (Max Amann), foreign affairs (Alfred Rosenberg's office) or the party treasury (Franz Xaver Schwarz). They were appointed directly by Hitler and reported to him.

Numbers and role

The number of Reichsleiters varied over the life of the regime; there were eventually around eighteen to twenty-one holders of the rank. Though nominally the party's highest stratum, real power depended on the individual's proximity to Hitler, and rivalries between Reichsleiters and other power centres were a hallmark of the Nazi state.
