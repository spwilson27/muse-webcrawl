Cherkasy Oblast is an oblast (province) in central Ukraine, straddling both banks of the middle Dnieper River. Its administrative centre is the city of Cherkasy. The oblast was created in 1954, making it one of the youngest in Ukraine, assembled from parts of neighbouring Kyiv, Poltava, and Kirovohrad oblasts.

Geography

The terrain is a rolling upland plain of the Dnieper Upland on the right bank and lowland on the left bank. The Kremenchuk and Kaniv reservoirs occupy much of the oblast's river frontage. The region lies in the forest-steppe zone with fertile black-earth (chernozem) soils.

Economy

Agriculture dominates, with grain, sugar beet, sunflower, and livestock production. Industry includes food processing, chemicals (notably the Azot fertilizer plant in Cherkasy), machinery, and light industry. The Kaniv Hydroelectric Station operates on the Dnieper.

History and culture

The area has deep historical roots: the Trypillian culture flourished nearby in prehistory, and the region was central to the Cossack era — Chyhyryn served as a hetman capital under Bohdan Khmelnytsky. The poet Taras Shevchenko was born in the village of Moryntsi and is buried on Chernecha Hill near Kaniv, a major national memorial. Uman is home to the Sofiyivka Park, a celebrated landscape park.
