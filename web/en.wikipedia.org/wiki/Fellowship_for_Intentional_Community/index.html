The Fellowship for Intentional Community (FIC) is a nonprofit organization in the United States that serves as a network and clearinghouse for intentional communities—including communes, cohousing, ecovillages, co-ops, and land trusts—and for people interested in cooperative and communal living.

History

The organization's roots trace to 1937 gatherings of communal groups; the modern fellowship took shape in the 1940s–1950s as the Fellowship of Intentional Communities and was reorganized in 1986 under its present name. It is based in northeastern Missouri, near several intentional communities including Sandhill Farm and Dancing Rabbit Ecovillage.

Publications and activities

The FIC publishes the Communities Directory, a comprehensive listing of intentional communities (now primarily maintained online at ic.org), and Communities magazine, founded in 1972 and published by the FIC since the 1990s (later transferred to the Global Ecovillage Network–US). It also hosts conferences and gatherings (such as the Art of Community), provides fiscal sponsorship and educational resources, and promotes cooperative culture, consensus decision-making, and sustainable living.
