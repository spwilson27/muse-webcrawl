Newsround (stylised newsround, originally John Craven's Newsround) is a BBC children's news programme, first broadcast on 4 April 1972. It was one of the world's first television news bulletins made specifically for children, and remains a flagship of the CBBC channel.

History

The programme was devised by BBC children's department producer Edward Barnes, who recognised that children were excluded by adult news formats. Its first and longest-serving presenter, John Craven, fronted it from 1972 to 1989, explaining stories such as the Challenger disaster and the fall of the Berlin Wall in language aimed at viewers aged roughly 6 to 12.

Format

Bulletins run through the school day on CBBC, with an online service of stories, quizzes and explainers. Newsround is credited by journalists with pioneering accessible explanation of difficult news for young audiences.
