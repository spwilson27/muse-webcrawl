GOOD Music (an acronym for Getting Out Our Dreams) is an American record label founded by rapper and producer Kanye West in 2004. The label was distributed through Def Jam Recordings for much of its history.

History

West founded the label after the success of his debut album The College Dropout. Early signings included John Legend and Common, whose albums Get Lifted (2004) and Be (2005) were early releases. The roster at various times included Big Sean, Pusha T, Kid Cudi, Q-Tip, Teyana Taylor, Desiigner, and 2 Chainz.

Releases

The label issued the compilation album Cruel Summer in 2012, preceded by the single "Mercy". Its "GOOD Fridays" free-music series in 2010 preceded West's album My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy. Pusha T served as label president from 2015.
