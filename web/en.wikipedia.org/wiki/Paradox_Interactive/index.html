Paradox Interactive

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Paradox Interactive AB is a Swedish video game publisher based in Stockholm. The company started out as the video game division of Target Games and then Paradox Entertainment; its parent company acquired its own publishing division, which had been split from the studio division, Paradox Development Studio.

Paradox is best known for its grand strategy games developed by Paradox Development Studio, including the Europa Universalis, Hearts of Iron, Crusader Kings, Victoria, and Stellaris series. It also publishes games from other developers, including Cities: Skylines, Pillars of Eternity, and Surviving Mars. Paradox Interactive was listed on Nasdaq First North in 2016, and the Chinese conglomerate Tencent holds a minority stake.

History

Paradox Interactive traces its roots to Target Games, a Swedish board game and role-playing game company. After Target's bankruptcy in 1999, the video game division continued as Paradox Entertainment. In 2004, CEO Theodore Bergquist purchased the video game division, forming the company that became Paradox Interactive, while the intellectual property library remained with Paradox Entertainment (later Cabinet Entertainment).

From the mid-2000s the company grew through digital distribution, and its grand strategy titles gained a large, dedicated audience. Success with Magicka (2011), Crusader Kings II (2012), Europa Universalis IV (2013), and Cities: Skylines (2015) fuelled expansion. Paradox acquired White Wolf Publishing from CCP Games in 2015, and purchased studios including Triumph Studios (2017) and Harebrained Schemes (2018). In 2018 Tencent acquired a stake of about 5%, later increasing it.

Game characteristics

Paradox's in-house grand strategy games are real-time (but pausable) strategy games played on a detailed historical map, emphasizing diplomacy, economy, and warfare over long in-game periods, and supporting a large modding community and post-release expansion packs (DLC). Its published titles span city-building, role-playing, and simulation genres.

Studios

Subsidiaries include Paradox Development Studio (Stockholm), Paradox Tinto (Barcelona), Paradox Arctic (Umeå), Triumph Studios (Delft), and Colossal Order as an external partner for Cities: Skylines. The company also operates the Paradox Arc publishing label for smaller titles.
