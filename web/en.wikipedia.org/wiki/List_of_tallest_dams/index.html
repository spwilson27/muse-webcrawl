List of tallest dams

This is a list of the tallest dams in the world, ranked by structural height. The tallest dam in the world is the Jinping-I Dam in China, a double-curvature arch dam on the Yalong River standing 305 metres (1,001 ft) high. The tallest embankment dam is the Nurek Dam in Tajikistan at 300 metres (980 ft).

By type

Tall dams are generally classified as arch, gravity, arch-gravity or embankment (earth-fill or rock-fill). Arch dams transfer water pressure to the canyon walls and suit narrow gorges; embankment dams rely on their mass and can be built on wider valleys. Most of the tallest dams are used primarily for hydroelectric power generation, often combined with irrigation and flood control.
