John Tobias

John Tobias (born August 24, 1962) is an American comic book artist, graphic designer, video game designer and writer. He is best known as the co-creator, with Ed Boon, of the Mortal Kombat fighting game franchise.

Career

Tobias began his career at Midway Games as an artist, working on titles such as Smash TV. Together with programmer Ed Boon, he created the original Mortal Kombat (1992), designing its characters, digitised graphics and the fictional mythology of its universe, including the backstories of fighters such as Liu Kang, Scorpion and Sub-Zero. The game's success spawned a major media franchise.

After Mortal Kombat

Tobias left Midway in 1999, during the development of Mortal Kombat: Special Forces. He later co-founded Studio Gigante, which developed the Xbox fighting game Tao Feng: Fist of the Lotus (2003). He has continued to work in comics and game design consulting.
