List of stock market indices

A stock market index measures the value of a section of a stock market and is used by investors as a benchmark. This list gives the principal indices by region.

Global

  • MSCI World Index
  • MSCI All Country World Index (ACWI)
  • FTSE All-World Index
  • S&P Global 1200

IndexDescription
Dow Jones Industrial Average30 large blue-chip companies; price-weighted; first published 1896.
S&P 500500 large-cap US companies; the most widely followed US benchmark.
Nasdaq CompositeAll stocks listed on the Nasdaq exchange; technology-heavy.
Nasdaq-100100 largest non-financial Nasdaq companies.
Russell 2000Small-cap index of 2,000 companies.
Wilshire 5000Total US market index.
VIXVolatility index derived from S&P 500 options (the "fear gauge").
