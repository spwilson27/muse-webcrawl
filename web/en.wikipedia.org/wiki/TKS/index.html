TKS

TKS (Russian: Транспортный корабль снабжения, "Transport Supply Spacecraft") was a Soviet spacecraft conceived in the late 1960s for resupply flights to the military Almaz space station.

Design

The TKS consisted of a functional cargo block and a return capsule capable of carrying crew. It was designed to launch atop the Proton rocket and could operate either crewed or as an automated cargo vehicle.

Legacy

Although the crewed Almaz programme was cancelled, TKS hardware flew in uncrewed form, and its functional cargo block became the basis for later Soviet and Russian station modules, including Zarya of the International Space Station.
