Tomica

Tomica (トミカ) is a line of die-cast toy vehicles produced since 1970 by Takara Tomy (formerly Tomy) of Japan. The cars are typically made to approximately 1/64 scale, though the scale varies to keep each model about three inches long.

Tomica models are based mainly on Japanese cars — sedans, buses, trucks, emergency vehicles, and trains — but also include foreign cars and licensed characters. Each model is sold in a distinctive red-and-white box, and numbering runs in series, with older numbers retired and replaced over time.

Collecting

Tomica is one of Japan's most popular toy car brands, comparable in its home market to Hot Wheels and Matchbox elsewhere. Limited editions, event exclusives, and long-running models are avidly collected. Related lines include Tomica Premium, with more detailed finishes, and the larger Tomica World and Plarail play systems.
