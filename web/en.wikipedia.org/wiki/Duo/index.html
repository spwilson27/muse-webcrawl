Duo

A duo is a pair of people or things, most commonly two musicians performing together, or a composition written for two performers. In music, a duo is the smallest ensemble after a solo; two performers with equal importance are sometimes distinguished as a duet, though terminology varies by tradition. Famous musical duos range from classical piano duos and violin–piano partnerships to popular double acts such as Simon & Garfunkel, Daft Punk and Outkast.

Other uses

The word is used broadly for any partnership of two: comedy double acts, dance partners, detective teams in fiction, and, by extension, product names and services marketed around pairs or two-factor concepts—such as Google Duo, a video-calling application launched in 2016 and later merged into Google Meet, and Duo Security, a two-factor authentication service.

Etymology

"Duo" derives from the Latin word for "two", passing through Italian, where it became a standard musical term in the 18th century alongside trio, quartet and larger ensemble designations.
