Orunmila

Orunmila (Yoruba: Òrúnmìlà), also rendered Orula or Orunla, is the orisha of wisdom, knowledge, and divination in the religion of the Yoruba people of south-western Nigeria and in its diaspora traditions, including Cuban Santería (Regla de Ocha) and Brazilian Candomblé. He is considered the grand priest who witnessed the creation of the universe and of every human destiny, and he presides over the Ifá divination system, through which devotees consult him for guidance.

Mythology

In Yoruba tradition, Orunmila was present when Olodumare created existence and when each person chose their orí — their personal destiny — before birth. Because he witnessed these choices, he alone knows the fate of every individual, and he is accordingly called elérí ípín, "witness of destiny." Myths recount his descent to earth, his time at Ile-Ife, and his instruction of the first diviners (babalawo, "fathers of the secrets"), to whom he entrusted the palm nuts and divining chain of Ifá before returning to heaven.

Ifá divination

Consultation with Orunmila proceeds through Ifá divination, performed by a babalawo. Using sixteen sacred palm nuts (ikin) or a divining chain (opele), the diviner generates one of 256 signs (odù), each associated with a large corpus of memorised verses, stories, and prescriptions. The verses interpret the client's situation and prescribe sacrifices or offerings (ebo) to align their life with their destiny. UNESCO proclaimed the Ifá divination system a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity in 2005.

Worship and diaspora

Orunmila is venerated on his feast days and receives offerings distinct from those of other orishas; his colours are green and yellow, representing the palm leaves and wisdom. His priests are exclusively male in traditional Yoruba practice. In the Americas, his cult was preserved under Catholic syncretism — in Cuba he is associated with Saint Francis of Assisi — and Orula remains central to Santería initiation, where the "hand of Ifá" (mano de Orula) ceremony reveals a devotee's guardian orisha and destiny.
