Judiciary of Georgia (country)

The judiciary of Georgia is the system of courts in the country of Georgia. It comprises the Constitutional Court, the Supreme Court, courts of appeal, and district and city courts, operating under the Constitution of Georgia and the Organic Law on Common Courts.

Structure

The Constitutional Court, based in Batumi, reviews the constitutionality of laws and acts. The Supreme Court in Tbilisi is the highest court of cassation for civil, criminal and administrative cases, while district (city) courts hear cases at first instance.

Reform

Georgia has carried out successive 'waves' of judicial reform since the early 2000s aimed at strengthening judicial independence, though the appointment process and alleged political influence remain subjects of domestic and international debate.
