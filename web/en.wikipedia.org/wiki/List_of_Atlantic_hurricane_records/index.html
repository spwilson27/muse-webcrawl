List of Atlantic hurricane records

This article is a list of Atlantic hurricane records, covering extremes of intensity, size, rainfall, longevity, cost and season activity among tropical cyclones in the Atlantic basin since reliable records began in 1851.

Season records

The busiest season on record was 2020, with 30 named storms, exhausting the standard naming list and requiring the Greek alphabet for only the second time (after 2005). The 2005 season produced the most major hurricanes (seven), the most Category 5 storms (four: Emily, Katrina, Rita and Wilma) and the costliest single storm, Hurricane Katrina, whose damage total of about $125 billion (tied with Hurricane Harvey of 2017) makes it the costliest US natural disaster. The deadliest Atlantic hurricane was the Great Hurricane of 1780, which killed an estimated 22,000 people in the Caribbean.

Other records

The largest storm by gale-force wind diameter was Hurricane Sandy (2012), at about 1,150 miles (1,850 km) across. The longest-lived Atlantic tropical cyclone was the 1899 San Ciriaco hurricane, which lasted 28 days. Hurricane John aside (a Pacific storm), tropical storm Ginger (1971) spent 27.25 days as a tropical storm. The wettest storm in the United States was Hurricane Harvey (2017), which dropped 60.58 inches (1,539 mm) of rain near Nederland, Texas.
