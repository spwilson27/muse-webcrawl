Thomas Buchanan McGuire Jr. (1 August 1920 – 7 January 1945) was an American fighter pilot and the second-highest-scoring American ace of the Second World War, credited with 38 aerial victories. Flying the P-38 Lightning in the Pacific theatre with the 475th Fighter Group, he was rivalled only by Richard Bong (40 victories), with whom he served and competed.

Death and honours

On 7 January 1945, while attempting to aid a fellow pilot during a low-altitude dogfight over Negros Island in the Philippines, McGuire's P-38 stalled and crashed; he was posthumously awarded the Medal of Honor for a volunteer mission over Luzon on 25–26 December 1944 in which he shot down seven Japanese fighters in two days. McGuire Air Force Base in New Jersey (now part of Joint Base McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst) is named in his honour.
