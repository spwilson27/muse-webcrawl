Betty Rubble is a fictional character in the animated television series The Flintstones (1960–1966), produced by Hanna-Barbera. She is the wife of Barney Rubble and the best friend and next-door neighbour of Wilma Flintstone, living in the Stone Age town of Bedrock.

Character

Betty is depicted as a devoted homemaker, generally sweeter and more even-tempered than Wilma, though fully capable of joining her friend in schemes and in standing up to their husbands, Fred and Barney. Her trademark features are her black hair worn with a small blue bow, her blue dress and her distinctive giggle. In the original series Betty and Barney were childless — a notable point for a 1960s family sitcom — until the couple adopted the immensely strong toddler Bamm-Bamm in the fourth season.

Portrayals

Betty was originally voiced by Bea Benaderet, who also voiced many other Hanna-Barbera characters; after Benaderet left to star in Petticoat Junction, Gerry Johnson took over the role for the remaining seasons. In the 1994 live-action film The Flintstones, Betty was played by Rosie O'Donnell, and Jane Krakowski played her in the 2000 prequel The Flintstones in Viva Rock Vegas. Betty has appeared in the many Flintstones spin-offs, specials and commercials, including the long-running advertisements for Flintstones vitamin supplements — where, in a piece of popular trivia, she was long absent from the chewable vitamin shapes because her slim waist made the candy moulds break, before finally being included in 1995 after a public campaign.
