International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking

The International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking is a United Nations international day observed annually on 26 June since 1988, the date chosen to commemorate Lin Zexu's dismantling of the opium trade in Humen, Guangdong, in 1839, just before the First Opium War.

History

The day was established by United Nations General Assembly Resolution 42/112 of 7 December 1987. It expresses the UN's determination to strengthen action and cooperation toward a society free of drug abuse.

Observance

The United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) launches its annual World Drug Report around this date and promotes a yearly theme under the long-running campaign slogan "Listen First" and successors. Events include public awareness campaigns, community programs, and drug-destruction ceremonies in some countries.
