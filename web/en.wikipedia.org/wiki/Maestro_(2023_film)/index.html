Maestro (2023 film)

Maestro is a 2023 American biographical romantic drama film that centers on the relationship between American composer Leonard Bernstein and his wife Felicia Montealegre. It was directed by Bradley Cooper, from a screenplay he wrote with Josh Singer. Cooper also stars as Bernstein, alongside Carey Mulligan as Montealegre. The film was produced by Cooper, Martin Scorsese, Steven Spielberg, Kristie Macosko Krieger, Fred Berner and Amy Durning.

Plot

The film opens with an elderly Bernstein giving an interview at his piano, then flashes back to 1943, when the 25-year-old assistant conductor of the New York Philharmonic makes a sensational last-minute conducting debut at Carnegie Hall. He meets actress Felicia Montealegre at a party; their courtship and marriage unfold amid Bernstein's rising fame, his affairs with men, Felicia's knowing acceptance and eventual disillusionment, and her illness and death from cancer in 1978. Set pieces include Bernstein conducting Mahler's Resurrection Symphony at Ely Cathedral in 1973.

Release and reception

Maestro premiered at the 80th Venice International Film Festival in September 2023, received a limited theatrical release in November, and streamed on Netflix from 20 December 2023. It received generally positive reviews praising the performances and cinematography, and was nominated for seven Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Actor and Best Actress, though it won none. Cooper's use of a prosthetic nose to portray Bernstein prompted public discussion, which the Bernstein family publicly defended.
