Citation1981 c. 69
ParliamentParliament of the United Kingdom
ExtentGreat Britain (with variations)
PurposeProtection of wild birds, animals, plants, and sites (SSSIs)

The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (c. 69) is an Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom and the principal legislation protecting wildlife in Great Britain. It implements the Bern Convention and the European Communities Birds Directive in national law.

Provisions

Part I protects wild birds (making it an offence to intentionally kill, injure, or take any wild bird, or to take or destroy nests and eggs), other animals listed in Schedule 5, and plants in Schedule 8, and prohibits the release of non-native species listed in Schedule 9. Part II underpins the notification and protection of Sites of Special Scientific Interest (SSSIs) and national nature reserves. Part III concerns public rights of way. Species schedules are reviewed every five years by the statutory nature conservation agencies.
