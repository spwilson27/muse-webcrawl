Bangsamoro Organic Law

Long titleOrganic Law for the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao
CitationRepublic Act No. 11054
SignedJuly 26, 2018, by President Rodrigo Duterte
StatusIn force; ratified by plebiscite in January–February 2019

The Bangsamoro Organic Law (BOL), officially Republic Act No. 11054, is a Philippine law that provides for the basic structure of government of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM). It abolished the former Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao and implemented the 2014 Comprehensive Agreement on the Bangsamoro between the Government of the Philippines and the Moro Islamic Liberation Front (MILF).

Provisions

The law establishes a parliamentary form of government for the region, headed by a chief minister accountable to an 80-member Bangsamoro Parliament. It provides for a block grant equivalent to a fixed share of national internal revenue, a regional share of taxes and resource revenues, a Shari'ah justice system for Muslims alongside the regular courts, and the phased decommissioning of MILF combatants. The region has authority over most areas of governance except foreign affairs, defence, currency and citizenship, which remain with the national government.

Ratification and transition

The law was signed by President Rodrigo Duterte on July 26, 2018, and ratified in plebiscites held on January 21 and February 6, 2019. The Bangsamoro Transition Authority, dominated by the MILF, was appointed to govern the region during a transition period that was later extended, with the first regular parliamentary elections scheduled to follow.
