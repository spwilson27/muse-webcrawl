Crofton Pumping Station

LocationCrofton, Wiltshire, England
Opened1809
Engines1812 Boulton & Watt; 1846 Harvey engine

Crofton Pumping Station, near the village of Crofton in Wiltshire, England, is a historic pumping station on the Kennet and Avon Canal. It houses two working steam beam engines that pump water from Wilton Water up to the canal's summit level.

Engines

The station's 1812 Boulton and Watt engine is believed to be the oldest working beam engine in the world still performing its original task in its original location. A second engine, built by Harveys of Hayle in 1846, replaced an earlier 1809 engine. Both were retired in 1952 in favour of electric pumps but restored by volunteers of the Kennet and Avon Canal Trust.

Present day

The Grade I listed station is operated by the Canal & River Trust and opens to visitors on steaming weekends through the year, when the engines again supply the canal as they have for two centuries.
