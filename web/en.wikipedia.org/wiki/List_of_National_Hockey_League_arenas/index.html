List of National Hockey League arenas

The List of National Hockey League arenas enumerates the buildings used by the 32 franchises of the NHL. Most modern NHL arenas are multipurpose venues shared with basketball teams and used for concerts. Seating capacities for hockey typically range from about 15,000 to just over 21,000.

History

The league's oldest continuously used venue is Madison Square Garden, though it has been thoroughly renovated. Several classic barns — the Montreal Forum, Chicago Stadium, Boston Garden and Maple Leaf Gardens — were replaced during the arena-building boom of the 1990s. The Arizona Coyotes' relocation to Utah in 2024 brought the Delta Center in Salt Lake City into the NHL.
