Extreme E

Extreme E is an FIA-sanctioned international off-road racing series that uses spec silhouette electric SUVs to race in remote parts of the world, such as the Saudi Arabian desert and Greenland. The series was founded by Formula E founder Alejandro Agag and former racing driver Gil de Ferran, and held its first event, the Desert X-Prix in Al-'Ula, Saudi Arabia, in April 2021.

Format and vehicles

All teams use the Spark-built Odyssey 21 electric SUV, with a battery supplied by Williams Advanced Engineering. Each team fields one female and one male driver, who share driving duties — a first in top-level motorsport intended to promote gender equality. Events, called X-Prix, take place over two days with qualifying sessions, semi-finals, and a final. Equipment is transported between venues by the ship RMS St Helena, which serves as a floating paddock and research vessel.

Environmental mission

Extreme E's stated purpose is to highlight climate change and environmental damage in the ecosystems it visits, pairing each race with a "Legacy Programme" of local environmental projects. Venues have included Senegal, Greenland, Sardinia, Chile's Atacama Desert, and Saudi Arabia. Teams have included Rosberg X Racing, founded by Nico Rosberg, which won the inaugural championship, and entries associated with Lewis Hamilton (X44) and Jenson Button.
