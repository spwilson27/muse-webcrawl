Digital Mars

IndustrySoftware
FounderWalter Bright
ProductsCompilers for C, C++, and D

Digital Mars is a small American software company owned by Walter Bright, best known for its compilers for the C, C++, and D programming languages. Its C++ compiler was notable in the 1990s as a fast, affordable implementation originating in Bright's earlier Zortech C++.

History

Bright wrote what is considered the first native C++ compiler (Zortech C++) in the late 1980s. After Zortech was acquired by Symantec, the compiler was sold as Symantec C++ before Bright reacquired the technology and released it under the Digital Mars name. Digital Mars produced the reference implementation (DMD) of the D language, designed by Bright with Andrei Alexandrescu.
