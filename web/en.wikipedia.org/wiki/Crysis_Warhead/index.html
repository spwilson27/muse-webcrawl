Crysis Warhead

Crysis Warhead is a first-person shooter video game developed by Crytek Budapest and published by Electronic Arts in September 2008 for Microsoft Windows. It is a standalone expansion to the 2007 game Crysis, telling a parallel story set on the other side of the Lingshan Islands during the same events.

Plot and gameplay

Players control Sergeant Michael "Psycho" Sykes, a member of Raptor Team, who is tasked with pursuing a North Korean colonel and recovering a captured alien container that the North Koreans intend to use as a weapon. The game adds new weapons, including dual-wieldable machine pistols, a grenade launcher and claymores, and new vehicles. It shipped with Crysis Wars, an expanded multiplayer component with additional maps and the team-based Power Struggle mode.

Reception

Warhead was praised for tighter pacing, better performance optimisation than the original game, and more action-focused set pieces, and it received a remaster as part of the Crysis Remastered Trilogy in 2021. See also Crysis (series) and Crysis 2.
