List of men's footballers with 100 or more international goals

Scoring 100 or more goals in international football is a rare achievement: only a handful of men's players have reached a century of goals for their national team since the first, Ali Daei of Iran, passed the mark in 2004.

Milestones

Ali Daei reached 100 goals in November 2004 against Laos. Cristiano Ronaldo matched and passed Daei's record of 109 in 2021, and Lionel Messi reached the century in March 2023 against Curaçao.
