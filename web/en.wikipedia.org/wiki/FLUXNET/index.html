FLUXNET

FLUXNET is a global network of micrometeorological tower sites that measure the exchanges of carbon dioxide, water vapour, and energy between the biosphere and atmosphere. The network comprises hundreds of flux tower sites across terrestrial ecosystems worldwide, coordinated through regional networks such as AmeriFlux, ICOS, and AsiaFlux.

A flux tower instrumented for eddy covariance measurements.

Method

Sites use the eddy covariance method, in which fast-response sensors measure vertical wind velocity and gas concentrations many times per second; the covariance of these fluctuations gives the net flux of carbon, water, and energy between the land surface and the atmosphere.

Data and use

FLUXNET data products, including the FLUXNET2015 dataset, are widely used to study the terrestrial carbon cycle, validate satellite remote-sensing products and land-surface models, and understand how ecosystems respond to climate variability and change.
