Barry Allen

Bartholomew Henry "Barry" Allen is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. He is the second character to take the name the Flash, first appearing in Showcase #4 (October 1956), created by writer Robert Kanigher and artist Carmine Infantino.

Fictional biography

Barry Allen is a forensic scientist with the Central City Police Department. Struck by lightning while being doused with chemicals in his laboratory, he gained superhuman speed and reflexes through a connection to the extradimensional Speed Force. As the Flash, he became a founding member of the Justice League.

His debut is generally credited with launching the Silver Age of Comic Books, reviving the superhero genre. Barry died heroically in the 1985–86 crossover Crisis on Infinite Earths, sacrificing himself to destroy the Anti-Monitor's antimatter cannon; he remained dead for over two decades before returning in Final Crisis (2008) and The Flash: Rebirth (2009).

In other media

Barry Allen is the protagonist of The CW television series The Flash (2014–2023), played by Grant Gustin, and has been portrayed in film by Ezra Miller in the DC Extended Universe.
