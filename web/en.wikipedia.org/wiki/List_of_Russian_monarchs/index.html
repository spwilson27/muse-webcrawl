List of Russian monarchs

This is a list of all reigning monarchs in the history of Russia, from the early rulers of Kievan Rus' through the Grand Princes of Moscow, the Tsars of Russia, and the Emperors of the Russian Empire. The monarchy ended in 1917 with the abdication of Nicholas II during the February Revolution.
