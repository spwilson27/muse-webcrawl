Machine learning

Machine learning (ML) is a field of study in artificial intelligence concerned with the development and study of statistical algorithms that can learn from data and generalise to unseen data, and thus perform tasks without explicit instructions.

Approaches

Machine learning approaches are traditionally divided into three broad categories: supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning. More recent approaches include deep learning, which uses multi-layered artificial neural networks.

Applications

Applications include computer vision, speech recognition, email filtering, natural language processing, agriculture, and medicine.

History

The term "machine learning" was coined by Arthur Samuel in 1959. The field grew out of pattern recognition research and took off with the availability of large datasets and powerful computing hardware in the 2010s.
