Technique

A technique is a way of carrying out a particular task, especially the practical methods and skills used in a specialised field — the procedural knowledge of how something is done, as distinct from the scientific knowledge of why it works.

In the arts and sport

In music, dance, painting and sport, technique refers to the mastered physical skills that underlie performance: a violinist's bowing, a painter's brushwork, a gymnast's form. Mastery of technique is generally considered necessary but not sufficient for artistry; performers must still bring interpretation and expression.

Philosophical views

Thinkers such as Jacques Ellul used "technique" more broadly for the totality of rationally arrived-at methods in modern society — the technological system itself — arguing that it develops autonomously and shapes human life. In science and engineering, technique denotes standardised procedures, such as laboratory techniques, that make results reproducible.
