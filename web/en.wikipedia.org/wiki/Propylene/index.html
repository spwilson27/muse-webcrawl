Propylene

Propylene, also known as propene, is an unsaturated organic compound with the chemical formula CH3CH=CH2. It is a colorless gas with a faint petroleum-like odor and is one of the most important feedstocks in the petrochemical industry, second only to ethylene.

Production

Propylene is produced mainly by steam cracking of hydrocarbon feedstocks and as a byproduct of fluid catalytic cracking in oil refining. On-purpose production methods such as propane dehydrogenation and olefin metathesis have grown as demand has increased. Global production exceeds 100 million tonnes per year.

Uses

The majority of propylene is polymerized to polypropylene, one of the most versatile plastics. Other major derivatives include propylene oxide, acrylonitrile, cumene (for phenol and acetone), oxo alcohols, and acrylic acid. Propylene is also used as a fuel gas.
