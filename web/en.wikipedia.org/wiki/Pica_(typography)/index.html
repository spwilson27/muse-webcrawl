Pica (typography)

Unit ofLength
Symbolpc
Conversions1 pc = 12 points; ≈ 4.233 mm (PostScript)

The pica is a unit of length used in typography and printing, equal to twelve points. There are 6 picas to an inch in the modern PostScript system, making one pica exactly 1/6 inch, or about 4.233 millimetres. The pica is the standard unit for measuring line length (measure), margins and column widths in page layout.

Variants

Historically, pica sizes varied between countries and printing systems. The traditional American printer's pica was approximately 0.1660 inches, slightly smaller than the PostScript pica of 0.1667 inches. In the continental European Didot system, the corresponding unit is the cicero, equal to 12 Didot points, about 4.51 mm. The term "pica" also denoted a size of type (12-point type was called pica) and, by extension, the 12-pitch typeface of typewriters, whose characters each occupy exactly one pica of height and a tenth of an inch in width in the case of "elite" pitch.

Use

In desktop publishing software such as Adobe InDesign, measurements can be specified in picas and points in the form "3p6" (3 picas, 6 points = 42 points). CSS includes the pc unit, defined as 1/6 of an inch.
