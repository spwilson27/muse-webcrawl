Street Fighter II


Developer: Capcom
Release: 1991 (arcade)
Genre: Fighting

Street Fighter II: The World Warrior is a 1991 fighting game developed and published by Capcom for arcades. It is the second installment in the Street Fighter series and the sequel to Street Fighter (1987). It is widely credited with launching the fighting-game boom of the 1990s and popularising the competitive one-on-one fighting genre.

Gameplay

Players select from eight playable World Warriors — Ryu, Ken, Chun-Li, Guile, Blanka, Dhalsim, E. Honda, and Zangief — each with their own stage and special techniques performed with joystick-and-button combinations, such as Ryu and Ken's Hadouken fireball, Shoryuken dragon punch, and Tatsumaki hurricane kick. Matches are best-of-three rounds. Four computer-controlled boss characters (Balrog, Vega, Sagat, and M. Bison) complete the single-player ladder.

Updated versions

Capcom released a famous series of revisions: Champion Edition (1992) made the bosses playable and allowed mirror matches; Hyper Fighting (1992) increased speed and added moves; Super Street Fighter II (1993) introduced four new characters (T. Hawk, Fei Long, Dee Jay, and Cammy); and Super Street Fighter II Turbo (1994) added super combos and Akuma. The Super Nintendo port of the original was a huge commercial success.

Legacy

Street Fighter II is one of the best-selling and most influential video games of all time, grossing billions of dollars in arcade revenue and establishing conventions — combo systems, character rosters, command-based special moves — that define the fighting genre to this day.
