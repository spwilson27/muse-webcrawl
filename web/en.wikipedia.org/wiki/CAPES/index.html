CAPES (Portuguese: Coordenação de Aperfeiçoamento de Pessoal de Nível Superior, "Coordination for the Improvement of Higher Education Personnel") is a Brazilian federal government agency under the Ministry of Education, responsible for graduate education policy, the evaluation of postgraduate programmes, and the award of scholarships and research funding in Brazil and abroad.

Functions

CAPES was created in 1951, in the government of Getúlio Vargas, to ensure a supply of qualified specialists for Brazil's development. It periodically evaluates all recognised master's and doctoral programmes in Brazil, grading them on a scale widely used by universities and employers, and funds scholarships for graduate students, postdoctoral researchers and visiting scholars. It also supports the training of basic-education teachers and administers the Portal de Periódicos, which gives Brazilian institutions access to international scientific journals.

Role in Brazilian science

Together with CNPq, CAPES is one of the twin pillars of Brazilian federal research support. Its evaluation system strongly shapes the behaviour of graduate programmes, and its scholarship programmes have funded the training of generations of Brazilian academics, including study abroad through schemes such as Science without Borders.
