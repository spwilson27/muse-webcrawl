COP28

The 2023 United Nations Climate Change Conference, or COP28, was the 28th UN Climate Change conference, held from 30 November to 13 December 2023 at Expo City, Dubai, in the United Arab Emirates. It concluded the first "global stocktake" under the Paris Agreement and produced the first COP decision text to call on countries to transition away from fossil fuels.

UAE Consensus

The final agreement, dubbed the UAE Consensus, called for "transitioning away from fossil fuels in energy systems, in a just, orderly and equitable manner" — the first explicit reference to moving away from all fossil fuels in three decades of climate talks, though it stopped short of a "phase-out" and included provisions for "transitional fuels" such as natural gas. It also set goals of tripling renewable energy capacity and doubling energy efficiency improvements by 2030.

Loss and damage fund

On the opening day, delegates agreed to operationalize the loss and damage fund first promised at COP27, with initial pledges of several hundred million dollars and interim hosting by the World Bank — a landmark for climate-vulnerable states, though far below estimated needs.

Controversy

The choice of Sultan Al Jaber, head of the Abu Dhabi National Oil Company, as COP president drew sustained criticism over conflicts of interest, and reporting on leaked documents suggesting the UAE planned to use the summit for oil and gas deals intensified scrutiny, though the final text exceeded many expectations.
