Midnight Sons

Midnight Sons
Publisher: Marvel Comics
First appearance: Ghost Rider vol. 3 #28 (1992)
Focus: Supernatural horror heroes

The Midnight Sons are a team of supernatural heroes in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. They were assembled by Doctor Strange to fight the demon Lilith and her children, the Lilin, in the "Rise of the Midnight Sons" crossover of 1992.

Members

The original grouping drew together Marvel's horror characters: Ghost Rider (Danny Ketch), Johnny Blaze, Morbius the Living Vampire, Blade, Hannibal King, Frank Drake, and the occult experts of the Nightstalkers, with appearances by the Darkhold Redeemers. Their titles carried the "Midnight Sons" banner and a distinctive dagger logo.

In other media

The team inspired the 2022 tactical role-playing video game Marvel's Midnight Suns, developed by Firaxis Games, which mixed classic members with mainstream Avengers characters.
