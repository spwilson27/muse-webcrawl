The following is a list of programs broadcast by the American Disney Channel, launched on 18 April 1983 as a premium channel before becoming a basic cable service in the 1990s. The channel is best known for its original live-action sitcoms and animated series aimed at children and pre-teens, including Lizzie McGuire, That's So Raven, Hannah Montana, Wizards of Waverly Place, Phineas and Ferb and Gravity Falls, and for its Disney Channel Original Movies such as High School Musical.
