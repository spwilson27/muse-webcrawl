Reuben College, Oxford

Established: 2019 (as Parks College)
Named for: Reuben Foundation
President: Lionel Tarassenko
Type: Graduate college, University of Oxford

Reuben College is a constituent college of the University of Oxford in England. Founded in 2019 as Parks College, it is a graduate-only college made possible by a £71 million endowment from the Reuben Foundation, one of the largest donations to the university for a college. It was renamed Reuben College in 2020.

History and site

The college is based in central Oxford in the Radcliffe Science Library building and nearby buildings on Parks Road, next to the University Museum of Natural History. It admitted its first students in 2021.

Academic focus

Reuben College's academic strategy is organised around interdisciplinary research themes that have changed over time, encompassing areas such as artificial intelligence and machine learning, environmental change, cellular life, ethics and values, global health, and innovation.
