Museo Ferrari

LocationMaranello, Italy
TypeAutomobile museum
OwnerFerrari S.p.A.

Museo Ferrari (formerly known as Galleria Ferrari) is a Ferrari company museum dedicated to the Ferrari sports car marque. It is located in Maranello, Italy, a short distance from the Ferrari factory. The museum first opened in February 1990.

Exhibitions

The museum displays cars, images and trophies of Ferrari's history. The exhibition space is spread over six rooms and also hosts rotating thematic exhibitions, including displays of Formula One cars, historic road cars, concept vehicles and engines. Cars on display have included examples from the company's racing and grand touring history, along with interactive exhibits and simulators.

Visitors

Museo Ferrari is one of the most visited museums in the Emilia-Romagna region, attracting several hundred thousand visitors each year. A second Ferrari museum, the Museo Enzo Ferrari, opened in Modena in 2012 and is dedicated to the life and work of the company's founder, Enzo Ferrari.
