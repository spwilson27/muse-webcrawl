Biohacking

Biohacking refers to various practices aimed at improving the performance, abilities and well-being of the human body through science, technology and self-experimentation. The term covers a broad range of communities and activities, from DIY biology to quantified-self tracking, implant "grinders", and lifestyle optimisation.

Types

DIY biology

Community laboratories and hobbyists perform genetics and biology experiments outside institutions, sometimes raising biosafety and regulatory questions, including self-administered gene therapies.

Grinders

Grinders implant magnets, RFID/NFC chips and other devices to gain new senses or functions, drawing on transhumanist ideas.

Nutrigenomics and quantified self

Others focus on sleep, diet, fasting, nootropics, cold exposure, continuous glucose monitors and wearable data.

Criticism and safety

Medical authorities warn that unregulated self-experimentation and unproven interventions can be dangerous, and regulators in several jurisdictions have acted against DIY gene-therapy kits.
