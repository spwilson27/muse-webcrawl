Fernando Alonso

Born: 29 July 1981, Oviedo, Spain

Championships: 2 (2005, 2006)

Teams: Minardi, Renault, McLaren, Ferrari, Alpine, Aston Martin

Fernando Alonso Díaz (born 29 July 1981) is a Spanish racing driver who competes in Formula One for Aston Martin. He won the Formula One World Drivers' Championship in 2005 and 2006 with Renault, becoming at the time the youngest double champion in the sport's history.

Career

Alonso debuted with Minardi in 2001 before joining Renault, where his two titles ended the era of Michael Schumacher's dominance. He later drove for McLaren, Ferrari — where he finished championship runner-up three times — and Alpine, before moving to Aston Martin in 2023.

Other racing

Alonso has twice won the 24 Hours of Le Mans with Toyota and the FIA World Endurance Championship, and has competed in the Indianapolis 500 and the Dakar Rally in pursuit of motorsport's informal Triple Crown of endurance and open-wheel racing.
