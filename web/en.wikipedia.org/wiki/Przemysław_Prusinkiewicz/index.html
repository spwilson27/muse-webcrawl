Przemysław Prusinkiewicz

Przemysław (Przemek) Prusinkiewicz is a Polish-Canadian computer scientist known for his work on the computational modelling of plant development. He is a professor emeritus at the University of Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

Work

Prusinkiewicz is best known for applying L-systems — formal grammars introduced by Aristid Lindenmayer in 1968 — to the realistic synthesis and simulation of plant growth. With Lindenmayer he co-authored The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants (1990), a landmark book in computer graphics demonstrating how branching structures, inflorescences and phyllotaxis can be generated algorithmically. His group developed the L-studio and Virtual Laboratory modelling environments and extended L-systems to incorporate environmental interaction, positional information and differential growth.

Recognition

His papers at ACM SIGGRAPH conferences have been widely cited, and his images of virtual plants are among the most recognisable applications of fractals and grammars in science. He received the Canadian Human–Computer Communications Society Achievement Award for his contributions to graphics and plant modelling.
