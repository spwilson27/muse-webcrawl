InfoWorld
Type: Technology media brand
Founded: 1978 (as Intelligent Machines Journal)
Publisher: IDG / Foundry

InfoWorld is an American technology media brand aimed at information-technology professionals, known for product reviews, analysis, and coverage of enterprise software, development tools, and computing platforms. It was published as a print magazine for nearly three decades before becoming an online-only publication in 2007.

History

The publication began in 1978 as a newsletter called the Intelligent Machines Journal and was relaunched as InfoWorld in 1980 by publisher IDG. During the personal-computer era it became a leading weekly for PC software and hardware reviews, notable for its comparative product reviews and its "Test Center" evaluations. Columns by writers such as Robert X. Cringely (the pseudonymous author of "Notes from the Field") made it widely read in the industry.

Digital era

InfoWorld ended its print edition in 2007 and continued on the web, focusing on application development, cloud computing, open source, and enterprise IT strategy. It has presented awards such as the Technology of the Year and Bossies (Best of Open Source Software) awards. The brand is now part of Foundry, formerly IDG Communications.
