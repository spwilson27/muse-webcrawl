The Super Bowl Shuffle

"The Super Bowl Shuffle" is a rap song performed by players of the Chicago Bears football team in 1985. Credited to the "Chicago Bears Shufflin' Crew", the song was released on December 3, 1985, weeks before the team won Super Bowl XX, and became a cultural phenomenon and one of the most famous novelty records in sports history.

Background

Produced by Chicago record executive Randy Weigand and released on Red Label Records, the song featured ten Bears players rapping verses, including Walter Payton, Jim McMahon, William "The Refrigerator" Perry, and Mike Singletary, with proceeds partly directed to charity, including food programs for needy Chicago families. The music video, showing the players dancing in uniform, received heavy rotation.

Reception

The single peaked at No. 41 on the Billboard Hot 100, sold over half a million copies, and earned a Grammy nomination for Best R&B Performance by a Duo or Group with Vocal — losing to Prince's "Kiss". Recorded while the Bears were dominating the 1985 season, the song's boastful prediction was fulfilled when Chicago demolished the New England Patriots 46–10 in Super Bowl XX. It spawned imitations by other teams and has been repeatedly referenced and parodied in popular culture.
