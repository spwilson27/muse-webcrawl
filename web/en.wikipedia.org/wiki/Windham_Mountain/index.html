Windham Mountain Club, commonly called Windham Mountain, is a ski resort located in the town of Windham, Greene County, New York, in the northern Catskill Mountains. It is one of the two major ski areas in the Catskills, along with nearby Hunter Mountain, and is a popular weekend destination for skiers from the New York metropolitan area, about two and a half hours away by car.

Mountain

The resort operates on two peaks — East Peak and West Peak — with a summit elevation of about 3,100 feet and a vertical drop of roughly 1,600 feet. It offers around 54 trails on 285 skiable acres, served by 12 lifts including high-speed detachable chairlifts. Terrain ranges from beginner slopes near the base lodge to steep expert trails such as Upper Wheelchair and the double-black runs on the East Peak. The resort has extensive snowmaking and night skiing on a portion of its terrain.

History and development

Skiing at Windham began in 1960, when the area opened as Cave Mountain Ski Area; it was later renamed Windham Mountain. Over the decades the resort expanded its lift system, snowmaking and base facilities, and added summer operations including a mountain bike park, which has hosted UCI Mountain Bike World Cup downhill races, as well as a golf course and adventure park in the surrounding resort community.

In the 2020s the resort rebranded as Windham Mountain Club, moving toward a semi-private membership model with upgraded lodges, dining and amenities while retaining public access to skiing.
