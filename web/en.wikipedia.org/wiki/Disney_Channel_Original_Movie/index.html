Disney Channel Original Movie

Disney Channel Original Movies (often shortened to DCOMs) are television films produced for and premiered on Disney Channel. The label was introduced in the late 1990s, succeeding earlier "Premiere Films" shown on the channel.

History

Early entries included Under Wraps (1997) and Halloweentown (1998). The franchise became a major part of Disney Channel's identity in the 2000s, with notable successes such as The Lizzie McGuire Movie's televised peers, High School Musical (2006) — whose premiere drew 7.7 million viewers and whose sequel drew 17.2 million, a record for basic cable at the time — Camp Rock (2008), and Descendants (2015). Later DCOMs have also premiered with simultaneous releases on Disney+.

Characteristics

DCOMs are generally family-oriented comedies, dramas, fantasy and musical films aimed at children and pre-teens, frequently starring actors from Disney Channel series. Many have spawned sequels, soundtracks, and merchandising lines.
