Preventive maintenance

Preventive maintenance (PM) is the care and servicing of equipment and facilities at regular, planned intervals to keep them in satisfactory operating condition and to prevent unexpected failures. It is one of the main maintenance strategies, alongside corrective (run-to-failure) maintenance and predictive maintenance based on condition monitoring.

Scheduling

PM tasks are triggered by elapsed time, operating hours, mileage, or production cycles, following manufacturer recommendations and failure-history analysis. Typical activities include inspection, lubrication, adjustment, cleaning, and replacement of wear parts such as filters and belts.

Advantages and limits

Well-designed PM reduces unplanned downtime and extends asset life, but excessive PM wastes labour and parts and can even introduce failures through unnecessary intervention. Modern computerised maintenance management systems (CMMS) plan work orders and track compliance, and reliability-centred maintenance optimises task selection.
