Publicis Groupe

Type: Public (Euronext Paris: PUB)
Founded: 1926, Paris
Founder: Marcel Bleustein-Blanchet
Headquarters: Paris, France
Industry: Advertising, communications
Key people: Arthur Sadoun (chairman and CEO)

Publicis Groupe S.A. is a French multinational advertising and public relations company headquartered in Paris. Founded in 1926, it is one of the world's largest communications groups — by the mid-2020s the largest advertising holding company by revenue — operating in more than 100 countries with agency networks including Leo Burnett, Saatchi & Saatchi, Publicis Worldwide, BBH, and the media operations Publicis Media, alongside the digital consultancy Publicis Sapient and the data company Epsilon.

History

Publicis was founded in Paris in 1926 by Marcel Bleustein-Blanchet, a pioneer of French advertising who introduced American-style methods, slogans and radio advertising. After the Second World War — during which Bleustein-Blanchet served in the French Resistance and the agency was closed — Publicis rebuilt and in 1958 moved into its landmark headquarters at 133 avenue des Champs-Élysées. Under Maurice Lévy, who became chief executive in 1987, the group expanded internationally through major acquisitions, including Saatchi & Saatchi (2000), Bcom3, owner of Leo Burnett (2002), the digital network Digitas, Razorfish, and the consultancy Sapient (2015, for $3.7 billion). In 2013 Publicis and the American group Omnicom announced a "merger of equals" that would have created the world's largest advertising company, but the deal collapsed in 2014.

Arthur Sadoun succeeded Lévy as chairman and CEO in 2017. The group reorganised into a country-based "Power of One" model, launched the artificial-intelligence platform Marcel, and in 2019 acquired the data marketing company Epsilon for about $4.4 billion. Driven by its data and technology businesses, Publicis overtook WPP in revenue in the mid-2020s.

Operations

Publicis organises its offer around creative agencies, media buying and planning, digital and technology consulting (Publicis Sapient), health communications (Publicis Health) and data services built on Epsilon's identity data. Major clients have included global advertisers in retail, automotive, consumer goods, finance and pharmaceuticals.
