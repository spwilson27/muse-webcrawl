Europlug

The Europlug is a flat, two-pole, round-pin domestic AC power plug, rated for voltages up to 250 V and currents up to 2.5 A, defined by the CENELEC standard EN 50075. Formally known as the Type C plug, it is designed to fit into the domestic socket types used in most of continental Europe, as well as several compatible socket designs elsewhere.

Design

The plug has two round pins 4 mm in diameter, spaced 19 mm apart, which converge slightly towards their free ends so the plug grips sockets of slightly different dimensions. The pins are insulated for part of their length so that live metal is not exposed when the plug is partially inserted. The Europlug is unearthed and intended only for low-power, double-insulated (Class II) appliances such as phone chargers, radios, and lamps.

Compatibility

Because several European countries historically used sockets of differing sizes, the Europlug was designed as a compromise fitting the French, German (Schuko), Italian, Swiss, Danish, and other socket families. It is not compatible with British BS 1363 sockets, and its use in the United Kingdom and Ireland is limited to shaver supply units.
