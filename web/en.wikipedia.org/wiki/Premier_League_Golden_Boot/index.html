Premier League Golden Boot

The Premier League Golden Boot is an annual association football award presented to the leading goalscorer in the Premier League. For sponsorship purposes, it has been known as the Cadbury Golden Boot and the Coca-Cola Zero Sugar Golden Boot at different times. In addition to the trophy, the winner receives a sum of money (historically £1,000 per goal) to donate to a charity of their choice.

History

The award has been presented since the first Premier League season in 1992–93, when Teddy Sheringham won with 22 goals. Thierry Henry holds the record for most wins, with four (2001–02, 2003–04, 2004–05, 2005–06). Erling Haaland set the single-season record with 36 goals in 2022–23.

When two or more players finish level, the award is shared; this happened notably in 2018–19, when Mohamed Salah, Sadio Mané, and Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang each scored 22 goals.
