Individual transferable quota (ITQ)

Field: Fisheries management / environmental economics

First adopted: Netherlands and Iceland, 1970s

An individual transferable quota (ITQ) is a type of catch share used in fisheries management. A regulator sets a total allowable catch for a fishery and divides it into quotas assigned to individual fishers or vessels, who may buy, sell, or lease their shares.

Purpose

ITQs aim to end the "race to fish" that occurs under open-access or season-limited regimes, improving safety, product quality, and profitability while keeping total catch within sustainable limits.

Criticism

Critics argue ITQs can concentrate quota ownership, disadvantage small-scale fishers and coastal communities, and create incentives to discard lower-value fish ("high-grading"). ITQ systems operate in countries including Iceland, New Zealand, Canada, and the United States.
