Toby Jones

Toby Edward Heslewood Jones (born 7 September 1966) is an English actor. The son of actor Freddie Jones, he is known for versatile character roles across film, television, theatre and radio, and for voicing Dobby the house-elf in the Harry Potter films.

Career

Jones studied drama at the University of Manchester and at L'École Internationale de Théâtre Jacques Lecoq in Paris. He won acclaim and an Olivier Award for The Play What I Wrote (2001). His breakthrough screen role was as Truman Capote in Infamous (2006). Other notable performances include Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy (2011), Claudius Templesmith in The Hunger Games, Arnim Zola in the Marvel films, and the title role in Berberian Sound Studio (2012). On television he won praise for playing Lance in the BBC series Detectorists and received a BAFTA for Marvellous (2014); he also played Alan Bates in Mr Bates vs The Post Office (2024).
