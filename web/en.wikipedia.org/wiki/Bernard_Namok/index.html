Bernard Namok

Born1989, Thursday Island, Queensland, Australia
Died31 May 2014 (aged 25), Thursday Island

Known forDesigning the Torres Strait Islander Flag

Bernard Namok (1989 – 31 May 2014) was a Torres Strait Islander radio broadcaster from Thursday Island in Queensland, Australia. He is best known as the designer of the Torres Strait Islander Flag, which was officially recognised by the Australian government in 1995 as a flag of national significance.

Life and career

Namok grew up on Thursday Island in the Torres Strait. He designed the Torres Strait Islander flag in 1992, when he was a young man, producing a design that combined symbols representing Torres Strait Islander identity: a dhari (headdress), a five-pointed star representing the island groups and navigation, and the colours green, black and blue representing the land, the people and the sea respectively.

Namok worked as a radio broadcaster, contributing to Indigenous media in the Torres Strait region. He died on 31 May 2014, aged 25.

Legacy

The Torres Strait Islander Flag flies alongside the Aboriginal Flag and the Australian national flag at government buildings and is widely flown throughout the Torres Strait. Namok's design is regarded as an enduring symbol of Torres Strait Islander identity and culture.
