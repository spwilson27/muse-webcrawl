Brighton and Hove Albion F.C.

Full name: Brighton and Hove Albion Football Club

Nickname: The Seagulls / Albion

Founded: 24 June 1901

Ground: Falmer Stadium (Amex Stadium)

Capacity: 31,876

Owner: Tony Bloom

League: Premier League

Brighton and Hove Albion Football Club, commonly known simply as Brighton, is an English professional football club based in the city of Brighton and Hove, East Sussex. Founded in 1901, the club competes in the Premier League, the top tier of English football, and plays its home matches at the Falmer Stadium, known commercially as the American Express Stadium.

History

Brighton joined the Football League in 1920 and spent much of its history in the lower divisions, playing at the Goldstone Ground. The club's first golden era came under Alan Mullery and then Mike Bailey, reaching the First Division in 1979 and the 1983 FA Cup Final, where they lost to Manchester United in a replay ("...and Smith must score" remains a famous moment of commentary from the drawn first match).

Financial crisis in the 1990s forced the controversial sale of the Goldstone Ground in 1997; the club nearly lost its league status the same year before a dramatic last-day draw at Hereford United preserved it. Brighton ground-shared at Gillingham's Priestfield Stadium and then played for twelve years at the small Withdean Stadium before the Falmer Stadium opened in 2011 under owner Tony Bloom.

Premier League era

Under manager Chris Hughton, Brighton won promotion to the Premier League in 2017 — their first season in the top flight since 1983. Graham Potter continued their development from 2019, and Roberto De Zerbi, appointed in 2022, led the club to a club-record sixth-place finish in 2022–23, qualifying for European competition (the UEFA Europa League) for the first time in Brighton's history. The club is widely praised for its data-driven recruitment model, repeatedly developing and selling players for large fees.

Colours and crest

Brighton's traditional home colours are blue and white stripes, worn since the 1970s, and their nickname is "the Seagulls", reflected in the seagull on the club crest, a nod to the seaside city. Their fiercest rivalry is with Crystal Palace, known as the M23 derby.
