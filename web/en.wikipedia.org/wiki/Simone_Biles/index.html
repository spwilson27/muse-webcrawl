Simone Arianne Biles Owens (born 14 March 1997) is an American artistic gymnast. Her 41 World and Olympic medals make her the most decorated gymnast in history, and she is widely considered the greatest gymnast of all time. Biles has five skills named after her in the Code of Points — more than any other gymnast — including the Yurchenko double pike vault, first performed internationally by a woman at the 2023 World Championships.

Biles dominated Rio 2016, winning four golds, then at Tokyo 2020 withdrew from most events after developing "the twisties", a loss of spatial awareness mid-air; her openness about mental health reshaped the conversation around athletes' wellbeing. After a two-year break she returned in 2023 to win a sixth world all-around title, and at Paris 2024 led the US women to team gold and won the all-around and vault titles, becoming the most decorated American Olympic gymnast. Raised in Spring, Texas, by her grandparents after time in foster care, she trains at the World Champions Centre her family built.
