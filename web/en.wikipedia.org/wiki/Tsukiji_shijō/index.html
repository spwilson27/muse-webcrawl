Tsukiji shijō

Tsukiji Market (Japanese: 築地市場, Tsukiji shijō) was a major wholesale food market in the Tsukiji district of Chūō, Tokyo, and for decades the largest wholesale fish and seafood market in the world, handling hundreds of thousands of tonnes of marine products annually. Opened in 1935 on reclaimed land along the Sumida River, it was famous for its pre-dawn tuna auctions and for the dense outer market (jogai) of retail shops and restaurants surrounding it.

History and closure

Tsukiji was established after the 1923 Great Kantō earthquake destroyed the earlier Nihonbashi fish market. Plans to relocate the ageing, crowded facility were debated for decades; the inner wholesale market finally moved to the new Toyosu Market in October 2018. The outer market at Tsukiji remains in operation, and the site continues to draw large numbers of visitors for its food stalls and sushi restaurants.
