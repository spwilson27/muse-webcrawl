List of governors of South Dakota

The governor of South Dakota is the head of the executive branch of the U.S. state of South Dakota. South Dakota was admitted to the Union on 2 November 1889, together with North Dakota. Since statehood there have been 33 governors (counting repeat terms by the same person once). Before statehood, the area was part of Dakota Territory, whose governors were appointed by the President of the United States.
