Boris Johnson (born 19 June 1964) is a British politician and writer who served as Prime Minister of the United Kingdom and Leader of the Conservative Party from 2019 to 2022, and Mayor of London from 2008 to 2016.

Early life and career

Educated at Eton and Balliol College, Oxford, he was a journalist at The Times and The Daily Telegraph (Brussels correspondent) and editor of The Spectator (1999–2005). He was MP for Henley (2001–2008) and Uxbridge and South Ruislip (2015–2023).

Premiership

A leading Vote Leave figure in the 2016 Brexit referendum, Johnson became PM in July 2019, won a large majority in the December 2019 election, and completed the UK's withdrawal from the EU on 31 January 2020. His government responded to COVID-19 with lockdowns and a fast vaccine rollout. The Partygate scandal over lockdown gatherings, and other controversies, led to mass ministerial resignations and his resignation in July 2022; he left Parliament in June 2023.
