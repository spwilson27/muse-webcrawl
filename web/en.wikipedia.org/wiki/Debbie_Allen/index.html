Deborah Kaye Allen (born January 16, 1950) is an American actress, dancer, choreographer, television director and producer. She is best known for her role as dance teacher Lydia Grant in the 1980 film Fame and its television series spin-off (1982–1987), for which she was also principal choreographer, winning two Primetime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Choreography.

Career

Born in Houston, Texas, Allen earned a degree in Greek literature and theatre from Howard University. She appeared on Broadway in productions such as Purlie, Raisin, and Sweet Charity, receiving a Tony Award nomination. As a director-producer, she reshaped the sitcom A Different World into a top-rated series, and has directed episodes of many television shows. Since 2013 she has played Dr. Catherine Avery (later Catherine Fox) on the medical drama Grey's Anatomy, where she also serves as an executive producer and director.

Legacy

In 2001 Allen founded the Debbie Allen Dance Academy in Los Angeles, a nonprofit school offering training to young dancers. She received the Kennedy Center Honors in 2020 and a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. Her older sister is actress Phylicia Rashad.
