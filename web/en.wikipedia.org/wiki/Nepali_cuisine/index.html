Nepali cuisine

Nepali cuisine comprises the diverse culinary traditions of Nepal, influenced by the country's geography, ethnicity and climate as well as by neighbouring Indian and Tibetan cuisines. The staple meal throughout the hills is dal bhat tarkari — steamed rice served with lentil soup, vegetable curry, pickles and sometimes meat — eaten twice a day.

Regional varieties

Newari cuisine of the Kathmandu Valley is known for dishes such as chatamari, bara, yomari and beaten rice (chiura) with spiced meats. Thakali cuisine from the Mustang region is famed for its refined dal bhat sets with buckwheat, millet and dried meats. In the mountains, Tibetan-influenced foods predominate, including momo dumplings — now popular nationwide — thukpa noodle soup and butter tea. The southern Terai shares many dishes with north Indian cooking.

Ingredients and flavourings

Common seasonings include cumin, coriander, turmeric, ginger, garlic, timur (Sichuan pepper), mustard oil and ghee. Fermented foods such as gundruk (fermented leafy greens) and achaar pickles are widely used.
