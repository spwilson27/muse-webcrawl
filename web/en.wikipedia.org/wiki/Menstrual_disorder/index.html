Menstrual disorder

Menstrual disorder
SpecialtyGynaecology
ExamplesAmenorrhea, dysmenorrhea, menorrhagia, PMS, PMDD
CausesHormonal imbalance, structural abnormalities, systemic illness

A menstrual disorder is any abnormal condition affecting a woman's menstrual cycle, including abnormalities of bleeding frequency, duration, volume or associated pain and premenstrual symptoms.

Types

Common menstrual disorders include amenorrhea (absent periods), oligomenorrhea (infrequent periods), menorrhagia (abnormally heavy bleeding), dysmenorrhea (painful periods), premenstrual syndrome (PMS) and its severe form, premenstrual dysphoric disorder (PMDD). Irregular cycles are common in the years just after menarche and before menopause.

Causes and treatment

Causes range from pregnancy and hormonal conditions such as polycystic ovary syndrome and thyroid disease to structural problems such as fibroids and endometriosis. Diagnosis may involve history-taking, blood tests and pelvic ultrasound; treatment is directed at the cause and may include hormonal contraception, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, or surgery in selected cases.
