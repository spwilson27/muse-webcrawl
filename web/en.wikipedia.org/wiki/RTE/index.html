Raidió Teilifís Éireann (RTÉ; meaning "Radio Television of Ireland") is the national broadcaster and public service media organisation of Ireland. It produces programmes and broadcasts them on television, radio and online. RTÉ is a statutory body, run by an authority appointed by the Government of Ireland, and is financed principally by a television licence fee, supplemented by commercial revenue such as advertising.

History

Irish public radio began with the station 2RN in 1926, evolving into Radio Éireann. Television broadcasts began on 31 December 1961 with the launch of Telefís Éireann. The Radio Éireann Authority was established in 1960 under the Broadcasting Authority Act; the organisation took the name Raidió Teilifís Éireann in 1966.

Services

Television services include RTÉ One, RTÉ2, RTÉjr and the RTÉ News channel; radio services include RTÉ Radio 1, 2fm, lyric fm and Raidió na Gaeltachta. RTÉ also operates the RTÉ Player streaming service and the RTÉ News website and app. Long-running productions include The Late Late Show, one of the world's longest-running chat shows, and the soap opera Fair City.

Recent controversies

In 2023 RTÉ was embroiled in a major governance and payments scandal after it emerged that undisclosed payments had been made to a leading presenter, prompting parliamentary inquiries, resignations, and a government-ordered review of the licence-fee funding model.
