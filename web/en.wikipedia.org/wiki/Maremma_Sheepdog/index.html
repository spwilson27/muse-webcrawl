Maremma Sheepdog

The Maremma Sheepdog (Italian: Cane da pastore Maremmano-Abruzzese) is a breed of livestock guardian dog from central Italy, used for centuries to protect sheep flocks against wolves and other predators in the Maremma plains of Tuscany and Lazio and in the mountains of Abruzzo.

Characteristics

The breed is a large, powerfully built dog with a dense, weather-resistant white or ivory double coat, a broad head, and a somewhat bear-like appearance. Males typically stand 65–73 cm at the withers and weigh 35–45 kg. White coloration allows the dog to blend with the flock and be distinguished from predators at night.

Temperament and work

Raised with the sheep from puppyhood, the Maremma bonds with its flock and works independently, patrolling and confronting threats rather than herding. It is loyal to its charges and family but typically aloof and territorial with strangers. Beyond Italy, Maremmas are used as guardian animals in Australia, North America, and elsewhere; in a notable Australian program, Maremmas guard the little penguin colony on Middle Island off Warrnambool, Victoria, protecting it from foxes.
