Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani

Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani (born 1 January 1952) is a member of Qatar's ruling Al Thani family. He was Emir of Qatar from 1995, when he deposed his father Khalifa bin Hamad Al Thani in a bloodless coup, until 2013, when he abdicated in favour of his son Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani.

Reign

Under Hamad, Qatar was transformed by natural gas wealth into one of the world's richest states per capita. He founded the Al Jazeera news network in 1996, established the Qatar Investment Authority and Qatar Foundation, promoted education and culture (including Education City and the Museum of Islamic Art), and pursued an active, mediating foreign policy. Qatar won the right to host the 2022 FIFA World Cup during his reign.

Personal life

Hamad, known as the Father Emir after his abdication, is married among others to Sheikha Moza bint Nasser, a prominent figure in Qatari education and social development. He graduated from the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst in 1971 and served as commander of Qatar's armed forces.
