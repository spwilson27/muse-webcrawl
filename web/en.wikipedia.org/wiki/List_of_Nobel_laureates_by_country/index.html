List of Nobel laureates by country

This is a list of Nobel laureates by country. The Nobel Prizes have been awarded since 1901 in Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature and Peace, with the Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences added in 1969. Laureates are listed by their country of birth or primary affiliation, and counts differ by convention.
