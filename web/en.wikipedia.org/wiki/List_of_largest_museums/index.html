List of largest museums

This list ranks the world's largest museums by gallery floor space. Measurements are approximate and reported figures vary by source, as institutions count exhibition, storage and administrative space differently.

Notes

The Louvre is also the world's most-visited museum, receiving around 8–9 million visitors in peak years. The National Museum of China, formed by merging two earlier museums on Tiananmen Square, has one of the largest total building footprints of any museum at nearly 200,000 m².
