Isobutane

Isobutane, also known as 2-methylpropane or by the refrigerant designation R-600a, is an alkane with the molecular formula C4H10. It is a structural isomer of butane (n-butane), differing in that its carbon skeleton is branched. It is a colourless, flammable gas with a faint petroleum odour, boiling at about −11.7 °C.

Uses

Isobutane is used as a feedstock in the petrochemical industry, notably for producing isobutylene (via dehydrogenation) and in alkylation units to make high-octane gasoline components. As refrigerant R-600a it has largely replaced chlorofluorocarbons in domestic refrigerators because of its low environmental impact, though its flammability requires care. It is also used as a propellant in aerosol sprays and as a fuel in camping stoves and lighters, typically blended with propane.
