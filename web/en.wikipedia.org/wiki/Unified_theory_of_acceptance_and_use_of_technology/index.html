Unified theory of acceptance and use of technology

UTAUT
Developed byVenkatesh, Morris, Davis & Davis (2003)
Core constructsPerformance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence, facilitating conditions

The unified theory of acceptance and use of technology (UTAUT) is a technology acceptance model formulated by Venkatesh and others in 2003. It synthesizes eight earlier models of technology adoption, including the technology acceptance model (TAM), the theory of reasoned action, and the diffusion of innovations theory.

UTAUT holds that four key constructs directly influence users' intention to adopt a technology and their actual usage behavior: performance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence, and facilitating conditions. These effects are moderated by gender, age, experience, and voluntariness of use.

UTAUT2

In 2012, Venkatesh, Thong, and Xu extended the model as UTAUT2, adding hedonic motivation, price value, and habit to explain technology acceptance in a consumer context. UTAUT is among the most widely cited frameworks in information systems research and has been applied to fields ranging from e-commerce and mobile banking to health information systems and e-learning.
