WRESAT

WRESAT (Weapons Research Establishment Satellite) was the first satellite built in Australia and the first launched from Australian soil. Launched on 29 November 1967 from the Woomera Test Range in South Australia, it made Australia the fourth nation to launch a satellite from its own territory.

Launch date29 November 1967
Launch siteWoomera, South Australia
RocketRedstone (Sparta)
Decay date10 January 1968

Mission

WRESAT weighed about 45 kg and was built by the Weapons Research Establishment with the University of Adelaide, roughly 96 weeks after approval using surplus hardware from the Sparta test programme. Its instruments measured solar radiation and the upper atmosphere during its 642 orbits, transmitting data until its batteries were exhausted after about five days; the satellite re-entered the atmosphere over the Atlantic in January 1968.
