Image stitching

Image stitching or photo stitching is the process of combining multiple photographic images with overlapping fields of view to produce a segmented panorama or a high-resolution image.

Stages

A typical pipeline performs image calibration, feature detection and registration (often using scale-invariant features such as SIFT), estimation of homographies between views, warping onto a common projection such as a cylinder or sphere, and blending to hide seams.

Applications

Stitching is used in smartphone panorama modes, aerial and satellite mosaics, microscopy, document scanning and virtual tours. Parallax and moving objects remain common sources of artefacts.
