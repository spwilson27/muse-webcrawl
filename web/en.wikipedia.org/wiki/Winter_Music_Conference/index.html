Winter Music Conference
Abbreviation: WMC
Location: Miami / Miami Beach, Florida
Founded: 1985
Industry: Electronic dance music
Timing: March (Miami Music Week)

The Winter Music Conference (WMC) is an annual week-long electronic music conference held in Miami, Florida, United States, founded in 1985 by DJs Louis Possenti and Bill Kelly. It brings together artists, DJs, record labels, promoters and media for panels, seminars, workshops and networking by day and showcases, club nights and pool parties by night, and for decades has served as the dance-music industry's principal North American gathering and the launching point for the year's summer hits.

Miami Music Week and Ultra

WMC anchors Miami Music Week, during which hundreds of events take place across Miami and Miami Beach. The Ultra Music Festival, founded in 1999, long coincided with the conference's closing weekend and became so large that it operated as a separate event; in 2018 Ultra's parent interests acquired the WMC, formally reuniting the industry's biggest conference and festival brands.

International Dance Music Awards

The conference long presented the International Dance Music Awards (IDMAs), honouring artists, tracks, labels and technology in dozens of categories voted on by the public and industry, becoming one of electronic music's most recognized awards programmes.

History

From a gathering of a few hundred at a Fort Lauderdale hotel in 1985, WMC grew with house and electronic music itself, relocating to Miami Beach and Miami as attendance climbed into the tens of thousands. Changes in the music business and the rise of competing events reshaped the conference in the 2010s, but it remains a landmark institution of dance music culture.
