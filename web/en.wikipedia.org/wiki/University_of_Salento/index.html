The University of Salento (Italian: Università del Salento) is a public university located mainly in Lecce, in the Salento peninsula of Apulia, southern Italy. It was founded in 1955 as the University of Lecce and adopted its present name in 2007 to reflect its reach across the provinces of Lecce, Brindisi, and Taranto.

History

The university began with a Faculty of Education (Magistero) and expanded over the following decades with faculties of humanities, mathematical and natural sciences, economics, engineering, and law. It was the first university established in the Salento region and has been a major driver of the area's cultural and economic development.

Organization and research

The university is organized into departments covering cultural heritage, biological and environmental sciences and technologies, innovation engineering, mathematics and physics, management and economics, history and society, humanistic studies, and legal sciences. Research strengths include nanotechnology, aerospace engineering (in cooperation with the Brindisi aerospace district), Mediterranean agriculture, and the study of the region's Greek-speaking (Griko) heritage.

Campuses

Facilities are concentrated at the Ecotekne campus outside Lecce and in historic buildings in the baroque old town, with additional sites in Brindisi and Mesagne.
