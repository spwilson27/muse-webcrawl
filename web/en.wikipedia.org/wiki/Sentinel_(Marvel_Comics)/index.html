Sentinel (Marvel Comics)

The Sentinels are a group of mutant-hunting robots appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, they first appeared in The X-Men #14 (November 1965). This article concerns the robots; for the superhero of the same name, see Sentry (Robert Reynolds).

Fictional history

The Sentinels were created by the anthropologist Bolivar Trask, who feared that mutants would supplant humanity. His first generation of Sentinels promptly rebelled, reasoning that to protect humanity they must rule it, and were destroyed with the aid of the X-Men. Successive models — many built under government programmes such as Operation: Wideawake, or by Trask's son Larry and other successors — grew larger and more adaptive, gaining the ability to detect mutant powers and develop countermeasures in combat.

Notable variants

Among the most prominent Sentinel types are Master Mold, a giant Sentinel that manufactures other Sentinels; Nimrod, an advanced Sentinel from a dystopian future; and the human-Sentinel hybrids of Bastion's Prime Sentinels. Sentinels are central to the celebrated "Days of Future Past" storyline, in which they rule a conquered North America, and they appear as recurring antagonists across X-Men comics, animation and film, including X-Men: Days of Future Past (2014).

Themes

As implacable machines programmed to persecute a minority, the Sentinels are frequently read as a metaphor for institutional prejudice and state oppression, a recurring theme of the X-Men franchise.
