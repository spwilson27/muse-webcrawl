List of metropolitan areas

A metropolitan area is a region consisting of a densely populated urban core and its surrounding territories sharing industry, housing and infrastructure. Definitions vary by country and by source. This article lists the world's largest metropolitan areas by population.

Greater Tokyo is the most populous metropolitan area in the world.
