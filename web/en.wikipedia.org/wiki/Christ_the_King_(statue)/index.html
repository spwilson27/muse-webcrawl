Christ the King (statue)

Christ the King (Pomnik Chrystusa Króla)
LocationŚwiebodzin, Poland
Height33 m (108 ft) statue; 52.5 m (172 ft) including mound and crown
Completed6 November 2010
DesignerMirosław Kazimierz Patecki

Christ the King (Polish: Pomnik Chrystusa Króla) is a statue of Jesus Christ in Świebodzin, Poland, completed on 6 November 2010. The figure itself is 33 metres (108 ft) tall — one metre for each year of Jesus's life on earth, according to tradition — and, together with its mound, reaches about 52.5 metres, making it among the tallest statues of Jesus in the world.

Construction

The project was conceived by retired local priest Sylwester Zawadzki and designed by sculptor Mirosław Kazimierz Patecki. The statue was built largely by volunteers and funded by donations from the local community and the Polish diaspora. The fibreglass-and-concrete figure wears a gilded crown 3 metres tall and extends its arms roughly 25 metres across.

Comparisons

It surpassed the Cristo Rei in Portugal and Cristo de la Concordia in Bolivia in the height of the figure itself, though comparisons vary depending on whether pedestals and mounds are counted; Rio de Janeiro's Christ the Redeemer (30 m figure on an 8 m pedestal) remains the most famous of the monumental Christs.
