Do They Know It's Christmas?

"Do They Know It's Christmas?" is a charity song written in 1984 by Bob Geldof and Midge Ure to raise money for relief of the 1983–1985 famine in Ethiopia. It was performed by the supergroup Band Aid, consisting mainly of British and Irish musicians, and recorded at Sarm West Studios in London on 25 November 1984.

Background

Geldof was moved by BBC news reports of the Ethiopian famine in October 1984 and, with Ure, wrote the song and recruited major acts of the day. Performers included Paul Young, Boy George, George Michael, Sting, Bono, Duran Duran, Spandau Ballet, and Status Quo.

Reception and legacy

The single was the UK Christmas number one of 1984, outselling all other records combined at points, and became one of the best-selling singles of all time. It inspired the American counterpart "We Are the World" (1985) and the Live Aid concerts. New versions by Band Aid II (1989), Band Aid 20 (2004), Band Aid 30 (2014), and a 40th-anniversary remix (2024) raised funds for later causes.
