New South Wales (commonly abbreviated as NSW) is a state on the east coast of Australia. It borders Queensland to the north, Victoria to the south, South Australia to the west, and is entirely surrounded by the Tasman Sea to the east; the Australian Capital Territory is an enclave within it. Its capital and largest city is Sydney. With about 8.5 million inhabitants, New South Wales is Australia's most populous state and its largest economy.

Sydney, the state capital, is Australia's largest city and financial centre.

History

Aboriginal Australians have inhabited the region for at least 40,000–60,000 years. In 1770, Lieutenant James Cook charted the east coast and claimed it for Britain, landing at Botany Bay. The colony of New South Wales was founded with the arrival of the First Fleet in January 1788, when Governor Arthur Phillip established a penal settlement at Sydney Cove in Port Jackson. The colony originally covered more than half the Australian mainland; over the 19th century separate colonies were carved out of it—Van Diemen's Land (Tasmania), South Australia, Victoria and Queensland. On 1 January 1901 the colonies federated to form the Commonwealth of Australia, with New South Wales as a founding state.

Geography

The Great Dividing Range runs parallel to the coast, dividing the state into a fertile coastal plain, mountainous highlands (including the Blue Mountains and the Snowy Mountains, home to Mount Kosciuszko, mainland Australia's highest peak at 2,228 m), and dry western plains that make up about two-thirds of the state's area. Major cities besides Sydney include Newcastle, Wollongong and the regional centres of Albury, Tamworth, Dubbo and Wagga Wagga.

Economy and government

New South Wales has a diversified, service-based economy dominated by finance, professional services, education, health, tourism and international trade, alongside significant coal mining, agriculture and manufacturing. The state is governed by a bicameral parliament—the Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council—modelled on the Westminster system, headed by a premier, with a governor as the representative of the Crown.
