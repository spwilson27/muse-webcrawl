Silo (TV series)

The series is set in a vast underground silo sheltering humanity from a toxic surface.

Silo is an American science fiction dystopian drama television series created by Graham Yost, based on the Silo trilogy of novels (Wool, Shift, and Dust) by Hugh Howey. Set in a dystopian future where a community exists in a giant underground silo comprising 144 levels, it stars Rebecca Ferguson as Juliette Nichols, an engineer whose investigation into a colleague's death unravels secrets about the silo's origins. The series premiered on Apple TV+ on May 5, 2023.

The cast includes Rashida Jones, David Oyelowo, Common, Tim Robbins, Harriet Walter, Avi Nash, and Rick Gomez in the first season, with Steve Zahn joining in season two. The first season adapts roughly the first half of Wool; the second season, premiering in November 2024, continues the story. The show was renewed for third and fourth seasons, with the fourth planned as the final season.

Reception

Silo received generally positive reviews, with praise for its worldbuilding, production design, and Ferguson's performance. It became one of Apple TV+'s most-watched drama series and was nominated for awards for its visual effects and title design.
