King's Day (Netherlands)

King's Day (Dutch: Koningsdag) is a national holiday in the Netherlands celebrated on 27 April, the birthday of King Willem-Alexander. It continues the tradition of Queen's Day (Koninginnedag), held on 30 April under Queen Beatrix and Queen Juliana.

Celebrations

The holiday is famous for its nationwide vrijmarkt (free market), in which citizens sell second-hand goods on the streets, and for crowds dressed in orange, the colour of the House of Orange-Nassau. The royal family visits a different municipality each year.
