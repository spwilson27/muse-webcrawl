Mork & Mindy is an American television sitcom that aired on ABC from September 14, 1978, to May 27, 1982. A spin-off of the series Happy Days, it stars Robin Williams as Mork, an alien from the planet Ork sent to Earth to study human behaviour, and Pam Dawber as Mindy McConnell, the young woman who takes him in at her home in Boulder, Colorado. The show was a breakout vehicle for Williams, whose improvised physical comedy became its defining feature.

Premise and characters

Mork arrives on Earth in an egg-shaped spacecraft and reports his observations back to his superior Orson in brief closing scenes, signing off with 'Nanu Nanu', accompanied by a distinctive hand gesture. Mindy explains human customs to him, and much of the humour derives from Mork's misunderstanding of everyday behaviour.

Later seasons introduced new characters, including Mindy's father Fred and, in the final season, Mearth, the couple's rapidly ageing child played by Jonathan Winters, a comic idol of Williams.

Production and reception

Williams's largely improvisational performance was unusual for a network sitcom of the era; scripts reportedly left gaps marked for his improvisation. The first season was a major ratings success, finishing in the top three, and Williams won a Golden Globe Award for his performance.

Ratings declined after scheduling changes and attempts to retool the format, and the series was cancelled after its fourth season. The show nevertheless launched Williams's film career and remains a well-remembered example of 1970s American network comedy.
