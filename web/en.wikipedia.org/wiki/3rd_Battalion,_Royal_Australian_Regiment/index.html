3rd Battalion, Royal Australian Regiment

The 3rd Battalion, Royal Australian Regiment (3 RAR) is a mechanised infantry battalion of the Australian Army, forming part of the Royal Australian Regiment. Based at Lavarack Barracks in Townsville, Queensland, it serves within the 3rd Brigade. The battalion is nicknamed "Old Faithful."

History

3 RAR was raised in 1945 as the 67th Battalion for occupation duties in Japan with the British Commonwealth Occupation Force. It served with distinction in the Korean War, most notably at the Battle of Kapyong in April 1951 and the Battle of Maryang San later that year. The battalion completed two tours of South Vietnam (1967–68 and 1971) and later converted to the parachute role in 1983, which it held for over two decades, including a company-group deployment to East Timor in 1999–2000. After further service in the Solomon Islands, Iraq, and Afghanistan, the battalion re-roled as mechanised infantry in 2017, relocating to Townsville.
