Ram Trucks

Ram Trucks (stylised as RAM) is an American brand of light- and medium-duty commercial vehicles, and a division of Stellantis (through FCA US LLC). It was established in 2010 when Chrysler spun the Ram pickup line out of the Dodge brand into its own marque.

History

The Ram nameplate dates to Dodge Ram pickups introduced for the 1981 model year, named for the ram's-head hood ornament long used by Dodge. As part of Chrysler's post-bankruptcy restructuring, the Ram brand was separated from Dodge in 2009–2010 so that Dodge could focus on passenger cars while Ram concentrated on pickups and commercial vans.

Products

The flagship model is the full-size Ram pickup (1500, and the heavy-duty 2500 and 3500 series), one of the best-selling vehicles in the United States. The brand also sells the ProMaster full-size van (derived from the Fiat Ducato), chassis-cab models, and the mid-size Rampage in Latin American markets. Ram trucks are known for coil-spring and available air suspension, the RamBox cargo system, and engines including the Hemi V8, the Cummins turbo-diesel inline-six, and newer Hurricane twin-turbo inline-six powerplants.
