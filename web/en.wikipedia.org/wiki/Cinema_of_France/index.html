Cinema of France

The cinema of France comprises the film industry and its productions in France. The nation has a strong tradition, being the birthplace of cinema itself, and is known for art films and strong state support.

PioneersLumière brothers, Georges Méliès
Major movementsPoetic realism, French New Wave
FestivalCannes Film Festival

History

The Lumière brothers gave the first public film screening in Paris in 1895; Méliès pioneered special effects. After poetic realism of the 1930s (Renoir, Carné), the French New Wave of the late 1950s — Truffaut, Godard, Varda — revolutionised world cinema with location shooting and auteur theory.

Industry today

France produces around 200+ feature films a year, sustains high cinema attendance, and protects domestic production through the CNC and quotas. See Film festival.
