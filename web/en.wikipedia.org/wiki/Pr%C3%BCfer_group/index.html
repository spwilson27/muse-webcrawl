NotationZ(p∞)
TypeInfinite abelian p-group

In mathematics, specifically in group theory, the Prüfer p-group or quasicyclic group Z(p∞), for a prime number p, is the unique p-group in which every element has p different p-th roots. It is named after the German mathematician Heinz Prüfer.

Construction

The group can be realised as the subgroup of the circle group consisting of all pn-th roots of unity for all n, or equivalently as the quotient Q/Z localised at p — the group of rationals whose denominators are powers of p, taken modulo the integers. It is the direct limit of the cyclic groups Z(pn).

Properties

The Prüfer group is an infinite abelian p-group whose subgroups are linearly ordered by inclusion, forming a chain indexed by the natural numbers. It is a standard example of an Artinian but not Noetherian module over the integers, and it appears in the classification of divisible abelian groups.
