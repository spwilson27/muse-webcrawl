A music festival is a community event oriented towards live performances of singing and instrumental music, often presented with a theme such as a musical genre, a composer, nationality, or locality, and typically held outdoors over one or more days. Festivals range from small local gatherings to some of the largest recurring events in the entertainment industry.

History

Musical contests formed part of the ancient Greek Pythian Games at Delphi. In Britain the Three Choirs Festival, rotating among Gloucester, Worcester and Hereford cathedrals since about 1715, is among the world's oldest classical festivals. The modern popular music festival emerged in the 1950s–60s with events such as the Newport Jazz Festival (1954) and Newport Folk Festival (1959); the Monterey International Pop Festival (1967) and Woodstock (1969) established the template of the multi-day rock festival and its countercultural associations. Britain's Glastonbury Festival, founded in 1970, and Denmark's Roskilde Festival (1971) became enduring institutions.

The modern festival industry

Today large festivals such as Coachella (California), Lollapalooza, Primavera Sound (Barcelona), Fuji Rock (Japan) and the free Donauinselfest in Vienna draw hundreds of thousands — in Vienna's case, millions over three days. Electronic dance music festivals (Tomorrowland, Ultra, Electric Daisy Carnival) form a parallel circuit. Festivals anchor regional tourism, anchor artists' touring income in the streaming era, and increasingly compete on experience — art installations, food, camping and site design — as much as on line-ups. Since the 2010s the sector has faced scrutiny over environmental impact, ticket pricing, safety at crowded events, and its post-pandemic economics.

Classical and specialist festivals

Classical music retains its own circuit — the BBC Proms (London, since 1895), Salzburg Festival (1920), Bayreuth Festival (devoted to Wagner), Edinburgh International Festival and countless summer opera and chamber-music festivals. Specialist events celebrate jazz (Montreux, New Orleans Jazz & Heritage), folk, early music, metal (Wacken Open Air) and nearly every other genre.
