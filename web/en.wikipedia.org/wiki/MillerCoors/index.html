MillerCoors

MillerCoors was an American brewing company formed in 2008 as a joint venture between SABMiller and Molson Coors, combining their brewing operations in the United States and Puerto Rico. It was headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and was the second-largest brewer in the United States, behind Anheuser-Busch.

History

The joint venture was announced in October 2007 and began operations on July 1, 2008. It combined the Miller Brewing Company, founded in Milwaukee in 1855, with the Coors Brewing Company, founded in Golden, Colorado, in 1873. The venture marketed brands including Miller Lite, Miller High Life, Miller Genuine Draft, Coors Light, Coors Banquet, Blue Moon and Leinenkugel's.

Dissolution

When Anheuser-Busch InBev agreed to acquire SABMiller in 2015, antitrust regulators required the divestiture of SABMiller's United States interests. Molson Coors acquired SABMiller's stake in MillerCoors, completing the purchase in October 2016 and becoming the sole owner. The business thereafter operated as the United States unit of Molson Coors, and in 2020 the MillerCoors name was retired as the parent company rebranded as Molson Coors Beverage Company and reorganized its North American operations under the name Molson Coors North America.

Operations

At its peak the company operated major breweries in Milwaukee, Wisconsin; Golden, Colorado; Fort Worth, Texas; Albany, Georgia; Eden, North Carolina; Irwindale, California; Chippewa Falls, Wisconsin; and Trenton, Ohio.
