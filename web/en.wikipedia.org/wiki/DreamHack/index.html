DreamHack is a Swedish production company specialising in esports tournaments and gaming conventions, best known for its festivals built around large LAN parties, in which thousands of attendees bring their own computers to play together around the clock. Founded in 1994 as a small gathering of school friends in Malung, Sweden, DreamHack grew into what has repeatedly been recognised as the world's largest LAN party and computer festival, at times holding records for the fastest internet connection and the most generated network traffic.

Events

The flagship events are DreamHack Summer and DreamHack Winter, held at the Elmia exhibition centre in Jönköping, Sweden, each drawing tens of thousands of visitors. Alongside the bring-your-own-computer halls, the festivals host professional esports competitions in games such as Counter-Strike, StarCraft II, League of Legends, Dota 2 and fighting games, as well as cosplay, concerts, expos and content-creator programming. DreamHack has expanded to festivals across Europe, North America, Asia and Australia, including events in Atlanta, Dallas, Melbourne, Hyderabad and Stockholm.

Ownership

DreamHack was acquired by Modern Times Group in 2015 and merged with ESL in 2020; following the 2022 acquisition of ESL and FACEIT by the Savvy Games Group, DreamHack operates as part of the ESL FACEIT Group, positioning its events as gaming-lifestyle festivals rather than pure LAN parties.
