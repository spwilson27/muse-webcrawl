Sikorsky UH-60 Black Hawk

The Sikorsky UH-60 Black Hawk is a four-blade, twin-engine, medium-lift utility military helicopter manufactured by Sikorsky Aircraft. It won the United States Army's Utility Tactical Transport Aircraft System competition in 1976 and entered service in 1979, replacing the Bell UH-1 Iroquois.

Design and service

The Black Hawk can transport a squad of 11 troops, carry sling loads, and perform aeromedical evacuation. It has been widely exported and adapted into numerous variants, including the naval SH-60 Seahawk, the Air Force MH-60G Pave Hawk and the HH-60 rescue aircraft. More than 4,000 have been built, and it has seen extensive combat service from Grenada and Panama to Iraq and Afghanistan.
