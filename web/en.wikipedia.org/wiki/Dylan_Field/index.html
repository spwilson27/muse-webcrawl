Dylan Field

Dylan Field (born c. 1992) is an American technology executive and designer, best known as the co-founder and chief executive officer of Figma, a collaborative web-based design and prototyping platform.

Early life and education

Field grew up in Penngrove, California. He studied computer science and mathematics at Brown University. In 2012, while a junior at Brown, he was awarded a Thiel Fellowship — a $100,000 grant encouraging young people to leave university to work on start-ups — and dropped out to found Figma with Evan Wallace, a teaching assistant he had met at Brown.

Career

Figma was founded in 2012 and launched publicly in 2016 as the first browser-based, multiplayer interface design tool. Under Field's leadership it grew into a dominant product-design platform. In September 2022, Adobe announced an agreement to acquire Figma for approximately $20 billion; the deal was abandoned in December 2023 after regulatory opposition in the United Kingdom and European Union. Figma subsequently completed an initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange in 2025.
