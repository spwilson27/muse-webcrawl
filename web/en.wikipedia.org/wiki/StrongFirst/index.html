StrongFirst

Kettlebell hardstyle training as taught by StrongFirst

StrongFirst is a strength-training education company founded in 2012 by Pavel Tsatsouline, the former Soviet special forces physical training instructor who popularised kettlebell training in the West. The organisation describes itself as a "school of strength" and offers instructor certifications, courses and seminars in kettlebell, barbell and bodyweight training.

History

Tsatsouline founded StrongFirst after parting ways with Dragon Door, with which he had created the RKC (Russian Kettlebell Challenge) instructor certification in 2001. StrongFirst took over and developed the certification curriculum, establishing levels such as SFG (StrongFirst Girya, for kettlebells), SFL (barbell) and SFB (bodyweight), along with demanding performance standards — most famously the "Sinister" standard of 100 one-arm kettlebell swings and 10 get-ups with heavy weights, an extension of the "Simple" standard from Tsatsouline's book Kettlebell Simple & Sinister.

Methods

The system teaches "hardstyle" kettlebell technique, emphasising maximal tension — full-body irradiation, power breathing and the "rooting" of lifts — derived from martial arts practice. Programming ideas associated with StrongFirst include "grease the groove" (frequent submaximal practice), anti-glycolytic training, and the minimalist programmes of Tsatsouline's earlier works such as Power to the People! and Enter the Kettlebell!
