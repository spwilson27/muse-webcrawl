Maximum power point tracking

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
The maximum power point is the knee of the panel's I–V curve.

Maximum power point tracking (MPPT) is a technique used with variable power sources, especially photovoltaic (PV) solar panels, to maximise power extraction under all conditions. The output of a solar module depends nonlinearly on sunlight intensity, temperature and the electrical load connected to it; for any given conditions there is a single operating point — the maximum power point — at which the product of current and voltage, and hence the power delivered, is greatest.

Principle

A PV cell's current–voltage (I–V) curve has a characteristic "knee". Connecting the panel directly to a battery or fixed load usually forces it to operate away from this point. An MPPT controller continuously measures the panel's voltage and current and adjusts the operating point, typically by varying the duty cycle of a DC–DC converter, so that the panel stays at or near its maximum power point as conditions change with clouds, shading and temperature.

Algorithms

The most widely used method is perturb and observe: the controller makes a small change to the operating voltage, observes whether power increased or decreased, and continues moving in the direction that increases power. Incremental conductance compares the instantaneous conductance (I/V) with the incremental conductance (dI/dV) to locate the point where dP/dV = 0. Simpler fractional open-circuit voltage and fractional short-circuit current methods estimate the point from a fixed fraction of the panel's open-circuit voltage. More advanced approaches use scanning or model-based prediction to avoid being trapped at local maxima under partial shading.

Applications

MPPT is standard in grid-connected solar inverters and in battery charge controllers for off-grid systems, where it can increase harvested energy by roughly 10–30 percent compared with simple pulse-width-modulation controllers, especially in cold or variable weather. The same principle is applied to wind turbines and other variable sources.
