List of Puerto Ricans

The following is a list of notable Puerto Ricans — people born in Puerto Rico, of Puerto Rican descent, or who have made Puerto Rico their home — organized by field of activity. Puerto Ricans have distinguished themselves in entertainment, sports, politics, science, and the military, both on the island and in the large diaspora in the continental United States.

Entertainment

  • Rita Moreno (born 1931) — actress, one of few EGOT winners
  • José Ferrer (1912–1992) — first Hispanic Academy Award winner (Best Actor, Cyrano de Bergerac)
  • Raúl Juliá (1940–1994) — actor (The Addams Family, Kiss of the Spider Woman)
  • Benicio del Toro (born 1967) — Academy Award-winning actor
  • Joaquin Phoenix (born 1974) — actor, born in San Juan
  • Jennifer Lopez (born 1969) — singer and actress of Puerto Rican descent
  • Ricky Martin (born 1971) — singer, former member of Menudo
  • Marc Anthony (born 1968) — salsa singer
  • Daddy Yankee (born 1976) — reggaeton pioneer ("Gasolina", "Despacito")
  • Bad Bunny (Benito Martínez Ocasio, born 1994) — reggaeton and Latin trap star
  • Luis Fonsi (born 1978) — singer of "Despacito"
  • Lin-Manuel Miranda (born 1980) — composer of Hamilton and In the Heights
  • Rosie Perez (born 1964) — actress and choreographer
  • Esai Morales (born 1962) — actor
  • Aubrey Plaza (born 1984) — actress of Puerto Rican descent
  • Chita Rivera (1933–2024) — Broadway legend
  • Héctor Lavoe (1946–1993) — salsa singer
  • Tito Puente (1923–2000) — "King of Latin Music", mambo bandleader
  • Celia Cruz — honorary mention via close association (Cuban-born; excluded from main list)

Sports

  • Roberto Clemente (1934–1972) — Hall of Fame baseball right fielder, Pittsburgh Pirates
  • Orlando Cepeda (1937–2024) — Hall of Fame baseball first baseman
  • Iván Rodríguez (born 1971) — Hall of Fame catcher
  • Roberto Alomar (born 1968) — Hall of Fame second baseman
  • Carlos Beltrán (born 1977) — baseball outfielder
  • Yadier Molina (born 1982) — baseball catcher
  • Francisco Lindor (born 1993) — baseball shortstop
  • Félix "Tito" Trinidad (born 1973) — boxing world champion
  • Miguel Cotto (born 1980) — four-division boxing champion
  • Monica Puig (born 1993) — tennis player, Olympic gold medalist (Rio 2016)
  • Jasmine Camacho-Quinn (born 1996) — Olympic gold medalist hurdler (Tokyo 2020)
  • Carlos Arroyo (born 1979) — basketball player
  • José Juan Barea (born 1984) — NBA champion guard

Politics and government

  • Luis Muñoz Marín (1898–1980) — first elected governor of Puerto Rico
  • Sonia Sotomayor (born 1954) — Associate Justice of the U.S. Supreme Court
  • Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (born 1989) — U.S. Representative, of Puerto Rican descent
  • Nydia Velázquez (born 1953) — U.S. Representative from New York
  • Luis Fortuño (born 1960) — former governor of Puerto Rico
  • Pedro Pierluisi (born 1959) — governor of Puerto Rico
  • Jenniffer González-Colón (born 1976) — resident commissioner; governor-elect 2024
  • Aníbal Acevedo Vilá (born 1962) — former governor
  • Sila María Calderón (born 1942) — first female governor of Puerto Rico

Science and medicine

  • Olga D. González-Sanabria — NASA engineer and inventor
  • Nilsa Paris — placeholder removed; see NASA scientists of Puerto Rican origin
  • Joseph M. Acabá (born 1967) — NASA astronaut of Puerto Rican descent
  • Franklin Chang-Díaz — astronaut of partial Puerto Rican heritage (Costa Rican-born)
  • Antonia Coello Novello (born 1944) — first woman and first Hispanic U.S. Surgeon General

Military

  • Juan César Cordero Dávila (1904–1965) — Major General, Korean War commander
  • Horacio Rivero Jr. (1910–2000) — first Puerto Rican four-star admiral
  • José M. Cabanillas (1901–1979) — Rear Admiral, WWII
  • 65th Infantry Regiment ("Borinqueneers") — segregated Puerto Rican regiment, Korean War; Congressional Gold Medal 2014
