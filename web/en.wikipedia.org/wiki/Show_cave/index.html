Show cave

A show cave is a cave that has been made accessible to the public for guided tours, usually with installed pathways, lighting, and safety features. Show caves are managed for tourism and often for conservation of their geological formations and cave-dwelling species.

Development

Developing a show cave involves building walkways and stairs, installing electric lighting (kept limited to reduce lampenflora, algae growth on lit surfaces), and controlling visitor numbers. Famous examples include Mammoth Cave in Kentucky, Postojna Cave in Slovenia, and the Reed Flute Cave in China.

Conservation

Because caves are fragile environments, operators monitor temperature, humidity, and carbon dioxide, and may close sections to protect bats and speleothems.
