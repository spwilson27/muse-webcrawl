Celtic League

The Celtic League is a pan-Celtic organisation founded in 1961 that aims to promote the identity, culture, and language of the Celtic nations: Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Brittany, Cornwall, and the Isle of Man.

Celtic League
Founded: 1961, Rhosllanerchrugog, Wales
Type: Pan-Celtic organisation
Publication: Carn
Nations: Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Brittany, Cornwall, Isle of Man

Aims and activity

The League campaigns for the political, cultural, social and economic freedom of the Celtic nations, supports their languages, and fosters cooperation between them. Its journal is Carn, published since 1973. It has held consultative status with United Nations bodies and has monitored military and human-rights issues in Celtic regions.

Note: the name is also used by a former Scottish football discussion and by other unrelated sporting contexts.
