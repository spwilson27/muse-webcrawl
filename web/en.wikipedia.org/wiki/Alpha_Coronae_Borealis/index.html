Alpha Coronae Borealis (α Coronae Borealis, abbreviated Alpha CrB) is an eclipsing binary star and the brightest star in the constellation Corona Borealis, the Northern Crown. It bears the traditional names Alphecca and Gemma (Latin for "jewel").

Properties

The system lies about 75 light-years from the Sun. The primary is a white main-sequence star of spectral type A0V, roughly 2.6 times the Sun's mass, while the secondary is a yellow main-sequence star similar to the Sun. The two orbit each other every 17.36 days; the secondary partially eclipses the primary, causing the system's brightness to dip slightly.

Nomenclature

The name Alphecca derives from Arabic nayyir al-fakka, "the bright star of the broken (ring)". In 2016 the International Astronomical Union approved the name Alphecca for the primary star.
