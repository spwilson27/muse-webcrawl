Milady de Winter

Milady de Winter, often simply Milady, is a fictional character in Alexandre Dumas's novel The Three Musketeers (1844). She is the novel's principal antagonist: a beautiful and ruthless spy in the service of Cardinal Richelieu and the secret wife of the musketeer Athos.

Role in the novel

Born Anne de Breuil, she was, in Athos's account, a young woman who seduced him — then the Comte de la Fère — into marriage before he discovered she bore a fleur-de-lis branded on her shoulder, the mark of a convicted criminal; he hanged her, but she survived. As Richelieu's agent she works against the Duke of Buckingham and the Queen's interests, steals the queen's diamond studs with the help of her lover, seduces and manipulates the musketeers' servant-planchet and others, and engineers the assassination of Buckingham by the fanatic John Felton, whom she beguiles in prison.

She murders Constance Bonacieux, d'Artagnan's beloved, by poison. Captured at last, she is tried by the musketeers and their allies in a makeshift court and beheaded by the executioner of Lille, whose brother she had destroyed — one of the most famous femme fatale arcs in 19th-century literature.

Portrayals

Milady has been played on screen by, among others, Lana Turner (1948), Faye Dunaway (1973), Rebecca De Mornay (1993), Milla Jovovich (2011) and Eva Green (2023).
