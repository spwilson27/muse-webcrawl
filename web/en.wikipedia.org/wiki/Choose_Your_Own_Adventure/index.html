Choose Your Own Adventure

Choose Your Own Adventure
Genre: gamebook
Publisher: Bantam Books (original series)
Published: 1979–1998 (original series)
Created by: Edward Packard, R. A. Montgomery

Choose Your Own Adventure is a series of children's gamebooks where the reader assumes the role of the protagonist and makes choices that determine the main character's actions and the plot's outcome. The series was created by Edward Packard and popularized by R. A. Montgomery, and was published by Bantam Books from 1979 to 1998.

Format

The stories are written in the second person, casting the reader as the hero ("You are..."). After a few pages of reading, the reader faces two or three options, each leading to a different page number. A book may have dozens of endings, many of them unhappy, and only one or a few fully successful outcomes. Readers frequently kept fingers in several pages to backtrack after a bad ending.

History

Packard's prototype, Sugarcane Island, was published in 1976 by Vermont Crossroads Press. The Bantam series began in 1979 with Packard's The Cave of Time and became one of the most popular children's series of the 1980s, selling more than 250 million copies worldwide and inspiring many imitators. Montgomery later revived the series through Chooseco beginning in 2005.

Influence

The branching structure of the books anticipated interactive fiction, visual novels, and narrative video games, and the trademark phrase became a generic term for reader-directed stories.
