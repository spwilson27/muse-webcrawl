Dal segno

Dal segno (often abbreviated D.S.) is a musical term and navigation marker meaning "from the sign". It instructs the performer to repeat a passage of music starting from the sign, a special mark resembling a stylized letter S (𝄋) placed above the staff, and to continue until a designated endpoint such as a fine marking or a concluding barline.

Usage

Dal segno instructions appear commonly in published sheet music where a passage is to be repeated without rewriting it in full. Common forms include:

D.S. al Fine — repeat from the sign and play until the word Fine, then stop. D.S. al Coda — repeat from the sign, then, at the coda mark, skip to the separate coda section. D.S. al Segno appears in some multipart repeats.

Related terms

The term is closely related to da capo ("from the head"), which directs a return to the very beginning of the piece. Both conventions flourished in Baroque and Classical music, and both remain in everyday use in jazz lead sheets and popular music notation.

In modern notation software, dal segno signs and codas are handled by playback engines that follow the same roadmap logic human performers use.
