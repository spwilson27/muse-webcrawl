Immortality (video game)

Immortality is a 2022 interactive film video game developed by Sam Barlow and Half Mermaid, and published by Half Mermaid. It was released for Windows and Xbox Series X/S in August 2022, with versions for Android, iOS, and macOS following later that year.

Gameplay

The player explores a trove of footage centred on fictional actress Marissa Marcel, who starred in three films — Ambrosio (1968), Minsky (1970), and Two of Everything (1999) — none of which were released, before she disappeared. Using a match-cut mechanic, the player clicks on objects or people in a clip to jump to other footage containing similar imagery, gradually uncovering a hidden supernatural narrative embedded within subverted frames of the film clips.

Cast and reception

Marissa Marcel is played by Manon Gage, with supporting performances including Charlot Daysh and Timothy Lee DePriest. The game received widespread critical acclaim for its performances, layered storytelling, and exploration of cinema and celebrity, winning several awards including the Seumas McNally Grand Prize nomination at the Independent Games Festival and a BAFTA Games Award for Narrative-adjacent categories recognition among critics' year-end lists.
