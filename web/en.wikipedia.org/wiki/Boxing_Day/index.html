Boxing Day

Boxing Day
Date: 26 December
Observed: UK, Canada, Australia, NZ & Commonwealth
Type: Public holiday

Boxing Day is a holiday celebrated on 26 December, the day after Christmas Day, in the United Kingdom and many Commonwealth countries.

Origins

The name comes from traditions of giving a "Christmas box" — money or gifts — to servants, tradesmen and the poor on the day after Christmas, when servants who had worked on Christmas Day received a day off to visit family. Church alms boxes were also opened and distributed. It coincides with Saint Stephen's Day in some traditions.

Modern observance

Today it is known for major retail sales, sporting events (Premier League football, the Boxing Day Test cricket match in Melbourne, horse racing), outdoor pursuits, and charity swims. See Christmas, Saint Stephen's Day.
