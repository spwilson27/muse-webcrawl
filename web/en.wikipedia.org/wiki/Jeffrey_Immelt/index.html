Jeffrey Robert Immelt (born 19 February 1956) is an American business executive who served as chairman and CEO of General Electric from 2001 to 2017, succeeding Jack Welch.

Tenure at GE

Immelt took over GE days before the September 11 attacks and steered the conglomerate through the 2008 financial crisis, which badly damaged GE Capital. He reoriented the company toward industrial businesses, acquiring Alstom's power business in 2015 while divesting GE Capital assets and NBC Universal. Under his leadership GE's stock price and market value fell substantially, and in 2018 the company was removed from the Dow Jones Industrial Average after 111 years.

He stepped down as CEO in 2017, succeeded by John Flannery, and later became a venture partner at New Enterprise Associates. His 2021 memoir Hot Seat recounts his GE years.
