Lists of volcanoes

This page lists articles that enumerate volcanoes of the world, organised by country, region, type and activity. There are roughly 1,350 potentially active volcanoes worldwide, and several thousand more that are dormant or extinct. Volcano lists are maintained by organisations such as the Smithsonian Institution's Global Volcanism Program, which catalogues volcanoes and their eruptions during the Holocene epoch.

Volcanoes occur mainly along plate boundaries, such as the Pacific Ring of Fire.
