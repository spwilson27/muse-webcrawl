Jonathan Rea

Jonathan Rea (born 2 February 1987) is a Northern Irish professional motorcycle racer who competes in the Superbike World Championship. He is the most successful rider in the championship's history, winning six consecutive world titles between 2015 and 2020 with the Kawasaki Racing Team, and holding the records for the most race wins and podium finishes in the series.

Early career

Rea grew up in Larne, County Antrim, in a motorcycling family — his father, Johnny Rea, was a successful road racer who won the Junior TT at the Isle of Man. Jonathan began in motocross before switching to circuit racing, progressing through the British Superbike Championship, where he finished runner-up in 2007, and a season in the World Supersport Championship with Ten Kate Honda.

World Superbike

Rea moved to World Superbikes with Honda in 2009, taking his first wins that year and establishing himself as a consistent front-runner despite machinery that was often outclassed. His breakthrough came after joining Kawasaki in 2015: he won the world championship that season and retained it for five further years, a run of six consecutive titles that surpassed the records of Carl Fogarty and Troy Bayliss. His dominance was ended by Toprak Razgatlıoğlu in 2021.

In 2024 Rea left Kawasaki after nine seasons to join the factory Yamaha team. Across his career he has won well over one hundred World Superbike races — by far the highest total in the championship's history — and his consistency and racecraft have led many observers to regard him as the greatest Superbike racer of all time.
