List of earthquakes in Peru

Peru is located in a seismically active region on the western margin of South America, where the Nazca Plate subducts beneath the South American Plate along the Peru–Chile Trench. This is a list of notable earthquakes in Peru.

Subduction of the Nazca Plate drives Peru's frequent large earthquakes and tsunamis.

Background

The convergence rate between the Nazca and South American plates offshore Peru is roughly 6–7 cm per year. The resulting megathrust has generated some of the largest earthquakes in South American history, while crustal faults in the Andes produce damaging inland events.
