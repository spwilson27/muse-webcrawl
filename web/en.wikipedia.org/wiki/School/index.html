School

School
DefinitionInstitution for educating learners under teachers' direction
LevelsPrimary, secondary; also pre-school and further education
[Image: Children in a bright classroom raising hands toward a teacher]

A school is an educational institution designed to provide learning spaces and environments for teaching students under the direction of teachers. Most countries have state-funded systems of primary and secondary schooling, compulsory for children through their teens.

Purpose and history

Formal schools date to antiquity, but mass schooling is a 19th-century development tied to industrialisation and democracy. Schools teach literacy, numeracy and knowledge — the foundation on which a university builds — and socialise children into society; debates concern curriculum, testing, funding and the role of technology. Education's universality is a secular public good in most modern states.
