Stunting (broadcasting)

In broadcasting, stunting is the practice of a radio or television station temporarily adopting an unusual, attention-grabbing format — often when transitioning between regular formats, or as a promotional or seasonal event — in order to generate publicity and listener curiosity.

Common stunts

Typical stunts include looping a single song or a handful of songs for days (for example, playing "We Built This City" or a novelty song on repeat), broadcasting sound effects or a simulated format, airing all-Christmas music for an extended season, or adopting parody formats with joke station names before the real new format launches. A station about to change format may stunt to clear the previous audience and build anticipation, sometimes misleading competitors about its plans.

Notable examples

Famous stunts include stations that played nothing but construction sounds before launching a talk format, and "all-podcast" or "all-commercial" teasers. Prolonged or deceptive stunts have occasionally drawn complaints to regulators such as the FCC.
