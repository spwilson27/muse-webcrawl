The 2018 FIFA World Cup Final was the final match of the 2018 FIFA World Cup, the 21st edition of FIFA's competition for national football teams. It was played at the Luzhniki Stadium in Moscow, Russia, on 15 July 2018, between France and Croatia. France won 4–2, claiming their second World Cup title.

Route to the final

France topped Group C before defeating Argentina, Uruguay and Belgium in the knockout rounds. Croatia topped Group D and reached the final via wins over Denmark and hosts Russia on penalties, and England after extra time in the semi-final. It was Croatia's first World Cup final.

Match

France took the lead in the 18th minute through a Mario Mandžukić own goal from an Antoine Griezmann free kick. Ivan Perišić equalised ten minutes later, but Griezmann restored the lead from a penalty awarded after a VAR review for handball. In the second half, Paul Pogba and Kylian Mbappé extended the lead to 4–1; Mandžukić pulled one back after a Hugo Lloris error. Mbappé became the first teenager to score in a World Cup final since Pelé in 1958.

Aftermath

France's manager Didier Deschamps became the third person to win the World Cup as both player and manager. Luka Modrić was awarded the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player. The match was briefly interrupted by a pitch invasion by members of the protest group Pussy Riot.
