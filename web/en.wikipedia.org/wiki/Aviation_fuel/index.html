Aviation fuel

Aviation fuels are petroleum-based fuels, or petroleum and synthetic fuel blends, used to power aircraft. They have more stringent requirements than fuels used for ground use, such as heating and road transport, and contain additives to enhance or maintain properties important to fuel performance or handling. They are kerosene-based (Jet A, Jet A-1, JP-5, and JP-8) for gas turbine-powered aircraft, while piston-engined aircraft use aviation gasoline (avgas).

Types

Conventional aviation fuels are divided into two main categories. Jet fuel is a kerosene-type fuel used in turbine engines; the most common grades are Jet A and Jet A-1, produced to standardized international specifications. Jet B is a naphtha-kerosene blend used for its enhanced cold-weather performance in very cold climates such as northern Canada and Alaska. Military grades include JP-5, a high-flash-point fuel designed for aircraft carrier use, and JP-8, widely used by NATO forces.

Avgas (aviation gasoline) is used in small piston-engined aircraft. The most common grade is 100LL (low lead), which still contains tetraethyllead additive, making general aviation a remaining source of lead emissions. Unleaded grades such as UL94 and G100UL have been developed and approved as replacements.

Properties

Aviation fuels must perform reliably over a wide range of temperatures and altitudes. Jet fuels are specified for low freezing points (Jet A-1 freezes at −47 °C), high energy content per unit mass, thermal stability, and cleanliness. Fuel additives may include antioxidants, metal deactivators, corrosion inhibitors, fuel system icing inhibitors, and static dissipators.

Sustainable aviation fuel

Sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) is produced from renewable feedstocks such as used cooking oil, municipal waste, and agricultural residues, and can reduce lifecycle carbon emissions substantially compared with fossil jet fuel. SAF is typically blended with conventional fuel and certified as a drop-in replacement. Research also continues into hydrogen and electric propulsion for short-range aircraft.
