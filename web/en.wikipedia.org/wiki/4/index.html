4

4 (four) is a number, numeral and digit. It is the natural number following 3 and preceding 5. It is a square number (2²), the smallest composite number, and the smallest Smith number. Four is the only composite number whose value equals the number of letters in its English name.

Mathematics

Four is the smallest prime power that is not prime, p² for p = 2. The four color theorem states that any planar map can be colored with no more than four colors so that adjacent regions differ. A tetrahedron has four faces and four vertices, and Lagrange's four-square theorem says every natural number is the sum of at most four squares. In Knuth's up-arrow notation and tetration, 4 appears in the sequence defining very large numbers.

In culture

There are four cardinal directions, four seasons, four classical elements (earth, air, fire, water), and four suits in a standard deck of cards. In several East Asian cultures the number is considered unlucky (tetraphobia) because the word for "four" sounds similar to the word for "death" in Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
