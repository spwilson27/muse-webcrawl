European Universities Debating Championship

Image: University debating (placeholder)

The European Universities Debating Championship (EUDC) is the annual European championship in university debating, contested in the British Parliamentary style. Founded in 1999, it brings together teams from universities across Europe and is governed by the European Universities Debating Council.

Format

Teams of two speakers debate motions announced shortly before each round, in the four-team British Parliamentary format used at the World Universities Debating Championship. After preliminary rounds, the best teams advance to knockout outrounds, with separate finals for the open category and for teams debating in English as a second language (ESL). The tournament typically draws several hundred participants and is hosted by a different university city each year.

Significance

EUDC is the largest regional debating championship in Europe and a major fixture of the competitive debating circuit, alongside the World Championship. Hosts have included universities in cities such as Athens, Zagreb, Warsaw, Tallinn, and Vienna.
