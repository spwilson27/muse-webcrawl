University of Mount Union

The University of Mount Union is a private liberal arts university in Alliance, Ohio. Founded in 1846 by Orville Nelson Hartshorn as Mount Union Seminary, it became Mount Union College and adopted its present name in 2011. It is affiliated with the United Methodist Church and enrolls roughly 2,000 students.

History and campus

Mount Union was coeducational and nonsectarian in admissions from early in its history. The 115-acre campus in Alliance includes Chapman Hall (1864) and a lakes district used for recreation and biology fieldwork. The university offers bachelor's degrees across the liberal arts and sciences, professional programmes in engineering, nursing, and business, and graduate programmes including physician assistant studies and education.

Athletics

The Purple Raiders compete in NCAA Division III and are famous for their football programme, which has won a record number of Division III national championships — thirteen between 1993 and 2017 under coaches Larry Kehres and Vince Kehres — and set an NCAA record with a 112-game regular-season winning streak.
