Provinces of Papua New Guinea

Number: 22 province-level divisions
Includes: 20 provinces, Bougainville (autonomous region), National Capital District
Newest: Hela and Jiwaka (2012)

Papua New Guinea is divided into 22 province-level divisions: 20 provinces, the Autonomous Region of Bougainville, and the National Capital District around Port Moresby. Provinces are the primary administrative subdivisions of the country, grouped informally into four regions: Highlands, Islands, Momase, and Southern (Papua).

Government

Each province has a provincial assembly and, since reforms in 1995, is headed by the province's governor, who is also its representative in the National Parliament. Bougainville has additional autonomy under its post-conflict peace agreement, including an eventual independence referendum held in 2019 in which voters overwhelmingly backed independence.

History

The provincial system was introduced in the 1970s around independence (1975), partly to manage the country's extreme linguistic and cultural diversity — Papua New Guinea has more than 800 living languages. Hela and Jiwaka provinces were created in 2012 from Southern Highlands and Western Highlands respectively.
