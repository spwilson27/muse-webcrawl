Walter Bruce Willis (born March 19, 1955) is a retired American actor. He achieved fame with the comedy-drama series Moonlighting (1985–1989) and became a leading action star as John McClane in the Die Hard franchise (1988–2013), one of the defining action roles of the era.

Career

Born on a US military base in West Germany and raised in New Jersey, Willis worked as a bartender and private investigator before acting. His films have grossed over $5 billion worldwide. Notable roles include Pulp Fiction (1994), 12 Monkeys (1995), The Fifth Element (1997), Armageddon (1998), The Sixth Sense (1999), Unbreakable (2000), and Looper (2012). He won an Emmy Award and a Golden Globe Award during his career.

Retirement and health

In March 2022 his family announced his retirement after a diagnosis of aphasia; in February 2023 they announced the more specific diagnosis of frontotemporal dementia.
