Emarosa

Origin: Lexington, Kentucky, U.S.
Genres: Post-hardcore, alternative rock, pop rock
Years active: 2006–present
Labels: Rise, Hopeless, Out of Line

Emarosa (/ɛməˈroʊsə/) is an American rock band formed in Lexington, Kentucky, in 2006. The band released its first EP, This Is Your Way Out, in 2007 through StandBy Records, and its debut album, Relativity, in 2008 on Rise Records with vocalist Jonny Craig.

History

Craig departed in 2011, and after a period of uncertainty the band recruited Bradley Walden as vocalist. With Walden, Emarosa released Versus (2014) and 131 (2016) on Hopeless Records, shifting toward a more melodic alternative and pop-influenced sound. Subsequent albums include Peach Club (2019) and Sting (2023).

Musical style

The band's early work was rooted in post-hardcore and metalcore; later releases incorporate pop, R&B, and funk influences alongside alternative rock.
