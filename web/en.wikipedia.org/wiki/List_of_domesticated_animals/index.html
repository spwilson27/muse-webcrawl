List of domesticated animals

This is a list of animals that have been domesticated by humans — bred in captivity over many generations and selectively modified from their wild ancestors for use by humans. Domestication dates are approximate; most livestock were domesticated during the Neolithic Revolution beginning around 10,000 years ago.
