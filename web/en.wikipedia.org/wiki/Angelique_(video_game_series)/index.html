Angelique (video game series)

DeveloperRuby Party (Koei / Koei Tecmo)
First releaseAngelique, 23 September 1994
GenreDating simulation / otome game
PlatformsSuper Famicom and successors

Angelique (Japanese: アンジェリーク) is a Japanese media franchise of dating simulation games developed by Ruby Party, the all-female development division of Koei (now Koei Tecmo), with character designs by shōjo manga artist Kairi Yura. The first game, released for the Super Famicom in 1994, is widely credited as the first otome game — a story-based game targeted towards women in which the goal includes developing a romantic relationship with one of several male characters.

Premise and gameplay

The heroine, Angelique Limoges, is a candidate to become Queen of the cosmos. Together with a rival candidate, she must cultivate a continent with the help of the nine Guardians, each embodying a power such as Light, Darkness, Wind or Fire. The player balances world-building tasks against building relationships with the Guardians, whom she may request for aid.

Legacy

The series expanded into numerous sequels and spin-offs, including Angelique Special, Angelique Trois and Angelique Luminarise (2021), as well as manga, anime television series and OVAs, drama CDs and stage productions. Its success established the otome genre and Ruby Party's Neo Romance label, which also produced the Harukanaru Toki no Naka de and La Corda d'Oro series.
