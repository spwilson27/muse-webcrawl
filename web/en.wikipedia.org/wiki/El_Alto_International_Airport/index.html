El Alto International Airport

El Alto International Airport (IATA: LPB, ICAO: SLLP) is an international airport serving La Paz, the seat of government of Bolivia. Located in the city of El Alto, about 13 km southwest of La Paz, it is one of the highest international airports in the world, at an elevation of 4,061.5 metres (13,325 ft) above sea level.

Operations

Because of the thin air at its altitude, aircraft operating at El Alto require long take-off runs and many wide-body types cannot depart at full load. The airport has been in use since the first half of the twentieth century and was modernised in the late 1960s. It is a hub for Boliviana de Aviación and serves mainly domestic routes plus limited international services.
