Associated Press

The Associated Press (AP) is an American non-profit news agency headquartered in New York City, founded in 1846 as a cooperative of newspapers pooling the cost of covering the Mexican–American War.

Cooperative model

AP is owned by its member newspapers, broadcasters and others, and its reporting is carried worldwide. It is known for its definitive US election vote count, its stylebook that sets standards for much American journalism, and extensive photography.
