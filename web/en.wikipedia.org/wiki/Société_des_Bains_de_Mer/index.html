Société des Bains de Mer


Full name: Société Anonyme des Bains de Mer et du Cercle des Étrangers à Monaco
Founded: 1863
Headquarters: Monte Carlo, Monaco
Industry: Hospitality, gaming

The Société des Bains de Mer (officially Société Anonyme des Bains de Mer et du Cercle des Étrangers à Monaco, abbreviated SBM) is a publicly traded company registered in Monaco. It is the largest employer and the dominant hospitality and gaming operator in the principality, running the Monte Carlo Casino, the Opéra de Monte-Carlo, the Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo, the Hôtel Hermitage, and numerous restaurants, clubs, and spas.

History

The company was founded in 1863 when Prince Charles III of Monaco granted a concession to develop a casino and resort at Monte Carlo. Under the direction of François Blanc, the enterprise transformed Monaco into a luxury destination for Europe's elite, financing much of the principality's development and allowing the prince to abolish direct taxation of residents in 1869.

Operations

SBM operates the Casino de Monte-Carlo and Casino Café de Paris, the Monte-Carlo Beach and Monte-Carlo Bay hotels, the Thermes Marins spa, the Jimmy'z and Buddha-Bar nightclubs, and the Sporting Monte-Carlo entertainment complex. The Monegasque state is the majority shareholder, and the company employs several thousand people.
