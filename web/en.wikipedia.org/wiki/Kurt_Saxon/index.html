Kurt Saxon

BornDonald Sisco; March 6, 1932
DiedAugust 16, 2021
OccupationAuthor, survivalist writer

Kurt Saxon (born Donald Sisco) was an American writer and survivalist best known as the author of The Poor Man's James Bond, a multi-volume series of books on improvised weapons and explosives first published in the 1970s.

Life and work

Saxon adopted his pen name in the 1960s and became a figure in the early American survivalist movement. His books compiled material on homemade devices, and he also wrote on self-sufficiency topics such as food storage and traditional crafts in other works. The Poor Man's James Bond became one of the best-known publications of its kind and remained in circulation for decades.
