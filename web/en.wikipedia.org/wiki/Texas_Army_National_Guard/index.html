Texas Army National Guard

Country: United States
Branch: Army National Guard
Part of: Texas Military Department
Headquarters: Camp Mabry, Austin, Texas

The Texas Army National Guard is a component of the United States Army and the Texas Military Forces. It is composed of Texas Army National Guard units and, together with the Texas Air National Guard, forms the state's organized militia under the Texas Military Department. Its units can be called into state service by the Governor of Texas for emergencies such as natural disasters, or federalized by the President of the United States.

Organization

Major formations have included the 36th Infantry Division, the 49th Armored Division (since reflagged), the 71st Troop Command, the 36th Combat Aviation Brigade, and the 176th Engineer Brigade. The 36th Infantry Division, the "Arrowhead" division, is the Texas Army National Guard's principal combat formation and has headquarters at Camp Mabry in Austin.

History

The Texas Army National Guard traces its lineage to militia units of the Republic of Texas and to the famed Texas Rangers tradition. Texas Guardsmen served in the Spanish-American War, on the Mexican border, and in both World Wars; the 36th Division landed at Salerno in 1943 in the first American division assault on mainland Europe. In the twenty-first century, Texas Guard units have deployed repeatedly to Iraq and Afghanistan and have been heavily engaged in domestic missions, including hurricane relief and border support operations such as Operation Lone Star, begun in 2021.
