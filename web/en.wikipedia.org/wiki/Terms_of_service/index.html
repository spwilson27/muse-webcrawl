Terms of service

Terms of service (also known as terms of use or terms and conditions) are the legal agreements between a service provider and a person who wants to use that service. The user must agree to abide by the terms in order to use the offered service, most commonly on websites and software applications.

Forms and enforceability

Online terms are typically presented as clickwrap agreements (requiring an explicit click to accept) or browsewrap agreements (posted via hyperlink). Courts have generally enforced clickwrap terms while scrutinising browsewrap for adequate notice. Terms commonly cover acceptable use, intellectual property, liability limitations, dispute resolution and arbitration, and the provider's right to terminate accounts.

Criticism

Terms of service are famously long and rarely read; studies show most users accept without reading. Critics raise concerns about unilateral changes, forced arbitration clauses, and broad licences over user content, prompting transparency projects such as "Terms of Service; Didn't Read," which grade sites' terms.
