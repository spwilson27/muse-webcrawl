Internet bot

An Internet bot, web robot, or simply bot, is a software application that runs automated tasks over the Internet, typically tasks that are simple, repetitive, and performed at a much higher rate than would be possible for a human.

Types

Benign bots include web crawlers that index content for search engines, chatbots that simulate conversation, and feed fetchers. Other bots monitor websites or assist with online transactions.

Malicious bots

Malicious bots are used for spam, credential stuffing, denial-of-service attacks, ad fraud, and scraping. Networks of compromised computers controlled remotely, called botnets, can coordinate such activity at large scale.

Detection and control

Sites use CAPTCHAs, rate limiting, and the robots exclusion standard (robots.txt) to manage bot traffic. Studies of web traffic have estimated that bots account for a substantial share of all requests on the Internet.
