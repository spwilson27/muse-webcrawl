Blood sport

A blood sport is a sport or pastime involving the bloodshed of animals, typically for entertainment or gambling. The term is generally applied pejoratively to activities such as bullfighting, cockfighting, dog fighting and animal baiting.

Historical forms

Bear-baiting, bull-baiting and dog fighting were popular public spectacles in England from the medieval period until they were prohibited by the Cruelty to Animals Act 1835. Cockfighting, practised since antiquity in Asia and later worldwide, remains legal in a few jurisdictions but is banned in most countries.

Legal status

Most blood sports are now illegal across the developed world on animal-welfare grounds, though enforcement against underground dog fighting and cockfighting continues. Hunting is sometimes classified as a blood sport by critics, a label its supporters dispute.
