Hilton Hotels & Resorts

Hilton Hotels & Resorts is a global brand of full-service hotels and resorts and the flagship brand of the American multinational hospitality company Hilton. Founded by Conrad Hilton in 1919 with the purchase of the Mobley Hotel in Cisco, Texas, the brand grew into one of the world's largest and most recognised hotel chains, with more than 600 properties across six continents.

History

Conrad Hilton bought his first hotel in 1919 and built a chain across Texas before expanding nationally, acquiring landmark properties such as the Stevens Hotel in Chicago (later the Conrad Hilton) and the Waldorf Astoria in New York (management rights, 1949). Hilton is credited with pioneering the airport hotel concept and standardising amenities including air conditioning, televisions in rooms, and direct-dial telephones. In 1946 the company became the first hotel chain listed on the New York Stock Exchange. Its international division was separated in 1964 and reunified in 2006; Blackstone acquired Hilton in 2007 in one of the largest leveraged buyouts of the era.

Brand and properties

Hilton Hotels & Resorts properties are typically large, upscale, full-service hotels in city centres, airports, and resort destinations, many with major conference and convention facilities. Famous properties include the Hilton Hawaiian Village, the London Hilton on Park Lane, and the Istanbul Hilton, opened in 1955. The brand sits within a portfolio of more than twenty Hilton brands, and reservations are integrated with the Hilton Honors loyalty programme, which has over 150 million members.
