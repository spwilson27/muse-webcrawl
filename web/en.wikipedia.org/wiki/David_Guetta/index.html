David Guetta

Pierre David Guetta (born 7 November 1967) is a French DJ and record producer, one of the pioneers of EDM and repeatedly ranked among the world's top DJs. He has sold tens of millions of records and billions of streams.


Born: 7 November 1967, Paris, France
Occupation: DJ, record producer
Genres: EDM, house, dance-pop

Career

Guetta DJed in Paris clubs from the 1980s and scored European hits in the early 2000s. His 2009 album One Love broke him globally with "When Love Takes Over", "Sexy Bitch", and "Memories". Follow-ups produced hits with Sia ("Titanium"), Nicki Minaj, Usher, and many others, bridging club house and mainstream pop.

Legacy

Guetta helped define the 2010s festival-EDM era and later revived piano-house and future-rave sounds with hits like "I'm Good (Blue)" (2022). He performs residencies in Ibiza and tours worldwide.
