Resistance (Star Wars)

The Resistance is a fictional private military organization in the Star Wars universe. Founded by Leia Organa after the New Republic dismissed the threat posed by the First Order, the Resistance operates as a small, under-resourced force monitoring and fighting the First Order. It is the principal heroic faction of the sequel trilogy films The Force Awakens (2015), The Last Jedi (2017), and The Rise of Skywalker (2019).

Background

After the Galactic Civil War, the New Republic demilitarized and adopted a policy of non-confrontation toward Imperial remnants. Senator Leia Organa, believing the rising First Order to be a serious threat, resigned her position and formed the Resistance with support from sympathetic Republic figures and veterans of the Rebel Alliance. Its starfighters include T-70 X-wings, and its personnel include pilot Poe Dameron and defected stormtrooper Finn.

Appearances

The Resistance is central to the sequel trilogy and the animated series Star Wars Resistance (2018–2020), set on the fueling station Colossus. Following the destruction of the New Republic capital in The Force Awakens, the Resistance becomes the main opposition to the First Order and ultimately rallies the galaxy in The Rise of Skywalker.
