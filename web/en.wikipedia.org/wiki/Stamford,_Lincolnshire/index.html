Stamford is a market town and civil parish on the River Welland in Lincolnshire, England. It is celebrated for its remarkably preserved Georgian architecture, with over 600 listed buildings of limestone construction.

History

Stamford was one of the Five Boroughs of the Danelaw and grew wealthy on the medieval wool trade. In 1333, scholars fleeing Oxford attempted to found a university there, an episode commemorated by Stamford's short-lived university. The town was a major coaching stop on the Great North Road.

Landmarks

Burghley House, a grand Elizabethan prodigy house, stands just outside the town and hosts the Burghley Horse Trials. Stamford has frequently been used as a filming location for period dramas.
