Slip (clothing)

A slip is a woman's undergarment worn beneath a dress or skirt. A full slip hangs from the shoulders by thin straps, extending from the bust to below the waist or knees; a half slip (or waist slip) hangs from an elasticised waistband and covers the lower body.

Purpose

Slips help a dress or skirt hang smoothly, prevent static cling and the transparency of thin fabrics, and protect outer garments from perspiration. They are traditionally made of silk, satin, nylon or other smooth materials, often trimmed with lace.

History

The modern slip developed in the early 20th century as dresses became lighter and closer-fitting. From the 1990s onward, slips also appeared as outerwear in the form of the "slip dress", popularised by designers and celebrities.
