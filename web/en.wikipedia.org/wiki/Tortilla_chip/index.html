Tortilla chip
Place of originMexico / United States
Main ingredientsCorn tortillas, oil, salt

A tortilla chip is a snack food made from corn tortillas cut into wedges and fried or baked until crisp. They are a staple of Mexican and Tex-Mex cuisine, served with salsa, guacamole or cheese sauce.

History

Tortilla chips were popularised in Los Angeles in the 1940s, notably by Rebecca Webb Carranza, who fried misshapen tortillas from an automated line. Mass production helped create nachos — chips topped with melted cheese and jalapeños, created by Ignacio 'Nacho' Anaya in 1943.

Production

Commercial chips are made from nixtamalised corn masa, rolled, cut, baked and fried, then salted or flavoured.
