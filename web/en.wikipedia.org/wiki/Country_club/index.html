Country club

A country club is a privately owned club, often membership-based, that offers recreational sports and social facilities in a suburban or rural setting. Facilities typically centre on a golf course and may include tennis courts, swimming pools, dining rooms and event spaces.

History

Country clubs originated in Scotland with golf and spread to the United States in the late 19th century, where they became fixtures of affluent suburban life. Membership is usually by invitation or application with initiation fees and annual dues; historically many clubs excluded applicants by race, religion or gender, practices that have been challenged and widely abandoned.
