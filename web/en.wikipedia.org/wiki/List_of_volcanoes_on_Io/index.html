List of volcanoes on Io

Io, the innermost Galilean moon of Jupiter, is the most volcanically active body in the Solar System, with more than 400 active volcanoes driven by extreme tidal heating. Volcanism was first discovered there by the Voyager 1 spacecraft in 1979. Volcanic depressions on Io are called paterae; many features are named after fire, sun, thunder and smith gods from world mythologies, and after figures from Dante's Inferno.
