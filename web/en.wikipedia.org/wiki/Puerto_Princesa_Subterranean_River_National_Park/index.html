The Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park is a protected area on the island of Palawan in the Philippines, centred on one of the world's longest navigable underground rivers. The Cabayugan River flows for about 8.2 km through a limestone cave system before emerging into the South China Sea.

Features

The cave contains major chambers, stalactites and stalagmites, and a river subject to tidal influence. The park's forests, from lowland to montane, harbour rich biodiversity including endemic species. It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1999 and was voted one of the New7Wonders of Nature in 2011.
