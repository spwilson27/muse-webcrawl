Equivalent dose

SymbolHT
SI unitSievert (Sv)
Derived fromAbsorbed dose × radiation weighting factor

Equivalent dose is a dose quantity representing the stochastic health effects of low levels of ionizing radiation on the human body, taking into account the type of radiation involved. It is derived from the physical quantity absorbed dose, multiplied by a radiation weighting factor that reflects the biological effectiveness of the radiation type.

Definition

For an organ or tissue T and radiation R, the equivalent dose is HT,R = wR × DT,R, where D is the absorbed dose in grays and wR is the radiation weighting factor (1 for photons and electrons, higher values for protons, neutrons, and alpha particles — 20 for alpha particles). The total equivalent dose sums over all radiation types.

Units

The SI unit is the sievert (Sv); the older unit is the rem (1 Sv = 100 rem). Equivalent dose is used in radiological protection for comparing doses received by different tissues.
