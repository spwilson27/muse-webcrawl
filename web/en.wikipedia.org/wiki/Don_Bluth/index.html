Don Bluth

Born13 September 1937, El Paso, Texas, U.S.
OccupationAnimator, film director, producer

Donald "Don" Bluth (born September 13, 1937) is an American animator and director. A former Walt Disney Productions animator, he left Disney in 1979 with colleagues including Gary Goldman and John Pomeroy to form an independent studio dedicated to classical hand-drawn animation.

Films

His directorial features include The Secret of NIMH (1982), An American Tail (1986), The Land Before Time (1988), All Dogs Go to Heaven (1989), Thumbelina (1994), Anastasia (1997) and Titan AE (2000). In the 1980s his films provided Disney's main competition in animation, and his studio also produced the pioneering laserdisc arcade game Dragon's Lair (1983).

Style

Bluth's work is known for lush, expressive character animation and often darker emotional themes than contemporary family films.
