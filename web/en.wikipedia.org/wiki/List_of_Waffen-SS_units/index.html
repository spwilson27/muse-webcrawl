List of Waffen-SS units

The Waffen-SS was the armed wing of the Nazi Party's SS organisation, operating as a multi-national, multi-ethnic military force of Nazi Germany during World War II. Beginning as a small guard unit in 1933, it grew to nominally field 38 divisions by 1945, though many existed only on paper or were far below strength. At its peak it numbered roughly 900,000 men, including volunteers and conscripts from German-occupied Europe and beyond. The SS — including the Waffen-SS — was declared a criminal organisation at the Nuremberg trials (with an exception for conscripts who had committed no crimes).

Other formations

Beyond the numbered divisions, the Waffen-SS included corps headquarters (I–XVIII SS corps), independent brigades (including the British Free Corps — a tiny unit of British and Commonwealth collaborators), legions (Indian Legion, later transferred to the SS; Arab and Turkic units), the SS heavy tank battalions 101–103 which supported the panzer divisions, and ad hoc Kampfgruppen. Its foreign volunteer system drew men from almost every occupied and neutral European country, motivated by anti-communism, nationalism, coercion or conscription as the war turned against Germany.
