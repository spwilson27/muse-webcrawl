Count Orlok

Count Orlok (German: Graf Orlok) is the main antagonist of the 1922 German Expressionist silent film Nosferatu: A Symphony of Horror, directed by F. W. Murnau. He was portrayed by Max Schreck. Orlok is a vampire modelled on Bram Stoker's Count Dracula, renamed because the production lacked the rights to Dracula.

Character

Unlike the aristocratic, seductive Dracula, Orlok is a grotesque, plague-bearing creature: bald, with sunken eyes, long talons, prominent front teeth like a rat's, and a stiff, spectral gait. Living in a ruined castle in the Carpathians, he brings a cargo of plague rats to the fictional German town of Wisborg. He is destroyed when Ellen, the pure-hearted heroine, lures him into staying until sunrise, whose light annihilates him — an ending invented for the film.

Legacy

Orlok's design became one of the most iconic images in horror cinema. Klaus Kinski echoed the role in Werner Herzog's 1979 remake Nosferatu the Vampyre, Willem Dafoe played Schreck-as-Orlok in Shadow of the Vampire (2000), and Bill Skarsgård portrayed Orlok in Robert Eggers's 2024 remake.
