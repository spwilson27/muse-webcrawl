She-Hulk

She-Hulk (Jennifer Walters) is a superheroine appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist John Buscema, she first appeared in The Savage She-Hulk #1 (February 1980). Walters is a lawyer who, after an emergency blood transfusion from her cousin Bruce Banner, acquired a milder version of his Hulk condition.

Fictional biography

Unlike Banner, Jennifer Walters largely retains her intelligence, personality and emotional control when transformed, and she eventually comes to prefer her confident, green-skinned form. She has served with the Avengers, the Fantastic Four (replacing the Thing during his leave of absence), the Lady Liberators and A-Force, while maintaining her legal career — frequently representing superhuman clients and litigating in the Marvel Universe's courts.

Characterisation

The 1989–1994 series The Sensational She-Hulk, written and drawn by John Byrne, made the character famous for metafictional humour, including regularly breaking the fourth wall to address readers and complain to her creators — years before such devices became common in superhero comics. Later runs, notably by Dan Slott and Charles Soule, emphasised her legal practice.

In other media

Tatiana Maslany portrays Walters in the Marvel Cinematic Universe series She-Hulk: Attorney at Law (2022) on Disney+, a legal comedy that carried over the comics' fourth-wall-breaking style.
