Hugh Howey

Hugh C. Howey (born 23 June 1975) is an American writer, known best for the science fiction series Silo, part of which was published independently through Amazon's Kindle Direct Publishing system. His success is frequently cited as a landmark in self-publishing.

Career

Howey worked as a bookshop clerk, yacht captain, and roofer before writing full-time. In 2011 he published the novella Wool as an e-book; reader demand led to four sequels, collected as the Silo trilogy with Shift and Dust. He famously signed a print-only distribution deal while retaining his e-book rights.

Adaptations

The Silo series was adapted into the Apple TV+ television series Silo, which premiered in 2023. Other works include Sand, Beacon 23 (also adapted for television), and the young-adult Molly Fyde saga.
