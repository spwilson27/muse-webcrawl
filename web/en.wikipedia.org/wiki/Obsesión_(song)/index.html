Obsesión (song)

Artist: Aventura
Album: We Broke the Rules
Released: 2002
Genre: Bachata

"Obsesión" is a song by Dominican-American bachata group Aventura. Written by lead singer Romeo Santos, it appeared on the group's 2002 album We Broke the Rules and became a major international hit, helping to popularise modern bachata far beyond the Dominican Republic and the Latino diaspora.

Composition and reception

The song is a dialogue between a man and a woman (voiced by singer Judy Santos, not an official group member) in which the man insists his infatuation is love, while the woman repeatedly corrects him: "No es amor, es una obsesión" — "It is not love, it is an obsession." Sung mostly in Spanish with some English, it topped charts across Europe and Latin America in 2004–2005, reaching number one in countries including Italy, France, Germany, and Spain, and became one of the best-selling bachata songs of all time.

Legacy

"Obsesión" is credited with breaking bachata into mainstream European markets and remains Aventura's signature song. It has been covered and remixed many times, and an English-language version was also recorded by the group.
