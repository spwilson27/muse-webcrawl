Windsor Park is a football stadium in Belfast, Northern Ireland, and the home ground of the Northern Ireland national football team and Linfield Football Club. Following a major redevelopment completed in 2016, it is officially known for sponsorship purposes as the National Football Stadium at Windsor Park.

History

The ground opened in 1905 and took its name from the Windsor district of south Belfast. It has hosted Northern Ireland internationals for over a century, including matches from the team's successful 1980s period and its Euro 2016 qualifying campaign. A government-funded redevelopment between 2014 and 2016 replaced three stands, raising capacity to about 18,500 and adding the Irish FA's headquarters and a national football education centre.

Other events

Windsor Park hosted the 2021 UEFA Super Cup between Chelsea and Villarreal. Linfield have played at the ground since its opening and hold a long-term lease arrangement with the Irish FA.
