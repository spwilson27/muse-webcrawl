India Meteorological Department

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) is the principal agency of the Government of India responsible for meteorological observations, weather forecasting and seismology. Founded in 1875, it is headquartered at Mausam Bhavan in New Delhi and operates under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.

Functions

The IMD issues weather forecasts and warnings for the general public, agriculture, aviation, shipping and defence, and is one of the World Meteorological Organization's Regional Specialized Meteorological Centres for tropical cyclones in the North Indian Ocean — it names and tracks cyclones in the Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea. It also runs a network of observatories, weather radars and the INSAT satellite programme's meteorological payloads, and monitors earthquakes across India.

History

After a disastrous tropical cyclone struck Calcutta in 1864 and famines followed monsoon failures, the British government established the IMD in 1875, with Henry Francis Blanford as its first Imperial Meteorological Reporter.
