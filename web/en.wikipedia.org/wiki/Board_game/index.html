Board game

A board game is a type of tabletop game that involves counters or pieces moved or placed on a pre-marked surface or board, according to a set of rules. Board games vary greatly in type and themes, from ancient abstract games like Go and senet to modern Euro-style strategy games such as Catan and hobby staples like chess and Monopoly.

History

Board games have been played in most cultures throughout history. Burial sites from predynastic Egypt yielded the game senet, one of the earliest known board games. The Royal Game of Ur dates to roughly 2600 BCE in Mesopotamia. Chess evolved from the Indian game chaturanga in the 6th century CE, while backgammon descends from Roman tabula. The 20th century saw mass-produced commercial games, and the late 1990s onward brought a renaissance of designer ("Eurogame") titles emphasizing strategy over luck.

Categories

Common categories include race games, abstract strategy games, roll-and-move games, wargames, and modern Eurogames. Games are also classified by their components and mechanisms, such as deck-building, worker placement, and cooperative play.
