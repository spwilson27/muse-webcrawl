KeyArena

Location: Seattle Center, Seattle, Washington
Opened: 1962 (as Washington State Coliseum)
Former names: Washington State Pavilion, Seattle Center Coliseum

KeyArena was a multi-purpose indoor arena in Seattle, Washington, located at Seattle Center. Originally built as the Washington State Pavilion for the 1962 Century 21 Exposition (the Seattle World's Fair), it was best known as the home of the Seattle SuperSonics of the NBA from 1967 to 1978 and again from 1985 to 2008, and of the Seattle Storm of the WNBA.

History

After the World's Fair, the building was converted into the Seattle Center Coliseum. KeyCorp acquired naming rights in 1995, renaming it KeyArena. Despite renovations, its small capacity by NBA standards and lack of modern revenue amenities contributed to the SuperSonics' controversial relocation to Oklahoma City in 2008.

Reconstruction

Between 2018 and 2021 the arena was almost entirely rebuilt beneath its preserved landmark roof, reopening as Climate Pledge Arena, home of the NHL's Seattle Kraken and the Storm.
