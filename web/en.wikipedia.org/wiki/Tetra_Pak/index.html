Tetra Pak

Founded: 1951, Lund, Sweden
Founder: Ruben Rausing
Industry: Food packaging and processing
Parent: Tetra Laval

Tetra Pak is a Swedish-Swiss multinational food packaging and processing company, part of the Tetra Laval group. It is best known for its aseptic carton packages for liquid foods such as milk and juice, which allow perishable products to be stored safely without refrigeration for months.

History

The company grew out of Åkerlund & Rausing, a packaging company founded in Lund, Sweden, in 1929 by Ruben Rausing and Erik Åkerlund. In 1944, work began on a paper carton for milk; the engineer Erik Wallenberg devised the tetrahedron-shaped package, and Tetra Pak was founded in 1951 as a subsidiary. The first Tetra Pak machine was delivered in 1952. In 1963 the company introduced Tetra Brik, the rectangular brick-shaped carton that became its most successful product.

Products

Tetra Pak provides both packaging materials and processing and filling equipment. Its package families include Tetra Classic, Tetra Brik Aseptic, Tetra Top, Tetra Prisma Aseptic, and Tetra Rex. The cartons are made mainly of paperboard laminated with polyethylene and, for aseptic long-life products, a thin layer of aluminium foil.

Environmental record

The company promotes the renewability of its paperboard and supports recycling schemes, though the composite nature of the cartons has drawn criticism because they require specialized facilities to recycle. Tetra Pak has set targets to increase the renewable content of its packages and reduce the climate impact of its value chain.
