Onslaught is a fictional supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writers Scott Lobdell and Mark Waid and artist Andy Kubert, the character first appeared in cameo in X-Men #53 (June 1996) and was fully revealed in X-Men #54. Onslaught is a sentient psionic entity formed from the consciousness of Professor Charles Xavier after Xavier's mind was tainted by the dark aspects of Magneto's psyche.

Onslaught was the central antagonist of a major 1996 crossover storyline that ran through Marvel's X-Men titles and much of the wider Marvel Universe, culminating in the "Heroes Reborn" relaunch of several flagship series.

Fictional character biography

During the events of the "Fatal Attractions" storyline, an enraged Charles Xavier used his telepathic powers to shut down Magneto's mind, rendering the Master of Magnetism catatonic. Unbeknownst to Xavier, a dark fragment of Magneto's persona — his anger, resentment, and ruthless willingness to act — crossed into Xavier's subconscious during the psychic contact, where it festered and merged with Xavier's own repressed frustrations and dark impulses.

This psychic infection gradually coalesced into an independent entity calling itself Onslaught. Initially manifesting as subtle influences on Xavier's behaviour, Onslaught eventually revealed itself by trapping the X-Men's minds, defeating the Juggernaut, and launching a campaign against humanity and mutantkind alike. Onslaught sought the powerful young mutant Franklin Richards and the psionic mutant Nate Grey (X-Man) to amplify its powers.

In the storyline's climax, Onslaught shed its physical form and became a being of pure psionic energy threatening to engulf New York City. The non-mutant heroes of the Marvel Universe — including the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, the Hulk, and Doctor Doom — sacrificed themselves by entering Onslaught's energy form to disrupt it from within, apparently dying in the process. In fact, Franklin Richards subconsciously created a "pocket universe" in which the sacrificed heroes survived, setting up the "Heroes Reborn" era; they returned to the mainstream Marvel Universe a year later in "Heroes Return".

Powers and abilities

Onslaught possesses the combined psionic abilities of Professor X, Magneto, Franklin Richards, and Nate Grey at various points in its development, making it one of the most powerful entities in the Marvel Universe. Its abilities include telepathy on a planetary scale, telekinesis, magnetic field manipulation, energy projection, matter manipulation, superhuman strength and durability in its armoured physical form, and limited reality-warping potential when augmented by Franklin Richards' powers.

In other media

Onslaught appears as a boss character in the fighting game Marvel vs. Capcom: Clash of Super Heroes (1998), depicted as a two-stage boss whose second form is a giant floating torso and head. The character has also appeared in animated series and various video games, and was revisited in later comics storylines including "Onslaught Unleashed" (2011) and the Red Onslaught arc of AXIS (2014), in which the Red Skull, having grafted Xavier's brain tissue to his own, was transformed by the Scarlet Witch's inverted spell.
