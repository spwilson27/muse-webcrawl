Complete game

In baseball, a complete game (CG) denotes the act of a pitcher pitching an entire game without the benefit of a relief pitcher. A pitcher who meets the criteria for a win and pitches the entire game is credited with a complete game regardless of the amount of innings played.

History and trends

Complete games were once the norm: in the early decades of professional baseball, starters routinely finished what they started. Cy Young holds the career record with 749 complete games. As bullpens became specialised through the 20th century, complete games grew rare; in modern Major League Baseball a pitcher may record only a handful in a season, and league totals have fallen to a small fraction of games played.

Complete game shutout

A complete game in which the pitcher allows no runs is a complete game shutout, tracked as a separate statistic.
