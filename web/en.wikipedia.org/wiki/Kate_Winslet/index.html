Kate Winslet

Kate Elizabeth Winslet (born 5 October 1975) is an English actress known for her portrayals of headstrong, complex women, largely in independent films and period dramas. She is the recipient of numerous accolades, including an Academy Award, two Primetime Emmy Awards, five BAFTA Awards and five Golden Globe Awards.

Career

Born in Reading, Berkshire, Winslet made her film debut in Peter Jackson's Heavenly Creatures (1994) and gained acclaim in Sense and Sensibility (1995). Global stardom followed with James Cameron's Titanic (1997), then the highest-grossing film of all time. She went on to acclaimed roles in Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004), Little Children (2006), Revolutionary Road (2008) and Steve Jobs (2015).

Awards and later work

Winslet won the Academy Award for Best Actress for The Reader (2008). On television she won Emmys for the miniseries Mildred Pierce (2011) and the crime drama Mare of Easttown (2021). In 2024 she produced and starred in Lee, a biopic of photographer Lee Miller. She was appointed CBE in 2012.
