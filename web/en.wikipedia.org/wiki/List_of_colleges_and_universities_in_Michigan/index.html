List of colleges and universities in Michigan

This is a list of colleges and universities in the U.S. state of Michigan. The state has fifteen public universities, numerous private colleges and universities, and a large system of community colleges. The University of Michigan (1817) is among the oldest universities in the United States.

Private institutions

Private colleges and universities include the University of Detroit Mercy, Calvin University, Hope College, Kalamazoo College, Albion College, Hillsdale College, Lawrence Technological University, and the College for Creative Studies.

Community colleges

Michigan's 28 community colleges, such as Grand Rapids Community College, Lansing Community College, Macomb Community College, and Oakland Community College, serve hundreds of thousands of students.
