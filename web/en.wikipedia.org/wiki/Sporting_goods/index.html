Sporting goods

Sporting goods, also called sports equipment, are the tools, materials, apparel and gear used to participate in a sport. Equipment ranges from balls, bats, rackets, nets and goals to protective items such as helmets, pads and gloves, and specialist clothing and footwear.

Categories

Sporting goods are commonly grouped into categories including team sports equipment, outdoor and adventure gear, fitness and exercise equipment, racquet sports, water sports, winter sports, and athletic apparel and footwear. Some items are consumable, such as shuttlecocks and tennis balls, while others are durable goods maintained over many seasons.

Industry

The sporting goods industry is a major global market dominated by large multinational brands in footwear and apparel, alongside specialist manufacturers of equipment for individual sports. Products are sold through dedicated sporting-goods retailers, department stores, brand outlets and online commerce. Governing bodies regulate the specifications of equipment used in competition, from the size and weight of balls to materials permitted in bats and rackets.

Safety

Protective equipment is a significant segment of the industry, and standards bodies set performance requirements for items such as bicycle helmets, mouthguards and body armour used in contact sports.
