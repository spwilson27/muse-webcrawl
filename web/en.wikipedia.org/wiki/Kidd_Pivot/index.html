Kidd Pivot

Kidd Pivot is a Canadian contemporary dance company based in Vancouver, British Columbia, founded in 2001 by choreographer Crystal Pite. The company is known for integrating movement, original music, text and rich visual design, exploring themes of trauma, addiction, conflict and mortality.

Works

Notable productions include Double Story (2004), Lost Action (2006), Dark Matters (2009), The Tempest Replica (2011), Betroffenheit (2015, created with Jonathon Young and the Electric Company Theatre) and Revisor (2019). Betroffenheit won the Olivier Award for Best New Dance Production.
