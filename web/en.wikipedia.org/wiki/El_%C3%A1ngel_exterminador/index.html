El ángel exterminador

El ángel exterminador (The Exterminating Angel) is a 1962 Mexican surrealist film directed by Luis Buñuel. It follows a group of wealthy guests at a dinner party who find themselves inexplicably unable to leave the room, and is regarded as one of Buñuel's finest works and one of the greatest films of all time.

Plot

After an opera performance, Edmundo and Lucía Nóbile host a dinner for upper-class friends. As the evening ends, the guests discover they cannot cross the threshold of the drawing room, though no barrier exists. Days pass; civility crumbles, a man dies, and hysteria mounts until a pattern of positions in the room is repeated, breaking the spell.

Themes

The film is a surreal critique of bourgeois complacency and ritual, characteristic of Buñuel's Mexican period. It was adapted into an opera by Thomas Adès, premiered in 2016.
