Aimpoint AB is a Swedish company that manufactures red dot sights for military, law-enforcement, and civilian firearms. Founded in 1974 in Malmö, the company introduced one of the first commercially successful electronic reflex sights, the Aimpoint Electronic, in 1975, based on a light-emitting diode reflected in a coated lens to produce an illuminated aiming dot.

Technology

Aimpoint sights project an LED-generated red dot onto a partially reflective lens; the shooter sees the dot superimposed on the target with virtually no parallax at practical ranges. The sights are known for extremely long battery life — many models run continuously for years on a single battery — and rugged, waterproof construction. Because both eyes can remain open, they offer rapid target acquisition at close and medium ranges.

Military use

Aimpoint sights have been adopted by many armed forces. The CompM2 is issued by the US Army as the M68 Close Combat Optic, and later models such as the CompM4 and Micro series are widely used by NATO forces, special operations units, and police agencies worldwide. By the 2020s the company reported more than two million sights in military service.

Products

Product lines include the Comp series of full-size tube sights, the Micro T-1/T-2 and H-1/H-2 miniature sights, the Patrol Rifle Optic for law enforcement, and hunting models such as the Hunter series.
