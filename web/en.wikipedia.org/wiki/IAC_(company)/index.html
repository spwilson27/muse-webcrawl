IAC (company)

IAC Inc.
Founded: August 1995 (as HSN Inc.)
Headquarters: New York City, U.S.
Chairman: Barry Diller
Industry: Media, Internet

IAC Inc. is an American holding company that owns brands across 100 countries, most notably in media and Internet services. Headquartered in the Frank Gehry-designed IAC Building in New York City, the company is led by chairman Barry Diller. IAC is known for a strategy of acquiring and incubating internet businesses and later spinning them off to shareholders as independent public companies.

History

The company traces its origins to 1986 and the Silver King Broadcasting group, later connected with the Home Shopping Network. Under Diller it grew through a long series of acquisitions, passing through names including USA Networks and USA Interactive before adopting InterActiveCorp, shortened to IAC in 2004. Through the 2000s IAC assembled properties such as Ticketmaster, Expedia, Hotels.com, LendingTree, Match.com, Ask.com, and Citysearch.

Spinoffs and current businesses

Beginning with Expedia in 2005, IAC repeatedly spun off mature businesses, distributing Ticketmaster, Interval International, HSN, and LendingTree in 2008, Match Group (owner of Tinder and Match.com) in a full separation completed in 2020, and video platform Vimeo in 2021. Remaining IAC businesses have included Dotdash Meredith, the publisher formed from IAC's Dotdash and its 2021 acquisition of Meredith Corporation's magazines; home-services marketplace Angi; Care.com; and the Ask Media Group search properties. The company has described itself as an acquirer and builder of category-leading internet businesses, sometimes characterised as an "anti-conglomerate" because of its willingness to dismantle itself through spinoffs.
