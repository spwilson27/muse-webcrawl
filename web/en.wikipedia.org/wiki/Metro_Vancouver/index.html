Metro Vancouver

Metro Vancouver is a federation of 21 municipalities, one electoral area, and one treaty First Nation in British Columbia, Canada, that administers regional services such as water, sewerage, and regional planning. The largest city is Vancouver, and the administrative centre is Burnaby.

Geography

The region, formerly known as the Greater Vancouver Regional District, occupies the Lower Mainland around the Fraser River delta and Burrard Inlet, and had a population of about 2.6 million in the 2021 census, making it the third-largest metropolitan area in Canada.

Governance

Metro Vancouver's board is composed of mayors and councillors appointed by member municipalities. The regional district adopted the name Metro Vancouver in 2017.
