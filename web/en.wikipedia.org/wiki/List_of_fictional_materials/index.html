List of fictional materials

Fictional materials are substances invented for works of fiction that do not exist in the real world, or that dramatise real substances beyond their actual properties. They are a staple of fantasy, science fiction, superhero comics and role-playing games, where they supply armour, weapons, starship hulls and magical artefacts. Many have become so familiar that their names are used allusively in engineering and popular culture — "unobtainium" is real aerospace slang for an ideal but unavailable material.
