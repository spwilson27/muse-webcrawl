Isabelle Rezazadeh (born 28 March 1995), known by her stage name Rezz (stylised in all caps), is a Canadian DJ and record producer. Born in Ukraine to a Ukrainian mother and Iranian father, she grew up in Niagara Falls, Ontario. She began producing in 2013 and drew attention uploading her dark, midtempo productions online, signing with deadmau5's label mau5trap.

Career

Her early EPs, including The Silence Is Deafening and Something Wrong Here (2016), established her hypnotic, bass-heavy style. Her debut studio album Mass Manipulation (2017) won the Juno Award for Electronic Album of the Year. She followed it with Certain Kind of Magic (2018), Beyond the Senses (2019), Spiral (2021) and Can You See Me? (2024). In 2022 she launched her own label, HypnoVizion Records.

Rezz is known for her signature LED "hypno" goggles and for a devoted fanbase sometimes called the "Cult of Rezz". She has headlined major festivals and sold out multiple shows at Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Colorado. Her sound — slow, pulsing bass music around 90–100 BPM — helped popularise the midtempo bass genre.
