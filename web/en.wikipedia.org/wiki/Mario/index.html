Mario (Japanese: マリオ) is a character from the Mario franchise of video games, created by Japanese game designer Shigeru Miyamoto. Serving as the mascot of Nintendo, Mario is one of the most famous and widely recognised video game characters in the world.

Characteristics

Mario is a short, Italian-American plumber who typically resides in the Mushroom Kingdom. He wears a red cap with an "M", blue overalls, a red shirt, white gloves and brown shoes, and has a large moustache — features originally shaped by the graphical limits of arcade hardware (the cap avoided animating hair; the moustache avoided drawing a mouth). In his platform games he usually sets out to rescue Princess Peach from the villain Bowser, aided by his younger brother Luigi.

History

Mario debuted in Donkey Kong (1981) as the carpenter "Jumpman". He was renamed Mario — reportedly after Mario Segale, the landlord of Nintendo of America's warehouse — and appeared in Donkey Kong Jr. (1982) and Mario Bros. (1983), which fixed his profession as a plumber and introduced Luigi. Super Mario Bros. (1985) for the Nintendo Entertainment System established the side-scrolling platform formula and became one of the best-selling games ever. The series has since spanned 2D and 3D platformers (Super Mario 64, Galaxy, Odyssey), and spin-offs including Mario Kart, Mario Party, sports, role-playing and fighting crossovers (Super Smash Bros.).

Cultural impact

The Mario franchise is the best-selling video game franchise of all time, with more than 800 million units sold across all series. Mario appears in hundreds of games and in films — including the animated The Super Mario Bros. Movie (2023), one of the highest-grossing animated films ever — as well as at the opening ceremony of the 2016 Rio Olympics. Voice actor Charles Martinet voiced Mario from the early 1990s until 2023, when he stepped into a "Mario Ambassador" role.
