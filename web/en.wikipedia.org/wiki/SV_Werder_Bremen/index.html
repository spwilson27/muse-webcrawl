Sportverein Werder Bremen von 1899 e. V., commonly known as Werder Bremen, is a German professional sports club based in the Free Hanseatic City of Bremen. Founded on 4 February 1899, Werder are best known for their professional association football team, who compete in the Bundesliga, the top tier of the German football league system.

Werder have been German champions four times (1965, 1988, 1993 and 2004), have won the DFB-Pokal six times, and won the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1992. The club's greatest era came under long-serving manager Otto Rehhagel between 1981 and 1995, and again under Thomas Schaaf, whose side completed the league and cup double in 2004. Werder share a fierce rivalry, the Nordderby, with Hamburger SV, and play in green and white at the Weserstadion on the banks of the Weser.

History

The club was founded by a group of schoolboys who had won a football in a tug-of-war contest. A founding member of the Bundesliga in 1963, Werder won their first national championship in 1965. After relegation in 1980, Rehhagel's side returned and became Bayern Munich's most consistent challengers of the late 1980s. The club endured a decline in the 2010s and were relegated for only the second time in 2021, returning to the Bundesliga at the first attempt in 2022.
