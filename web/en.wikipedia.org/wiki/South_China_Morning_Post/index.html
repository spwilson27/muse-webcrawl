The South China Morning Post (SCMP) is an English-language newspaper based in Hong Kong, founded in 1903, the city's paper of record for English readers. Owned by the Kerry Group for two decades, it was bought by Alibaba Group in 2016, and its China coverage for a global digital audience became its growth engine.

Position

SCMP occupies a distinctive place: inside China's media environment yet written in English to international standards, which has made its Beijing and Washington coverage closely watched — and its editorial independence periodically debated since the Alibaba acquisition.
