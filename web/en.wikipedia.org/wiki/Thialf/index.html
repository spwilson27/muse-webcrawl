Thialf

Thialf is an ice arena in Heerenveen, Friesland, Netherlands, widely regarded as one of the fastest and most famous speed skating venues in the world. The complex houses a 400-metre indoor speed skating oval and ice hockey facilities.

The original outdoor Thialf rink opened in 1967; the roofed stadium was inaugurated in 1986, making Thialf one of the first covered speed skating ovals. Because of its slightly elevated location and high-quality ice, numerous world records have been set there, and it regularly hosts ISU Speed Skating World Cup events and World Championships.

History and renovation

A major renovation completed in the 2010s modernised the venue while preserving its reputation as the "skating temple" of the Netherlands. The venue is home to the Dutch speed skating community and has hosted the World Allround and World Sprint Championships many times.

Name

The arena is named after Thialfi, a figure from Norse mythology known for his speed as a runner and servant of the god Thor.
