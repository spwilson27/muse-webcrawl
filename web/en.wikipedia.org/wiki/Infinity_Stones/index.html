Infinity Stones

The Infinity Stones are fictional objects in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) media franchise, based on the Infinity Gems of Marvel Comics. They are six immensely powerful stones, each controlling an essential aspect of existence: Space, Mind, Reality, Power, Time and Soul. Together, wielded in the Infinity Gauntlet, they grant their bearer near-omnipotence.

In the franchise

The Stones were introduced individually across the MCU's early films, concealed in objects such as the Tesseract (Space), the Scepter and later Vision (Mind), the Aether (Reality), the Orb (Power), the Eye of Agamotto (Time) and the Soul Stone hidden on Vormir. The overarching plot of the Infinity Saga culminates in Avengers: Infinity War (2018), in which Thanos gathers all six and erases half of all life, and Avengers: Endgame (2019), in which the Avengers use time travel to assemble the Stones again and reverse his act.

Comics origin

In the comics, the Infinity Gems were largely popularised by writer Jim Starlin's Thanos storylines of the 1970s and 1990s, including The Infinity Gauntlet.
