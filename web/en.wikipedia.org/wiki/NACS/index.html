NACS

NACS most commonly refers to the North American Charging Standard, the electric vehicle charging connector system developed by Tesla, Inc. and now standardised as SAE J3400. It may also refer to other topics listed below.

North American Charging Standard

Tesla opened its proprietary charging connector design to other manufacturers in November 2022, renaming it the North American Charging Standard. The connector handles both AC and DC fast charging through a single compact plug, with a rated capacity of up to 1 MW in later specifications. In 2023, Ford and General Motors announced they would adopt NACS on their EVs, followed by nearly every other major automaker selling in North America. In December 2023 the standard was published by SAE International as J3400, making it an official industry standard. Access to Tesla's Supercharger network was a major driver of adoption.

Other uses

  • National Association of Convenience Stores — a US trade association founded in 1961, which also runs a major annual trade show.
  • National Association for Campus Activities — supporting student programming in American higher education.
