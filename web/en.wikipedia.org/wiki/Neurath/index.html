Neurath

Neurath is a surname of German origin. Notable people with the name include:

  • Otto Neurath (1882–1945), Austrian philosopher of science, sociologist and political economist; a leading member of the Vienna Circle, originator of the ISOTYPE pictorial statistics method, and author of the image of "Neurath's boat" — the idea that knowledge must be rebuilt at sea, plank by plank, like sailors repairing their ship.
  • Marie Neurath (1898–1986), German designer and educator who, with Otto Neurath and Gerd Arntz, developed ISOTYPE visual education in Vienna and later in Britain.
  • Konstantin von Neurath (1873–1956), German diplomat, foreign minister under Hitler (1932–1938) and Reich Protector of Bohemia and Moravia, convicted at Nuremberg.
  • Hans Neurath (1909–2002), Austrian-American biochemist known for work on protein structure.
  • Paul Neurath (1911–2001), Austrian-American sociologist.
