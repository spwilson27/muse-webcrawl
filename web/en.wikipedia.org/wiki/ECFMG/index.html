ECFMG

The Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates (ECFMG) is a United States nonprofit organization that assesses the readiness of international medical graduates (IMGs) to enter residency or fellowship programs in the United States that are accredited by the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME). It was founded in 1956 and is based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Certification

ECFMG Certification is required for IMGs who wish to pursue graduate medical education in the United States and, in many jurisdictions, to obtain an unrestricted license to practice medicine. To be certified, an applicant must be a graduate of a medical school listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools, must satisfy the medical science examination requirements — historically the United States Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE) Step 1 and Step 2 Clinical Knowledge — and must demonstrate proficiency in English and clinical and communication skills.

Changes to requirements

Following the discontinuation of the USMLE Step 2 Clinical Skills examination in 2021, ECFMG introduced alternative "pathways" for assessing clinical and communication skills, including the use of the Occupational English Test (OET) Medicine. Since 2024, ECFMG's Sponsoring Institution and certification framework has operated under the umbrella of Intealth, an integrated organization formed with the Foundation for Advancement of International Medical Education and Research (FAIMER).

Functions

Beyond certification, ECFMG sponsors foreign national physicians for J-1 exchange visitor visas for graduate medical education, operates the Electronic Residency Application Service (ERAS) support services for IMGs, and provides primary-source verification of medical credentials through its EPIC (Electronic Portfolio of International Credentials) service.
