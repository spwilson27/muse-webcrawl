Latin pop

Latin pop is a popular music genre that blends Latin American and Iberian musical traditions — such as salsa, merengue, cumbia, bachata, and flamenco — with mainstream pop song structures and production. It is performed primarily in Spanish and Portuguese, though many artists have crossed over into English-language markets.

History

Latin pop developed through the twentieth century as Latin dance music merged with international pop and rock. Artists such as Julio Iglesias and Gloria Estefan brought Spanish-language pop to global audiences in the 1970s and 1980s. The late-1990s "Latin explosion" saw performers including Ricky Martin, Shakira, Enrique Iglesias, and Jennifer Lopez achieve worldwide success, often recording in both Spanish and English.

Characteristics

The genre typically features catchy melodic hooks, danceable rhythms drawn from Latin percussion traditions, and romantic lyrical themes. In the 2010s, Latin pop increasingly fused with reggaeton and urbano styles, producing global hits and making Spanish-language music a dominant force on streaming platforms.
