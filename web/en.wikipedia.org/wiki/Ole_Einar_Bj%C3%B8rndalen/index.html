Ole Einar Bjørndalen (born 27 January 1974) is a retired Norwegian professional biathlete, widely regarded as the greatest biathlete of all time and nicknamed the "King of Biathlon." He is the most successful Winter Olympian in terms of total medals won by a male athlete, with 13 Olympic medals, including eight golds.

Career

Bjørndalen won a record 94 Biathlon World Cup races and 20 World Championship gold medals. At the 2002 Salt Lake City Winter Olympics he achieved the unprecedented feat of winning all four men's biathlon events. He also competed internationally in cross-country skiing, winning a World Cup race in 2006.

Later life

After retiring in 2018, Bjørndalen served as a member of the International Olympic Committee's Athletes' Commission and coached the Chinese national biathlon team with his wife, Belarusian biathlete Darya Domracheva.
