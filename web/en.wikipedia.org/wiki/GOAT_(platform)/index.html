GOAT (platform)

GOAT is an American online platform and marketplace for sneakers, streetwear, luxury fashion, and collectibles. Founded in 2015 in Los Angeles, California, by Eddy Lu and Daishin Sugano, GOAT operates a resale model in which items are authenticated before being shipped to buyers.

The GOAT app allows users to buy and sell authenticated sneakers and apparel.

History

GOAT was founded after co-founder Eddy Lu bought a pair of counterfeit sneakers online, inspiring a platform built around authentication. The name derives from the sports acronym "greatest of all time". The company grew out of the sneaker-resale boom of the 2010s, expanding from sneakers into apparel, accessories, and luxury goods.

In 2019, GOAT merged with the consignment retailer Flight Club, a well-known sneaker store founded in New York in 2005. The company raised several large funding rounds, reaching a valuation of about $3.7 billion in 2021, and expanded internationally, including operations in Asia and Europe.

Business model

Sellers list new and used items; when an item sells, it is shipped to GOAT for authentication and condition verification before delivery to the buyer. The company also sells new retail products directly through brand partnerships. GOAT Group operates the GOAT platform, Flight Club, and the curated retail concept store alias.
