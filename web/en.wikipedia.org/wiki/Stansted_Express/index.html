Stansted Express

The Stansted Express is a direct train service linking London Stansted Airport to London Liverpool Street station in the City of London. Operated by Greater Anglia, the service runs every 15 minutes at peak times, with a typical journey time of around 48-50 minutes, stopping at Tottenham Hale with some services also calling at intermediate stations.

History

The service began with the opening of Stansted Airport station in 1991. It was initially operated under British Rail's Network SouthEast sector, passing through various franchises after privatisation, including West Anglia Great Northern, National Express East Anglia, and Abellio Greater Anglia.

Rolling stock

The service was long operated by Class 379 Electrostar units, introduced in 2011. From 2020, Greater Anglia began replacing its fleet with Stadler FLIRT Class 745 electric multiple units, which took over Stansted Express duties with longer, twelve-car formations offering increased capacity and improved facilities.
