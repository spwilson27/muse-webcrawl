List of Attack on Titan characters

The Attack on Titan manga and anime series feature an extensive cast of characters created by Hajime Isayama. The story is set in a world where humanity lives within cities surrounded by enormous walls that protect them from gigantic man-eating humanoids called Titans.

Main characters

Eren Yeager

The main protagonist. After witnessing his mother being devoured by a Titan during the fall of Wall Maria, Eren enlists in the military and discovers he possesses the ability to transform into a Titan himself, later inheriting the Attack Titan and the Founding Titan.

Mikasa Ackerman

Eren's adoptive sister and one of the most skilled soldiers of the 104th Training Corps. A descendant of the Ackerman clan, she is fiercely protective of Eren.

Armin Arlert

Eren's childhood friend, physically weak but a brilliant strategist. He later inherits the Colossus Titan from Bertholdt Hoover.

Survey Corps

Levi Ackerman, humanity's strongest soldier and captain of the Special Operations Squad; Erwin Smith, the commander who leads the regiment until his death in the battle to retake Wall Maria; Hange Zoë, the squad leader obsessed with Titan research who succeeds Erwin as commander; Jean Kirstein, Connie Springer and Sasha Blouse, fellow graduates of the 104th Training Corps.

Warriors of Marley

Reiner Braun (Armored Titan), Bertholdt Hoover (Colossus Titan), Annie Leonhart (Female Titan), Zeke Yeager (Beast Titan), Pieck Finger (Cart Titan) and Porco Galliard (Jaw Titan) are Eldian child soldiers sent by the nation of Marley to infiltrate the Walls and seize the Founding Titan.

Other characters

Historia Reiss, the true heir to the throne who becomes queen of the Walls; Ymir, a former Titan shifter; Gabi Braun and Falco Grice, Warrior candidates in Marley; and Grisha Yeager, Eren's father, whose basement holds the truth about the world outside the Walls.
