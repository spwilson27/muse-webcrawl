Muscat (grape)

Muscat (grape)
TypeWine and table grape
OriginAncient; Mediterranean
Notable varietiesMuscat blanc, Muscat of Alexandria

Muscat is a family of grapes that includes over 200 varieties grown around the world. Muscats are among the oldest known grape varieties, possibly the first to be domesticated, and are characterised by their pronounced floral, grapey aroma.

Varieties

The most important members are Muscat blanc à Petits Grains, widely regarded as the finest, and Muscat of Alexandria, used for both wine and table grapes. Other members include Muscat Ottonel, Moscato Giallo, and Black Muscat (Muscat Hamburg).

Wines

Muscat wines range from dry Alsatian styles to sweet fortified wines such as Spain's Moscatel and Australia's liqueur Muscats, and to lightly sparkling Italian Moscato d'Asti. The grapes are also eaten fresh and dried as raisins.
