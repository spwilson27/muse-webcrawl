Staatsburg, New York

Staatsburg is a hamlet and census-designated place (CDP) in the town of Hyde Park in Dutchess County, New York, United States. The population was 377 at the 2010 census. It is part of the Poughkeepsie–Newburgh–Middletown metropolitan area as well as the larger New York–Newark–Bridgeport combined statistical area.

History

The area was originally settled by Henry Beekman, who obtained a patent for the land in the 1690s. The community takes its name from Dr. Samuel Staats, an early settler, combined with the German word burg (town).

During the Gilded Age, Staatsburg became home to a number of wealthy families who built country estates along the Hudson River. The most famous of these is Mills Mansion, a Beaux-Arts country house designed by McKim, Mead & White for financier Ogden Mills and his wife Ruth Livingston Mills. The 65-room mansion and its grounds are now preserved as the Staatsburgh State Historic Site.
