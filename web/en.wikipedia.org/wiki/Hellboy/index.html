Hellboy is a fictional superhero created by writer-artist Mike Mignola. The character first appeared in San Diego Comic-Con Comics #2 (August 1993) and has since appeared in various miniseries, one-shots and crossovers published by Dark Horse Comics.

Fictional biography

A well-meaning half-demon summoned from Hell to Earth as an infant by Nazi occultists in 1944, Hellboy was discovered by Allied forces and raised by Professor Trevor Bruttenholm of the Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense (B.P.R.D.). As an adult he works for the Bureau, investigating supernatural threats with fellow agents including Abe Sapien and Liz Sherman. He wields the massive stone "Right Hand of Doom", and is destined — a fate he rejects — to herald the apocalypse as Anung Un Rama.

Adaptations

The character was portrayed by Ron Perlman in Guillermo del Toro's films Hellboy (2004) and Hellboy II: The Golden Army (2008), by David Harbour in the 2019 reboot, and in animated features.
