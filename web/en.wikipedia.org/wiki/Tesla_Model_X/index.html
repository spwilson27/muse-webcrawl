The Tesla Model X is a battery electric luxury crossover SUV developed and produced by Tesla, Inc. First unveiled as a prototype in 2012, deliveries began in September 2015. It is notable for its rear "falcon wing" doors, which hinge upward with sensors to adjust their arc in confined spaces, and for offering seating for up to seven passengers.

Design and features

The Model X shares its platform with the Model S sedan and features a panoramic windshield, a large central touchscreen, and Tesla's Autopilot driver-assistance hardware. High-performance Plaid variants use a tri-motor powertrain; the vehicle was marketed as one of the quickest SUVs in production, with the Plaid accelerating from 0 to 60 mph in about 2.5 seconds.

Safety

The Model X received five-star overall ratings from the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, and Tesla has promoted its low center of gravity — created by the underfloor battery pack — as reducing rollover risk. Early production was complicated by the complexity of the falcon wing doors and seating, which delayed volume deliveries in 2016.
