Chang'e 4 is a Chinese lunar exploration mission operated by the China National Space Administration. On 3 January 2019 its lander and the Yutu-2 rover touched down in Von Kármán crater in the South Pole–Aitken Basin, achieving the first soft landing on the far side of the Moon.

Mission

Because the far side always faces away from Earth, communications were relayed through the Queqiao satellite positioned at the Earth–Moon L2 point. The mission carried international payloads from Germany, Sweden, the Netherlands and Saudi Arabia, and a small biosphere experiment in which a cotton seed sprouted aboard the lander.

Legacy

Yutu-2 became the longest-lived lunar rover, operating for years beyond its three-month design life, and studied the composition of the lunar mantle material exposed in the basin.
