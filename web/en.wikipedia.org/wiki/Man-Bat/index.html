Man-Bat (Dr. Kirk Langstrom) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by writer Frank Robbins and artist Neal Adams, he first appeared in Detective Comics #400 (June 1970) as an adversary of Batman, though later depictions have portrayed him as an antihero and occasional ally. Langstrom is a zoologist who, in an attempt to cure his own encroaching deafness, developed a serum from bat gland extract that transforms him into a monstrous human-bat hybrid.

Fictional character biography

Dr. Kirk Langstrom, a scientist specialising in the study of bats (mammalian chiropterology) at the Gotham City zoo, began losing his hearing. Determined to give humans the bats' sense of echolocation ("sonar"), he concocted a serum using bat DNA and glandular extracts and tested it on himself. The serum granted him the hearing he wanted but horrifically mutated him into a giant humanoid bat creature. In this form, his intellect and humanity recede, leaving a savage creature driven by instinct. Batman was forced to fight him while seeking a cure, and Langstrom was restored — but the transformation proved recurring, and his wife Francine and later other family members have also been drawn into the Man-Bat curse in various storylines.

Across the decades, Langstrom has oscillated between villainy and attempts at redemption. He has been a member of incarnations of the Secret Society of Super Villains, and in later continuity joined the magical strike team Justice League Dark, where his scientific mind and monstrous form made him a valuable, if unstable, member. His daughter and son have inherited bat forms in some versions, and the Man-Bat formula has repeatedly been weaponised by others, including the organisation behind the "Man-Bat commandos".

Powers and abilities

In his Man-Bat form, Langstrom possesses a pair of large leathery wings granting flight, a sophisticated echolocation system, greatly heightened hearing and night vision, superhuman strength and agility, and sharp claws and teeth. His intellect varies with the depiction: in the transformed state he is often near-feral, though some versions allow Kirk's scientific mind to remain in control.

In other media

Man-Bat made a celebrated appearance as the first villain seen in Batman: The Animated Series (1992), in the premiere episode "On Leather Wings". He has since appeared in numerous animated series and films, video games including the Batman: Arkham series, and in live-action in the television series Gotham and in Titans. A live-action appearance was also planned in various unproduced Batman film projects over the years.
