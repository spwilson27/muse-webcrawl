Kajol

Kajol Devgan (née Mukherjee; born 5 August 1974), known mononymously as Kajol, is an Indian actress who works in Hindi cinema. One of the most successful and acclaimed actresses of the 1990s and 2000s, she has won six Filmfare Awards, including a record-tying five Best Actress awards, and was honoured with the Padma Shri in 2011. Her pairing with Shah Rukh Khan produced a string of landmark romantic films.

Early life and breakthrough

Kajol was born in Bombay into a film family: she is the daughter of actress Tanuja and director Shomu Mukherjee, the niece of actress Nutan, and the cousin of actress Rani Mukerji. She debuted at seventeen in Bekhudi (1992) and had her first commercial success with the thriller Baazigar (1993). In 1995 she starred as Simran in Aditya Chopra's Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge, one of the biggest hits in Indian cinema history, winning her first Filmfare Best Actress award.

Career highlights

Hits followed in rapid succession: Karan Arjun (1995), Gupt (1997) — in which she played a murderer and won the Filmfare Award for Best Villain — Pyaar Kiya To Darna Kya and the blockbuster Kuch Kuch Hota Hai (both 1998), and Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham… (2001). After a hiatus, she returned to acclaim with Fanaa (2006) and My Name Is Khan (2010), both opposite Shah Rukh Khan, and later appeared in the ensemble comedy Dilwale (2015), the Tamil film Velaiilla Pattadhari 2 (2017), the streaming drama Tribhanga (2021), and the legal drama series The Trial (2023).

Personal life

Kajol married actor Ajay Devgn in 1999; they have two children, Nysa and Yug. Outside acting she is involved in social work concerning children and widows, and has served as a goodwill ambassador and on the board of Prasar Bharati.
