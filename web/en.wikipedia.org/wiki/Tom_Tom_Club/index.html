Tom Tom Club is an American new wave band founded in 1981 by husband-and-wife team Tina Weymouth and Chris Frantz, the bassist and drummer of Talking Heads.

Career

Their self-titled debut album (1981), recorded in the Bahamas, produced the hits "Genius of Love" and "Wordy Rappinghood." "Genius of Love" became one of the most sampled tracks in hip-hop, famously underpinning Grandmaster Flash's "It's Nasty (Genius of Love)" and Mariah Carey's "Fantasy."

Later work

The band's subsequent albums include Close to the Bone (1983) and Boom Boom Chi Boom Boom (1988). Tom Tom Club has continued to tour intermittently alongside Weymouth and Frantz's work with Talking Heads. Weymouth and Frantz entered the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as members of Talking Heads in 2002.
