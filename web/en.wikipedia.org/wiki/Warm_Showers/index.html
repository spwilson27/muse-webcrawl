Warm Showers

Warm Showers (Warmshowers.org) is a non-profit hospitality exchange network for people engaged in bicycle touring. Members offer touring cyclists free accommodation — typically a bed, a floor, or space to camp, along with access to a shower, from which the service takes its name — and in return may stay with other members when they travel.

History and operation

The network grew out of informal lists of touring-cyclist hosts maintained in North America from the 1970s and 1990s, and was established as an online community in 2005, with roots in the work of Terry Zmrhal and Geoff Cashman. It operates through a website and mobile apps on which hosts and guests create profiles, exchange messages, and leave feedback after stays. Membership is free, though donations are solicited, and the community has grown to well over 100,000 members in nearly 200 countries.

Community

Warm Showers is organized around reciprocal hospitality rather than payment: guests are expected to be self-sufficient, provide their own food unless invited to share, and respect hosts' house rules. It is frequently compared to Couchsurfing, but is restricted to travelling cyclists and those willing to host them.
