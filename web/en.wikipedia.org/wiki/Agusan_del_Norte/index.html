Agusan del Norte

Agusan del Norte, officially the Province of Agusan del Norte, is a province in the Caraga region of the Philippines, located in northeastern Mindanao. Its capital and largest city is Cabadbaran, while Butuan, a highly urbanised city that is geographically within the province but administratively independent, serves as the regional centre of Caraga. The province borders Surigao del Norte to the north, Surigao del Sur to the east, Agusan del Sur to the south, and Misamis Oriental to the west.

History

The area takes its name from the Agusan River and the Manobo word agasan, "where the water flows." The old province of Agusan, created during the American colonial period, was divided by Republic Act No. 4979 in 1967 into Agusan del Norte and Agusan del Sur. Butuan, historically the original capital, became a highly urbanised city in 1985; although the provincial government long sat in Butuan, the capital was formally transferred to Cabadbaran, a status confirmed after legal disputes in the 2000s. The region has a rich precolonial history: Butuan was a major trading polity, famous for the balangay boat burials and gold artefacts, and is remembered in local tradition as a claimant site of the first Mass in the Philippines.

Geography

The province is largely flat in its coastal and riverine areas, rising to mountains in the east and west; Mount Hilong-Hilong on the border area is among the higher peaks of Caraga. The Agusan River, one of the longest rivers in the Philippines, flows north through the province to Butuan Bay. Lake Mainit, shared with Surigao del Norte, lies in the north. The climate is tropical with rainfall distributed through the year and no pronounced dry season.

Economy and demographics

The economy is based on agriculture—rice, coconut, banana, abaca, and palm oil—fishing, and wood processing; mining and mineral processing have also been significant. Most residents are Cebuano-speaking migrants from the Visayas and other regions, alongside indigenous Manobo, Mamanwa, and Higaonon communities. Cebuano is the dominant language, with Surigaonon, Butuanon, and Manobo also spoken.

Local government units

Agusan del Norte comprises the city of Cabadbaran and ten municipalities: Buenavista, Carmen, Jabonga, Kitcharao, Las Nieves, Magallanes, Nasipit, Remedios T. Romualdez, Santiago, and Tubay.
