Midway Games

Midway Games Inc.
Founded: 1958 (as Midway Manufacturing)
Defunct: 2010
Headquarters: Chicago, Illinois, US
Industry: Video games

Midway Games Inc. was an American video game developer and publisher based in Chicago, Illinois. Founded in 1958 as an amusement equipment manufacturer, Midway became one of the most successful arcade game producers of the 1980s and 1990s, licensing titles such as Space Invaders, Pac-Man and Ms. Pac-Man for North American release from their Japanese creators.

History

Midway Manufacturing began by producing arcade amusement machines and later electromechanical games. In 1969 it was acquired by Bally Manufacturing. Midway's 1978 licensing of Taito's Space Invaders sparked the arcade boom, and its distribution of Namco's Pac-Man (1980) produced the best-selling arcade game in American history.

In the 1990s, under the Williams and Midway labels, the company created defining arcade hits including Mortal Kombat (1992), NBA Jam (1993), Cruis'n USA (1994) and San Francisco Rush. As the arcade market declined, Midway pivoted to home console publishing, with series such as Spy Hunter, Gauntlet, Rampage and later The Suffering and Psi-Ops.

Bankruptcy and legacy

After years of losses, Midway filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in February 2009. Most of its assets, including the Mortal Kombat franchise, were acquired by Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment; the Mortal Kombat team became NetherRealm Studios. The Midway name and remaining properties were gradually wound down, with the company ceasing operations in 2010.
