Hiram Maxim

Sir Hiram Stevens Maxim (5 February 1840 – 24 February 1916) was an American-born British inventor best known as the creator of the Maxim gun, the first recoil-operated machine gun. He also held patents on electric lighting and other inventions, and founded the Maxim Gun Company, later absorbed into Vickers.

Life

Born in Maine, Maxim worked as a carriage maker and engineer before moving into electrical engineering, competing with Edison's lighting interests. He emigrated to England in 1881 and was knighted in 1901.

The Maxim gun

Patented in 1884, the water-cooled, belt-fed Maxim gun used the energy of recoil to eject and reload automatically, firing about 600 rounds per minute. It was adopted by many armies and reshaped warfare.
