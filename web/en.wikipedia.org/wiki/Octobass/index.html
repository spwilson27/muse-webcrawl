Octobass

The octobass of the Montreal Symphony Orchestra, played with its lever system.

The octobass is an extremely large and rare bowed string instrument, first built around 1850 by the French luthier Jean-Baptiste Vuillaume. Standing about 3.45 metres (11 ft 4 in) tall, it is tuned an octave below the double bass and its strings are stopped not by the fingers alone but by a system of keys and levers operated by the player's hands and feet.

History and use

Vuillaume built three octobasses; surviving originals are held by museums in Paris and Vienna. Hector Berlioz praised the instrument's deep, powerful tone. Modern replicas have been built, and the Montreal Symphony Orchestra acquired an octobass in 2016, making it the only orchestra to own one.
