Christen Raunkiær

Christen Raunkiær most commonly refers to Christen Christensen Raunkiær (1860–1938), the Danish botanist and pioneer of plant ecology best known for the Raunkiær plant life-form system and the concept of the biological spectrum.

Summary

Raunkiær (1860–1938) classified the world's plants by where they keep their renewal buds during hard seasons — high in the canopy (phanerophytes), near the ground (chamaephytes), at the surface (hemicryptophytes), underground (cryptophytes), or as seed (therophytes) — and showed that the proportions of these forms in a flora reflect its climate. He was professor of botany at the University of Copenhagen and a founder of modern quantitative vegetation science.
