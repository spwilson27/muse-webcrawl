Rihanna

Born: Robyn Rihanna Fenty, 20 February 1988, Saint Michael, Barbados
Occupations: Singer, businesswoman, actress
Genres: Pop, R&B, dancehall, dance
Labels: Def Jam, Roc Nation, Westbury Road

Robyn Rihanna Fenty (born 20 February 1988) is a Barbadian singer, businesswoman and actress. One of the best-selling music artists of all time, she has sold over 250 million records and released eight studio albums, earning nine Grammy Awards and worldwide hits including "Umbrella", "We Found Love", "Diamonds" and "Work".

Music career

Spotted by producer Evan Rogers in Barbados, Rihanna signed with Def Jam in 2005 and released Music of the Sun that year. Her third album, Good Girl Gone Bad (2007), made her a global superstar; subsequent albums Rated R, Loud, Talk That Talk, Unapologetic and Anti (2016) topped charts worldwide.

Business and honours

Her Fenty Beauty cosmetics line (2017) made her a billionaire and one of entertainment's wealthiest women. Barbados named her a National Hero in 2021, entitling her to the style "The Right Excellent".
