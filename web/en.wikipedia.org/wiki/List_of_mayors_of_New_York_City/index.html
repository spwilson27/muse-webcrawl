List of mayors of New York City

The mayor of New York City is the chief executive of the government of New York City. The mayor's office administers all city services and enforces all city and state laws within New York City. This article lists the mayors of New York City from the first English mayor in 1665 to the present, with emphasis on the modern (post-consolidation) era.
