Pain and Glory (Spanish: Dolor y gloria) is a 2019 Spanish drama film written and directed by Pedro Almodóvar. It stars Antonio Banderas as Salvador Mallo, an ageing film director in physical and creative decline who, through a series of reunions and recollections — with an actor he had fallen out with, a former lover, and his memories of childhood with his mother (played in youth by Penélope Cruz) — reflects on his life and art.

Plot

Suffering chronic pain and unable to work, Salvador attends a restoration screening of his early film Sabor and reconciles with its lead actor, Alberto Crespo, from whom he has been estranged for decades. Through Alberto he encounters his own rediscovered autobiographical monologue, meets his former lover Federico, now visiting from Argentina, and in closing flashbacks recalls his childhood in a cave dwelling in Paterna, his first desire, and his mother's final days.

Reception

The film premiered in competition at the 2019 Cannes Film Festival, where Banderas won the Best Actor Award. It was selected as Spain's entry for Best International Feature Film at the 92nd Academy Awards and was nominated in that category; Banderas was nominated for Best Actor. Critics praised it as Almodóvar's most personal and restrained work, frequently comparing it to Fellini's 8½. At the Goya Awards it won seven prizes including Best Film, Best Director and Best Actor.
