Ministry of Jal Shakti

The Ministry of Jal Shakti (Hindi: जल शक्ति मंत्रालय, "Ministry of Water Power") is a ministry of the Government of India formed in May 2019 by merging the Ministry of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation with the Ministry of Drinking Water and Sanitation. It is responsible for water resources, river development, the rejuvenation of the river Ganga, drinking water, and sanitation in India.

Organisation

The ministry comprises two departments: the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation, and the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation. Subordinate and attached organisations include the Central Water Commission, the Central Ground Water Board, the National Water Development Agency, and the National Mission for Clean Ganga, which implements the Namami Gange programme.

Programmes

Major initiatives under the ministry include the Jal Shakti Abhiyan, a water-conservation campaign launched in 2019; the Jal Jeevan Mission, which aims to provide functional household tap connections supplying potable water to rural households; and the Swachh Bharat Mission – Gramin (Phase II), administered by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation. The first Minister of Jal Shakti was Gajendra Singh Shekhawat, appointed in 2019.
