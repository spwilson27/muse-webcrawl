Friesland

Friesland (West Frisian: Fryslân) is a province of the Netherlands located in the northern part of the country. Its capital is Leeuwarden (Ljouwert). The province is known for its distinct language, West Frisian, which has official status alongside Dutch.

Geography

Friesland includes a mainland coastal region along the Wadden Sea and several West Frisian Islands. Its landscape of lakes (the Frisian Lakes) makes it a center for sailing and for the traditional skûtsjesilen regattas, and its frozen canals host the Elfstedentocht, the famous eleven-cities ice-skating tour, when winters are cold enough.

Culture

Frisian is spoken by roughly half the population. The province is the heartland of the black-and-white Friesian horse and Friesian cattle breeds.
