Pope John Paul II

Pope John Paul II (born Karol Józef Wojtyła, 1920–2005) headed the Catholic Church from 1978 to 2005, the first non-Italian pope in over 450 years and one of the longest-serving popes in history.

Pontificate

Elected on 16 October 1978, he travelled more widely than all previous popes combined, played a remembered role in the fall of communism in Eastern Europe, survived an assassination attempt in 1981, and was canonised in 2014.
