National Assembly of Pakistan

National Assembly of Pakistan
Type: Lower house of the Majlis-e-Shoora
Seats: 336
Meeting place: Parliament House, Islamabad
Presiding officer: Speaker

The National Assembly of Pakistan (Urdu: ایوانِ زیریں) is the lower house of Pakistan's bicameral parliament, the Majlis-e-Shoora, the upper house being the Senate. It is a sovereign legislative body, elected for a five-year term, that embodies the federal will of the people under the Constitution of 1973.

Composition

The Assembly has 336 members: 266 elected by direct vote in single-member constituencies, plus 60 seats reserved for women and 10 for religious minorities, filled by proportional representation from party lists. A member is called an MNA (Member of the National Assembly). The Leader of the House is the Prime Minister, whom the Assembly elects; it can remove the government by a vote of no confidence.

Powers

For money bills the National Assembly has primacy: the Senate may only make recommendations. The Assembly shares ordinary legislative power with the Senate, and sits with it in joint session for certain matters, including electing the President of Pakistan and resolving deadlocks between the houses.
