Sweden in the Eurovision Song Contest

Sweden has participated in the Eurovision Song Contest more than sixty times since its debut in 1958, missing only a handful of contests in the 1960s and 1970s. The Swedish broadcaster Sveriges Television (SVT), and before it Sveriges Radio, has organised the Swedish entry through the national selection Melodifestivalen, which has been held annually since 1959 and is one of the most watched television programmes in Sweden.

History

Sweden is one of the contest's most successful countries. It has won seven times, tying Ireland for the most victories. The first win came in 1974, when ABBA performed "Waterloo" in Brighton — a performance widely credited with launching the group's international career and often voted the best Eurovision entry of all time. Subsequent victories were achieved by Herreys with "Diggi-Loo Diggi-Ley" (1984), Carola with "Fångad av en stormvind" (1991), Charlotte Nilsson with "Take Me to Your Heaven" (1999), and Loreen with "Euphoria" (2012).

In 2015, Måns Zelmerlöw won in Vienna with "Heroes", and in 2023 Loreen became only the second artist ever — after Ireland's Johnny Logan — to win the contest twice, taking the trophy in Liverpool with "Tattoo".

Melodifestivalen

The Swedish entry is chosen through Melodifestivalen, a multi-week televised competition that typically features heats held in cities around Sweden, a second-chance round and a grand final in Stockholm. The winner is decided by a combination of international jury votes and public televoting.

Hosting

Sweden has hosted the contest seven times: in Stockholm (1975, 2000, 2016), Gothenburg (1985), Malmö (1992, 2013, 2024). The 2024 contest was held at the Malmö Arena following Loreen's victory.
