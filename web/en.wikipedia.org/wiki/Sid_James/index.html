Sid James

Sidney James (born Solomon Joel Cohen, 8 May 1913 – 26 April 1976) was a South African-born British actor and comedian. He became one of Britain's best-loved comedy performers through his work with Tony Hancock in Hancock's Half Hour and as the lecherous, cackling star of 19 of the Carry On films. His gravelly voice, dirty laugh, and "cheeky chappie" persona made him a fixture of British popular culture.

Early life and career

Born in Johannesburg to Jewish parents, Cohen trained as a hairdresser before serving in an entertainment unit of the South African Army during the Second World War. He emigrated to Britain in 1946, working as a coalman and dance instructor while trying to enter acting. Changing his name to Sid James (from a wartime nickname), he obtained small film roles from 1947, often playing heavies and villains, and served an apprenticeship in repertory theatre.

Success

James's breakthrough came in 1954 when he joined Tony Hancock in the radio (later television) comedy Hancock's Half Hour, playing Hancock's shady, sharp-witted friend — a role written for him by Galton and Simpson. The double act made him a household name, though the partnership ended when James left the show in 1960.

That same year he appeared in Carry On Constable, the first of his 19 Carry On films, which included Carry On Cleo, Carry On Cowboy, Carry On Screaming!, Carry On Up the Khyber, and Carry On Camping. His screen persona — the lecherous everyman with the famous leering laugh — was refined across these films. On television he starred in the sitcoms Citizen James (1960–62), George and the Dragon (1966–68), and Bless This House (1971–76).

Death and legacy

James collapsed and died of a heart attack on stage at the Sunderland Empire Theatre on 26 April 1976, during a performance of the farce The Mating Season; the audience initially believed his stillness was part of the act. He was cremated at Golders Green Crematorium. His image remains iconic: his face appears in murals and commemorations at Pinewood Studios, and he is consistently rated among Britain's favourite comedy actors.
