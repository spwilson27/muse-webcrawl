List of tallest statues

This list of tallest statues includes statues that are at least 30 metres (98 ft) tall, measured from the base of the figure (excluding pedestals unless noted). The tallest statue in the world is the Statue of Unity in India, depicting Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, completed in 2018 at 182 metres (597 ft).

Historical statues

Among the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World was the Colossus of Rhodes, a statue of Helios about 33 metres tall, destroyed by earthquake in 226 BC. The Colossus of Nero in Rome and the great bronze Buddhas of Nara (752) were among the largest statues of the pre-modern world.
