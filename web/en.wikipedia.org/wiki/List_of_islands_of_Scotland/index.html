List of islands of Scotland

The List of islands of Scotland covers the more than 790 offshore islands surrounding the Scottish mainland. Fewer than one hundred are inhabited. The islands fall into several major groups: Shetland and Orkney (the Northern Isles), the Hebrides off the west coast (divided into the Inner and Outer Hebrides), the islands of the Firth of Clyde, and smaller groups in the Firth of Forth and elsewhere.

Culture and history

The Northern Isles were ruled by Norway until the fifteenth century and retain a strong Norse heritage, while the Hebrides blend Gaelic traditions with a history of clan lordship under the Lordship of the Isles. Island economies today rely on fishing, aquaculture, whisky distilling, renewable energy, tourism, and crofting.
