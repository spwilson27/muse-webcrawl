Bridgerton

[Image placeholder: Bridgerton]

Bridgerton is an American historical romance television series created by Chris Van Dusen for Netflix, based on the novel series by Julia Quinn. Produced by Shondaland, it is set in an alternative-history Regency-era London in which people of colour are integrated into high society, and follows the eight close-knit siblings of the Bridgerton family as they seek love during the social season.

Release and reception

The first season, adapting The Duke and I and starring Phoebe Dynevor and Regé-Jean Page, premiered on 25 December 2020 and became one of Netflix's most-watched series launches. Subsequent seasons adapted The Viscount Who Loved Me (2022) and Romancing Mister Bridgerton (2024), and the prequel Queen Charlotte: A Bridgerton Story aired in 2023. The series is noted for its sumptuous costumes, classical covers of pop songs and colour-conscious casting, and has won Emmy and Screen Actors Guild awards.
