Rosé (singer)

Born: Roseanne Park, 11 February 1997, Auckland, New Zealand

Occupation: Singer

Genres: K-pop, pop

Years active: 2016–present

Roseanne Park (born 11 February 1997), known mononymously as Rosé (Korean: 로제), is a New Zealand and South Korean singer. Born in Auckland and raised in Melbourne, Australia, she signed with YG Entertainment after a successful audition in Sydney in 2012 and trained for four years. She rose to fame as a member of the girl group Blackpink, which debuted in August 2016 and became one of the best-selling girl groups of all time.

Career

With Blackpink, Rosé recorded two Korean studio albums and performed on record-breaking world tours, while also becoming a global ambassador for fashion houses Yves Saint Laurent and Tiffany & Co. She made her solo debut in March 2021 with the single album R, containing "On the Ground" and "Gone." "On the Ground" topped the Billboard Global 200 and made her the first artist to reach number one on the chart both as a soloist and as part of a group; R set a record for first-week sales by a Korean female soloist.

In 2024 she signed with The Black Label for Korean activities and Atlantic Records internationally, and released "APT.", a collaboration with Bruno Mars, which became a global number-one hit. Her debut studio album, Rosie, followed in December 2024, reaching the top of charts in several countries and the top three of the US Billboard 200, the highest position for a Korean female soloist.

Artistry

Rosé is the main vocalist of Blackpink, noted for a distinctive, airy vocal timbre that a vocal coach praised as a "unique voice" suited to pop. She plays guitar and piano, often accompanying herself in stripped-back performances, and cites artists such as Tori Kelly as influences.
