Duke of Abercorn is a title in the Peerage of Ireland created in 1868 for James Hamilton, 2nd Marquess of Abercorn. The dukedom takes its name from Abercorn in West Lothian, Scotland. The family seat is Baronscourt, near Newtownstewart in County Tyrone, Northern Ireland.

History

The Hamilton family descended from Claude Hamilton, a younger son of James Hamilton, 2nd Earl of Arran. The Marquessate of Abercorn was created in 1790. The first Duke served as Lord Lieutenant of Ireland. The third Duke was the first Governor of Northern Ireland, serving from 1922 to 1945.

The present holder is James Hamilton, 5th Duke of Abercorn, who succeeded in 1979. The heir apparent uses the courtesy title Marquess of Hamilton. Diana, Princess of Wales, was descended from the family through her mother, Frances Shand Kydd, a granddaughter of the 3rd Duke.
