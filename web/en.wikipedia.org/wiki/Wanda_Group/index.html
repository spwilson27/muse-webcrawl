Wanda Group

Wanda Group (Dalian Wanda Group Co., Ltd.) is a Chinese multinational conglomerate founded in 1988.

Founded: 1988, Dalian, China
Founder: Wang Jianlin
Headquarters: Beijing, China

Wanda Group (Chinese: 万达集团), officially Dalian Wanda Group Co., Ltd., is a Chinese multinational conglomerate founded in Dalian, Liaoning, and headquartered in Beijing. Founded in 1988 by Wang Jianlin, it grew from a residential real-estate developer into one of the world's largest private property developers and operators of shopping malls, cinemas and entertainment businesses.

Business

The group's core business is commercial property, centred on its Wanda Plaza shopping mall complexes, which combine retail, dining, entertainment and hotels in cities across China. Through Wanda Film and its former ownership of the American cinema chain AMC Theatres and the studio Legendary Entertainment, the group became a major global player in the film industry.

History

Wanda expanded aggressively in the 2000s and 2010s, acquiring AMC Entertainment in 2012 and Legendary Entertainment in 2016, and investing in sports properties including a stake in Atlético Madrid and the sports marketing firm Infront. From 2017, facing regulatory scrutiny of overseas acquisitions and high debt levels, the group sold many assets, including hotel and theme-park portfolios to Sunac China and R&F Properties, and reduced its stakes in AMC and other businesses.
