Battle of Savenay

Battle of Savenay
Date: 23 December 1793
Location: Savenay, France
Part of: War in the Vendée

The Battle of Savenay was fought on 23 December 1793 during the War in the Vendée in revolutionary France. Republican forces defeated the remnants of the Catholic and Royal Army, ending the Virée de Galerne, the royalist army's campaign north of the Loire.

Background

After defeats at Le Mans, the Vendéan army, accompanied by thousands of civilians, retreated west hoping to cross the Loire or reach the sea.

The battle

Republican troops under generals including Kléber and Marceau attacked at dawn. The royalists were routed; many were killed in the fighting or in reprisals that followed.

Significance

Savenay destroyed the main royalist field army, though guerrilla warfare in the Vendée continued for years.
