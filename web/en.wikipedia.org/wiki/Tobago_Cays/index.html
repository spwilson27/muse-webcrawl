Tobago Cays

The Tobago Cays are an archipelago located in the Southern Grenadines in the Caribbean Sea, part of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. They comprise five small, uninhabited islands — Petit Rameau, Petit Bateau, Baradal, Petit Tabac and Jamesby — surrounded by a large horseshoe-shaped coral reef.

The cays are protected as the Tobago Cays Marine Park, established by the government of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines to preserve the marine environment. The clear, shallow lagoon is a popular destination for yachting, snorkelling and diving, with abundant sea turtles, rays and tropical fish.

Geography and ecology

The reef provides shelter from Atlantic swells. Petit Tabac was a filming location for the film Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl. The park is managed with mooring fees used for conservation.
