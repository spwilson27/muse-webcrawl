National Football Conference

The National Football Conference (NFC) is one of the two conferences of the National Football League (NFL), the other being the American Football Conference (AFC). The NFC was created after the AFL–NFL merger in 1970 from the teams of the pre-merger NFL.

Structure

The NFC comprises 16 teams in four divisions — East, North, South and West — each of four teams. The conference champion is decided by the NFC Championship Game and receives the George Halas Trophy; the winner advances to the Super Bowl to face the AFC champion.

Teams

Teams include the Dallas Cowboys, Green Bay Packers, Chicago Bears, San Francisco 49ers, New York Giants and Philadelphia Eagles.
