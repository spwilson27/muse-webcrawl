Rimutaka Tunnel

The Rimutaka Tunnel is a railway tunnel through the Remutaka Range in the Wellington Region of New Zealand, on the Wairarapa Line between Maymorn (near Upper Hutt) and Featherston. At 8.79 km (5.46 mi) long, it was the longest railway tunnel in New Zealand when it opened and remained so until the Kaimai Tunnel opened in 1978.

History

The tunnel, together with the Rimutaka Deviation, replaced the famous Rimutaka Incline, a steep section worked by Fell locomotives from 1878. Construction began in 1948 using contract and Public Works labour, and the deviation was opened in 1955, greatly reducing journey times between Wellington and the Wairarapa.

Operation

The tunnel is single-track with ventilation provided by a vertical shaft near its midpoint. Diesel and electric passenger services of the Wairarapa Connection, as well as freight trains, pass through it daily.
