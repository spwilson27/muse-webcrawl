Beat Saber

Developer: Beat Games
Released: Early access 2018; full release 21 May 2019
Genre: Virtual reality rhythm game
Platforms: PlayStation VR, Meta Quest, Windows (SteamVR)

Beat Saber is a virtual reality rhythm game developed and published by Czech studio Beat Games. The player uses two glowing blades, one red and one blue, to slash coloured blocks flying toward them in time with music, matching the block's colour and the direction of arrows printed on it, while ducking under walls and dodging obstacles.

Gameplay

Blocks approach along four lanes to the beat of a track. Points are awarded for the swing angle before and after each cut and for accuracy near the block's centre; chains build a score multiplier. Songs are offered at multiple difficulty levels, from Easy to Expert+. The game includes a campaign, party mode, and a level editor on PC; official music packs have featured artists such as Imagine Dragons, Linkin Park, BTS, Billie Eilish, and Queen.

Development and reception

Beat Saber was created by Vladimír Hrinčár, Ján Ilavský, and Jaroslav Beck, whose electronic music accompanies the base game. It launched in early access in May 2018 and became one of the best-selling VR games of all time, selling more than 4 million copies by 2021, with revenues exceeding US$100 million on Quest alone by 2022. Facebook (Meta) acquired Beat Games in November 2019. The game won several awards, including Best VR/AR Game at The Game Awards 2019, and is frequently used as an exercise game and as a showcase title for VR headsets.
