General Dynamics Corporation is an American multinational aerospace and defence corporation headquartered in Reston, Virginia. It is one of the world's largest defence contractors, and is organised into four business groups: Aerospace, Marine Systems, Combat Systems, and Technologies.

General Dynamics builds Gulfstream business jets and US Navy submarines.

History

The company's roots lie in the Electric Boat Company, founded in 1899, which built the US Navy's first submarines. After the Second World War the investor Henry Crown assembled a group of companies under the name General Dynamics, adopted in 1952. The company developed the F-16 Fighting Falcon (its aircraft division was sold to Lockheed in 1993) and acquired Gulfstream Aerospace in 1999.

Business units

Marine Systems includes Electric Boat, builder of nuclear-powered submarines, Bath Iron Works, and NASSCO shipyards. Combat Systems produces the Abrams main battle tank and Stryker vehicles through General Dynamics Land Systems. The Aerospace group is Gulfstream, maker of business jets. Technologies, anchored by GDIT, provides information technology and mission-support services to government customers.
