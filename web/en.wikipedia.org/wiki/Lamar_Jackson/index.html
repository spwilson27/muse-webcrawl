Lamar Demeatrice Jackson Jr. (born January 7, 1997) is an American professional football quarterback for the Baltimore Ravens of the National Football League (NFL). He played college football for the Louisville Cardinals, winning the Heisman Trophy in 2016, and was selected by the Ravens with the final pick in the first round of the 2018 NFL draft.

Jackson became the Ravens' starting quarterback during his rookie season and in 2019 led the league in touchdown passes while setting the single-season rushing record for a quarterback, earning unanimous NFL Most Valuable Player honours — only the second unanimous selection in league history. He won a second MVP award for the 2023 season and holds the NFL record for career rushing yards by a quarterback.

Early life

Jackson was raised in Pompano Beach, Florida, and attended Boynton Beach Community High School, where he starred as a dual-threat quarterback. He committed to the University of Louisville.

College career

At Louisville, Jackson broke out in 2016 with 3,543 passing yards, 30 passing touchdowns, 1,571 rushing yards and 21 rushing touchdowns, winning the Heisman Trophy as a sophomore. He followed with another prolific season in 2017 before declaring for the NFL draft.

Professional career

Drafted 32nd overall in 2018, Jackson took over as Baltimore's starter midway through his rookie year and led the team to the playoffs. His 2019 season — 3,127 passing yards, 36 touchdown passes, 1,206 rushing yards — is regarded as one of the greatest quarterback seasons ever. In 2023 he signed a five-year contract extension with the Ravens and won his second MVP award while leading Baltimore to the AFC Championship Game.

Playing style

Jackson is celebrated for his speed, elusiveness and open-field running, combined with efficient downfield passing; his dual-threat ability reshaped perceptions of the quarterback position in the modern NFL.
