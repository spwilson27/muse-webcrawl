Marine One

Marine One is the call sign of any United States Marine Corps aircraft carrying the president of the United States. It most commonly denotes the helicopters of Marine Helicopter Squadron One (HMX-1), which is responsible for presidential helicopter transport.

Aircraft

The familiar green-and-white helicopters seen on the White House South Lawn have included the Sikorsky VH-3D Sea King and the smaller VH-60N White Hawk. A replacement programme produced the VH-92A Patriot, based on the Sikorsky S-92, which began presidential service in the 2020s. Identical decoy helicopters fly in formation so observers cannot tell which carries the president. When the vice president is aboard, the call sign is Marine Two.
