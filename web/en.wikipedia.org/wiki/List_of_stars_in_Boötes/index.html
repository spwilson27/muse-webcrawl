List of stars in Boötes

This is a list of notable stars in the constellation Boötes, the Herdsman. Its brightest star, Arcturus, is the third-brightest star in the night sky and the brightest in the northern celestial hemisphere. The constellation also contains Tau Boötis, one of the first stars found to host a "hot Jupiter" exoplanet.
