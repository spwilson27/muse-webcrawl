The Times is a British daily national newspaper based in London, founded as The Daily Universal Register in 1785 and renamed The Times in 1788. One of the world's most famous newspaper names — 'the Thunderer' — it is published by News UK, part of Rupert Murdoch's News Corp, which bought Times Newspapers in 1981.

History

The Times shaped 19th-century journalism — war reporting from Crimea, the leader column's influence — and gave English the word 'Times' as a synonym for newspaper in many languages. Its 1966 move of news to the front page and its 2004 switch to compact format were milestones; it adopted a metered paywall in 2010.

Sister paper

The Sunday Times, founded 1822 and merged in ownership in 1967, is editorially separate and Britain's best-selling quality Sunday paper.
