Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide

Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide, commonly referred to as simply AAA (an abbreviation of its original name, Asistencia Asesoría y Administración), is a Mexican lucha libre (professional wrestling) promotion based in Mexico City. It was founded in 1992 by Antonio Peña after he left the Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre (CMLL).

History

AAA split from CMLL in 1992 when Peña, then CMLL's head booker, broke away with many of the promotion's younger stars. AAA became known for its innovative characters, storyline-driven product and annual flagship events such as Triplemanía, Guerra de Titanes and Rey de Reyes. The promotion has held talent-exchange agreements with promotions in the United States and Japan, and in 2025 World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) acquired a controlling stake in AAA.

Championships

AAA's principal titles include the AAA Mega Championship, the AAA Latin American Championship, the AAA World Tag Team Championship and the AAA World Mixed Tag Team Championship.
