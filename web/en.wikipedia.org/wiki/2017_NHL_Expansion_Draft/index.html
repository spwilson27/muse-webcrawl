2017 NHL Expansion Draft

Draft details
Date21 June 2017
LocationT-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada
TeamVegas Golden Knights
Selections30 (one from each existing NHL team)

The 2017 NHL Expansion Draft was held on 21 June 2017 to fill the roster of the Vegas Golden Knights, the National Hockey League's 31st franchise, ahead of their inaugural 2017–18 season. Vegas selected one unprotected player from each of the league's thirty existing teams.

Rules

Each existing team could protect either seven forwards, three defencemen and one goaltender, or eight skaters and one goaltender. Players with no-movement clauses had to be protected, while first- and second-year professionals were exempt. Vegas was required to select at least fourteen forwards, nine defencemen and three goaltenders, and to take on a minimum proportion of the salary cap.

Notable selections

Vegas's choices included goaltender Marc-André Fleury from Pittsburgh, forward James Neal from Nashville, defenceman Nate Schmidt from Washington, and forward William Karlsson from Columbus. Side deals between Vegas general manager George McPhee and other clubs — involving draft picks and additional players — heavily shaped the final selections.

Aftermath

The assembled roster, nicknamed the "Golden Misfits", far exceeded expectations: Vegas won its division and advanced to the 2018 Stanley Cup Final in its first season, the most successful debut by an expansion team in North American major league sports history.
