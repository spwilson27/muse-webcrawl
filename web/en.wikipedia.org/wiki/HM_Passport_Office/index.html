His Majesty's Passport Office (HMPO) is an executive agency of the Home Office in the United Kingdom. It is responsible for issuing British passports to citizens of the United Kingdom and for overseeing civil registration services in England and Wales through the General Register Office, which became part of the agency in 2008.

History

The Passport Office traces its origins to the nineteenth century; a dedicated Passport Department was established after the First World War. The United Kingdom Passport Agency became an executive agency in 1991. It was merged with the UK Identity and Passport Service in 2006 and, after the Identity Cards Act 2006 scheme was abolished, relaunched as HM Passport Office in May 2013.

Operations

HMPO operates offices in London, Liverpool, Peterborough, Durham, Newport, Glasgow and Belfast, and processes several million passport applications each year; British passports are printed centrally and are burgundy-to-blue in their post-Brexit design, reverting to the traditional blue cover from 2020. Overseas applicants apply through HMPO for British passports issued abroad.
