Hong Kong Island

Hong Kong Island is an island in the southern part of Hong Kong. With a population of about 1.2 million and an area of roughly 79 square kilometres, it is the territory's second-most-populous island and its historical and financial heart.

Geography and history

The island's northern shore, facing Victoria Harbour, contains the central business district of Central, Admiralty and Wan Chai, crowded with skyscrapers. The island was ceded by Qing China to Britain under the Treaty of Nanking in 1842, becoming the founding territory of the colony of Hong Kong. The southern side of the island, with districts such as Aberdeen and Stanley, is hillier and more residential, with beaches and country parks.
