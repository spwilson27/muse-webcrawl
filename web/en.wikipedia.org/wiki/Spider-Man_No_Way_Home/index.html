Directed by: Jon Watts
Starring: Tom Holland, Zendaya, Benedict Cumberbatch, Willem Dafoe
Released: 17 December 2021
Box office: about $1.9 billion

Spider-Man: No Way Home is a 2021 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character Spider-Man, co-produced by Columbia Pictures and Marvel Studios and distributed by Sony Pictures Releasing. It is the sequel to Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017) and Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019), and the 27th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). The film was directed by Jon Watts and stars Tom Holland as Peter Parker / Spider-Man, alongside Zendaya, Benedict Cumberbatch, Jacob Batalon, Jon Favreau, Marisa Tomei, and returning actors from earlier Spider-Man film franchises.

Plot

After Mysterio reveals Spider-Man's identity to the world, Peter Parker asks Doctor Strange to cast a spell to make everyone forget; the spell goes wrong and breaks open the multiverse, bringing in villains — and two earlier versions of Peter Parker — from other realities. Parker must capture the villains and grapples with the cost of sending them back, culminating in a final spell that erases the world's memory of his identity.

Reception

The film received widespread critical acclaim, particularly for its emotional weight, performances and fan service, and became the highest-grossing film of 2021 and one of the highest-grossing films of all time, earning over $1.9 billion worldwide including its 2022 re-release. It was nominated for Best Visual Effects at the 94th Academy Awards.
