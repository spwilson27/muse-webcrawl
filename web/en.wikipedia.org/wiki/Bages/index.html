Bages

Bages (Catalan pronunciation: [ˈbaʒəs]) is a comarca (county) in the central part of Catalonia, Spain. Its capital is Manresa, the largest town, and it covers an area of about 1,295 square kilometres with a population of roughly 185,000. The comarca lies in the Central Catalan Depression, in a basin carved by the Llobregat and Cardener rivers, and is overlooked by the distinctive serrated mountain of Montserrat on its southern edge.

Geography and economy

The Bages plain is encircled by pre-coastal ranges, and its agriculture has traditionally included vineyards — the Pla de Bages is a protected designation of origin for wine — along with cereals and olive groves. Manresa developed as an important textile and industrial centre during Catalonia's industrialisation, and the comarca has significant potash mining around Súria, Sallent and Cardona, whose famous "Salt Mountain" is a notable geological site.

Heritage

Sights include the monastery of Sant Benet de Bages, the Collegiate Basilica of Santa Maria in Manresa (known as La Seu), the Cave of Saint Ignatius, where Ignatius of Loyola spent months in prayer in 1522, and the castle of Cardona.
