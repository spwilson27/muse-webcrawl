Seal of California

Great Seal of the State of California
Adopted: 1849
Motto: Eureka

The Great Seal of the State of California was adopted at California's first constitutional convention in 1849 and has undergone minor changes since, most recently in 1937. Because the seal was never officially described in complete detail at adoption, small variations exist among renderings.

Design

The central figure is Minerva, Roman goddess of wisdom, said to have sprung fully grown from the head of Jupiter — a symbol of California's admission to the Union without first passing through territorial status. At her feet is a California grizzly bear feeding on grapevines, representing the state's wildlife and wine industry. A miner works in the foreground, ships sail on San Francisco Bay, and the Sierra Nevada rises behind. Above appear the word "Eureka" (Greek for "I have found it"), the state motto, and 31 stars representing the states of the Union when California was admitted in 1850.
