Rolls-Royce Motor Cars

Rolls-Royce Motor Cars Limited is a British luxury automobile maker, a wholly owned subsidiary of BMW AG since 2003. It manufactures cars at the Home of Rolls-Royce at Goodwood, West Sussex, England.

The brand descends from Rolls-Royce Limited, founded by Charles Rolls and Henry Royce in 1906 and famed for cars such as the Silver Ghost and Phantom. After the original company's car division passed through Rolls-Royce Motors and Vickers ownership, BMW acquired the rights to the Rolls-Royce name for automobiles in 1998 (Volkswagen took Bentley and the Crewe factory).

Models

The first BMW-era car was the Phantom (2003), followed by the Ghost, Wraith, Dawn, the Cullinan SUV (2018), and the all-electric Spectre (2023). Cars are heavily bespoke; the Spirit of Ecstasy bonnet ornament and coach doors are signature features.
