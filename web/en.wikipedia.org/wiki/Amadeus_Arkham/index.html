Amadeus Arkham

Amadeus Arkham
PublisherDC Comics
First appearanceArkham Asylum: A Serious House on Serious Earth (1989)
Created byGrant Morrison, Dave McKean
Full nameAmadeus Arkham
OccupationPsychiatrist; founder of Arkham Asylum

Amadeus Arkham is a fictional character in DC Comics publications, most notably those featuring the superhero Batman. He is the founder of Arkham Asylum, the psychiatric hospital and prison in Gotham City where many of Batman's most dangerous enemies are confined. Arkham's tragic backstory — in which the psychiatrist who founded the asylum himself descends into madness and becomes one of its inmates — was introduced in the 1989 graphic novel Arkham Asylum: A Serious House on Serious Earth by Grant Morrison and Dave McKean.

In the story, Arkham is motivated by his mother's mental illness and suicide to found an institution for the humane treatment of the criminally insane in his family home on the outskirts of Gotham. After one of his patients, the serial killer Martin "Mad Dog" Hawkins, escapes and murders Arkham's wife and infant daughter, Arkham kills Hawkins during electroshock treatment — and, declared insane himself, dies an inmate of the asylum he created, having carved an occult binding ritual into the floor of his cell with his fingernails.

Fictional character biography

Amadeus Arkham grew up at the Arkham family estate, caring for his mentally ill mother Elizabeth, who believed she was being eaten by insects; Arkham eventually euthanized her and repressed the memory as a natural death. Studying psychiatry, he converted the estate into the Elizabeth Arkham Asylum for the Criminally Insane, treating its first patients in the early 1920s. The murder of his family by Hawkins, and Arkham's revenge killing, complete his own collapse into the madness he sought to cure.

Legacy in the Batman mythos

The asylum's name and cursed character derive from Arkham's story. Later comics expanded the family line, including his descendant Jeremiah Arkham, a psychiatrist who runs the modern asylum. The surname itself was an homage by DC writers to Arkham, the fictional Massachusetts town in the works of H. P. Lovecraft, whose fiction also centres on madness and a notorious sanitarium.

In other media

Amadeus Arkham appears or is referenced in the Batman: Arkham video game series, where the spirit of Arkham and the "Chronicle of Arkham" writings form part of the backstory of Batman: Arkham Asylum (2009), voiced in the game's recordings as a tormented founder figure.
