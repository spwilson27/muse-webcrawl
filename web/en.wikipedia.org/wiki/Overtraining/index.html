Overtraining

Overtraining occurs when a person exceeds their body's ability to recover from strenuous exercise. Overtraining syndrome results in long-term decreased performance capacity, along with symptoms such as persistent fatigue, mood changes, sleep disturbance, frequent illness and loss of motivation.

It arises from an imbalance between training load and recovery, often compounded by inadequate nutrition, rest and psychological stress. Short-term overreaching is a normal, planned part of training, but prolonged overload without recovery can take weeks or months to resolve.

Prevention involves periodised training programmes, adequate sleep and energy intake, monitoring of performance and mood, and scheduled rest days. Treatment is primarily rest and gradual return to activity.
