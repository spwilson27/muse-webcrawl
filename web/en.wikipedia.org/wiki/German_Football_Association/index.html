The German Football Association (German: Deutscher Fußball-Bund, DFB) is the governing body of football, futsal and beach soccer in Germany. Founded in Leipzig in 1900 by representatives of 86 clubs, it is one of the largest national sports federations in the world, with more than seven million members.

Organisation

The DFB is a founding member of both FIFA (1904) and UEFA (1954) and is based in Frankfurt am Main, where its DFB Campus opened in 2022. It comprises five regional associations and 21 state associations. Together with the DFL (Deutsche Fußball Liga), it oversees professional football; the DFB directly runs the 3. Liga, the DFB-Pokal cup competition, women's football and the German national teams. After the Second World War, East Germany had its own association (DFV), which was absorbed into the DFB upon reunification in 1990.

National teams

The men's national team has won the FIFA World Cup four times (1954, 1974, 1990 and 2014) and the European Championship three times. The women's team has won two World Cups (2003, 2007) and eight European Championships, making the DFB the only association whose teams have won both the men's and women's World Cups.
