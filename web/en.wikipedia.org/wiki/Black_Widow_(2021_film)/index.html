Black Widow is a 2021 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character Natasha Romanoff. Produced by Marvel Studios, it is the 24th film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe and the first film of the franchise's Phase Four.

Plot

Set after the events of Captain America: Civil War (2016), the film follows Romanoff on the run, forced to confront her past as a Russian spy trained in the Red Room. Reunited with her surrogate family — Yelena Belova, Alexei Shostakov, and Melina Vostokoff — she sets out to destroy the Red Room and its leader, General Dreykov.

Release and reception

Delayed repeatedly by the COVID-19 pandemic, the film was released simultaneously in cinemas and on Disney+ with Premier Access in July 2021. It grossed over $379 million worldwide and was generally well received, with praise for the performances, particularly Florence Pugh's as Yelena Belova, who continued in later Marvel productions. Scarlett Johansson's lawsuit over the hybrid release was settled with Disney later that year.
