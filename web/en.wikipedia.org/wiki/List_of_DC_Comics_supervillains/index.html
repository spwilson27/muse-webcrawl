List of DC Comics supervillains

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of supervillains appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. DC's villains are among the most recognisable in popular culture, opposing heroes such as Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, and the Flash, and teams such as the Justice League.

Batman villains

Batman's "rogues gallery" is often considered the strongest in comics. Notable entries include the Joker, the Penguin, the Riddler, Catwoman (an anti-heroine as often as a villain), Two-Face, Poison Ivy, Mr. Freeze, the Scarecrow, Bane, Harley Quinn, Ra's al Ghul, and Killer Croc. Many are confined in Gotham City's Arkham Asylum.

Superman villains

Superman's enemies include his arch-nemesis, the billionaire scientist Lex Luthor; the android Brainiac; the Kryptonian General Zod; the monster Doomsday, who killed Superman in the 1992 storyline; the imp Mister Mxyzptlk; and Darkseid, the tyrant god of Apokolips, who is also a principal foe of the Justice League.

Other heroes' villains

Wonder Woman's foes include Ares, Cheetah, and Circe. The Flash faces the "Rogues" — Captain Cold, Mirror Master, Weather Wizard — and the speedster Reverse-Flash. Green Lantern contends with Sinestro, and Aquaman with Black Manta and Ocean Master. Major cosmic and team-level threats include the Anti-Monitor, Vandal Savage, Deathstroke, and the villain organisations the Legion of Doom, the Injustice League, and the Secret Society of Super Villains.
