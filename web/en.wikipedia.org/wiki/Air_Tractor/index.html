Air Tractor Inc. is an American aircraft manufacturer based in Olney, Texas. Founded in 1978 by Leland Snow, the designer of the Snow agricultural aircraft and a founder of Thrush Aircraft, the company builds agricultural aircraft used for crop dusting and aerial application, and derivatives adapted for aerial firefighting and light attack.

Aircraft

Models include the AT-300, AT-401, AT-402, AT-501, AT-502, AT-602, AT-802 and AT-1002 series, ranging from small piston and turboprop crop-dusters to the large AT-802, one of the largest single-engine agricultural aircraft. The AT-802 Fire Boss is an amphibious scooper air tanker for wildfire suppression, and the AT-802U is an armed intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance variant.

Operations

Air Tractor aircraft are exported worldwide. The company is employee-owned, having been sold by Snow to its employees in 2008.
