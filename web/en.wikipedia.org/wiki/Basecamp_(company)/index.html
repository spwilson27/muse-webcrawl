Basecamp (company)

Basecamp, LLC is an American software company based in Chicago, Illinois, best known for the project management and team communication application of the same name. The company was founded in 1999 as 37signals, a web design firm, and renamed itself after its flagship product in 2014. It later reverted to the name 37signals in 2022 while continuing to market Basecamp and the email service HEY.

History

37signals began as a web design consultancy. During internal project work it developed Basecamp, launched in 2004, one of the first hosted project management tools for small businesses. The company became known for its opinionated approach to software and work culture, expressed in books such as Getting Real (2006), Rework (2010), and Remote: Office Not Required (2013).

Ruby on Rails

David Heinemeier Hansson, who joined the company, extracted the Ruby on Rails web framework from the Basecamp codebase and released it as open source in 2004. Rails went on to power many major websites and remains one of the company's most influential contributions.

Business model and culture

The company has remained small, privately held, and deliberately remote-first, and has publicly advocated for calm, bootstrapped business practices over venture-funded growth. In 2020 it launched HEY, a subscription email service. Its "Once" line of pay-once software began with the Campfire group-chat application in 2023.
