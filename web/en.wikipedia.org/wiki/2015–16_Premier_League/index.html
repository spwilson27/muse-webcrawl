The 2015–16 Premier League was the 24th season of the Premier League. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest upsets in sporting history: Leicester City, who had narrowly avoided relegation the previous season and began the campaign as 5,000–1 outsiders, won their first top-flight title under manager Claudio Ranieri. Leicester secured the championship on 2 May 2016 with two matches remaining, when Tottenham Hotspur drew 2–2 at Chelsea, and finished ten points clear of second-placed Arsenal.

Leicester's title

Leicester's counter-attacking side was built around striker Jamie Vardy, who scored in a record eleven consecutive Premier League matches in the autumn, winger Riyad Mahrez, who was voted PFA Players' Player of the Year, and midfielder N'Golo Kanté. Ranieri's team lost only three league matches all season. Meanwhile, defending champions Chelsea endured a disastrous title defence — José Mourinho was dismissed in December with the club 16th — and eventually finished tenth, the worst defence by champions in the Premier League era.

Other stories

Tottenham mounted their strongest title challenge of the Premier League era before falling away in the final fortnight and finishing third, below Arsenal for the 21st consecutive season. Manchester City took the final Champions League place ahead of Manchester United on goal difference in Pep Guardiola's predecessor Manuel Pellegrini's final season. Aston Villa were relegated for the first time in the Premier League era, joined by Norwich City and Newcastle United.
