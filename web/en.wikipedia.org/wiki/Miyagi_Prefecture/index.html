Miyagi Prefecture

Miyagi Prefecture (宮城県, Miyagi-ken) is a prefecture of Japan located in the Tōhoku region of Honshu. Its capital and largest city is Sendai. The prefecture had a population of about 2.3 million and is known for Matsushima, a bay dotted with pine-covered islands counted among Japan's Three Views.

History

Miyagi corresponds largely to the former province of Mutsu and the Date clan's Sendai Domain, founded by Date Masamune. The prefecture took its present form in the Meiji era.

2011 earthquake and tsunami

Miyagi was the prefecture closest to the epicentre of the March 11, 2011 Tōhoku earthquake; the resulting tsunami devastated coastal cities such as Ishinomaki, Kesennuma, and Minamisanriku, causing the majority of the disaster's deaths.

Geography

The Ōu Mountains form the western border; the Pacific coast includes the Sanriku ria coastline to the north and Sendai Bay to the south.
