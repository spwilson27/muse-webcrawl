Polish National Ballet


Polish National Ballet in performance

The Polish National Ballet (Polish: Polski Balet Narodowy) is the largest ballet company in Poland, based at the Teatr Wielki (Grand Theatre) in Warsaw, home of the Polish National Opera. The company traces its origins to the ballet ensembles of the Warsaw stage in the late 18th century, including the troupe of His Majesty's National Dancers established in 1785 under King Stanisław August Poniatowski.

History

Ballet in Warsaw developed through the 19th century at the Teatr Wielki, which opened in 1833. After periods of touring stars and the interruptions of two world wars — the theatre was destroyed in 1944 and rebuilt by 1965 — the resident company performed as the Ballet of the Teatr Wielki. In 2009 it was granted autonomous status and the name Polish National Ballet. Since 2009 the company has been directed by the choreographer Krzysztof Pastor.

Repertoire

The company performs classical ballets such as Swan Lake, The Sleeping Beauty, and The Nutcracker, as well as neoclassical and contemporary works and Pastor's own choreography, including productions based on Polish themes and literature. It tours internationally and maintains a company of over eighty dancers, with an affiliated ballet school.
