A flower is the reproductive structure of flowering plants (angiosperms), the dominant land plants. Flowers contain stamens (male parts making pollen) and carpels (female parts with ovules); after pollination and fertilisation they form seeds and often fruit.

Pollination

Many flowers are pollinated by insects, birds or wind, offering nectar as reward — a coevolutionary partnership central to agriculture, from wheat (wind-pollinated) to orchard fruit. Flowers also carry deep cultural meaning — in art from Petrarch's Laura poems to Monet, and in the funerals and weddings of every city.
