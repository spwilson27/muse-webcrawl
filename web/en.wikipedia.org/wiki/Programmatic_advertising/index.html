Programmatic advertising

Programmatic advertising is the automated buying and selling of online advertising inventory using software, data and algorithms, in contrast to traditional processes involving human negotiation and manual insertion orders. Most programmatic transactions occur through real-time bidding auctions held in the milliseconds while a web page loads.

How it works

Publishers offer impressions through supply-side platforms and ad exchanges; advertisers bid through demand-side platforms using audience data to value each impression. The highest bidder's advertisement is served instantly. Programmatic deals also include private marketplaces and "programmatic guaranteed" arrangements with fixed prices.

Scope and criticism

Programmatic methods account for the majority of digital display advertising spending worldwide and have extended to video, audio, connected television and digital out-of-home media. The ecosystem has been criticised for ad fraud, lack of transparency in fees, brand-safety failures (ads appearing beside harmful content) and privacy concerns arising from behavioural targeting and third-party cookies.
