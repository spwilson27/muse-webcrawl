Apokolips

Apokolips is a fictional planet appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. Created by Jack Kirby for his Fourth World saga, it first appeared in Superman's Pal Jimmy Olsen #134 (1970). Apokolips is the hellish homeworld of the New Gods ruled by the tyrant Darkseid, standing in eternal opposition to the paradise planet New Genesis.

Description

Apokolips is depicted as a vast, industrialised ecumenopolis covered in fire pits, armament factories and the slave district of the Hunger Dogs, ruled from Darkseid's capital. Its society is a totalitarian theocracy dedicated to Darkseid's search for the Anti-Life Equation, which would eliminate free will throughout the universe. Notable inhabitants include Granny Goodness, Desaad, Kalibak, Steppenwolf and the Female Furies; the hero Orion, Darkseid's son, was raised on New Genesis, while Scott Free (Mister Miracle) escaped from Apokolips.

In other media

Apokolips and its armies feature in animated series such as Superman: The Animated Series and Justice League, and in the film Zack Snyder's Justice League (2021).
