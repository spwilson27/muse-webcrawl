Branchial cyst

A branchial cyst, also called a branchial cleft cyst, is a congenital lesion that arises from incomplete obliteration of the branchial (pharyngeal) apparatus during embryonic development. It typically presents as a smooth, painless swelling on the side of the neck, most often along the anterior border of the sternocleidomastoid muscle.

Presentation

Branchial cysts usually appear in late childhood or early adulthood, sometimes becoming noticeable after an upper respiratory infection causes them to swell. Second branchial cleft anomalies are by far the most common type. Related anomalies include branchial sinuses and fistulas, which open to the skin or pharynx.

Treatment

Definitive treatment is complete surgical excision. Infected cysts are first treated with antibiotics (and sometimes drainage) before elective surgery to reduce the risk of recurrence.
