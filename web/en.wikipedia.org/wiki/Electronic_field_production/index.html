Electronic field production

Electronic field production (EFP) is a television industry term referring to a video production which takes place outside a formal television studio, in a practical location or special event venue. EFP ranges from a relatively small crew using a single camera to large multi-camera coverage of major events.

Characteristics

Typical EFP subjects include concerts, sporting events, award shows, conventions, and magazine-style programmes. Unlike electronic news gathering (ENG), which emphasises speed and portability, EFP productions are usually planned in advance with greater production values, lighting, and production control comparable to studio work, but executed on location with portable equipment and, for larger events, outside broadcast trucks.
