Shed

A shed is a simple, typically single-storey outbuilding used for storage, hobbies or as a workshop. Sheds are commonly found in gardens and allotments, and vary from small metal or plastic structures to large timber buildings.

Types and uses

Garden sheds store tools, lawnmowers and bicycles; potting sheds incorporate windows for starting plants; and larger sheds serve as workshops or studios. Industrial and agricultural "sheds" can be very large, such as woolsheds on Australasian sheep stations and train sheds covering railway platforms. In Australia the shed is also a common social and workspace for hobbies, a culture reflected in the Men's Shed movement.

Construction

Sheds are typically built from wood, metal or plastic panels on a simple frame, with a pitched or single-sloped (pent) roof covered in felt, shingles or corrugated sheet. Small sheds are often sold as flat-pack kits that can be assembled without planning permission in many jurisdictions, subject to size and height limits.
