Square mile

The square mile (abbreviated sq mi or mi²) is an imperial and US customary unit of area. It is equal to the area of a square with sides of one mile (5,280 feet) in length. One square mile contains 640 acres and is equal to approximately 2.59 square kilometres.

Conversions

One square mile is equal to:

  • 4 square furlongs
  • 640 acres
  • 27,878,400 square feet
  • 2.58999 square kilometres
  • 258.999 hectares

Usage

The square mile is commonly used in the United States and the United Kingdom for expressing the area of land divisions, counties, states, and other geographic regions, as well as population density (people per square mile). In the US Public Land Survey System, a standard township is 36 square miles, divided into 36 sections of one square mile (640 acres) each.

When used to describe the area of a city or other place, the term "square miles" should not be confused with "miles square", which describes a square with each side measuring the given number of miles; for example, a region "ten miles square" is 100 square miles in area.
