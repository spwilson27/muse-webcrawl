Malala Yousafzai

Malala Yousafzai is a Pakistani activist for girls' education who, at 17 in 2014, became the youngest-ever Nobel Peace Prize laureate, sharing the prize with Indian children's rights activist Kailash Satyarthi.

Story

Malala blogged for the BBC about life under Taliban rule in Pakistan's Swat Valley and spoke publicly for girls' schooling. In 2012 a Taliban gunman shot her on her school bus; she survived after treatment in Pakistan and the United Kingdom, and continued campaigning worldwide through the Malala Fund.
