Cocker Spaniels are dogs belonging to two breeds of the spaniel dog type: the English Cocker Spaniel and the American Cocker Spaniel. They were originally bred as hunting dogs in the United Kingdom, with the term "cocker" deriving from their use to flush woodcock.

History

Spaniels were mentioned in literature as early as the 14th century. By the 19th century, spaniels were divided into field and show types, and the smaller land spaniels used for woodcock became known as cocking or cocker spaniels. The two modern breeds diverged in the 20th century as American breeders favoured a smaller dog with a more domed head and profuse coat.

Temperament

Cocker Spaniels are known for their gentle, affectionate and cheerful temperaments, earning the English breed the nickname "merry cocker". They are intelligent, eager to please, and generally good with children and other animals.

Health

Common health concerns include ear infections (owing to their long, pendulous ears), progressive retinal atrophy, hip dysplasia and, in some lines, rage syndrome.
