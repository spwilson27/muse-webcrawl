Nanny McPhee and the Big Bang

Nanny McPhee and the Big Bang (released in the United States as Nanny McPhee Returns) is a 2010 British fantasy comedy film directed by Susanna White, written by and starring Emma Thompson, and based on Christianna Brand's Nurse Matilda books.

Plot

During the Second World War, Isabel Green struggles to run the family farm while her husband is at war, coping with her three unruly children, their spoiled evacuated cousins, and her brother-in-law's scheme to force her to sell. The mysterious Nanny McPhee arrives to teach the children five lessons, with Maggie Gyllenhaal, Rhys Ifans, Maggie Smith and Ralph Fiennes in supporting roles.

Reception

The sequel to Nanny McPhee (2005), the film was a modest box-office success and received generally favourable reviews for its cast and visual style.
