Chernobyl

Chernobyl is a partially abandoned city in northern Ukraine, near the border with Belarus. It is known internationally as the site, together with the nearby city of Pripyat, of the Chernobyl disaster of 26 April 1986, when reactor No. 4 of the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant exploded during a safety test, releasing large quantities of radioactive material across Europe.

History

First mentioned in the 12th century, Chernobyl was a small town for most of its history. In the 1970s the Soviet Union built the nuclear power plant about 15 kilometres northwest of the town, and the purpose-built city of Pripyat housed its workers. After the 1986 accident, Chernobyl's roughly 14,000 residents were evacuated.

Exclusion Zone

The surrounding Chernobyl Exclusion Zone remains restricted, though some workers and a small number of returning residents live in the area, and the site has become a destination for controlled tourism.
