Transport in Bangladesh

Transport in Bangladesh is dominated by road and river transport in a densely populated delta country. Since independence in 1971, the transport sector has expanded greatly, though infrastructure quality remains a major constraint on development. Dhaka, the capital, is famous as the "rickshaw capital of the world", with hundreds of thousands of cycle rickshaws, and severe traffic congestion.

Roads

Bangladesh has roughly 370,000 km of roads, of which the national highways form the backbone. Major structures include the Jamuna Bridge (Bangabandhu Bridge), opened in 1998 linking east and west across the Jamuna River, and the Padma Bridge, opened in June 2022, a road-rail bridge linking the southwest to Dhaka. Buses carry the majority of intercity passengers; in Dhaka, rickshaws, auto-rickshaws (CNGs), buses and a growing ride-hailing sector share congested streets. The Dhaka Metro Rail (MRT Line 6) opened in December 2022, the country's first metro service.

Railways

Bangladesh Railway operates about 3,600 km of track in two largely separate networks divided by the Jamuna River: a broad-gauge (1,676 mm) system in the west and a metre-gauge (1,000 mm) system in the east, with dual-gauge sections around Dhaka and the Jamuna Bridge. Rail carries freight and intercity and commuter passengers, with services centred on Dhaka, Chattogram, Khulna, Rajshahi and Sylhet.

Water transport

The Ganges–Brahmaputra delta gives Bangladesh some 24,000 km of navigable waterways (about 6,000 km in the monsoon season commonly usable), and inland waterways carry a large share of freight and passengers. Major river ports include Dhaka (Sadarghat), Narayanganj, Chandpur and Barisal. The seaports of Chattogram (Chittagong) and Mongla handle nearly all foreign trade, with a deep-sea port under development at Matarbari.

Air transport

Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport in Dhaka is the main gateway; other international airports serve Chattogram and Sylhet. The flag carrier is Biman Bangladesh Airlines, alongside private carriers such as US-Bangla Airlines and Novoair.
