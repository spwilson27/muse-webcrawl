Steeleye Span is an English folk rock band formed in 1969 in London. Along with Fairport Convention, they are among the best-known acts of the British folk revival, specialising in electric arrangements of traditional songs and ballads from Britain and Ireland. The band's name comes from a character, John "Steeleye" Span, in the Lincolnshire folk song "Horkstow Grange".

History

The band was formed by bassist Ashley Hutchings, who had just left Fairport Convention, together with the duo Tim Hart and Maddy Prior. Guitarist Terry Woods and his wife Gay Woods completed the first line-up, which recorded Hark! The Village Wait (1970). Hutchings left in 1971 (later replaced by Rick Kemp), and fiddler Peter Knight joined, shaping the classic folk-rock sound of albums such as Below the Salt (1972) and Parcel of Rogues (1973).

Commercial success

Steeleye Span achieved two unusual chart hits with traditional material: the Latin Christmas carol "Gaudete", sung a cappella, reached No. 14 in the UK in 1973, and "All Around My Hat" reached No. 5 in 1975, becoming a gold-selling single; the album of the same name was their biggest seller. The band broke up in 1978 but reformed in 1980 and has continued, with many changes of personnel, ever since — Maddy Prior remaining the most constant presence. Notable later albums include Time (1996) and collaborations such as the Wintersmith album (2013) with author Terry Pratchett, based on his novel.
