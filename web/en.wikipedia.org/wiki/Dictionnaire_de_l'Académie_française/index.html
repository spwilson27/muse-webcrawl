The Dictionnaire de l'Académie française is the official dictionary of the French language published by the Académie française. The Academy was charged at its foundation in 1635 with producing a dictionary that would codify and regulate French usage; the first edition appeared in 1694.

Editions

Successive editions appeared in 1718, 1740, 1762, 1798, 1835, 1878, and 1932–35 (eighth edition). The ninth edition, begun in the 1980s, was published volume by volume over three decades and completed in 2024, when the final volume was presented. The dictionary is deliberately conservative and prescriptive: it registers the vocabulary of "good usage", often lagging behind everyday language, and the Academy's rulings carry symbolic authority rather than legal force.
