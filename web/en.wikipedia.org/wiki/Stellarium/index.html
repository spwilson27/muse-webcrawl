Stellarium

Stellarium is a free and open-source planetarium software program, licensed under the GNU General Public License, that shows a realistic 3D simulation of the night sky as seen from any location on Earth at any date and time. It was originally developed by French programmer Fabien Chéreau, who launched the project in 2001, and is maintained by an international team.

Features

Stellarium's default catalogue includes more than 600,000 stars, with add-on catalogues reaching over a billion, plus planets, moons, nebulae, galaxies and artificial satellites. It renders atmospheric effects, light pollution settings, constellation art from many cultures ("sky cultures"), and can project onto planetarium domes via fisheye and spherical mirror modes. Plugins add telescope control, ocular views and historical supernovae, among other tools.

Use

The software is widely used by amateur astronomers, educators and digital planetariums, and runs on Linux, Windows, macOS, and as mobile apps and a web version.
