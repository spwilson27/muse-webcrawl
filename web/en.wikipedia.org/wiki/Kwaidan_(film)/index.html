Kwaidan (film)

Directed byMasaki Kobayashi
Released1964 (Japan)
Running time183 minutes

Kwaidan ("Ghost Stories") is a 1964 Japanese anthology horror film directed by Masaki Kobayashi, based on stories from Lafcadio Hearn's collections of Japanese folk tales, principally Kwaidan: Stories and Studies of Strange Things (1904).

Segments

The film comprises four tales: "The Black Hair", about a swordsman haunted by his first wife; "The Woman of the Snow", in which a woodcutter encounters the yuki-onna; "Hoichi the Earless", about a blind biwa player who performs for the ghosts of the Taira clan; and "In a Cup of Tea", an unfinished story about a writer whose character sees a soul reflected in tea.

Reception

The film won the Special Jury Prize at the 1965 Cannes Film Festival and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film. Its stylised, painterly sets and Toru Takemitsu's score are widely celebrated.
