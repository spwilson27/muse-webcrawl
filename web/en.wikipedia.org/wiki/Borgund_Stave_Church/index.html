Borgund Stave Church

Borgund Stave Church
Location: Borgund, Lærdal, Vestland, Norway
Built: c. 1180–1250
Type: Stave church, museum

Borgund Stave Church (Norwegian: Borgund stavkyrkje) is a former parish church of the Church of Norway in Lærdal Municipality, Vestland county. Built around 1180–1250, it is one of Norway's best-preserved stave churches.

Architecture

The church features tiered roofs with dragon heads, a raised central nave surrounded by an ambulatory, stave posts, and carved portals mixing Christian and Norse motifs. Runic inscriptions include 'Ave Maria' and names of visitors.

History

Dedicated to Saint Andrew, it served as a parish church until 1868, when a new church was built nearby. It is now a museum owned by the Society for the Preservation of Ancient Norwegian Monuments.

Influence

Its copies exist in Norway, Germany, and at Epcot in Florida; it inspired designs in popular culture.
