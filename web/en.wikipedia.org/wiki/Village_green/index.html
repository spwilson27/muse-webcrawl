Village green

A village green is a common open area of grassland within a settlement, traditionally used for grazing, sports, fairs and community gatherings. In England and Wales, village greens enjoy legal protection as registered common land.

History

Greens originated as shared grazing at village centres, hosting markets, maypoles, stocks and cricket — the game developed on such greens. The Commons Registration Act 1965 and the Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000 protect registered greens from development; land used "as of right" for lawful sports and pastimes for 20 years may qualify for registration.

Around the world

New England's town commons descend from the same tradition; central squares and Maidan-like grounds serve analogous roles elsewhere.
