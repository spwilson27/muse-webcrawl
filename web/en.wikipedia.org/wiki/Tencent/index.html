Tencent Holdings Ltd. is a Chinese multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in Shenzhen. Founded in 1998 by Ma Huateng and colleagues, Tencent is among the world's largest companies by market capitalization and one of the largest venture capital and investment firms. It is listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.

Products and services

Tencent's first successful product was the instant-messaging service QQ (launched 1999 as OICQ). Its super-app WeChat (Weixin), launched in 2011, grew to over a billion monthly users and integrates messaging, social media, mobile payments (WeChat Pay), and mini-programs. Tencent is the world's largest video-game vendor, owning Riot Games (League of Legends), the hit mobile title Honor of Kings, and major stakes in Epic Games, Supercell, and other developers. Other operations include Tencent Video, Tencent Music, QQ Browser, cloud computing, and fintech.

Investments

Through hundreds of investments, Tencent has taken stakes in companies including JD.com, Meituan, Pinduoduo, Snap, Spotify (via a share swap with Tencent Music), Tesla, and Reddit. Its largest shareholder has historically been the South African group Naspers and its listed subsidiary Prosus.

Regulation

From 2020 China tightened regulation of its technology sector, freezing new game approvals for extended periods and restricting minors' playing time; Tencent's share price fell sharply during the crackdown before partially recovering. Tencent has also faced antitrust penalties, including the blocking of its proposed merger of the Huya and DouYu streaming platforms in 2021.
