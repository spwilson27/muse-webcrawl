An Evening with Silk Sonic is the debut studio album by American musical superduo Silk Sonic, composed of Bruno Mars and Anderson .Paak. Released on 12 November 2021, it is a homage to 1970s R&B, soul and funk, framed as a live show "hosted" by funk legend Bootsy Collins, who also coined the duo's name.

Background and singles

Mars and .Paak met while touring together in 2017 and began collaborating in the studio, drawing on the sounds of Motown, Philadelphia soul and artists such as Stevie Wonder and Earth, Wind & Fire. The lead single, "Leave the Door Open" (March 2021), reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 and won four Grammy Awards, including Record of the Year and Song of the Year. Further singles were "Skate" and "Smokin Out the Window".

Reception

The album debuted at number two on the Billboard 200 and received widespread critical acclaim for its musicianship, production and faithful recreation of the 1970s soul sound. The duo supported it with a Las Vegas residency, "An Evening with Silk Sonic at Park MGM", in 2022.
