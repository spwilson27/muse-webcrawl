Dexter Fletcher (born 31 January 1966) is an English actor and film director. He began his career as a child actor, appearing as Baby Face in Bugsy Malone (1976), and gained wider recognition as a teenager in the television series Press Gang. As an adult he appeared in films including The Long Good Friday (1980), Caravaggio (1986), Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels (1998) and the television miniseries Band of Brothers (2001).

Directing career

Fletcher made his directorial debut with Wild Bill (2011), which was nominated for a BAFTA. He followed it with the musical Sunshine on Leith (2013) and the sports biopic Eddie the Eagle (2016). In 2017 he took over direction of the Queen biographical film Bohemian Rhapsody after the departure of Bryan Singer, though Singer retained the directing credit. His next film, the Elton John musical fantasy Rocketman (2019), was a critical and commercial success. He later directed The Offer miniseries episodes and the action film Ghosted (2023).
