Michael David Apted CMG (10 February 1941 – 7 January 2021) was an English television and film director and producer. He is best known for directing the Up documentary series (1964–2019), which followed the lives of fourteen British children every seven years from age seven, and for the James Bond film The World Is Not Enough (1999).

Career

Apted began at Granada Television, where he worked on Coronation Street and helped cast the children for Seven Up! (1964), taking over as director from 7 Plus Seven onward. His feature films include Coal Miner's Daughter (1980), Gorillas in the Mist (1988), Nell (1994), Enigma (2001), and The Chronicles of Narnia: The Voyage of the Dawn Treader (2010).

Honours

He served as president of the Directors Guild of America from 2003 to 2009 and was appointed a Companion of the Order of St Michael and St George in 2008. The Up series is frequently ranked among the greatest documentaries ever made.
