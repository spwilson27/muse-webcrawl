JetBrains s.r.o. (formerly IntelliJ Software s.r.o.) is a Czech software development company founded in Prague in 2000, best known for its integrated development environments (IDEs) and the Kotlin programming language. Its tools are used by millions of developers worldwide.

History

JetBrains was founded by three Russian software developers, Sergey Dmitriev, Valentin Kipiatkov and Eugene Belyaev. Its first product was IntelliJ Renamer, followed in 2001 by IntelliJ IDEA, a Java IDE praised for its code analysis and refactoring. Over the following decades the company released a family of language-specific IDEs — PyCharm (Python), WebStorm (JavaScript), PhpStorm, RubyMine, GoLand, Rider (.NET) and CLion (C/C++) — sharing the IntelliJ platform, as well as the .NET tool ReSharper, the continuous-integration server TeamCity, and the issue tracker YouTrack.

Kotlin

In 2011 JetBrains unveiled Kotlin, a statically typed programming language for the JVM designed to interoperate fully with Java. Google announced first-class support for Kotlin in Android development in 2017, and in 2019 named it the preferred language for Android app development.

Company

JetBrains is privately held and has offices in several countries; after Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine the company suspended its operations in Russia and Belarus. Its products are sold mainly by subscription, with free community editions and licences for students and open-source projects.
