Acer Inc.

Acer Inc. is a Taiwanese multinational hardware and electronics corporation specialising in personal computers, displays, servers and related technology products. Founded in 1976 by Stan Shih and others as Multitech, it adopted the Acer name in 1987 and is headquartered in New Taipei City, Taiwan.

Acer became one of the world's largest PC vendors, growing through acquisitions that included Gateway, Inc. and Packard Bell in 2007–2008. Its product lines include Aspire laptops and desktops, Predator gaming hardware, TravelMate business notebooks and Chromebooks, of which it has been a leading supplier.

The company also sells monitors, projectors and peripherals, and has expanded into lifestyle and e-mobility products under various subsidiaries.
