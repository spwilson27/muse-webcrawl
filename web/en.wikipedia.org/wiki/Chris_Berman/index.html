Chris Berman

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Christopher James Berman (born May 10, 1955), nicknamed "Boomer", is an American sportscaster. He has been an anchor for SportsCenter on ESPN since 1979, joining a month after its initial launch, and hosted the network's Sunday NFL Countdown program from 1985 to 2016. He is one of ESPN's longest-tenured employees and the first ESPN personality to have had a role in the network's flagship NFL and MLB coverage across five decades.

Career

Berman, a graduate of Brown University, began his career at radio and television stations in Connecticut before joining ESPN in October 1979. He became famous for his energetic delivery, his signature home-run call "back, back, back, back… gone!", his "whoop!" during highlight reels, and the punning nicknames he gave athletes, such as "Bert 'Be Home' Blyleven" and "Jake 'Daylight Come and You Gotta' Delhomme".

He hosted Monday Night Countdown, covered the Super Bowl for ABC and ESPN, and was the longtime studio host of ESPN's baseball coverage. In 2017 he stepped back from several roles but continued as host of NFL Primetime and other specials.

Honors

Berman received the Pete Rozelle Radio-Television Award from the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2018 and was inducted into the Sports Broadcasting Hall of Fame. He has a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame.
