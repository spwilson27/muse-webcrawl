Kamsarakan

The Kamsarakan (Armenian: Կամսարական) were a noble family of Armenia, one of the prominent nakharar houses of the late antique and early medieval period. They claimed descent from the Arsacid royal house, through a prince named Kamsar who, according to tradition, fled persecution and settled in Armenia.

Domains

The family's hereditary lands lay in the districts of Shirak and Ars sharunik in the region of Ayrarat, in the northwest of historic Armenia. Their principal seats included the fortress town of Eruandashat and the city of Ani's surrounding district; members of the house were closely associated with the early Christianisation of Armenia.

History

Kamsarakan nobles appear frequently in the histories of the fourth to eighth centuries, serving as generals and courtiers under Arsacid kings and later under Persian and Byzantine overlordship. Gregory the Illuminator is said to have had a Kamsarakan wife, and the family supported the Armenian Church. The house declined after the Arab conquests of the seventh century, and its remaining lands eventually passed to the Bagratuni family.
