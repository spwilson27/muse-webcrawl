POSTECH (Pohang University of Science and Technology) is a private research university in Pohang, South Korea, dedicated to science and engineering. It was founded in 1986 by the steel company POSCO under founding president Kim Ho-gil, a physicist, with the aim of providing elite, research-focused education with small class sizes.

The POSTECH campus in Pohang

History and profile

POSTECH admitted its first undergraduate students in 1987 and deliberately kept enrolment small — a few hundred students per year — with a very high faculty-to-student ratio and generous scholarships. Courses are largely taught in English. The university operates major research facilities including the Pohang Accelerator Laboratory, home to the PAL-XFEL X-ray free-electron laser, one of only a few such facilities worldwide.

Reputation

Despite its youth and small size, POSTECH consistently ranks among the top universities in South Korea and Asia and performs strongly in per-capita research measures, and it was ranked first in the Times Higher Education list of the world's top young universities for several years in the 2010s.
