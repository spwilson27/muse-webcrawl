Tank Girl

Tank Girl is a British comic book created in 1988 by artist Jamie Hewlett and writer Alan Martin. Set in a post-apocalyptic Australia, it follows the anarchic adventures of Rebecca Buck — "Tank Girl" — who lives in and drives a tank, accompanied by her mutant kangaroo boyfriend Booga.

Publication history

The strip debuted in the first issue of Deadline magazine in 1988 and quickly became a cult phenomenon of British alternative comics, mixing punk attitude, surreal humour, and pop-culture collage. Collections and later series were published by Dark Horse, Vertigo, IDW, and Titan Comics, with additional artists and writers over the decades.

Film adaptation

A feature film, Tank Girl (1995), directed by Rachel Talalay and starring Lori Petty, was a commercial failure on release but developed a cult following. The character remains an icon of 1990s alternative culture, and Hewlett went on to co-create the virtual band Gorillaz.
