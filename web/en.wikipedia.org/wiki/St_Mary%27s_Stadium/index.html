St Mary's Stadium is a football stadium in Southampton, England, which has been the home stadium of Southampton F.C. since 2001. With a capacity of over 32,000, it is the largest football stadium in South East England outside London.

History

Southampton moved to St Mary's from The Dell, their home for over a century, in August 2001. The stadium is named after St Mary's Church, near which the club was founded in 1885 by members of the St Mary's Church of England Young Men's Association.

Design

The ground is a complete bowl with four stands of roughly equal height: the Itchen, Kingsland, Northam and Chapel stands, named after local areas and features. A statue of club legend Ted Bates stands outside the stadium.

Other events

The stadium has hosted England international matches, European football, concerts and other sporting events.
