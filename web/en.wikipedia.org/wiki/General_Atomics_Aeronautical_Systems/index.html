General Atomics Aeronautical Systems

General Atomics Aeronautical Systems, Inc. (GA-ASI) is an American technology company based in San Diego, California, best known for designing and manufacturing unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), including the MQ-1 Predator and the MQ-9 Reaper. It is a subsidiary of the privately held General Atomics corporation.

Industry: Aerospace and defense
Headquarters: San Diego, California, United States
Parent: General Atomics
Products: Unmanned aerial vehicles, radar, electro-optical sensors

History

The company's roots lie in Leading Systems Inc., founded by Abraham Karem, a pioneer of long-endurance drone design. General Atomics acquired the business in the early 1990s and developed it into GA-ASI. Its Avenger (Predator C) jet-powered UAV extended the Predator family into faster, stealthier designs.

Products

GA-ASI's product line includes the MQ-1 Predator, MQ-9 Reaper, MQ-1C Gray Eagle, MQ-20 Avenger, the MQ-9B SkyGuardian and SeaGuardian, and smaller systems such as the GNAT and Altair. The company also produces the Lynx multi-mode radar and ground control stations used to operate its aircraft from satellite links.

Operations

GA-ASI aircraft are flown by the United States Air Force, the U.S. Army, the Royal Air Force, and numerous allied air forces. Its flight test facilities include Gray Butte Field Airport near Palmdale, California.
