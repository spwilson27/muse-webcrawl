Mark Edward Whitacre (born 1957) is an American business executive who, while a senior executive at Archer Daniels Midland (ADM), became the highest-ranking corporate whistleblower in FBI history, exposing the lysine price-fixing conspiracy of the 1990s.

The ADM scandal

Whitacre, president of ADM's BioProducts division, wore a wire for nearly three years from 1992, recording cartel meetings with Japanese and Korean competitors. The investigation led to ADM paying a then-record 00 million fine and prison terms for senior executives.

Prison and aftermath

Whitacre himself was convicted of embezzling .5 million from ADM during the investigation and served about eight and a half years in federal prison. His story was told in Kurt Eichenwald's book The Informant! and the 2009 film of the same name starring Matt Damon. After release he rebuilt a career in biotechnology and speaks publicly about ethics and redemption.
