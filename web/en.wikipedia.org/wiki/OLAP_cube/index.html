OLAP cube

An OLAP cube is a multi-dimensional array of data used in online analytical processing (OLAP). It organises data along multiple dimensions—such as product, region and time—so that measures like sales revenue can be quickly aggregated and examined from different perspectives. Despite the name, a cube may have fewer or more than three dimensions; it is also called a hypercube when there are more than three.

Operations

Cubes support characteristic operations: slicing selects a single value of one dimension, dicing selects sub-ranges on several dimensions, drill-down and roll-up move between levels of detail along a hierarchy (for example from year to month), and pivoting rotates the axes of the display. Cubes are built from fact tables and dimension tables in a data warehouse, often in a star schema, and may be implemented relationally (ROLAP), multidimensionally (MOLAP), or in hybrid form (HOLAP).

Query language

The standard query language for OLAP cubes is MDX (MultiDimensional eXpressions), originally developed by Microsoft for its OLAP Services and Analysis Services products. Cubes underlie many business intelligence dashboards and reporting tools.
