List of rivers of Asia

Asia is home to some of the world's longest and most populous river systems. The Yangtze is the longest river in Asia and the third-longest in the world; the Yellow River, Mekong, Ganges and Indus support the agriculture and cities of hundreds of millions of people.


The Yangtze at the Three Gorges — Asia's longest river.
