501(c) organization

A 501(c) organization is a nonprofit organization in the United States federal law that is exempt from some federal income taxes. Section 501(c) of the United States Internal Revenue Code (26 U.S.C. § 501(c)) lists types of organizations — subdivided in over 29 paragraphs, from 501(c)(1) through 501(c)(29) — that qualify for exemption. The best known is 501(c)(3), covering religious, charitable, scientific, educational, and similar organizations, contributions to which are generally tax-deductible for donors.

Types

Major categories include:

  • 501(c)(3) — religious, educational, charitable, scientific, literary, and similar organizations; includes most public charities and private foundations.
  • 501(c)(4) — civic leagues and social welfare organizations; may engage in some political activity and substantial lobbying, but donations are generally not tax-deductible.
  • 501(c)(5) — labor, agricultural, and horticultural organizations.
  • 501(c)(6) — business leagues, chambers of commerce, and trade associations.
  • 501(c)(7) — social and recreational clubs.
  • 501(c)(19) — veterans' organizations.

Requirements

To obtain recognition of exemption, most organizations must apply to the Internal Revenue Service, typically using Form 1023 for 501(c)(3) status; churches and certain small organizations are exceptions. Exempt organizations must be organized and operated exclusively for exempt purposes, and no part of their net earnings may inure to the benefit of private shareholders or individuals. Most must file an annual information return, Form 990, and revocation is automatic after three consecutive years of failure to file.

Political activity

Section 501(c)(3) organizations are absolutely prohibited from intervening in political campaigns for or against candidates — a restriction known as the Johnson Amendment (1954) — and may engage in only limited lobbying. Other 501(c) categories face fewer restrictions, which has made 501(c)(4) groups prominent vehicles for politically oriented spending.
