Tonight's the Night (Neil Young album)

Tonight's the Night is the sixth studio album by Canadian-American singer-songwriter Neil Young, released in June 1975 on Reprise Records, though most of it was recorded in August–September 1973.

The album was recorded in the aftermath of the drug-related deaths of Crazy Horse guitarist Danny Whitten and roadie Bruce Berry, and its songs are suffused with grief, weariness, and ragged, late-night despair. Tracks include "Tonight's the Night", "Speakin' Out", "World on a String", "Albuquerque", "Mellow My Mind", and "Tired Eyes". Young's record label initially resisted releasing the raw, unpolished sessions, and the album appeared only after the release of On the Beach.

Reception and legacy

Although initially received coolly, Tonight's the Night is now widely regarded as one of Young's greatest albums and one of the finest records of the 1970s; it has been placed on numerous critics' lists of the best albums of all time. Its loose, unvarnished performances were a deliberate rejection of the polished sound of Young's then-recent commercial success.
