Philip Treacy

Philip Anthony Treacy OBE (born 26 May 1967) is an Irish haute couture milliner, or hat designer, based in London. He has designed hats for many well-known clients, including members of the British royal family, and for fashion houses such as Alexander McQueen, Chanel and Valentino.

Treacy studied fashion at the National College of Art and Design in Dublin and later at the Royal College of Art in London, where his graduate collection attracted the attention of Isabella Blow, who became an important patron and friend. His sculptural, often surreal hats revived interest in millinery as high fashion in the 1990s.
