Joel Thomas Zimmerman (born January 5, 1981), known professionally as Deadmau5 (stylized in all lowercase; pronounced "dead mouse"), is a Canadian electronic music producer and DJ. He is known for his progressive house productions, his elaborate live shows featuring the "Cube" stage design, and his oversized mouse-head costume, the "mau5head." He founded the record label Mau5trap in 2007.

Career

Zimmerman adopted his stage name after finding a dead mouse inside his computer while changing a video card, shortening "dead mouse" using leetspeak. He gained recognition with tracks such as "Faxing Berlin" and rose to prominence with the albums Random Album Title (2008) and For Lack of a Better Name (2009), the latter including "Ghosts 'n' Stuff" and "Strobe," the latter frequently cited among the greatest electronic tracks of all time. Subsequent albums include 4×4=12 (2010), Album Title Goes Here (2012), While(1<2) (2014), and W:/2016Album/ (2016).

Live performances and technology

Deadmau5 is noted for technically elaborate tours and for livestreaming his studio work. His "Cube" and later "Cube V3" productions featured a large rotating, LED-covered structure. He has been a prominent and outspoken figure in electronic music culture, known for candid criticism of industry practices, and has received multiple Grammy Award nominations, including for Best Dance/Electronic Album.

Collaborations

He has collaborated with artists including Rob Swire, Wolfgang Gartner, Kaskade (including as one half of the duo Kx5 with their 2023 album), and The Neptunes, and has remixed numerous acts. His label Mau5trap has released music by artists such as Skrillex (early in his career), Rezz, and Feed Me.
