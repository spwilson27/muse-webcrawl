The FA Cup, officially the Football Association Challenge Cup, is an annual knockout association football competition in English football. First played during the 1871–72 season, it is the oldest national football competition in the world. It is open to clubs across the English football pyramid, from the Premier League to village sides entering the qualifying rounds, and is famous for "giant-killings" in which lower-division clubs eliminate top-flight teams.

The first winners were Wanderers, in a final played at Kennington Oval; the final moved to the original Wembley in 1923 — the "White Horse Final" — and has been held at the new Wembley since 2007. Arsenal is the record holder with 14 wins; Manchester United has 13. In 2025, Crystal Palace beat Manchester City 1–0 to win the first major trophy in the club's history, Eberechi Eze scoring the goal.
