Gävle

Gävle is a city in Sweden, the seat of Gävle Municipality and the capital of Gävleborg County. It is the oldest city in Norrland, having received its charter in 1446, and lies at the mouth of the Gavleån river on the Gulf of Bothnia.

History

Gävle developed as a fishing and trading port, exporting iron and timber. Much of the city south of the river was destroyed by fire in 1869 and rebuilt with wide streets and esplanades as firebreaks.

Gävle goat

The city is internationally known for the Gävle goat, a giant straw Yule goat erected annually since 1966 in Castle Square — famous in part because it has so often been burned down by vandals before Christmas.

Economy and culture

Gävle is home to the coffee roaster Gevalia, a major port, the University of Gävle, a railway museum, and the ice hockey club Brynäs IF.
