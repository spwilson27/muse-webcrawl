Adam Back (born July 1970) is a British cryptographer and cypherpunk. He is best known for inventing Hashcash, a proof-of-work system later used in a similar form by Bitcoin for mining and limiting email spam and denial-of-service attacks. He is the CEO and co-founder of Blockstream, a company providing blockchain technologies founded in 2014.

Career

Back received a PhD in computer science from the University of Exeter. In 1997 he proposed Hashcash, which required senders to perform a modest computational puzzle whose result could be cheaply verified. His work was cited in the Bitcoin white paper by Satoshi Nakamoto, one of only a handful of references in the paper.

Blockstream

With Blockstream, Back has worked on sidechains, the Liquid Network, and Bitcoin infrastructure. He is a prominent voice in debates about Bitcoin protocol development and block size.
