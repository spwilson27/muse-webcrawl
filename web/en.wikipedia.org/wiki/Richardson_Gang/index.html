Richardson Gang

Founded: 1950s
Founders: Charlie Richardson, Eddie Richardson
Territory: South London
Activities: Fraud, protection rackets, scrap metal, torture
Rivals: Kray twins

The Richardson Gang, led by brothers Charlie and Eddie Richardson, was an English crime gang based in South London in the 1950s and 1960s. Rivals of the Kray twins of the East End, the Richardsons built their power on scrap metal dealing, fruit machines, protection rackets and elaborate frauds.

Torture gang

The gang became infamous for torturing debtors and rivals — using electric shocks, pulling teeth and nailing victims to floors — earning them the press label "the Torture Gang". In 1966–67, following testimony from former associates, Charlie Richardson was convicted of grievous bodily harm and sentenced to 25 years in prison; Eddie and other members also received long sentences.

Legacy

Charlie Richardson served much of his sentence and was released in 1984; he died in 2012. The gang's feud with the Krays and its association with the South African "Mad" Frankie Fraser remain staples of London criminal folklore.
