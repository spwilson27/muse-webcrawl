Regions of Eritrea

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
The six regions (zobas) of Eritrea

The regions of Eritrea, known locally as zobas (Tigrinya: ዞባ), are the first-level administrative divisions of the State of Eritrea. There are six regions, established in April 1996 when the ten provinces inherited from the Ethiopian period were reorganised. Each region is administered by an elected regional assembly and a governor appointed within the regional administration, and is further divided into subregions (sub-zobas) and local areas.

History

During the Italian colonial period Eritrea was divided into commissariats; under Ethiopian rule it was organised into provinces (Awrajja and later Kifle Hager divisions). At independence in 1993 Eritrea had ten provinces. The 1996 reorganisation consolidated these into the present six regions, drawn partly along ethnic and geographic lines. The regions administer local services under the central government, as Eritrea is a unitary state.
