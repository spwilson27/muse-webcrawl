God Help the Girl is a 2014 British musical romantic drama film written and directed by Stuart Murdoch of the band Belle and Sebastian. It follows three friends who form a band in Glasgow over one summer. The film stars Emily Browning, Olly Alexander (of Years & Years), and Hannah Murray.

The project began as a musical side project for Murdoch, who in 2009 released an album also titled God Help the Girl, featuring various female vocalists performing songs he had written for a planned film. The film was partly crowdfunded via Kickstarter and premiered at the Sundance Film Festival in 2014, where it won a Special Jury Prize.

Plot

Eve, an Australian girl recovering from an eating disorder in a Glasgow psychiatric clinic, escapes and finds solace in music. She befriends James, a lifeguard and aspiring musician, and his guitar student Cassie. The three form a band and spend the summer making music, while Eve struggles with her illness and her feelings about returning to Australia when her visa expires.

Music

The soundtrack features songs written by Murdoch, performed by the cast and by members of Belle and Sebastian. Several songs were reworked from the 2009 concept album of the same name.

Reception

The film received mixed reviews, with praise for its music, performances and visual style, and criticism directed at its lightweight narrative.
