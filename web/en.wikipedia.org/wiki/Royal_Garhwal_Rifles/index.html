Royal Garhwal Rifles

The Garhwal Rifles, formerly the Royal Garhwal Rifles, is an infantry regiment of the Indian Army. It was raised in 1887 as the 39th (Garhwal) Regiment of Bengal Infantry.

History

Recruited from the Garhwali people of Uttarakhand, the regiment served with distinction in both World Wars. At Neuve-Chapelle in 1915, Naik Darwan Singh Negi and Rifleman Gabar Singh Negi won the Victoria Cross. The title "Royal" was awarded in 1921 for First World War service. After Indian independence in 1947 the regiment joined the Indian Army and has served in subsequent conflicts, with its regimental centre at Lansdowne.

Its war cry is "Bajrang Bali Ki Jai" and "Durga Nandini".
