List of Holy Roman Emperors

The Holy Roman Emperor was the ruler of the Holy Roman Empire, crowned — in theory — by the Pope in Rome. The list runs from Charlemagne, crowned on Christmas Day 800, to Francis II, who abdicated in 1806 during the Napoleonic Wars. Dating conventions vary: some lists begin with Otto I (962), the first emperor of the revived medieval empire.

Carolingians and early emperors (800–962)

  • Charlemagne (800–814)
  • Louis the Pious (816–840)
  • Lothair I (840–855)
  • Louis II (855–875)
  • Charles the Bald (875–877)
  • Charles the Fat (881–888)
  • Arnulf of Carinthia (896–899)
  • Berengar I of Italy (915–924)

Ottonian dynasty (962–1024)

  • Otto I "the Great" (962–973)
  • Otto II (973–983)
  • Otto III (996–1002)
  • Henry II (1014–1024), later canonised

Salian dynasty (1024–1125)

  • Conrad II (1027–1039)
  • Henry III (1046–1056)
  • Henry IV (1084–1105)
  • Henry V (1111–1125)

Hohenstaufen (1138–1254)

  • Conrad III (king; never crowned emperor)
  • Frederick I "Barbarossa" (1155–1190)
  • Henry VI (1191–1197)
  • Otto IV (1209–1215)
  • Frederick II "Stupor Mundi" (1220–1250)

Late medieval emperors (1273–1493)

  • Rudolf I of Habsburg (king, 1273–1291)
  • Henry VII of Luxembourg (1312–1313)
  • Louis IV of Bavaria (1328–1347)
  • Charles IV (1355–1378) — issued the Golden Bull of 1356
  • Sigismund (1433–1437)
  • Frederick III (1452–1493) — first Habsburg emperor of the continuous line

Habsburg emperors (1493–1806)

  • Maximilian I (1508–1519) — first to style himself "elected emperor"
  • Charles V (1530–1556) — also king of Spain; abdicated
  • Ferdinand I (1558–1564)
  • Maximilian II (1564–1576)
  • Rudolf II (1576–1612)
  • Ferdinand II (1619–1637)
  • Ferdinand III (1637–1657)
  • Leopold I (1658–1705)
  • Joseph I (1705–1711)
  • Charles VI (1711–1740) — Pragmatic Sanction
  • Charles VII of Bavaria (1742–1745) — the Wittelsbach interruption
  • Francis I (1745–1765) — husband of Maria Theresa
  • Joseph II (1765–1790)
  • Leopold II (1790–1792)
  • Francis II (1792–1806) — dissolved the Empire; became Francis I of Austria
