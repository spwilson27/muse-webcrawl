Solicitation

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Solicitation is the act of offering, or attempting to purchase, goods or services. In a broad sense it describes any request, appeal, or entreaty made to another person or organization. The term is used in several distinct contexts: in law, where solicitation can constitute a criminal offence; in commerce, where it refers to the marketing of products door to door, by telephone, or by mail; and in professional life, where a solicitor in Commonwealth jurisdictions is a class of lawyer.

Criminal law

In criminal law, solicitation is an inchoate offence consisting of asking, encouraging, or commanding another person to commit a crime. The offence is complete once the request is made, regardless of whether the other party agrees or the underlying crime is carried out. Many jurisdictions punish solicitation of serious felonies, and solicitation of prostitution is a common statutory offence. In the United States, solicitation law is constrained by the First Amendment when the speech involves lawful commercial activity, though false or misleading commercial speech, and speech proposing illegal transactions, may be regulated.

Commercial solicitation

Commercial solicitation includes door-to-door sales, telemarketing, direct mail, and electronic marketing. Many municipalities require door-to-door solicitors to obtain permits, and "no soliciting" signs are commonly posted by homeowners. Telephone solicitation is regulated in the United States by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act of 1991 and by the National Do Not Call Registry, administered by the Federal Trade Commission. Unsolicited bulk electronic mail is governed by legislation such as the CAN-SPAM Act of 2003 in the United States and the GDPR and ePrivacy rules in the European Union.

Charitable solicitation is separately regulated: in most US states, charities and professional fundraisers must register with a state agency before soliciting donations from the public.

Procurement

In government and business procurement, a solicitation is a formal request for bids or proposals, such as a request for proposal (RFP), request for quotation (RFQ), or invitation for bid (IFB). Federal procurement in the United States is conducted under the Federal Acquisition Regulation, and solicitations are published through official portals so that prospective contractors can respond on equal terms.
