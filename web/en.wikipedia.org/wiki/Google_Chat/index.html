Google Chat is a communication software developed by Google. It is one of two applications that constitute the replacement for Google Hangouts in Google Workspace, the other being Google Meet. Google planned to begin retiring Google Hangouts in October 2019, moving Workspace users to Chat, and in 2022 it completed the migration of free consumer Hangouts users to Chat as well.

Google Chat features direct messages, group conversations in "spaces", threaded replies, integration with other Google Workspace products such as Google Drive, Docs, and Google Meet, and support for bots and third-party integrations. Spaces can be used for project-based collaboration, with shared files, tasks, and chat history.

History

Announced in March 2017 as a revamp of Hangouts aimed at business teams, Google Chat was originally part of the Hangouts brand ("Hangouts Chat") before being rebranded in April 2020. It became available to free Google account holders in 2021, and Google announced in early 2022 that Hangouts would be fully replaced by Chat, with the Hangouts web and mobile apps shut down later that year.

Features

Chat supports one-to-one and group direct messages, spaces with inline topic threads, @mentions, emoji reactions, GIF search, message editing and deletion, and smart reply suggestions. Workspace customers can apply data retention and Vault policies, and administrators control external sharing.
