The Emirates Stadium (known as Arsenal Stadium for UEFA competitions) is a football stadium in Holloway, London, England, and the home of Arsenal Football Club. With a capacity of 60,704, it is among the largest football stadiums in England. It opened in July 2006, replacing the club's previous home, Highbury.

History

Arsenal announced plans for a new stadium at Ashburton Grove in 1999, seeking greater capacity and revenue than the 38,000-seat Highbury could provide. Construction began in 2004; the project cost about £390 million, partly financed by redeveloping the old Highbury site for housing. Naming rights were sold to Emirates airline in a deal announced in 2004; the stadium is formally called Emirates Stadium until at least 2028. The first match was Dennis Bergkamp's testimonial against Ajax on 22 July 2006.

Design

Designed by Populous, the stadium has four tiers with a continuous bowl, a translucent polycarbonate roof, and the pitch oriented north–south. "Arsenalisation" works from 2009 added murals, statues of club legends including Thierry Henry and Tony Adams, and a clock echoing Highbury's famous Clock End.

Other uses

The stadium has hosted international friendlies, concerts, and NFL-related events, and serves as a conference venue.
