List of cocktails

A cocktail is a mixed drink typically made with a distilled liquor as its base ingredient, combined with other ingredients or mixers. This is a list of notable cocktails, broadly grouped by their principal spirit or style. Many of the classics below appear on the International Bartenders Association's official list of IBA cocktails.

Gin cocktails

  • Martini — gin and dry vermouth, garnished with an olive or lemon twist
  • Negroni — gin, Campari, and sweet vermouth in equal parts
  • Tom Collins — gin, lemon juice, sugar, and soda water
  • Gimlet — gin and lime cordial
  • French 75 — gin, lemon juice, sugar, and Champagne
  • Last Word — gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, and lime juice

Whisky cocktails

  • Old Fashioned — bourbon or rye, bitters, sugar, and a twist of citrus
  • Manhattan — rye whisky, sweet vermouth, and bitters
  • Whisky Sour — whisky, lemon juice, and sugar, sometimes with egg white
  • Mint Julep — bourbon and fresh mint over crushed ice
  • Sazerac — rye whisky, absinthe rinse, and Peychaud's bitters
  • Rob Roy — Scotch whisky and sweet vermouth

Rum cocktails

  • Mojito — white rum, lime, mint, sugar, and soda
  • Daiquiri — rum, lime juice, and sugar
  • Piña Colada — rum, pineapple juice, and coconut cream
  • Mai Tai — rum, lime, curaçao, and orgeat, a tiki classic
  • Dark 'n' Stormy — dark rum and ginger beer
  • Cuba Libre — rum, cola, and lime

Tequila, vodka, and other cocktails

  • Margarita — tequila, orange liqueur, and lime juice
  • Paloma — tequila and grapefruit soda
  • Moscow Mule — vodka, ginger beer, and lime, served in a copper mug
  • Bloody Mary — vodka, tomato juice, and spices
  • Cosmopolitan — vodka, triple sec, cranberry, and lime
  • White Russian — vodka, coffee liqueur, and cream
  • Caipirinha — cachaça, lime, and sugar, the national cocktail of Brazil
  • Pisco Sour — pisco, lime, and egg white, claimed by both Peru and Chile
  • Aperol Spritz — Aperol, prosecco, and soda
  • Espresso Martini — vodka, espresso, and coffee liqueur
