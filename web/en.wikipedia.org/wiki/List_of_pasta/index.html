List of pasta

There are many different varieties of pasta, the staple Italian food made from durum wheat dough. They are usually sorted by size — long, short, stuffed, and shapes for soup — and by whether they are made fresh (pasta fresca) or dried (pasta secca). Italy recognises hundreds of distinct shapes, many tied to particular regions and sauces.


A selection of common pasta shapes.

Long pasta

NameDescription
SpaghettiLong, thin, solid cylinders; the most popular shape worldwide
LinguineFlattened spaghetti, from Liguria; often served with seafood or pesto
FettuccineFlat ribbons, classic with Alfredo sauce and ragù
TagliatelleLong flat ribbons from Emilia-Romagna and Marche
BucatiniThick spaghetti-like pasta with a hole through the centre
Capellini (angel hair)Very thin long strands
PiciThick hand-rolled strands from Tuscany

PenneMedium cylinders cut diagonally; smooth (lisce) or ridged (rigate)
FusilliHelical spirals that trap sauce
RigatoniLarge ridged tubes
FarfalleBow-tie or butterfly shapes
ConchiglieShells; large ones (conchiglioni) are often stuffed
OrecchietteSmall ear-shaped discs from Apulia
MacaroniNarrow tubes, famous in macaroni and cheese
RotiniTight corkscrews
GemelliTwisted twin strands

Stuffed pasta

NameDescription
RavioliSquare or round pillows of pasta enclosing a filling
TortelliniRing-shaped, from Bologna and Modena
AgnolottiPiedmontese stuffed pasta, pinched at the edges
Cappelletti"Little hats", similar to tortellini
MezzeluneHalf-moon shapes

NameDescription
LasagneWide flat sheets, layered in baked dishes
