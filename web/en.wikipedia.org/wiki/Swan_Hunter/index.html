Swan Hunter

Swan Hunter, formerly known as Swan, Hunter & Wigham Richardson, was a British shipbuilding company based in Wallsend, Tyne and Wear. One of the best-known shipbuilders in the world, it built many famous vessels including the RMS Mauretania, for many years the fastest ocean liner afloat.

History

The company was formed through the merger of the yards of Charles Sheridan Swan and George Burton Hunter, later combining with Wigham Richardson in 1903. Its Wallsend yard launched the Mauretania in 1906 and built numerous warships for the Royal Navy, including aircraft carriers of the Illustrious class.

Decline

Nationalised in the 1970s as part of British Shipbuilders and later privatised, the yard built Type 23 frigates and the survey vessel work before shipbuilding ended at Wallsend in the 2000s as production moved overseas.
