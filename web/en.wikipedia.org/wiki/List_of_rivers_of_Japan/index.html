List of rivers of Japan

Japan's rivers are characteristically short, steep and fast-flowing, reflecting the country's mountainous terrain and heavy rainfall. Under the River Law, the most important systems are designated Class A rivers and are administered nationally. The longest river is the Shinano, and the river with the largest basin is the Tone.
