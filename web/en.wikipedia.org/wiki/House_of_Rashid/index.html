House of Rashid

The House of Rashid (Arabic: آل رشيد, Āl Rashīd) was a historic Arabian dynasty that ruled the Emirate of Jabal Shammar in the north of the Arabian Peninsula from 1836 to 1921, with its capital at Ha'il. For much of that period the Rashidis were the chief rivals of the House of Saud for control of Arabia.

History

The dynasty was founded by Abdullah bin Rashid, who took Ha'il in 1836. Under Muhammad ibn Rashid (1869–1897) the emirate reached its greatest extent, capturing Riyadh in 1891 and exiling the Saudi family to Kuwait. British and Ottoman rivalry during the First World War divided the peninsula; after the war, Ibn Saud's forces besieged Ha'il, and the last Rashidi emir, Muhammad bin Talal, surrendered in November 1921, ending the emirate. Many members of the family were taken to Riyadh, where they later intermarried with the Saudis.
