Jackfruit

The jackfruit (Artocarpus heterophyllus) is a species of tree in the fig, mulberry, and breadfruit family (Moraceae). It is the largest tree fruit in the world, with individual fruits commonly weighing 15–25 kg and sometimes much more.

Origin and cultivation

The jackfruit is believed to originate from the Western Ghats of India and is widely cultivated throughout South and Southeast Asia; it is the national fruit of Bangladesh and Sri Lanka. It thrives in tropical lowlands.

Uses

Ripe fruit is sweet and eaten fresh or in desserts; unripe jackfruit has a fibrous texture and is cooked as a vegetable or used as a meat substitute. The seeds are also edible when cooked, and the timber is valued.
