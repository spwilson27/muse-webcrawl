José Luis Sérsic (1933–1993) was an Argentine astronomer best known for his work on the morphology and structure of galaxies. He spent most of his career at the Córdoba Astronomical Observatory.

Contributions

Sérsic generalized the de Vaucouleurs r1/4 law into the flexible surface-brightness profile now universally known as the Sérsic profile (or Sérsic law), parameterized by the Sérsic index n, which describes how the intensity of a galaxy varies with radius. Together with his wife Pastoriza he compiled the Atlas de Galaxias Australes (1968), an influential atlas of southern galaxies, and he identified peculiar galaxy features now called Sérsic–Pastoriza galaxies.
