Shrek (franchise)

Shrek is an American animated media franchise created by DreamWorks Animation, loosely based on William Steig's 1990 picture book Shrek!. It follows a gruff but good-hearted ogre, his talking companion Donkey and wife Princess Fiona, in a parodic fairy-tale world. The franchise is one of the highest-grossing animated series of all time, and its characters — especially Shrek — have become cultural icons and internet memes.

Films

The main series comprises Shrek (2001), which won the first Academy Award for Best Animated Feature; Shrek 2 (2004), for years the highest-grossing animated film ever; Shrek the Third (2007); and Shrek Forever After (2010). A fifth film, Shrek 5, was announced for release in the mid-2020s. The spin-off Puss in Boots (2011) and its sequel Puss in Boots: The Last Wish (2022), centred on Antonio Banderas's swashbuckling cat, won wide critical acclaim.

Other media

The franchise includes television specials such as Shrek the Halls and Scared Shrekless, the series The Adventures of Puss in Boots, a Broadway musical (Shrek The Musical, 2008), video games and major theme-park attractions. Mike Myers voices Shrek (with a Scottish accent), Eddie Murphy voices Donkey and Cameron Diaz voices Fiona.
