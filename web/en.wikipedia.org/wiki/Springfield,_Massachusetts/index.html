Springfield is a city in and the seat of Hampden County, Massachusetts, United States, situated on the eastern bank of the Connecticut River near its confluence with three rivers: the western Westfield River, the eastern Chicopee River, and the Mill River. As of recent censuses it has a population of about 155,000, making it the third-largest city in Massachusetts and the fourth-largest in New England after Boston, Worcester, and Providence. It is the principal city of the Springfield metropolitan area, one of two metropolitan areas in the Pioneer Valley.

History

Springfield was settled in 1636 by English fur trader William Pynchon and was named after Springfield in Essex, England. It was the first Springfield established in the New World. During the American Revolution, George Washington designated Springfield as a national armory site, and the city became a centre of manufacturing, particularly precision manufacturing, in the 19th and 20th centuries. The Springfield Armory, founded in 1794, produced firearms for the U.S. military until its closure in 1968.

In 1787, the city was the site of Shays' Rebellion's climactic confrontation at the Springfield Armory, an event that helped galvanise support for a stronger federal constitution. Springfield was also a major stop on the Underground Railroad.

Culture and sport

Springfield is the birthplace of basketball: the game was invented in 1891 by James Naismith, a physical education instructor at the YMCA Training School (now Springfield College). The city hosts the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame. Theodor Seuss Geisel, better known as Dr. Seuss, was born in Springfield, and the city celebrates his legacy with the Amazing World of Dr. Seuss Museum and a sculpture garden. The Springfield Museums form a well-known cultural complex, and the city hosts The Big E, New England's largest agricultural fair, in neighbouring West Springfield.

Economy

Historically a manufacturing hub ("Precision Valley"), Springfield's modern economy is anchored by healthcare (Baystate Health), insurance (MassMutual), education, and the MGM Springfield casino resort, which opened in 2018. The city remains a significant freight and rail junction in western New England.
