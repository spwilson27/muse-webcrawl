Bradley Charles Cooper (born January 5, 1975) is an American actor and filmmaker. He is the recipient of various accolades, including a British Academy Film Award and two Grammy Awards, in addition to nominations for twelve Academy Awards, six Golden Globe Awards, and a Tony Award.

Career

Cooper gained recognition for his roles in the television series Alias and achieved a breakthrough with the comedy The Hangover (2009). He received critical acclaim and Academy Award nominations for Silver Linings Playbook (2012), American Hustle (2013), and American Sniper (2014). He voices Rocket in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

His directorial debut, A Star Is Born (2018), in which he starred opposite Lady Gaga, earned him further acclaim, and his second film, Maestro (2023), about Leonard Bernstein, premiered at Venice.

Personal life

Cooper was born in Philadelphia and graduated from Georgetown University. He studied acting at the Actors Studio Drama School at The New School in New York City.
