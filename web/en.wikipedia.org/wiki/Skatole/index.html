Skatole

Skatole or 3-methylindole is an organic compound belonging to the indole family. It occurs naturally in the feces of mammals and birds and is the primary contributor to fecal odor. At low concentrations, however, it has a flowery smell and is found in several flowers and essential oils, including orange blossoms and jasmine, and is used in perfumery.

Skatole is produced by bacterial breakdown of the amino acid tryptophan in the digestive tract. Together with androstenone it is responsible for boar taint in pork from uncastrated male pigs. It is also present in coal tar and can be synthesised from indole derivatives.

Properties

It is a white crystalline solid, slightly soluble in water, that browns on exposure to air and light. Its name derives from the Greek skato-, meaning dung.
