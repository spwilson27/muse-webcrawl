The Carabinieri (formally the Arma dei Carabinieri, "Arm of Carabineers"; previously Corpo dei Carabinieri Reali, "Royal Carabineers Corps") are the national gendarmerie of Italy who primarily carry out domestic and foreign policing duties. It is one of Italy's main law enforcement agencies, alongside the Polizia di Stato and the Guardia di Finanza. As with the French gendarmerie, the Carabinieri are a military force: a branch of the Italian Armed Forces, they come under the authority of the Ministry of Defence for military duties and the Ministry of the Interior for public security tasks.

History

The corps was founded by King Victor Emmanuel I of Piedmont-Sardinia on 13 July 1814, modelled on the French gendarmerie, to provide a police force with military status. Carabinieri took part in all of Italy's wars and colonial campaigns, earning battle honours at Pastrengo (1848) — commemorated by the mounted charge celebrated in the annual Carosello — and at Podgora in the First World War. During the Second World War, Carabinieri both served with the Axis forces and, after the 1943 armistice, joined the resistance; the vice-brigadiere Salvo D'Acquisto, who sacrificed himself to save civilians from a Nazi reprisal, is among Italy's most honoured servicemen.

Organisation and duties

The Carabinieri are organised into territorial commands covering provincial and station level — the local stazione is a familiar institution in Italian towns — plus specialised units: the ROS (for organised crime and terrorism), the NAS (food and health fraud), the TPC (cultural property protection, the first such unit in the world), environmental and forestry commands, and the paratroopers and GIS special forces. The Carabinieri also provide military police for all Italian armed forces and guard Italian embassies abroad.

Uniform and traditions

The dark blue uniform with red trouser stripes and the distinctive two-pointed lucerna hat (still worn for ceremonial duties) make the corps instantly recognisable. The grenade-with-flame badge (the fiamma) is worn on headgear. The mounted Carabinieri band performs the famous Carosello Storico at the Piazza di Siena horse show in Rome each year.
