Afghan Girl

Afghan Girl
Photographer: Steve McCurry
Taken: 1984, Nasir Bagh refugee camp, Pakistan
Published: National Geographic cover, June 1985
Subject identified (2002): Sharbat Gula

Afghan Girl is a 1984 photographic portrait by journalist Steve McCurry, depicting an Afghan refugee girl with striking green eyes. It appeared on the June 1985 cover of National Geographic and became one of the most recognised magazine images in the world.

The photograph

McCurry took the portrait at the Nasir Bagh refugee camp near Peshawar, Pakistan, during the Soviet–Afghan War. The girl, then about twelve, was a Pashtun orphan whose identity was unknown for years; the image was often compared to the Mona Lisa for its haunting expression.

Identification and later life

In 2002 a National Geographic team located the subject, Sharbat Gula, in Afghanistan and verified her identity using iris recognition. Her story later drew attention again in 2016, when she was arrested in Pakistan over identity documents, and in 2021, when she was evacuated to Italy after the Taliban takeover.
