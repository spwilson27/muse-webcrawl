Michel François Platini (born 21 June 1955) is a French football administrator and former player and manager. Regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time, he won the Ballon d'Or three consecutive times — in 1983, 1984, and 1985 — and came sixth in the FIFA Player of the Century vote. A gifted playmaker and dead-ball specialist with Nancy, Saint-Étienne, and Juventus, he captained France to victory at UEFA Euro 1984, where he scored nine goals in five matches, a tournament record.

Playing career

Platini began at Nancy, winning the Coupe de France in 1978, then a league title with Saint-Étienne in 1981, before joining Juventus in 1982. In Turin he won two Serie A titles, the European Cup in 1985 (scoring the winning penalty in the final overshadowed by the Heysel Stadium disaster), the European Cup Winners' Cup, and three consecutive capocannoniere top-scorer titles as a midfielder. For France he was the centrepiece of the celebrated carré magique ("magic square") midfield with Jean Tigana, Alain Giresse, and Luis Fernández, reaching World Cup semi-finals in 1982 and 1986 and triumphing on home soil at Euro 1984. He retired in 1987 with 41 goals in 72 internationals, then a French record.

Management and administration

Platini managed the French national team from 1988 to 1992 and co-chaired the 1998 World Cup organising committee. He became president of UEFA in 2007 and was re-elected in 2011 and 2015, championing Financial Fair Play and the expansion of the European Championship to 24 teams. In December 2015, after investigation into a 2 million Swiss franc payment he received from FIFA president Sepp Blatter in 2011, FIFA's ethics committee banned both men from football for eight years (later reduced); Swiss prosecutors ultimately acquitted Platini and Blatter of fraud in 2022, a verdict upheld on appeal in 2025.

Legacy

Renowned for his vision, passing range, and bending free kicks, Platini's number is retired by Nancy. Despite his administrative fall from grace, he remains routinely ranked among the finest midfielders in football history.
