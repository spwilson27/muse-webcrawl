Pikachu

Pikachu is a fictional species in the Pokémon media franchise owned by Nintendo and created by Game Freak. It is a yellow, mouse-like Pokémon with electrical abilities and is the mascot of the Pokémon franchise, appearing on the cover art of many games and as the partner of Ash Ketchum, the protagonist of the Pokémon anime series.

Design and characteristics

Designed by Atsuko Nishida and finalized by Ken Sugimori, Pikachu is a small, chubby rodent-like Pokémon covered in yellow fur, with long pointed ears tipped in black, red circular cheek pouches that store electricity, and a tail shaped like a lightning bolt. Pikachu is an Electric-type Pokémon that evolves from Pichu and can evolve into Raichu when exposed to a Thunder Stone. In the games it is species number 025 in the National Pokédex.

Appearances and reception

Pikachu debuted in Pokémon Red and Green (1996) in Japan. Its role as Ash's starter in the anime made it the most recognizable Pokémon worldwide; it is often regarded as the Japanese equivalent of Mickey Mouse in popular culture. Pikachu has appeared in every main-series game, starred in Pokémon Yellow and the live-action film Detective Pikachu (2019), and features in the Super Smash Bros. series. In 2003, Forbes ranked Pikachu among the highest-earning fictional characters.
