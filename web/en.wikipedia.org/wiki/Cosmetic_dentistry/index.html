Cosmetic dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry is dentistry aimed at improving the appearance of the teeth, mouth and smile rather than treating disease or restoring function alone, although many procedures have both cosmetic and restorative benefits. It is not recognised as a formal specialty by some dental bodies, but is widely practised and marketed.

Overview

Field: Dentistry

Common procedures: Whitening, veneers, bonding, crowns, orthodontics, implants

Common procedures

Typical treatments include teeth whitening (bleaching), dental veneers of porcelain or composite, dental bonding, enamel shaping, crowns, bridges and implants, and orthodontic treatments such as clear aligners. "Smile makeovers" combine several procedures planned around the patient's facial aesthetics.

Considerations

Cosmetic procedures can involve irreversible removal of tooth structure and carry risks such as sensitivity, failure of restorations and the need for future maintenance. Professional bodies caution that treatment should be based on diagnosis and informed consent, and regulators in several countries restrict advertising claims.
