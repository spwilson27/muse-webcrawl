Lockheed (character)

Lockheed is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. A small, purple, dragon-like alien, he is best known as the companion of the mutant superhero Kitty Pryde (Shadowcat) and a member of the X-Men's extended family. Lockheed was created by writer Chris Claremont and artist Paul Smith, and first appeared in The Uncanny X-Men in 1983.

Fictional biography

Lockheed comes from a highly advanced alien species whose society resembles a hive. He encountered the X-Men during their adventures in space involving the Brood, and later stowed away — or was brought — to Earth, where he bonded with Kitty Pryde. Although he usually behaves like an affectionate pet and does not speak Earth languages in the presence of others, Lockheed is in fact fully intelligent, and has been shown to be an agent reporting on Earth to his own people; he has periodically been parted from Kitty by his duties, to her distress. He breathes fire and can fly, and despite his small size he is brave and resourceful in battle.

Associations

Lockheed was a founding member of the Pet Avengers, a team of animal heroes, and has appeared alongside the X-Men, Excalibur, and S.W.O.R.D. His name is commonly assumed by fans to honour the aviation company Lockheed, reflecting the X-Men's aircraft associations.

In other media

Lockheed has appeared in animated X-Men television series and in minor roles in other adaptations.
