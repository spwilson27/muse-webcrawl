Linear system

A linear system is a mathematical model of a system based on the use of a linear operator. Linear systems typically exhibit features much simpler than the general, nonlinear case, and the superposition principle applies: the response to a sum of inputs is the sum of the individual responses, and scaling an input scales the output by the same factor.

Linear time-invariant systems

Linear time-invariant (LTI) systems are characterised completely by their impulse response; the output for any input is the convolution of the input with that response. They are analysed with the Laplace and Fourier transforms, transfer functions, and state-space methods, and they form the foundation of signal processing and control theory.

Examples and applications

Electrical circuits of resistors, capacitors and inductors, mechanical spring–mass–damper systems, and small-signal models of many physical processes are linear systems. Systems of linear equations in algebra, and linear differential equations, are studied with matrix methods; questions of controllability and observability are central in control engineering.
