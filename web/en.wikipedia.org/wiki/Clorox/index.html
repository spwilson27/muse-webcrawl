Clorox

The Clorox Company (formerly Clorox Chemical Company) is an American global manufacturer and marketer of consumer and professional products. Founded in 1913 in Oakland, California, the company is best known for Clorox bleach, but its portfolio includes brands such as Pine-Sol, Liquid-Plumr, Tilex, Kingsford charcoal, Hidden Valley Ranch dressings, Brita water filters, Burt's Bees natural personal care products, and Glad trash bags (through a joint venture with Procter & Gamble).

History

Clorox was founded on May 3, 1913, by five businessmen who invested $100 each to establish the first commercial-scale liquid bleach factory in the United States, in Oakland. The company's first product was industrial-strength bleach sold to laundries, breweries, and municipal water works. The name "Clorox" combines the names of its two main ingredients, chlorine and sodium hydroxide. Household-size bottles were introduced in the 1930s, aided by advertising featuring the trademark "Free Rinso" era campaigns and later the character "Mama" bleach advertisements.

The company went public in 1928 and was acquired by Procter & Gamble in 1957; the merger was overturned by the U.S. Supreme Court in 1967 on antitrust grounds, and Clorox became independent again in 1969. It subsequently diversified through acquisitions, buying brands including Combat insecticides, Pine-Sol (1990), and Burt's Bees (2007) for $925 million.

Products and brands

Clorox operates across four segments: Health and Wellness (disinfecting wipes and sprays, vitamins), Household (charcoal, cat litter, bags and wraps), Lifestyle (dressings, Brita filters, Burt's Bees), and International. Its disinfecting products saw exceptional demand during the COVID-19 pandemic, when supply shortages made headlines.

Environmental and safety record

The company markets a "Green Works" line of plant-based cleaners and has set sustainability goals including plastic reduction. In 2023, Clorox suffered a major cyberattack that disrupted operations and product availability for weeks.
