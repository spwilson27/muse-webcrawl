Black Bird is an American true-crime drama miniseries developed by Dennis Lehane, based on the 2010 autobiographical novel In with the Devil: A Fallen Hero, a Serial Killer, and a Dangerous Bargain for Redemption by James Keene with Hillel Levin. It premiered on Apple TV+ on 8 July 2022 and ran for six episodes.

Plot

Jimmy Keene (Taron Egerton), a charismatic former high-school football star serving a ten-year sentence for drug trafficking, is offered a deal: transfer to a maximum-security prison for the criminally insane, befriend suspected serial killer Larry Hall (Paul Walter Hauser), and elicit from him the location of the body of one of his victims—Tricia Reitler—before Hall's appeal is decided. If he succeeds, Keene wins full release and a clean record; if he fails or is exposed, he faces the dangers of the new prison.

Reception

The series won widespread critical acclaim, with particular praise for Hauser's unsettling performance, which won him an Emmy Award and a Golden Globe for supporting actor. It was also one of Ray Liotta's final roles, playing Egerton's character's father; Liotta died shortly before the premiere and the series is dedicated in part to his memory. The series holds a near-unanimous positive rating from critics, who highlighted its writing and psychological tension.
