Drug Policy Alliance

The Drug Policy Alliance (DPA) is a New York City–based non-profit organisation that works to advance policies intended to reduce the harms of both drug use and drug prohibition, and to promote alternatives to the war on drugs. It was formed in 2000 from the merger of the Lindesmith Center and the Drug Policy Foundation.

Activities

The DPA advocates decriminalisation and legal regulation of drugs, harm reduction measures such as syringe exchange and naloxone access, sentencing reform, and the legalisation of cannabis. It has played a central role in campaigns for state-level marijuana legalisation ballot measures in the United States and for California's Proposition 47, which reduced certain drug and property offences from felonies to misdemeanours. Ethan Nadelmann was its founding executive director, serving until 2017.
