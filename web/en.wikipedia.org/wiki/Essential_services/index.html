Essential services

Essential services are services considered so important to public health, safety and welfare that governments may restrict industrial action in them, require minimum service levels during strikes, or designate their workers as essential and exempt from lockdowns or other restrictions.

Scope

Lists vary by jurisdiction but commonly include healthcare and emergency medicine, police and fire services, water and sanitation, electricity and energy supply, food distribution, transport, telecommunications and refuse collection. During the COVID-19 pandemic, governments published lists of essential or key workers permitted to continue working and travelling.

Labour law

The International Labour Organization accepts strike restrictions only in essential services "in the strict sense of the term" — those whose interruption would endanger life, personal safety or health — and expects compensatory guarantees such as impartial arbitration. Many countries ban strikes by police and military personnel and impose minimum-service requirements in hospitals and utilities. Disputes over designation are common, as broadening the category limits the right to strike.
