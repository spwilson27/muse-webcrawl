Getxo

Getxo is a town and municipality located in the province of Biscay, in the Basque Country, Spain. It lies on the right bank of the mouth of the Nervión estuary, within Greater Bilbao, about 14 kilometres from central Bilbao.

Getxo comprises the neighbourhoods of Algorta, Las Arenas, Romo, Neguri and Santa María de Getxo. Neguri developed as an affluent residential area for Bilbao's industrial bourgeoisie, known for its palatial mansions.

Landmarks

The Bizkaia Bridge (Puente Colgante), a transporter bridge linking Las Arenas with Portugalete across the estuary, opened in 1893 and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Getxo also has popular beaches such as Ereaga and Arrigunaga and hosts an international jazz festival.
