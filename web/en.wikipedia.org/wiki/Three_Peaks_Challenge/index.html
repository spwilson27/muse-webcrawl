Three Peaks Challenge

National Three Peaks
PeaksBen Nevis (1,345 m), Scafell Pike (978 m), Snowdon / Yr Wyddfa (1,085 m)
Total ascentc. 3,064 m
Walking distancec. 23 miles (37 km)
Target time24 hours including travel

The Three Peaks Challenge is an event in which participants attempt to climb the highest mountains of Scotland, England and Wales—Ben Nevis, Scafell Pike and Snowdon—within 24 hours, travelling by road between them.

Route

Teams usually start at Ben Nevis in the late afternoon, climb Scafell Pike at night, and finish on Snowdon. The driving distance is around 462 miles. Related events include the Yorkshire Three Peaks (Pen-y-ghent, Whernside, Ingleborough, in under 12 hours) and various county and five-peaks variants.

Charity and controversy

The challenge is a popular charity fundraiser, but its scale has drawn criticism from mountain rescue teams, local residents and conservation bodies over night-time disturbance, litter, speeding between peaks, and erosion; organisers promote codes of conduct and staggered, guided events.
