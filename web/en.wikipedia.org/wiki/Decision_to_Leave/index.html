Decision to Leave

Decision to Leave (Korean: 헤어질 결심, Heeojil gyeolsim, lit. "Resolution to Break Up") is a 2022 South Korean neo-noir romantic mystery film directed by Park Chan-wook, who co-wrote the screenplay with Chung Seo-kyeong. It stars Tang Wei as Seo-rae, the widow of a man who fell to his death from a mountain peak, and Park Hae-il as Hae-jun, the insomniac Busan detective investigating the death who gradually falls in love with her.

Plot

Detective Hae-jun, a courteous, meticulous investigator suffering from insomnia, is assigned to the case of a retired immigration officer found dead at the base of a mountain he frequently climbed. The man's much younger Chinese widow, Seo-rae, seems insufficiently grief-stricken, and Hae-jun begins surveilling her while interviewing her about the death. As the investigation proceeds, the two develop an unspoken attachment, complicated by Seo-rae's past, a second death connected to her, and the question of what each is willing to confess. The case is ultimately closed, but the relationship resumes under new circumstances on Korea's coast, ending in a celebrated final sequence on a beach at high tide.

Production and release

The film premiered in competition at the 2022 Cannes Film Festival, where Park Chan-wook won the award for Best Director. It was released theatrically in South Korea in June 2022 and distributed internationally by Mubi and other partners. Park described the film as a restrained, classical melodrama in contrast to his earlier, more violent works, and it is noted for its elaborate camerawork, including shots framed through phones, mirrors, and surveillance footage, and for its use of the song "The Mist".

Reception

Decision to Leave received widespread critical acclaim for its direction, cinematography, and the performances of Tang Wei and Park Hae-il. It was selected as South Korea's submission for Best International Feature Film at the 95th Academy Awards and made the December shortlist. It won numerous honours in South Korea, including at the Blue Dragon Film Awards and the Baeksang Arts Awards, and appeared on many critics' year-end lists of the best films of 2022.
