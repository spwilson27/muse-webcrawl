List of firefighting museums

Firefighting museums
Museums dedicated to the history of fire services, apparatus and firefighters around the world.

Firefighting museums preserve and display the history of fire services, including hand-drawn and horse-drawn apparatus, steam pumpers, motorised fire engines, uniforms, helmets, alarms and communications equipment. Many are housed in former fire stations.
