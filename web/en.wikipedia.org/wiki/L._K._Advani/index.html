L. K. Advani

Lal Krishna Advani (born 8 November 1927) is an Indian politician who served as the 7th Deputy Prime Minister of India from 2002 to 2004. He is one of the co-founders of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and a member of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), a right-wing Hindu nationalist volunteer organisation. He is the longest-serving president of the BJP, having held the office three times since its founding in 1980.

L. K. Advani

Early life

Advani was born in Karachi, in British India (now Pakistan), into a Sindhi Hindu family. He was educated at St. Patrick's High School, Karachi, and D. G. National College, Hyderabad, Sindh, before studying law at the Government Law College, Bombay University. He joined the RSS in 1942 and migrated to India during the Partition of 1947.

Political career

Advani began his political career with the Bharatiya Jana Sangh, becoming its president in 1973. He served as Minister of Information and Broadcasting in the Janata Party government of Morarji Desai (1977–1979). A founder of the BJP in 1980, he led the party through the Ram Janmabhoomi movement; his 1990 Rath Yatra across India in support of a Ram temple at Ayodhya propelled the BJP from a marginal party to a major national force.

He served as Minister of Home Affairs (1998–2004) in the government of Atal Bihari Vajpayee, and as Deputy Prime Minister from 2002. He was the BJP's prime ministerial candidate in the 2009 general election. Advani was a member of the Lok Sabha for many terms, representing constituencies including New Delhi and Gandhinagar. In 2015 he was awarded the Padma Vibhushan, India's second-highest civilian honour, and in 2024 the Bharat Ratna, its highest.
