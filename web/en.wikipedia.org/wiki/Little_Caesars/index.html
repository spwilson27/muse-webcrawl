Little Caesars is an American multinational pizza chain. Founded on May 8, 1959, by Mike Ilitch and his wife Marian Ilitch, it is headquartered in Detroit, Michigan, and operates carryout and delivery pizza restaurants in the United States and internationally. It is one of the largest pizza chains in the United States and is known for its "Pizza! Pizza!" slogan and its budget-oriented Hot-N-Ready concept.

History

The first Little Caesars restaurant opened in a strip mall in Garden City, Michigan. The name was Marian's nickname for Mike. The chain grew through franchising, and its 1979 advertising campaign introduced the slogan "Pizza! Pizza!", which promoted two pizzas for the price of one competitor's pizza. In the 1990s the company experimented with dine-in locations and drive-through service. The Hot-N-Ready concept, launched in the early 2000s, offered a large pepperoni pizza at a low price, ready for immediate carryout, and became central to the brand.

Products

Little Caesars sells pizzas with a variety of toppings, as well as side items such as Crazy Bread, stuffed crust pizzas, and the Pretzel Crust pizza introduced in 2014. Its menus emphasize low prices and speed of service rather than table service.

Corporate affairs

The company is part of Ilitch Holdings, the family holding company that also owns the Detroit Red Wings of the NHL and the Detroit Tigers of MLB. Little Caesars Arena, which opened in Detroit in 2017, is named for the chain.
