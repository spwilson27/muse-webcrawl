Horace Slughorn

Horace Slughorn, Potions professor at Hogwarts

Horace Eugene Flaccus Slughorn is a fictional character in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series. He is a pure-blood wizard who served two terms as Potions master and Head of Slytherin House at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He first appears in Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince (2005), where he is persuaded out of retirement by Albus Dumbledore and Harry Potter. In the film adaptations he is portrayed by Jim Broadbent.

Character overview

Slughorn is a genial, comfort-loving man fond of crystallised pineapple, fine meads, and the company of the talented and well-connected. At Hogwarts he founded the Slug Club, a social circle of promising students whom he "collects" and later cultivates as useful connections. Though a Slytherin, he is not a supporter of Lord Voldemort; his great shame is that, as a young teacher, he explained the concept of Horcruxes to the student Tom Riddle, a memory he altered out of guilt and which Dumbledore needs Harry to retrieve in its true form.

Role in the series

In Half-Blood Prince, Harry, with the help of Felix Felicis, obtains Slughorn's true memory, which confirms that Riddle intended to create multiple Horcruxes. Slughorn remains at Hogwarts as Potions master in Deathly Hallows and, after initially fleeing the Battle of Hogwarts, returns with reinforcements — including Hogsmeade residents and Centaurs — and duels Voldemort himself alongside Minerva McGonagall and Kingsley Shacklebolt.
