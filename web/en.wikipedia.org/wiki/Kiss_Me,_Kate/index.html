Kiss Me, Kate is a musical with music and lyrics by Cole Porter and book by Bella and Samuel Spewack. It is a play-within-a-play: a touring theatre company staging Shakespeare's The Taming of the Shrew in Baltimore, whose leading actors — the divorced Fred Graham and Lilli Vanessi — bicker offstage in parallel with their onstage roles as Petruchio and Katharine.

History

The show opened on Broadway in 1948, ran for 1,077 performances, and in 1949 won the first-ever Tony Award for Best Musical. Songs include "Another Op'nin', Another Show", "So in Love", "Too Darn Hot", "Brush Up Your Shakespeare", and "Always True to You in My Fashion". A celebrated film version appeared in 1953, and major revivals in 1999 (Broadway) and 2019 (Studio 54) confirmed its place as one of the classic American musicals.
