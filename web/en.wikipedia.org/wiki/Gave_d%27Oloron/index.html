The Gave d'Oloron is a river in southwestern France, in the department of Pyrénées-Atlantiques. It is formed at Oloron-Sainte-Marie by the confluence of two Pyrenean torrents, the Gave d'Aspe and the Gave d'Ossau, and flows northwest for about 149 kilometres.

Course

From Oloron-Sainte-Marie the river passes Navarrenx, Sauveterre-de-Béarn and Peyrehorade, where it joins the Gave de Pau to form the Gaves réunis, a short stretch that flows into the Adour. Tributaries include the Saison (or Gave de Mauléon) and the Joos.

Name and use

"Gave" is a local word for a Pyrenean mountain river. The Gave d'Oloron is noted for salmon and sea-trout fishing and for whitewater sports on its upper tributaries.
