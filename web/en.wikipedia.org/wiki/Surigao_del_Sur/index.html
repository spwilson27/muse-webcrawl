Surigao del Sur

Surigao del Sur (Surigaonon: Probinsya nan Surigao del Sur; Cebuano: Lalawigan sa Surigao del Sur) is a province of the Philippines in the Caraga region of Mindanao. Its capital is Tandag, and its largest city is Bislig. Facing the Philippine Sea to the east, the province is known for the Enchanted River in Hinatuan, the Tinuy-an Falls in Bislig, and an economy based on timber, fishing, agriculture and mining. Its population is roughly 640,000.

History

Surigao del Sur was created on 19 June 1960 under Republic Act No. 2786, which divided the old province of Surigao into Surigao del Norte and Surigao del Sur. The area was historically inhabited by Manobo, Mandaya, Mamanwa and Kamayo peoples; the Kamayo language remains widely spoken in the southern municipalities.

Geography and attractions

The province stretches along Mindanao's Pacific coast, backed by the Diwata Mountain Range. The Hinatuan Enchanted River, a deep spring river of striking blue water, and the multi-tiered Tinuy-an Falls — nicknamed the "Niagara Falls of the Philippines" — are its best-known natural attractions, alongside the surf and island-hopping sites of Lanuza and Cantilan and the Britania Islands in San Agustin. Typhoons periodically strike the coast, most severely Tropical Storm Auring in 2021 and Super Typhoon Odette (Rai) in December 2021.
