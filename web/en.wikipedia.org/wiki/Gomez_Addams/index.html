Gomez Addams

Gomez Addams is a fictional character from The Addams Family multimedia franchise, created by cartoonist Charles Addams. He is the patriarch of the Addams family, the husband of Morticia Addams, and the father of Wednesday and Pugsley Addams.

Character

Gomez is a flamboyant, childlike, and exuberant man of Castilian Spanish descent, invariably dressed in a chalk-striped suit with slicked-back hair and a pencil moustache. A former lawyer and independently wealthy, he delights in losing money on absurd stock-market gambles. He is passionately devoted to Morticia: whenever she speaks French, he is driven into a frenzy of kissing her arm, exclaiming "Tish! That's French!" His hobbies include playing with model trains (which he crashes for fun), fencing, and smoking cigars.

Portrayals

Gomez was played by John Astin in the 1964–1966 television series, by Raul Julia in the films The Addams Family (1991) and Addams Family Values (1993), by Tim Curry in Addams Family Reunion (1998), voiced by Oscar Isaac in the animated films of 2019 and 2021, and by Luis Guzmán in the Netflix series Wednesday (2022).
