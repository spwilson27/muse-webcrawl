Deimos (deity)

Deimos is the Greek god of terror and dread, son of Ares and Aphrodite and twin brother of Phobos (fear), who accompanied his father into battle.

Mythology

Deimos personified the terror felt before and during battle. He appears with Ares and Phobos in epic poetry and on depictions of warriors.

Namesake

The smaller moon of Mars, discovered in 1877 by Asaph Hall, is named Deimos, matching Mars as the Roman counterpart of Ares.

Family

His siblings included Phobos, Harmonia, Eros in some traditions through Aphrodite, and other children of Ares.
