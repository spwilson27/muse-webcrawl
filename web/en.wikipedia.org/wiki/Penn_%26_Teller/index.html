Penn & Teller are an American magic duo consisting of Penn Jillette (born 1955), the act's tall, vocal spokesman, and Raymond Teller (born 1948), who traditionally performs in silence. Together since the mid-1970s, they are known for combining elaborate illusions with comedy, and for frequently explaining how tricks work while performing them.

Career

The duo rose to prominence with Off-Broadway and Broadway shows in the 1980s, followed by frequent television appearances. Their Showtime series Penn & Teller: Bullshit! (2003–2010) applied skeptical scrutiny to pseudoscience and popular claims. Since 2011 they have hosted Penn & Teller: Fool Us, in which aspiring magicians attempt to perform a trick the duo cannot work out.

Style

Their act mixes classic sleight of hand, large-scale illusions, and tricks that expose their own methods as a form of misdirection. Both are prominent advocates of skepticism, atheism, and libertarian thought, and have long held a headline residency in Las Vegas.
