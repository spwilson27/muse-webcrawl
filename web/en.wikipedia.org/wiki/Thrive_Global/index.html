Thrive Global is an American behaviour-change technology and media company founded by Arianna Huffington in 2016. The company aims to improve employee well-being and performance by combating burnout and stress, drawing on Huffington's books Thrive (2014) and The Sleep Revolution (2016).

History

Huffington announced Thrive Global in August 2016, after stepping down as editor-in-chief of the Huffington Post. The venture launched in November 2016 with corporate training programmes, workshops, and an online media platform featuring content on health, well-being, and productivity. The company has raised venture funding from investors including Institutional Venture Partners and Lerer Hippeau, and has partnered with organisations such as Accenture, JPMorgan Chase, and Walmart on well-being initiatives.

Products and services

Thrive Global offers a behaviour-change platform that combines storytelling and content with small, incremental habit recommendations ("microsteps") covering sleep, food, movement, focus, and connection. In 2020 the company acquired the behaviour-change app company Boundless Mind. Its offerings are delivered through corporate partnerships and integrations with workplace tools.
