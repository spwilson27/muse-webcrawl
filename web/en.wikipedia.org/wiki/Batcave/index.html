Batcave

The Batcave is a fictional underground headquarters in DC Comics, serving as the base of operations for the superhero Batman (Bruce Wayne). Located in caverns beneath Wayne Manor outside Gotham City, it is reached by secret entrances, classically through a grandfather clock in the manor's study or by an elevator hidden behind a bookcase.

Contents

The cave houses the Batmobile and other vehicles, a crime laboratory, workshops, a gymnasium, archives and the Batcomputer, as well as a trophy room displaying souvenirs of past cases — most famously a giant penny, a mechanical Tyrannosaurus rex and an oversized Joker playing card. A colony of bats inhabits the caverns above.

History in publication

A "secret laboratory" beneath Wayne Manor appeared in early comics and the 1943 Batman film serial; the cave was named the Batcave in the 1940s and expanded over decades of stories into the high-technology complex familiar from the 1966 television series, Tim Burton and Christopher Nolan films, and animated series.
