Star Wars: The Last Jedi

Star Wars: The Last Jedi (also known as Star Wars: Episode VIII – The Last Jedi) is a 2017 American epic space opera film written and directed by Rian Johnson. It is the second installment of the Star Wars sequel trilogy, following The Force Awakens (2015), and the eighth episode of the Skywalker saga.

Plot

As the First Order closes in on the fleeing Resistance fleet, Rey (Daisy Ridley) seeks training from the exiled Luke Skywalker (Mark Hamill) on the remote island of Ahch-To, hoping to bring him back to the fight. Luke, embittered by his failure with his nephew Ben Solo — now Kylo Ren (Adam Driver) — refuses, even as Rey forms a mysterious Force connection with Ren himself. Meanwhile Poe Dameron (Oscar Isaac), Finn (John Boyega), and the mechanic Rose Tico (Kelly Marie Tran) pursue a desperate plan to disable the First Order's tracking device.

The film culminates on the salt planet Crait, where Luke projects an image of himself across the galaxy to confront Kylo Ren and buy the Resistance time to escape, dying peacefully on Ahch-To as his projection fades.

Production and reception

The ensemble cast includes Carrie Fisher in her final film role as Leia Organa. Filmed principally at Pinewood Studios and on location in Ireland and Croatia, the film grossed over $1.3 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing film of 2017. It received strong critical reviews for its direction, performances, and visuals, though audience reaction was notably divided, particularly over its portrayal of Luke Skywalker and its subversion of saga conventions.
