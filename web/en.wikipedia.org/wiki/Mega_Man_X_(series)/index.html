Mega Man X (series)

Mega Man X (known in Japan as Rockman X) is a series of action-platform video games developed by Capcom, and a sub-series of the Mega Man franchise. It began with Mega Man X for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System in 1993/1994 and comprises eight main titles through Mega Man X8 (2004), along with spin-offs, remakes, and collections.

Setting and story

The series is set roughly a century after the original Mega Man series, in a world of advanced sentient robots called Reploids, modelled on X — the final creation of Dr Light, sealed away for decades of ethical testing. When Reploids go rogue ("Maverick"), the Maverick Hunters, including X and his partner Zero, fight them, most often against the recurring antagonist Sigma. The tone is darker and more story-driven than the classic series, and the continuity continues into the Mega Man Zero and Mega Man ZX series.

Gameplay

The games retain the run-and-gun platforming and boss-weapon copying of the classic series but add wall-jumping, dashing, chargeable weapons, hidden armour upgrades (in capsules left by Dr Light), ride armours, and, in later entries, multiple playable characters such as Zero (wielding the Z-Saber) and Axl.
