Noah Hawley

Noah Hawley (born 1967) is an American author, television writer, producer and showrunner. He is best known as the creator and primary writer of the FX anthology series Fargo (2014– ) and Legion (2017–2019), and as the creator of the series Alien: Earth (2025). He is also the author of five novels, including Before the Fall (2016).

Hawley created the acclaimed FX anthology series Fargo.

Career

After working in publishing and as a paralegal, Hawley wrote for the television series Bones and created the short-lived series The Unusuals (2009) and My Generation (2010). His breakthrough came with Fargo, inspired by the Coen brothers' 1996 film, which premiered in 2014 to widespread acclaim and won multiple Emmy Awards. He followed with Legion, a visually experimental Marvel series centred on David Haller. Hawley made his feature directorial debut with Lucy in the Sky (2019), and created the first television series in the Alien franchise, Alien: Earth, which premiered in 2025.

Novels

Hawley's fiction includes A Conspiracy of Tall Men (1998), Other People's Weddings (2004), The Punch (2008), The Good Father (2012), the bestselling thriller Before the Fall (2016), which won the Edgar Award for Best Novel, and Anthem (2022).
