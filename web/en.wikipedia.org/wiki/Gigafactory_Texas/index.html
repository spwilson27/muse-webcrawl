Gigafactory Texas

Gigafactory Texas (also known as Giga Texas or Giga Austin) is an automotive manufacturing facility for Tesla, Inc. in Austin, Texas. The factory, announced in July 2020, is Tesla's main vehicle factory for the eastern United States and also serves as the company's corporate headquarters following Tesla's relocation from California in 2021.

Location: Austin, Texas, U.S.
Opened: 7 April 2022
Operated by: Tesla, Inc.
Products: Model Y, Cybertruck, battery cells

The plant sits on about 2,500 acres along the Colorado River near Austin–Bergstrom International Airport. Its main building is more than 1.2 km (0.75 mi) long. Construction began in July 2020, and the factory opened with a "Cyber Rodeo" event on 7 April 2022, delivering its first production Model Y vehicles with 4680 structural battery packs.

Products

Gigafactory Texas builds the Tesla Model Y and, from late 2023, the Tesla Cybertruck. The site also produces 4680-format battery cells and cathodes, and employs over 20,000 people, making it one of the largest manufacturing facilities in the United States by floor area.
