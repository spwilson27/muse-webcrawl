Overview

Ürümqi is the capital of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region in northwestern China and, with several million inhabitants, the largest city in Central Asia. It claims the distinction of being the major city most distant from any coastline — the Eurasian pole of inaccessibility lies in the desert to its north.

History and geography

The city grew from a Qing-dynasty garrison town (Dihua) commanding routes between China proper and the Tarim Basin, and was renamed Ürümqi ("fine pasture" in Oirat Mongol) in 1954. It sits against the eastern Tianshan (Bogda Shan). July 2009 riots between Uyghurs and Han Chinese killed nearly 200 people by official count and led to a long communications shutdown.
