Declaratory Act

The Declaratory Act of 1766 was an act of the Parliament of Great Britain accompanying the repeal of the Stamp Act.

While repealing the unpopular stamp tax, Parliament asserted its authority to make laws binding the American colonies "in all cases whatsoever" — including the right to tax them. Colonists celebrated the repeal but rejected the declaratory principle, and the dispute over parliamentary sovereignty became a central cause of the American Revolution.
