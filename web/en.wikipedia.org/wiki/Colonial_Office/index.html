Colonial Office

The Colonial Office was a government department of the Kingdom of Great Britain and later of the United Kingdom, first created in 1768 to deal with the colonial affairs of British North America, and responsible thereafter for administering the British Empire's colonies until its abolition in 1966.

History

A Secretary of State for the Colonies was first appointed in 1768; the post lapsed in 1782 and colonial affairs passed between the Home and War Offices before a separate Colonial Office was re-established in 1854. At its peak it administered dozens of colonies, protectorates and mandates.

Abolition

As decolonisation shrank the empire, the Colonial Office merged with the Commonwealth Relations Office in 1966 to form the Commonwealth Office, which itself merged into the Foreign and Commonwealth Office in 1968.
