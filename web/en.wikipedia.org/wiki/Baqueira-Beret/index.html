Baqueira-Beret

Baqueira-Beret is a ski resort located in the heart of the Pyrenees, in the Val d'Aran and Àneu valleys of Catalonia, Spain. It is the largest and most-visited winter resort in Spain, with a ski area of more than 160 kilometres of marked pistes and a lift capacity among the highest in the country.

Geography

The resort spans the municipalities of Naut Aran and Alt Àneu, with base areas at Baqueira (1,500 m), Beret (1,850 m), and Bonaigua. Its Atlantic-facing slopes receive abundant snowfall relative to other Pyrenean resorts.

History

Baqueira opened in December 1964 and expanded steadily over subsequent decades. It has long been a favoured resort of the Spanish royal family and has hosted FIS Alpine Ski World Cup events. The nearby village of Baqueira developed as a purpose-built resort settlement of hotels and apartments in traditional stone-and-slate Aranese style.
