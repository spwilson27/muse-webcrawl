The Spiel des Jahres (German for "Game of the Year") is a German award for board and card games, created in 1978 and awarded annually by a jury of German-speaking game critics. It is widely considered the most prestigious award in the board-game industry, and winning it can multiply a game's sales many times over.

The awards

The main award honours family games accessible to a broad audience. Since 1989 there has also been a Kinderspiel des Jahres for children's games, and since 2011 the Kennerspiel des Jahres for more complex "connoisseur" games. Famous winners of the main award include The Settlers of Catan (1995), Carcassonne (2001), Ticket to Ride (2004), Dominion (2009), Codenames (2016) and Cascadia (2022).

Influence

The award helped establish Germany as the centre of modern board-game design and popularised the style of game sometimes called the "German-style" or Eurogame, emphasising strategy and low player conflict over luck and elimination.
