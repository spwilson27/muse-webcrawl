BCE Inc. (Bell Canada Enterprises) is a Canadian telecommunications and media holding company, the parent of Bell Canada and Bell Media. Headquartered in Montreal, it is one of Canada's largest corporations and its largest telecommunications company by revenue.

History

The company traces its origin to the Bell Telephone Company of Canada, incorporated in 1880. In 1983 a corporate reorganisation created BCE as the holding company for Bell Canada and other assets. BCE once held interests in Nortel Networks and also owned stakes in various technology and satellite ventures.

Operations

Through Bell Canada it provides wireless (Bell Mobility), broadband internet, television (Bell Fibe TV and satellite) and landline services. Bell Media owns the CTV television network, specialty channels and radio stations. A proposed leveraged buyout in 2007–08, which would have been the largest in history at the time, collapsed during the financial crisis.
