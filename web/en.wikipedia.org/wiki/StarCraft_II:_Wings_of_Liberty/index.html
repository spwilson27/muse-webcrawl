StarCraft II: Wings of Liberty

DeveloperBlizzard Entertainment
PublisherBlizzard Entertainment
ReleaseJuly 27, 2010
GenreReal-time strategy
PlatformsMicrosoft Windows, macOS

StarCraft II: Wings of Liberty is a real-time strategy video game developed and published by Blizzard Entertainment. It was released worldwide on July 27, 2010, as the sequel to the 1998 game StarCraft. The game is set in the 26th century in the Koprulu sector of the Milky Way, where three species — the Terrans (human exiles), the insectoid Zerg, and the psionic Protoss — fight for dominance. Wings of Liberty contains a single-player campaign focused on the Terran protagonist Jim Raynor, plus a full multiplayer mode featuring all three races.

Gameplay

As in the original, players gather resources (minerals and vespene gas), construct bases, and produce military units with complementary strengths and weaknesses. Each race plays distinctly: Terrans are versatile and defensive, Zerg overwhelm with cheap mass-produced units, and Protoss field few but powerful units. The campaign includes branching mission choices, purchasable upgrades, and research decisions. Multiplayer was served through Blizzard's Battle.net platform with a ranked ladder and league system.

Reception and sales

The game sold more than 1.5 million copies in its first two days and over 3 million in its first month, becoming the best-selling PC game of 2010. Critics praised its polished gameplay, campaign variety, and production values, while some criticized the decision to split the story into three games and the lack of LAN play. Wings of Liberty was followed by two expansions, Heart of the Swarm (2013, Zerg campaign) and Legacy of the Void (2015, Protoss campaign). In 2017 the base game became free-to-play.

Esports

StarCraft II became a major esport, inheriting the professional scene built around the original game in South Korea, where leagues such as the GSL (Global StarCraft II League) drew large audiences. At its height it was among the most-watched competitive games in the world and helped popularize esports streaming in the West.
