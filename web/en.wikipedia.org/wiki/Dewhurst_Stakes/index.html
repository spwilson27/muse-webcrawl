Dewhurst Stakes

Dewhurst Stakes
Location: Newmarket Racecourse, England
Race type: Flat / Thoroughbred
Distance: 7 furlongs (1,408 metres)
Surface: Turf
Qualification: Two-year-olds

The Dewhurst Stakes is a Group 1 flat horse race in Great Britain open to two-year-old horses. It is run on the Rowley Mile course at Newmarket over a distance of 7 furlongs (1,408 metres), and it is scheduled to take place each year in October.

History

The event was founded in 1875 and was originally titled the Dewhurst Plate. It was named after Dewhurst Stud, a thoroughbred breeding operation located in Sussex owned by Captain Henry de la Poer Beresford. The race was established by Thomas Gee, who established Dewhurst Stud in 1874.

The Dewhurst Stakes is Britain's most prestigious race for two-year-olds. The leading participants usually become major contenders for the following season's 2000 Guineas, and the race has been won by many subsequent classic winners, including Frankel, who won impressively in 2010. It forms part of the Future Champions Festival at Newmarket.

Records

Leading jockeys in the race include Lester Piggott and Frankie Dettori, and leading trainers include Aidan O'Brien and Henry Cecil. Winners such as Nijinsky, Mill Reef, Grundy, El Gran Senor, Generous, Rock of Gibraltar and City of Troy have gone on to major success at three.
