Defensive design

Defensive design is the practice of designing products, systems and software to continue functioning or fail safely under misuse, errors and unforeseen circumstances.

Principles

It anticipates invalid inputs, component failures and user mistakes, using validation, redundancy, clear feedback and safe defaults.

Software

Defensive programming checks assumptions, handles exceptions and avoids undefined states.

Everyday examples

Examples include circuit breakers, overflow drains, confirmation prompts and guardrails.
