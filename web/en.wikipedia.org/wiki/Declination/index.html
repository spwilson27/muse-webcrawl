Declination

In astronomy, declination (symbol delta) is one of two coordinates of the equatorial coordinate system, analogous to latitude on Earth: the angular distance of a point north or south of the celestial equator, measured in degrees, arcminutes and arcseconds.

Use

Together with right ascension, declination locates any object on the celestial sphere. The Sun's declination varies between +23.4 and -23.4 degrees over the year, causing the seasons. Objects at declination near +90 are circumpolar for northern observers.

Variations

Declinations shift slowly due to precession, so coordinates are quoted for an epoch such as J2000. Magnetic declination - the angle between true and magnetic north - is a separate, navigational concept.
