Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty (RFE/RL) is a United States government-funded international media organization that broadcasts and reports news, information, and analysis in 27 languages to 23 countries in Eastern Europe, Central Asia, the Caucasus, and the Middle East, where it says the free flow of information is either banned by authorities or not fully developed.

History

Radio Free Europe was founded in 1949 by the National Committee for a Free Europe as an anti-communist propaganda source, broadcasting to Soviet satellite states from Munich, West Germany; its first broadcast, to Czechoslovakia, aired on 4 July 1950. Radio Liberty, originally Radio Liberation, began broadcasting to the Soviet Union in 1953. Both were covertly funded by the Central Intelligence Agency until 1972, after which they received overt congressional funding through the Board for International Broadcasting and later the Broadcasting Board of Governors, now the U.S. Agency for Global Media.

Operations

RFE/RL moved its headquarters from Munich to Prague in 1995 at the invitation of Czech president Václav Havel. It operates bureaus across its broadcast region and produces radio, television, and digital content, including the Persian-language Radio Farda (a joint venture with Voice of America) and Current Time, a Russian-language television network launched in 2017.

Controversies

Authoritarian governments have jammed its signals, blocked its websites, and designated it a "foreign agent" — notably Russia in 2017 — while critics have questioned the independence of state-funded media. RFE/RL maintains an editorial firewall codified in U.S. law.
