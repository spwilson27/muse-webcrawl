Cary Guffey

Cary Guffey (born May 27, 1972) is an American former child actor and financial planner. He is best known for playing Barry Guiler, the young boy abducted by aliens, in Steven Spielberg's Close Encounters of the Third Kind (1977), cast at age four from his home state of Alabama.

Career

Spielberg praised Guffey's natural reactions, achieved by surprising him off-camera. He later appeared in the Italian science-fiction comedies The Sheriff and the Satellite Kid (1979) and Why Did You Pick on Me? (1980) with Bud Spencer, the miniseries Chiefs (1983), and Stroker Ace (1983), retiring from acting as a teenager.

Later life

Guffey studied marketing at Jacksonville State University and worked in finance in Birmingham, Alabama, later becoming a financial planner, occasionally appearing at science-fiction conventions.
