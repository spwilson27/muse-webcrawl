Ken Townsend
Occupation: Audio engineer
Employer: Abbey Road Studios (EMI)
Known for: Work with the Beatles

Ken Townsend is an English audio engineer best known for his work at EMI's Abbey Road Studios in London, where he engineered numerous recordings by the Beatles during the 1960s.

Work with the Beatles

Townsend is credited with inventing Artificial Double Tracking (ADT) in 1966 during the sessions for Revolver, after John Lennon asked for a way to sound as if he had sung his vocal twice without re-recording it. ADT was created by copying a vocal to a second tape machine with a variable-speed oscillator and mixing it back slightly delayed. Townsend also contributed to techniques such as flanging.

Later career

Townsend later became manager of Abbey Road Studios and served as president of the Audio Engineering Society. He was appointed MBE for services to music.
