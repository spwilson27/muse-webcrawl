StarCraft: Brood War

Developer: Blizzard Entertainment; Saffire
Publisher: Blizzard Entertainment
Release: 30 November 1998
Genre: Real-time strategy (expansion pack)
Requires: StarCraft

StarCraft: Brood War is the expansion pack for the real-time strategy game StarCraft, developed by Blizzard Entertainment and Saffire and released in 1998. It continues the story of the Koprulu sector after the death of the Zerg Overmind, centring on Sarah Kerrigan's rise to power as Queen of Blades, and introduces new campaigns, units and maps. Brood War is widely considered the definitive version of the original StarCraft and the foundation of professional StarCraft competition in South Korea.

Campaign

The expansion's three campaigns follow the Protoss exodus to the dark templar homeworld of Shakuras and their alliance against the Zerg; the invasion of the Koprulu sector by the United Earth Directorate (UED), which seeks to subjugate the sector and enslaves a new Overmind; and Kerrigan's campaign of manipulation and betrayal that leaves her the dominant power in the sector. The story is notably darker than the original, ending with Kerrigan's victory and the exhaustion of all her enemies.

New units and balance

Brood War added two units per race, carefully designed to deepen strategy without breaking balance: the Terran Medic and Valkyrie frigate; the Zerg Lurker (a burrowing area-denial unit evolved from the Hydralisk) and Devourer; and the Protoss Dark Templar (permanently cloaked infantry, previously a campaign-only hero unit), Dark Archon and Corsair. New tilesets and campaign mechanics were also included. These additions are credited with perfecting StarCraft's competitive balance, helping the game remain a premier esport for over a decade; it was remastered alongside the base game in 2017's StarCraft: Remastered.

Reception

The expansion was critically acclaimed and sold millions of copies. In South Korea, Brood War tournaments on channels such as OnGameNet and MBCGame drew huge audiences, and the game is considered the archetypal esport title. See StarCraft in esports.
