Mid-size car

A mid-size car is an automobile size class originating in the United States for vehicles larger than a compact car and smaller than a full-size car. In Europe the equivalent is generally the D-segment.

Definitions

The US Environmental Protection Agency classifies mid-size cars by combined interior and cargo volume. Best-known examples have included the Toyota Camry, Honda Accord, Ford Fusion and Chevrolet Malibu, long among the best-selling passenger cars in North America.

Market

Mid-size sedans dominated family car sales for decades before buyers shifted toward crossover SUVs in the 2010s, shrinking the segment in North America while D-segment models remained significant in Europe and Asia.
