Overview

The Spencer Davis Group was a British beat and R&B band formed in Birmingham in 1963 by Spencer Davis (guitar), the brothers Steve Winwood (vocals, keyboards, guitar) and Muff Winwood (bass), and Pete York (drums). Though named for Davis, the band was powered by the extraordinary talent of the teenage Steve Winwood, whose soulful voice and organ playing drew comparisons to Ray Charles.

Career

After signing with Island Records founder Chris Blackwell's organisation, the group scored UK number-one hits with 'Keep On Running' (1965) and 'Somebody Help Me' (1966), followed by 'Gimme Some Lovin'' (1966) and 'I'm a Man' (1967), both co-written by Steve Winwood and major hits in the United States as well.

Steve Winwood left in 1967 to form Traffic, and although Davis continued the group with new lineups, its commercial fortunes waned; the band split in 1969. 'Gimme Some Lovin'' remains a rock standard, covered by many artists including the Blues Brothers. Davis re-formed versions of the group in later decades and performed until his death in 2020.
