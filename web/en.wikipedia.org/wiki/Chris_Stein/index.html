Chris Stein

Christopher Stein (born January 5, 1950) is an American musician and photographer, best known as the guitarist and co-founder of the new wave band Blondie, which he formed with Debbie Harry in 1974.

Chris Stein
Born: January 5, 1950, Brooklyn, New York
Genres: New wave, punk rock, pop
Instruments: Guitar, bass
Associated acts: Blondie

Career

Stein co-wrote many of Blondie's hits, including "Heart of Glass" and "Rapture". He co-founded Animal Records and produced acts associated with the downtown New York scene. In the 1980s he was diagnosed with pemphigus vulgaris, a rare autoimmune disease, during which Harry cared for him; he recovered and Blondie later reformed.

Photography

Stein documented the 1970s New York punk and hip-hop scenes; his books include Negative: Me, Blondie and the Advent of Punk (2014) and Point of View (2018).
