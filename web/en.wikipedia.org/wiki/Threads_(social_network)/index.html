Threads is a social media microblogging service developed by Meta Platforms, launched on July 5, 2023. Closely integrated with Instagram, it allows users to post text, images, and videos and to engage in public conversations, positioning it as a direct competitor to Twitter (now X).

History

Meta developed Threads following the acquisition of Twitter by Elon Musk in 2022, amid advertiser and user uncertainty about that platform. Accounts on Threads are tied to Instagram accounts, letting users import their profiles and follow lists. The app reached 100 million sign-ups within five days of launch, making it the fastest-growing consumer application in history at the time. An initial decline in daily active users followed, after which Meta added features including a desktop web version, a following feed, search, editing, voice posts, and hashtags ("tags"). Threads launched in the European Union in December 2023 after regulatory delays.

Features and federation

Posts can be up to 500 characters and include links, photos, and videos up to five minutes long. Meta has integrated Threads with the ActivityPub protocol, allowing limited federation with the fediverse (such as Mastodon), with posts from Threads users able to be followed from compatible platforms. The service is free and funded through Meta's broader advertising business, with advertising introduced in 2025.

Reception and rivalry with X

Threads has been characterized in the press as a "Twitter killer" attempt; its launch prompted a legal threat from X Corp. and a much-publicized proposed "cage match" between Musk and Mark Zuckerberg that never occurred. By late 2024, Meta reported around 275–320 million monthly active users, and Threads has continued to grow as one of the largest text-based social networks.
