Borage

Borage (Borago officinalis), also known as starflower, is an annual herb in the flowering plant family Boraginaceae, native to the Mediterranean region. Its leaves and vivid blue, star-shaped flowers have a mild cucumber flavour and are used in salads, soups and drinks — the flower is a traditional garnish for the Pimm's cocktail.

The plant grows to about 60 cm tall, is covered in coarse bristly hairs, and self-seeds readily. It is valued by gardeners as a companion plant that attracts bees and other pollinators. Borage seed oil is one of the richest plant sources of gamma-linolenic acid (GLA) and is sold as a dietary supplement; the herb was traditionally believed to promote courage and cheerfulness, a reputation reflected in its use since classical times.
