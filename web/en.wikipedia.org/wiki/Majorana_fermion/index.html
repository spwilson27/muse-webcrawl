Majorana fermion

A Majorana fermion, proposed by Ettore Majorana in 1937, is a fermion that is its own antiparticle. This contrasts with Dirac fermions (such as electrons), which have distinct antiparticles.

Particle physics

Neutrinos may be Majorana fermions; experiments searching for neutrinoless double beta decay test this, which would have implications for the origin of neutrino mass and the matter–antimatter asymmetry via leptogenesis. Supersymmetric theories also predict Majorana particles such as neutralinos.

Condensed matter

Quasiparticles called Majorana zero modes can occur in topological superconductors and certain nanowires; they obey non-Abelian statistics and are pursued as robust qubits for topological quantum computing. High-profile experimental claims in 2012–2018 drew excitement, though a key 2018 result was later retracted.
