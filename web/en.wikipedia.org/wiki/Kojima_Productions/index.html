Kojima Productions Co., Ltd. is a Japanese video game development studio founded by game designer Hideo Kojima. The name was originally used for a production division within Konami, formed in 2005, which developed the Metal Gear series. Following Kojima's departure from Konami in 2015, he re-established Kojima Productions as an independent studio, announcing it on December 16, 2015 in partnership with Sony Interactive Entertainment.

History

The studio's mascot, Ludens, was designed by artist Yoji Shinkawa. Its first game, Death Stranding, was released for PlayStation 4 in November 2019 to critical acclaim for its originality, and later ported to PC and other platforms. A sequel, Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, was released in 2025. The studio has also announced further projects including the horror game OD with Xbox Game Studios and the action-espionage title Physint, and expanded into film and other media.
