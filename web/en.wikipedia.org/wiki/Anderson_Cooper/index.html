Anderson Cooper

Anderson Cooper
Born3 June 1967, New York City
OccupationJournalist, CNN anchor
[Image: Cooper reporting live during storm coverage]

Anderson Cooper (born 1967) is an American journalist and author, anchor of CNN's Anderson Cooper 360° since 2003 and a 60 Minutes correspondent, known for on-the-ground disaster and war reporting — notably Hurricane Katrina (2005) and the Haiti earthquake (2010). He is a son of Gloria Vanderbilt and a member of the Vanderbilt family.

Career

Cooper began by shooting his own reports for Channel One News with a forged press pass, joined ABC News, then CNN in 2001. His books (Dispatches from the Edge; Vanderbilt, with Katherine Howe) mix memoir and family history.
