Hong Kong permanent resident

A Hong Kong permanent resident is a person who holds the right of abode in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of China under the Basic Law and the Immigration Ordinance. Permanent residents enjoy rights including the right to vote in certain elections, unrestricted entry to and residence in Hong Kong, and protection from deportation.

Acquisition

Categories of permanent residents include Chinese citizens born in Hong Kong, Chinese citizens who have ordinarily resided in Hong Kong for a continuous period of at least seven years, and persons of Chinese nationality born outside Hong Kong to such residents. Non-Chinese citizens may also qualify after seven years' ordinary residence, subject to conditions, but can lose the status after long absences. Permanent residents are issued Hong Kong permanent identity cards and are eligible for the Hong Kong SAR passport if they are Chinese citizens.
