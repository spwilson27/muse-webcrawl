Márta Károlyi

Márta Károlyi (née Erős, born 29 August 1942) is a Romanian-born American gymnastics coach of Hungarian ethnicity. Together with her husband Béla Károlyi she coached in Romania, most famously guiding Nadia Comăneci, before the couple defected to the United States in 1981.

Career in the United States

The Károlyis built a training centre in Texas and coached American champions including Mary Lou Retton. From 2001 to 2016 Márta Károlyi served as national team coordinator for USA Gymnastics' women's programme, overseeing a dominant era that produced team gold medals at the 2012 and 2016 Olympics and stars such as Simone Biles, Gabby Douglas, and Aly Raisman. The remote Karolyi Ranch in Texas served as the women's national team training centre during her tenure.

Later scrutiny

After her retirement following the Rio 2016 Games, the Károlyis and their ranch became subjects of scrutiny in the USA Gymnastics abuse scandal centred on team doctor Larry Nassar; the ranch was closed as a training site in 2018. Márta Károlyi has denied knowledge of the abuse.
