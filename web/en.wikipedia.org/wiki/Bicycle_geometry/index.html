Bicycle geometry

Key measuresStack, reach, head angle, trail, wheelbase
AffectsHandling, fit, stability

Bicycle geometry is the set of measurements that define a bicycle frame's dimensions and angles, determining how the bike fits its rider and how it handles. Key parameters include stack and reach, head tube angle, fork rake and trail, chainstay length and wheelbase.

Handling

A slacker head angle and longer wheelbase produce stable handling favoured by mountain bikes and endurance road bikes, while steeper angles and shorter stays give the quicker steering of racing bikes. Trail — the distance between the steering axis contact point and the tyre contact patch — strongly influences self-centring behaviour.

Fit

Stack and reach describe the position of the handlebars relative to the bottom bracket and are the standard modern way to compare frame sizes across brands.
