Bettie Page

Born22 April 1923, Nashville, Tennessee, U.S.
Died11 December 2008 (aged 85), Los Angeles, California, U.S.
OccupationModel
Years active1950–1957

Bettie Mae Page (22 April 1923 – 11 December 2008) was an American model who gained notoriety in the 1950s for her pin-up photographs. Known as the "Queen of Pinups," her jet-black hair, blue eyes and trademark bangs influenced generations of artists, photographers and fashion designers.

Career

Page began modelling in New York in 1950, appearing in men's magazines and in the mail-order fetish photographs of Irving Klaw. She was Playboy's Playmate of the Month for January 1955. Her work drew the attention of a U.S. Senate committee investigating pornography in 1955.

Later life and legacy

In 1957 Page left modelling, became a born-again Christian, and lived for decades in relative obscurity, unaware of her cult following. A revival of interest in the 1980s and 1990s brought her renewed fame and income. She died in Los Angeles in 2008 and remains an enduring icon of 1950s Americana, commemorated in films, biographies and popular art.
