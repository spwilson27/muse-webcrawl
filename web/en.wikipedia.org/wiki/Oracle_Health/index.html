Oracle Health

Oracle Health
Formerly: Cerner Corporation
Industry: Health care information technology
Founded: 1979 (as Cerner)
Parent: Oracle Corporation (from 2022)

Oracle Health, formerly Cerner Corporation, is a business unit of Oracle Corporation that supplies electronic health record (EHR) systems and other health information technology to hospitals and health systems. Its flagship product is the Oracle Health Millennium platform, an integrated clinical, financial and operational record system used by thousands of hospitals worldwide.

History

Cerner was founded in 1979 in Kansas City, Missouri, by Neal Patterson, Paul Gorup and Cliff Illig, former Arthur Andersen consultants. The company grew with its Millennium architecture, launched in 1997, to become one of the two dominant US hospital EHR vendors alongside Epic Systems. In December 2021 Oracle announced its acquisition of Cerner for approximately $28.3 billion — Oracle's largest acquisition — completed in June 2022, and the business was renamed Oracle Health.

Products and projects

Oracle Health supplies the EHR used by the US Department of Veterans Affairs under a major modernisation contract (initially awarded to Cerner in 2018), the US Department of Defense's MHS GENESIS system, and many NHS and international customers. Oracle has announced plans for a next-generation, cloud-native EHR with an AI-driven voice interface, and in 2024 began relocating the unit's headquarters functions to Nashville, Tennessee.
