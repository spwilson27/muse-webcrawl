Deadpool (film)

Deadpool is a 2016 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. It is the eighth installment in the X-Men film series. The film was directed by Tim Miller from a screenplay by Rhett Reese and Paul Wernick, and stars Ryan Reynolds in the title role alongside Morena Baccarin, Ed Skrein, T. J. Miller, and Gina Carano.

Plot

In the film, mercenary Wade Wilson undergoes an experimental treatment that leaves him with accelerated healing powers and a disfigured appearance, and hunts down the man responsible. The character frequently breaks the fourth wall.

Reception

Released in February 2016, Deadpool grossed over $782 million worldwide on a $58 million budget, becoming the highest-grossing R-rated film at the time and earning praise for Reynolds's performance, the humor, and the action. A sequel, Deadpool 2, was released in 2018.
