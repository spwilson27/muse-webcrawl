CNH Industrial

Industry: Capital goods
Founded: 2013 (merger); roots 1842
Headquarters: Basildon, United Kingdom
Products: Agricultural and construction equipment
Brands: Case IH, New Holland, Steyr, Iveco (until 2022)

CNH Industrial N.V. is an American-Italian multinational corporation that designs, produces, and sells agricultural machinery and construction equipment. Its brands include Case IH, New Holland Agriculture, and Steyr in agriculture, and CASE Construction Equipment and New Holland Construction in construction equipment.

History

CNH Industrial was formed in September 2013 through the merger of CNH Global—itself created in 1999 by the merger of Case Corporation and New Holland N.V.—with its majority owner Fiat Industrial S.p.A. The roots of its brands reach back to 1842 (Case) and to Abe Zimmerman's New Holland Machine Company of 1895.

In January 2022, CNH Industrial completed the spin-off of its on-highway businesses (trucks, buses, and powertrains, including Iveco and FPT Industrial) into the separate Iveco Group, leaving CNH focused on agriculture and construction. The company is incorporated in the Netherlands, operationally headquartered in Basildon, England, and listed on the New York Stock Exchange. It is controlled by Exor, the Agnelli family's holding company.
