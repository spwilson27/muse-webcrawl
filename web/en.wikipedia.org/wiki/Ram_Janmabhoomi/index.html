Ram Janmabhoomi

Ram Janmabhoomi is the site in Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh, India, believed by many Hindus to be the birthplace of Rama, the seventh avatar of Vishnu. The site was the location of the Babri Masjid, built in the 16th century, whose demolition in 1992 sparked nationwide riots and decades of litigation.

Dispute and resolution

The Ayodhya dispute centred on whether a temple had been demolished to build the mosque. In 2019 the Supreme Court of India awarded the disputed land for a Hindu temple and directed that alternative land be given for a mosque. The Ram Mandir was constructed and consecrated on 22 January 2024.
