Super Bowl LV

Super Bowl LV was an American football game played on February 7, 2021, at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, Florida, to determine the champion of the National Football League (NFL) for the 2020 season. The National Football Conference (NFC) champion Tampa Bay Buccaneers defeated the American Football Conference (AFC) champion and defending champion Kansas City Chiefs, 31–9.

Background

The game was the first Super Bowl in which a team played in its home stadium, the Buccaneers having reached the championship game in their home city. Attendance was limited to 24,835 fans because of the COVID-19 pandemic, including 7,500 vaccinated health care workers invited as guests of the league. Tampa Bay quarterback Tom Brady, in his first season with the Buccaneers after leaving the New England Patriots, appeared in his tenth Super Bowl.

Game summary

Tampa Bay's defence dominated, pressuring Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes throughout while holding Kansas City to three field goals and no touchdowns. Brady threw three touchdown passes — two to Rob Gronkowski and one to Antonio Brown — and Leonard Fournette added a rushing touchdown, as the Buccaneers built a 21–6 half-time lead and never looked back.

Records and aftermath

Brady was named Super Bowl MVP for a fifth time, and won his seventh championship, more than any single NFL franchise. At 43, he became the oldest player to appear in and win a Super Bowl as a starting quarterback. The halftime show was headlined by The Weeknd. Amanda Gorman read an original poem before the game, and Eric Church and Jazmine Sullivan performed the national anthem.
