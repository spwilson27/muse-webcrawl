University of Lausanne

The University of Lausanne (UNIL; French: Université de Lausanne) is a public research university in Lausanne, Switzerland, on the shore of Lake Geneva at Dorigny. Founded as a school of theology in 1537, it became a university in 1890.

Structure

UNIL has about 17,000 students and seven faculties, including law, business and economics (HEC Lausanne), biology and medicine, geosciences and environment, arts, social and political sciences, and theology. Teaching is primarily in French.

Campus

Since the 1970s the university has shared the Dorigny campus with EPFL, beside Lake Geneva, with its own metro stop.
