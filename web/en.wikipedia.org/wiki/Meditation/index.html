Meditation

Meditation
DefinitionPractices focusing attention to train awareness and calm
TraditionsBuddhist, Hindu, Taoist, secular mindfulness
EvidenceModest benefits for stress, anxiety, pain (clinical research)
[Image: A person meditating cross-legged on a cushion in soft window light]

Meditation is a practice in which an individual uses a technique — such as mindfulness, focusing on a particular object, thought or activity — to train attention and awareness and achieve a mentally clear and emotionally calm state.

Science and spread

Rooted in contemplative traditions (Buddhism, Tibetan Buddhism in this crawl), meditation was secularised as mindfulness-based stress reduction and exploded via apps in the 2010s. Clinical trials show modest-to-moderate benefits for anxiety, depression relapse and pain; fMRI studies map its effects in the Brain.
