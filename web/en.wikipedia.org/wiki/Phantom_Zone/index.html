Phantom Zone

The Phantom Zone is a fictional prison dimension in the Superman universe published by DC Comics. Created by writer Robert Bernstein and artist George Papp, it first appeared in Adventure Comics #283 (1961). Kryptonian criminals were projected into the Zone by a projector device; there they existed as intangible phantom-like wraiths who could observe, but not interact with, the physical world, and did not age.

Inhabitants

The most famous Phantom Zone prisoner is General Zod, along with Faora/Ursa and Non, who escaped to menace Superman in the films Superman II (1980) and Man of Steel (2013). Other prisoners in the comics include Jax-Ur, Quex-Ul, and Xa-Du. The hero Mon-El was confined to the Zone for a thousand years to survive lead poisoning until a cure was found by the Legion of Super-Heroes.

In other media

The Zone appears in the Supergirl television series as the place where Fort Rozz prisoners and later Kara Zor-El herself were stranded, and in The Flash (2023), where Zod's forces return from it. Its depiction usually involves a barren, spectral void entered through the Phantom Zone projector.
