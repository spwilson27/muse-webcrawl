"I Got You (I Feel Good)" is a song by American singer James Brown. First recorded in 1964 and re-recorded and released as a single in 1965, it is one of Brown's signature songs and the highest-charting single of his career, reaching No. 3 on the Billboard Hot 100 and No. 1 on the R&B chart. It is built on an arrangement developed from Brown's earlier song "I Found You", written for Yvonne Fair.

Music

The song rides a driving blues-form groove in D major, powered by the horn section — featuring Maceo Parker on alto saxophone, whose solo anchors the record — and punctuated by Brown's trademark screams and the refrain "I feel good". It extends the rhythmic revolution of "Papa's Got a Brand New Bag", released the same year.

Legacy

The song has ranked on Rolling Stone's list of the 500 greatest songs of all time and in the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame's 500 Songs that Shaped Rock and Roll. Ubiquitous in films, advertising and sporting events, it remains the recording most identified with Brown's exuberant stage persona.
