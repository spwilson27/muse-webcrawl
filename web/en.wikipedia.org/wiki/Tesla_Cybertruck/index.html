Tesla Cybertruck

The Tesla Cybertruck is a battery-electric pickup truck produced by Tesla, Inc. Unveiled as a concept in November 2019 — in a launch remembered for its "armour glass" windows cracking during a steel-ball demonstration — it entered production at Gigafactory Texas in late 2023, with first customer deliveries on 30 November 2023.

Design

The Cybertruck's wedge-shaped body is an exoskeleton of unpainted Ultra-Hard 30X cold-rolled stainless steel, intended to resist dents and corrosion. It introduced a 48-volt low-voltage architecture and steer-by-wire (with variable-ratio steering and rear-wheel steering) to a production vehicle. The powered stainless tonneau covers a 6-foot bed.

Specifications and reception

Launch versions included dual-motor all-wheel drive and a tri-motor "Cyberbeast", with range estimates up to about 340 miles (extended by an optional bed-mounted range-extender pack), towing capacity of 11,000 lb (4,990 kg), and a payload of about 2,500 lb (1,134 kg) — earlier publicity had sometimes conflated payload and curb weight figures. The design proved polarising; the truck nevertheless attracted large reservation numbers and became a cultural flashpoint, and it has been subject to several recalls typical of new-vehicle launches.
