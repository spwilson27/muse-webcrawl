A population is the number of people in a country, city or the world. Earth's human population passed 8 billion in 2022, having doubled since 1974 — growth now slowing as fertility falls below replacement in most countries.

Dynamics

Demographers track births, deaths and migration; the growth debates from Malthus to the population bomb gave way to worries about ageing and shrinking populations in East Asia and Europe. Every statistic in the Economy and Health pages divides, in the end, by population.
