Combined DNA Index System

The Combined DNA Index System (CODIS) is the United States national DNA database created and maintained by the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI). Established under the DNA Identification Act of 1994 and launched nationally in 1998, CODIS links DNA profiles from federal, state, and local crime laboratories so that evidence from unsolved crimes can be matched against offender profiles and against evidence from other crime scenes.

Structure

CODIS operates at three levels: the Local DNA Index System (LDIS), used by individual laboratories; the State DNA Index System (SDIS); and the National DNA Index System (NDIS), which is administered by the FBI and allows comparisons across state lines. The software that ties the levels together is also called CODIS. Indexes within the system include the convicted offender index, the forensic index (profiles from crime-scene evidence), the arrestee index, and indexes for missing persons and unidentified human remains.

DNA profiles

CODIS profiles are based on short tandem repeat (STR) markers. For many years the system used 13 "core loci"; in 2017 the FBI expanded the core set to 20 STR loci to increase discriminating power and improve international compatibility. A profile contains only the allele designations at these non-coding markers, the originating laboratory, and specimen identifiers — no medical or trait information.

Use and impact

Law prohibits the use of CODIS profiles for anything other than law-enforcement identification purposes, with penalties for unauthorized disclosure. By the 2020s the national index held well over ten million offender profiles and had produced hundreds of thousands of "hits" assisting criminal investigations, including cold cases. Familial searching and the later rise of forensic genetic genealogy (which uses separate commercial databases) are related but distinct practices and raised continuing policy debate about privacy and the scope of DNA collection, particularly from arrestees never convicted of a crime.
