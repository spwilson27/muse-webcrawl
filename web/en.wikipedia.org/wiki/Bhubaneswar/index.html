Bhubaneswar is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of Odisha, located in the eastern part of the country. The city has a history spanning over 3,000 years, beginning with the Mahamegha-bahana dynasty, and is famous for its ancient temples, which earned it the nickname "Temple City of India". Together with Puri and Konark, it forms the Swarna Tribhuja ("Golden Triangle"), one of eastern India's most visited pilgrimage circuits.

History

The region around Bhubaneswar was part of the ancient kingdom of Kalinga. The Kalinga War, fought nearby at Dhauli around 261 BCE, led Emperor Ashoka of the Mauryan Empire to embrace Buddhism, an event commemorated by the Shanti Stupa at Dhauli. From the 7th to the 13th centuries CE, Bhubaneswar flourished as a temple-building centre under Hindu dynasties including the Somavamshis and the Eastern Gangas. The Lingaraja Temple, dedicated to Shiva, was built around the 11th century and remains the city's most prominent landmark. The city was formally made the capital of Odisha in 1948, shortly after Indian independence.

Geography and climate

Bhubaneswar lies on the western banks of the Kuakhai River, a distributary of the Mahanadi, at an average elevation of about 58 metres. It has a tropical savanna climate with hot, humid summers, a monsoon season from June to September, and mild winters. Cyclones from the Bay of Bengal occasionally affect the city, most severely in the 1999 Odisha cyclone.

Economy

Traditionally an administrative and religious centre, Bhubaneswar has grown into a hub for information technology, education, and tourism. It hosts software parks and offices of major IT firms, and was ranked among India's fastest-growing IT destinations. The city is home to institutions such as IIT Bhubaneswar, AIIMS Bhubaneswar, and Utkal University. It has also emerged as a sports city, hosting major international hockey tournaments at the Kalinga Stadium.

Culture

The city is renowned for its Kalinga architecture, with surviving temples such as Lingaraja, Ananta Vasudeva, Rajarani, and Mukteshvara showcasing centuries of sculptural artistry. Odissi, one of India's classical dance forms, originated in the region. Major festivals include Durga Puja, Rath Yatra celebrations, and the Ekamra Utsav.

Transport

Bhubaneswar is served by Biju Patnaik International Airport, a major railway station on the Howrah–Chennai main line, and a network of national highways. A metro rail project has been planned to serve the growing metropolitan area, which includes the twin city of Cuttack.
