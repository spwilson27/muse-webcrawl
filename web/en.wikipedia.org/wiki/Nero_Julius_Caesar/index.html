Nero Julius Caesar

Nero Julius Caesar Germanicus
Born: AD 6
Died: AD 31
Parents: Germanicus and Agrippina the Elder
Spouse: Julia Livia

Nero Julius Caesar Germanicus (AD 6–31) was the eldest son of Germanicus and Agrippina the Elder, and the grandson of the emperor Tiberius, who adopted Germanicus. After Germanicus's death in AD 19, Nero stood close to the succession; he was married to Julia, daughter of Drusus the Younger, and was advanced in public honours, serving as quaestor in AD 26.

Downfall

The ambitions of the praetorian prefect Sejanus and hostility between Agrippina and Tiberius destroyed the family. Nero was denounced, declared a public enemy by the Senate, and exiled to the island of Ponza in AD 29 after his mother and brother Drusus were arrested. He died there in AD 31, by starvation — whether forced or self-inflicted, the sources differ — shortly before Sejanus's own fall.

Legacy

His remains were later recovered under Caligula, his younger brother, and interred in the Mausoleum of Augustus. His death illustrates the dynastic struggles that consumed the Julio-Claudian house under Tiberius.
