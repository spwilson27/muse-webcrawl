Tokyo University of the Arts

Tokyo University of the Arts (東京藝術大学, Tōkyō Geijutsu Daigaku), abbreviated Geidai, is Japan's premier national arts university, located in Ueno Park, Taito, Tokyo. It was formed in 1949 by the merger of the Tokyo Fine Arts School (founded 1887) and the Tokyo Music School (founded 1879).

It has faculties of Fine Arts and Music, plus graduate schools including film and new media in Yokohama. Alumni include many leading Japanese artists, composers and performers. Admission is highly competitive.
