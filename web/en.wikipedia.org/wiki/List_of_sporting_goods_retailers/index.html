List of sporting goods retailers

This is a list of notable retailers specialising in sporting goods — equipment, apparel and footwear for sports and outdoor recreation — organised by country or region of origin.

United States

  • Dick's Sporting Goods — the largest US sporting goods retailer
  • Academy Sports + Outdoors
  • REI (Recreational Equipment, Inc.) — consumer co-operative focused on outdoor gear
  • Bass Pro Shops and Cabela's — hunting, fishing and outdoor superstores
  • Scheels, Dunham's Sports, Hibbett Sports, Big 5 Sporting Goods, Sportsman's Warehouse
  • Foot Locker, Champs Sports, Finish Line — athletic footwear and apparel
  • Golf Galaxy, PGA Tour Superstore
  • Defunct: Sports Authority (closed 2016), Modell's (closed 2020), Sport Chalet, MC Sports

Europe

  • Decathlon (France) — the world's largest sporting goods retailer, known for own-brand products (Quechua, Domyos, B'Twin/Rockrider)
  • Intersport (Switzerland-based international buying group)
  • Sports Direct / Frasers Group (United Kingdom)
  • JD Sports (United Kingdom)
  • GO Outdoors, Mountain Warehouse, Snow+Rock (United Kingdom)
  • SportScheck, Sport 2000 (Germany)
  • XXL (Norway), Stadium (Sweden), Sportland (Baltics)
  • El Corte Inglés sports departments (Spain)

Asia-Pacific

  • Xtep, Anta Sports and Li-Ning operate extensive retail networks in China (primarily their own brands)
  • Alpen, Xebio (Japan)
  • Rebel Sport, Kathmandu (Australia and New Zealand)
  • Sports Central, Toby's Sports (Philippines)
  • Decathlon and local chains in India

Canada

  • Canadian Tire's Sport Chek and Atmosphere banners
  • Sporting Life, La Cordée, MEC (Mountain Equipment Company)
  • Sail, Decathlon Canada
