Anousheh Ansari

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Anousheh Ansari
Born12 September 1966, Mashhad, Iran
Known forFirst female space tourist; first Iranian in space (2006)
FlightSoyuz TMA-9 to the ISS
Ansari aboard the ISS.

Anousheh Ansari (born 1966) is an Iranian-American engineer and entrepreneur who on 18 September 2006 became the first female space tourist, the first Iranian in space, and the first Muslim woman in space, flying aboard Soyuz TMA-9 to the International Space Station for an eight-day stay arranged through Space Adventures — originally as backup to Daisuke Enomoto, who was medically disqualified weeks before launch, promoting her to the seat.

Ansari co-founded Telecom Technologies and, with her family, sponsored the Ansari X Prize — the $10 million competition won by SpaceShipOne in 2004 that jump-started commercial spaceflight. Her flight carried both flags' weight: she sewed an Iranian flag patch (removed from official photos by some agencies at the time, by her own telling) and blogged from orbit to millions of readers, including schoolgirls across Iran.

After

Ansari co-founded and leads the XPRIZE Foundation, extending the prize model from spaceflight to health, energy and AI — the tourist who became the patron of the industry she flew with. Her memoir My Dream of Stars (2010) frames the flight as an argument: that access to space should not be rationed by birthplace.
