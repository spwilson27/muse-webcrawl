Lipizzan

The Lipizzan or Lipizzaner is a European breed of riding horse developed in the Habsburg Empire in the sixteenth century. It takes its name from the stud farm at Lipica (Lipizza), now in Slovenia, founded in 1580.

Characteristics

Lipizzans are typically grey: foals are born dark and lighten gradually with age, most becoming white-grey as adults. They are compact, muscular horses standing about 14.2 to 15.2 hands, renowned for strength, stamina, and the ability to perform the demanding "airs above the ground" — the levade, courbette, and capriole.

Spanish Riding School

The breed is inseparably associated with the Spanish Riding School in Vienna, where its stallions perform classical dressage. Traditions of Lipizzan breeding were inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2022.
