Tesco plc is a British multinational groceries and general merchandise retailer headquartered in Welwyn Garden City, England. It is the market leader in grocery retail in the United Kingdom and one of the largest retailers in the world by revenue, with operations across Europe and a major presence in Ireland and Central Europe.

History

Tesco was founded in 1919 by Jack Cohen as a group of market stalls in London's East End; the name combined the initials of tea supplier T. E. Stockwell with the first two letters of Cohen's surname. The first Tesco shop opened in 1931, and the company floated on the London Stock Exchange in 1947. Tesco expanded rapidly through acquisitions and new store formats, from superstores and hypermarkets (Tesco Extra) to convenience shops (Tesco Express and Metro).

Operations

Beyond groceries, Tesco sells clothing (F&F), electronics, financial services, and previously mobile phones and banking. Its Clubcard loyalty programme, launched in 1995, became one of the most successful in retail and supplies the data business Dunnhumby. In the 2010s Tesco withdrew from several international ventures, including Fresh & Easy in the United States, to refocus on its core markets.
