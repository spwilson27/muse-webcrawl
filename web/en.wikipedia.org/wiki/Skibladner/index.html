Skibladner

DS Skibladner is a paddle steamer operating on Lake Mjøsa, Norway's largest lake. Launched in 1856, she is the world's oldest paddle steamer in scheduled service and is nicknamed the "White Swan of Mjøsa".

Launched: 2 August 1856
Type: Paddle steamer
Route: Lake Mjøsa, Norway
Status: In seasonal service

History

Skibladner was built by Motala Verkstad in Sweden, transported in pieces and assembled at Minnesund. She was lengthened in 1888, when a new steam engine and boiler were installed. The ship has carried passengers and cargo between the towns around Mjøsa — Lillehammer, Hamar, Gjøvik and Eidsvoll — for over 160 years, and also serves as a floating post office.

Incidents and preservation

The vessel sank at her moorings in 1937 and again in 1960, but was raised and restored both times. She is protected as a heritage vessel and operates tourist cruises during the summer season.
