Around the World in 80 Days (1956 film)

Around the World in 80 Days is a 1956 American epic adventure film directed by Michael Anderson and produced by Mike Todd, based on the 1873 novel by Jules Verne. It stars David Niven as Phileas Fogg and Cantinflas as his valet Passepartout, with Shirley MacLaine and Robert Newton.

Production

The film was shot in Todd-AO widescreen and on location in many countries, employing a huge cast including dozens of celebrity cameo appearances — among them Noël Coward, John Gielgud, Frank Sinatra and Buster Keaton.

Reception

The film was a major commercial success and won five Academy Awards from eight nominations, including Best Picture, Best Adapted Screenplay and Best Cinematography (Color), with Victor Young's score winning Best Music.
