AeroVironment Switchblade

TypeLoitering munition
ManufacturerAeroVironment
OriginUnited States

The AeroVironment Switchblade is a miniature loitering munition — a portable, tube-launched unmanned aerial vehicle carrying an explosive warhead — designed by the American company AeroVironment for use by infantry units. It is designed to be carried in a backpack and launched from a tube, after which its wings unfold, giving the system its "switchblade" name.

Design

The original Switchblade 300 is about 60 cm long, weighs roughly 2.5 kg including its launcher, and is electrically powered, giving it an endurance of about 15 minutes and a range of around 10 km. It carries electro-optical and infrared cameras and GPS, sending video back to its operator, and is armed with a small explosive charge designed to limit collateral damage. The larger Switchblade 600 is a vehicle- and tripod-launched anti-armour variant with an anti-tank warhead derived from the Javelin missile, with endurance around 40 minutes.

Operational history

The system entered United States Army service in the early 2010s and was used in Afghanistan. Switchblade 300 and 600 systems were supplied to Ukraine following the 2022 Russian invasion, where they were used against vehicles, artillery, and personnel.
