Wiebe Bijker

Wiebe E. Bijker (born 1951) is a Dutch sociologist of technology at Maastricht University, known as a co-founder, with Trevor Pinch, of the social construction of technology (SCOT) approach within science and technology studies.

Work

Bijker's case study of the bicycle — showing how relevant social groups (racers, women riders, manufacturers) attributed different meanings to designs such as the safety bicycle — exemplified SCOT's concepts of interpretive flexibility and closure. His books include Of Bicycles, Bakelites, and Bulbs (1995) and, with Pinch, the programmatic chapter in The Social Construction of Technological Systems (1987), which he co-edited with Pinch and Thomas P. Hughes.

Career

Trained in physics and philosophy, Bijker helped build STS at Maastricht, advised Dutch government on science and technology policy, and worked on vulnerability, risk and innovation. He has held leadership roles in the Society for Social Studies of Science.
