Chormaqan

Chormaqan (died 1241), also spelled Chormagan or Jormaqan, was a Mongol general under Genghis Khan and Ögedei Khan. He led the Mongol conquest of Persia and the Caucasus in the 1230s.

Died: 1241
Allegiance: Mongol Empire
Rank: General (noyan)

Career

A member of the Sunud tribe, Chormaqan was one of Genghis Khan's trusted commanders who had participated in the campaign against the Khwarazmian Empire. In 1230 Ögedei dispatched him with about 30,000 men to finish the conquest of Persia, defeating Jalal al-Din Mangburni and occupying Azerbaijan, Ray, and Hamadan.

Caucasus campaigns

From his base in the Mughan steppe, Chormaqan subdued Georgia, Armenia, and parts of Anatolia, imposing Mongol administration and tribute. He fell ill and became paralyzed in his later years; command passed in effect to his subordinate Baiju. He died in 1241.
