Suprême NTM, pioneers of French hardcore hip hop.

Suprême NTM (often simply NTM) is a French hip hop group formed in 1989 in the Seine-Saint-Denis department, north of Paris. The group consists of rappers Joeystarr (Didier Morville) and Kool Shen (Bruno Lopes). Their name is derived from the French slang initialism "NTM", and the group is regarded as one of the founding acts of French rap.

Career

NTM emerged from the Paris banlieues with a confrontational, hardcore style addressing police violence, racism, and life in the housing estates. Their debut album Authentik (1991) was followed by 1993... J'appuie sur la gâchette and Paris sous les bombes (1995). Their self-titled fourth album, Suprême NTM (1998), featuring the hit "Ma Benz", was their greatest commercial success. The group's lyrics provoked repeated clashes with authorities; in 1996 the members received suspended prison sentences and a temporary performance ban after remarks directed at police during a concert.

Later years

The duo separated in 2001 to pursue solo careers, reunifying for tours in 2008 and again in 2018–2019, including sold-out shows at Paris's AccorHotels Arena, before Joeystarr announced the group's definitive end in 2019.

Legacy

Suprême NTM's raw depiction of banlieue life shaped generations of French rappers, and the group remains one of the best-selling acts in French hip hop history.
