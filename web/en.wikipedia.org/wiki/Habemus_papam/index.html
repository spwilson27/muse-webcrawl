Habemus papam

Habemus papam (Latin for "We have a pope") is the announcement traditionally given by the cardinal protodeacon, the senior cardinal deacon, upon the election of a new pope by the College of Cardinals in conclave. The announcement is made from the central balcony (loggia) of St Peter's Basilica in Vatican City, following the appearance of white smoke (fumata bianca) from the chimney of the Sistine Chapel.

The formula

The full announcement, delivered in Latin, is:

Annuntio vobis gaudium magnum: habemus papam! Eminentissimum ac reverendissimum dominum, dominum [first name], Sanctae Romanae Ecclesiae cardinalem [surname], qui sibi nomen imposuit [papal name].

"I announce to you a great joy: we have a pope! The most eminent and most reverend lord, Lord [first name], Cardinal of the Holy Roman Church [surname], who has taken for himself the name [papal name]."

History

The form of the announcement developed over centuries as conclaves became regularised. Phrases announcing a pope's election are found in earlier sources, and the ritual as it now stands — white smoke, the deacon's announcement from the balcony, then the new pope's appearance and his first Urbi et Orbi blessing — crystallised in the modern era. The phrase is also echoed in the earlier part of the conclave, when the master of ceremonies dismisses all non-electors from the Sistine Chapel with the words extra omnes ("everybody out").

Notable announcements

Announcements have sometimes carried drama or confusion. In 1958 the announcement of Angelo Roncalli (John XXIII) was preceded by ambiguous smoke signals. In 1978 the protodeacon Pericle Felici announced first Albino Luciani (John Paul I) in August and then, some six weeks later after Luciani's sudden death, Karol Wojtyła (John Paul II) — the first non-Italian pope in over 450 years. In 2013 Cardinal Jean-Louis Tauran announced Jorge Mario Bergoglio, who took the name Francis; in 2025 Cardinal Dominique Mamberti announced Robert Francis Prevost, who took the name Leo XIV, the first pope born in the United States.
