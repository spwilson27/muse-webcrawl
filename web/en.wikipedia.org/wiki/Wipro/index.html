Wipro

Wipro Limited is an Indian multinational corporation that provides information technology, consulting and business process services. It is headquartered in Bangalore, Karnataka, India. Wipro is one of the largest IT services companies in India and employs more than 230,000 people serving clients in over 60 countries.

History

The company was founded in 1945 by Mohamed Premji as Western India Products, a manufacturer of vegetable and refined oils based in Amalner, Maharashtra. The name "Wipro" derives from the company's original name. After Mohamed Premji's death in 1966, his son Azim Premji took over at the age of 21 and gradually diversified the business, entering the information technology sector in the late 1970s and 1980s. Under Azim Premji's leadership Wipro grew into a global IT services provider; he remained chairman until 2019, when his son Rishad Premji succeeded him.

Business

Wipro's services include digital strategy, cloud computing, cybersecurity, data analytics, artificial intelligence and business process outsourcing. The company is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange, the National Stock Exchange of India and the New York Stock Exchange.
