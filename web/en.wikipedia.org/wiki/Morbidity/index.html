Morbidity

Morbidity is the condition of suffering from a disease or medical condition. In epidemiology, the term may refer qualitatively to the state of illness in an individual, or quantitatively to measures of the incidence or prevalence of disease in a population. Morbidity is contrasted with mortality, which describes death. A person can be affected by several morbidities at the same time, known as comorbidities.

Morbidity statistics are used to quantify the burden of disease in populations

Measurement

Morbidity in a population is commonly measured using incidence, the number of new cases of a disease during a specified period, and prevalence, the total number of cases existing at a given time. Rates are usually expressed per 1,000 or 100,000 population. Composite measures such as the disability-adjusted life year (DALY) and years lived with disability (YLD) combine morbidity with mortality to summarize overall disease burden.

Comorbidity

Comorbidity is the presence of one or more additional conditions co-occurring with a primary condition. Comorbidities can complicate diagnosis and treatment, worsen prognosis, and increase healthcare costs. Indices such as the Charlson Comorbidity Index are used in clinical research to quantify comorbidity burden and predict outcomes such as mortality.

Morbidity and mortality

Many health conditions cause substantial morbidity without high mortality; for example, musculoskeletal disorders and mental illnesses are leading contributors to years lived with disability worldwide, while conditions such as cardiovascular disease and cancer contribute heavily to both morbidity and mortality. Public health surveillance systems track morbidity through hospital records, disease registries, surveys, and notifiable disease reporting.

Occupational and perioperative uses

In occupational health, morbidity statistics include rates of work-related illness and injury. In surgery and anaesthesia, "morbidity and mortality" conferences review complications and adverse outcomes to improve practice, and perioperative morbidity describes complications arising in connection with an operation.
