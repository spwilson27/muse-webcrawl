Heaven Hill

Heaven Hill Brands is an American, privately held, family-owned distillery and spirits company headquartered in Bardstown, Kentucky. Founded in 1935, shortly after the repeal of Prohibition, it is one of the largest independent suppliers of distilled spirits in the United States and the largest independent bourbon producer, holding a substantial share of the world's aging bourbon inventory in its rickhouses.

History

The company was founded by the Shapira family and a group of investors as the Old Heavenhill Springs Distillery, on land whose springs gave the company its name (the name "Heaven Hill" derives from a previous landowner, William Heavenhill). Unlike most of its competitors, the company remained family-owned and was not absorbed by a multinational drinks group. It long operated without its own brands of major prominence, selling bulk and aged whiskey to other labels, before building its own portfolio.

In November 1996, a catastrophic fire, ignited during a severe storm, destroyed the company's Bardstown distillery and several warehouses, consuming tens of thousands of barrels of aging whiskey — about two percent of the world's bourbon supply at the time. Production was subsequently centred on the Bernheim distillery in Louisville, acquired in 1999, while aging and bottling continued in Bardstown.

Products

Heaven Hill's portfolio includes Evan Williams, Elijah Craig, Larceny, Henry McKenna, Parker's Heritage Collection and Old Fitzgerald bourbons; Rittenhouse rye; Bernheim wheat whiskey; and other spirits brands such as Deep Eddy vodka, Lunazul tequila, Admiral Nelson's rum and Black Velvet Canadian whisky. The company operates the Heaven Hill Bourbon Experience visitor centre in Bardstown and the Evan Williams Bourbon Experience in Louisville.
