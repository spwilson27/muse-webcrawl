WGN-TV

WGN-TV (channel 9) is a television station in Chicago, Illinois, United States, affiliated with The CW. Founded in 1948 by the Chicago Tribune, its call sign derives from the newspaper's slogan, "World's Greatest Newspaper". From 1978 to 2016 its signal was distributed nationally as the superstation WGN America.

History

WGN-TV became famous nationally for Chicago Cubs baseball, Chicago Bulls basketball, Bozo the Clown, and family film programming. Uploaded to satellite in 1978, the "superstation" version carried most of the local schedule across the United States until it was separated into the distinct cable channel WGN America in 2014 and the Chicago station returned to purely local service. In 2016 the local station became a CW affiliate.

News

WGN is known for its extensive local news, including the long-running WGN Morning News, and for personalities such as meteorologist Tom Skilling. The station is owned by Nexstar Media Group following its 2019 acquisition of Tribune Media.
