Deepwater Horizon

Deepwater Horizon was a semi-submersible offshore drilling rig whose explosion on 20 April 2010 caused the largest marine oil spill in history in the Gulf of Mexico.

Explosion and spill

A blowout killed 11 workers and the rig sank two days later. Oil flowed for 87 days, releasing millions of barrels before the well was capped.

Environmental impact

Oil contaminated coastlines from Texas to Florida, harmed fisheries, marshes, dolphins and seabirds, and required a massive cleanup.

Legal aftermath

BP agreed to settlements totalling tens of billions of dollars, including a $20.8 billion US government settlement. The disaster led to stricter offshore drilling regulation.
