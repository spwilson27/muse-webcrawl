IFA (German: Internationale Funkausstellung Berlin, "International Radio Exhibition Berlin") is one of the world's largest and oldest trade fairs for consumer electronics and home appliances, held annually in Berlin, Germany. The first exhibition took place in December 1924, showcasing radio technology; in 1928 Albert Einstein delivered an opening address at the fair. Over its history, many landmark technologies were presented at IFA, including early television broadcasts, the European launch of videocassette systems, colour television demonstrations, and later digital television, smart home systems, and wearables.

The modern fair

Held at the Messe Berlin exhibition grounds beneath the landmark Funkturm tower, IFA takes place each September and is open to both trade visitors and the general public. Major manufacturers use the show to unveil televisions, smartphones, computers, audio equipment, and household appliances ahead of the year-end shopping season. Regular attendance in recent pre-pandemic years approached a quarter of a million visitors with well over a thousand exhibitors from dozens of countries.

Organization

The IFA brand is owned by the German consumer electronics association gfu Consumer & Home Electronics GmbH and the event is organized by Messe Berlin (with Clarion Events partnering in recent years). In 2023 the show was extended under the new operating company IFA Management GmbH. Related formats have included IFA Global Markets, a sourcing show, and SHIFT conferences on mobility and innovation.
