Mitsubishi SpaceJet

A SpaceJet M90 test aircraft in flight

The Mitsubishi SpaceJet (originally the Mitsubishi Regional Jet, or MRJ) was a regional jet aircraft project developed by Mitsubishi Heavy Industries through its Mitsubishi Aircraft Corporation subsidiary. It was to be the first Japanese-built airliner since the NAMC YS-11 of the 1960s, seating roughly 70–90 passengers and powered by Pratt & Whitney PW1000G geared turbofan engines.

Development and cancellation

The programme was launched in 2008 with All Nippon Airways as launch customer. Despite a successful first flight in 2015, development was repeatedly delayed by design changes and certification difficulties, and the design was rebranded "SpaceJet" in 2019. Development was suspended in 2020 amid the COVID-19 pandemic, and in February 2023 Mitsubishi Heavy Industries announced the project's termination, having spent an estimated ¥1 trillion.
