Fairytale (Alexander Rybak song)

"Fairytale" is a song by Belarusian-Norwegian singer and violinist Alexander Rybak. It was the winner of the Eurovision Song Contest 2009, held in Moscow, Russia, representing Norway. The song was composed and written by Rybak himself and was released as the first single from his debut album Fairytales.

ArtistAlexander Rybak
Released12 January 2009
GenreFolk-pop
Eurovision result1st place, 387 points (2009)

Eurovision Song Contest

Rybak won the Norwegian national final, Melodi Grand Prix 2009, by a record margin, and at Eurovision he scored 387 points — then the highest total under the voting system in use — receiving 12 points from 16 countries. His performance combined singing, violin playing, and dancers from the Norwegian folk ensemble Frikar.

Chart performance

"Fairytale" topped charts in Norway, Russia, and several other European countries, and became one of the most recognizable Eurovision-winning songs of its decade.
