List of countries by population

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of countries and dependencies by population, based principally on United Nations estimates (World Population Prospects). The world's population passed eight billion in November 2022. In April 2023, India surpassed China to become the world's most populous country, ending China's position at the top which it had held for centuries.

At the other end of the scale, the least populous sovereign states are Vatican City (about 800 residents), Tuvalu and Nauru (about 11,000–13,000 each) and Palau (about 18,000). More than half of humanity lives in Asia. The United Nations projects the world population to peak at around 10.3 billion in the 2080s.
