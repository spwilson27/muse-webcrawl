Adele Goldberg (linguist)

Adele Eva Goldberg
Born: 1963
Known for: Construction grammar

Adele Eva Goldberg (born 1963) is an American linguist known for her work on construction grammar and the constructionist approach to language.

Career

Goldberg received her PhD from the University of California, Berkeley, and has taught at UC San Diego and Princeton University. Her 1995 book Constructions: A Construction Grammar Approach to Argument Structure helped establish that argument structure patterns themselves carry meaning.

Research

Her work addresses how speakers learn form–meaning pairings, the role of generalisation and analogy in acquisition, and why languages share constructional patterns. Later books include Constructions at Work (2006) and Explain Me This (2019).
