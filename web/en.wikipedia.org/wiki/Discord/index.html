Discord
Developer: Discord Inc.
Released: 13 May 2015
Type: VoIP, instant messaging, digital distribution

Discord is an instant messaging and VoIP social platform allowing communication through voice calls, video calls, text messaging and media files, in private chats or as part of communities called "servers". It was conceived by Jason Citron and Stanislav Vishnevskiy, growing out of the game-development studio Hammer & Chisel, and released in 2015.

History and use

Originally aimed at video game communities, Discord broadened during the COVID-19 pandemic into a general community platform, reaching hundreds of millions of registered users. It introduced features such as forums, activities and a premium subscription, Nitro. In 2021 Microsoft was reported to have explored an acquisition exceeding $10 billion, which did not proceed.
