This list of volcanoes in Indonesia includes the country's major active and dormant volcanoes. Indonesia, straddling the Sunda Arc and the "Ring of Fire", has more active volcanoes than any other country — around 130 — including Krakatoa and Tambora, whose eruptions in 1883 and 1815 were among the largest in recorded history.
