The second season of the South Korean dystopian survival thriller television series Squid Game, created by Hwang Dong-hyuk for Netflix, was released on 26 December 2024. The season stars Lee Jung-jae, Lee Byung-hun, Wi Ha-joon, and an ensemble of new cast members including Im Si-wan, Kang Ha-neul, Park Gyu-young, and Jo Yu-ri.

Premise

Three years after winning the 33rd Squid Game, Seong Gi-hun (Player 456) has given up his plan to travel to the United States and returns with a new resolution in mind. Having spent his fortune funding a private search for the game's recruiters, he allows himself to be taken back into the competition, determined to end the games from within. Inside, he attempts to organise the players to vote to end the games and later to rebel against the guards, while the Front Man, Hwang In-ho, enters the game disguised as Player 001 to undermine him.

Episodes

The season comprises seven episodes: "Bread and Lottery", "Halloween Party", "001", "Six Legs", "One More Game", "O X", and "Friend or Foe". Games depicted include a new round of Red Light, Green Light; a six-legged pentathlon combining five traditional children's games; and "Mingle", played on a rotating platform. The season ends on a cliffhanger, with the rebellion crushed and a third and final season following in 2025.

Reception

The season set a Netflix record for the biggest television premiere week, drawing 68 million views in its first three days and topping the charts in all 93 countries with Top 10 lists. Critical response was generally positive, praising Lee Jung-jae's performance and the new games, though some reviewers considered the pacing slower and the ending unsatisfying as a standalone story. It received a Golden Globe nomination for Best Television Series – Drama.
