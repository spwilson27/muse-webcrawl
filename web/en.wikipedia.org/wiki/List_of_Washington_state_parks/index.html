List of Washington state parks

The U.S. state of Washington maintains a system of more than 120 state parks, administered by the Washington State Parks and Recreation Commission, founded in 1913. The system covers roughly 260,000 acres (1,050 km²) and includes ocean beaches, Puget Sound islands, Cascade mountain sites, historic forts, and Columbia Plateau landscapes. Vehicle access to most parks requires a Discover Pass.
