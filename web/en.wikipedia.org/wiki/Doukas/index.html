Doukas

The Doukas family (Greek: Δούκας, pl. Δούκαι; feminine Δούκαινα), Latinised as Ducas, was a Byzantine Greek noble family that provided several notable generals and rulers to the Byzantine Empire in the 9th to 11th centuries. Their rise began in the late 9th century, and a branch of the family supplied the imperial dynasty that ruled from 1059 to 1067/1078, as well as the emperors of the Komnenian era through the female line.

Origins

Later family tradition claimed descent from a cousin of the Roman emperor Constantine I who migrated to Constantinople in the 4th century. The name probably derives from the Latin title dux ("leader", "general"). The first securely attested member is Andronikos Doukas, a general under Leo VI the Wise in the early 10th century; his son Constantine Doukas was killed in a failed coup attempt in 913.

Rise to the throne

The family suffered under Romanos I Lekapenos but returned to prominence under Constantine VII, who married his daughter to a Doukas. The family's great ascendancy came when Constantine X Doukas became emperor in 1059 on the abdication of Isaac I Komnenos. His widow Eudokia Makrembolitissa — herself a Doukas by marriage — ruled as regent, and their son Michael VII Doukas reigned until 1078. Through Irene Doukaina, wife of Alexios I Komnenos, Doukas blood passed into the Komnenian dynasty, and later emperors used the surname prominently.

Later branches

Descendants of the family remained prominent aristocrats throughout the Komnenian and Angelos periods, and Doukai appear in the Empire of Nicaea, the Despotate of Epirus and the later Palaiologan empire. The "Doukas" historian of the 15th century chronicled the empire's final decades. The family name survives in modern Greece as a surname.

Notable members

  • Andronikos Doukas (general under Leo VI)
  • Constantine Doukas (died 913)
  • Constantine X Doukas, emperor 1059–1067
  • Michael VII Doukas, emperor 1071–1078
  • Irene Doukaina, empress consort of Alexios I Komnenos
  • John Doukas, Caesar and kingmaker
