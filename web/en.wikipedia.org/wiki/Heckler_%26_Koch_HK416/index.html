The Heckler & Koch HK416 is a gas-operated assault rifle chambered for 5.56×45mm NATO, designed by Heckler & Koch. It combines the ergonomics and controls of the AR-15/M16 family with a proprietary short-stroke gas piston system derived from the G36, intended to improve reliability over direct impingement.

Development and use

Originally developed in collaboration with US special operations forces (as the HK M4), the HK416 was adopted by Norway as its standard rifle, by France as the HK416F to replace the FAMAS, and by the United States Marine Corps as the M27 Infantry Automatic Rifle. A 7.62mm derivative is the HK417. The rifle was famously used by the US Navy SEAL team that killed Osama bin Laden in 2011.
