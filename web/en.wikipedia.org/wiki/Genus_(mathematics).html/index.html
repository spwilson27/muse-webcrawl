Genus (mathematics)

In mathematics, genus (plural genera) has a few different, but closely related, meanings. The most common concept is the genus of an orientable surface, intuitively the number of 'holes' it has: a sphere has genus 0, a torus genus 1, a two-holed torus genus 2, and so on.

For a closed orientable surface, the genus g is related to the Euler characteristic by χ = 2 − 2g. Genus is a topological invariant, so surfaces of different genus are not homeomorphic.

Other uses

In algebraic geometry, the genus of an algebraic curve can be defined via its function field or arithmetic genus. In graph theory, the genus of a graph is the smallest genus of a surface on which it can be drawn without crossings.
