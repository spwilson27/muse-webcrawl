Montserrat (mountain)

Elevation1,236 m (Sant Jeroni)
LocationCatalonia, Spain
GeologyConglomerate rock

Montserrat (Catalan pronunciation: [munsəˈrat]) is a multi-peaked mountain range near Barcelona, in Catalonia, Spain. It is part of the Catalan Pre-Coastal Range. The main peaks are Sant Jeroni (1,236 m), Montgrós (1,120 m) and Les Agulles (903 m).

The mountain's name means "serrated mountain" in Catalan, describing its distinctive jagged profile of pink conglomerate rock, a form of sedimentary rock. In 1987, the mountain was declared a natural park.

Santa Maria de Montserrat Abbey

Montserrat is home to the Benedictine abbey of Santa Maria de Montserrat, which houses the Virgin of Montserrat, one of the famous Black Madonnas of Europe and the patron saint of Catalonia. The abbey is a major pilgrimage site and is renowned for its boys' choir, the Escolania de Montserrat, one of the oldest in Europe.

Access

The monastery can be reached by road, by the Montserrat Rack Railway, or by cable car (Aeri de Montserrat) from the valley below, connected by rail to Barcelona.
