Cringe

Cringe is an emotional response of acute embarrassment, typically felt on behalf of another person — a form of vicarious or second-hand embarrassment. The word derives from the Old English cringan, meaning to bend or recoil, and as a verb it also describes a physical flinch of disgust or submission.

In psychology

Psychologists link the cringe response to empathy: observers simulate the social pain of someone humiliating themselves. Related concepts include the German Fremdschämen and the discomfort deliberately exploited by cringe comedy.

In popular culture

As internet slang, "cringe" labels content judged awkward, embarrassing, or trying too hard, and gave rise to genres such as cringe comedy (e.g. The Office) and "cringe compilations" online. Its slang use expanded so rapidly that it has itself become the subject of debate and parody.
