Looper is a 2012 American science fiction action film written and directed by Rian Johnson, and produced by Ram Bergman and James D. Stern. It stars Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Bruce Willis, and Emily Blunt.

In the film, time travel is invented by the year 2074 and, though immediately outlawed, is used by criminal organisations to send people they want killed into the past to be executed by "loopers" — assassins paid in silver. Joe, a looper, is confronted when his next target turns out to be his older self, sent back from 2074.

Plot

In 2044, Joe works for a crime syndicate in Kansas City, killing victims sent back hooded from 2074. Loopers know they will one day be "closed" — sent their own older selves to kill. When Old Joe escapes instead of being executed, young Joe hunts him while pursued by the syndicate's Gat Men. Old Joe seeks to kill the child who will become the Rainmaker, the future crime lord who ordered the loop closings; young Joe protects the boy, Cid, a telekinetic living on a farm with his mother Sara. Joe ultimately sacrifices himself, erasing Old Joe and breaking the cycle.

Reception

Looper premiered as the opening film of the 2012 Toronto International Film Festival and was released to critical acclaim, praised for its script, performances and originality within the time-travel genre. It grossed over $176 million worldwide against a $30 million budget and has frequently appeared on lists of the best science fiction films of the 2010s.
