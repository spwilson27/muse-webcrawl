Detective Chimp

Detective Chimp (Bobo T. Chimpanzee)
Publisher: DC Comics
First appearance: Adventures of Rex the Wonder Dog #4 (1952)
Created by John Broome and Carmine Infantino
Species: common chimpanzee (enhanced intelligence)

Detective Chimp is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics. He is a common chimpanzee with intelligence and speech comparable to a human, who solves crimes as a private detective, typically depicted in a deerstalker hat in homage to Sherlock Holmes. Originally the trained animal companion "Bobo" in the Rex the Wonder Dog feature, he was reimagined in modern continuity as an intelligent chimp who drank from the Fountain of Youth.

Publication history

The character debuted in Adventures of Rex the Wonder Dog #4 (July–August 1952). After the Silver Age, he appeared sporadically until being revived as a drunken, world-weary detective in the 2005 Day of Vengeance miniseries, which led to a role as a founding member of the Shadowpact, a team of magical heroes. A 2007 follow-up, Helmet of Fate: Detective Chimp, sent him across the DC Universe trying on the helm of Doctor Fate. He later became a regular in Justice League Dark — inheriting the Oblivion Bar and joining Wonder Woman's team of magic users — where his detective skills and knowledge of the occult are central.

Characterisation

Detective Chimp is generally considered one of the finest detectives in the DC Universe, comparable to Batman and Ralph Dibny. He can speak to animals in their own languages and, thanks to the Fountain of Youth, ages very slowly. His modern portrayal mixes noir-detective wit with melancholy about outliving his human friends.
