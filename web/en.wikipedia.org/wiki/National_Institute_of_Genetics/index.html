National Institute of Genetics
Established: 1949
Type: Research institute
Location: Mishima, Shizuoka, Japan

The National Institute of Genetics (NIG) is a Japanese research institute in Mishima, Shizuoka Prefecture, conducting research in genetics and serving as a center for genetic resources and education. It is one of the institutes of the Research Organization of Information and Systems.

Activities

The NIG was founded in 1949. It operates the DNA Data Bank of Japan (DDBJ), one of the world's three major public repositories of nucleotide sequence data, alongside GenBank in the United States and the European Nucleotide Archive. The institute also maintains genetic stock centers for model organisms and runs a graduate program in genetics through the Graduate University for Advanced Studies (SOKENDAI).
