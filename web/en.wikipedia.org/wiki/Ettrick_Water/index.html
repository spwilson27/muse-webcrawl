Ettrick Water

Ettrick Water is a river in the Scottish Borders region of southern Scotland. It rises near Ettrick in the Border hills and flows northeast through the Ettrick Valley to join the River Tweed near Selkirk. It is one of the principal tributaries of the upper Tweed system.

SourceNear Ettrick, Scottish Borders

Course

The river flows from the moorlands around Ettrick through a pastoral valley, receiving the Yarrow Water near Philiphaugh, and passes the town of Selkirk before meeting the Tweed. The valley is associated with James Hogg, the "Ettrick Shepherd", a celebrated poet and novelist born in the parish of Ettrick.

Flooding and fishing

The Ettrick is known for salmon and trout fishing, and like other Border rivers it is prone to spate flooding; Selkirk and riverside communities have flood protection works. The valley forms part of the historic Ettrick Forest, once a royal hunting forest.
