Weekly Shōnen Magazine

Weekly Shōnen Magazine (Japanese: 週刊少年マガジン, Shūkan Shōnen Magajin) is a weekly shōnen manga magazine published in Japan by Kodansha. It was first published on March 17, 1959. One of the longest-running and formerly best-selling manga magazines, it is a long-standing rival of Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump and Shogakukan's Weekly Shōnen Sunday.

History

The magazine launched in 1959, following its competitor Weekly Shōnen Sunday by a week. Its circulation climbed steeply through the 1960s and 1970s with landmark series such as Tomorrow's Joe (Ashita no Joe) and Devilman. Circulation peaked at over 4 million copies in the mid-1990s, when it briefly overtook Weekly Shōnen Jump, driven by series such as Shoot! and GTO. Print circulation has since declined with the wider magazine market, though individual series remain highly successful.
