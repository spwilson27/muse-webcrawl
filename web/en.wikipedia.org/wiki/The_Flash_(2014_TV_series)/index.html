The Flash is an American superhero television series developed by Greg Berlanti, Andrew Kreisberg, and Geoff Johns, airing on The CW. It is based on the Barry Allen incarnation of the DC Comics character the Flash, a crime-scene investigator who gains superhuman speed. It is a spin-off of Arrow, existing in the same fictional universe known as the Arrowverse.

Premise

After the S.T.A.R. Labs particle accelerator explodes, CSI investigator Barry Allen is struck by lightning and awakens with the power of super speed. With the help of scientists Harrison Wells, Caitlin Snow, and Cisco Ramon, he fights metahuman criminals in Central City while investigating the murder of his mother, for which his father was wrongly imprisoned. Over nine seasons, Barry faces the Reverse-Flash, Zoom, Savitar, the Thinker, Cicada, Bloodwork, the Forces, and Godspeed, participates in multiverse crossovers including Crisis on Infinite Earths, and eventually passes the mantle toward a new generation.

Reception

The pilot was the second most-watched premiere in CW history at the time. The series was praised for its lighter tone and emotional storytelling and ran for nine seasons and 184 episodes, concluding on May 24, 2023, the longest-running Arrowverse series alongside Arrow.
