Warez

Warez is a term referring to pirated media — most often software — distributed without the copyright holder's permission, and to the underground subculture (the "warez scene") that produces and circulates it. The word derives from software, respelled in the leetspeak style of early BBS culture, with a plural -z.

The scene

The warez scene is organised into competing release groups that race to be first to crack and release new software, films, music and games, often before or on the official release date. Releases circulate through a hierarchy of private FTP sites and encrypted channels governed by strict rules ("scene rules") about quality and formatting. Groups traditionally package releases with NFO files and ASCII art.

History

The scene originated in the 1980s on bulletin board systems for the Commodore 64, Amiga and PC, growing with the Internet in the 1990s. Law-enforcement operations such as Operation Buccaneer (2001) and Operation Fastlink (2004) targeted major groups. The term is now used loosely for any pirated content found on the public web, though scene members distinguish their closed network from mass file-sharing.
