Hyde Park (CDP), New York

Hyde Park is a census-designated place (CDP) in the town of Hyde Park, Dutchess County, New York, United States. The CDP corresponds roughly to the hamlet of Hyde Park, the principal settlement of the town, located on the east bank of the Hudson River north of Poughkeepsie. Its population was about 1,900 at the 2010 census.

Geography

The CDP is centred on U.S. Route 9 (Albany Post Road) in the western part of the town. It includes the historic core of the community, with the Franklin D. Roosevelt High School and town offices nearby. According to the United States Census Bureau, the CDP has a total area of roughly 1.5 square miles, a small portion of which is water along the Hudson shore.

Points of interest

Although some fall just outside the CDP boundary, the Hyde Park area is known for the Home of Franklin D. Roosevelt National Historic Site, the Franklin D. Roosevelt Presidential Library and Museum, the Vanderbilt Mansion National Historic Site, and the Hyde Park campus of the Culinary Institute of America.
