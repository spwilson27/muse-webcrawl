Ram Khamhaeng Inscription

The Ram Khamhaeng Inscription, also known as Sukhothai Inscription No. 1, is a stone stele bearing the earliest known writing in the Thai script. It is traditionally dated to 1292 and attributed to King Ram Khamhaeng of the Sukhothai Kingdom, describing the prosperity of his realm, its laws, religion and the invention of the Thai script in 1283.

The inscription was discovered in 1833 by Prince Mongkut (later King Rama IV) at Sukhothai. It is now held in the National Museum Bangkok and was listed in UNESCO's Memory of the World register in 2003. Its authenticity has occasionally been debated by scholars.
