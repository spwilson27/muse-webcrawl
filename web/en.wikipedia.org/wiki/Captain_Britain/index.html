Captain Britain

Captain Britain (Brian Braddock) is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, created by writer Chris Claremont and artist Herb Trimpe. He first appeared in Captain Britain #1 (October 1976), published by Marvel UK. Chosen by the wizard Merlyn and his daughter Roma to champion Britain, Brian Braddock draws power from the mystical Amulet of Right and later the Sword of Might, and from the energy of Otherworld; he is the designated protector of Earth-616's British Isles and a member of the multiversal Captain Britain Corps. He is the twin brother of the mutant Betsy Braddock (Psylocke/Kwannon storylines), who herself took up the Captain Britain mantle in the 2019 Excalibur series, while Brian assumed the title Captain Avalon.

Publication history

Captain Britain was launched as a weekly Marvel UK title in 1976. After the series' cancellation he appeared in Black Knight and other anthology strips, where writer Alan Moore and artist Alan Davis reshaped the character in the early 1980s with the "Jaspers' Warp" storyline, introducing the Corps and the reality-manipulating Mad Jim Jaspers. In 1988 Claremont and Davis reunited to launch Excalibur, pairing Braddock with his wife Meggan and X-Men members Nightcrawler, Shadowcat, Phoenix (Rachel Summers), and Lockheed in a British-based team. Later series include Captain Britain and MI13 (2008), written by Paul Cornell.

Fictional character biography

Brian Braddock, son of Sir James Braddock, was a physics student working at the Darkmoor nuclear research centre when an attack left him near death. Offered a choice by Merlyn between the Amulet of Right and the Sword of Might, Brian chose the amulet and became Captain Britain, empowered to defend Britain. His powers, originally channeled through his costume and artefacts, were later tied to the interdimensional energy matrix centred on the British Isles and Otherworld, growing stronger near it and weakening abroad unless sustained by his uniform. He married the elemental shapeshifter Meggan, served as king of Otherworld for a time, and has been a member of the Illuminati.

Powers and abilities

Captain Britain possesses superhuman strength (able to lift roughly 90 tons), speed, stamina, and near-invulnerability, together with flight and heightened senses. His powers are sustained by Otherworld energy and by his confidence in his role; the costume acts as an energy reservoir when he travels beyond its sources.
