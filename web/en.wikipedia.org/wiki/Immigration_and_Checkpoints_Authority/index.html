Immigration and Checkpoints Authority

The Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA) is a statutory board under the Ministry of Home Affairs of Singapore. It is responsible for immigration control, border security at Singapore's checkpoints, and the issuance of travel documents, identity cards, and various permits and passes to citizens, permanent residents, and visitors.

History

The ICA was formed on 1 April 2003 through the merger of the Singapore Immigration and Registration department and the border control functions of the Customs and Excise Department. Its headquarters is the ICA Building on Kallang Road, near Lavender MRT station.

Functions

ICA officers staff Singapore's air, land, and sea checkpoints, including Changi Airport and the Woodlands and Tuas crossings with Malaysia. The agency administers the National Registration Identity Card (NRIC), Singapore passports, birth and death registration, and citizenship matters. It has progressively introduced automated clearance systems, including iris and facial recognition, for travellers.
