Warriors Orochi

Warriors Orochi, known in Japan as Musō Orochi, is a series of hack-and-slash video games developed by Omega Force and published by Koei (later Koei Tecmo). It is a crossover of the Dynasty Warriors and Samurai Warriors series.

Premise

The serpent king Orochi creates a merged world combining China's Three Kingdoms era and Japan's Sengoku period, forcing their heroes to fight or ally against him. Players control teams of three characters, switching between them in battle, drawn from both parent series plus original and guest characters.

Games

The main entries are Warriors Orochi (2007), Warriors Orochi 2 (2008), Warriors Orochi 3 (2011, expanded as Ultimate), and Warriors Orochi 4 (2018), which featured a roster of 170 playable characters, a record for the genre.
