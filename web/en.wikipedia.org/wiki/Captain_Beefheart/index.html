Don Van Vliet (born Don Glen Vliet; 15 January 1941 – 17 December 2010), known by the stage name Captain Beefheart, was an American singer, musician, songwriter, and visual artist. His musical work was conducted with a rotating ensemble of musicians called the Magic Band (1965–1982), with whom he recorded 13 studio albums. Known for his powerful singing voice and wide vocal range, Van Vliet also played the harmonica, saxophone and numerous other wind instruments. His music blended blues, rock, psychedelia and free jazz with avant-garde composition and surrealist poetic imagery.

Life and career

Early years and the Magic Band

Van Vliet grew up in Lancaster, California, in the Mojave Desert, an environment that left a strong mark on his art. A child sculptor of some promise, he won a scholarship to study art in Europe but his parents declined it. In high school he befriended Frank Zappa, with whom he shared a love of blues and R&B records. Adopting the Captain Beefheart persona (derived from a movie treatment he and Zappa had devised), he formed the first Magic Band in 1964. The band's debut album, Safe as Milk (1967), featuring Ry Cooder on guitar, combined blues with psychedelic rock.

Trout Mask Replica

His third album, Trout Mask Replica (1969), produced by Zappa and released on Zappa's Straight Records, is regarded as his masterpiece: a sprawling double album of dissonant, rhythmically complex music rehearsed intensively over months in a communal house in Woodland Hills, Los Angeles. Long a critics' favourite, it was later inducted into the United States National Recording Registry. Subsequent albums such as Lick My Decals Off, Baby (1970), The Spotlight Kid (1972) and Clear Spot (1972) continued to attract critical acclaim while achieving limited commercial success.

Later music and painting

After a commercially oriented detour in the mid-1970s, Van Vliet returned to form with Shiny Beast (Bat Chain Puller) (1978), Doc at the Radar Station (1980) and Ice Cream for Crow (1982). In 1982 he retired from music to devote himself to painting, developing a successful second career as an abstract expressionist artist whose works were exhibited internationally and commanded high prices. He lived reclusively in northern California and died in 2010 from complications of multiple sclerosis.

Legacy

Although his records sold poorly during his lifetime, Captain Beefheart became a foundational influence on punk, new wave and alternative rock artists, and on experimental musicians generally; figures such as Tom Waits, John Lydon and the bands Devo and Pere Ubu have cited his influence. Trout Mask Replica routinely appears in lists of the greatest albums of all time.
