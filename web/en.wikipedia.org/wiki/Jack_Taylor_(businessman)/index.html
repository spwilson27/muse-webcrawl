Jack Taylor (businessman)

Jack Crawford Taylor (April 14, 1922 – July 2, 2016) was an American businessman and billionaire who founded the Enterprise Rent-A-Car Company.

Life

Born in St. Louis, Missouri, Taylor served as a US Navy fighter pilot in the Second World War, flying from the aircraft carrier USS Enterprise, after which he later named his company. In 1957 he founded a car leasing business in St. Louis that grew into Enterprise Rent-A-Car, the largest car rental company in North America.

Philanthropy

Taylor and his family became major philanthropists in St. Louis, supporting education, the arts and conservation. He appeared on Forbes lists of billionaires with a fortune built on the privately held Enterprise Holdings.
