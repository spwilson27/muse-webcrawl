Oklahoma State University–Stillwater (often shortened to Oklahoma State, OSU, or OK State) is a public land-grant research university in Stillwater, Oklahoma. Founded in 1890 as Oklahoma Agricultural and Mechanical College under the Morrill Act, it is the flagship campus of the Oklahoma State University System.

Academics

The university enrolls more than 25,000 students on its Stillwater campus across colleges of agriculture, engineering, business, education, human sciences, arts and sciences, and veterinary medicine. It is classified among research universities with high research activity.

Campus and athletics

The campus is known for its Georgian architecture and landscaped grounds. Its athletic teams, the Cowboys and Cowgirls, compete in the Big 12 Conference.
