Piauí is one of the 26 states of Brazil, located in the country's Northeast Region. Its capital and largest city is Teresina. Piauí has the shortest coastline of any Brazilian state, at 66 kilometres along the Atlantic Ocean.

Geography

The state is characterised by the semi-arid sertão in its interior and the Parnaíba River delta on the coast, one of the few open-sea deltas in the Americas. The Serra da Capivara National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, preserves some of the oldest prehistoric rock art and human remains in the Americas.

Economy

Agriculture — including soybeans, cotton, and cashew nuts — livestock raising, and services dominate the economy, and Piauí is one of Brazil's poorer states by GDP per capita, though it has become a significant producer of wind and solar energy.
