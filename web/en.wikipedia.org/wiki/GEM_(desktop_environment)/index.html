GEM (desktop environment)

GEM (Graphics Environment Manager) is a graphical user interface and windowing system created by Digital Research in the 1980s. It is best known as the built-in interface of the Atari ST computer range and was also available for IBM PC compatibles.

History

GEM was first released in 1984–85, deriving in part from work on GSX. The Atari ST (1985) shipped with GEM on top of TOS, giving it a Mac-like desktop at a low price. On the PC, GEM was used with applications such as Ventura Publisher.

Legal dispute

Apple sued Digital Research, claiming GEM infringed the Macintosh interface; the resulting settlement forced changes to the PC version of GEM, removing features such as overlapping desktop windows in early releases.

Legacy

Digital Research released the GEM source under the GNU GPL in 1999, and the system lives on in open-source descendants such as FreeGEM and OpenGEM, and in the Atari ST community.
