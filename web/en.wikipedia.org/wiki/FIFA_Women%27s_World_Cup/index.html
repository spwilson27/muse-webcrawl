The FIFA Women's World Cup is the premier international championship for women's national association football teams, organised by FIFA and contested every four years. The first tournament was held in China in 1991 and was won by the United States.

History

The tournament grew from 12 teams in 1991 to 32 in 2023, when Australia and New Zealand co-hosted and Spain won its first title. The United States is the most successful nation with four championships (1991, 1999, 2015, 2019), followed by Germany with two; Norway, Japan and Spain have one each.

Format

Teams qualify through continental competitions for a finals tournament consisting of a group stage followed by knockout rounds culminating in the final. The 2023 edition set attendance and viewership records, reflecting the rapid growth of the women's game.
