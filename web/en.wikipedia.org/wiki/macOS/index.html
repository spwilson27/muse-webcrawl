macOS, originally Mac OS X and later OS X, is a Unix operating system developed and marketed by Apple since 2001. It is the primary operating system for Apple's Mac computers.

History

macOS succeeded the classic Mac OS and was based on technology developed at NeXT, which Apple acquired in 1997. Major releases, initially named after big cats and later after California locations, have included Cheetah, Tiger, Snow Leopard, El Capitan, Mojave, Big Sur, Monterey, Ventura, Sonoma, and Sequoia.

Architecture

At its core is Darwin, a free and open-source Unix-like operating system built on the XNU kernel, with graphical layers including Aqua, Quartz, and Metal.
