Eurojackpot

Eurojackpot is a transnational European lottery launched in March 2012, modelled on the older EuroMillions but with a jackpot cap and odds structure designed to produce more frequent top-prize wins. Draws are held every Tuesday and Friday evening in Helsinki, Finland.

Format

Players choose five main numbers from 1 to 50 and two Euro numbers from 1 to 12 (the pool was enlarged from 10 in March 2022). Matching all seven wins the jackpot, which starts at €10 million and rolls over until it reaches a cap of €120 million, at which point excess funds cascade to the next prize tier. There are twelve prize tiers in total. Tickets cost €2 per line in most participating countries, though prices and supplementary national games vary.

Participating countries

The founding participants in 2012 were Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands and Slovenia, with Spain joining later that year. The game has since expanded to include Croatia, Latvia, Lithuania, Norway, Sweden, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Slovakia, Poland and Greece, making it one of the largest lottery pools in the world, with a potential player base of over 300 million people.

Record wins

The first jackpot, won in May 2012, paid €19.5 million to a German ticket. Since the cap was raised to €120 million in 2022, several jackpots have reached the ceiling, including a €120 million win in Denmark in July 2022 and further maximum wins in Germany.
