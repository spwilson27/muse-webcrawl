Adam Shankman

Adam Michael Shankman (born November 27, 1964) is an American film director, producer, writer and choreographer. He began his career as a dancer in music videos and films, then became a sought-after choreographer before moving into directing.

Career

Shankman's directing credits include The Wedding Planner (2001), A Walk to Remember (2002), Bringing Down the House (2003), The Pacifier (2005), the hit musical Hairspray (2007), Bedtime Stories (2008), Rock of Ages (2012), What Men Want (2019) and Disenchanted (2022). He served as a producer and judge on the television dance competition So You Think You Can Dance, produced the 82nd Academy Awards ceremony in 2010, and has directed episodes of series including Glee and Modern Family.
