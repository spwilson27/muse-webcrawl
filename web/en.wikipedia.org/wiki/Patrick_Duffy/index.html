Patrick Duffy (born March 17, 1949) is an American actor and director, widely known for his role as Bobby Ewing on the CBS primetime soap opera Dallas (1978–1991), a role he reprised in the 2012 revival series.

Career

Duffy was born in Townsend, Montana, and graduated from the University of Washington. His first starring television role was as Mark Harris in the NBC series Man from Atlantis (1977–1978). After Dallas, he starred as Frank Lambert on the ABC/CBS sitcom Step by Step (1991–1998).

The departure and return of his character Bobby Ewing on Dallas produced one of television's most famous plot devices: an entire season was retrospectively explained as a dream, in the so-called "Dream Season". Duffy has also done voice work, including appearances on animated series, and continues to act and direct.
