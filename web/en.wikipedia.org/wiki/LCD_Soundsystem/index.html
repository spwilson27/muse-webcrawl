LCD Soundsystem

LCD Soundsystem is an American rock band from Brooklyn, New York, formed in 2002 by James Murphy, co-founder of the DFA Records label. The band comprises Murphy (vocals), Nancy Whang (synthesizer, keyboards, vocals), Pat Mahoney (drums), Tyler Pope (bass, guitar, synthesizer), Al Doyle (guitar, synthesizer, percussion), Matt Thornley (guitar, synthesizer, percussion), and Korey Richey (synthesizer, piano, percussion).

LCD Soundsystem performing in 2016

The band began with the single "Losing My Edge" (2002), a cult favorite, followed by "Yeah" (2004). Their self-titled debut album (2005) brought them a Grammy nomination and included the single "Daft Punk Is Playing at My House". Their second album, Sound of Silver (2007), was widely acclaimed and named one of the best albums of the decade by several publications; it contains the songs "North American Scum", "Someone Great", and "All My Friends", the last frequently cited among the greatest songs of the 2000s.

Breakup and reunion

Their third album, This Is Happening (2010), was their first to enter the US top ten. In 2011 Murphy announced the band's dissolution, culminating in a sold-out farewell concert at Madison Square Garden on 2 April 2011, documented in the film Shut Up and Play the Hits (2012) and the live album The Long Goodbye (2014).

The band reunited in 2015, releasing the single "Christmas Will Break Your Heart", and their fourth album, American Dream (2017), became their first number-one album in the United States; its song "Tonite" won the Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording. The band has continued to tour and release occasional material, including the 2022 single "New Body Rhumba" for the film White Noise.
