StudioCanal

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

StudioCanal (formerly known as Canal+ Image and StudioCanal) is a French film production and distribution company and a subsidiary of the Canal+ Group. Founded in 1988 as an offshoot of the French pay-television channel Canal+, it is one of Europe's largest film studios, with operations in France, the United Kingdom, Germany, and other territories.

History

The company grew through the 1990s by co-financing and distributing films and by acquiring film libraries. Major acquisitions included the catalogue of Carolco Pictures and, in the United Kingdom, the library of EMI Films, giving StudioCanal control of a large catalogue of classic British cinema, including titles from Ealing Studios and Hammer Film Productions. Its total library comprises several thousand titles, making it one of the largest film catalogues in Europe.

Productions

StudioCanal has produced or co-produced numerous internationally successful films, including Paddington (2014) and Paddington 2 (2017), Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy (2011), The Imitation Game (2014, international distribution), Legend (2015), and Back to Black (2024). The Paddington films, produced through its British arm and Heyday Films, became signature family releases for the studio. StudioCanal also operates in television production through subsidiaries and partnerships, producing series for European and international markets.

Restoration

The company has an active programme of restoring films from its catalogue, releasing remastered editions of classics such as The Third Man, The Ladykillers, and works from the Ealing and Hammer catalogues in cinemas and on home video.
