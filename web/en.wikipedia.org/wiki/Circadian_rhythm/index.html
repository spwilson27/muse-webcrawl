Circadian rhythm

Circadian rhythm
PeriodAbout 24 hours (Latin circa diem)
Master clockSuprachiasmatic nucleus (hypothalamus)
Key signalLight; hormone melatonin
[Image: A diagram of a 24-hour body clock ring with sleep, temperature and hormone curves]

A circadian rhythm is any biological process displaying an endogenous, entrainable oscillation of about 24 hours — sleep–wake cycles, body temperature, hormone release and gene expression in nearly every cell. The master clock in the brain's suprachiasmatic nucleus synchronises to light.

Health

Disrupted rhythms (jet lag, shift work, late screens) impair sleep, mood and metabolism; chronobiology won the 2017 Nobel Prize for the clock genes' discovery. Melatonin supplements and morning light are common countermeasures.
