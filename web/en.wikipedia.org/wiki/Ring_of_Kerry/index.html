Ring of Kerry

The Ring of Kerry (Irish: Mórchuaird Chiarraí) is a 179-kilometre (111-mile) circular tourist route in County Kerry, south-western Ireland, following the N70, N71 and R562 roads around the Iveragh Peninsula. Starting and ending in Killarney, the route passes through Kenmare, Sneem, Waterville, Cahersiveen, and Killorglin.

Sights

Attractions along the ring include Muckross House and Killarney National Park, the Gap of Dunloe, Staigue stone fort, Derrynane House (home of Daniel O'Connell), the Skellig Ring spur overlooking the Skellig Islands, and Ladies' View, a celebrated vantage over the Killarney lakes. Tour buses traditionally travel anti-clockwise to avoid meeting on the narrow roads.

The annual Ring of Kerry Charity Cycle, held since the 1980s, attracts thousands of cyclists riding the full circuit in one day and has raised tens of millions of euro for Irish charities.
