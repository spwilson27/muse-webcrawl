Prüfer domain


Commutative algebra concept diagram

In mathematics, a Prüfer domain is a type of commutative ring, generalising Dedekind domains to the non-Noetherian setting. An integral domain R is a Prüfer domain if every finitely generated nonzero ideal of R is invertible, or equivalently if the localisation of R at every prime (or maximal) ideal is a valuation domain.

Properties

Prüfer domains are named after the German mathematician Heinz Prüfer. Every Dedekind domain that is not a field-like special case, every valuation domain, and every Bézout domain is a Prüfer domain. Prüfer domains are coherent and integrally closed, and their ideal theory closely parallels that of Dedekind domains despite the absence of Noetherian hypotheses.

Examples

The ring of entire functions on the complex plane and rings of algebraic integers in infinite extensions provide classical non-Noetherian examples.
