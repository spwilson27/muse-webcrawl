Brusio spiral viaduct

The Brusio spiral viaduct carries the Bernina railway over its own loop.

The Brusio spiral viaduct (Italian: Viadotto elicoidale di Brusio) is a single-track, nine-arched stone spiral railway viaduct on the Bernina line of the Rhaetian Railway in the canton of Graubünden, Switzerland. Opened in 1908, it lies just south of the village of Brusio and lets the railway gain (or lose) elevation within a very small area while respecting the line's maximum gradient of 7 percent.

Design

The viaduct forms a near-complete circle: the track curves around a spiral of roughly 360 degrees, passing over itself, so the line descends about 20 metres without the aid of rack rails or a tunnel. Each of its nine arches has a span of about 10 metres. The structure, built of stone, has become one of the most photographed landmarks of the Bernina line, which together with the Albula line was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2008.
