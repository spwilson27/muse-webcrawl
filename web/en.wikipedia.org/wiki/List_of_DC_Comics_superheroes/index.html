List of DC Comics superheroes

This is a list of superheroes appearing in DC Comics publications. DC Comics (founded as National Comics) launched the superhero genre with the debut of Superman in Action Comics #1 (1938), followed by Batman in 1939 and Wonder Woman in 1941. Its roster grew across the Golden and Silver Ages to include the Flash, Green Lantern, Aquaman, and many others, organized in teams such as the Justice League, the Justice Society of America, and the Teen Titans.

Major heroes

Core figures include Superman (Clark Kent/Kal-El), Batman (Bruce Wayne), Wonder Woman (Diana Prince), the Flash (Barry Allen, Wally West, Jay Garrick), Green Lantern (Hal Jordan and other Corps members), Aquaman (Arthur Curry), Green Arrow (Oliver Queen), Shazam (Billy Batson), Martian Manhunter (J'onn J'onzz), and the various Robins, Batgirls, and other members of the extended Batman family.

By era

The list is commonly organized by era: Golden Age heroes of the Justice Society (such as the original Flash and Green Lantern), Silver Age heroes of the Justice League, and later heroes introduced in the Bronze and Modern Ages, including the Teen Titans' later members and heroes acquired from other publishers such as Charlton and Fawcett Comics.

Teams

Superhero teams covered include the Justice League, Justice Society of America, Teen Titans, Doom Patrol, Legion of Super-Heroes, Birds of Prey, and the Green Lantern Corps.
