2-1-1

TypeN11 telephone code
CountriesUnited States, Canada
PurposeCommunity information and referral services

2-1-1 is a telephone number reserved in the United States and Canada for community information and referral services. Dialing 2-1-1 connects callers, free of charge, to trained specialists who can refer them to local health and human services.

Services

Referrals typically cover food assistance, housing and shelter, utility assistance, healthcare, mental health and crisis support, childcare, disaster relief, and services for seniors and people with disabilities. Many 2-1-1 services also operate websites and text-based help.

History

The number was first established in Atlanta, Georgia, in 1997, and the US Federal Communications Commission assigned 2-1-1 nationally in 2000. Coverage now reaches the great majority of the US population, often in partnership with United Way organizations.
