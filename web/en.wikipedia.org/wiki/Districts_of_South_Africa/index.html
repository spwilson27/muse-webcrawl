Districts of South Africa

South Africa is divided into nine provinces, which are subdivided into 52 districts: 44 district municipalities and 8 metropolitan municipalities. District municipalities are further divided into local municipalities. The metropolitan municipalities — including the City of Cape Town, eThekwini (Durban), City of Johannesburg, City of Tshwane (Pretoria), Ekurhuleni, Nelson Mandela Bay, Buffalo City and Mangaung — function as both district and local government. This system of district municipalities was established under the Municipal Structures Act of 1998 and first implemented after the 2000 municipal elections.
