Al Hilal Saudi Football Club (Arabic: نادي الهلال السعودي), simply known as Al-Hilal, is a professional multi-sports club based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Founded in 1957, its football team competes in the Saudi Pro League and is the most successful club in Saudi and Asian football history by major trophies.

Honours

Al-Hilal has won a record number of Saudi Pro League titles, King Cups and Saudi Super Cups, and four AFC Champions League titles (1991, 2000, 2019 and 2021), making it the most successful club in Asian continental competition. It finished runner-up at the 2022 FIFA Club World Cup.

History

The club was founded as the Olympic Club and renamed Al-Hilal ("The Crescent") in 1958. Backed by large support and, from 2023, majority ownership by the Public Investment Fund, the club has signed internationally prominent players and is a central rival of Al-Nassr in the Riyadh derby.
