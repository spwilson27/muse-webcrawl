Stiefel–Whitney class

In mathematics, and specifically in algebraic topology and differential geometry, a Stiefel–Whitney class is a characteristic class associated to a real vector bundle. The classes take values in cohomology with mod-2 coefficients and are named after Eduard Stiefel and Hassler Whitney, who developed them in the 1930s.

FieldAlgebraic topology
Named afterEduard Stiefel; Hassler Whitney
Notationwi(E) ∈ Hi(X; Z/2)

Properties

The total Stiefel–Whitney class w(E) = 1 + w₁ + w₂ + … is multiplicative under Whitney sum of bundles. The first class w₁ is the obstruction to orientability of the bundle; the second class w₂ is related to the existence of a spin structure. Stiefel–Whitney numbers, obtained by pairing products of the classes with the fundamental class of a manifold, are cobordism invariants.
