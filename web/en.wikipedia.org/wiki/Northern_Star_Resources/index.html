Northern Star Resources Limited is an Australian gold mining company listed on the Australian Securities Exchange. It operates gold mines in Western Australia and Alaska and is one of the world's largest gold producers.

History

The company grew rapidly after acquiring the Paulsens gold mine in 2010, followed by a series of acquisitions from Barrick Gold and Newmont in the Kalgoorlie and Pilbara regions. In 2018 it acquired the Pogo gold mine in Alaska. In February 2021 Northern Star merged with Saracen Mineral Holdings, creating a top-ten global gold producer and consolidating ownership of the Kalgoorlie Super Pit (KCGM).

Operations

Production centres include Kalgoorlie (including the Super Pit and Kanowna Belle), Yandal (Jundee and Bronzewing) and Pogo in Alaska. The company has pursued a strategy centred on underground mining, reserve growth through exploration, and progressive expansion of the Kalgoorlie mill.
