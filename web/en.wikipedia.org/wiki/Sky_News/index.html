Sky News

Sky News
Launched5 February 1989
OwnerSky Group (Comcast)
HeadquartersLondon
[Image: The Sky News studio at Sky's Osterley campus in west London]

Sky News is a British free-to-air television news channel and news organisation launched on 5 February 1989 by Sky plc, pioneering 24-hour news in the UK a decade after CNN did so in the US. It is known for its 'first with breaking news' emphasis and has won many Royal Television Society news awards.

History and ownership

Sky News launched alongside the early Sky satellite service, beating the BBC's rolling-news plans to air. It became part of Comcast's portfolio when Comcast acquired Sky in 2018. The channel's output includes the flagship evening programmes, Sunday political shows and a strong digital and YouTube presence.
