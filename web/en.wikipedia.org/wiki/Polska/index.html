Polska (disambiguation)

Polska is the Polish name for Poland.

Polska is the name of Poland in the Polish language. The word may also refer to several other things:

Places

  • Polska, the endonym of the country of Poland in Central Europe.
  • Various villages and settlements in Poland and neighbouring countries bearing the name.

Music and dance

  • Polska, a Scandinavian folk dance and the associated fiddle tunes, in 3/4 time, traditionally danced by couples in Sweden, Norway, and parts of Finland. Despite the shared name, the dance's connection to Poland is indirect and debated.
  • "Polska" appears in the titles of numerous traditional Scandinavian fiddle tunes named after the players or villages they came from.

Other uses

  • Radio stations, newspapers and media outlets in Poland using the name.
  • Polska (grape), a grape variety.

The Swedish polska should not be confused with the polka, a different, faster dance in 2/4 time originating in Bohemia.
