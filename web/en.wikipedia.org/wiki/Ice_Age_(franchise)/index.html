Ice Age (franchise)

Ice Age is an American animated media franchise centering on a group of mammals surviving the Pleistocene ice age. Created by Blue Sky Studios and distributed by 20th Century Fox (later Disney), it consists of five theatrical feature films, a spin-off film, television specials, short films, and video games. The franchise has grossed over $3.2 billion worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing animated media franchises of all time.

Films

The series follows the odd "herd" formed by Manny, a woolly mammoth (voiced by Ray Romano); Sid, a talkative ground sloth (John Leguizamo); and Diego, a saber-toothed tiger (Denis Leary), joined in later films by Ellie the mammoth, the possums Crash and Eddie, and the weasel Buck. Interwoven through the films are the silent, acorn-obsessed travails of Scrat, a saber-toothed squirrel.

  • Ice Age (2002)
  • Ice Age: The Meltdown (2006)
  • Ice Age: Dawn of the Dinosaurs (2009)
  • Ice Age: Continental Drift (2012)
  • Ice Age: Collision Course (2016)
  • The Ice Age Adventures of Buck Wild (2022), a spin-off released on Disney+ after the closure of Blue Sky Studios in 2021

Other media

The franchise also includes the television specials Ice Age: A Mammoth Christmas (2011) and Ice Age: The Great Egg-Scapade (2016), a series of Scrat short films, the Disney+ series Ice Age: Scrat Tales (2022), numerous video games, and theme park attractions.

Reception

While critical reception declined across the later sequels, the films remained commercially successful, particularly internationally, with the third and fourth installments each grossing close to $900 million worldwide.
