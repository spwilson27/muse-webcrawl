List of rail accidents (2000–2009)

List of rail accidents (2000–2009) is a list of notable railway accidents — including derailments, collisions, fires and other incidents involving trains — that occurred during the first decade of the 21st century. It forms part of a series of lists covering rail accidents by decade.

Scope

The list generally includes accidents resulting in multiple fatalities, major infrastructure damage, or significant historical importance, such as the Amagasaki rail crash and the Kaprun disaster. Terrorist attacks on railways, such as the 2004 Madrid train bombings (which killed 193 people), are sometimes treated separately.
