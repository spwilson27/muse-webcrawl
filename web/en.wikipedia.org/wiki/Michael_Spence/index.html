Michael Spence

Andrew Michael Spence (born 7 November 1943) is a Canadian-American economist, Nobel laureate known for his work on information asymmetry and signalling.

Michael Spence
Born: 7 Nov 1943, Montclair, New Jersey
Prize: Nobel Economics 2001 (with Akerlof and Stiglitz)
Institutions: Harvard, Stanford

Work

Spence's 1973 job-market signalling model showed how education can signal ability to employers under asymmetric information, even apart from productivity effects. He also contributed to industrial organisation and development economics, chairing the Commission on Growth and Development and writing The Next Convergence (2011).

Career

Educated at Princeton, Oxford (Rhodes Scholar) and Harvard (PhD), he taught at Harvard Business School and was dean of Stanford Graduate School of Business (1990–1999). See Signalling, Information asymmetry.
