Haynes International

Haynes International, Inc. is an American manufacturer of high-performance nickel- and cobalt-based alloys used in aerospace, chemical processing, and gas turbine applications. The company is headquartered in Kokomo, Indiana.

History

The company traces its origins to 1912, when inventor Elwood Haynes founded Haynes Stellite Works in Kokomo to produce his Stellite alloys. After passing through several owners, including Union Carbide, the business was established as an independent company, Haynes International, in 1989. Its best-known trademarked alloys include Hastelloy and Haynes series alloys, valued for corrosion and high-temperature resistance.

Operations

Haynes operates manufacturing facilities in Kokomo, Indiana, and Arcadia, Louisiana, along with service centres in the United States, Europe, and Asia. In 2024 the company agreed to be acquired by North American Stainless, a subsidiary of Spain's Acerinox group.
