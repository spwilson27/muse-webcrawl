Otto Graham

Otto Everett Graham Jr. (December 6, 1921 – December 17, 2003) was an American football quarterback who played for the Cleveland Browns in the All-America Football Conference (AAFC) and National Football League (NFL) from 1946 to 1955. Nicknamed "Automatic Otto" for his passing accuracy, he is regarded as one of the greatest quarterbacks of all time.

Graham led the Browns to a championship game in every one of his ten seasons, winning seven titles — four in the AAFC and three in the NFL (1950, 1954, 1955) — an unmatched record of sustained success. At Northwestern University he had been a star in football, basketball and baseball, and in 1946 he also played professional basketball for the Rochester Royals, winning an NBL title, making him a champion in two sports in the same year.

After retiring he coached at the Coast Guard Academy and served briefly as head coach and general manager of the Washington Redskins. Graham was inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 1965. He died in Sarasota, Florida, in 2003.
