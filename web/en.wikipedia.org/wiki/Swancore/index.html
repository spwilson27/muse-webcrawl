Swancore

Swancore is a subgenre and scene of post-hardcore music that emerged in the late 2000s and early 2010s. The term derives from Will Swan, guitarist of the Sacramento band Dance Gavin Dance, whose label Blue Swan Records, founded in 2013, released many of the style's key acts.

Characteristics

Swancore combines the aggressive vocals and dynamics of post-hardcore with elements of progressive rock, math rock, funk, jazz fusion and pop. Typical features include intricate, technical guitar work with prominent tapping and riffing, alternating harsh and clean sung vocals, frequent tempo and time-signature changes, and melodic, pop-influenced choruses.

Bands

In addition to Dance Gavin Dance, bands associated with the scene include Hail the Sun, Royal Coda, Stolas, Sianvar, Eidola, Wolf & Bear, Icarus the Owl and Adventurer. Many of these groups feature overlapping line-ups of musicians centred on Swan and vocalist Kurt Travis.
