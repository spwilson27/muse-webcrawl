His Majesty's Ship, or Her Majesty's Ship when the monarch is female, abbreviated HMS, is the ship prefix used for ships of the navy of the United Kingdom — the Royal Navy. The prefix has been in use since the late 18th century.

Usage

The prefix applies both to commissioned warships and to shore establishments ("stone frigates") of the Royal Navy. In the reign of Queen Elizabeth II (1952–2022) ships were "Her Majesty's Ship"; following the accession of Charles III, newly referenced ships are "His Majesty's Ship." Similar prefixes are used by other Commonwealth realms' navies, such as HMAS (Australia), HMCS (Canada) and HMNZS (New Zealand), as well as in navies such as Sweden's (HSwMS in English).

History

Earlier usage included "His Majesty's Frigate" and similar forms; the generic HMS became standard around the 1780s. Pennant numbers supplement the prefix in identifying individual vessels, since names are frequently reused in the Royal Navy.
