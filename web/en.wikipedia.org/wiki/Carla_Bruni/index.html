Carla Bruni-Sarkozy (born 23 December 1967) is an Italian-born French singer-songwriter and former supermodel. She was one of the highest-paid fashion models of the 1990s before turning to music, releasing the acclaimed debut album Quelqu'un m'a dit in 2002. In 2008 she married Nicolas Sarkozy, then President of France, becoming First Lady of France.

Early life

Bruni was born in Turin, Italy, into a wealthy family connected to the CEAT tyre company. Her mother, Marisa Borini, was a concert pianist; her legal father was industrialist Alberto Bruni Tedeschi. She later learned that her biological father was Maurizio Remmert, a classical guitarist. The family moved to France in 1973, partly fearing kidnapping by the Red Brigades. Bruni was educated in Paris and briefly studied art and architecture before leaving to pursue modelling at 19.

Modelling career

Signed to City Models in 1987, Bruni became one of the defining faces of 1990s fashion, working with designers including Christian Dior, Givenchy, Yves Saint Laurent, Chanel and Versace. She was among the top-earning models of the era, reportedly earning around $7.5 million a year at her peak. In 1997 she left fashion to focus on music.

Music career

Bruni wrote most of the songs for Julien Clerc's 2000 album Si j'étais elle. Her own debut, Quelqu'un m'a dit (2002), a gentle collection of French-language folk-pop songs, was a major success in France and sold over two million copies worldwide. Subsequent albums include No Promises (2007), settings of English-language poems; Comme si de rien n'était (2008); Little French Songs (2013); French Touch (2017), a covers album; and Carla Bruni (2020).

First Lady of France

Bruni married President Nicolas Sarkozy on 2 February 2008 at the Élysée Palace, the first French president to marry while in office since the 1930s. As First Lady she became a global ambassador for the Global Fund to Fight AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria, and founded a foundation supporting access to culture and education. In October 2011 she gave birth to a daughter, Giulia, the first child born to a serving French president and his wife.
