Matching gift

Matching gifts are a form of corporate philanthropy in which companies match charitable donations made by their employees to eligible nonprofit organisations, commonly at a 1:1 ratio, though ratios and caps vary by employer.

History

The first corporate matching gift programme is credited to the General Electric Foundation in 1954, matching employees' gifts to their colleges and universities.

Process and impact

Employees submit proof of donation, and the employer verifies eligibility and disburses the match. Billions of dollars in potential matching funds go unclaimed each year because employees are unaware of programmes. Some companies also match volunteer time with grants ("volunteer grants").
