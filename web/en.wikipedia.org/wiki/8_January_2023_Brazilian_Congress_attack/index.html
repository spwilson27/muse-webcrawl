8 January 2023 riots in Brasília

The 8 January 2023 Brazilian Congress attack was a riot in which thousands of supporters of former president Jair Bolsonaro, refusing to accept his defeat in the 2022 presidential election, stormed the Praça dos Três Poderes in Brasília and invaded and vandalized the buildings of the National Congress, the Planalto presidential palace, and the Supreme Federal Court — one week after the inauguration of President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.

Events

Since the October 2022 election, pro-Bolsonaro groups had camped outside army headquarters demanding military intervention, spreading unfounded claims of electoral fraud. On Sunday, January 8, crowds marched to the Praça dos Três Poderes, overwhelmed a limited police presence, and broke into the three buildings, smashing windows, furniture, and artworks — including Emiliano Di Cavalcanti's painting As Mulatas — and damaging the Congress, the presidential palace, and the Supreme Court. Security forces regained control by the evening; around 1,500 people were detained at the site and many more at the army camp the next day.

Reactions and aftermath

President Lula decreed federal intervention in the Federal District's public security until January 31 and blamed security officials' failures; the district's governor was temporarily suspended and its security secretary, a former Bolsonaro minister, was arrested. The attack drew comparisons with the January 6 United States Capitol attack and was widely condemned internationally. Subsequent investigations by the Federal Police and a congressional inquiry examined financing and planning, and the Supreme Federal Court tried hundreds of defendants, handing down sentences of up to 17 years for those convicted of crimes including attempted coup d'état. Bolsonaro, who was in Florida at the time, was investigated for alleged involvement in plotting to overturn the election.
