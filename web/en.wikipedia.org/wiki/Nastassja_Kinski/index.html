Nastassja Kinski

Nastassja Aglaia Kinski (born 24 January 1961) is a German actress and former model who has appeared in more than 60 films in Europe and the United States. The daughter of actor Klaus Kinski, she came to international prominence with Roman Polanski's Tess (1979), for which she won the Golden Globe Award for New Star of the Year, and starred in Wim Wenders's Paris, Texas (1984), which won the Palme d'Or at Cannes.

Early life and breakthrough

Kinski was born in West Berlin to actor Klaus Kinski and his second wife, Ruth Brigitte Tocki. After her parents' divorce she had little contact with her father. Discovered as a teenager, she worked as a model and made her film debut in Wenders's The Wrong Move (1975), credited as Nastassja Nakszynski. She appeared in the Hammer horror film To the Devil a Daughter (1976) and the German television crime series Tatort before Polanski cast her as Thomas Hardy's heroine Tess Durbeyfield, a role that made her an international star at eighteen.

Career

In the 1980s Kinski alternated between European art cinema and Hollywood productions: Paul Schrader's Cat People (1982), Unfaithfully Yours (1984), The Hotel New Hampshire (1984), Francis Ford Coppola's One from the Heart (1982), and Wenders's Paris, Texas, in which her performance as the estranged Jane Henderson, delivered largely in a peep-show booth monologue, was widely praised. Richard Avedon's famous 1981 photograph of Kinski nude with a python became an iconic poster. Later roles included Faraway, So Close! (1993), One Night Stand (1997), Saving Grace (2000), An American Rhapsody (2001) and Inland Empire (2006).

Personal life

Kinski has three children; her daughter Sonja Kinski is a model and actress. She was married to Egyptian filmmaker Ibrahim Moussa from 1984 to 1992. Her half-siblings Pola and Nikolai Kinski are also actors; in 2013 Pola published a memoir alleging abuse by their father, and Nastassja expressed support for her sister.
