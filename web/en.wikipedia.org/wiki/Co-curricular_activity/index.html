Co-curricular activity

Co-curricular activities are activities that complement the formal academic curriculum of a school or university, taking place outside regular classroom instruction but usually organised by the institution. Examples include sports teams, music ensembles, drama productions, debate clubs, student publications and volunteering.

Purpose

Educators argue that co-curricular activities develop social skills, leadership, teamwork, time management and civic engagement, and that participation is associated with improved academic outcomes and school attachment. The term is often used interchangeably with "extracurricular activity", though strictly co-curricular activities are those tied more closely to coursework.

Examples

Common examples include athletics, orchestra and choir, chess club, science fairs, Model United Nations, scouting, and student government.
