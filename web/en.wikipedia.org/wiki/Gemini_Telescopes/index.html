Gemini Observatory

The Gemini Observatory comprises two 8.1-metre optical/infrared telescopes operated by an international partnership. Gemini North stands on Mauna Kea in Hawaii, and Gemini South on Cerro Pachón in Chile, giving the pair full coverage of both celestial hemispheres.

Instrumentation

Both telescopes use thin meniscus mirrors with adaptive optics systems — including the GeMS multi-conjugate system at Gemini South — to deliver images rivalling space telescopes. Instruments include the GMOS spectrographs, near-infrared cameras and the Gemini Planet Imager, used for direct imaging of exoplanets.

Operations

The observatory is managed by the Association of Universities for Research in Astronomy (AURA) under a cooperative agreement with the US National Science Foundation; NOIRLab took over operations in 2019.
