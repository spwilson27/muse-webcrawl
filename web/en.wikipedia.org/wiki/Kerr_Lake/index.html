Kerr Lake

Kerr Lake
Location: Virginia – North Carolina border
Surface area: ~50,000 acres
Created: 1952 (John H. Kerr Dam)

Kerr Lake, also known as Buggs Island Lake, is a reservoir on the Roanoke River straddling the Virginia–North Carolina border. It was created by the John H. Kerr Dam, completed in 1952 by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers for flood control and hydroelectric power.

Name

The dam is named for North Carolina congressman John H. Kerr, who supported the project. The lake's alternate name, Buggs Island Lake, comes from an island in the Roanoke downstream of the dam site and remains in common local use in Virginia.

Recreation

With about 850 miles of shoreline, the lake supports boating, camping and fishing for largemouth bass, crappie and catfish. Kerr Lake State Recreation Area in North Carolina and Staunton River State Park and Occoneechee State Park in Virginia border its shores.
