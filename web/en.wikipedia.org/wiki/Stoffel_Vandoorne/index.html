Stoffel Vandoorne

Stoffel Vandoorne (born 26 March 1992) is a Belgian professional racing driver who won the 2021–22 Formula E World Championship with Mercedes-EQ. He previously competed in Formula One for McLaren from 2016 to 2018, and has served as a reserve and test driver for Formula One teams (including Aston Martin) alongside racing in Formula E and endurance racing.

Early career

Vandoorne progressed through karting and junior single-seaters, winning the F4 Eurocup 1.6 title in 2010 and the Eurocup Formula Renault 2.0 championship in 2012, when he joined the McLaren Young Driver Programme. He dominated the 2015 GP2 Series season with ART Grand Prix, taking the title with a record points tally, and won races in Super Formula in Japan in 2016.

Formula One

Vandoorne made his Formula One debut at the 2016 Bahrain Grand Prix, substituting for the injured Fernando Alonso and scoring a point for tenth place. He became a full-time McLaren driver for 2017 and 2018 alongside Alonso, in uncompetitive Honda- and Renault-powered cars, and was out-scored by his team-mate; McLaren replaced him with Lando Norris for 2019.

Formula E and later career

Vandoorne moved to Formula E with HWA Racelab in 2018 and then the factory Mercedes-EQ team, winning his first race in Berlin in 2020. In the 2021–22 season he won the Monaco E-Prix and, with consistent podium finishes, secured the Drivers' World Championship. He subsequently raced for DS Penske and Peugeot's World Endurance Championship Hypercar programme, while serving as an Aston Martin Formula One reserve and test driver.
