Wookiee

Wookiees are a fictional species of tall, hairy humanoids native to the planet Kashyyyk in the Star Wars universe. The best-known Wookiee is Chewbacca, co-pilot of the Millennium Falcon and companion of Han Solo.

Characteristics

Wookiees are covered in long fur, stand well over two metres tall, and possess great physical strength and long lifespans of several centuries. They are known for their fierce loyalty, strong sense of honour and hot tempers; a Wookiee who owes a "life debt" will serve the one who saved them. They communicate in Shyriiwook, a language of growls and roars that other species can learn to understand but cannot easily speak.

Homeworld

Kashyyyk is a jungle world of immense wroshyr trees, in whose branches Wookiees build their cities. During the Clone Wars the planet was the site of a major battle depicted in Revenge of the Sith (2005); under the Galactic Empire, Wookiees were enslaved for labour.

Creation

George Lucas based Chewbacca partly on his Alaskan Malamute dog, Indiana, who rode beside him in his car. The name "Wookiee" reportedly came from an ad-libbed line during the dubbing of Lucas's earlier film THX 1138. Peter Mayhew portrayed Chewbacca in the original trilogy and later films.
