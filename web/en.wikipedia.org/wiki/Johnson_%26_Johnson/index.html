Johnson & Johnson (J&J) is an American multinational corporation founded in 1886 that develops medical devices and pharmaceutical products. It is one of the largest healthcare companies in the world and is headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey.

History

The company was founded by brothers Robert Wood Johnson I, James Wood Johnson, and Edward Mead Johnson, initially to produce sterile surgical dressings. It became known for products such as Band-Aid bandages and Johnson's Baby products. In 2023 the company spun off its consumer health division as a separate company, Kenvue, leaving J&J focused on pharmaceuticals and medical technology.

Products and business

J&J's pharmaceutical arm, Janssen, produces major medicines including treatments for cancer, immunological diseases, and infectious diseases. Its MedTech segment makes surgical instruments, orthopaedics, and vision-care products such as contact lenses.
