Hilary term

Hilary term is the second academic term of the University of Oxford and the University of Dublin. It runs from January to March and is so named because the feast day of St Hilary of Poitiers, 14 January, falls near the beginning of term. The term is known as Lent term at Cambridge and some other institutions, and as Epiphany term at Durham.

Dates

At Oxford the Hilary full term lasts eight weeks, beginning on the Sunday after St Hilary's day and ending on the Saturday before Palm Sunday. The corresponding legal sittings in England and Wales were historically also called the Hilary term (or Hilary sittings), one of four terms of the law courts, running from January to Easter.

Usage

Oxford's three terms are Michaelmas (autumn), Hilary (spring, in the academic sense of the start of the year), and Trinity (summer). Trinity College Dublin likewise uses the name Hilary term for its second term. Many schools and older universities in Britain, Ireland, and the Commonwealth retain the traditional term names.
