List of ISO 639-1 codes

ISO 639 is a standardized nomenclature used to classify languages. Each language is assigned a two-letter (639-1) and three-letter (639-2 and 639-3) lowercase abbreviation, amended in later versions of the nomenclature. The list of ISO 639-1 codes is the subset of codes consisting of two letters; there are 184 such codes covering the world's major languages.

History

The ISO 639-1 standard was first published in 1988 and revised in 2002. Codes are administered by the ISO 639 Joint Advisory Committee. Two-letter codes are also used as the basis for many language tags in computing, including in HTML and in Wikipedia's own language editions.
