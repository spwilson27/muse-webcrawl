Sam Altman

Born: Samuel Harris Altman, 22 April 1985, Chicago, Illinois, U.S.

Occupation: Entrepreneur, investor, programmer

Known for: CEO of OpenAI; president of Y Combinator

Samuel Harris Altman (born April 22, 1985) is an American entrepreneur and investor best known as the chief executive officer of OpenAI since 2019. He was president of the startup accelerator Y Combinator from 2014 to 2019 and is considered a leading figure in the artificial intelligence industry.

Early life and education

Altman was born in Chicago and grew up in St. Louis, Missouri. He attended John Burroughs School and studied computer science at Stanford University, leaving in 2005 without completing a degree to co-found Loopt, a location-based social networking company.

Career

Loopt was acquired by Green Dot Corporation in 2012 for $43.4 million. Altman joined Y Combinator in 2011 and became its president in 2014, expanding the accelerator's scale and launching initiatives such as YC Continuity. In 2015 he co-founded OpenAI, a research organisation aimed at ensuring artificial general intelligence benefits humanity.

In November 2023, OpenAI's board removed Altman as CEO, prompting widespread reaction from employees and investors; he was reinstated five days later following a board restructuring. Under his leadership OpenAI launched products including GPT-4 and ChatGPT, and reached a valuation among the highest of any private company.

Investments

As an angel investor and through funds such as Hydrazine Capital, Altman has invested in companies including Airbnb, Reddit, Stripe, and nuclear energy ventures Helion Energy and Oklo.
