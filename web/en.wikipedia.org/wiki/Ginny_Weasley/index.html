Ginevra Molly "Ginny" Weasley is a fictional character in J. K. Rowling's Harry Potter series. She is the youngest child and only daughter of Arthur and Molly Weasley, and the younger sister of Ron Weasley. Ginny first appears briefly in Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (1997) and plays a central role in Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets (1998), in which she is possessed by Lord Voldemort through his enchanted diary.

Character development

Across the later novels Ginny develops from a shy girl with a crush on Harry Potter into a confident, talented witch. She is a skilled flyer and Quidditch player, first playing as Seeker and later as Chaser for Gryffindor, and she excels at the Bat-Bogey Hex. In Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix she joins Dumbledore's Army and fights in the Battle of the Department of Mysteries. In Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows she helps lead the student resistance at Hogwarts.

Later life

After the war, Ginny plays professional Quidditch for the Holyhead Harpies before becoming the senior Quidditch correspondent for the Daily Prophet. She marries Harry Potter, and they have three children: James Sirius, Albus Severus and Lily Luna Potter.

Reception

Ginny has been described by Rowling as "tough, not in an unpleasant way, but gutsy." The character is portrayed in the film adaptations by Bonnie Wright.
