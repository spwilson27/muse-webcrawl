List of current and historical women's universities and colleges in the United States

Women's colleges in the United States are single-sex undergraduate institutions that admit only women. At their mid-20th-century peak there were more than 200; today roughly 30 remain, after most became coeducational, merged, or closed following the widespread adoption of coeducation in the 1960s and 1970s. The most famous group is the Seven Sisters of the Northeast.
