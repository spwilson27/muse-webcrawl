Navy Exchange

Navy Exchange (NEX)
Founded: 1946
Headquarters: Virginia Beach, Virginia
Parent: Navy Exchange Service Command

The Navy Exchange (NEX) is a retail store chain owned and operated by the United States Navy under the Navy Exchange Service Command (NEXCOM). Like the Army and Air Force Exchange Service, it provides goods and services to authorized patrons — active-duty, reserve, and retired service members and their families — at United States Navy installations around the world.

Operations

NEX stores range from small shipboard stores and mini-marts to large department-store-style exchanges, and the system includes Navy Lodges, uniform shops, gas stations, and barber and beauty shops. Sales are generally exempt from state and local sales taxes, and prices are typically set below commercial retail.

Purpose

The Exchange is a non-appropriated fund activity: its profits fund Navy Morale, Welfare and Recreation (MWR) programmes, contributing a majority share of its earnings annually to quality-of-life services for sailors and their families. The precursor ship's store system dates to the 19th century; the modern centralized Navy Exchange system was established in 1946.
