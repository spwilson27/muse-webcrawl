Corporate housing

Corporate housing is a furnished apartment, condominium or house rented on a temporary basis — typically for 30 days or more — to individuals or companies as an alternative to a hotel for extended stays. The term is used mainly in the United States, where a specialised industry provides housing for relocating employees, consultants, project teams, medical staff, military personnel and others on long assignments.

Features

Corporate units are fully furnished and equipped, with kitchens, linens, housekeeping services and utilities and internet included in a single rate. They offer considerably more space than a hotel room — usually a full apartment — and are generally marketed as costing substantially less per night than comparable extended hotel stays, though more than an ordinary unfurnished lease.

Industry

Providers range from large relocation and serviced-apartment companies to local firms that lease apartments from landlords and re-let them furnished. The industry is represented in the U.S. by the Corporate Housing Providers Association (CHPA). Corporate housing differs from serviced apartments in some markets mainly in branding and typical minimum-stay conventions; both are forms of temporary housing regulated variously by local tenancy and short-term rental laws.

Uses

Common uses include employee relocation, training programs, intern housing, medical treatment away from home, insurance placements after fires or floods, film production, and government or military assignments.
