Memorandum of association

A memorandum of association is a legal document prepared in the formation and registration process of a company to define its relationship with shareholders and to specify the company's objectives and powers. It is one of the constitutional documents of a company in many Commonwealth jurisdictions.

Contents

Traditionally, the memorandum contains the company's name, the location of its registered office, its objects (purposes), a statement of limited liability, and details of its authorised share capital.

History

Under the United Kingdom's Companies Act 2006, the memorandum was reduced to a simple statement that the subscribers wish to form a company and agree to become members, with the substantive rules moved to the articles of association.
