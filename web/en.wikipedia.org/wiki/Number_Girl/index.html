Number Girl (Japanese: ナンバーガール, stylised number girl) was the debut Japanese mini-album concept associated with singer Aiko Kayo, released in Japan in the early 2000s. The name is shared with, but unrelated to, the Japanese rock band Number Girl (stylised NUMBER GIRL), a four-piece from Fukuoka active from 1995 to 2002 and again from 2019, led by guitarist and vocalist Shutoku Mukai.

The band Number Girl

Number Girl formed in Fukuoka in 1995 and became one of the most influential Japanese alternative rock bands of their generation, known for frantic, tightly wound songs drawing on American indie rock such as Pixies and Sonic Youth. Their albums include School Girl Bye Bye (1997), School Girl Distortional Addict (1999), Sappukei (2000) and Num-Heavymetallic (2002). The band broke up in 2002; Mukai went on to form Zazen Boys. Number Girl reunited in 2019, playing festivals including Rising Sun Rock Festival, but disbanded again after a final tour in 2022.

Legacy

Number Girl's jagged guitar style and Mukai's distinctive vocals influenced a generation of Japanese rock bands. Their catalogue has been reissued on vinyl, and the reunion concerts of 2019–2022 drew large audiences in Japan.
