Skagafjörður

Skagafjörður is a fjord and a municipality in northern Iceland, situated between the Tröllaskagi and Skagi peninsulas. The fjord is about 40 km long and is known for its broad agricultural valley, horse breeding, and the islands of Drangey, Málmey and Lundey.

The main town is Sauðárkrókur. The region features prominently in the Icelandic sagas, including Grettis saga, which is associated with Drangey island, where the outlaw Grettir Ásmundarson made his last stand. In 2022 the municipalities of Skagafjörður and Akrahreppur merged into a single municipality named Skagafjörður.

Geography

The Héraðsvötn river flows into the fjord, building up fertile flatlands used for farming. The area is noted for the Icelandic horse and for turf-house heritage sites such as Glaumbær.
