The 2003–04 FA Premier League (known as the FA Barclaycard Premiership for sponsorship reasons) was the twelfth season of the Premier League. It is remembered above all for Arsenal winning the title without losing a single league match — a feat not achieved in the English top flight since Preston North End in 1888–89 — earning Arsène Wenger's team the nickname "The Invincibles".

Season summary

Arsenal finished with 90 points, winning 26 matches and drawing 12, finishing eleven points clear of Chelsea, who in their first season under Roman Abramovich's ownership spent over £100 million on players. Manchester United, the defending champions, finished third. Liverpool took the fourth Champions League place.

The Invincibles

Arsenal's unbeaten run, powered by Thierry Henry's 30 league goals and the midfield of Patrick Vieira, Robert Pires, and Freddie Ljungberg, extended into the following season and reached 49 matches before defeat at Old Trafford in October 2004. The Premier League awarded Arsenal a unique golden version of the trophy to mark the achievement.

European qualification

Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, and Liverpool qualified for the UEFA Champions League; Newcastle United, Aston Villa, and FA Cup runners-up Millwall qualified for the UEFA Cup.

Relegation

Leeds United, champions of England as recently as 1992 and Champions League semi-finalists in 2001, were relegated amid a severe financial crisis, as were Leicester City and Wolverhampton Wanderers — all three finishing on 33 points, the tightest relegation battle of the Premier League era to that date.
