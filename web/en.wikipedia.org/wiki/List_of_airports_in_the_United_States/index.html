List of airports in the United States

The United States has more airports than any other country, with over 19,000 landing facilities, of which roughly 5,000 are public-use and about 500 have scheduled commercial airline service. The Federal Aviation Administration classifies primary commercial service airports by passenger enplanements into large, medium, small and non-hub categories. This article lists the principal airports by state and links to the full state-by-state lists.
