Grubhub

Grubhub Inc. is an American online food ordering and delivery platform. Founded in 2004 in Chicago by Mike Evans and Matt Maloney, it connects diners with local restaurants for pickup and delivery through its website and mobile apps.

Grubhub went public on the New York Stock Exchange in 2014. In 2020 it was acquired by the Dutch company Just Eat Takeaway.com in an all-stock deal valued at about $7.3 billion; Just Eat Takeaway later sold Grubhub to Wonder Group in 2024. Over the years Grubhub absorbed or merged with several competitors and brands, including Seamless, AllMenus, Campusfood and Eat24.

The company earns revenue mainly from commissions charged to restaurants and delivery fees. Like other gig-economy platforms, it has faced debate over its treatment of couriers, its commission rates, and practices such as listing restaurants without their consent.
