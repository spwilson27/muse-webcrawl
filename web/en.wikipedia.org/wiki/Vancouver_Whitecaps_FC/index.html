Vancouver Whitecaps FC is a Canadian professional soccer club based in Vancouver, British Columbia, that competes in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the league's Western Conference. The club plays its home matches at BC Place. The current franchise, established in 2009 and admitted to MLS in 2011, is the latest in a succession of teams carrying the Whitecaps name, which dates back to the original North American Soccer League club founded in 1973 that won the Soccer Bowl in 1979.

History

The original Whitecaps were a popular draw in the NASL, winning Soccer Bowl '79 against the Tampa Bay Rowdies. After the NASL folded, successor clubs played in the Canadian Soccer League, the A-League and USL First Division, winning USL championships in 2006 and 2008. On 18 March 2009, MLS commissioner Don Garber announced Vancouver as the league's seventeenth franchise, and the Whitecaps began MLS play in 2011 at Empire Field before moving into the renovated BC Place later that year.

The club's first MLS seasons were lean, though it reached the playoffs in 2012 — the first Canadian club to do so — and in 2014. Under coach Carl Robinson, Vancouver won its first Canadian Championship in 2015, defeating the Montreal Impact in the final. The Whitecaps won three straight Canadian Championships from 2022 to 2024 under head coach Vanni Sartini, and produced notable players including Camilo Sanvezzo, the 2013 MLS Golden Boot winner, Alphonso Davies, whose transfer to Bayern Munich in 2018 was then an MLS record, and Ryan Gauld, who joined as a Designated Player in 2021.

Rivalries and supporters

The Whitecaps contest the Cascadia Cup with the Seattle Sounders and Portland Timbers, a rivalry inherited from the NASL era, and compete with Toronto FC and CF Montréal for the Canadian Championship and the title of best Canadian club in MLS. The club's supporters' groups include the Southsiders, founded in 1999, and Curva Collective.

Stadium

BC Place, a retractable-roof stadium in downtown Vancouver originally built for Expo 86 and renovated for the 2010 Winter Olympics, seats about 22,000 in its soccer configuration. The club has long discussed building a soccer-specific stadium, but no project has been finalized.

Honours

The Whitecaps have won the Canadian Championship four times (2015, 2022, 2023, 2024) and have qualified for the CONCACAF Champions Cup on several occasions, reaching the semi-finals in 2017.
