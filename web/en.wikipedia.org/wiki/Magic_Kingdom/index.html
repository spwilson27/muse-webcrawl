Magic Kingdom Park, originally called the Magic Kingdom, is a theme park at the Walt Disney World Resort in Bay Lake, Florida, near Orlando. It is owned and operated by the Walt Disney Company through its Parks, Experiences and Products division. The park opened on 1 October 1971 as the first of four theme parks at the resort, and is dedicated to fairy tales and Disney characters.

Design

The park is laid out like Disneyland in California, with lands radiating from Cinderella Castle at the end of Main Street, U.S.A.: Adventureland, Frontierland, Liberty Square, Fantasyland and Tomorrowland. A network of service tunnels, the "utilidors," runs beneath the park, allowing costumed staff and supplies to move unseen. Magic Kingdom is frequently the most visited theme park in the world, drawing around 17 million guests in a typical year.

Attractions

Classic attractions include Space Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, the Haunted Mansion, "it's a small world", Jungle Cruise, Big Thunder Mountain Railroad and Splash Mountain (rethemed as Tiana's Bayou Adventure). The park's railroad circles the grounds, and nightly fireworks are presented over Cinderella Castle.

History

Walt Disney oversaw early planning but died in 1966, before construction began; his brother Roy O. Disney postponed his retirement to supervise building and named the resort Walt Disney World in his honour.
