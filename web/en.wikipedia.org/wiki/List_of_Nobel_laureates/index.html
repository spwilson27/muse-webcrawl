List of Nobel laureates

The Nobel Prizes are awarded annually by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, the Swedish Academy, the Karolinska Institutet, and the Norwegian Nobel Committee to individuals and organizations that make outstanding contributions in the fields of Chemistry, Physics, Literature, Peace, and Physiology or Medicine. A prize in Economic Sciences was added in 1968. Between 1901 and 2024, the prizes have been awarded to more than 1,000 laureates.

Laureates by category

Dedicated lists exist for each prize:

  • Physics — first awarded to Wilhelm Röntgen (1901); other laureates include Marie Curie, Albert Einstein, Niels Bohr, and Richard Feynman.
  • Chemistry — first awarded to Jacobus Henricus van 't Hoff (1901); Marie Curie became the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences.
  • Physiology or Medicine — first awarded to Emil von Behring (1901); includes Alexander Fleming, and Francis Crick and James Watson.
  • Literature — first awarded to Sully Prudhomme (1901); includes Rabindranath Tagore, the first non-European laureate, and Bob Dylan (2016).
  • Peace — first awarded jointly to Henry Dunant and Frédéric Passy (1901); the International Committee of the Red Cross has won three times. Laureates include Martin Luther King Jr., Mother Teresa, Nelson Mandela, and Malala Yousafzai, the youngest laureate (17).
  • Economic Sciences — first awarded in 1969 to Ragnar Frisch and Jan Tinbergen.

Multiple laureates

Five laureates have received more than one Nobel Prize: Marie Curie (Physics 1903, Chemistry 1911), Linus Pauling (Chemistry 1954, Peace 1962), John Bardeen (Physics 1956 and 1972), Frederick Sanger (Chemistry 1958 and 1980), and K. Barry Sharpless (Chemistry 2001 and 2022). The International Committee of the Red Cross has received the Peace Prize three times (1917, 1944, 1963), and the UNHCR twice (1954, 1981).

Statistics

As of the mid-2020s, the prizes have gone to laureates from dozens of countries, with the United States, United Kingdom, and Germany accounting for the largest totals. Women have received the prizes on more than 60 occasions.
