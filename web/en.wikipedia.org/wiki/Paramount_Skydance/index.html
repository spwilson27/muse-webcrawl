Paramount Skydance

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Paramount Skydance Corporation is an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate formed on August 7, 2025, through the merger of Paramount Global and Skydance Media. Headquartered in Los Angeles, the company owns the Paramount Pictures film studio, the CBS broadcast network, cable channels including MTV, Nickelodeon, Comedy Central and BET, and the streaming services Paramount+ and Pluto TV.

Formation

Paramount Global, controlled by Shari Redstone through National Amusements, had been formed by the 2019 re-merger of CBS Corporation and Viacom and renamed in 2022. After extended negotiations, Skydance Media — founded by David Ellison in 2006 and a frequent co-financier of Paramount films such as Top Gun: Maverick — agreed in July 2024 to acquire National Amusements and merge with Paramount Global in a transaction valuing the combined company at about $28 billion. Following regulatory approval by the FCC in July 2025, the merger closed on August 7, 2025, with David Ellison as chairman and chief executive officer.

Assets and divisions

The company's operations are organized around filmed entertainment (Paramount Pictures and Skydance's film, animation, and television labels), TV media (CBS and the cable networks), and direct-to-consumer streaming (Paramount+, Pluto TV, BET+). Its film library includes franchises such as Star Trek, Mission: Impossible, Transformers, Top Gun, SpongeBob SquarePants, and Sonic the Hedgehog.

Aftermath

After the merger, Paramount Skydance announced cost-cutting and restructuring, including significant layoffs across its divisions in late 2025, and pursued new licensing and distribution agreements, including a deal making Paramount+ content available through other platforms. In late 2025 the company also made a hostile takeover bid for Warner Bros. Discovery, challenging an agreed deal for Netflix to acquire that company's studio and streaming assets.
