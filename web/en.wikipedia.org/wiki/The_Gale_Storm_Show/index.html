The Gale Storm Show is an American sitcom starring Gale Storm that aired from 1956 to 1960. Also known as Oh! Susanna in syndication, the series ran for four seasons and 125 episodes, first on CBS and later on ABC.

Premise

The series stars Gale Storm as Susanna Pomeroy, an effervescent cruise ship social director aboard the luxury liner S.S. Ocean Queen. Susanna's mischievous schemes and romantic entanglements constantly exasperate the ship's stern Captain Miller. ZaSu Pitts co-starred as Elsie, the ship's manicurist and Susanna's partner in misadventure.

Production and broadcast

The show premiered on CBS on September 29, 1956, where it ran for three seasons before moving to ABC for its final season in 1959–1960. The setting aboard an ocean liner allowed for a rotating cast of guest stars playing passengers, a device that kept the episodic plots varied.

Legacy

The series was one of several vehicles that made Gale Storm one of the most familiar faces of 1950s American television, following her earlier success in My Little Margie. Reruns circulated for years in syndication under the title Oh! Susanna.
