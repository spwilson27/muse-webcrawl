Prüfer sequence


A labelled tree and its Prüfer sequence

In combinatorial mathematics, a Prüfer sequence (also called a Prüfer code) is a unique sequence associated with a labelled tree. A tree on n vertices labelled 1 to n corresponds bijectively to a sequence of length n − 2, giving a proof of Cayley's formula that there are nn−2 labelled trees on n vertices.

Algorithm

The sequence is generated by repeatedly removing the leaf with the smallest label and recording the label of its only neighbour, until two vertices remain. The tree can be reconstructed uniquely from the sequence: each vertex's degree equals one plus the number of times it appears in the sequence.

History

Prüfer sequences were introduced by Heinz Prüfer in 1918 as part of a new proof of Cayley's formula.
