List of Lehendakaris

The Lehendakari is the president of the Basque Government, the head of government of the Basque Autonomous Community in Spain. The title (in Basque, lehendakari means "president") was first used by the Basque government formed in 1936 during the Spanish Civil War, which continued in exile until the restoration of autonomy under the 1979 Statute of Gernika.
