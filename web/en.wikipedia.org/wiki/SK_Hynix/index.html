SK hynix Inc. (Korean: 에스케이하이닉스) is a South Korean semiconductor manufacturer headquartered in Icheon. It is the world's second-largest manufacturer of memory chips, after Samsung Electronics, supplying dynamic random-access memory (DRAM) and NAND flash memory chips to major technology companies worldwide.

History

The company was founded in 1983 as Hyundai Electronics, part of the Hyundai conglomerate. In 2001 it was spun off and renamed Hynix Semiconductor. After financial difficulties, creditors took control until 2012, when the SK Group acquired a controlling stake and the company became SK hynix. It subsequently acquired Intel's NAND and solid-state drive business (rebranded as Solidigm) in a deal announced in 2020.

SK hynix became the leading supplier of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) used in artificial-intelligence accelerators, notably supplying HBM3 and HBM3E stacks to Nvidia, which drove record revenues during the AI investment boom of the mid-2020s.

Products

Its product lines include DRAM for servers, PCs and mobile devices, NAND flash, HBM, CMOS image sensors and foundry services, with fabrication plants in South Korea and China.
