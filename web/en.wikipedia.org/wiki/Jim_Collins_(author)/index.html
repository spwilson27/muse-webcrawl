Jim Collins (author)

James C. Collins
Born: 25 January 1958, Aurora, Colorado, US
Education: Stanford University
Known for: Built to Last, Good to Great

James C. "Jim" Collins (born 25 January 1958) is an American business researcher, author and consultant known for empirical studies of why some companies succeed over long periods while others fail. His books, based on large research teams' analysis of matched comparison companies, have sold millions of copies.

Works

With Jerry I. Porras he wrote Built to Last: Successful Habits of Visionary Companies (1994), which identified practices of enduring companies, including core ideology and "BHAGs" (big hairy audacious goals). Good to Great (2001) studied companies that made a sustained leap in stock performance and introduced concepts such as Level 5 Leadership, the Hedgehog Concept, a culture of discipline, and "first who, then what". Later works include How the Mighty Fall (2009), Great by Choice (2011, with Morten T. Hansen) and BE 2.0 (2020).

Career

Collins taught at Stanford Graduate School of Business, where he received a distinguished teaching award, before founding a management research laboratory in Boulder, Colorado. He is also an avid rock climber, a pursuit he has written about as a parallel to disciplined endeavour.
