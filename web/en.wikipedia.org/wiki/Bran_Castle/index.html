Bran Castle (Romanian: Castelul Bran) is a castle in Bran, 25 kilometres southwest of Brașov, Romania. It is a national monument and landmark, situated on the Transylvanian side of the historical border with Wallachia. The castle is commonly marketed abroad as "Dracula's Castle", although Bram Stoker's fictional Count Dracula bears little resemblance to the historical Vlad the Impaler, and Stoker never visited Romania.

History

The stone castle was built by the Saxons of Brașov between 1377 and 1388 on the site of an earlier Teutonic Order stronghold. It guarded the mountain pass between Transylvania and Wallachia and served as a customs post. In 1920 it was given to Queen Marie of Romania, who restored it as a summer residence. It is now a museum.

Tourism

Bran Castle is one of Romania's most visited attractions, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors annually, many drawn by the Dracula association.
