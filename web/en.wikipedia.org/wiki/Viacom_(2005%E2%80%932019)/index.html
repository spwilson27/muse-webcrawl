Viacom Inc. was an American multinational mass media conglomerate that existed from 2005 to 2019. It was created on 31 December 2005 when the original Viacom split into two companies: CBS Corporation, which took the broadcast television, radio and publishing assets, and the new Viacom, which retained the cable networks and the Paramount Pictures film studio.

Holdings

The company's principal businesses were its Media Networks division — including MTV, Nickelodeon, Comedy Central, VH1, CMT, TV Land and, from 2006, BET Networks — and its Filmed Entertainment division centred on Paramount Pictures. Viacom also operated channels and ventures internationally in Europe, Latin America and Asia. Throughout its existence it was controlled by Sumner Redstone through National Amusements' super-voting shares.

Reunification

After several years of on-and-off negotiations, and leadership changes that saw Philippe Dauman replaced as chief executive by Bob Bakish in 2016, CBS and Viacom agreed in August 2019 to recombine. The merger closed on 4 December 2019, forming ViacomCBS (renamed Paramount Global in 2022), ending the second Viacom's fourteen years as a separate company.
