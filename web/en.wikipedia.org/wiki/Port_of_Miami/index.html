Port of Miami

The Port of Miami (stylised PortMiami) is a seaport located in Biscayne Bay in Miami, Florida, United States. It is often called the "Cruise Capital of the World" and handles more cruise passengers than any other port.

History

The port sits largely on Dodge Island, created by dredging in the early 20th century. In 1960 the port and the older facilities on the mainland were consolidated. A tunnel, opened in 2014, links the port directly to the interstate highway system.

Operations

PortMiami is a major cargo gateway for trade with Latin America and the Caribbean and a leading cruise homeport for lines such as Royal Caribbean, Carnival and Norwegian. It is a significant contributor to the Miami-Dade County economy.
