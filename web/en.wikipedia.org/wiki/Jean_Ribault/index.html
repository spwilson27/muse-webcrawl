Jean Ribault

Jean Ribault (1520 – 12 October 1565) was a French naval officer and explorer who led the first French expedition to the southeastern coast of North America and helped found French Florida.

Florida expedition

In 1562 Ribault sailed for the French crown, explored the coast of present-day Florida, Georgia, and South Carolina, and established the short-lived colony of Charlesfort on Parris Island. He returned to France for supplies but was detained by the Wars of Religion.

Death

In 1565 he returned to Florida with reinforcements for Fort Caroline, but was shipwrecked by a hurricane and captured by Spanish forces under Pedro Menéndez de Avilés, who executed Ribault and most of his men at Matanzas Inlet. See also French Florida.
