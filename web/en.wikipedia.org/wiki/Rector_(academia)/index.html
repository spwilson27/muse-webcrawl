Rector (academia)

A rector is the head of certain universities and other academic institutions. In much of Europe and Latin America, the rector is the chief executive and academic leader of a university, roughly equivalent to a president or vice-chancellor in the English-speaking world. Rectors are typically elected by the academic community or appointed for a fixed term.

In Scotland, the rector of an ancient university is instead an elected representative of the students who chairs the university court, a role dating back centuries. The term is also used for the heads of some schools, seminaries and colleges.
