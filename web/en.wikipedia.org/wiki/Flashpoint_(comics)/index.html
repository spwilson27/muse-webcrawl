Flashpoint is a 2011 comic book crossover story arc published by DC Comics, consisting of an eponymous core miniseries written by Geoff Johns and illustrated by Andy Kubert, and a large number of tie-in series and one-shots. The story stars Barry Allen, the Flash, who awakens in an altered present in which his mother was never murdered.

Plot

In the altered timeline, many heroes are changed: Aquaman and Wonder Woman lead Atlantis and Themyscira in a devastating war that has ravaged western Europe; Superman never landed in Kansas and was raised in government captivity; and Bruce Wayne was killed as a child, leaving his father Thomas Wayne to become a brutal Batman. Barry Allen, gradually recovering his memories and powers, realises that the Reverse-Flash, Eobard Thawne, claims Barry himself created the divergent timeline by travelling back in time to save his mother.

Aftermath

Barry Allen's attempt to restore the timeline instead merged DC's universes, resulting in the line-wide reboot known as The New 52, launched in September 2011 with new issue #1s across DC's titles. A letter from Thomas Wayne to Bruce Wayne, carried back by Barry, became a famous emotional coda to the story. The arc was adapted into the animated film Justice League: The Flashpoint Paradox (2013) and loosely inspired the film The Flash (2023).
