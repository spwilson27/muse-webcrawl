A Different World is an American sitcom television series that aired on NBC from September 24, 1987, to July 9, 1993. The series originally centered on Denise Huxtable (Lisa Bonet) and the life of students at Hillman College, a fictional historically black college in Virginia. It was a spin-off of The Cosby Show.

Overview

After Bonet's departure at the end of the first season, the series was retooled around Whitley Gilbert (Jasmine Guy) and Dwayne Wayne (Kadeem Hardison), whose on-and-off romance became the show's centrepiece. The series frequently addressed social issues such as racism, class, date rape, and the AIDS crisis, and is credited with increasing enrolment at real historically black colleges and universities during its run.

Legacy

The show was one of the most-watched series on American television among African-American audiences, at times ranking among the top five programmes overall in the Nielsen ratings.
