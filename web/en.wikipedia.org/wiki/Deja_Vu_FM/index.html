Deja Vu FM

Deja Vu FM (also styled Deja 92.3) is a British pirate radio station based in London, broadcasting on 92.3 FM. Founded in 1994, it became one of the most influential stations in the development of UK garage and, later, grime music.

History

Deja Vu began broadcasting in the mid-1990s from rooftops in east London, playing jungle and UK garage. Around the turn of the millennium the station became a crucial platform for the emerging grime sound: many of the genre's foundational MCs and producers — including members of Pay As U Go Cartel and later Roll Deep, and a young Dizzee Rascal — performed on its sets. Live "clash" sessions and roof-top freestyles recorded from the station circulated widely and helped define early grime culture.

Legacy

Although pirate stations operated outside the licensing system, Deja Vu is regularly cited by journalists and historians of British music as one of the key incubators of grime, alongside stations such as Rinse FM and Heat FM. The station has continued broadcasting and streaming into the streaming era, retaining its 92.3 frequency identity.
