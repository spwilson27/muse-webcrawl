Developmental milestones
Field: Developmental psychology, pediatrics
Domains: Motor, language, social, cognitive
Related: Child development

Developmental milestones are physical or behavioral signs of development or maturation of an infant or child, such as rolling over, first steps, first words and social smiling. Reaching them within typical age ranges is used to screen development.

Domains

Milestones are grouped into gross motor, fine motor, language and communication, cognitive, and social-emotional domains. Examples: sitting without support around 6 months, walking independently around 12 months, and combining words around age two — with wide normal variation.

Screening

Pediatric guidelines, such as the CDC checklists revised in 2022, help parents and clinicians identify possible developmental delays early, when intervention is most effective. A milestone checklist is a screening aid, not a diagnosis.

Variation

Timing varies with genetics, health, prematurity, environment and culture; patterns such as loss of skills at any age warrant prompt evaluation.
