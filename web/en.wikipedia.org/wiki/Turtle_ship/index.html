Turtle ship

A turtle ship (Korean: 거북선, geobukseon) was a type of Korean warship used from the early 15th century, most famously by Admiral Yi Sun-sin during the Imjin War (1592–1598) against Japanese invasions.

Design

The ship had a fully covered, curved deck — likened to a turtle shell — studded with spikes to repel boarders, cannon firing through ports on all sides, and a dragon head at the bow that could emit smoke and fire cannon. Propulsion was by oars and sails.

Legacy

Turtle ships helped Yi win battles such as Hansan Island despite being outnumbered; exact construction details remain debated by historians. See Yi Sun-sin.
