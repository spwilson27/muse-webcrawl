My Hero Academia is a Japanese superhero manga series written and illustrated by Kōhei Horikoshi. It was serialised in Weekly Shōnen Jump from July 2014 to August 2024, with its chapters collected in 42 volumes.

Plot

The story is set in a world where most people possess superpowers called "Quirks." Izuku Midoriya, born without a Quirk, is chosen by the world's greatest hero, All Might, to inherit his power and enrols at U.A. High School to train as a professional hero.

Adaptations

The manga was adapted into an anime television series by studio Bones beginning in 2016, along with several animated films. The franchise became one of the best-selling manga series of all time, with over 100 million copies in circulation.
