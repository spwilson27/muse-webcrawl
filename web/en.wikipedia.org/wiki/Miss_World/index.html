Miss World is the oldest international beauty pageant, created in the United Kingdom by Eric Morley in 1951. It is one of the "Big Four" international pageants, alongside Miss Universe, Miss International and Miss Earth. The winner is crowned by her predecessor at an annual final watched by a global television audience.

History

The contest began as a one-off bikini pageant held during the Festival of Britain in 1951, won by Kiki Håkansson of Sweden. Renamed Miss World, it became an annual event; from the 1980s, under chair Julia Morley, it adopted the motto "Beauty with a Purpose", emphasising charitable work by contestants, who raise funds for children's causes in their countries.

Format

Contestants are national titleholders selected in licensed national pageants. The competition includes fast-track events such as Top Model, Talent, Sports, Multimedia and the Beauty with a Purpose project presentations, culminating in a final show at which finalists answer questions before the winner is announced.

Titleholders

Notable winners include Aishwarya Rai (1994) and Priyanka Chopra (2000) of India, and Rolene Strauss of South Africa (2014). India and Venezuela are among the most successful countries in the pageant's history.
