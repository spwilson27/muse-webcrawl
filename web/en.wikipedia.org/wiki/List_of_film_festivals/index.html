List of film festivals

Film festivals are organised screenings of films, usually held annually in particular cities, combining premieres, competition awards, markets and industry events. Thousands exist worldwide. The most prestigious competitive festivals accredited by FIAPF are sometimes called the "Big Five": Venice (the oldest, founded 1932), Cannes, Berlin, Toronto and Sundance.

Other notable festivals

  • Genre: Sitges (fantasy/horror), Fantasia (Montreal), Fantastic Fest (Austin), Annecy (animation), IDFA Amsterdam (documentary), Hot Docs (Toronto, documentary).
  • Regional/national: BFI London Film Festival, New York Film Festival, Telluride, SXSW (Austin), Tribeca, Hong Kong International Film Festival, Melbourne International Film Festival, Sydney Film Festival, Edinburgh International Film Festival, Sarajevo Film Festival, Thessaloniki, Gothenburg.
  • Shorts and student: Clermont-Ferrand International Short Film Festival, Oberhausen.
  • LGBT and specialist: Frameline (San Francisco), Outfest, BFI Flare.
