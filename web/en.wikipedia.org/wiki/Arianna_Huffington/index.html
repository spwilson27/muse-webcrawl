Arianna Stassinopoulos Huffington (born 15 July 1950) is a Greek-American author, syndicated columnist, and businesswoman. She is a co-founder of The Huffington Post (now HuffPost) and the founder and CEO of Thrive Global, a behaviour-change company focused on well-being.

Early life and career

Born in Athens, Huffington studied economics at Girton College, Cambridge, where she became president of the Cambridge Union. She wrote several books, including a biography of Maria Callas and The Female Woman (1973), and became a prominent commentator in Britain before moving to the United States in 1980.

Huffington Post and later work

In 2005 she co-launched the news and blog site The Huffington Post, which won a Pulitzer Prize in 2012. After leaving in 2016 she founded Thrive Global and wrote books on sleep and well-being, including The Sleep Revolution.
