Royal Netherlands Navy

An air-defence frigate of the Royal Netherlands Navy

The Royal Netherlands Navy (Dutch: Koninklijke Marine) is the naval force of the Kingdom of the Netherlands and one of the oldest navies in the world, with origins in the late 15th century. Its tasks include defending Dutch maritime interests, protecting sea lines of communication and supporting international operations.

History

The Dutch navy rose to prominence during the Dutch Golden Age, when admirals such as Michiel de Ruyter won notable victories in the Anglo-Dutch Wars, including the Raid on the Medway in 1667. In the Second World War the navy fought in the North Sea, the Mediterranean and the Pacific; its submarines and the cruiser force in the Netherlands East Indies were largely destroyed in the Java Sea campaign of 1942. After the war the navy rebuilt as a NATO anti-submarine force centred on Den Helder.

Fleet

The modern fleet includes De Zeven Provinciën-class air defence and command frigates, Karel Doorman-class multipurpose frigates, Holland-class offshore patrol vessels, Walrus-class submarines, amphibious transport docks and the Netherlands Marine Corps.

Bases

The main naval base is Den Helder, with additional facilities at Vlissingen, Rotterdam and in the Dutch Caribbean.
