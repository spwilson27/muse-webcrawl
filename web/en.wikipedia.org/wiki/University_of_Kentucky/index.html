The University of Kentucky (UK) is a public land-grant research university in Lexington, Kentucky. Founded in 1865 by John Bryan Bowman as the Agricultural and Mechanical College of Kentucky, it is the state's largest university, with around 30,000 students and 16 colleges.

Academics and health care

UK operates a major academic medical centre, UK HealthCare, and is known for programs in agriculture, pharmacy, medicine, and equine research reflecting Kentucky's horse industry. The William T. Young Library is the largest university library collection in the state.

Athletics

The Kentucky Wildcats men's basketball program is among the most successful in NCAA history, with multiple national championships, and plays at Rupp Arena. The university competes in the Southeastern Conference.
