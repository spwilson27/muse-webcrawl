Drug policy of Malaysia

The drug policy of Malaysia is among the strictest in the world. Under the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, trafficking in specified quantities of drugs such as heroin, morphine, cannabis and methamphetamine carried a mandatory death penalty until reforms in 2023, when Malaysia abolished the mandatory death penalty and gave judges discretion to impose life imprisonment instead. Possession of smaller amounts carries heavy fines, imprisonment and caning. Malaysia maintains a dual approach of supply reduction through enforcement and demand reduction through compulsory and voluntary treatment programmes administered by the National Anti-Drugs Agency (AADK).
