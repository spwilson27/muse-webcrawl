Plastic

[Image: A pile of colourful plastic bottles and containers awaiting recycling]

Plastics are synthetic or semi-synthetic materials made of polymers, mouldable into almost any shape, light, cheap and durable. From Bakelite (1907) through nylon, PVC, polyethylene and polypropylene, plastics remade manufacturing, medicine, packaging and electronics in the 20th century.

Problems

Derived mainly from oil and gas, most plastic is used briefly and persists for centuries; only a small fraction is recycled, and microplastics are found from ocean depths to human blood. Bans on single-use items, better recycling and bioplastics are partial responses within sustainability policy.
