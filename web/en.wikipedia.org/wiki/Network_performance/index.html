Network performance

Network performance refers to measures of the quality of service of a computer network, as experienced by its users. It is characterised by parameters such as bandwidth (throughput), latency, jitter, bit error rate and packet loss.

Key measures

Bandwidth is the maximum rate of data transfer across a path, usually measured in bits per second. Latency is the time taken for data to travel from source to destination. Jitter is the variation in latency over time, which particularly affects real-time applications such as voice and video. Packet loss occurs when data packets fail to reach their destination, often due to congestion.

Management

Network performance management involves monitoring and optimising these measures using quality of service (QoS) mechanisms, traffic shaping, capacity planning and diagnostic tools. Different applications have different requirements: streaming tolerates some delay but needs sustained throughput, while online gaming is highly sensitive to latency and jitter.
