Euronews is a European television news network headquartered in Lyon, France, launched on 1 January 1993 by a consortium of European public broadcasters. Its founding idea — the same news pictures with voiceovers in many languages, and no on-screen presenters — made it a symbol of pan-European media.

Development

Euronews added languages and digital services over three decades, pioneered the 'No Comment' raw-video format, and passed through several ownership structures, including stakes held by NBCUniversal (2017–2020) and later investment funds, with editorial HQ remaining in Lyon.
