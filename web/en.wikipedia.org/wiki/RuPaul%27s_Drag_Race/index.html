RuPaul's Drag Race is an American reality competition television series produced by World of Wonder, in which drag queens compete in challenges — including design, comedy, and performance — for the title of America's Next Drag Superstar. It is hosted and produced by RuPaul, who also serves as head judge.

Format

Each episode typically features a mini-challenge and a main challenge; the two lowest-performing contestants face elimination in a lip-sync performance, after which one is told to "sashay away". Seasons culminate in a finale crowning a winner, who receives a cash prize.

Impact

Premiering on Logo TV in 2009, the show grew into a global franchise with spin-offs such as All Stars and international editions including Drag Race UK. It has won numerous Emmy Awards and is widely credited with bringing drag culture into the mainstream.
