King of Prussia, Pennsylvania

Township: Upper Merion
County: Montgomery
State: Pennsylvania

King of Prussia is a census-designated place in Upper Merion Township, Montgomery County, Pennsylvania, United States. It is a major suburb of Philadelphia, located about 25 miles northwest of the city, and is known as a large retail and business centre.

Name and history

The community takes its name from the King of Prussia Inn, an 18th-century tavern named in honour of King Frederick the Great of Prussia. During the American Revolutionary War, George Washington's Continental Army camped at nearby Valley Forge in the winter of 1777–78. For much of its history the area was rural, but post-war highway construction — including the Pennsylvania Turnpike and the Schuylkill Expressway — turned it into a commercial hub.

Economy

King of Prussia is home to the King of Prussia Mall, one of the largest shopping malls in the United States, as well as numerous corporate offices, business parks, and the Valley Forge National Historical Park nearby. It is often described as one of the largest "edge cities" in the Philadelphia metropolitan area.
