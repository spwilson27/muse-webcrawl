List of countries by sex ratio

The sex ratio is the ratio of males to females in a population, expressed here as the number of males per 100 females. Globally the ratio is about 101–102 males per 100 females. It is above 105 at birth, falls with age because of higher male mortality, and in some countries is distorted by labour migration or sex-selective practices.

Interpretation

Sex ratios vary by age group: the ratio at birth is typically about 105 boys per 100 girls; by old age women usually outnumber men substantially. Extremes in the Gulf states reflect temporary labour migration rather than birth ratios, while very low ratios in Eastern Europe reflect excess male mortality from cardiovascular disease, alcohol and accidents.
