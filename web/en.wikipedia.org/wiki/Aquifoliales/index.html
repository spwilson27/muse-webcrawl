Aquifoliales

The Aquifoliales are an order of flowering plants (angiosperms) in the asterid group of eudicots. The order is best known for the family Aquifoliaceae, which contains the hollies (genus Ilex).

Rank: Order
Group: Asterids (eudicots)
Families: Aquifoliaceae, Cardiopteridaceae, Helwingiaceae, Phyllonomaceae, Stemonuraceae

Classification

Under the APG IV system, Aquifoliales contains five families. Aquifoliaceae includes the single large genus Ilex, with several hundred species of trees and shrubs, many grown ornamentally for their glossy leaves and red berries. Cardiopteridaceae and Stemonuraceae are mainly tropical woody plants, while Helwingiaceae and Phyllonomaceae are small families from Asia and the Americas respectively.

Characteristics

Members of the order are mostly woody plants with simple leaves and small, often unisexual flowers. Many are dioecious, with male and female flowers on separate plants.
