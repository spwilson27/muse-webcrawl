The Plains Indians are the Indigenous peoples who traditionally lived on the Great Plains of North America, from the Canadian prairies to Texas and from the Rocky Mountains to the Missouri River valley. Their cultures — strongly shaped by the American bison and, after the 17th century, by the horse — are among the most widely recognised images of Native American life.

Peoples and languages

Plains peoples speak languages of several families, including Siouan (Lakota, Dakota, Crow, Mandan), Algonquian (Cheyenne, Arapaho, Blackfoot, Plains Cree), Caddoan (Pawnee, Arikara, Wichita), Uto-Aztecan (Comanche, Shoshone) and Athabaskan (Plains Apache, Sarcee). Some groups, such as the Mandan, Hidatsa and Pawnee, were semi-sedentary farmers living in earth-lodge villages along the rivers, combining maize agriculture with seasonal bison hunts; others, such as the Lakota, Cheyenne and Comanche, became fully nomadic equestrian hunters.

Culture

The bison supplied food, hides for tipis and clothing, bone tools and sinew; virtually every part was used. The reintroduction of the horse to North America by the Spanish transformed Plains life in the 1700s, expanding hunting range, wealth and warfare. Social life centred on bands and tribal councils; ceremonies included the Sun Dance; pictographic winter counts recorded each year. A developed intertribal sign language (Plains Indian Sign Language) enabled communication across language barriers, and trade networks exchanged horses, guns, corn and captives.

History

Through the 19th century, expanding United States settlement brought treaties, epidemics of smallpox and cholera, the extermination of the bison herds, and wars — including Red Cloud's War, the Great Sioux War of 1876 (with the Battle of the Little Bighorn), and the Ghost Dance movement, which ended in the Wounded Knee massacre of 1890. Survivors were confined to reservations. In the 20th and 21st centuries Plains nations have pursued land claims, cultural revival and political protest, such as the 2016 Dakota Access Pipeline protests at Standing Rock.
