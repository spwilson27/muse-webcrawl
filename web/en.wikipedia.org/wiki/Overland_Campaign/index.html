Overland Campaign

The Overland Campaign, also known as Grant's Overland Campaign, was a series of battles fought in Virginia during May and June 1864, in the American Civil War. Union Lt. Gen. Ulysses S. Grant led the Army of the Potomac against Confederate Gen. Robert E. Lee's Army of Northern Virginia.

Battles included the Wilderness, Spotsylvania Court House, North Anna, and Cold Harbor. Despite heavy Union casualties, Grant pressed south, crossing the James River and beginning the Siege of Petersburg.
