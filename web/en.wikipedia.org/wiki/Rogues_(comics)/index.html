The Rogues are a team of supervillains appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics, principally as enemies of the Flash. Unlike many rogue galleries, the Flash's villains banded together early and often, operating as a loose brotherhood of working-class criminals with a shared code — no killing of women or children, no unnecessary killing at all, and (traditionally) no drugs — enforced by their leader, Captain Cold.

Members

Core members across eras include Captain Cold (freeze gun), Mirror Master (mirrors, reflections, and the Mirror World), Heat Wave (pyromaniac with a heat gun), Weather Wizard (weather wand), The Trickster (lethal gags and pranks), Captain Boomerang (weaponised boomerangs), Pied Piper (sonic/hypnotic flute, later reformed), The Top (spinning and mind powers), and Golden Glider. Later additions and successors include Abra Kadabra, Dr. Alchemy, Tar Pit, Girder, Double Down, and the time-travelling Professor Zoom-adjacent rivalries the Rogues themselves resent.

Fictional history

The Rogues formed in the Silver Age as the loosely organised foes of Barry Allen and later united against Wally West. Writer Geoff Johns's early-2000s run redefined them as blue-collar criminals motivated by money and community rather than world domination, giving them a hideout, rules, and funeral customs for fallen members. During Final Crisis they broke their code's greatest taboo under duress in the killing of Bart Allen (as Inertia's accomplices), a turning point explored in Final Crisis: Rogues' Revenge. In the New 52 relaunch several Rogues gained actual superpowers and clashes with new leader dynamics under Captain Cold; in DC Rebirth the classic arrangement returned.

In other media

The Rogues appear prominently in the CW's The Flash television series — Captain Cold (Wentworth Miller), Heat Wave (Dominic Purcell), Golden Glider, Weather Wizard, Captain Boomerang, Pied Piper, and the Trickster (Mark Hamill) — as well as in animated series such as Justice League Unlimited and The Batman.
