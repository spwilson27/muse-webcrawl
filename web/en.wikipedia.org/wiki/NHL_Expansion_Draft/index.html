NHL Expansion Draft

An NHL expansion draft is a special draft conducted by the National Hockey League whenever it adds one or more expansion teams, allowing the new franchise(s) to select players from the existing clubs to build an initial roster.

Rules

Existing teams may protect a limited number of their players — typically either seven forwards, three defencemen and one goaltender, or eight skaters and one goaltender — and must expose players meeting minimum contract and games-played requirements. The expansion team must select one player from each existing club and meet salary-cap and positional requirements.

Notable drafts

The 1967 expansion draft stocked six new teams that doubled the league's size. More recent drafts stocked the Vegas Golden Knights in 2017 and the Seattle Kraken in 2021; the Golden Knights famously reached the Stanley Cup Final in their inaugural season.
