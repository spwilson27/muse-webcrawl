List of female Nobel laureates

The Nobel Prizes are awarded annually in Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature and Peace (and, since 1969, the Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences). Women have received a small minority of the prizes — roughly 65 prizes to women out of more than 950 individual laureates up to the mid-2020s. Marie Curie remains the only woman to win twice, and the only person ever to win Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields (Physics 1903, Chemistry 1911).
