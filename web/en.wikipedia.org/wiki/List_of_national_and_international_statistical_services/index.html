This is a list of national and international statistical services. Nearly every country has a principal statistical agency responsible for censuses, demographic data, and economic statistics such as inflation and gross domestic product. International bodies — including the United Nations Statistics Division, Eurostat, the International Monetary Fund, the World Bank and the OECD — coordinate standards and publish cross-country data.
