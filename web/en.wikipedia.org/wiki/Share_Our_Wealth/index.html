Share Our Wealth

Share Our Wealth
Founder: Huey Long
Founded: February 1934
Type: Political movement / programme

Share Our Wealth was a movement and proposed redistributive programme launched in February 1934 by Louisiana Senator Huey P. Long during the Great Depression. Its slogan was "Every Man a King".

Programme

Long proposed capping personal fortunes (eventually at around $5–8 million), incomes at $1 million a year, and inheritances, using steep taxes to fund a guaranteed household wealth floor, annual family income, old-age pensions, free college education, shorter working hours, and veterans' benefits. Thousands of Share Our Wealth clubs formed, claiming millions of members nationwide.

End

The movement collapsed after Long's assassination in September 1935, though it pressured Franklin D. Roosevelt toward the more redistributive policies of the Second New Deal.
