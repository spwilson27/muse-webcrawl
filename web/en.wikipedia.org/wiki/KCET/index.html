KCET

KCET (channel 28) is a public television station in Los Angeles, California, United States. It is the flagship station of the Public Media Group of Southern California, which merged KCET with the former PBS member station KOCE-TV in 2018. KCET was for decades one of the most prolific producing stations of the PBS system, though it operated as an independent educational station from 2011 until its return to PBS as a secondary member in 2019.

History

KCET signed on the air on September 28, 1964, as an affiliate of National Educational Television (NET), under license to the Community Television of Southern California organization. It became a founding member of PBS in 1970. The station established its studios on Sunset Boulevard in Los Feliz, on a lot that had served as a film studio since the 1910s. Over the following decades KCET produced nationally distributed programs including the science series Cosmos-era documentary content, the long-running news magazine Life & Times, and in association with the BBC and others, The Ascent of Man and drama co-productions.

In October 2010, citing financial disputes over dues and programming costs, KCET announced it would leave PBS and become independent on January 1, 2011. Ratings and donations fell sharply, and the station sold its studio lot. In 2018 KCETLink Media Group merged with the PBS SoCal organization (KOCE), forming the Public Media Group of Southern California; KCET rejoined PBS in 2019 as a secondary member station and adopted PBS programming alongside locally produced series such as Lost LA and Artbound.

Programming

Well-known KCET productions include Huell Howser's travel programs such as California's Gold and Visiting… with Huell Howser, which the station aired and distributed for many years, and the news program SoCal Connected. The station continues to produce arts, history, and environmental documentaries for regional and national distribution.
