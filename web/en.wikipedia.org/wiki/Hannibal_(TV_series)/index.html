Hannibal is an American psychological horror-thriller television series developed by Bryan Fuller for NBC. Based on characters from Thomas Harris's novels Red Dragon, Hannibal and The Silence of the Lambs, it centres on the relationship between FBI criminal profiler Will Graham (Hugh Dancy) and forensic psychiatrist Dr. Hannibal Lecter (Mads Mikkelsen), who is secretly a cannibalistic serial killer.

Premise

Graham, whose "empathy disorder" allows him to reconstruct killers' thought processes at crime scenes, is referred by FBI chief Jack Crawford (Laurence Fishburne) to Lecter for psychiatric supervision. Lecter, fascinated by Graham, manipulates him while concealing his own murders. Across three seasons the series adapts and re-imagines elements of Red Dragon and Hannibal, culminating in Lecter's capture, escape and a final confrontation alongside Graham.

Reception

The series was acclaimed for its visual style, food and production design, writing and the performances of Mikkelsen and Dancy, and it developed a devoted fan community (the "Fannibals"). Despite modest ratings, both broadcast seasons won Saturn Awards for Best Network Television Series. NBC cancelled the show after three seasons in 2015; Fuller and the cast have repeatedly expressed interest in a revival.
