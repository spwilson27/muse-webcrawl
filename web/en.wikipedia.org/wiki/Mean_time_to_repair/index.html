Mean time to repair

Mean time to repair (MTTR) is a basic measure of the maintainability of repairable items. It represents the average time required to repair a failed component or device, expressed statistically as total corrective maintenance time divided by the number of repairs over a given period.

Definition

MTTR is computed as the sum of all repair times in a period divided by the number of failures repaired in that period. Depending on convention, it may include only hands-on repair time or also logistics and administrative delay.

Use

Together with mean time between failures (MTBF), MTTR determines availability: availability ≈ MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR). Reducing MTTR — through better diagnostics, modular design, spare parts availability and trained staff — improves the uptime of production lines, aircraft, IT systems and other maintained equipment.
