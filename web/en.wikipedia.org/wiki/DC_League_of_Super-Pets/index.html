DC League of Super-Pets is a 2022 American animated superhero comedy film produced by Warner Animation Group, DC Films and Seven Bucks Productions and distributed by Warner Bros. Pictures. Based on the DC Comics team the Legion of Super-Pets, it was directed by Jared Stern (in his feature directorial debut) and co-directed by Sam J. Levine, from a screenplay by Stern and John Whittington.

[Image: Promotional poster for DC League of Super-Pets — placeholder]

Plot

Krypto the Superdog, the loyal pet of Superman, forms a team of shelter animals who have accidentally gained superpowers — Ace the Bat-Hound, PB the pig, Merton the turtle and Chip the squirrel — to rescue the Justice League after they are captured by Lex Luthor and the guinea pig Lulu.

Voice cast

The film stars the voices of Dwayne Johnson as Krypto, Kevin Hart as Ace, Kate McKinnon as Lulu, John Krasinski as Superman, Vanessa Bayer as PB, Natasha Lyonne as Merton, Diego Luna as Chip, and Keanu Reeves as Batman.

Reception

The film grossed over $207 million worldwide and received generally positive reviews from critics, who praised its humour, animation and voice performances.
