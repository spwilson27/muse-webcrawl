Andre Levrone Jr. (born 9 March 1995) is an American former professional football wide receiver. He played college football at the University of Virginia and signed with the Baltimore Ravens as an undrafted free agent in 2018.

Early life and college

Levrone attended Our Lady of Good Counsel High School in Maryland, where he was a highly regarded recruit. At Virginia he developed into a deep-threat receiver, enjoying a breakout senior season in 2017 with over 600 receiving yards and seven touchdowns while averaging more than 21 yards per catch.

Professional career

After going undrafted in 2018, Levrone spent time with the Baltimore Ravens, Oakland Raiders, and Jacksonville Jaguars, primarily on practice squads. He is the son of bodybuilder Kevin Levrone, and later became known publicly through his marriage to Olympic gymnast Simone Biles's contemporary gymnastics circles — he is married to former gymnast Brenna Dowell.
