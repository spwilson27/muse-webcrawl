International DN

The International DN is a class of iceboat. The name stands for Detroit News, whose home-workshop program sponsored a contest in 1936–1937 to design an inexpensive one-person ice yacht that could be built at home. The winning design, by Joseph Lodge and others, became the DN.

Design

The DN is 12 feet (3.7 m) long with a sail area of about 60 square feet (5.6 m²). It is steered with a tiller connected to a runner-plank and can reach speeds well above the wind speed — commonly 50–60 mph in good conditions — making it one of the fastest iceboat classes and by far the most popular, with thousands built.

Racing

Racing is governed in North America by the International DN Ice Yacht Racing Association (IDNIYRA) and in Europe by its European counterpart. Annual World Championships alternate between North America and Europe, drawing fleets of over 100 boats.
