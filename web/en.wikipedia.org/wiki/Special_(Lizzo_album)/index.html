Special is the fourth studio album by American singer and rapper Lizzo, released on July 15, 2022, through Atlantic Records and Nice Life Recording Company. It is the follow-up to her breakthrough third studio album, Cuz I Love You (2019). The album leans heavily into disco, funk, and dance-pop sounds, and centres on themes of self-love, body positivity, confidence, and joy.

Background and release

Lizzo began working on the album in 2019, shortly after the success of Cuz I Love You, and continued writing and recording during the COVID-19 pandemic. She described the record as a "love letter" to her fans and to herself, aiming to produce an album that felt celebratory and uplifting during a difficult period. The album's title track was previewed in a commercial and performed on Saturday Night Live in April 2022, when Lizzo hosted and served as musical guest.

The lead single, "About Damn Time", was released on April 14, 2022. After going viral on TikTok, the song reached number one on the US Billboard Hot 100, becoming Lizzo's second chart-topping single after "Truth Hurts". Subsequent singles included the title track "Special" and "2 Be Loved (Am I Ready)".

Reception

Special received generally favourable reviews from music critics, who praised its exuberant production and Lizzo's charisma, though some felt it was less adventurous than her earlier work. Commercially, the album debuted at number two on the US Billboard 200. At the 65th Annual Grammy Awards in 2023, "About Damn Time" won Record of the Year, and the album was nominated for Album of the Year and Best Pop Vocal Album.

Tour

Lizzo supported the album with The Special Tour, which began in North America in September 2022 and later extended to Europe, Australia, New Zealand, and Japan.
