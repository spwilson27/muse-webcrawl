List of awareness ribbons

Awareness ribbons are short pieces of colored ribbon folded into a loop, worn to signify support for a cause or issue. The practice grew to prominence in the United States in the early 1990s, most famously with the red ribbon for HIV/AIDS awareness, created in 1991 by the Visual AIDS Artists Caucus. Many colors are claimed by multiple causes.

Different ribbon colors represent different causes; many are shared.

History

The awareness ribbon descends from earlier symbolic uses of ribbons, including the yellow ribbons associated with soldiers in the 1970s–80s (popularized by the song "Tie a Yellow Ribbon Round the Ole Oak Tree" and the Iran hostage crisis). The red ribbon's debut at the 1991 Tony Awards launched the modern proliferation of colors.
