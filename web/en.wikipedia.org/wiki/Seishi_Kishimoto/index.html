Seishi Kishimoto (Japanese: 岸本 聖史, born November 8, 1974) is a Japanese manga artist. He is the identical twin brother of Masashi Kishimoto, the creator of Naruto, and the similarity of the twins' art styles and story themes has frequently been remarked upon; both drew inspiration from Dragon Ball and other shōnen manga they read growing up.

Career

Kishimoto's best-known work is O-Parts Hunter, published in Japan as 666 Satan, which was serialized in Monthly Shōnen Gangan from 2001 to 2007. It follows Jio Freed, a boy harbouring the spirit of Satan, in a world of ancient relics called O-Parts. He subsequently created the action series Blazer Drive (2008–2010) and the one-shot-derived series Kurenai no Ōkami to Ashikaga Nyannosuke and Sukedachi 09, and has worked on other series including Madara projects and the manga Monster Hunter-related work. His series Madara (with writer Shōu Tajima) predates his solo fame.
