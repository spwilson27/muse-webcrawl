Madden curse

The Madden curse is a superstition involving the video game series Madden NFL, named after video game star and former NFL head coach and sportscaster John Madden. It is speculated that the player featured on the cover of the game will suffer a decline in performance or a serious injury that season.

History

Before 1999, John Madden himself appeared on every cover. The first athlete featured alone was Garrison Hearst (Madden NFL 99), who broke his ankle that season and missed two years. Subsequent cover athletes — including Daunte Culpepper, Michael Vick, Donovan McNabb, Shaun Alexander, Rob Gronkowski and Antonio Brown — frequently suffered injuries or sharp declines in the season following their cover appearance, reinforcing the superstition, although some players, such as Tom Brady and Patrick Mahomes, largely escaped it.
