The Ministry of Rural Development (Hindi: ग्रामीण विकास मंत्रालय) is a branch of the Government of India charged with accelerating socio-economic development in rural India. Headquartered at Krishi Bhavan, New Delhi, the ministry administers programmes for rural employment, housing, drinking water (historically), road connectivity, social assistance and land resource management, working through state governments and Panchayati Raj institutions. It comprises two departments: the Department of Rural Development and the Department of Land Resources.

The ministry is headquartered at Krishi Bhavan, New Delhi.

Major programmes

  • MGNREGA — the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (2005), guaranteeing 100 days of wage employment per year to rural households, the largest public works programme of its kind in the world.
  • Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G) — rural housing assistance for households below the poverty line.
  • Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY) — all-weather road connectivity for unconnected habitations, launched in 2000.
  • National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM / Aajeevika) — organising rural poor women into self-help groups and federations.
  • National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) — pensions for the elderly, widows and persons with disabilities.
  • Watershed development through the Department of Land Resources (WDC-PMKSY) and the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme.

Organisation

The ministry is headed by a cabinet minister, assisted by ministers of state and a secretary. Attached and autonomous bodies have included the Council for Advancement of People's Action and Rural Technology (CAPART) and the National Institute of Rural Development and Panchayati Raj (NIRDPR) in Hyderabad, which serves as the ministry's think tank and training institution.
