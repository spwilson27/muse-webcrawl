Fallout (American TV series)

Fallout is an American post-apocalyptic drama television series created by Graham Wagner and Geneva Robertson-Dworet for Amazon Prime Video, based on the Fallout video game franchise. It was developed by Jonathan Nolan and Lisa Joy's Kilter Films; Nolan directed the first three episodes. The series premiered on 10 April 2024, with all eight episodes of the first season released at once.

Premise and cast

Two hundred years after a 2077 nuclear war between the United States and China destroyed civilisation, descendants of survivors sheltered in luxury underground Vaults venture into the irradiated wasteland of Los Angeles. The story follows Lucy MacLean (Ella Purnell), a Vault 33 dweller who leaves to search for her abducted father; Maximus (Aaron Moten), a squire of the Brotherhood of Steel; and the Ghoul (Walton Goggins), a bounty hunter who before the war was actor Cooper Howard. Their paths converge around a scientist's secret — cold fusion technology coveted by every faction.

Reception

The series was a major critical and audience success, widely praised for its performances (Goggins in particular), production design, tone balancing dark humour with the games' retro-futuristic aesthetic, and fidelity to the source material. It received 17 Primetime Emmy Award nominations, including Outstanding Drama Series, and was renewed for a second season within days of release. It also drove a large surge in players of the Fallout games.
