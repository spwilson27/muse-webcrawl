Hexahedron

A hexahedron is any polyhedron with six faces. The most familiar is the cube — the regular hexahedron, one of the five Platonic solids, with six square faces, twelve edges and eight vertices.

Types

There are seven topologically distinct convex hexahedra, including the cube, cuboid, parallelepiped, trigonal trapezohedron, and pentagonal pyramid (counted with its base). Chiral and self-intersecting forms add further possibilities.

Applications

Hexahedral elements are widely used in finite-element meshes for computational fluid dynamics and structural analysis, where they often give better accuracy per element than tetrahedra.
