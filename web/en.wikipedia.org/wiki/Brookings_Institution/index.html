Brookings Institution

The Brookings Institution, often styled simply as Brookings, is an American think tank based in Washington, D.C. One of the oldest think tanks in the United States, it conducts research and education in the social sciences, primarily in economics, metropolitan policy, governance, foreign policy and economic development. It is generally regarded as one of the most influential think tanks in the world, and has been ranked first in the University of Pennsylvania's Global Go To Think Tank Index.

History

Brookings was founded in 1916 by the St. Louis businessman and philanthropist Robert S. Brookings as the Institute for Government Research, the first private organisation devoted to analysing public policy issues at the national level. In 1922 and 1924 the institute merged with the Institute of Economics and the Robert Brookings Graduate School, taking its present form in 1927. During the Second World War and post-war eras its economists helped shape the Marshall Plan, the federal budget process and the Employment Act of 1946.

Research

Brookings organises its work into research programmes in Economic Studies, Foreign Policy, Governance Studies, Global Economy and Development, and Metropolitan Policy. Its scholars include former officials from both Democratic and Republican administrations. The institution describes itself as nonpartisan, though media and academic observers often characterise it as centrist or centre-left. It publishes the journal Brookings Papers on Economic Activity and, with the Brookings Institution Press, hundreds of books and reports.

Funding and influence

Brookings is funded by foundations, corporations, individuals and foreign governments, disclosures of which have periodically attracted scrutiny — including investigations of funding from Qatar, which hosted the Brookings Doha Center (2008–2021). Its president from 2017 to 2022, retired Marine general John R. Allen, resigned amid a federal investigation into alleged unregistered lobbying for Qatar; he was not charged.
