Bain & Company is an American management consulting firm headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts. Together with McKinsey & Company and Boston Consulting Group, it forms the "Big Three" (MBB) of strategy consulting. The firm advises chief executives and senior management on strategy, operations, technology, mergers and acquisitions, private equity and sustainability, working under a partnership model organised around measurable results for clients.

History

Bain was founded in 1973 when Bill Bain left Boston Consulting Group with several colleagues to start a firm that would work intensively with a single client per industry and tie its advice to implementation and outcomes. The firm grew rapidly in the 1970s and 1980s. In the late 1980s financial difficulties — aggravated by a leveraged employee stock ownership plan — led to a restructuring in which Mitt Romney, who had left Bain in 1984 to found the spin-off private-equity firm Bain Capital, returned as interim CEO and led a turnaround. Bain Capital is today a separate, unaffiliated company.

In South Africa, Bain became embroiled in the state-capture scandal surrounding the South African Revenue Service (SARS): judicial commissions found that the firm's restructuring work under Jacob Zuma-era leadership had undermined the agency, and Bain apologised, repaid its fees, and was temporarily barred from state contracts in South Africa and the United Kingdom.

Services and ideas

Bain's practice areas include private equity (where it is a market leader in due diligence), consumer products, financial services, healthcare, technology and advanced manufacturing. The firm popularised the Net Promoter Score (NPS), developed by Bain partner Fred Reichheld as a measure of customer loyalty, and publishes research such as the annual Global Private Equity Report.

Careers and culture

Bain recruits associates from undergraduate programmes and consultants from MBA programmes, typically staffing cases from local offices. The firm consistently ranks near the top of workplace surveys, including Glassdoor's Best Places to Work list. Notable alumni include Mitt Romney, and executives across business and politics.
