Audi RS 4

Audi RS 4
Manufacturer: Audi Sport GmbH
Production: 1999–present (with gaps)
Class: Compact executive performance car
Layout: Front-engine, all-wheel drive (quattro)
Predecessor lineage: Audi RS 2 Avant

The Audi RS 4 is the high-performance variant of the Audi A4 range, produced by Audi Sport GmbH, the performance subsidiary of Audi. The "RS" initials are taken from the German RennSport, meaning "racing sport". The RS 4 sits above the S4 in the lineup and is offered primarily as a five-door estate (Avant), though saloon and cabriolet versions were made in one generation.

Generations

The first RS 4 (B5, 1999–2001) was an Avant only, powered by a 2.7-litre twin-turbocharged V6 developed with Cosworth, producing 380 PS. The B7 generation (2006–2008) used a naturally aspirated 4.2-litre V8 revving to 8,250 rpm and was offered as saloon, Avant and cabriolet. The B8 (2012–2015) returned to Avant-only form with a 4.2-litre V8 and a seven-speed dual-clutch gearbox. The B9 (from 2017) downsized to a 2.9-litre twin-turbo V6 producing 450 PS, shared in concept with the RS 5.

Characteristics

All RS 4 models use Audi's quattro permanent all-wheel drive, widened bodywork with flared wheel arches, uprated brakes and suspension, and discreet badging. The RS 4 is often cited as a benchmark fast estate car, combining supercar pace with family practicality.
