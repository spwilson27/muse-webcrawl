International Bartenders Association

The International Bartenders Association (IBA) is an international organisation that represents bartenders. Founded on 24 February 1951 in Torquay, England, the IBA's aims are to promote and maintain international relations among bar guilds, encourage high standards, and set standards for bartending competitions and training.

Official cocktails

The IBA is best known for its list of IBA official cocktails, divided into three categories: The Unforgettables (e.g., Martini, Negroni), Contemporary Classics (e.g., Mojito, Cosmopolitan), and New Era Drinks (e.g., Espresso martini, Aperol Spritz).

World Cocktail Championships

The IBA organises the annual World Cocktail Championships, including flair bartending competitions, hosted by rotating member countries.
