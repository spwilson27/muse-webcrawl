Platform as a service

Platform as a service (PaaS) or application platform as a service (aPaaS) is a category of cloud computing services that allows customers to provision, instantiate, run, and manage a modular bundle comprising a computing platform and one or more applications, without the complexity of building and maintaining the infrastructure typically associated with developing and launching the application(s). PaaS allows developers to develop, run, and manage business applications without managing servers, storage, networking, or the underlying operating system and runtime.

Characteristics

A PaaS provider hosts hardware and software on its own infrastructure. As a result, PaaS frees users from having to install in-house hardware and software to develop or run a new application. Typical offerings include hosting, operating systems, middleware, database management systems, development tools, and business intelligence services. Applications are usually deployed from source code or containers through a web interface or command-line tools, and the platform handles scaling, load balancing, patching, and availability.

Examples

Well-known PaaS offerings include Heroku, Google App Engine, Microsoft Azure App Service, AWS Elastic Beanstalk, Red Hat OpenShift, Cloud Foundry, Render, and Netlify. Salesforce's Force.com (now Lightning Platform) was one of the earliest examples; Heroku, founded in 2007, popularised the git-based deployment workflow and the "twelve-factor app" methodology.

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages include faster time to market, reduced operational overhead, built-in scalability, and the ability for geographically distributed teams to collaborate. Disadvantages include potential vendor lock-in, less control over the underlying infrastructure, runtime and language constraints, and pricing that may become uneconomical at very large scale.

Types

Variants include public PaaS, private PaaS (delivered from on-premises infrastructure), hybrid PaaS, and specialised forms such as integration platform as a service (iPaaS), mobile platform as a service (mPaaS), and communications platform as a service (CPaaS).
