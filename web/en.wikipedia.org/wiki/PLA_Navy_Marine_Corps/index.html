The People's Liberation Army Navy Marine Corps (PLANMC) is the marine corps of China's People's Liberation Army Navy (PLAN). It is responsible for amphibious operations, defending Chinese-held islands and reefs, expeditionary deployments ashore, and providing shipboard security detachments aboard naval vessels operating abroad. Its marines wear a distinctive blue-toned digital camouflage uniform.

History

Naval infantry units were first organized within the PLA in the early 1950s, during the campaigns to seize offshore islands from Nationalist forces, and the Marine Corps was formally established in 1953. The force was disbanded during the Cultural Revolution-era reorganizations of 1957 and then re-established in 1979–1980 as tensions with Vietnam rose, with its long-standing 1st Marine Brigade based at Zhanjiang. For decades the PLANMC remained a relatively small, two-brigade force oriented mainly toward a Taiwan contingency and defense of Chinese claims in the South China Sea.

Expansion and modern role

As part of the PLA's sweeping 2017 reforms, the Marine Corps was greatly expanded — reportedly growing from two brigades to a force of around eight brigades — and reorganized to support a much broader expeditionary role, including elements in every theater command's naval forces. PLAN Marines have deployed with the navy's anti-piracy task forces in the Gulf of Aden, provided the ground-combat element at China's first overseas support base in Djibouti (opened 2017), and taken part in bilateral amphibious exercises and international competitions. Equipment includes ZBD-05 and ZTD-05 amphibious fighting vehicles, the ZBL-09 family, PLZ-07 self-propelled howitzers and associated air-defense and aviation support, with large amphibious ships such as the Type 071 amphibious transport docks and Type 075 amphibious assault ships providing lift.

Organization

The corps is commanded from headquarters in Chaozhou, Guangdong, and comprises combined-arms marine brigades typically built around amphibious mechanized infantry, armor, artillery, air defense, reconnaissance and combat-support battalions, together with special-operations elements such as the "Jiaolong" commando unit, which has featured in evacuations and counter-piracy operations.
