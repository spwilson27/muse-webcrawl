Govia

Govia is a British transport company, a joint venture between the Go-Ahead Group (65%) and Keolis (35%). Since 1996 it has operated passenger rail franchises in England, and for a time it was the largest rail operator in the country by passenger numbers.

Govia's best-known undertaking is Govia Thameslink Railway (GTR), which from 2014 ran the Thameslink, Southern and Great Northern franchise — the largest rail franchise in Britain — encompassing Thameslink, Southern, Great Northern, and Gatwick Express services. It also previously operated the Southeastern franchise (2006–2021) and London Midland (2007–2017).

The company's operations have covered commuter and regional services around London and the South East, carrying hundreds of millions of passengers a year.
