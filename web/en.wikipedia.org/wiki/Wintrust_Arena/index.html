Wintrust Arena is a 10,387-seat multi-purpose arena in the McCormick Place convention complex in the Near South Side of Chicago, Illinois. It opened in 2017 as the home of the DePaul Blue Demons men's and women's basketball teams and, since 2018, the Chicago Sky of the WNBA.

History

The arena was developed by the Metropolitan Pier and Exposition Authority as part of the McCormick Place entertainment district, designed by Pelli Clarke Pelli Architects. It is connected by skybridge to the convention center and the Marriott Marquis Chicago. Wintrust Financial acquired naming rights in 2017. The Sky won the 2021 WNBA championship while playing home games at the arena.

Events

Beyond basketball, Wintrust Arena hosts concerts, esports, gymnastics, and convention events. It has served as a backup venue for the Chicago Bulls and Blackhawks and was used as a mass vaccination site during the COVID-19 pandemic.
