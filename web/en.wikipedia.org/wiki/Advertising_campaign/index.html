Advertising campaign

An advertising campaign is a series of advertisement messages that share a single idea and theme and form part of an integrated marketing communication strategy. Campaigns are designed to achieve a particular business objective — such as raising brand awareness, launching a product, or increasing sales — and are delivered across multiple media channels over a defined period.

Elements

A campaign typically begins with a creative brief defining the target audience, the key message, and the desired response. Creative development produces the advertisements themselves, unified by consistent slogans, imagery, and tone, while media planning determines which channels — television, print, outdoor, radio, social media, and search — will carry them and with what frequency and reach.

Measurement

Campaign effectiveness is measured through metrics such as reach, impressions, engagement, brand recall, and conversion or sales lift, increasingly in real time through digital analytics. Famous long-running campaigns include Nike's "Just Do It", De Beers' "A Diamond Is Forever", and Apple's "Think Different".
