Roud Folk Song Index

The Roud Folk Song Index is a database of around 250,000 references to nearly 25,000 songs collected from oral tradition in the English language, from all over the world. It was compiled by Steve Roud (born 1949), a former librarian, and is maintained in association with the Vaughan Williams Memorial Library of the English Folk Dance and Song Society in London.

Scope

Each song is assigned a unique "Roud number". The index cross-references published collections, broadsides, songsters, field recordings and manuscripts, allowing researchers to trace variants of the same song under different titles. It complements the Child Ballads (Francis James Child's collection) and the Laws numbers for American songs. Roud also maintains the companion Broadside Index. The database is freely searchable online through the Vaughan Williams Memorial Library website, and is a standard tool of folk-song scholarship and revival performers.
