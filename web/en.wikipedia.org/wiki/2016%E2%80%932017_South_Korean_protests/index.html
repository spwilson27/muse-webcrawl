2016–2017 South Korean protests

Protest details
Date26 October 2016 – April 2017
LocationSeoul and nationwide, South Korea
CausesCorruption scandal involving President Park Geun-hye and Choi Soon-sil
ResultImpeachment and removal of Park Geun-hye

The 2016–2017 South Korean protests, also known as the Candlelight Demonstrations, were a series of massive, peaceful protests against President Park Geun-hye following revelations that she had allowed her confidante Choi Soon-sil extraordinary influence over government affairs despite Choi holding no official position.

The scandal

Investigations revealed that Choi had reviewed confidential state documents, influenced appointments and extorted large donations from conglomerates to foundations she controlled. Weekly Saturday rallies in central Seoul grew through the winter, with the largest drawing well over a million participants; cumulative attendance over the course of the demonstrations is estimated in the tens of millions.

Impeachment

The National Assembly voted to impeach Park on 9 December 2016. On 10 March 2017 the Constitutional Court unanimously upheld the impeachment, removing her from office. An early presidential election in May 2017 was won by Moon Jae-in.

Legacy

The protests, notable for their scale, orderliness and family atmosphere, are widely regarded as a landmark of South Korean democracy. Park was subsequently convicted on corruption charges and imprisoned, though she received a presidential pardon in late 2021.
