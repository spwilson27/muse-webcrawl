List of busiest airports by passenger traffic

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This article lists the world's busiest airports by total passenger traffic (enplaned plus deplaned passengers), based on statistics published by Airports Council International. Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International Airport has been the busiest airport in the world in nearly every year since 1998, interrupted only in 2020, when Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport took the top position during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Historical highlights

  • Before Atlanta's run, Chicago O'Hare was the world's busiest airport through much of the 1960s–1990s.
  • Beijing Capital became the second-busiest airport in the world in 2010 and held the position for a decade.
  • In 2020, pandemic travel restrictions cut global traffic by more than half; Guangzhou Baiyun ranked first, with Atlanta second — the only year since 1997 in which Atlanta did not lead.
  • Traffic recovered strongly by 2023–2024, with Atlanta again exceeding 100 million passengers.
