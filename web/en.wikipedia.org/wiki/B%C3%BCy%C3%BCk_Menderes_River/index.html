The Büyük Menderes River ("Great Meander", historically the Maeander) is a river in south-western Turkey, rising near Dinar and flowing westward through a broad graben valley past Aydın to enter the Aegean Sea near the ancient city of Miletus.

The meander

The river's famously winding course gave English the word "meander" for a sinuous bend in a river. Its sediment load has steadily advanced the coastline since antiquity, leaving the once-maritime ruins of Miletus and Priene several kilometres inland.

Valley

The fertile Büyük Menderes valley is a major agricultural region, noted for figs, cotton and olives, and is paralleled by one of Turkey's first railway lines.
