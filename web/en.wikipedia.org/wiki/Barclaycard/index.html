Barclaycard

Barclaycard is a brand of credit cards and payment services owned by Barclays Bank. Launched on 29 June 1966, Barclaycard was the first credit card introduced in the United Kingdom, and for a time was the only one; its early cards were distinctive for their blue, white, and gold design. Today Barclays issues cards under the Barclaycard name in the UK and, through Barclays Bank Delaware and other subsidiaries, co-branded and own-brand cards in the United States and Germany.

History

Barclaycard was modelled on American charge and credit cards, particularly BankAmericard, but launched as the first all-purpose credit card in Britain at a time when only store cards and charge cards such as Diners Club existed. It built a network of accepting merchants from scratch, reaching around a million cardholders within its first years. In 1977 it became part of the Visa network, adopting the Visa brand alongside its own; for decades UK-issued cards carried both names, while merchant acquiring was split off into Barclaycard Merchant Services. In the 2000s Barclaycard pioneered contactless payments in the UK, launching OnePulse (combining Oyster travel, credit, and cashless payment) in 2007 and wearable payment devices such as the bPay band. Barclaycard US grew by acquiring portfolios such as the former Juniper Bank and issuing co-branded cards for airlines, retailers, and Apple (the Barclaycard with Apple Rewards, succeeded by the Apple Card partnership changes).

Operations

Barclays' card operations span consumer credit cards, commercial and corporate payment cards, and merchant acquiring, which was reorganized under Barclaycard Payments. The business is headquartered in Northampton, England, where Barclaycard's operations have been based since the 1970s.
