King's College Chapel, Cambridge

Construction1446–1544
StylePerpendicular Gothic
Length289 ft (88 m)
Vault height94 ft (29 m)

King's College Chapel is the chapel of King's College in the University of Cambridge. Built in phases between 1446 and 1544 under a succession of Tudor monarchs, it is considered one of the greatest examples of late Gothic English architecture.

Features

The chapel has the world's largest fan vault, constructed between 1512 and 1515, and a celebrated collection of medieval stained glass. The painting The Adoration of the Magi by Rubens hangs behind the altar.

Choir

The Choir of King's College is world-famous for the Festival of Nine Lessons and Carols, first held in 1918 and broadcast by the BBC since 1928.
