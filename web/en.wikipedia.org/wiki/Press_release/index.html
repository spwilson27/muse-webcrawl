Press release

[Image: A stack of printed press releases on a newsroom desk]

A press release (or news release) is an official written or recorded communication issued by an organisation to journalists to announce something newsworthy, in the hope of coverage. Invented in modern form by public-relations pioneer Ivy Lee in 1906, it is written to be easily lifted into news copy — a fact journalists both rely on and complain about ('churnalism').

Structure

The classic release has a headline, dateline, a lead paragraph answering who/what/when/where/why, quotations from executives or officials (composed for the purpose), background boilerplate and a contact. Embargoes coordinate publication times for major announcements.
