Creed II is a 2018 American sports drama film directed by Steven Caple Jr. It is the sequel to Creed (2015) and the eighth instalment in the Rocky film series. Michael B. Jordan stars as Adonis Creed, trained by Rocky Balboa (Sylvester Stallone).

Plot

Newly crowned heavyweight champion Adonis Creed is challenged by Viktor Drago, son of Ivan Drago — the boxer who killed Adonis's father Apollo Creed in the ring in Rocky IV. Torn between his family's legacy and his own future with Bianca, Adonis accepts the fight and must confront the past in a climactic rematch in Moscow.

Reception

The film grossed over $214 million worldwide and received generally positive reviews, with praise for Jordan's performance and the emotional continuation of the Drago storyline. A sequel, Creed III, followed in 2023, directed by Jordan himself.
