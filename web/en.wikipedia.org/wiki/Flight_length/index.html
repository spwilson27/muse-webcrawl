Flight length

Flight length is a classification of airline routes by distance or scheduled duration. While definitions vary between airlines and regulators, flights are commonly grouped into short-haul, medium-haul, long-haul and ultra-long-haul categories.

Typical definitions

  • Short-haul: flights under about 3 hours, roughly less than 1,500 km.
  • Medium-haul: flights of about 3 to 6 hours.
  • Long-haul: flights of about 6 to 12 hours, typically intercontinental.
  • Ultra-long-haul: flights over about 12 hours, or exceeding roughly 14,000 km, such as Singapore–New York services.

Operational significance

Flight length determines aircraft selection, crew rostering rules and rest requirements, in-flight service, and fuel planning. Longer flights can benefit from the fuel efficiency of cruising but suffer from the weight penalty of carrying fuel for later stages of the journey.
