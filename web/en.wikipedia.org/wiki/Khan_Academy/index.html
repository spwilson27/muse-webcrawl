Khan Academy

Khan Academy is an American non-profit educational organization created in 2006 by Sal Khan. Its mission is to provide a free, world-class education for anyone, anywhere. The platform offers practice exercises, instructional videos, and a personalized learning dashboard covering mathematics, science, computing, history, economics and test preparation, in dozens of languages.

History

Sal Khan began by tutoring his cousin in mathematics remotely, uploading videos to YouTube in 2006. The videos' popularity led him to found Khan Academy as a non-profit in 2008. The organization has received major support from donors including the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and Google. Its content is used by tens of millions of learners and by schools worldwide.

Features

The platform emphasizes mastery learning: students practise skills until they demonstrate proficiency. Teachers can track class progress through dashboards. In 2023 Khan Academy launched Khanmigo, an AI-powered tutoring assistant built on large language models, designed to guide students rather than simply give answers.
