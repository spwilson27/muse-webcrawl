Stephen (name)

Stephen is a masculine given name derived from the Greek Stephanos (Στέφανος), meaning "crown" or "garland". The spelling Steven is a common variant. The name's popularity in the Christian world stems from Saint Stephen, the first Christian martyr, stoned to death as recounted in the Acts of the Apostles.

History and variants

Stephen was borne by kings of England and Hungary (Saint Stephen I, Hungary's first king and patron saint) and by ten popes. Cognates include Étienne, Stefan, Stefano, Esteban and István. In English, "Stephen" is traditionally pronounced STEE-vən, though the spelling also appears with a "ph" pronounced as in the saint's name styling; the variant "Steven" shares the same pronunciation.

Popularity

The name was especially common in English-speaking countries in the mid-20th century. Feminine forms include Stephanie and Stefania.
