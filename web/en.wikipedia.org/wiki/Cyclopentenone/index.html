Cyclopentenone

2-Cyclopentenone
FormulaC5H6O
Molar mass82.10 g/mol
AppearanceColorless liquid
Boiling point150 °C
CAS Number930-30-3

Cyclopentenone is a chemical compound of the family of α,β-unsaturated cyclic ketones, containing a five-membered cyclopentene ring fused with a ketone functional group. The parent compound is 2-cyclopenten-1-one (often simply called cyclopentenone or 2-cyclopentenone), with the formula C5H6O. Cyclopentenones are important building blocks in organic synthesis and occur as structural motifs in a number of natural products.

Structure and reactivity

As an enone, cyclopentenone is a good Michael acceptor: nucleophiles add to the β-carbon of the conjugated system in conjugate (1,4-) addition reactions. This reactivity makes cyclopentenones versatile intermediates for building substituted cyclopentane rings and fused ring systems, including in the synthesis of prostaglandins and jasmonates such as methyl jasmonate.

Related compounds

Substituted cyclopentenones include the jasmonate family of plant hormones, the prostaglandins, and various antibiotics and antitumor agents containing cyclopentenone rings. The corresponding six-membered analogue is cyclohexenone. Cyclopentenone motifs are also of interest for their biological activity, including anti-inflammatory and cytotoxic effects observed in some natural products.

Synthesis

2-Cyclopentenone can be prepared by several routes, including the Nazarov cyclization of divinyl ketones and ring-closing reactions of acyclic enone precursors. Intramolecular aldol condensation of 1,4-diketones also provides access to substituted cyclopentenones.
