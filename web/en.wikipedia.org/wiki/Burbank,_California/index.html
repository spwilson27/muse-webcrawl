Burbank, California

Burbank is a city in the southeastern San Fernando Valley of Los Angeles County, California, United States. Located about 12 miles (19 km) northwest of downtown Los Angeles, it had a population of 107,337 at the 2020 census. Nicknamed the "Media Capital of the World", Burbank is home to the headquarters and studios of major entertainment companies including The Walt Disney Company, Warner Bros., and Nickelodeon Animation Studio, and was long home to NBC Studios and The Tonight Show.

History

The area was part of the Spanish land grants Rancho San Rafael and Rancho La Providencia. The city is named for David Burbank, a New Hampshire-born dentist and entrepreneur who purchased ranch land in 1867 and built a sheep ranch. Burbank was incorporated in 1911. Its growth was driven by the Southern Pacific Railroad, the arrival of the Warner Bros. studio in 1926 and the Walt Disney Studios in 1939, and by Lockheed Aircraft, whose plant built P-38 fighters and other aircraft during the Second World War and made the city an aviation hub.

Geography and economy

The city covers about 17 square miles between the Verdugo Mountains and the Los Angeles River. Hollywood Burbank Airport (formerly Bob Hope Airport) serves the region. Aside from media, significant employers include Providence Saint Joseph Medical Center and the studios' service businesses. Downtown Burbank and the Empire Center are the principal retail districts, and the city is served by Metrolink and the Interstate 5 and State Route 134 freeways.

In popular culture

Burbank was a running gag on Rowan & Martin's Laugh-In ("beautiful downtown Burbank"), echoed by Johnny Carson on The Tonight Show, which taped at NBC Studios there from 1972 to 2014.
