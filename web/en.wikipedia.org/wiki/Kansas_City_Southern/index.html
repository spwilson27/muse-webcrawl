Kansas City Southern

Kansas City Southern (KCS) was a transportation holding company whose principal subsidiary, the Kansas City Southern Railway, operated a rail network in the central and south-central United States and, through Kansas City Southern de México, in northeastern and central Mexico. Founded in 1887 by Arthur Edward Stilwell, it was the smallest of the major North American Class I railroads.

History

Stilwell built the Kansas City, Pittsburg and Gulf Railroad southward from Kansas City to the Gulf of Mexico, founding Port Arthur, Texas, as its terminus; the line was reorganised as Kansas City Southern in 1900. In the 1990s KCS acquired concessions in Mexico and a stake in the Panama Canal Railway, positioning itself as a NAFTA trade carrier linking the United States, Mexico and Canada.

Merger

In 2021 Canadian Pacific Railway and Canadian National competed to acquire KCS; Canadian Pacific prevailed. The merger was approved by the U.S. Surface Transportation Board in March 2023 and completed on 14 April 2023, creating Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC), the first single-line railway connecting Canada, the United States and Mexico.
