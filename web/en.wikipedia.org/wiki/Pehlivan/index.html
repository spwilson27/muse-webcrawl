Pehlivan

CountryTurkey (also Balkans)
SportYağlı güreş (oil wrestling)
Premier eventKırkpınar, Edirne
Champion's titleBaşpehlivan

A pehlivan is a wrestler in the Turkish tradition of yağlı güreş, or oil wrestling, in which competitors, wearing only leather trousers called kıspet, grapple while covered in olive oil.

Oil wrestling

Oil wrestling is regarded as Turkey's national sport. Wrestlers douse themselves with olive oil, making grips difficult and contests a test of strength, balance and endurance. Bouts are accompanied by drumming and the announcements of the cazgır, and wrestlers traditionally begin by performing ritual movements and touching the ground.

Kırkpınar

The most prestigious tournament is the Kırkpınar festival held annually at Edirne, said to date from 1360 and recognised by UNESCO as part of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. Its champion receives the title başpehlivan (chief pehlivan) and a golden belt. Famous pehlivans, such as Koca Yusuf and Kurtdereli Mehmet, became folk heroes in Turkey and the Balkans.
