The Age of Innocence (1993 film)

Directed by: Martin Scorsese

Starring: Daniel Day-Lewis, Michelle Pfeiffer, Winona Ryder

Based on: The Age of Innocence by Edith Wharton

Release date: 1993

The Age of Innocence is a 1993 American historical romantic drama film directed by Martin Scorsese. The screenplay, by Scorsese and Jay Cocks, is adapted from the 1920 novel of the same name by Edith Wharton. The film stars Daniel Day-Lewis as Newland Archer, Michelle Pfeiffer as Countess Ellen Olenska, and Winona Ryder as May Welland, with narration by Joanne Woodward.

Plot

In 1870s New York, wealthy lawyer Newland Archer is engaged to the conventional May Welland. His life is disrupted by the arrival of May's cousin, Ellen Olenska, who has scandalously separated from her Polish husband. Archer falls in love with Ellen but is trapped by the rigid codes of old New York high society; the two renounce their love, he marries May, and Ellen returns to Europe. Decades later, after May's death, Archer sits outside Ellen's Paris apartment but sends his son up alone and walks away, choosing to keep his memory of their love rather than meet her again.

Reception and awards

The film was praised for its visual richness — Scorsese meticulously recreated the manners and oppressive formality of Gilded Age New York — and its emotional restraint. It was nominated for five Academy Awards, winning for Best Costume Design (Gabriella Pescucci); Winona Ryder received an Oscar nomination and won a Golden Globe for her supporting performance. Although not a major commercial success on release, it is now frequently ranked among Scorsese's finest films.
