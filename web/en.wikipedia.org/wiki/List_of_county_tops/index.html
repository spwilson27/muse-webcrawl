List of county tops

A county top is the highest point within a county. This list gives the county tops of the historic and ceremonial counties of England. The highest county top in England is Scafell Pike (978 m), the top of Cumberland and of the ceremonial county of Cumbria, and the highest mountain in England. Visiting all county tops is a popular hill-bagging challenge.

Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland

In Scotland the county tops include Ben Nevis (1,345 m, Inverness-shire) — the highest point in the British Isles — while in Wales the highest county top is Snowdon / Yr Wyddfa (1,085 m, Caernarfonshire). In Northern Ireland the highest is Slieve Donard (850 m, County Down).
