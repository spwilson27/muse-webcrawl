Cameronians (Scottish Rifles)

The Cameronians (Scottish Rifles) was a rifle regiment of the British Army, formed in 1881 by the amalgamation of the 26th (Cameronian) Regiment of Foot and the 90th (Perthshire) Regiment of Foot. It was the only regiment to carry the name of a religious movement — the Cameronians, followers of the Covenanter Richard Cameron.

Traditions

Unique customs included issuing each recruit with a Bible and posting armed sentries outside church services, recalling the regiment's Covenanting origins. It was the only British regiment to wear a rifle-green doublet with trews of Douglas tartan after later reforms.

History and disbandment

The regiment served in both World Wars and in colonial campaigns including the Second Boer War. Rather than amalgamate, it chose disbandment, which took place in 1968 at Douglas, Lanarkshire — the last British infantry regiment so disbanded.
