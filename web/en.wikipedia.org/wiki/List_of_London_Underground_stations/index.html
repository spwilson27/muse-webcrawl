List of London Underground stations

The London Underground is a rapid transit system serving London and some parts of the adjacent counties. It has 272 stations across eleven lines. The busiest stations include Waterloo, Victoria, London Bridge, King's Cross St Pancras, Oxford Circus and Liverpool Street.
