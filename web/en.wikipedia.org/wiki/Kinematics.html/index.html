Kinematics

Kinematics is a branch of classical mechanics that describes the motion of points, bodies and systems of bodies without regard to the forces that cause the motion. It employs quantities such as displacement, velocity and acceleration.

Description of motion

Kinematics studies trajectories in terms of position as a function of time; velocity is the rate of change of position, and acceleration the rate of change of velocity. Equations of motion for constant acceleration relate displacement, initial and final velocity, acceleration and time.

Applications

The field underlies the analysis of machinery, robotics, projectile motion, and celestial mechanics, and is complemented by dynamics, which relates motion to forces and mass.
