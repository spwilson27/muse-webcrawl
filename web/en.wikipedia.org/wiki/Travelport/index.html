Travelport is a global travel retail platform and technology company that operates a global distribution system (GDS) used by travel agencies and corporations to book airline tickets, hotel rooms, car rentals and other travel services. It is one of the three major GDS operators, alongside Amadeus IT Group and Sabre Corporation.

History

Travelport's roots lie in the Galileo and Apollo reservation systems created by airlines in the 1970s–1990s, and in Worldspan, formed by Delta, Northwest and TWA. Cendant Corporation assembled Galileo and other travel assets in the early 2000s; in 2006 the travel distribution business was sold to affiliates of the Blackstone Group and renamed Travelport. Travelport acquired Worldspan in 2007. The company listed on the New York Stock Exchange in 2014, and in 2019 was taken private by Siris Capital Group and Evergreen Coast Capital (an affiliate of Elliott Management) in a deal valued at about $4.4 billion.

Products

Travelport operates the Galileo, Apollo and Worldspan GDS platforms and has consolidated its offering into Travelport+, a modern retailing platform with APIs for air, hotel and car content, including support for IATA's New Distribution Capability (NDC) content. Its customers include online and offline travel agencies, travel management companies and suppliers.
