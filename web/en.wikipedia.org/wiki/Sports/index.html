Sports

Sport is a form of physical activity or game. Often competitive and organised, sports use, maintain, or improve physical ability and skills. They typically provide enjoyment to participants and, in some cases, entertainment to spectators. Sports can be individual or team-based, take place in venues ranging from stadiums to open countryside, and are usually governed by rules or customs that ensure fair competition and consistent adjudication of winners. Hundreds of sports exist, from single-player contests to those with simultaneous participants competing as teams or individuals.

Definition and recognition

Sports are usually governed by organisations such as the International Olympic Committee (IOC) and the Global Association of International Sports Federations, which recognise activities involving physical exertion, an element of competition, and rules that are not harmful to living creatures. The boundary of "sport" is contested: activities such as chess and esports involve competition and skill but little physical exertion, yet are recognised by the IOC or national sports bodies in various jurisdictions. The word "sport" derives from the Old French desport, meaning leisure or diversion.

History

Competitive physical contests are ancient: wrestling and gymnastics appear in Egyptian art, and the ancient Olympic Games began in Greece in 776 BC, featuring running, jumping, discus, wrestling, boxing, and chariot racing. China, Persia, and Mesoamerica all had sporting traditions, including ballgames. The modern codification of sports began in 19th-century Britain, where public schools and clubs standardised rules for football, cricket, rugby, and athletics. From there, organised sport spread worldwide, culminating in the revival of the Olympic Games in 1896 and the rise of professional leagues and global tournaments such as the FIFA World Cup (first held in 1930), now the most watched sporting event on Earth.

Types of sport

Sports are categorised in many ways: team sports (football, basketball, ice hockey) and individual sports (tennis, golf, boxing); racquet, ball, water, winter, motor, combat, target, and mind sports; and by environment, such as athletics on track and field. Association football is the world's most popular sport by participation and viewership, followed by cricket, field hockey, tennis, and volleyball in participation estimates.

Sport and society

Sport is a major global industry encompassing media rights, sponsorship, merchandising, and betting, and a powerful vehicle of national and local identity. Participation is promoted for its health benefits, while elite sport raises issues including doping, corruption, injury—particularly concussion—and the cost of hosting mega-events. Women's sport, long marginalised, has grown rapidly in professionalism and audience in the 21st century.
