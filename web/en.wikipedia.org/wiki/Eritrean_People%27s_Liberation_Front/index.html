Eritrean People's Liberation Front

Eritrean People's Liberation Front
Founded: 1970
Dissolved: 1994 (became People's Front for Democracy and Justice)
Leader: Isaias Afwerki
Goal: Eritrean independence from Ethiopia

The Eritrean People's Liberation Front (EPLF) was an armed organisation that fought for the independence of Eritrea from Ethiopia. It emerged in 1970 as a breakaway faction of the Eritrean Liberation Front and, after a thirty-year armed struggle, defeated Ethiopian forces in Eritrea in 1991, leading to Eritrea's internationally recognised independence in 1993.

Struggle

The EPLF built a disciplined guerrilla army and extensive underground infrastructure, including hospitals and workshops, in its base areas around Nakfa. In alliance with the Tigray People's Liberation Front, it helped overthrow Ethiopia's Derg regime in 1991. After independence, the EPLF transformed itself into the People's Front for Democracy and Justice (PFDJ), Eritrea's sole legal political party, under President Isaias Afwerki.
