List of Ottoman grand viziers

The Grand Vizier (Ottoman Turkish: Sadr-ı Azam) was the chief minister of the Ottoman Empire, holding absolute power of attorney for the Sultan and presiding over the Imperial Council (Divan). The office existed from the 14th century until the abolition of the Sultanate in 1922. The last Grand Vizier was Ahmed Tevfik Pasha. Notable long-serving and famous holders are listed below in chronological order.

In total the office was held by well over 200 individuals, several serving multiple non-consecutive terms. The Grand Vizier's residence and office, the Sublime Porte (Bab-ı Ali), became a metonym for the Ottoman government itself.
