The International Commerce Centre (ICC) is a 108-storey, 484 m (1,588 ft) supertall skyscraper in West Kowloon, Hong Kong. Completed in 2010, it is the tallest building in Hong Kong and was the fourth-tallest building in the world at the time of its completion.

Development

The tower forms part of the Union Square development above Kowloon Station on the Airport Express line. It was developed by Sun Hung Kai Properties and designed by the architectural firm Kohn Pedersen Fox Associates.

Features

The building houses Grade A offices, the Sky100 observation deck on the 100th floor, and the Ritz-Carlton, Hong Kong, which occupies the top floors and was among the highest hotels in the world when it opened.
