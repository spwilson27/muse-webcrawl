BBC Radio Scotland
First air date: 23 November 1978
Format: News, music, talk, sport
Owner: BBC Scotland
Headquarters: Pacific Quay, Glasgow

BBC Radio Scotland is a Scottish national radio network owned and operated by BBC Scotland. It broadcasts a wide range of programming in English, including news, current affairs, music, arts, comedy, drama and live sport.

History

The station launched on 23 November 1978, replacing the Scottish opt-outs of BBC Radio 4. It broadcasts from BBC Scotland's Pacific Quay headquarters in Glasgow, with regional studios around Scotland producing local news bulletins and programmes, including opt-outs for Orkney, Shetland, the Highlands and Islands, the North East, the Borders and Dumfries and Galloway.

Programming

Flagship programmes include Good Morning Scotland, Mornings, Drivetime and Sportsound, which covers Scottish football. Its sister service BBC Radio nan Gàidheal broadcasts in Scottish Gaelic.
