EngineeringUK is a United Kingdom not-for-profit organisation and registered charity that works to inspire young people to choose careers in engineering and technology and to grow the engineering talent pipeline. It works in partnership with the engineering community, business, government and education to address the UK's shortage of engineers and technicians and to improve diversity in the profession.

History

The organisation was established in 2002 as the Engineering and Technology Board (ETB), following recommendations on the promotion of engineering, and relaunched under the name EngineeringUK in 2009. It is funded by professional engineering institutions, employers and government contracts.

Activities

EngineeringUK organises The Big Bang Fair, the UK's largest annual celebration of science, technology, engineering and maths for young people, held at the NEC in Birmingham, along with regional Big Bang events. It also runs or supports programmes including Tomorrow's Engineers, the Big Bang Competition for young scientists and engineers, and research publications such as its annual Engineering UK report on the state of engineering skills in the UK workforce. Its campaigns seek in particular to increase the participation of girls and under-represented groups in engineering.
