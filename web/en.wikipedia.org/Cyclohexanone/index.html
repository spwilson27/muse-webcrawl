Cyclohexanone

FormulaC₆H₁₀O
AppearanceColourless oily liquid
Boiling point156 °C

Cyclohexanone is the organic compound with the formula (CH₂)₅CO. The molecule consists of a six-carbon cyclic molecule with a ketone functional group. This colourless, oily liquid has an odour reminiscent of acetone and peppermint, and is a key industrial intermediate.

Production

Cyclohexanone is produced on a scale of billions of kilograms annually, mainly by the air oxidation of cyclohexane (giving a mixture with cyclohexanol, "KA oil") or by hydrogenation of phenol.

Uses

The great majority of cyclohexanone is consumed in the production of nylon precursors: oxidation gives adipic acid, and conversion to the oxime followed by Beckmann rearrangement gives caprolactam, the monomer for nylon-6. It is also used as a solvent and as a building block in the synthesis of pharmaceuticals and fragrances. In the laboratory it undergoes typical ketone reactions, including α-alkylation via its enamine derivatives.
