Neymar

Born: 5 February 1992, Mogi das Cruzes, Brazil
Position: Forward
Clubs: Santos, Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, Al Hilal

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior (born 5 February 1992), known as Neymar, is a Brazilian professional footballer regarded as one of the most skillful players of his generation. Known for his dribbling, flair, and playmaking, he is Brazil's all-time top scorer.

Career

Neymar emerged at Santos, winning the Copa Libertadores in 2011. He joined Barcelona in 2013, forming the "MSN" attacking trio with Lionel Messi and Luis Suárez that won the treble in 2015. In 2017 he moved to Paris Saint-Germain for €222 million, still the world-record transfer fee. With PSG he won multiple Ligue 1 titles but was repeatedly hampered by foot injuries. He transferred to Saudi club Al Hilal in 2023, suffered a serious knee injury, and returned to Santos in 2025.

International

Neymar won the 2013 FIFA Confederations Cup and an Olympic gold medal at Rio 2016. He surpassed Pelé as Brazil's record scorer in 2023, though injuries limited his roles at the 2014, 2018, and 2022 World Cups.
