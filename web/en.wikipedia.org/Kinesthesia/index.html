Kinesthesia

Kinesthesia (or kinaesthesia) is the sense of the position and movement of the parts of the body, closely related to and often grouped with proprioception. Receptors in muscles, tendons and joints — muscle spindles, Golgi tendon organs and joint receptors — report limb position and motion to the nervous system.

Function

Kinesthesia lets a person touch their nose with eyes closed, judge weights, coordinate movement and learn motor skills through practice. It can be disrupted by neurological injury or disease, and it is sharpened by training in sport, dance and music. The term is also used metaphorically in film and art theory for a bodily sense of movement in viewers.
