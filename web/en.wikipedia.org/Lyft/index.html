Lyft

Industry: Transportation / ridesharing
Founded: 2012, San Francisco
Founders: Logan Green, John Zimmer
Headquarters: San Francisco, California

Lyft, Inc. is an American transportation company offering ride-hailing, vehicles for hire, motorized scooters, a bicycle-sharing system and food delivery in the United States and Canada, accessed through its mobile app.

History

Lyft was launched in 2012 by Logan Green and John Zimmer as a service of Zimride, a long-distance intercity carpool company founded in 2007. It became the second-largest ridesharing company in the United States after Uber, and went public in March 2019, ahead of its larger rival.

Services

Users request rides from drivers using their personal vehicles. Lyft also operates bike-share systems including Citi Bike in New York and has pursued autonomous vehicle partnerships.
