Painted bunting

The painted bunting (Passerina ciris) is a species of bird in the cardinal family, Cardinalidae. It is native to North America and is noted for the male's exceptionally colourful plumage, combining red underparts with a blue head, green back, and red rump.

Distribution and habitat

Two populations breed in the southern United States — one along the Atlantic coast from North Carolina to Florida, and one in the south-central states from Texas to Mississippi — and winter in Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean, and southern Florida.

Conservation

Populations have declined owing to habitat loss and capture for the cage-bird trade, and the species has been assessed as Near Threatened. Females and immature males are yellow-green, providing camouflage in brushy habitat.
