Lee Harvey Oswald
Born: 18 October 1939, New Orleans, Louisiana
Died: 24 November 1963, Dallas, Texas
Cause of death: Gunshot wound (shot by Jack Ruby)
Known for: Assassinating John F. Kennedy

Lee Harvey Oswald (18 October 1939 – 24 November 1963) was an American former US Marine who assassinated US president John F. Kennedy on 22 November 1963, according to the findings of the Warren Commission and subsequent investigations.

Life

Oswald grew up in New Orleans and New York, joined the Marine Corps in 1956, and served in Japan and the Philippines. In 1959 he defected to the Soviet Union, living in Minsk until 1962, when he returned to the United States with his Russian wife Marina. In April 1963 he attempted to assassinate retired general Edwin Walker. He took a job at the Texas School Book Depository in Dallas in October 1963.

Assassination and death

On 22 November 1963 Oswald fired from the Depository's sixth floor at Kennedy's motorcade, then fled and shot police officer J. D. Tippit before being captured in a cinema. On 24 November, while being transferred through the basement of Dallas police headquarters, he was shot by Jack Ruby and died at Parkland Hospital.
