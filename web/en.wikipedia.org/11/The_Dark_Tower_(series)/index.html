The Dark Tower (series)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Author: Stephen King
Books: 8 novels (1982–2012)
Genres: Dark fantasy, horror, science fiction, Western

The Dark Tower is a series of eight books and a novella written by American author Stephen King. Blending dark fantasy, science fantasy, horror and Western genres, it describes a gunslinger, Roland Deschain, on his quest to reach a tower whose top he must climb; the Dark Tower is the nexus of all universes, and its decay threatens existence itself. King has called the series his magnum opus, and it connects to many of his other novels.

Overview

Roland, the last of the gunslingers of Gilead, pursues the man in black across the desert of Mid-World and draws companions — Eddie Dean, Susannah Dean and Jake Chambers — from different whens of New York City. Inspired in part by Robert Browning's poem "Childe Roland to the Dark Tower Came" and by Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings, the series was begun in 1970 and concluded more than three decades later. A film adaptation was released in 2017, and an extensive comic-book expansion has been published by Marvel.
