The Shining is a 1977 horror novel by American author Stephen King. It is King's third published novel and first hardcover bestseller, and it established him as a preeminent author in the horror genre.

Plot

Jack Torrance, a struggling writer and recovering alcoholic, takes a job as the winter caretaker of the isolated Overlook Hotel in Colorado, bringing his wife Wendy and his psychically gifted son Danny. As a winter storm cuts the hotel off, the building's malevolent influence works on Jack's mind, driving him toward violence against his family, while Danny's gift — "the shining" — reveals the hotel's horrors.

Adaptations

Stanley Kubrick's 1980 film adaptation is considered a classic of horror cinema, though King was critical of its changes; a 1997 television miniseries, scripted by King, followed the novel more closely. A sequel novel, Doctor Sleep, was published in 2013.
