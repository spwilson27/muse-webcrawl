It is a 1986 horror novel by American author Stephen King. The story follows the experiences of seven children — the "Losers' Club" — as they are terrorised by an evil entity that exploits the fears of its victims to disguise itself while hunting its prey, most often appearing as the clown Pennywise.

Plot

The novel alternates between two time periods: 1957–58, when the Losers first confront It in the town of Derry, Maine, and 1984–85, when they return as adults after the creature re-emerges. The Losers trace It to the sewers beneath Derry and confront it in its true form.

Adaptations

The novel was adapted as a 1990 television miniseries starring Tim Curry, and as two films, It (2017) and It Chapter Two (2019), starring Bill Skarsgård as Pennywise.
