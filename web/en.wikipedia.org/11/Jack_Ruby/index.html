Jack Leon Ruby (born Jacob Leon Rubenstein; 25 March 1911 – 3 January 1967) was an American nightclub owner who murdered Lee Harvey Oswald on 24 November 1963, two days after Oswald assassinated President John F. Kennedy. Ruby shot Oswald, live on television, in the basement of Dallas Police Headquarters.

Background

Ruby operated strip clubs and dance halls in Dallas and had many contacts among police officers, who frequented his clubs. He said he acted out of grief over the assassination and to spare Jacqueline Kennedy the ordeal of a trial.

Trial and death

Ruby was convicted of murder in 1964; the conviction was overturned on appeal due to publicity and improper admission of testimony. He died of a pulmonary embolism in 1967 while awaiting retrial. His act has remained a focal point of speculation about the assassination.
