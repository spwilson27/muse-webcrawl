The Dark Tower is a series of eight novels by American author Stephen King that incorporates themes from multiple genres, including dark fantasy, science fantasy, horror and western. It describes a "gunslinger" and his quest toward a tower, the nature of which is both physical and metaphorical.

Overview

The series follows Roland Deschain, last of the gunslingers of Gilead, as he pursues the man in black and quests for the Dark Tower, the nexus of all universes. The main sequence runs from The Gunslinger (1982) to The Dark Tower (2004), with a further interquel, The Wind Through the Keyhole (2012). King has called it his magnum opus, and its story threads connect to many of his other works, including 'Salem's Lot and It.

Adaptation

A film adaptation was released in 2017, starring Idris Elba as Roland and Matthew McConaughey as the man in black.
