List of apple cultivars

There are more than 7,500 known cultivars of the domestic apple (Malus domestica). Cultivars vary in colour, size, flavour, texture, disease resistance and suitability for cooking, eating raw or cider production.

Well-known cultivars

  • Fuji — sweet, crisp, developed in Japan
  • Gala — sweet, from New Zealand, among the most grown worldwide
  • Granny Smith — green, tart, from Australia
  • Honeycrisp — exceptionally crisp and sweet, from Minnesota, USA
  • Golden Delicious — yellow, sweet, from West Virginia
  • Red Delicious — classic American red apple
  • Braeburn — firm, tangy, from New Zealand
  • Pink Lady (Cripps Pink) — sweet-tart, Australian
  • McIntosh — Canadian, soft flesh, national apple of Canada
  • Cox's Orange Pippin — classic English dessert apple
  • Bramley — large English cooking apple
  • Jonagold, Elstar, Empire, Cortland, Idared, Jonathan, Rome, Winesap, Northern Spy, Gravenstein, Russet varieties, Jazz, Envy, Cosmic Crisp, Ambrosia, Cameo, Pacific Rose, SweeTango

Cider apples

Cultivars such as Kingston Black, Dabinett and Yarlington Mill are grown specifically for cider, classified as sweet, bittersweet, bitter sharp or sharp.

Crabapples

Small-fruited types grown for ornament, pollination, jelly and wildlife.
