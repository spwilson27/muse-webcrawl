Drag king

A drag king performing

Drag kings are mainly female performance artists who dress in masculine drag and personify male gender stereotypes as part of an individual or group routine. Drag kings often perform as exaggeratedly macho male characters, sometimes also paying homage to or impersonating specific male celebrities. They are the counterpart to drag queens, who perform in feminine drag.

History

Male impersonators featured in 19th- and early 20th-century music hall and vaudeville, including Vesta Tilley and Ella Shields in Britain and Annie Hindle in the United States. The modern drag king scene emerged in lesbian and queer nightlife in the 1990s, notably in New York, London, and San Francisco, with troupes such as San Francisco's Drag Kings of Comedy.

Performance

A drag king performance typically involves lip-syncing or singing, dancing, and comedy, using costume, makeup, fake facial hair, and body language to construct a masculine persona. Many performers adopt stage names and parody or critique ideals of masculinity and gender norms.
