The Walt Disney Company

Type: Public
Industry: Media and entertainment
Founded: October 16, 1923
Founders: Walt Disney, Roy O. Disney
Headquarters: Burbank, California, U.S.

The Walt Disney Company, commonly known as Disney, is an American multinational mass media and entertainment conglomerate headquartered at the Walt Disney Studios complex in Burbank, California. Founded on October 16, 1923, by brothers Walt and Roy O. Disney as the Disney Brothers Studio, it became a leader in animation with characters such as Mickey Mouse (introduced in Steamboat Willie, 1928) and feature films beginning with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937).

History and divisions

Disney diversified into television, live-action film and theme parks, opening Disneyland in California in 1955 and Walt Disney World in Florida in 1971. After a period of decline, the company revived under Michael Eisner and Frank Wells from 1984, with an animation renaissance including The Lion King. Under CEO Bob Iger it acquired Pixar (2006), Marvel Entertainment (2009), Lucasfilm (2012) and most of 21st Century Fox (2019), and launched the streaming service Disney+ in 2019. Its principal divisions include Disney Entertainment (studios, networks and streaming), ESPN, and Disney Experiences (parks, resorts and cruise line). Disney is one of the largest media companies in the world by revenue.
