Elevator (aeronautics)

Elevators are flight control surfaces, usually at the rear of an aircraft, that control the pitch of the machine — its rotation about the lateral axis — and thus the angle of attack and lift of the wing.

Function

Elevators are typically hinged to the back of the horizontal stabiliser. Moving the control column or stick deflects both elevators together: raising them pushes the tail down and the nose up, increasing lift and climb, while lowering them does the opposite.

Variants

Some aircraft combine the stabiliser and elevator into a single moving surface called a stabilator. On canard designs the pitch control surface sits at the front of the aircraft. On many flying wings, elevons combine the functions of elevator and aileron.
