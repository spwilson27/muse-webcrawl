Spiennes

Spiennes is a village near Mons in the province of Hainaut, Belgium, best known for the Neolithic flint mines located there. The mines cover more than 100 hectares and are among the largest and earliest known Neolithic mining complexes in Europe.

The flint mines

From around 4300 to 2200 BC, Neolithic miners dug shafts up to 16 metres deep through chalk to extract flint nodules, which were knapped into axes and blades. The mines were inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2000, together with other Neolithic flint mines in Europe.

Archaeology

Excavations have revealed shafts, galleries, knapping workshops, and human remains. An interpretation centre, SILEX'S, opened in 2015, allowing visitors to view the archaeological site.
