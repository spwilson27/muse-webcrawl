Nuevo León is a state in northeastern Mexico, bordering Tamaulipas, Coahuila, San Luis Potosí and Zacatecas, with a short frontier with the US state of Texas. Its capital and largest city is Monterrey, one of Mexico's principal industrial and commercial centres.

History

The region was settled by Spanish colonists in the late 16th century; Luis Carvajal y de la Cueva established the New Kingdom of León (Nuevo Reino de León) in the 1580s, and Monterrey was founded in 1596. The state took its modern form in the 19th century.

Economy

Nuevo León is among Mexico's wealthiest and most industrialised states, with steel, cement, brewing, glass and advanced manufacturing, plus major universities such as the Autonomous University of Nuevo León and Tecnológico de Monterrey.
