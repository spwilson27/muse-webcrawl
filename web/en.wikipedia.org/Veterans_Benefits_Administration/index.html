Veterans Benefits Administration

Formed1988 (as part of the Department of Veterans Affairs)

The Veterans Benefits Administration (VBA) is one of three administrations within the United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), alongside the Veterans Health Administration and the National Cemetery Administration. The VBA is responsible for administering programs of veterans' benefits, including disability compensation, pension, education assistance, home loan guaranty, vocational rehabilitation, and life insurance.

Programs

The VBA administers a wide range of benefits earned by veterans through their military service. Its largest program is disability compensation, which provides monthly tax-free payments to veterans with disabilities resulting from diseases or injuries incurred or aggravated during active military service. The administration also manages the GI Bill education benefits, the VA home loan program, and dependency and indemnity compensation for survivors.

Organization

The VBA operates through a network of regional offices across the United States, which process claims and deliver services to veterans in their areas. The head of the VBA is the Under Secretary for Benefits, appointed by the President and confirmed by the Senate.
