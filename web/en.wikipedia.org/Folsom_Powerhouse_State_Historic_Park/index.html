Folsom Powerhouse State Historic Park

Folsom Powerhouse State Historic Park is a California state park in Folsom, Sacramento County, preserving the 1895 Folsom Powerhouse, one of the oldest alternating-current hydroelectric power stations in the United States.

History

Built by the Folsom Water Power Company under Horatio G. Livermore, the powerhouse began operating on 13 July 1895, transmitting three-phase alternating current 22 miles (35 km) to Sacramento — one of the longest electrical transmissions in the country at the time and a landmark demonstration of the viability of AC power distribution. The plant generated power until 1952, when the new Folsom Dam powerhouse replaced it. The site was designated a National Historic Landmark in 1981.

Museum

The preserved building houses its original General Electric generators and turbines, along with interpretive exhibits on hydroelectricity and the history of power in California. A second, smaller powerhouse on the site, built in 1897, also survives.
