Eastern Hemisphere
Region: East of the prime meridian to 180° longitude
Includes: Most of Europe, Africa, Asia, Australia and Oceania

The Eastern Hemisphere is the half of Earth that lies east of the prime meridian (0° longitude) and west of the 180th meridian. It is the counterpart of the Western Hemisphere. The Eastern Hemisphere contains most of Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia, and the majority of the world's population.

Geography

The dividing line between the hemispheres conventionally follows the prime meridian through Greenwich, London, and the 180th meridian in the Pacific Ocean. An alternative convention uses the 20° west and 160° east meridians so that no landmass is split; under this convention, Europe and mainland Africa fall wholly within the Eastern Hemisphere.

Usage

The term is also used culturally and historically to refer to the "Old World" of Europe, Asia and Africa, as distinct from the "New World" of the Americas.
