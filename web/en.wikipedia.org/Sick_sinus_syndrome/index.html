Sick sinus syndrome

Sick sinus syndrome (SSS), also known as sinus node dysfunction (SND), is a group of abnormal heart rhythms (arrhythmias) caused by a malfunction of the sinus node, the heart's primary pacemaker. It is largely a disease of older adults.

Manifestations include sinus bradycardia, sinus pauses or arrest, sinoatrial exit block, and the bradycardia-tachycardia syndrome, in which slow rhythms alternate with rapid ones such as atrial fibrillation. Symptoms may include fatigue, dizziness, palpitations, and syncope.

Treatment of symptomatic sick sinus syndrome typically involves implantation of a permanent pacemaker, which effectively relieves symptoms, along with management of any associated tachyarrhythmias.
