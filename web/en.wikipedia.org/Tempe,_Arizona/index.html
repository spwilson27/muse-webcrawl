Tempe is a city in Maricopa County, Arizona, United States, in the East Valley section of metropolitan Phoenix. It is named after the Vale of Tempe in Greece. With a population of about 180,000, Tempe is home to the main campus of Arizona State University, one of the largest public universities in the United States.

History

The area was settled by the Hohokam people centuries ago. Modern Tempe grew around Hayden's Ferry, a crossing of the Salt River established in the 1870s. The city was incorporated in 1894.

Geography and culture

Tempe is bordered by Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, and Chandler. Landmarks include Tempe Town Lake, "A" Mountain (Hayden Butte), and the Mill Avenue entertainment district. The city hosts events such as the Tempe Festival of the Arts.
