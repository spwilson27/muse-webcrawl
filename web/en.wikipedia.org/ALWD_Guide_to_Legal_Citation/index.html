The ALWD Guide to Legal Citation is a legal citation system for the United States compiled by the Association of Legal Writing Directors. First published in 2000 as the ALWD Citation Manual, it was created as a simpler, classroom-friendly alternative to The Bluebook.

Features

The Guide uses a single, consistent system for all types of legal documents, with clear rules, diagrams, and examples for citing cases, statutes, regulations, and secondary sources. Unlike The Bluebook, it uses one typeface convention and avoids the separate practitioner/scholarly formats.

Reception

It has been adopted by many law schools for legal writing courses, though The Bluebook remains dominant in law reviews and courts.
