Ngspice

Ngspice is a free and open-source mixed-level, mixed-signal circuit simulator. It is a successor of Berkeley SPICE 3f5, incorporating code from Cider and XSPICE, and is widely used for simulating analogue and digital electronic circuits.

Features

Ngspice performs DC, AC, transient, noise and other standard SPICE analyses, supports a large library of semiconductor device models (including BSIM MOSFET models), and can run in batch mode, interactively, or as a shared library embedded in other applications. It is the simulation engine bundled with the KiCad electronic design suite and underlies several other EDA tools. The software is developed by a volunteer community and released under free software licences.
