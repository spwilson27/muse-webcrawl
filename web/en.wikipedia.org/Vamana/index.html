Vamana

Vamana (Sanskrit: वामन, "Dwarf") is the fifth avatar of the Hindu god Vishnu and the first to appear fully in human form. He is also known as Trivikrama ("He of the Three Strides") and Upendra. The Vamana legend appears in the Rigveda and is developed in the Puranas, especially the Bhagavata Purana and the Vamana Purana.

The legend

The benevolent demon king Bali, grandson of Prahlada, had conquered the three worlds and displaced the gods. Vishnu was born as Vamana, a dwarf Brahmin, son of Aditi and Kashyapa. Approaching Bali during a sacrifice, Vamana asked only for as much land as he could cover in three paces. Despite the warning of his guru Shukra, Bali agreed.

Vamana then grew to cosmic size. With his first stride he covered the earth, with the second the heavens, and there was no room for the third. In humility, Bali offered his own head; Vamana placed his foot upon it and sent Bali down to rule Sutala, the netherworld, granting him immortality and the promise of becoming Indra in a future age.

Worship

The festival of Onam in Kerala celebrates Bali's annual return to visit his subjects. Vamana is depicted in temples such as the Ulagalantha Perumal Temple in Tamil Nadu.
