European Court of Justice

The European Court of Justice (ECJ), formally the Court of Justice of the European Union, is the supreme court of the European Union in matters of EU law. Established in 1952, it is based in Luxembourg.

Function

The Court interprets EU law to ensure its equal application across all EU member states, and settles legal disputes between national governments and EU institutions. It can also, in certain circumstances, be used by individuals, companies or organisations to take action against an EU institution.

Composition

The Court is composed of one judge per member state, assisted by Advocates-General. Judges are appointed for renewable six-year terms.
