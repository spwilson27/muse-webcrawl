Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020 video game)

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a 2020 flight simulation video game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Xbox Game Studios. It is an entry in the Microsoft Flight Simulator series, first released in 1982, and was released on August 18, 2020 for Windows, with an Xbox Series X/S version following in July 2021.

Gameplay and technology

The game simulates the entire Earth using satellite imagery and terrain data streamed from Bing Maps, enhanced by Azure cloud computing and procedural generation of buildings and vegetation. It models real-time weather and air traffic, and features detailed recreations of tens of thousands of airports. The base game includes 20 highly detailed aircraft and 37,000 airports.

Reception

The simulator was acclaimed by critics for its graphical fidelity and realism, winning Best Sim/Strategy Game at The Game Awards 2020. A sequel, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, was released in November 2024.
