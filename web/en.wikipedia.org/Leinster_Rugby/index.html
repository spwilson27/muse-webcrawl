Leinster Rugby

Leinster Rugby is one of the four professional provincial rugby union teams of Ireland, representing the province of Leinster. Based in Dublin, Leinster compete in the United Rugby Championship and the European Rugby Champions Cup, playing mainly at the RDS Arena and the Aviva Stadium.

History

Founded as a provincial side in the amateur era, Leinster became a powerhouse in the professional period, winning the European Cup/Champions Cup four times (2009, 2011, 2012 and 2018) and multiple league titles. Notable players include Brian O'Driscoll, Johnny Sexton and Tadhg Furlong. The team's academy is renowned for producing Ireland internationals.
