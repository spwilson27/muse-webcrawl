Mount Stanley

Elevation5,109 m (16,762 ft)
LocationUganda / DR Congo border
RangeRwenzori Mountains
First ascent1906, Duke of the Abruzzi's expedition

Mount Stanley is a massif in the Rwenzori Mountains on the border between Uganda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its highest summit, Margherita Peak at 5,109 metres, is the highest point of both countries and the third-highest peak in Africa, after Kilimanjaro and Mount Kenya. The massif carries several subsidiary summits — Alexandra, Albert, Savoia and Elena among them — and some of equatorial Africa's last glaciers.

Climbing history

The Rwenzori, identified with the legendary Mountains of the Moon, were mapped and climbed by the 1906 expedition of Prince Luigi Amedeo, Duke of the Abruzzi, whose party made the first ascent of Margherita Peak, named for Queen Margherita of Italy, and photographed the range's peaks and glaciers extensively.

Glaciers

Stanley's glaciers, like all those of the Rwenzori, have retreated drastically since 1906 — of the ice cover recorded by the Abruzzi expedition, only a small fraction remains, and studies project the range may lose its glaciers entirely within coming decades. The summit route now crosses the shrinking Margherita Glacier from the Elena Hut.
