Legendre polynomials

The Legendre polynomials Pn(x), named after Adrien-Marie Legendre, are a system of complete, orthogonal polynomials on the interval [−1, 1]. They are solutions of Legendre's differential equation and can be defined by Rodrigues' formula: Pn(x) = (1/2nn!) dn/dxn [(x²−1)n].

Properties

The first few are P0=1, P1=x, P2=½(3x²−1). They satisfy a three-term recurrence and orthogonality relation, and arise in physics wherever Laplace's equation is solved in spherical coordinates — gravitation, electrostatics and multipole expansions.
