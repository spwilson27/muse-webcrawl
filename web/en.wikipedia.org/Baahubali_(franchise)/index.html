Baahubali (franchise)

Created by: S. S. Rajamouli, K. V. Vijayendra Prasad
Original work: Baahubali: The Beginning (2015)
Country: India

Baahubali is an Indian media franchise created by K. V. Vijayendra Prasad and S. S. Rajamouli. It consists primarily of two epic action films directed by Rajamouli — Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017) — filmed simultaneously in Telugu and Tamil. The story follows the hero Amarendra Baahubali and his son Mahendra (Shivudu) in their struggle against the tyrant Bhallaladeva for the throne of the kingdom of Mahishmati.

The films

The first film ends on a famed cliffhanger — the loyal commander Kattappa reveals that he killed Amarendra Baahubali — spawning the popular question "Why did Kattappa kill Baahubali?" The second film, The Conclusion, became the highest-grossing Indian film of all time at its release, earning over ₹1,800 crore worldwide, and set records for Indian cinema in scale and box office. The films star Prabhas in the title roles, alongside Rana Daggubati, Anushka Shetty, Tamannaah Bhatia, Ramya Krishnan, and Sathyaraj.

Other media

The franchise includes the novel The Rise of Sivagami by Anand Neelakantan, the animated series Baahubali: The Lost Legends, comic books, and a planned live-action prequel series. The films are noted for their extensive visual effects and for demonstrating the pan-Indian appeal of regional-language cinema.
