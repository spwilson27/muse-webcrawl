Septum piercing

A septum piercing is a body piercing through the nasal septum — more precisely through the soft tissue (the "columella" or "sweet spot") just below the septal cartilage rather than through the cartilage itself. Common jewellery includes circular barbells (horseshoes), captive bead rings, and retainers that can be flipped up inside the nostrils to conceal the piercing.

History

Nose and septum piercings have long histories among Indigenous peoples of the Americas, the Aztecs and Maya, and various cultures in South Asia and the Pacific, where they could signify status, coming of age, or group identity. The septum piercing became popular in Western alternative and punk subcultures from the 1980s onward and entered mainstream fashion in the 2010s.

Procedure and healing

Initial healing typically takes about 6–8 weeks, with full healing up to six months. Standard gauges are 16G or 14G, and the piercing can be stretched.
