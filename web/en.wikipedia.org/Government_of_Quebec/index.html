Government of Quebec

The Government of Quebec (French: Gouvernement du Québec) is the body responsible for the administration of the Canadian province of Quebec. A constitutional monarchy, the Crown is the corporation sole, assuming distinct roles: the executive, as the Crown-in-Council; the legislature, as the Crown-in-Parliament; and the courts, as the Crown-on-the-Bench. Its powers are granted by the Constitution Act, 1867.

Country: Canada

Province: Quebec

Seat: Quebec City

Monarch: Charles III (represented by the Lieutenant Governor)

Head of government: Premier of Quebec

Legislature: National Assembly of Quebec

Executive

Executive authority is formally vested in the Crown and exercised by the Lieutenant Governor on the advice of the Executive Council (cabinet), led by the Premier. In practice the Premier and cabinet govern. Key provincial institutions include Hydro-Québec, the provincially owned electricity utility, and the Caisse de dépôt et placement, which manages public pension funds.

Legislature

The Legislature of Quebec consists of the Lieutenant Governor and the National Assembly of Quebec, a unicameral body of 125 members elected from single-member districts. (The upper house, the Legislative Council, was abolished in 1968.) The legislature sits at the Parliament Building in Quebec City.

Judiciary

Quebec's courts include the Court of Appeal of Quebec (the province's highest court), the Superior Court, and the Court of Québec. Uniquely in Canada, Quebec private law is governed by the civil law tradition, codified in the Civil Code of Québec, while public and criminal law follow the common law framework of the federal system.

Jurisdiction

Under the Canadian constitution, Quebec has jurisdiction over areas including health, education, municipal institutions, property and civil rights, natural resources, and language policy; the Charter of the French Language (1977) makes French the province's official language. Quebec held referendums on sovereignty in 1980 and 1995, both of which were defeated.
