Victoria Beckham

Victoria Caroline Beckham (née Adams)
Born: 17 April 1974, Harlow, Essex, England
Occupations: fashion designer, singer, television personality
Spouse: David Beckham (m. 1999)

Victoria Caroline Beckham (born 17 April 1974) is an English fashion designer, singer and television personality. She rose to fame in the 1990s as "Posh Spice", a member of the Spice Girls, one of the best-selling girl groups of all time. After the group's hiatus she pursued a solo music career before reinventing herself as a fashion designer.

Fashion career

Beckham launched her eponymous label with a collection of dresses for spring/summer 2009, shown at New York Fashion Week. The brand grew to encompass ready-to-wear, eyewear, accessories and a diffusion line, VVB, and a beauty line, Victoria Beckham Beauty, launched in 2019. Though critically acclaimed — winning Designer Brand of the Year at the British Fashion Awards in 2011 — the business reported losses for many years before moving towards profitability in the 2020s.

Personal life

She married footballer David Beckham in 1999; the couple, dubbed "Posh and Becks", have four children: Brooklyn, Romeo, Cruz and Harper. She was appointed OBE in 2017 for services to the fashion industry.
