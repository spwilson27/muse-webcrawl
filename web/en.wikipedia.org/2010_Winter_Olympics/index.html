The 2010 Winter Olympics, officially the XXI Olympic Winter Games, were held from 12 to 28 February 2010 in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, with some events held in the resort town of Whistler. Around 2,600 athletes from 82 nations participated in 86 events in 15 disciplines.

Highlights

Canada won 14 gold medals, the most by a host nation at a Winter Games, including a victory over the United States in the men's ice hockey final, decided by Sidney Crosby's overtime "golden goal". The United States won the most medals overall with 37.

Opening day tragedy

The Games were marred on the opening day by the death of Georgian luger Nodar Kumaritashvili in a training crash at the Whistler Sliding Centre, which led to modifications of the track for safety.

Legacy

Venues such as the Richmond Olympic Oval and the Whistler Olympic Park remained in use after the Games. The Vancouver Olympics were credited with a surge of national pride in Canada and set audience records for winter sports broadcasts.
