Down (band)

Origin: New Orleans, Louisiana, U.S.
Genres: Sludge metal, southern metal, stoner metal
Years active: 1991–present
Members include: Phil Anselmo, Pepper Keenan, Kirk Windstein, Jimmy Bower

Down is an American heavy metal supergroup that formed in New Orleans, Louisiana, in 1991. The band's current line-up consists of vocalist Phil Anselmo (Pantera and Superjoint), guitarist Pepper Keenan (Corrosion of Conformity), guitarist Kirk Windstein (Crowbar), bassist Pat Bruders, and drummer Jimmy Bower (Eyehategod).

History

Down released its debut album, NOLA, in 1995, which drew on the members' shared love of Black Sabbath, Saint Vitus, and southern rock, and is regarded as a landmark of sludge metal. Follow-up albums include Down II: A Bustle in Your Hedgerow... (2002) and Down III: Over the Under (2007). Between 2012 and 2014 the band released the two-part Down IV series of EPs.

Musical style

Down's sound blends the slow, heavy riffs of sludge and doom metal with blues and southern rock influences, reflecting the musical heritage of New Orleans and the members' various parent bands.
