Festool is a German manufacturer of high-end power tools, based in Wendlingen. The company traces its roots to Festo, founded in 1925 by Albert Fezer and Gottlieb Stoll; the tool division became an independent brand under the name Festool in 2000, within the TTS Tooltechnic Systems holding.

Products

Festool is known for system-integrated tools aimed at professional woodworkers, painters, and cabinetmakers, emphasizing dust extraction, precision, and durability. Signature products include the TS plunge-cut track saws used with guide rails, the Domino biscuit/dowel joiner (introduced 2006), Rotex and ETS sanders, routers, drills, and dust extractors. Most tools are delivered in stackable "Systainer" cases designed to interlock for transport and storage.

Market position

Festool tools command premium prices and are sold mainly through specialist dealers rather than mass retailers. The company manufactures primarily in Germany and the Czech Republic. Its emphasis on integrated dust collection anticipated tightening occupational health rules on wood and silica dust.
