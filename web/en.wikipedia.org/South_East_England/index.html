South East England

South East England is one of the nine official regions of England. It consists of the counties of Buckinghamshire, East Sussex, Hampshire, the Isle of Wight, Kent, Oxfordshire, Berkshire, Surrey and West Sussex. It is the most populous of the nine English regions, with a population of over nine million.

Geography

The region borders London to the north-east, with which it shares a close economic relationship; many of its towns form part of the London commuter belt. The coastline runs along the English Channel, and the region includes the North Downs and South Downs chalk ranges, the New Forest, and the Chiltern Hills.

Major settlements

Major cities and towns include Southampton, Portsmouth, Brighton and Hove, Oxford, Reading, Milton Keynes, Canterbury, Guildford, Winchester, Chichester, and Dover. The region's largest port is Southampton, a major centre for cruise ships and container shipping.

Economy

The South East is one of the most prosperous regions of the United Kingdom, with strong sectors in finance, technology, pharmaceuticals, and defence. It hosts Gatwick Airport, the second busiest airport in the UK, and the Channel ports of Dover and Portsmouth.

Politics

The region is traditionally a stronghold of the Conservative Party, though recent elections have seen gains by the Liberal Democrats and Labour in urban areas. There is no regional assembly; local government is provided by county and unitary councils.
