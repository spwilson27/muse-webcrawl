Allan Hills 84001 (ALH84001) is a fragment of a Martian meteorite found in the Allan Hills of Antarctica on December 27, 1984, by a team of American meteorite hunters from the ANSMET project.

Claimed fossil evidence

In 1996 a team led by David McKay announced that the meteorite contained structures and chemical signatures that might be fossil evidence of ancient Martian bacteria, including carbonate globules, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, magnetite crystals, and tube-like forms resembling nanobacteria. The claim prompted worldwide attention and a statement by U.S. President Bill Clinton.

Assessment

Subsequent studies showed that the features could be explained by non-biological processes, and the scientific consensus is that ALH84001 does not provide conclusive evidence of past life on Mars, though it remains an intensively studied specimen.
