ChatGPT

ChatGPT is a generative artificial intelligence chatbot developed by OpenAI. Launched on November 30, 2022, it is built on large language models (initially GPT-3.5, later GPT-4 and successors) and can converse, answer questions, write text and code, summarize, and perform many language tasks.

ChatGPT reached an estimated 100 million monthly active users within two months, making it the fastest-growing consumer application in history at the time. It sparked widespread public attention, investment, and debate about AI's effects on education, work, and misinformation.

The model is trained on large text corpora and fine-tuned with reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF). It is offered in free and subscription (Plus, Team, Enterprise) tiers, with features including web browsing, image and voice interaction, and custom GPTs.

Criticisms include hallucinations (confident-sounding falsehoods), bias, and use in academic dishonesty. OpenAI is based in San Francisco and led by Sam Altman.
