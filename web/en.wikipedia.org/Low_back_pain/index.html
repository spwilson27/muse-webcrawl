Low back pain

Low back pain is pain felt in the lower back, between the lower rib cage and the buttocks. It is extremely common — most adults experience it at some point — and is a leading cause of disability and missed work worldwide.

Causes and types

Most cases are "non-specific" or mechanical, related to muscles, ligaments, discs and facet joints, and resolve within weeks. A minority arise from specific causes such as disc herniation with nerve root compression (sciatica), spinal stenosis, vertebral fracture, infection or malignancy; new bladder or bowel dysfunction, saddle anaesthesia or progressive leg weakness are red flags requiring urgent assessment.

Management

Guidelines recommend staying active, reassurance, simple analgesics and physical therapies for most cases, reserving imaging and surgery for selected patients. Prevention focuses on exercise and avoiding prolonged immobility.
