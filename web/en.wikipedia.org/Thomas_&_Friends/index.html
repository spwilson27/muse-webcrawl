Thomas & Friends (originally known as Thomas the Tank Engine & Friends) is a British children's television series created by Britt Allcroft. Based on The Railway Series books by the Reverend W. Awdry and his son Christopher, it follows the adventures of Thomas, an anthropomorphised blue steam tank engine, and his friends on the fictional Island of Sodor, where the engines work on the North Western Railway under the direction of the Fat Controller, Sir Topham Hatt.

History

The series was first broadcast on ITV in the United Kingdom on 9 October 1984. Allcroft had acquired the television rights to Awdry's books in 1979 and, working with producer David Mitton, developed a live-action model series filmed at Shepperton Studios. The first two series adapted stories directly from the books; later series introduced original storylines and new characters such as Emily, Spencer and Hiro.

The show was narrated for many years by former Beatles drummer Ringo Starr, who served as storyteller for the first two series and helped popularise the programme in the United States through the spin-off Shining Time Station. Other narrators included George Carlin, Alec Baldwin, Michael Brandon and Mark Moraghan.

Production changes

Originally produced with live-action models, the series moved to a hybrid of models and CGI in its twelfth series (2008) and to full computer animation from the thirteenth series, Hero of the Rails (2009), produced by Nitrogen Studios and later by Arc Productions and Jam Filled Toronto. In 2018 the series was revamped as Thomas & Friends: Big World! Big Adventures!, adding international settings and female lead characters including Nia from Kenya and Rebecca.

Legacy

The original series ended in 2021 after 24 series and 584 episodes, and was succeeded by the animated reboot Thomas & Friends: All Engines Go. The franchise has generated extensive merchandising, feature-length specials and stage adaptations, and Thomas himself has become one of the most recognisable characters in children's television worldwide.
