Barsoom

Barsoom is a fictional representation of the planet Mars created by American pulp fiction author Edgar Rice Burroughs. The world first appeared in A Princess of Mars (1912) and features prominently in the eleven-volume Barsoom series.

Setting

Barsoom is a dying world of red deserts, dead sea bottoms, canal cities and warring races, including the tall, four-armed green Martians and the human-like red Martians.

Characters

Earthman John Carter is transported to Barsoom and becomes a hero there; other central figures include Dejah Thoris, princess of Helium, and the green warrior Tars Tarkas.

Influence

The series influenced generations of science fiction and science figures, and helped popularise the planetary romance genre.
