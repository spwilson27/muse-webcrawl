Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine

Saint-Ouen-sur-Seine (called Saint-Ouen until 2018) is a commune in the Seine-Saint-Denis department in the northern suburbs of Paris, France, located 6.6 kilometres (4.1 mi) from the centre of Paris. It borders the 17th and 18th arrondissements of the capital.

History and landmarks

The town is named for Saint Ouen (Audoenus), a 7th-century bishop of Rouen. Saint-Ouen is best known for the Marché aux Puces de Saint-Ouen, founded in the late 19th century and considered one of the largest antique and flea markets in the world, attracting millions of visitors annually. The Château de Saint-Ouen and the Stade Bauer, home of Red Star FC, are also notable landmarks.

Recent development

The commune hosted the Olympic Village for the Paris 2024 Olympic Games (shared with Saint-Denis and L'Île-Saint-Denis), and has undergone significant redevelopment, including the extension of Métro Line 14.
