PASMO

PASMO (パスモ) is a rechargeable contactless smart card electronic money system used in Japan, primarily for public transport in the Tokyo metropolitan area. It was introduced on March 18, 2007, by the PASMO Co., Ltd. consortium of railway and bus operators.

Overview

Based on Sony's FeliCa technology, PASMO is interoperable with JR East's Suica, and since the nationwide mutual-use agreement of 2013 it can be used on most major transit IC systems across Japan. Cards are available anonymously or registered to a named individual, and a Mobile PASMO service for smartphones was launched in 2020.

Usage

Beyond transit fares, PASMO functions as electronic money at convenience stores, vending machines, and other participating merchants.
