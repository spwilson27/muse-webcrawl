Strategy video game

SubgenresReal-time strategy, turn-based strategy, 4X, tower defence, MOBA
Notable titlesDune II, Civilization, StarCraft, Age of Empires

A strategy video game is a genre of video game emphasising careful planning, resource management and tactical or strategic decision-making to achieve victory, usually by commanding units or building an economy and military rather than through direct reflex-based action.

Subgenres

In real-time strategy (RTS) games such as StarCraft and Age of Empires, all players act simultaneously, while turn-based strategy (TBS) games such as Civilization alternate moves. The 4X subgenre — explore, expand, exploit, exterminate — blends empire building with conquest; other offshoots include tower defence, grand strategy and the multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA).

History

The genre grew from board and tabletop wargames; Dune II (1992) established the RTS template of harvesting, base-building and unit production, and strategy games remain a staple particularly on personal computers.
