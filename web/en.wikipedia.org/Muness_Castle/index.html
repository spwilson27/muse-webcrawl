Muness Castle

Muness Castle is a ruined castle on the island of Unst, in Shetland, Scotland. It is the most northerly castle in the United Kingdom. Built in 1598 by Laurence Bruce of Cultmalindie, half-brother of Robert Stewart, Earl of Orkney, it is a small Z-plan tower house standing near the village of Uyeasound.

An inscription above the door asks visitors to neither harm the building nor its stones. Bruce, who had become factor of Shetland, quarrelled with his half-brother Patrick Stewart, 2nd Earl of Orkney. In August 1608 Earl Patrick's men attacked and burned Muness, one episode in the feud between the Stewart earls and Bruce.

Later history

The castle was sold in 1718 and gradually fell into ruin, losing its roof. Today it is in the care of Historic Environment Scotland and open to visitors. Despite its ruined state, the building retains much of its character, including corbelled turrets at diagonally opposite corners and a vaulted ground floor.
