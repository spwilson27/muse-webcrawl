Chiquita Brands International


Chiquita bananas

Chiquita Brands International S.à.r.l., formerly known as United Fruit Company and United Brands, is a producer and distributor of bananas and other produce, famous for its Chiquita brand. The company was formed from the merger in 1899 of Minor C. Keith's banana-trading concerns with the Boston Fruit Company. For much of the 20th century, as the United Fruit Company, it was a dominant force in the banana trade of Central America, with extensive landholdings, railways and political influence that made it emblematic of the "banana republic".

History

United Fruit's operations in Guatemala, Honduras, Colombia and elsewhere drew sustained criticism for labour practices and for its influence over governments. The company was deeply implicated in the politics that preceded the 1954 Guatemalan coup d'état; its lobbyists and shareholders were connected to the US officials who authorised the operation against President Jacobo Árbenz, whose land reforms had expropriated uncultivated United Fruit land. In Colombia, troops massacred striking United Fruit workers in the Banana Massacre of 1928, an event later fictionalised by Gabriel García Márquez in One Hundred Years of Solitude.

After a series of mergers and a 1975 bribery scandal in Honduras (the "Bananagate" affair), the company became United Brands and in 1990 adopted the Chiquita name. The European Union's banana import regime, favouring former colonies over Latin American "dollar bananas", triggered the long-running "banana wars" trade dispute of the 1990s.

Recent developments

In 2007 Chiquita pleaded guilty in the United States to making payments to a designated Colombian terrorist organisation, the AUC, and paid a $25 million fine. In 2014–2015 the company was acquired by the Brazilian Cutrale and Safra groups and taken private; its headquarters functions moved to Switzerland, with operational offices in Florida. Chiquita remains one of the world's largest banana companies, marketing bananas, pineapples and packaged salads.
