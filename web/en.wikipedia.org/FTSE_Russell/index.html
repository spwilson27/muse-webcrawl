FTSE Russell
Type: Subsidiary of London Stock Exchange Group
Industry: Financial indexes
Known for: FTSE 100, Russell 2000

FTSE Russell is a British-American provider of stock market indices, benchmarks and related data services, owned by the London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG). It was formed by combining the FTSE Group, originally a joint venture between the Financial Times and the London Stock Exchange, with the Russell index business acquired from Northwestern Mutual in 2014–2015.

Products

The company's best-known indices include the FTSE 100 Index of the largest companies listed in London, the FTSE 250, the FTSE All-Share, and the US Russell 1000 and Russell 2000 indices, the latter a widely used benchmark for small-cap stocks. FTSE Russell also produces global equity indices (the FTSE Global Equity Index Series), fixed-income indices (including the former Citigroup World Government Bond Index), and ESG and climate indices. Trillions of dollars of assets are benchmarked to its indices.

Index events

The annual reconstitution of the Russell indices each June ("Russell reconstitution") is one of the busiest trading events of the year in US markets, as index funds adjust their holdings to match additions and deletions. Quarterly reviews similarly reshuffle the FTSE UK index series.
