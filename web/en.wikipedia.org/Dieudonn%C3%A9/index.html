Dieudonné

Dieudonné is a French name meaning "given by God" (equivalent to the Latin Deodatus and the English-language surname Deusdedit). It may refer to several people and a surname borne notably by the French comedian Dieudonné M'bala M'bala.

Dieudonné M'bala M'bala

Dieudonné M'bala M'bala (born 11 February 1966 in Fontenay-aux-Roses), known mononymously as Dieudonné, is a French comedian, actor, and political activist. Initially successful in a double act with Élie Semoun and then as a solo performer, he later became a highly controversial figure in France: his performances were repeatedly condemned, and several shows were banned, over material that courts and critics judged antisemitic. He popularised the "quenelle" gesture, which French authorities have treated as an inverted Nazi salute, and he has been convicted multiple times for incitement and related offences.

Other uses

The name is also borne by the mathematician Jean Dieudonné (1906–1992), a founding member of the Bourbaki group, and appears as a given name for several medieval and early-modern figures.
