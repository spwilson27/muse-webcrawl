The Republican National Convention (RNC) is the quadrennial presidential nominating convention of the United States Republican Party. Convened by the Republican National Committee, it formally selects the party's nominees for president and vice president of the United States, adopts the party platform and rules, and serves as the ceremonial launch of the general-election campaign.

History

The first Republican National Convention was held in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in February 1856, followed by a formal nominating convention in Philadelphia that June, which nominated John C. Frémont. In the era before presidential primaries became widespread in the twentieth century, conventions were genuine deliberative bodies: delegates were largely uncommitted, and multi-ballot contests were common — the 1920 convention famously selected Warren G. Harding after deadlock, in what was called a "smoke-filled room" decision.

The last convention to require more than one ballot to pick the presidential nominee was 1948 (Thomas E. Dewey, three ballots); the 1976 convention in Kansas City was the last genuinely contested one, with Ronald Reagan narrowly losing to incumbent Gerald Ford. Since the primary reforms of the 1970s, conventions have ratified results determined in primaries and caucuses.

Delegates and voting

Delegates are apportioned to states and territories based on population and Republican electoral success, and are selected through state primaries, caucuses, and state conventions. Most are bound, on the first ballot, to candidates according to primary results under state and party rules. The convention's proceedings — presided over by a permanent chair, traditionally the Speaker of the House when the party holds it — culminate in a state-by-state roll call vote and the nominee's acceptance speech.

Recent conventions

Recent conventions have been held in St. Paul (2008), Tampa (2012), Cleveland (2016), Charlotte and Washington, D.C. (2020, scaled back during the COVID-19 pandemic), and Milwaukee (2024). The 2016 and 2024 conventions nominated Donald Trump; the 2024 convention in Milwaukee nominated him with JD Vance as his running mate.
