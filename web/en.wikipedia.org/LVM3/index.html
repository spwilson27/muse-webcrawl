LVM3

The LVM3, previously known as the Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle Mark III (GSLV Mk III), is a medium-lift launch vehicle developed by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

Design

The three-stage vehicle uses two S200 solid strap-on boosters, an L110 liquid core stage with Vikas engines, and a C25 cryogenic upper stage powered by the CE-20 engine. It can place about 8,000 kg into low Earth orbit and about 4,000 kg into geostationary transfer orbit.

History

Its first experimental flight was in December 2014, carrying the CARE crew module test; the first orbital launch followed in 2017. LVM3 launched the Chandrayaan-2 and Chandrayaan-3 lunar missions and OneWeb satellite batches, and a human-rated version is being prepared for the Gaganyaan programme.
