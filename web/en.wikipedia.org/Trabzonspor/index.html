Trabzonspor Kulübü is a Turkish professional sports club located in the city of Trabzon. Formed in 1967 through a merger of several local clubs, its football team is one of the most successful in Turkey, notable as the first club from outside Istanbul to win the national league championship, a feat it achieved in 1975–76 — an era in which the club won six league titles in nine seasons.

History

Football had been played in Trabzon since the early 20th century, with clubs such as İdmanocağı and İdmangücü dominating the local scene. After the Turkish Football Federation pressed provincial cities to form unified professional clubs, Trabzonspor was founded on 2 August 1967 and adopted claret and blue as its colours. Promoted to the First League in 1974, the club won championships in 1976, 1977, 1979, 1980, 1981 and 1984 under managers including Ahmet Suat Özyazıcı and Özkan Sümer, breaking the monopoly of Istanbul's "big three" (Fenerbahçe, Galatasaray and Beşiktaş).

Later years and honours

Trabzonspor has also won multiple Turkish Cups and produced leading players such as Şenol Güneş, the goalkeeper who later managed the Turkish national team. After decades of near misses, the club ended its long title drought by winning the Süper Lig again in 2021–22, sparking mass celebrations in Trabzon. Its home is the Papara Park (opened 2016 as the Şenol Güneş Sports Complex), which replaced the historic Hüseyin Avni Aker Stadium. The club also fields teams in other sports and maintains a passionate regional fan base across the Black Sea region and the Turkish diaspora.
