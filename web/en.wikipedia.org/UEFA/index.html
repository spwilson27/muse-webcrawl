The Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) is one of six continental bodies of governance in association football. It governs football, futsal and beach football in Europe, as well as in transcontinental countries such as Turkey, Russia, and Kazakhstan. UEFA consists of 55 national association members.

UEFA was founded on 15 June 1954 in Basel, Switzerland, after consultation between the Italian, French, and Belgian associations. Its headquarters moved to Bern and, in 1995, to Nyon, Switzerland, on the shore of Lake Geneva.

Competitions

UEFA organizes the UEFA European Championship ("the Euros") for national teams, first held in 1960, and the UEFA Nations League. Its flagship club competitions are the UEFA Champions League (founded 1955 as the European Cup), the UEFA Europa League, and the UEFA Conference League, plus the UEFA Women's Champions League and the UEFA Futsal Champions League. The Champions League is the most prestigious club tournament in world football and one of the most valuable sporting properties.

Governance

UEFA is led by a president and an Executive Committee elected by its Congress of member associations. Since the Platini era, UEFA has enforced Financial Fair Play regulations (now "financial sustainability" rules) on clubs, and it operates the "solidarity" system that distributes competition revenue to smaller associations and grassroots football. Russia's member associations and teams were suspended from UEFA competitions following the 2022 invasion of Ukraine.
