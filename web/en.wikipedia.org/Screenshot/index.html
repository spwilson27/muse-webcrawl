Screenshot

A screenshot (also known as a screencap or screen grab) is a digital image that shows the contents of a computer display. A screenshot can be created by the operating system or by dedicated software, and may capture the entire screen, a single window, or a user-selected region.

Techniques

Most operating systems include built-in screenshot functions activated by keyboard shortcuts, such as the Print Screen key on Windows or Command-Shift combinations on macOS. Modern systems also provide graphical tools for selecting a region, delaying capture, or recording the screen as video (screencasting). Mobile devices typically capture screenshots with a combination of hardware buttons.

Uses

Screenshots are used to demonstrate software, document errors for technical support, archive content that may change or be deleted, and share visual information in communication. They are a standard feature of software documentation, tutorials, and video game culture.

Legal and technical issues

A screenshot of copyrighted material may itself implicate copyright in the underlying work, though such use is often covered by fair use or fair dealing. Some streaming services apply digital rights management that blocks or blacks out screenshots of protected video content.
