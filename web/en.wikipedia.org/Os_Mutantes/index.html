Os Mutantes

Os Mutantes ("The Mutants") are a Brazilian rock band formed in São Paulo in 1966 by brothers Arnaldo and Sérgio Dias Baptista with singer Rita Lee. They were central figures of the Tropicália movement, fusing psychedelic rock with Brazilian popular music, concrete poetry, and avant-garde experimentation.

Career

Their self-titled debut album (1968), recorded alongside Gilberto Gil and Caetano Veloso, is considered a landmark of Brazilian music. After Lee's departure in 1972 the band moved toward progressive rock before dissolving in 1978. Long cult figures in Brazil, they were rediscovered internationally in the 1990s—David Byrne's Luaka Bop label issued the compilation Everything Is Possible!—and influenced artists such as Kurt Cobain and Beck. The band reunited in 2006 and released the album Haih Or Amortecedor in 2009.
