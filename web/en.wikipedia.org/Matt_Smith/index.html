Matt Smith

Matthew Robert Smith (born 28 October 1982) is an English actor. He is best known for his roles as the Eleventh Doctor in the BBC science fiction television series Doctor Who (2010–2013), Prince Philip in the Netflix royal drama The Crown (2016–2017), and Daemon Targaryen in the HBO fantasy series House of the Dragon (2022–present).

Smith initially aspired to be a professional footballer, but a back injury forced him to give up the sport. After joining the National Youth Theatre and studying drama and creative writing at the University of East Anglia, he became an actor in 2003, performing in London theatre productions including Murder in the Cathedral, Fresh Kills, The History Boys, and On the Shore of the Wide World.

Doctor Who

In January 2009, Smith was revealed to be the actor cast as the Eleventh Doctor, succeeding David Tennant. At 26, he was the youngest actor to play the Doctor at the time of his casting. His portrayal, characterised by a bow tie, tweed jacket, boundless energy, and an old soul in a young body, earned critical acclaim and a BAFTA nomination for Best Actor in 2011. He departed the role in the 2013 Christmas special "The Time of the Doctor".

Later career

Smith played Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh, opposite Claire Foy's Queen Elizabeth II in the first two seasons of The Crown, receiving a Primetime Emmy Award nomination. His film roles include Terminator Genisys (2015), Last Night in Soho (2021), and Morbius (2022). Since 2022 he has played the rogue prince Daemon Targaryen in House of the Dragon, the Game of Thrones prequel.
