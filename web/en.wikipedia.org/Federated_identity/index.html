Federated identity

A federated identity in information technology is the means of linking a person's electronic identity and attributes, stored across multiple distinct identity management systems. Federated identity is related to single sign-on (SSO), in which a user's single authentication token is trusted across multiple systems.

Standards and use

Federation relies on standards such as SAML, OpenID Connect and OAuth, through which an identity provider authenticates the user and a relying party trusts that assertion without handling the user's credentials directly. It is widely used in enterprise cross-domain access, academic federations such as eduroam and research-and-education identity federations, and consumer "sign in with" services.

Advantages and risks

Federation reduces password fatigue and duplicate account provisioning, but concentrates risk: compromise of an identity provider can expose access to all connected services, and trust between parties must be carefully managed.
