Leni Riefenstahl

Leni Riefenstahl (1902–2003) was a German film director, photographer and actress, celebrated for the technical brilliance of her films and condemned for the Nazi propaganda they embodied, above all Triumph of the Will (1935) and Olympia (1938).

Career

Riefenstahl began as a dancer and actress in Arnold Fanck mountain films, then directed The Blue Light (1932), which brought her to Hitler attention. Triumph of the Will, filming the 1934 Nuremberg rally, and Olympia, on the 1936 Berlin Olympics, pioneered camera techniques — tracking shots, telephoto lenses, underwater photography — that reshaped documentary film.

After the war

Arrested and classified as a fellow traveller rather than a war criminal, she was never convicted but remained a pariah. She reinvented herself as a photographer of the Nuba people of Sudan and as an underwater photographer, working into her nineties and dying at 101.
