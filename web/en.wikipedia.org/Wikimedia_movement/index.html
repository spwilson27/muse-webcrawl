Wikimedia movement

The Wikimedia movement is the global community of contributors to the Wikimedia projects, together with the organisations that support them: principally the Wikimedia Foundation, based in San Francisco, and a network of national chapters, thematic organisations, and user groups. Its best-known project is Wikipedia, the free online encyclopedia launched in 2001 by Jimmy Wales and Larry Sanger.

Projects

The movement maintains a family of free-knowledge projects, including Wikipedia, Wiktionary, Wikibooks, Wikiquote, Wikisource, Wikivoyage, Wikinews, Wikiversity, Wikispecies, and Wikifunctions, as well as the shared repositories Wikimedia Commons (media) and Wikidata (structured data). The software underlying the projects, MediaWiki, is free and open source.

Organisations

The Wikimedia Foundation, founded in 2003, is a nonprofit organisation that operates the projects' servers, owns the trademarks, and raises most of the movement's funding through small donations. Around forty national chapters and numerous user groups support outreach, education, and cultural partnerships in their regions. The annual conference of the movement is Wikimania, first held in 2005.

Values and governance

The movement is guided by a commitment to free knowledge: content is published under free licences such as Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike, and anyone may edit most pages. Governance is highly decentralised, with editorial policies set by each project's volunteer community. Strategic debates — over editor retention, diversity, monetisation, and, in the 2020s, a proposed movement charter — are ongoing features of movement life.
