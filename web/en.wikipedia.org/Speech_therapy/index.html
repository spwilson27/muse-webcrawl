Speech therapy

Speech therapy is the assessment and treatment of communication problems and speech disorders. It is performed by speech-language pathologists (SLPs), often called speech therapists. Therapy techniques are used to improve communication, including articulation therapy, language intervention activities, and exercises for oral-motor and swallowing difficulties.

Areas of practice

Speech therapists work with children with developmental delays, people recovering from stroke or brain injury, adults with voice disorders, and individuals who stutter. They also treat dysphagia (swallowing disorders) and help users of augmentative and alternative communication devices.

Techniques

Common approaches include modelling and repetition of target sounds, language stimulation through play and conversation, fluency-shaping techniques for stuttering, and voice therapy. Early intervention in childhood is associated with better outcomes.
