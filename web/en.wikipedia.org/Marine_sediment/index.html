Marine sediment

Marine sediment, or ocean sediment, is any deposit of insoluble material, primarily rock and soil particles, transported from land areas to the ocean, as well as the remains of marine organisms, products of submarine volcanism, chemical precipitates from seawater, and materials from outer space.

Sediments are classified by origin as lithogenous (from rock), biogenous (from organisms, such as calcareous and siliceous oozes), hydrogenous (precipitated from seawater, such as manganese nodules), and cosmogenous (from space).

Deep-sea sediment cores provide records of past climates used in paleoceanography.
