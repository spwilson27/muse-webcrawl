Legionellosis

Legionellosis is a form of bacterial infection caused by species of Legionella, most often Legionella pneumophila, acquired by inhaling contaminated water aerosols from sources such as cooling towers, hot tubs and plumbing systems.

Forms

It ranges from the severe pneumonia known as Legionnaires' disease to the milder, flu-like Pontiac fever. Legionnaires' disease was first recognised after a 1976 outbreak at an American Legion convention in Philadelphia.

Treatment and prevention

Treatment is with antibiotics such as macrolides or fluoroquinolones; prevention centres on maintaining and disinfecting water systems to control bacterial growth.
