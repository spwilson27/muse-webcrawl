The Astronomical Almanac

The Astronomical Almanac is an almanac published by the United States Naval Observatory (USNO) and Her Majesty's Nautical Almanac Office (HMNAO); it also includes data supplied by many other organisations. It is considered a worldwide resource for fundamental astronomical data.

The Astronomical Almanac is published annually, two years in advance.

Contents

The almanac contains ephemerides of the Sun, Moon, planets, and bright stars; times of sunrise, sunset, moonrise, moonset, twilight, and eclipses; positions of the planets' satellites; and reference data on time scales, coordinate systems, and astronomical constants. It is used by professional and amateur astronomers, navigators, surveyors, and calendar makers.

History

The current title dates from 1981, when the British and American ephemerides — the British Nautical Almanac and Astronomical Ephemeris and the American Ephemeris and Nautical Almanac — were unified under a common title, continuing a collaboration begun in 1960. The British series traces back to the first Nautical Almanac published for the year 1767 under Nevil Maskelyne.
