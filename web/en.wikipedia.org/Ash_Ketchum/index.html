Ash Ketchum, known as Satoshi in Japan, is a fictional character in Nintendo, Game Freak and Creatures' Pokémon franchise. He was the main protagonist of the Pokémon anime television series from its debut in 1997 until 2023, a ten-year-old Pokémon Trainer from Pallet Town whose goal is to become a Pokémon Master.

Character

Ash begins his journey with a reluctant Pikachu given to him by Professor Oak. Travelling through successive regions of the Pokémon world with a changing cast of companions, he catches and trains Pokémon, earns gym badges and competes in regional leagues. For many years the character remained ten years old despite the length of the series.

Championship and departure

After many near misses, Ash won his first regional league in Alola and then defeated Leon to win the Pokémon World Coronation Series in the episode arc concluding in 2022, becoming the world champion ("Monarch"). His journey as protagonist ended with the series Pokémon: Aim to Be a Pokémon Master in 2023, after which the anime continued with new lead characters in Pokémon Horizons.

Reception

Ash is one of the most recognisable characters in Japanese popular culture worldwide. His long-running quest and eventual world championship victory attracted wide media attention, with the win trending internationally.
