Dutch football league system

The Dutch football league system is a series of interconnected leagues for association football clubs in the Netherlands. The system has a hierarchical format with promotion and relegation between leagues at different levels, which was introduced in the 2016–17 season after years of debate.

Structure

The professional pyramid is headed by the Eredivisie, the top flight of Dutch football, contested by 18 clubs including historic sides such as Ajax, PSV Eindhoven and Feyenoord. Below it sits the Eerste Divisie, the second tier, which includes reserve ("Jong") teams of the big clubs. The third tier, the Tweede Divisie, forms a bridge to the amateur game.

Amateur leagues

Below the professional divisions the KNVB organises amateur football through the Derde Divisie, Hoofdklasse and a descending series of district classes (Eerste Klasse down to Vijfde Klasse). Historically Saturday and Sunday amateur football were kept strictly separate, reflecting the religious traditions of Dutch society, although the distinction has been relaxed in recent years.

Promotion and relegation

Since 2016–17, promotion and relegation between the second and third tiers has operated on a limited basis. Champions of the Tweede Divisie may be promoted to the Eerste Divisie, subject to licensing requirements, while the lowest-placed clubs in the Eerste Divisie can be relegated.
