NBC News

NBC News
Type: Division of NBCUniversal
Founded: February 21, 1940
Headquarters: 30 Rockefeller Plaza, New York City
Parent: NBCUniversal News Group
Key programs: NBC Nightly News, Today, Meet the Press, Dateline

NBC News is the news division of the American broadcast television network NBC, a subsidiary of Comcast's NBCUniversal. The division is responsible for the network's newscasts and news programming, and operates alongside cable channels MSNBC and CNBC within the NBCUniversal News Group.

History

NBC's news operation traces to the network's radio era. Its first regularly scheduled television newscast began in 1948 as the Camel Newsreel Theatre, soon replaced by the Camel News Caravan anchored by John Cameron Swayze. In 1956 the Huntley-Brinkley Report, with Chet Huntley in New York and David Brinkley in Washington, became the dominant American evening newscast. The broadcast was renamed NBC Nightly News in 1970 and has been anchored by Tom Brokaw and later Lester Holt, among others.

Programs

The morning program Today, launched in 1952, pioneered the breakfast television format. Meet the Press, which debuted in 1947, is the longest-running program in American television history. The newsmagazine Dateline premiered in 1992. NBC News also produces Nightly News, Weekend Today, and extensive special-events and election coverage.

Digital and international

NBC News operates NBCNews.com, the streaming service NBC News Now, and shares newsgathering with Britain's Sky News following Comcast's acquisition of Sky. Its correspondents are based in bureaus around the world. See also NBC and MSNBC.
