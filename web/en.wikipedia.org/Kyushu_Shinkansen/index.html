The Kyushu Shinkansen is a Japanese high-speed railway network on the island of Kyushu, operated by the Kyushu Railway Company (JR Kyushu). Its Kagoshima Route links Hakata in Fukuoka with Kagoshima-Chūō, running parallel to the west coast through Kumamoto.

Services

Mizuho, Sakura, and Tsubame services operate on the Kagoshima Route, with through Sakura and Mizuho trains to Shin-Osaka via the Sanyo Shinkansen. The Nishi Kyushu Shinkansen opened in September 2022 between Takeo-Onsen and Nagasaki, using Kamome services with a cross-platform relay at Takeo-Onsen.
