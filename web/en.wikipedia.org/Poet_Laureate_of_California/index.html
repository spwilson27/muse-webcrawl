Poet Laureate of California

The Poet Laureate of California is the poet laureate for the U.S. state of California. California was historically among the first states to name such a position; Ina Donna Coolbrith was appointed in 1915, often described as the first poet laureate of any American state. The modern position is established in state law: the laureate is appointed by the Governor and confirmed by the Senate for a two-year term, with duties that include giving public readings and promoting poetry and the literary arts in the state, including a significant cultural project.

History

Early holders of the honor included Ina Coolbrith (1915) and Juan Felipe Herrera, who later became Poet Laureate of the United States. The post was formalized by legislation in 2001, with Quincy Troupe among the first appointed under the statute. Subsequent laureates included Carol Muske-Dukes, Juan Felipe Herrera, Dana Gioia, and Lee Herrick, appointed in 2022.

List of poets laureate

Selected holders: Ina Donna Coolbrith; Quincy Troupe (2002); Al Young (2005); Carol Muske-Dukes (2008); Juan Felipe Herrera (2012); Dana Gioia (2015); Lee Herrick (2022).
