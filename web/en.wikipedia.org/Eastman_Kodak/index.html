Eastman Kodak Company
Founded: 1892
Founder: George Eastman
Headquarters: Rochester, New York, U.S.
Industry: Imaging technology

The Eastman Kodak Company, commonly called Kodak, is an American imaging technology company headquartered in Rochester, New York. Founded by George Eastman, Kodak dominated the photographic film market for most of the 20th century and made photography accessible to the general public with products such as the Brownie camera and Kodachrome film.

History

Eastman introduced the first Kodak camera in 1888 with the slogan "You press the button, we do the rest." The company built an enormously profitable business on film, paper and photofinishing. Ironically, a Kodak engineer, Steven Sasson, built the first digital camera in 1975, but the company was slow to exploit digital imaging and lost its market as film declined.

Bankruptcy and restructuring

Kodak filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in January 2012 and emerged in 2013 as a much smaller company focused on commercial printing, advanced materials and chemicals, while licensing its brand for consumer products.
