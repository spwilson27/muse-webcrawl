Dune: Part Two

Dune: Part Two is a 2024 American epic science fiction film directed by Denis Villeneuve, who co-wrote the screenplay with Jon Spaihts. It is the sequel to Dune (2021) and the second part of Villeneuve's adaptation of Frank Herbert's 1965 novel Dune, covering the latter half of the book.

Plot and cast

The film follows Paul Atreides (Timothée Chalamet) as he unites with Chani (Zendaya) and the Fremen, wages war against House Harkonnen, and moves toward a fateful confrontation with the Emperor. The cast also includes Rebecca Ferguson, Austin Butler as Feyd-Rautha, Florence Pugh, Christopher Walken, Javier Bardem, and Josh Brolin. Music is by Hans Zimmer.

Reception

Released on 1 March 2024, the film grossed over $700 million worldwide and received widespread critical acclaim. At the 97th Academy Awards it won Best Sound and Best Visual Effects.
