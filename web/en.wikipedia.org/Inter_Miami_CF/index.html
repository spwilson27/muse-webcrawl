Club Internacional de Fútbol Miami, known as Inter Miami CF or Inter Miami, is an American professional soccer club based in the Miami metropolitan area. The club competes in Major League Soccer (MLS) as a member of the Eastern Conference, and began play in the 2020 season.

History

The club grew out of David Beckham's 2007 contract option to purchase an MLS expansion team at a discounted fee. MLS officially awarded the Miami franchise on January 29, 2018. The team's name, crest — featuring two great white herons — and pink, black, and white colours were unveiled in September 2018. Inter Miami played its first MLS match on March 1, 2020, a 1–0 loss to Los Angeles FC.

The Messi era

In July 2023 the club signed Argentine superstar Lionel Messi, together with former Barcelona teammates Sergio Busquets and Jordi Alba, and appointed Gerardo "Tata" Martino as head coach. Messi's arrival transformed the club's global profile and commercial value. Inter Miami won the 2023 Leagues Cup, the first trophy in club history, and the 2024 Supporters' Shield with a league-record points total, though it was eliminated in the first round of the 2024 MLS Cup playoffs by Atlanta United.

Stadium

The club plays at Chase Stadium in Fort Lauderdale (originally Inter Miami CF Stadium, later DRV PNK Stadium), an 18,000-seat venue expanded amid the surge in demand after 2023. A permanent home, Miami Freedom Park, is under construction near Miami International Airport.

Ownership

The ownership group includes Beckham and brothers Jorge and Jose Mas, whose group assumed control from earlier partners in 2021.
