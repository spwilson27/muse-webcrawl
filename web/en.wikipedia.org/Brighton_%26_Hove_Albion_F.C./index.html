Brighton & Hove Albion Football Club, commonly referred to simply as Brighton, is a professional football club based in Brighton and Hove, East Sussex, England. The club competes in the Premier League, the top tier of English football. Nicknamed "the Seagulls", the club's home colours are blue-and-white stripes.

History

Founded in 1901, Brighton played in the Southern League before election to the Football League in 1920. The club reached the FA Cup Final in 1983, losing to Manchester United after a replay. Financial difficulties in the 1990s saw the Goldstone Ground sold and the club nearly relegated from the Football League in 1997. Brighton later played at the Withdean Stadium before moving to the Falmer Stadium in 2011, and were promoted to the Premier League for the first time in 2017.

European football

In the 2022–23 season the club finished sixth in the Premier League, qualifying for European competition for the first time and reaching the last 16 of the UEFA Europa League.
