Ivan Tsarevich

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Ivan Tsarevich (Russian: Иван Царевич) is a folk hero of Russian fairy tales, a stock character who appears as the hero of many tales. He is usually the youngest of three sons of a tsar, initially underestimated, who succeeds where his elder brothers fail.

In the tales

Ivan Tsarevich appears in tales such as "Ivan Tsarevich and the Grey Wolf", in which a magic wolf helps him capture the Firebird and win a princess; tales of the Frog Princess, in which he marries a frog who is a transformed princess; and stories involving the sorcerer Koschei the Deathless. In some tales he is instead foolish — Ivan the Fool is a closely related figure.

Interpretation

Folklorists read Ivan Tsarevich as an embodiment of the humble, kind-hearted hero whose success comes through compassion and help from magical allies rather than strength. His adventures were illustrated by artists such as Viktor Vasnetsov and Ivan Bilibin.
