Gambit (Remy Etienne LeBeau) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics, commonly in association with the X-Men. Created by writer Chris Claremont and artists Andy Kubert and Jim Lee, he first appeared briefly in The Uncanny X-Men #266 (August 1990). A Cajun mutant from New Orleans and a master thief, Gambit can charge objects — most famously playing cards — with explosive kinetic energy.

Fictional biography

Abandoned at birth because of his burning red eyes, Remy was kidnapped from the hospital by the Thieves' Guild of New Orleans, who believed he was prophesied to unite the feuding guilds. Raised as a pickpocket and thief, he later married Bella Donna Boudreaux of the Assassins' Guild in a peace pact, but was exiled after a duel. Wandering as a thief for hire, he encountered a de-aged Storm, helped restore her, and joined the X-Men.

Relationships and adaptations

Gambit's on-again, off-again romance with Rogue is one of the X-Men franchise's central relationships; the two eventually married. He has featured prominently in X-Men animated series, notably X-Men: The Animated Series and X-Men '97, and was portrayed by Taylor Kitsch in X-Men Origins: Wolverine (2009) and by Channing Tatum in Deadpool & Wolverine (2024).
