Romain David Jerôme Grosjean (born 1986) is a French racing driver, born in Geneva, who competed in Formula One for the Renault, Lotus and Haas teams between 2009 and 2020, achieving ten podium finishes. He later raced in the American IndyCar Series.

Junior career and first F1 stint

Grosjean won the 2007 Formula 3 Euro Series and the inaugural GP2 Asia Series title in 2008, becoming a Renault test driver. In mid-2009 he was promoted to the Renault race seat vacated after Nelson Piquet Jr.'s dismissal in the "Crashgate" affair, but was himself dropped at the end of the season. After rebuilding his career — winning the 2011 GP2 Series championship — he returned to Formula One with Lotus in 2012.

Lotus and Haas years

At Lotus, Grosjean matured into a regular podium finisher, taking second places in Canada (2012) and several thirds in 2013 alongside Kimi Räikkönen, though his 2012 season also brought a one-race ban for causing a multi-car pile-up at Spa. When Lotus declined, he moved in 2016 to the new American Haas F1 Team, scoring all of the team's points in its debut season.

Bahrain crash and IndyCar

Grosjean's Formula One career is inseparable from his extraordinary escape at the 2020 Bahrain Grand Prix, when his Haas split the barriers and burst into flames; protected by the halo, he emerged from the fire after 28 seconds with burns to his hands — the burns that ended his F1 career two races early. From 2021 he raced in IndyCar, first for Dale Coyne Racing, then Andretti Autosport and Juncos Hollinger Racing, scoring poles and podiums and becoming a popular figure in the American series.
