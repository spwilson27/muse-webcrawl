Adidas

Adidas AG is a German multinational corporation, headquartered in Herzogenaurach, Bavaria, that designs and manufactures shoes, clothing and accessories. It is the largest sportswear manufacturer in Europe, and the second largest in the world, after Nike. The company is known for its three-stripe motif and the trefoil and mountain-bar logos.

History

The company was founded by Adolf "Adi" Dassler, who made sports shoes in his mother's washroom in Herzogenaurach after the First World War. He registered the "Gebrüder Dassler Schuhfabrik" in 1924 with his brother Rudolf. After a bitter family split in 1948, Rudolf went on to found Puma, while Adi registered Adidas in 1949 — the name a blend of his nickname "Adi" and the first syllables of "Dassler". Adidas famously supplied the West German football team that won the 1954 World Cup, and it has been a FIFA World Cup ball supplier since 1970.

Products and sponsorships

Adidas produces footwear (including the Stan Smith, Superstar, and Ultraboost lines), apparel, and equipment, and has acquired and later sold brands such as Reebok (2006–2022) and TaylorMade. It sponsors numerous football clubs, national teams, athletes, and major events, and has long-running collaborations in fashion and music, including with Stella McCartney and the Yeezy line (discontinued in 2022).
