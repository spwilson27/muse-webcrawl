Lil Wayne

Born: Dwayne Michael Carter Jr., 27 September 1982, New Orleans, Louisiana
Genres: Hip hop
Labels: Cash Money, Young Money, Republic

Dwayne Michael Carter Jr. (born September 27, 1982), known professionally as Lil Wayne, is an American rapper, singer and songwriter regarded as one of the most influential hip hop artists of his generation. He joined Cash Money Records as a teenager and was a member of the group Hot Boys.

Career

His breakthrough came with the Tha Carter album series; Tha Carter III (2008) sold over one million copies in its first week and won the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album. He founded the label Young Money Entertainment, which launched the careers of Drake and Nicki Minaj.

Legacy

Lil Wayne has sold over 120 million records worldwide and is noted for his prolific mixtape output and distinctive wordplay.
