Pevensey Castle

Pevensey Castle is a medieval castle and former Roman Saxon Shore fort at Pevensey in the English county of East Sussex. The site is a Scheduled Monument in the care of English Heritage and is open to visitors.

Pevensey CastleLocation: Pevensey, East Sussex, England
Built: c. 290 AD (Roman fort); c. 1100 (medieval castle)
Owner: English Heritage

History

The Romans built the fort of Anderitum around 290 AD as part of the Saxon Shore defences. After the Roman withdrawal it was occupied by Saxons, and in 1066 William the Conqueror landed nearby at Pevensey Bay before the Battle of Hastings, temporarily fortifying the Roman walls. A Norman stone castle was subsequently built within the fort. The castle withstood sieges in 1088 and 1147, was slighted in the later Middle Ages, and was refortified with a pillbox and gun emplacements during the Second World War.
