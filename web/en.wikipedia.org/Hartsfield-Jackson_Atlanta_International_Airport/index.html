Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (IATA: ATL, ICAO: KATL) is the principal airport serving Atlanta, Georgia, and the largest hub airport in the world by passenger numbers. (This page title uses the hyphenated form of the airport's name, which is also styled with an en dash as Hartsfield–Jackson.)

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport

Serves: Atlanta metropolitan area

Facilities

The airport's passenger facilities consist of the Domestic Terminal (terminals North and South) and the international terminal, linked by the Transportation Mall and the Plane Train automated people mover. Seven concourses (T and A–F) provide more than 190 gates. A rental car centre and MARTA rail station connect the airport to the city.

Traffic

Before the COVID-19 pandemic the airport handled over 110 million passengers in a single year (2019), more than any other airport worldwide, and it regained the top ranking as traffic recovered. Delta Air Lines and its partners account for the large majority of flights, operating one of the largest single-airline hubs in existence.
