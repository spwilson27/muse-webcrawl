Meduza

Meduza (Russian: Медуза) is a Russian- and English-language independent news website based in Riga, Latvia. It was founded in 2014 by Galina Timchenko, the former editor-in-chief of the Russian news site Lenta.ru, together with a group of journalists who had left Lenta.ru after her dismissal under pressure from its owners. The name means "jellyfish" in Russian (also evoking Medusa).

History

Meduza launched on 20 October 2014, publishing in Russian, with an English-language edition following in 2015. It registered in Latvia in order to be able to report freely on Russia without interference. The outlet became known for explanatory journalism, long-form features and a popular daily newsletter and podcasts, reaching tens of millions of readers.

Designation in Russia

In April 2021 the Russian Ministry of Justice designated Meduza a "foreign agent" media outlet, a label that sharply reduced its advertising revenue in Russia; an emergency crowdfunding appeal from readers raised enough money to keep it operating. In January 2023, after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine, the Russian Prosecutor General's Office declared Meduza an "undesirable organization", effectively criminalizing cooperation with it in Russia. Meduza continues to publish from exile and relies heavily on donations from readers outside Russia.
