Dolby Laboratories


Illustration: Dolby Laboratories

Dolby Laboratories, Inc. is an American technology corporation specializing in audio noise reduction, audio encoding and compression, spatial audio and high-dynamic-range imaging. Founded by Ray Dolby in London in 1965, the company moved to San Francisco in 1976 and is headquartered there today.

Technologies

Dolby is known for Dolby Noise Reduction for analogue tape, Dolby Digital (AC-3) surround sound, Dolby TrueHD, the object-based Dolby Atmos format, and the Dolby Vision HDR imaging standard. Its theatre sound systems became the industry standard after the success of films using Dolby Stereo.

Business model

Dolby primarily licenses its technologies to consumer electronics manufacturers, cinema operators and content creators rather than selling products directly to consumers.
