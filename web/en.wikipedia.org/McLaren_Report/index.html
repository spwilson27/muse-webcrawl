McLaren Report

The McLaren Report is an independent investigation commissioned by the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) and led by Canadian law professor Richard McLaren into allegations of state-sponsored doping in Russia. Its two parts were published in July and December 2016.

Findings

The report concluded that Russia operated a state-directed system — the "Disappearing Positive Methodology" — in which positive doping samples of protected athletes were swapped or destroyed through the Moscow laboratory, with involvement of the Ministry of Sport, the FSB, and anti-doping bodies. It found sample swapping at the 2014 Sochi Winter Olympics via holes in the laboratory wall, and implicated over 1,000 athletes across more than 30 sports between 2011 and 2015.

Consequences

Findings led to bans on Russian teams and officials, the exclusion of Russian flags and anthems from the 2018, 2020, and 2022 Olympics (athletes competing as neutrals), and reforms at WADA. Whistleblower Grigory Rodchenkov's testimony was central evidence. Russia disputed the report's conclusions.
