Malshej Ghat

Malshej Ghat is a mountain pass in the Western Ghats range of Maharashtra, India, on the Kalyan–Ahmednagar road (National Highway 61). Lying at an average elevation of about 700 metres, the pass is known for its misty monsoon scenery, waterfalls that spill across the road in the rainy season, and the rugged peaks of the Sahyadri range, including Harishchandragad nearby.

Tourism and nature

Malshej Ghat is a popular weekend destination from Mumbai, Thane, and Pune. The area is a noted birdwatching site: the wetlands and reservoirs around the pass attract migratory birds, including flamingos that arrive in the monsoon and winter months. Trekking routes lead to forts such as Harishchandragad and the Pimpalgaon Joga Dam is a local attraction. The Maharashtra Tourism Development Corporation maintains a resort at the pass.

Access

The ghat road is winding and prone to landslides in heavy monsoon rain, and it has a reputation for accidents. The nearest railway stations are at Kalyan and Karjat.
