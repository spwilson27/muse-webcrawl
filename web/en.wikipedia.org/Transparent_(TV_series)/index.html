Transparent is an American comedy-drama television series created by Joey Soloway and produced by Amazon Studios. It premiered in 2014 and concluded with a musical finale film in 2019. The story revolves around the Pfefferman family in Los Angeles, whose patriarch, Mort (Jeffrey Tambor), comes out as a trans woman named Maura, prompting revelations and self-examination among her adult children.

Reception

The series was critically acclaimed and seen as landmark representation of transgender people on television, employing many trans cast and crew. It won the Golden Globe Award for Best Television Series – Musical or Comedy, and Tambor won both a Golden Globe and a Primetime Emmy Award for his performance.

Controversy and conclusion

In 2017–2018 Tambor was accused of sexual harassment by co-stars and an assistant and was dismissed from the show; he denied the allegations. Rather than continue with a fifth season, the creators ended the series with Transparent: Musicale Finale (2019), which addressed Maura's death offscreen.
