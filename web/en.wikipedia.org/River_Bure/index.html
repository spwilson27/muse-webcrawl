The River Bure is a river in the English county of Norfolk. It rises near Melton Constable and flows for about 50 km (31 miles) south-eastwards through Aylsham, past Wroxham and Horning, before joining the sea at Gorleston near Great Yarmouth via Breydon Water.

The Broads

The Bure is one of the principal rivers of the Norfolk Broads and is navigable from the sea as far as Coltishall. Several of the Broads lie along its course, including Wroxham Broad, Salhouse Broad and Hoveton Great Broad. The river is popular for boating, sailing and angling, and its marshes and reedbeds support significant wildlife populations.
