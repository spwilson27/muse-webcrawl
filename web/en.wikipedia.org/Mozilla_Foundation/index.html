Mozilla Foundation

The Mozilla Foundation is an American non-profit organization that exists to support and collectively lead the open source Mozilla project. Founded in July 2003 after AOL shuttered Netscape, it is best known for the Firefox web browser.

Structure and mission

The foundation, based in San Francisco, owns the Mozilla trademarks and sets the project's governance and policy direction, guided by the Mozilla Manifesto's principles of an open, accessible internet. Development work is carried out largely through its taxable subsidiary, the Mozilla Corporation (established 2005), which employs Firefox developers and is funded mainly by search-engine royalties, historically from Google. The foundation also runs advocacy, education and grant programmes, and oversees related projects such as the Thunderbird email client and the Common Voice speech dataset.
