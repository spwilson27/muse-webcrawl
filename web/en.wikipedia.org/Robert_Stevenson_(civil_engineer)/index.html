Robert Stevenson (civil engineer)

Robert Stevenson, engineer to the Northern Lighthouse Board

Robert Stevenson, FRSE, FRSGS, FRSSA, MICE (8 June 1772 – 12 July 1850) was a Scottish civil engineer, and designer and builder of lighthouses. His works include the Bell Rock Lighthouse, which has stood for over 200 years. He was the father of engineers Alan, David and Thomas Stevenson, and grandfather of author Robert Louis Stevenson.

Career

Born in Glasgow, Stevenson was apprenticed to his stepfather Thomas Smith, first engineer to the Northern Lighthouse Board, whom he succeeded in 1797. Over his career he designed and built some two dozen lighthouses around the Scottish coast, as well as harbours, bridges and early railway works.

Bell Rock Lighthouse

His masterpiece, the Bell Rock Lighthouse (1807–1810), was built on a wave-washed reef 18 km off Arbroath in the North Sea, using interlocking granite blocks on the model of Smeaton's Eddystone. It is the world's oldest surviving sea-washed lighthouse and a celebrated feat of engineering.

Legacy

Stevenson founded a dynasty: the "Lighthouse Stevensons" built most of Scotland's lighthouses over four generations. His inventions included intermittent and flashing light characters that made individual lighthouses identifiable at night.
