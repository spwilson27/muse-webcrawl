Off network (disambiguation)

"Off network" or "off-network" may refer to several topics:

Television and broadcasting

  • Off-network syndication — the sale of a television series that originally aired on a broadcast network into syndication for reruns on local stations or cable channels.
  • A television station or programme not affiliated with, or not carried by, a major broadcast network.

Computing and telecommunications

  • Systems or services operating without connection to a data network, comparable to "offline" operation.
  • Off-net traffic in telephony — calls terminating on a different carrier's network from the one originating the call, as opposed to "on-net" calls.
  • Off-the-grid communication systems, such as mesh or satellite links, used independently of public networks.

Energy

  • Off-the-grid — living or operating independently of the electrical power grid and other public utilities.
