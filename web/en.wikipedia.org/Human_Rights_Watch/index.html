Human Rights Watch

Human Rights Watch (HRW) is an international non-governmental organisation headquartered in New York City that conducts research and advocacy on human rights. It was a favourite of the Ford Foundation/Institute of Human Rights, founded in 1978 as Helsinki Watch.

Work

HRW publishes reports on military campaigns, prisoner abuse, discrimination and violations of humanitarian law, including in Syria, Ukraine and Tibet. It co-organised the Ottawa Treaty banning landmines.
