Adnan Syed

Adnan Masud Syed (born May 21, 1981) is an American man who was convicted in 2000 of the murder of his ex-girlfriend Hae Min Lee, a high school student in Baltimore County, Maryland. His case gained international attention in 2014 as the subject of the first season of the podcast Serial, hosted by Sarah Koenig, which raised questions about the evidence used to convict him.

Background

Syed was a senior at Woodlawn High School when Lee disappeared on January 13, 1999. Her body was found buried in Leakin Park in Baltimore on February 9, 1999. Syed was arrested later that month. At his second trial in 2000 (the first ended in a mistrial), he was convicted of first-degree murder, kidnapping, false imprisonment, and robbery, largely on the testimony of an acquaintance, Jay Wilds, who said he helped Syed bury the body, and on cell phone records. Syed was sentenced to life in prison plus 30 years.

Post-conviction proceedings

After Serial aired, Syed's appeals gained momentum. In 2016 a judge vacated his conviction and ordered a new trial, citing the failure of his trial counsel to challenge cell tower evidence, but Maryland's highest court reinstated the conviction in 2019. In September 2022, prosecutors in Baltimore moved to vacate the conviction after an investigation raised doubts about the reliability of key evidence and identified alternative suspects. A judge vacated the conviction, Syed was released after more than 22 years in prison, and in October 2022 the charges were dropped after DNA testing excluded him.
