National Ignition Facility

The National Ignition Facility (NIF) is a laser-based inertial confinement fusion research device at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory in California. Its 192 laser beams focus nearly 2 megajoules of ultraviolet light onto a millimetre-scale fuel pellet to compress and heat it to fusion conditions.

LocationLivermore, California
Completed2009
Lasers192 beams
MilestoneScientific breakeven, 5 December 2022

Purpose

NIF supports stockpile stewardship — studying nuclear weapons physics without explosive testing — as well as fusion energy research and high-energy-density astrophysics experiments.

Ignition achievement

On 5 December 2022, a NIF shot produced 3.15 MJ of fusion energy from 2.05 MJ of laser energy delivered to the target, the first controlled fusion experiment to achieve scientific breakeven (target gain above one). The result was repeated in 2023 and afterward with higher yields, though wall-plug efficiency remains far below what a power plant would require.
