Liège–Bastogne–Liège

Nickname: La Doyenne ("The Old Lady")
First held: 1892
Region: Wallonia, Belgium
Type: One-day road cycling race, Monument

Liège–Bastogne–Liège is a one-day professional road cycling race held each April in the French-speaking Wallonia region of Belgium. First run in 1892, it is the oldest of the five Monuments of the European professional road cycling calendar and is nicknamed La Doyenne, "the Old Lady".

Course

The route runs from Liège south to Bastogne and back, covering roughly 250 kilometres through the hilly Ardennes. Famous climbs include the Côte de la Redoute, the Côte de Stockeu and the Côte de Saint-Nicolas near the finish.

Winners

Eddy Merckx holds the record with five victories. The race forms the climax of the Ardennes classics week, following the Amstel Gold Race and La Flèche Wallonne.
