American River

American River
Location: California, United States
Length: About 119 miles (192 km) including forks
Source: Sierra Nevada
Mouth: Sacramento River at Sacramento

The American River is a river in California that runs from the Sierra Nevada mountain range to its confluence with the Sacramento River in downtown Sacramento. It is famous as the site where gold was discovered in 1848 at Sutter's Mill in Coloma, an event that started the California Gold Rush.

Course

The river has three main forks — the North, Middle, and South Forks — which drain the western slope of the Sierra Nevada and join in Folsom Lake, a reservoir formed by Folsom Dam. Below the dam, the Lower American River flows through the American River Parkway to the Sacramento River.

Recreation and ecology

The South Fork is one of the most popular whitewater rafting rivers in California, while the Lower American River supports salmon and steelhead runs and is designated a National Wild and Scenic River.
