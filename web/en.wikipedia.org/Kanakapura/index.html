Kanakapura is a town and taluk headquarters in Ramanagara district of Karnataka, India, about 55 kilometres south of Bengaluru. It lies near the Arkavathi River and is surrounded by the hills and forests of the Bilikal Rangaswamy Betta range, making it a popular base for trekking and adventure camps serving the Bengaluru metropolitan area.

Economy

The town is one of India's notable centres of silk production, with sericulture and silk reeling employing many local families; it is also known for granite quarrying and for agriculture including ragi, coconut, and mulberry. Kanakapura Road has become a major growth corridor linking the town to Bengaluru's expanding southern suburbs.

Tourism and surroundings

Nearby attractions include Chunchi Falls on the Arkavathi, the forested temple hill of Bilikal Rangaswamy Betta, Banantimari Betta, and nature and adventure resorts along the Kanakapura road. The region's rocky hills and scrub forests are home to wildlife including elephants on seasonal movements through the Bannerghatta–Cauvery corridor.
