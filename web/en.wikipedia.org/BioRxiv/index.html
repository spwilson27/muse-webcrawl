bioRxiv

bioRxiv (pronounced "bio-archive") is an open access preprint repository for the biological sciences, operated by Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory. Launched in November 2013, it allows researchers to share manuscripts before formal peer review and publication in a journal.

History and operation

bioRxiv was modelled on the physics preprint server arXiv. Submissions are screened for basic appropriateness and plagiarism, assigned DOIs, and posted typically within days. Authors may revise versions and link preprints to subsequent journal publications. A companion server, medRxiv, for health sciences, launched in 2019.

Growth and impact

Usage grew rapidly, especially during the COVID-19 pandemic, when rapid dissemination was critical but also highlighted risks of unreviewed findings reaching media and policy discussions. Many journals now allow or encourage preprint posting, and funders including NIH permit citing preprints.

Features

The server offers subject categories from animal behavior to zoology, direct transfer to journals, usage metrics and a channel for community comments. It is free for readers and authors.
