Encanto is a 2021 American animated musical fantasy film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios. Set in Colombia, it tells the story of the Madrigals, a multigenerational family whose members have received magical gifts from a miracle — all except the protagonist, Mirabel Madrigal, whose lack of a gift sets her at odds with the family until she becomes the one who must save its fading magic. The film was directed by Jared Bush and Byron Howard, with songs written by Lin-Manuel Miranda.

Casita, the enchanted Madrigal family home

Plot

Fleeing violence, Alma Madrigal loses her husband Pedro but witnesses a miracle: her candle creates an enchanted valley, the Encanto, and a living house, Casita. Her descendants each receive a gift at age five — super-strength, healing through food, weather control, prophecy, and more. Mirabel alone receives nothing. When the magic begins to crack, Mirabel investigates, uncovering the family's hidden burdens — including her exiled uncle Bruno, whose prophecies were feared — and learning that the pressure to be perfect is destroying the miracle. The family rebuilds its home together, and the magic returns.

Music

The soundtrack blends Colombian musical styles with Broadway songwriting. "We Don't Talk About Bruno" became a viral phenomenon, topping the Billboard Hot 100 for five weeks in early 2022 — the first Disney song to reach number one in nearly three decades. Other songs include "Surface Pressure" and the Oscar-nominated "Dos Oruguitas", performed by Sebastián Yatra.

Reception

Following a modest theatrical run during the COVID-19 pandemic, Encanto became a major streaming hit on Disney+. Critics praised its animation, music, and themes of generational trauma and family expectation. It won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature, as well as the corresponding Golden Globe and BAFTA awards.
