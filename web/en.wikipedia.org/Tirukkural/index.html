Tirukkural

The Tirukkural (Tamil: திருக்குறள்), also known as the Kural, is a classic Tamil text consisting of 1,330 short couplets (kurals) in 133 chapters of ten each. Traditionally attributed to the poet-philosopher Thiruvalluvar, it is one of the most important works in Tamil literature.

Tirukkural
Author: Thiruvalluvar (attributed)
Language: Tamil
Chapters: 133
Couplets: 1,330
Books: Virtue, Wealth, Love

Structure

The work is divided into three books: Aram (virtue), Porul (wealth / polity) and Inbam (love). Each couplet is in the concise kural venpa metre.

Themes

It offers aphoristic guidance on ethics, non-violence, honesty, governance, economics, friendship and domestic life, largely without sectarian religious doctrine, which has given it universal appeal in Tamil Nadu and beyond.

Legacy

The Kural has been translated into dozens of languages, first into Latin and English in the 18th–19th centuries, and is celebrated with the towering Thiruvalluvar Statue at Kanyakumari.
