Marfa is a city in the high desert of the Trans-Pecos region of West Texas, between the Davis Mountains and Big Bend National Park. It is the county seat of Presidio County and was founded in the early 1880s as a railroad water stop.

Arts community

In the 1970s the minimalist artist Donald Judd moved to Marfa and began acquiring buildings and land, later establishing the Chinati Foundation, a contemporary art museum installed in a former artillery base. Judd's presence transformed the small ranching town into an internationally known arts destination; the nearby Prada Marfa installation (2005) became an iconic roadside artwork.

Marfa lights

The town is also famous for the Marfa lights, unexplained glowing orbs reported east of the town along U.S. Route 67, which draw tourists to an official viewing area.
