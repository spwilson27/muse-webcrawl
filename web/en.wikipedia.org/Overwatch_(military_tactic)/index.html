Overwatch (military tactic)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Overwatch is a military tactic in which one small unit supports another friendly unit while the latter is moving. The overwatching unit takes a position — often elevated or otherwise offering good fields of observation and fire — where it can observe the terrain ahead of the friendly unit and engage the enemy if the moving unit comes under fire. The overwatch position is typically chosen so that the supporting unit can suppress enemy positions while the manoeuvre element advances, withdraws, or crosses exposed ground.

Bounding overwatch

The tactic is extended in bounding overwatch, also called leapfrogging, in which two or more elements alternate movement: one element moves forward while the other remains stationary in overwatch, and the stationary element then advances past the first once it is set. Bounds may be successive (each element moving to a line roughly abreast of the other) or alternate (each element passing beyond the other). The length of each bound depends on terrain, visibility, and the effective range of the overwatching weapons.

Usage

Overwatch is a basic technique of infantry and combined-arms tactics at squad through battalion level. Armoured vehicles frequently provide overwatch for dismounted infantry in urban or broken terrain, and snipers and machine-gun teams are often assigned overwatch roles. The concept applies in defensive as well as offensive operations: an overwatch element may cover a withdrawal or the crossing of an obstacle such as a river, road, or minefield.

Principles

Effective overwatch requires a position with observation of the objective and likely enemy locations, fields of fire covering the moving unit's route, communications between the elements, and a plan for when the overwatch element itself displaces. The overwatch unit must avoid becoming decisively engaged in a way that prevents it from supporting the movement, and the moving element should move between covered positions and minimise its exposure.
