List of flags by color

National flags grouped by their dominant colours.

This is a list of flags by color, organizing national, regional and other flags according to the colors they contain. Flags are grouped in the list by color combinations, such as flags that are red, white and blue; red and yellow; or green, yellow and red (the pan-African colors), among many others.

Common combinations

  • Red, white and blue — used by the United States, France, the United Kingdom, Russia, the Netherlands, Australia and many others; among the most common combinations in the world.
  • Green, yellow and red — the pan-African colors derived from Ethiopia's flag, used across Africa and in reggae culture.
  • Red and white — including Canada, Japan, Poland, Indonesia and Monaco.
  • Blue and yellow — including Sweden, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the European Union (blue with gold stars).
  • Red — historically associated with socialist and communist states, such as the former Soviet Union.
  • Black, red and gold — the German tricolour and related designs.

Some flags are notable exceptions: Libya's flag under Muammar Gaddafi (1977–2011) was a plain green field, the only national flag of a single plain color with no emblem.
