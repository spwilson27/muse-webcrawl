Comparison of American and Canadian football

American football and Canadian football are closely related gridiron codes that evolved from rugby football in the late 19th century. They share the same basic structure — advancing an oval ball by running or passing, downs, and scoring by touchdowns and field goals — but differ in a number of fundamental rules.

Key differences

Effects on play

The larger field, extra player, and three-down system make the Canadian game more open and pass-oriented, with greater emphasis on special teams returns; the rouge and no-yards rule also change kicking strategy. The professional leagues are the NFL in the United States and the CFL in Canada, whose championship is the Grey Cup.
