Square Enix Holdings Co., Ltd. is a Japanese multinational holding company, production enterprise and entertainment conglomerate, best known for its Final Fantasy, Dragon Quest, and Kingdom Hearts role-playing video game franchises, among numerous others.

History

The company was formed on 1 April 2003 by the merger of Square Co., Ltd. and Enix Corporation, long-standing rivals in Japanese role-playing games. It later acquired arcade and game publisher Taito (2005) and Eidos Interactive (2009), and sold its western studios (including Crystal Dynamics and Eidos-Montréal) to Embracer Group in 2022. Square Enix also publishes manga through its Gangan Comics imprint and produces films and merchandise.

Key franchises

Final Fantasy (from 1987), Dragon Quest (from 1986), Kingdom Hearts (from 2002), and the MMORPG Final Fantasy XIV are among its most significant properties.
