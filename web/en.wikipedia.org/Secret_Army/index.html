Secret Army

Secret Army is a British television drama series created by Gerard Glaister, broadcast by BBC1 from 1977 to 1979. Set in German-occupied Belgium during the Second World War, it dramatises the work of a resistance organisation — based on the real-life Comet Line — that sheltered Allied airmen shot down over Europe and smuggled them back to Britain via France and Spain.

Plot and characters

The series centres on the Café Candide in Brussels, run by Albert Foiret, whose basement hides evaders handled by the "Lifeline" organisation led by Lisa Colbert ("Yvette") and, after her death, by Albert himself. Opposing them are the Gestapo officer Sturmbannführer Ludwig Kessler and Luftwaffe police chief Major Erwin Brandt, whose contrasting methods and gradual disillusionment form a major strand of the drama.

Reception and legacy

Secret Army was praised for its morally complex portrayal of occupiers and collaborators. A sequel, Kessler (1981), followed its villain after the war. The series was also the inspiration for the sitcom 'Allo 'Allo!, which affectionately parodied its characters and situations.
