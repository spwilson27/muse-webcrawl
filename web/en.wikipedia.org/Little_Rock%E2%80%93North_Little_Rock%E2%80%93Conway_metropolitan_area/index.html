The Little Rock–North Little Rock–Conway metropolitan statistical area is a metropolitan area in central Arkansas, United States, centred on the state capital of Little Rock. It is defined by the U.S. Office of Management and Budget and covers several counties, including Pulaski, Faulkner, Saline, Lonoke, Grant and Perry.

Communities

The principal cities are Little Rock, North Little Rock (across the Arkansas River) and Conway. Other significant communities include Benton, Bryant, Sherwood, Jacksonville, Cabot and Maumelle.

Economy and transport

The metro area is Arkansas's largest centre of government, banking, health care and logistics. It is served by Clinton National Airport, the Port of Little Rock on the Arkansas River, and interstate highways I-30 and I-40, which intersect in the area.
