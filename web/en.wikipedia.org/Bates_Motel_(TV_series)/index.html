Bates Motel is an American psychological horror drama television series that aired on A&E from 2013 to 2017. Developed by Carlton Cuse, Kerry Ehrin and Anthony Cipriano, it serves as a contemporary prequel to Alfred Hitchcock's 1960 film Psycho, itself based on Robert Bloch's novel, and depicts the early life of Norman Bates and his relationship with his mother, Norma.

Plot

After the death of her husband, Norma Bates (Vera Farmiga) buys a motel in the coastal town of White Pine Bay, Oregon, hoping for a fresh start with her teenage son Norman (Freddie Highmore). As Norman's mental state deteriorates and his blackouts become more dangerous, the series charts the path toward the events of Psycho, with the final season adapting elements of the film directly.

Reception

The series received critical acclaim, particularly for the performances of Farmiga and Highmore. Farmiga was nominated for a Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series and won a Saturn Award; the show itself won People's Choice Awards for favorite cable TV drama.
