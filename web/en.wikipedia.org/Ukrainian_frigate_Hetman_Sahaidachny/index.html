Ukrainian frigate Hetman Sahaidachny

Hetman Sahaidachny (F130)
Class: Krivak III / Nerei-class frigate
Launched: March 29, 1992
Commissioned: April 2, 1993
Fate: Scuttled March 3, 2022

Hetman Sahaidachny (Ukrainian: Гетьман Сагайдачний) was a Nerei-class (Krivak III) frigate and the flagship of the Ukrainian Navy, named after the 17th-century Cossack hetman Petro Konashevych-Sahaidachny.

Service

Originally built for the Soviet Border Troops as Kirov, the ship entered Ukrainian service in 1993. She participated in international exercises and in counter-piracy operations off the Horn of Africa in 2013–2014.

Loss

Undergoing repairs at Mykolaiv at the start of the Russian invasion of Ukraine, she was scuttled on March 3, 2022 to prevent her capture.
