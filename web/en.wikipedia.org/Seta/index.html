Seta

A seta (plural setae) is a bristle- or hair-like structure found on many living organisms. In animals, setae occur on annelid worms (the "chaetae" of polychaetes and earthworms, used in locomotion), on arthropods (sensory hairs and the adhesive pads of geckos and insects), and on many other invertebrates. In plants and fungi, setae are stiff bristles on surfaces such as the sporophyte of liverworts and the fruiting bodies of some fungi.

Functions

Setae serve diverse functions: locomotion and anchorage in worms, mechanoreception and chemoreception in arthropods, and adhesion — the millions of microscopic setae on a gecko's toes exploit van der Waals forces to grip smooth surfaces.
