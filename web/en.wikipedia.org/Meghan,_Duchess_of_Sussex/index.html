Meghan, Duchess of Sussex (born Rachel Meghan Markle; August 4, 1981) is an American member of the British royal family and former actress. She is married to Prince Harry, Duke of Sussex, the younger son of King Charles III. Before her marriage she was known for playing Rachel Zane in the American legal drama Suits (2011–2018) and for her lifestyle blog The Tig.

Early life and career

Markle was born and raised in Los Angeles, California, and graduated from Northwestern University in 2003 with a double major in theatre and international studies. She worked briefly at the U.S. Embassy in Buenos Aires and pursued acting, appearing in films including Remember Me (2010) and Horrible Bosses (2011), and as a briefcase model on the game show Deal or No Deal. Her breakthrough came with Suits, filmed in Toronto, where she played paralegal-turned-lawyer Rachel Zane for seven seasons. Alongside acting she ran The Tig (2014–2017) and undertook humanitarian work, including as a UN Women advocate.

Marriage and royal life

Markle met Prince Harry in 2016; their engagement was announced in November 2017, and she retired from acting. They married on May 19, 2018, at St George's Chapel, Windsor Castle, whereupon she became Duchess of Sussex. The couple undertook royal tours and patronages; their son Archie was born in May 2019. In January 2020 they announced their intention to step back as senior working royals, an arrangement completed in March 2020, after which they settled in Montecito, California. Their daughter Lilibet was born in June 2021.

Later work

Through their Archewell organization and production companies, the couple signed deals with Netflix and Spotify, producing the documentary series Harry & Meghan (2022) among other projects. Meghan authored the children's book The Bench (2021), hosted the podcast Archetypes (2022), and launched the lifestyle brand American Riviera Orchard (later As Ever) in 2024. A widely watched 2021 interview with Oprah Winfrey, and subsequent litigation with British tabloid publishers, kept her at the centre of debates about the media, race, and the monarchy.
