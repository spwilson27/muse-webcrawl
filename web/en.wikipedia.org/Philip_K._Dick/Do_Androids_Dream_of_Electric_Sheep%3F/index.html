Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?

Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? is a 1968 science fiction novel by American writer Philip K. Dick. Set in a post-apocalyptic San Francisco in 2021, it follows bounty hunter Rick Deckard, who is tasked with "retiring" six escaped Nexus-6 androids that are nearly indistinguishable from humans.

Plot

After a nuclear war has devastated the Earth and driven most humans to emigrate off-world, Deckard hunts the androids while dreaming of owning a real animal — a status symbol in a world where most species are extinct and people keep electric replicas. His pursuit forces him to question the empathy that supposedly separates humans from androids.

Themes

The novel explores what it means to be human, the role of empathy, and the blurring boundary between the real and the artificial. The empathy religion of Mercerism and the mood-altering Penfield mood organ frame Deckard's crisis of conscience.

Adaptation

The novel loosely inspired the 1982 film Blade Runner and its 2017 sequel; after the film's release the book was often reprinted under the Blade Runner title.
