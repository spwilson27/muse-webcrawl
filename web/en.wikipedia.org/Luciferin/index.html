Luciferin

Luciferin (from Latin lucifer, "light-bearer") is a generic term for light-emitting compounds found in organisms that generate bioluminescence. Luciferins are oxidised under the catalytic effect of the enzyme luciferase, producing light.

Types

Different lineages use chemically distinct luciferins: firefly luciferin (a benzothiazole), coelenterazine in many marine animals, bacterial luciferin, dinoflagellate luciferin and vargulin in ostracods. The firefly luciferin–luciferase system is widely used in molecular biology as a reporter gene and in ATP detection assays.
