Wikimedia Commons

[Image: Wikimedia Commons]

Wikimedia Commons (also simply called Commons) is a media repository of free-to-use images, sounds, videos and other media files. It is a project of the Wikimedia Foundation and was launched on 7 September 2004. Files uploaded to Commons can be used across all Wikimedia projects, including Wikipedia, Wiktionary and Wikibooks, without the need to upload them separately to each project.

Content and licensing

Commons accepts only media that are freely licensed or in the public domain, such as works released under Creative Commons licenses or the GFDL. Unlike many individual Wikipedia language editions, it does not permit non-free "fair use" content. The repository hosts well over one hundred million media files and is one of the largest repositories of freely licensed media in the world.

Quality designations

Contributors can nominate images as "Featured pictures", "Quality images" or "Valued images" according to community standards. Annual highlights include the Picture of the Year competition and photography contests such as Wiki Loves Monuments and Wiki Loves Earth, which are among the largest photographic competitions in the world.
