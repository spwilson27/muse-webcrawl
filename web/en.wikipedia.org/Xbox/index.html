Xbox is a video gaming brand created and owned by Microsoft. It launched with the Xbox console in North America on 15 November 2001 and has since spanned four generations of home consoles, the Xbox network online service, the Xbox Game Studios publishing arm, the Game Pass subscription service, and cloud streaming.

Consoles

The original Xbox (2001) was Microsoft's entry into the console market, built largely from PC components. The Xbox 360 (2005) became the brand's most successful system, aided by Xbox Live and the Kinect motion sensor. The Xbox One (2013) was followed by the mid-generation Xbox One X (2017). The current generation comprises the Xbox Series X and the smaller, all-digital Series S, both released on 10 November 2020.

Services

The Xbox network (formerly Xbox Live) provides online multiplayer, digital distribution, and social features; the premium tier was long sold as Xbox Live Gold before being folded into Game Pass Core in 2023. Game Pass offers a rotating library of downloadable games, and Xbox Cloud Gaming streams titles to phones, PCs, and televisions.

Software and studios

Xbox Game Studios publishes franchises such as Halo, Gears of War, Forza, and Fable. Microsoft's acquisitions of ZeniMax Media (2021) and Activision Blizzard (2023) greatly expanded the brand's first-party catalogue.
