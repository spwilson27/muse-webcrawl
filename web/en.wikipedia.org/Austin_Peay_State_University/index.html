Austin Peay State University

Established: 1927
Location: Clarksville, Tennessee, United States
Mascot: Governors

Austin Peay State University (APSU) is a public university in Clarksville, Tennessee. Founded in 1927 as a normal school, it is named for Austin Peay, governor of Tennessee at the time.

Campus and programmes

The university enrols around 9,000–10,000 students and offers undergraduate and graduate programmes through colleges including arts and letters, business, education, health and human services, and science, technology, engineering and mathematics. It also serves many military-affiliated students from nearby Fort Campbell.

Athletics

The Governors compete in NCAA Division I, joining the Atlantic Sun Conference in 2022. The student cheer "Let's Go Peay!" is a well-known part of university tradition.
