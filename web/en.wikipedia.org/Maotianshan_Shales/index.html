Maotianshan Shales

The Maotianshan Shales are a series of Early Cambrian geological formations near Chengjiang in Yunnan Province, China, renowned for their extraordinary fossil preservation — the Chengjiang biota.

Fossils

Dating to about 518 million years ago, the deposit preserves soft tissues in fine detail and contains some of the earliest known complex animal fossils, including early arthropods, sponges, worms and the early vertebrate-like Myllokunmingia and Haikouichthys. Fossils were first recognised in the area in the early 20th century, but their full significance emerged after Hou Xianguang's discovery of a rich fauna in 1984.

Significance

Together with the later Burgess Shale of Canada, the Chengjiang biota provides the principal window into the Cambrian explosion of animal life. The site is inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List as the Chengjiang Fossil Site.
