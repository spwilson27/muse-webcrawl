Juan Pablo Montoya Roldán (born 20 September 1975) is a Colombian racing driver. One of the most versatile drivers of his era, he won the CART championship (1999), the Indianapolis 500 twice (2000 and 2015), seven Formula One Grands Prix for Williams and McLaren including the Monaco Grand Prix, and races in the NASCAR Cup Series — and is one of only two drivers to have won the CART title, the Indianapolis 500 and the Monaco Grand Prix.

Early career and CART

Montoya, whose father was an architect and racing enthusiast, progressed through karting and junior formulae and won the International Formula 3000 championship in 1998. Moving to American racing with Chip Ganassi Racing, he won the 1999 CART championship as a rookie and dominated the 2000 Indianapolis 500 at his first attempt.

Formula One

Montoya joined Williams-BMW in 2001, quickly making his mark with audacious overtaking moves on Michael Schumacher and a first win at Monza. His peak came in 2003, when he won at Monaco and the Nürburgring and contended for the world championship. Moving to McLaren in 2005, he won three races but struggled against Kimi Räikkönen, and in mid-2006 he left Formula One abruptly for NASCAR after a first-lap collision at Indianapolis.

NASCAR and endurance racing

In NASCAR, Montoya raced for Earnhardt Ganassi Racing from 2006 to 2013; his two Cup wins both came on road courses (Sonoma 2007 and Watkins Glen 2010), and he made the 2009 Chase for the Sprint Cup. He returned to IndyCar with Team Penske, finishing second in the 2015 championship after winning his second Indianapolis 500, and won the Race of Champions in 2016. He later raced in IMSA sports cars, winning the 2019 DPi championship with Acura Team Penske, and contested the 24 Hours of Le Mans.
