Isaac Herzog

Isaac "Bougie" Herzog (born 22 September 1960) is an Israeli politician who has served as the eleventh President of Israel since 2021. A member of the Labor Party, he previously served as a Knesset member, minister in several governments, leader of the opposition, and chairman of the Jewish Agency.

Background

Herzog comes from a prominent family: his father, Chaim Herzog, was the sixth President of Israel, and his grandfather, Yitzhak HaLevi Herzog, was the first Chief Rabbi of Ireland and later of Israel. He studied law at Tel Aviv University and Cornell, and worked as an attorney.

Political career

Elected to the Knesset in 2003, Herzog held portfolios including tourism, housing, and welfare and social services. He became Labor Party leader in 2013 and led the Zionist Union alliance in the 2015 election, losing to Benjamin Netanyahu. From 2018 he headed the Jewish Agency for Israel. In June 2021 the Knesset elected him President by an unprecedented margin of 87 votes, and he took office on 7 July 2021, succeeding Reuven Rivlin.
