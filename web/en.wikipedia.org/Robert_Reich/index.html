Robert Reich

Robert Bernard Reich (born June 24, 1946) is an American economist, professor, author, and political commentator. He served as United States Secretary of Labor under President Bill Clinton from 1993 to 1997.

Career

Reich studied at Dartmouth, Oxford (as a Rhodes Scholar), and Yale Law School, where he was a classmate of Bill and Hillary Clinton. He taught at Harvard's Kennedy School and later became a professor of public policy at the University of California, Berkeley. As Labor Secretary he oversaw the implementation of the Family and Medical Leave Act and advocated for raising the minimum wage and investing in workforce training.

Writing

His books include The Work of Nations (1991), Locked in the Cabinet (1997), Supercapitalism (2007), Aftershock (2010), and Saving Capitalism (2015), the last adapted into a documentary. A prominent critic of rising income inequality, Reich co-founded the media organisation Inequality Media.
