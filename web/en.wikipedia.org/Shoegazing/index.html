Shoegazing (also shoegaze) is a subgenre of indie and alternative rock that emerged in the United Kingdom in the late 1980s. It is characterised by overwhelming waves of guitar noise shaped by distortion, reverb, delay, and tremolo — often produced with the vibrato arm of the guitar to bend chords — with soft, androgynous vocals submerged in the mix. The name, coined mockingly by the British music press, refers to performers' tendency to stand still and stare down at the array of effects pedals at their feet.

History

Shoegaze drew on the feedback storms of The Jesus and Mary Chain, the dream pop of Cocteau Twins, the noise rock of Sonic Youth and Dinosaur Jr., and neo-psychedelia. Bands such as My Bloody Valentine (whose 1988 album Isn't Anything and EPs defined the glide-guitar sound), Ride, Lush, Chapterhouse, and Slowdive formed the core of a loose London-and-Thames-Valley milieu that Melody Maker dubbed "the scene that celebrates itself". My Bloody Valentine's Loveless (1991) — recorded over two years at great expense for Creation Records — is regarded as the genre's masterpiece and one of the landmark albums of alternative rock.

Sound and aesthetics

Central techniques include layering multiple modulated guitar tracks into a "wall of sound", oscillating pitch with the tremolo arm ("glide guitar"), generous use of effects chains, and treating the voice as another texture rather than the focus. Lyrics are typically impressionistic and secondary to atmosphere. The visual style favoured abstraction, blurred photography, and a studied introversion that the press contrasted with the extroversion of Madchester and American grunge.

Decline and revival

By the mid-1990s Britpop's directness displaced shoegaze in the UK, and several bands broke up or changed direction. Interest revived in the 2000s–2010s: influence spread into nu-gaze and metal-adjacent styles, Slowdive, Ride, and Lush reformed to acclaim, and My Bloody Valentine released m b v (2013). A new wave of artists worldwide — from American, Japanese, and Latin American scenes — adopted the sound, and shoegaze became a durable strand of alternative music.
