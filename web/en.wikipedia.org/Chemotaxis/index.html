Chemotaxis

Chemotaxis is the movement of a cell or organism toward or away from a chemical stimulus. Bacteria swim up gradients of attractants such as sugars and away from repellents; immune cells use chemotaxis to reach infections.

Positive chemotaxis moves toward higher concentrations of an attractant; negative chemotaxis moves away from a repellent. It is fundamental to development, wound healing and fertilisation.
