Kedarnath is a Hindu pilgrimage town in the Rudraprayag district of Uttarakhand, India, in the Garhwal Himalayas near the head of the Mandakini River. It is home to the Kedarnath Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlinga shrines of Shiva and one of the four sites of the Chota Char Dham pilgrimage, traditionally attributed to the 8th-century saint Adi Shankaracharya.

Temple

The stone temple stands behind a large statue of Nandi the bull. Because of extreme winter weather, the temple is open only between the end of April and November; in winter the deity is carried down to Ukhimath.

2013 floods

In June 2013, a glacial lake outburst and torrential rain devastated Kedarnath, killing thousands of pilgrims and residents. Remarkably, the temple itself survived, shielded by a massive boulder that lodged behind it. Pilgrimage resumed the following year along a rebuilt route.
