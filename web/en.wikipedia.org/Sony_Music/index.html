Sony Music Entertainment (SME), commonly known as Sony Music, is an American multinational music company owned by Sony Group Corporation. It is one of the "Big Three" record companies, alongside Universal Music Group and Warner Music Group.

History

The company's lineage runs through the Columbia Phonograph Company (founded 1889) and the American Record Corporation (1929), which became Columbia Records. CBS owned the label group until Sony acquired CBS Records in 1988, renaming it Sony Music Entertainment in 1991. In 2004 Sony merged its music arm with Bertelsmann's BMG to form Sony BMG; Sony bought out Bertelsmann's share in 2008, restoring the Sony Music name. Sony also acquired EMI Music Publishing in stages, completing full ownership in 2018, making Sony the world's largest music publisher.

Labels and operations

Major labels include Columbia Records, RCA Records, Epic Records, and Arista Records, plus Nashville, Latin, and classical divisions. Through Sony Music Group, formed in 2019, the recorded-music and publishing businesses are coordinated globally.

Artists

Its rosters have included Michael Jackson, Beyoncé, Adele, Bruce Springsteen, Bob Dylan, and many others across genres.
