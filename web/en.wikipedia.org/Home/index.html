Home

A home, or domicile, is a space used as a permanent or semi-permanent residence for one or many human occupants, and sometimes various companion animals. It is a fully or semi sheltered space and can have both interior and exterior aspects to it. Homes provide sheltered spaces, for instance rooms, where domestic activity can be performed such as sleeping, preparing food, eating, and hygiene, as well as providing spaces for work and leisure such as remote working, studying, and playing.

Types

Homes take many forms, including houses, apartments, condominiums, and informal dwellings. The physical forms vary across cultures and climates, from caves and tents to high-rise apartment blocks. Legally, a home is often distinguished from a house: "home" connotes the social and emotional relationship of residents to the place, and in many jurisdictions dwellings enjoy special legal protections, such as rights against unreasonable search.

Social significance

The concept of home carries strong emotional and cultural weight, associated with family, security, identity, and belonging. Its absence, homelessness, is a major social problem worldwide. Home design and domestic architecture reflect changing social structures, from multigenerational households to single-person dwellings.
