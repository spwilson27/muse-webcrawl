Neurodevelopmental disorder

Neurodevelopmental disorders are a group of conditions that begin in the developmental period, typically before a child enters school, and are characterised by developmental deficits that produce impairments of personal, social, academic, or occupational functioning.

Classification

The DSM-5 groups under this heading intellectual disabilities, communication disorders, autism spectrum disorder, attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), specific learning disorders, and motor disorders such as developmental coordination disorder and tic disorders. The ICD-11 uses a similar grouping.

Causes and management

Causes are multifactorial, involving genetic variants, prenatal exposures such as alcohol, prematurity, and environmental factors. Disorders frequently co-occur. Management is supportive and individualised, including early intervention, special education, behavioural therapy, speech and occupational therapy, and medication for specific symptoms such as inattention or irritability.
