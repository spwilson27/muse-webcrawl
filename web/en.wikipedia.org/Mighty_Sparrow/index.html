Mighty Sparrow

Slinger Francisco (born 9 July 1935), better known as Mighty Sparrow, is a Trinidadian calypso singer, songwriter, and guitarist. Widely regarded as one of the greatest calypsonians of all time, he is known as the "Calypso King of the World" for his witty, socially and politically pointed lyrics, delivered in a distinctive voice, and for his energetic stage performances.

Born in Grand Roy, Grenada, Sparrow moved as a child to Trinidad, where he rose to fame in the 1950s with songs such as "Jean and Dinah" (1956), with which he won the Calypso King title at Carnival. He won the Calypso Monarch competition eight times and the Road March title eight times. His extensive catalogue includes commentaries on Caribbean politics, taxation ("Pay as You Earn"), federation, and social life, as well as classics like "Only a Fool Breaks His Own Heart" and "Sparrow Dead".

Recognition

Sparrow received numerous honours, including the Order of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago, a Nigerian chieftaincy title, and an honorary doctorate from the University of the West Indies. His career spanned more than six decades and helped carry calypso to international audiences.
