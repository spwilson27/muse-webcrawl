6 (six) is the natural number following 5 and preceding 7. It is a composite number and the smallest perfect number.

In mathematics

Six is the first perfect number: its proper divisors 1, 2, and 3 sum to 6 itself. It is the only number that is both the sum and the product of the same three numbers (1 + 2 + 3 = 1 × 2 × 3 = 6). Six is a triangular number, a hexagonal number, and the smallest number that is neither a prime nor a square. A six-sided polygon is a hexagon, and six equilateral triangles fit around a point in the plane. The smallest non-abelian group, the symmetric group S3, has order 6.

In science

Six is the atomic number of carbon, the element on which all known life is based. Insects have six legs, and snowflakes typically display six-fold symmetry. A standard die has six faces.

In culture

Six is the number of points on the Star of David, the number of strings on a standard guitar, and the number of players on a volleyball or ice hockey team on the court or ice at once. In many East Asian traditions influenced by Chinese culture, six (六, liù) is considered lucky because it sounds like the word for "flow" or "smooth."
