Born16 November 1976, Sagamihara, Kanagawa, Japan

Hiroyuki Nishimura (西村 博之, born 1976) is a Japanese entrepreneur best known as the founder of the textboard website 2channel (2ch) and for purchasing the imageboard 4chan from its founder Christopher Poole in 2015. His nickname online is hiroyuki.

Career

Nishimura founded 2channel in 1999 while studying in the United States; it grew into one of Japan's largest and most influential online forums. He was also involved with the video-sharing site Niconico. After disputes over the ownership and management of 2channel, he launched 2ch.sc, and a rival administration continued the original board as 5channel.

4chan

In September 2015 Poole announced that Nishimura had acquired 4chan, citing their shared history with anonymous textboard culture. Nishimura, who has lived in Paris, is also a bestselling author and media commentator in Japan.
