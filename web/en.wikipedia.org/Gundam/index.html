Gundam

Gundam is a Japanese science fiction media franchise created by Yoshiyuki Tomino and the studio Sunrise (now Bandai Namco Filmworks), centred on giant piloted combat robots called mobile suits. It began with the television anime Mobile Suit Gundam in 1979 and has grown into one of the highest-grossing media franchises in the world.

Mobile Suit Gundam (1979)

The original series depicted a war between the Earth Federation and the Principality of Zeon in the "Universal Century", fought with mobile suits including the RX-78-2 Gundam piloted by teenager Amuro Ray against the ace Char Aznable. Its realistic treatment of war, flawed characters and politics — the "real robot" approach — set it apart from earlier super-robot anime. Though its first broadcast had modest ratings, reruns and compilation films made it a phenomenon.

Franchise and model kits

Dozens of sequels, spin-offs and alternate-universe series followed, including Zeta Gundam, Gundam Wing, Gundam SEED and Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury. The franchise is equally famous for its plastic model kits, "Gunpla", sold since 1980 in grades from High Grade to the intricate Perfect Grade; Gunpla sales are a major revenue source for Bandai. Life-size Gundam statues in Japan, including a moving 18-metre Gundam in Yokohama (2020–2024), have become tourist attractions.
