The swimming competitions at the 2008 Summer Olympics in Beijing took place from 9 to 17 August 2008 at the Beijing National Aquatics Center, nicknamed the "Water Cube," with the new 10-kilometre open water marathon events held on 20 and 21 August at the Shunyi Olympic Rowing-Canoeing Park. A total of 34 events were contested.

Highlights

The meet was dominated by American swimmer Michael Phelps, who won eight gold medals — in every event he entered — breaking Mark Spitz's record of seven golds at a single Olympics set in 1972. His victories included a famous fingertip win over Milorad Čavić in the 100 metre butterfly and a world-record men's 4×100 metre freestyle relay in which Jason Lezak overtook France's Alain Bernard on the final leg. Phelps finished his career with a record 28 Olympic medals, 23 of them gold.

Records and technology

An extraordinary 25 world records were broken at the meet. The speed of the times was widely attributed in part to the LZR Racer full-body swimsuits introduced by Speedo in 2008 and to the Water Cube pool itself, which was deeper and wider than previous Olympic pools, reducing turbulence. The controversy over high-technology suits led FINA to ban non-textile suits from 2010. Other stars of the meet included Britain's Rebecca Adlington, who won the 400 and 800 metre freestyle, and Germany's Britta Steffen, who won both women's sprint freestyle titles.

Medal table

The United States topped the swimming medal table with 12 golds and 31 medals overall, followed by Australia. Host nation China's swimmers won one gold, through Liu Zige in the women's 200 metre butterfly.
