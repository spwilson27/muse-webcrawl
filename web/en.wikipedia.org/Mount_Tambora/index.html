Mount Tambora

Mount Tambora is an active stratovolcano on the island of Sumbawa in Indonesia. Its eruption of April 1815 was the largest volcanic eruption in recorded history, rated VEI-7, ejecting roughly 150 km³ of material and killing at least 71,000 people directly and by famine.

The Year Without a Summer

The eruption injected vast quantities of sulfate aerosols into the stratosphere, causing global climate anomalies: 1816 became the "Year Without a Summer", with crop failures across Europe and North America, snow in July, and food riots and famine. The eruption destroyed the small kingdom of Tambora and its language; the summit, once about 4,300 m, was reduced to 2,851 m, leaving a caldera 6–7 km wide. The gloomy summer of 1816 indirectly inspired Mary Shelley's Frankenstein, written during a stormbound stay on Lake Geneva.
