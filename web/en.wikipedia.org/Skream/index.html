Skream (born Oliver Dene Jones, 1 June 1986) is an English electronic music producer and DJ. One of the pioneers of dubstep, he helped shape the genre from its beginnings in South London's UK garage scene in the early 2000s.

Career

Jones grew up in Croydon and worked at the Big Apple Records shop in the town, a hub of the early dubstep scene, where he began producing tracks as a teenager using PlayStation software before moving to FruityLoops. His 2005 track "Midnight Request Line" became a breakthrough anthem for the genre, championed by DJs across UK garage, grime and techno. His debut album Skream! followed in 2006, and the remix of La Roux's "In for the Kill" (2009) brought his sound to a mainstream audience.

Magnetic Man and later work

With fellow Big Apple alumni Benga and Artwork, Skream formed the dubstep supergroup Magnetic Man, whose self-titled 2010 album reached the UK top five. In the 2010s Jones increasingly moved away from dubstep toward house, techno and disco, becoming a fixture of clubs and festivals as a DJ and releasing music under projects including his Open to Close all-night sets.
