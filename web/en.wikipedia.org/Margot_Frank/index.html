Margot Frank

Margot Frank
Born: Margot Betti Frank, 16 February 1926, Frankfurt, Germany
Died: February or March 1945 (aged 19), Bergen-Belsen concentration camp
Known for: Elder sister of Anne Frank

Margot Betti Frank (16 February 1926 – c. February/March 1945) was the elder daughter of Otto and Edith Frank and the elder sister of diarist Anne Frank. She is known through Anne's diary, in which she appears as a quiet, studious and diligent presence in the family's hiding place.

Life

Margot was born in Frankfurt and emigrated with her family to Amsterdam in 1933–34 after the Nazis came to power. She attended the Jewish Lyceum and excelled at school, with a particular interest in mathematics and Latin; friends recalled her plan to emigrate to Mandate Palestine and train as a midwife. According to Anne's diary, Margot also kept a diary of her own, which was never found.

Hiding and deportation

On 5 July 1942 Margot received a call-up notice for a labour camp, which prompted the family to go into hiding the next day in the secret annex behind Otto Frank's business premises. The eight occupants were arrested on 4 August 1944 and deported; Margot and Anne were sent via Auschwitz to Bergen-Belsen in late 1944. Weakened by hunger, cold and a typhus epidemic, Margot died there shortly before Anne, in February or March 1945, only weeks before the camp's liberation.

Legacy

Margot is commemorated together with her family at the Anne Frank House in Amsterdam, and her surviving letters, school reports and a handful of photographs are held in its collections.
