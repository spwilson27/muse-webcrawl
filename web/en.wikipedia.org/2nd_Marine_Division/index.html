2nd Marine Division

The 2nd Marine Division is a United States Marine Corps division headquartered at Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune, North Carolina. It is the ground combat element of II Marine Expeditionary Force and specialises in amphibious and expeditionary warfare.

World War II

Activated on 1 February 1941 at Camp Elliott, California, the division fought across the Pacific, including the Guadalcanal campaign, the Battle of Tarawa in November 1943, the battles of Saipan and Tinian, Okinawa, and supporting operations at Iwo Jima. The assault on Tarawa's Betio island became one of the best-known actions in the division's history.

Cold War and after

After the war the division relocated to Camp Lejeune. Elements deployed to Lebanon in 1958 and 1982–84, participated in the Cuban Missile Crisis, and fought as part of I Marine Expeditionary Force in the Gulf War of 1991.

Recent operations

Division units were heavily committed to the Iraq War, including the 2003 invasion and counter-insurgency operations in Al Anbar Province, and to the war in Afghanistan. The division continues to train for distributed maritime operations.
