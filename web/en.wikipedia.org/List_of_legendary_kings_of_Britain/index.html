List of legendary kings of Britain

The legendary kings of Britain are the monarchs named in the medieval chronicle tradition, principally Geoffrey of Monmouth's Historia Regum Britanniae (c. 1136) and the earlier Historia Brittonum attributed to Nennius. Geoffrey traced the British royal line from Brutus of Troy, a supposed descendant of Aeneas, through more than a hundred kings down to the 7th century AD. These kings are generally considered legendary rather than historical, though a few — such as Cunobelinus and Cassivellaunus — are based on real Iron Age rulers known from Roman sources and coinage.
