List of leaders of the Soviet Union

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The leaders of the Soviet Union were the heads of government and, in practice, the paramount rulers of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics (USSR) from its founding in 1922 until its dissolution in 1991. Power was concentrated in the Communist Party of the Soviet Union, and the party's General Secretary (a title that changed over time) was usually the country's de facto leader, sometimes combined with the offices of head of government (Premier) or head of state (Chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme Soviet, later President).

The office

The Soviet Union had no single office equivalent to a president for most of its history. Formally, the head of state was the chairman of the legislature's presidium, while the head of government was the premier. Real power, however, lay with the leader of the Communist Party. Stalin consolidated personal dictatorship by the 1930s; after his death in 1953, leadership became more collective, though Khrushchev and Brezhnev each accumulated dominant authority. Gorbachev created an executive presidency in 1990 as part of his reforms, becoming the first and only President of the Soviet Union before the state dissolved in December 1991.
