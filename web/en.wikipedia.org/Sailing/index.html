Sailing

Sailing employs wind-acting on sails, wingsails or kites to propel a craft on the surface of the water (sailing ship, sailboat, windsurfer), on ice (iceboat) or on land (land yacht) over a chosen course. For millennia it was the principal means of maritime transport and exploration; today it is chiefly recreational and competitive.

Points of sail

A vessel's course relative to the wind is described by its point of sail: in irons (head to wind), close-hauled, close reach, beam reach, broad reach and running. Sail trim and heading are continuously adjusted to the apparent wind, which combines the true wind with the wind created by the vessel's own motion.

Sport

Sailing has been an Olympic sport since 1900. Major events include the America's Cup, the Ocean Race and the Vendée Globe single-handed race. Dinghy and keelboat racing under the Racing Rules of Sailing is organised by World Sailing and national authorities.
