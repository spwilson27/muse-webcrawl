Mahabaleshwar is a hill station and municipal council in Satara district, Maharashtra, India, located in the Western Ghats (Sahyadri range) at an elevation of about 1,353 metres. It was the summer capital of the Bombay Presidency during British rule and remains one of Maharashtra's most popular tourist destinations.

Geography and attractions

The town is the source of the Krishna River, which rises at the Mahadeo temple at Old Mahabaleshwar. Famous viewpoints include Arthur's Seat, Kate's Point, Needle Hole Point and Wilson Point, the highest point in the town. Nearby attractions include the Venna Lake, the Pratapgad fort associated with Shivaji, and the neighbouring hill station of Panchgani.

Strawberries

Mahabaleshwar is renowned for its strawberries; the Mahabaleshwar strawberry received a Geographical Indication (GI) tag. The region accounts for a large share of India's strawberry production, and strawberries with cream are a signature local delicacy.
