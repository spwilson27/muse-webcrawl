Herbie, the "Love Bug"

Herbie is a fictional anthropomorphic 1963 Volkswagen Beetle, a character featured in several Walt Disney Pictures films starting with The Love Bug (1968). Herbie has a mind of its own, is capable of driving itself, and can perform extraordinary feats such as wheelies and driving up walls; it communicates by honking, rocking, and squirting oil.

Character and appearance

Herbie is distinguished by red, white and blue racing stripes from front to rear bumper, a pearl-white paint job, and the racing number "53" in roundels on the doors, front bonnet and engine lid. Producer Bill Walsh chose the number 53 in honour of baseball player Don Drysdale; the name "Herbie" came from a crew member's recollection of a joke by comedian Buddy Hackett. More than a hundred Beetles were prepared for the films, modified for effects such as driverless operation and wheelies; only a fraction survive in collectors' hands.

Films

The original series comprises The Love Bug (1968), Herbie Rides Again (1974), Herbie Goes to Monte Carlo (1977) and Herbie Goes Bananas (1980). A made-for-television remake of The Love Bug followed in 1997, and the theatrical film Herbie: Fully Loaded (2005), starring Lindsay Lohan, revived the character for a new generation. Herbie remains one of Disney's most recognisable live-action properties, with replicas displayed at Disney parks and museums.
