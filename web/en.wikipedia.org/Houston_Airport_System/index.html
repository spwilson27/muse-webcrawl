Houston Airport System

The Houston Airport System (HAS) is an entity within the City of Houston government that administers the city's airports. It operates three facilities: George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH), William P. Hobby Airport (HOU), and Ellington Airport (EFD), which also serves as the Houston Spaceport.

George Bush Intercontinental Airport is the system's primary international gateway.

Airports

George Bush Intercontinental Airport, opened in 1969, is Houston's principal international airport, serving more than 40 million passengers a year and acting as a major hub for United Airlines. William P. Hobby Airport, the city's older commercial airport dating to the 1920s, handles mainly domestic services and is a focus base for Southwest Airlines; it returned to international service in 2015. Ellington Airport, a joint civil-military facility obtained from the U.S. Air Force in 1984, supports military operations, NASA flights, and general aviation, and was licensed by the Federal Aviation Administration as a commercial spaceport in 2015.

Operations

The system serves tens of millions of passengers annually and describes its mission as providing safe, efficient, and customer-friendly air service for the Houston region. Its director of aviation is appointed by the mayor of Houston. Capital projects have included international terminal development at Bush Intercontinental and concourse expansion at Hobby.
