FS1

FS1 (German: Fernsehen 1, "Television 1") was the first television channel of the Austrian Broadcasting Corporation (ORF). It was launched on 1 August 1955, when regular television broadcasting began in Austria, and was known simply as ORF1 until a rebranding in the early 1990s. In 1992 the channel was renamed FS1's successor service, ORF eins, often stylised as ORF 1.

History

Television trials in Austria began in the 1950s. Regular broadcasting commenced on 1 August 1955 with a small schedule of programming broadcast a few hours per day. As television ownership grew through the late 1950s and 1960s, the channel expanded its schedule to include news, cultural programming, films and sport. A second channel, FS2, followed in 1961, allowing regional programming and a broader range of content.

During the FS1 era the flagship news programme Zeit im Bild was a central part of the schedule. The channel became known for its mix of Austrian domestic productions, imported series and major sporting events such as football internationals and ski racing.

Rebranding

In October 1992 ORF undertook a major relaunch of its television services. FS1 became ORF 1 (marketed as ORF eins), and FS2 became ORF 2. The relaunch introduced new on-screen identities and a clearer distinction between the two channels, with ORF 1 aimed more at entertainment and younger audiences.
