Prolactinoma

A prolactinoma is a benign tumor (adenoma) of the pituitary gland that produces excess prolactin. It is the most common type of functioning pituitary adenoma.

Symptoms and diagnosis

Raised prolactin (hyperprolactinemia) causes galactorrhea, amenorrhea and infertility in women, and erectile dysfunction and low libido in men; large tumors (macroprolactinomas, ≥10 mm) can compress the optic chiasm, causing visual field defects, and cause hypopituitarism. Diagnosis uses serum prolactin measurement and pituitary MRI.

Treatment

Dopamine agonists — cabergoline preferred over bromocriptine — normalize prolactin and shrink most tumors. Surgery (transsphenoidal) and radiotherapy are reserved for resistant or intolerant cases.
