HC Davos is a Swiss professional ice hockey club based in the Alpine resort town of Davos, founded in 1921. Playing in Switzerland's National League, it is the country's most successful club, with a record 31 Swiss championship titles, and it hosts the Spengler Cup, the world's oldest invitational ice hockey tournament.

History

Davos dominated Swiss hockey in the 1920s–40s and again under long-time coach Arno Del Curto (1996–2018), whose attacking style won six titles and made the club a fixture of European competition. The club has developed numerous NHL and international players, and its yellow-and-blue jerseys are among the most recognisable in Swiss sport.

Spengler Cup

Since 1923 the club has staged the Spengler Cup each December, inviting club teams and a select Team Canada; HC Davos has won the trophy a record number of times, most recently in 2023. The tournament is a highlight of the European hockey calendar and a major event for the Davos resort economy.
