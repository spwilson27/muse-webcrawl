Zakopane

Zakopane is a town in southern Poland, in the Lesser Poland Voivodeship, lying at the foot of the Tatra Mountains. With a population of roughly 27,000, it is Poland's most famous mountain resort and is often called the country's "winter capital".

Geography

The town sits in a valley between the Tatra range to the south and Gubałówka Hill to the north. Nearby peaks include Giewont, whose distinctive cross-topped silhouette dominates the town's skyline.

Tourism and sport

Zakopane is a centre for skiing, ski jumping (at the Wielka Krokiew hill), hiking, and climbing. It has hosted World Cup ski jumping events and numerous world championships in winter sports. In the late nineteenth century it became a cultural hub for Polish artists and writers, and the distinctive Zakopane style of wooden architecture developed there.
