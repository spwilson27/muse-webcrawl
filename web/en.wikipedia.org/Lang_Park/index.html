Lang Park, currently known as Suncorp Stadium for sponsorship reasons, is a multi-purpose stadium in Milton, Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. Built on the site of a former cemetery and the historic Lang Park sporting ground, it is regarded as the home of rugby league in Queensland and hosts the Brisbane Broncos, Queensland Reds, Brisbane Roar and State of Origin matches.

History

Lang Park was established as a sporting ground in 1914, named after John Dunmore Lang. It became rugby league's main Brisbane venue in the 1950s.

Redevelopment

The stadium was redeveloped into a modern all-seater venue, reopening in 2003, with a capacity of around 52,500. The statue of Wally Lewis outside is an icon of Queensland sport.

Events

Lang Park hosted matches at the 2003 Rugby World Cup, the 2015 AFC Asian Cup, the 2017 Rugby League World Cup final, and the 2023 FIFA Women's World Cup.
