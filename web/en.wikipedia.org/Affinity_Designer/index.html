Affinity Designer
Developer: Serif
Initial release: 1 October 2014
Platforms: macOS, Windows, iPadOS
Type: Vector graphics editor

Affinity Designer is a vector graphics editor developed by Serif for macOS, Microsoft Windows and iPadOS. It is part of the Affinity suite alongside Affinity Photo (raster editing) and Affinity Publisher (desktop publishing). It was first released for Mac in October 2014, followed by a Windows version in 2016 and an iPad version in 2018.

The application combines vector design tools with raster ("pixel") editing in separate workspaces called personas, supports unlimited zoom up to 1,000,000%, non-destructive effects and adjustments, and can open and export formats including PDF, SVG, EPS, PSD and AI. It has frequently been positioned as an alternative to Adobe Illustrator, sold initially under a one-time purchase model rather than a subscription. Affinity Designer won an Apple Design Award in 2015. Version 2 was released in November 2022. Serif was acquired by Canva in 2024.
