Prepar3D

Prepar3D (pronounced "prepared") is a visual simulation platform developed by Lockheed Martin. It evolved from Microsoft's ESP commercial simulation software, itself based on Microsoft Flight Simulator X technology, which Lockheed Martin licensed in 2009.

Overview

The software allows users to create training scenarios across aviation, maritime and ground domains. It supports the entire flight envelope from subsea to suborbital space. Under its licence terms it is sold for professional and academic training use rather than consumer entertainment.

Versions

The initial version was released in November 2010. Subsequent major versions (v2 in 2013, v3 in 2015, v4 in 2017, and v5 in 2020) added 64-bit support, physically based rendering, and dynamic lighting, among other improvements.
