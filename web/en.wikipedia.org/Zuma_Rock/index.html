Zuma Rock

Zuma Rock
Location: Niger State, Nigeria
Elevation: about 725 metres above sea level
Type: Igneous intrusion (monolith)

Zuma Rock is a large natural monolith in Niger State, Nigeria, just west of the capital Abuja along the main road from Abuja to Kaduna. It is sometimes called the "Gateway to Abuja from Suleja". The igneous intrusion rises to about 725 metres above sea level and roughly 300 metres above its surroundings.

Description

The rock's face bears natural contours that resemble a human face, with features suggestive of eyes, nose and mouth — a likeness often remarked on by travellers. Zuma Rock is depicted on the Nigerian 100-naira banknote.

Cultural significance

The rock holds significance for the Gbagyi people of the area, with traditions and legends attached to it, including beliefs about its protective and spiritual power. It is one of Nigeria's best-known natural landmarks.
