Belarus at the Olympics

Belarus has competed in the Olympic Games as an independent nation since the 1994 Winter Olympics in Lillehammer. Previously, Belarusian athletes competed as part of the Soviet Union team (1952–1988) and, following the dissolution of the Soviet Union, as part of the Unified Team in 1992.

History

The National Olympic Committee of the Republic of Belarus was formed in 1991 and recognised by the International Olympic Committee in 1993. Belarus first appeared at the Summer Olympics at Atlanta 1996, winning 15 medals. Its most successful Summer Games was Sydney 2000, with 17 medals.

Medals

Belarusian athletes have won medals particularly in athletics, canoeing, rowing, gymnastics, freestyle skiing and biathlon. Notable champions include gymnast Vitaly Scherbo (competing for the Unified Team in 1992), sprinter Yulia Nestsiarenka, shot putter Nadzeya Astapchuk (later disqualified), biathlete Darya Domracheva and trampoline gymnast Ivan Litvinovich.

2024 suspension

Following Russia's invasion of Ukraine in 2022, in which Belarus was complicit, Belarusian athletes were barred from competing under their flag at the Paris 2024 Games; a small number competed as Individual Neutral Athletes.
