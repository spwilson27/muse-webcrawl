Muggletonianism

FoundersLodowicke Muggleton, John Reeve
Founded1652, London, England
TypeProtestant sect / new religious movement

Muggletonianism was an English Protestant sect founded in 1652 in London by Lodowicke Muggleton and his cousin John Reeve, two tailors who claimed to be the "two witnesses" of Revelation 11. The movement was among the many radical religious groups that emerged during the English Civil War and Interregnum.

Beliefs

Muggletonians held distinctive doctrines, including that God had ceased to take direct notice of events on Earth after the incarnation, that the soul is mortal, and a rejection of Trinitarian theology and organised worship; members generally met informally in taverns or homes rather than churches.

History

The sect never had large numbers but proved remarkably long-lived, maintaining a small community in London into the 20th century; the last known Muggletonian died in 1979. Its archive of writings and letters survives.
