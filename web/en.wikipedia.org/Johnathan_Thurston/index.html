Johnathan Dean Thurston (born 25 April 1983) is an Australian former professional rugby league footballer who played as a halfback. Regarded as one of the greatest players in the sport's history, he captained the North Queensland Cowboys to their first NRL premiership in 2015.

Career

Thurston, of Indigenous Australian and New Zealand Māori heritage, debuted for the Canterbury-Bankstown Bulldogs in 2002 and won a premiership with them in 2004. Joining the Cowboys in 2005, he became the game's dominant playmaker, winning a record four Dally M Medals (2005, 2007, 2014, 2015) and the Golden Boot Award in 2011. His field goal in golden-point extra time decided the 2015 Grand Final against Brisbane.

Representative career

For Queensland, Thurston was central to the Maroons' era of State of Origin dominance, and for Australia he won the 2013 Rugby League World Cup and multiple Four Nations titles.

Legacy

Thurston retired at the end of 2018. A prominent Indigenous role model, he has worked extensively in education and community programmes in North Queensland; a statue of him stands outside the Cowboys' home stadium in Townsville.
