Ez-Zitouna University

Ez-Zitouna University is a public university in Tunis, Tunisia. Its origins trace to the teaching circles of the Great Mosque of Zitouna, founded around 737, making it one of the oldest centres of Islamic learning in the world. The modern university was formally established in 1956.

Ez-Zitouna University
Type: Public
Established: 1956 (origins c. 737)
Location: Tunis, Tunisia

History

For centuries, the Zitouna mosque-university attracted scholars from across the Maghreb and taught theology, law, Arabic language, and the sciences. Under the French protectorate and after independence its role was reorganised, and religious instruction was separated into new institutions.

Structure

The university includes the Higher Institute of Theology and the Higher Institute of Islamic Civilisation, among other units.
