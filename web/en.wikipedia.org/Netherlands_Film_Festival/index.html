Netherlands Film Festival

The Netherlands Film Festival (Dutch: Nederlands Film Festival) is an annual film festival held in Utrecht, Netherlands, usually in late September or early October. Founded in 1981, it is the principal showcase for Dutch cinema and television production.

History

The festival was founded by filmmaker Jos Stelling, who organised the first edition in 1981 under the name Dutch Film Days (Nederlandse Filmdagen). It grew from a small gathering of film professionals into the main national platform for Dutch film, drawing tens of thousands of visitors each year.

Golden Calves

The festival's most famous prizes are the Golden Calves (Gouden Kalveren), awarded in categories such as Best Feature Film, Best Director, Best Actor and Best Actress. The Golden Calf is the best-known film award in the Netherlands. Since 2021, the acting categories have been gender-neutral.

Programme

Alongside premieres of new Dutch features, documentaries, shorts and television productions, the festival hosts the Holland Film Meeting, an industry programme connecting Dutch filmmakers with international producers, sales agents and festival programmers. The Netherlands Film Festival also presents retrospectives, debates and educational programmes for young audiences.
