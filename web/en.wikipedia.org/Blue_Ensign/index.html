Blue Ensign

The Blue Ensign is a British ensign consisting of a blue field with the Union Jack in the canton. It is flown by certain British government vessels, by Royal Navy auxiliaries, and—often defaced with a badge or emblem—by vessels and organisations authorised by warrant.

History

The Blue Ensign originated in the 17th century as one of the ensigns of the squadrons of the Royal Navy. The 1864 reorganisation of naval ensigns reserved the White Ensign for the Royal Navy, the Red Ensign for the merchant service, and the Blue Ensign for government and naval auxiliary vessels.

Defaced ensigns

Many British colonies and overseas territories adopted defaced Blue Ensigns as their flags, including historically Australia, New Zealand, Fiji and numerous Caribbean and Pacific territories.
