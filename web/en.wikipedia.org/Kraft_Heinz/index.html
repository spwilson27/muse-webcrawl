The Kraft Heinz Company is an American multinational food and beverage company formed by the 2015 merger of Kraft Foods Group and H. J. Heinz Company. Headquartered jointly in Chicago and Pittsburgh, it is one of the largest food companies in the world, selling products in more than 190 countries.

History

The merger was orchestrated by Berkshire Hathaway and the Brazilian private-equity firm 3G Capital, which had acquired Heinz in 2013. The combined company brought together Heinz ketchup and beans with Kraft cheese products, Oscar Mayer meats, Philadelphia cream cheese and Planters nuts. The aggressive cost-cutting culture of the new owners drew scrutiny, and in 2019 the company announced a $15.4 billion write-down of its Kraft and Oscar Mayer brands amid falling sales and an investigation into its accounting practices.

Products and brands

Its portfolio includes Heinz Tomato Ketchup, Kraft Macaroni & Cheese (Kraft Dinner in Canada), Maxwell House coffee, Jell-O, Lunchables, Capri Sun (under licence in some markets) and Classico sauces. The company has sought to revive growth by renovating core brands and expanding into snacks and foodservice.
