Nouri Kamil Muhammad-Hasan al-Maliki (born 20 June 1950), also known as Jawad al-Maliki, is an Iraqi politician who served as Prime Minister of Iraq from 2006 to 2014. A Shia Muslim and longtime member of the Islamic Dawa Party, he spent much of Saddam Hussein's era in exile in Iran and Syria.

Premiership

Maliki became prime minister in 2006 during the Iraq War and sectarian civil war. His first term saw the US troop surge and a reduction in violence; he was re-elected in 2010 after months of deadlock. His second term drew criticism from Sunni and Kurdish leaders over centralisation of power and sectarian grievances.

Fall from power

After the Islamic State seized Mosul in June 2014 and the Iraqi army collapsed in the north, pressure mounted on Maliki to step aside. He relinquished the premiership to Haider al-Abadi in September 2014, subsequently serving as a vice president and remaining leader of the State of Law Coalition.
