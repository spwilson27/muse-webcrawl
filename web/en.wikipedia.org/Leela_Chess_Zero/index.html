Leela Chess Zero

Leela Chess Zero (Lc0) is a free, open-source neural-network chess engine, launched in 2018 as a community, distributed-computing adaptation of DeepMind's AlphaZero approach to chess.

Method

Lc0 learns by self-play reinforcement learning: volunteers donate computing power to generate games with which its deep neural network is trained, and the resulting network weights are publicly distributed. It uses Monte Carlo tree search guided by the network and runs best on GPUs.

Results

Leela won the Top Chess Engine Championship and other computer-chess events, competing closely with the traditional engine Stockfish, and is widely used for analysis.
