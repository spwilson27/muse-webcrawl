The South Australia cricket team, named the West End Redbacks for sponsorship, is an Australian men's professional first-class cricket team based in Adelaide, South Australia. It represents the state in the Sheffield Shield and the One-Day Cup, and is administered by the South Australian Cricket Association.

History

South Australia has played first-class cricket since the 19th century and was a founding participant in the Sheffield Shield in 1892–93, which it has won multiple times, though historically less often than New South Wales or Victoria. Its home has long been the Adelaide Oval, one of the world's most picturesque Test venues.

Notable players

The state produced the Bradman legacy after Sir Donald Bradman moved from New South Wales in 1934, as well as Clarrie Grimmett, and brothers Ian and Greg Chappell. In the modern era, players such as Travis Head, Alex Carey, and Darren Lehmann (later coach) have been central figures.

Colours and identity

The team's colours are red, gold, and blue, shared with the state's other sporting teams; the "Redbacks" nickname refers to the redback spider.
