Châtelet–Les Halles

Châtelet–Les Halles is a major railway hub station in the centre of Paris, France, and one of the largest underground stations in the world. It is served by three lines of the Réseau Express Régional (RER) — the RER A, RER B and RER D — and is directly connected to the Châtelet station of the Paris Métro, forming with it a single vast interchange complex.

Location and layout

The station lies beneath the Forum des Halles in the 1st arrondissement, on the site of the former central food market of Les Halles. The RER station opened in December 1977, when the RER A and RER B lines were joined in central Paris, and was extended in 1995 to accommodate the RER D. The station hall is arranged with platforms on several levels, linked by mezzanines, escalators and long travelators to the adjoining Métro station.

Usage

Châtelet–Les Halles is among the busiest stations in Europe, used by several hundred thousand passengers each day. Together with the connected Métro station Châtelet, which serves lines 1, 4, 7, 11 and 14, it provides interchange between five Métro lines and three RER lines. A major renovation of the station and the surrounding Forum des Halles quarter was completed in the 2010s, including the construction of the Canopée, a large glass-and-steel canopy above the complex.
