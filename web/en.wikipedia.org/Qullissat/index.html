Qullissat

Abandoned houses at Qullissat, Disko Island

Qullissat is a former coal-mining town on Disko Island in western Greenland, within the Qeqertalik municipality. Founded in 1924 to work a coal mine, it grew into one of Greenland's largest settlements, with a population of about 1,400 at its peak, before being abandoned in 1972.

History

The mine at Qullissat (Danish: Qutdligssat) was operated by the Danish state and supplied coal to much of Greenland. The town had a school, hospital, power station, shops, and a football team, and was an important centre of Greenlandic cultural and political life; the trade union movement in Greenland had roots there.

Closure and abandonment

In 1960 the Danish government decided the mine was uneconomic, and it was closed despite local protests. The town was evacuated by 1972 and its residents relocated, many to Ilulissat and Aasiaat. Qullissat became a potent symbol in Greenlandic politics of decisions imposed from Copenhagen, and the episode is frequently invoked in debates about Greenlandic self-determination. The empty buildings still stand, visited occasionally by former residents and their descendants.
