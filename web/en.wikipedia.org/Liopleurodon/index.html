Liopleurodon

Liopleurodon is a genus of large pliosaurid plesiosaur that lived in the seas of Europe during the Middle to Late Jurassic, about 166 to 155 million years ago.

Named by Henri Émile Sauvage in 1873 from teeth found in France, the best-known species, L. ferox, reached roughly 5 to 7 metres in length — far smaller than the exaggerated 25-metre estimates popularised by the BBC series Walking with Dinosaurs. It was an apex predator with four paddle-like flippers, a short neck and powerful jaws lined with conical teeth, feeding on fish, ichthyosaurs and other marine reptiles.
