Roni Size, a leading figure of the Bristol drum and bass scene.

Roni Size (born Ryan Owen Granville Williams, 29 October 1969) is a British DJ and record producer, and one of the most influential figures in drum and bass and jungle music. He came to prominence as leader of the live band Roni Size & Reprazent, whose 1997 album New Forms won the Mercury Prize and brought drum and bass to a mainstream audience.

Career

Williams grew up in the St Andrew's area of Bristol, absorbing reggae sound-system culture before becoming involved in the city's house-party and warehouse scene. With Krust, DJ Die and Suv he founded the Full Cycle label in 1993, central to the "Bristol sound" of drum and bass, and worked closely with Bryan Gee's V Recordings.

Reprazent combined Size's programming with live drums, double bass and vocals from Onallee and MC Dynamite. New Forms (1997), released on Talkin' Loud, mixed jazz, hip hop and breakbeat influences and won the Mercury Prize over nominees including Radiohead's OK Computer. The follow-up In the Møde (2000) leaned toward hip hop, and the second Reprazent album, New Forms², appeared in 2008.

Later work

Size's solo albums include Touching Down (2002) and Take Your Time (2014). He has continued to tour internationally as a DJ, marked 20 years of New Forms with orchestral live performances of the album, and remains a reference point for the fusion of electronic production with live musicianship in British dance music.
