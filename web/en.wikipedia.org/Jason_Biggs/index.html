Jason Biggs

Jason Matthew Biggs (born May 12, 1978) is an American actor best known for playing Jim Levenstein in the American Pie comedy film series (1999–2012). He also voiced Leonardo in the Nickelodeon animated series Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012–2014) and played Larry Bloom in the Netflix series Orange Is the New Black (2013–2019).

Early life

Biggs was born in Pompton Plains, New Jersey, and raised in Hasbrouck Heights. He began acting at the age of five and appeared in television commercials before making his Broadway debut in Conversations with My Father (1992).

Career

After roles in the soap opera As the World Turns, for which he received a Daytime Emmy nomination, Biggs achieved fame with American Pie (1999) and its sequels. His other films include Loser (2000), Saving Silverman (2001), Anything Else (2003) and Jersey Girl (2004). On television he starred in the sitcom Outmatched (2020).

Personal life

Biggs married actress Jenny Mollen in 2008; they have two sons.
