Akwa Ibom is a state in the South-South geopolitical zone of Nigeria, bordered to the east by Cross River State, to the west by Rivers State and Abia State, and to the south by the Atlantic Ocean. Named for the Qua Iboe River, which bisects the state before flowing into the Bight of Bonny, it was created on 23 September 1987 by the military government of Ibrahim Babangida out of the former Cross River State.

Geography and people

The state's capital is Uyo, and its largest ethnic groups are the Ibibio, Annang and Oron peoples. Akwa Ibom is one of Nigeria's largest oil- and gas-producing states, with offshore fields operated from terminals near Eket and the Qua Iboe export terminal.

Economy

Petroleum production dominates revenue, alongside agriculture such as palm oil, cassava, cocoyam and fishing along its Atlantic coastline. The state hosts Victor Attah International Airport and the Ibom Deep Seaport project at Ibaka.
