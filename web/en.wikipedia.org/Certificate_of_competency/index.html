Certificate of competency

A certificate of competency is a formal document issued by a competent authority certifying that its holder has demonstrated the knowledge and skills required for a particular occupation or task. The term is used in several fields, including maritime, mining, electrical, and lifting-equipment work.

Maritime use

In shipping, certificates of competency are issued to masters, officers, and engineers under the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers (STCW). Grades range from officer of the watch to master mariner, each requiring examinations, sea service, and medical fitness.

Other fields

Mining managers, blasters, electricians, and crane and forklift operators in many countries must hold statutory certificates of competency, obtained through examination and experience, and sometimes subject to periodic renewal to ensure safety in high-risk work.
