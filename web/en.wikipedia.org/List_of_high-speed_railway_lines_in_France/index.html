List of high-speed railway lines in France

France pioneered high-speed rail in Europe with the TGV, beginning with the LGV Sud-Est between Paris and Lyon in 1981. The network of lignes à grande vitesse (LGV) now spans more than 2,800 km, with trains running at up to 320 km/h in commercial service.

World record

In 2007, a modified TGV set the world rail speed record of 574.8 km/h on the LGV Est.
