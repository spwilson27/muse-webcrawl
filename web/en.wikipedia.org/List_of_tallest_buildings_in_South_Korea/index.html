List of tallest buildings in South Korea

South Korea's tallest building is the Lotte World Tower in Seoul, a 123-storey, 555-metre supertall skyscraper completed in 2016 and among the tallest buildings in the world. Busan, South Korea's second city, hosts the country's next cluster of supertalls in the Haeundae district, including the LCT The Sharp towers and Haeundae Doosan We've the Zenith. Incheon is home to the Northeast Asia Trade Tower in Songdo International City.

History

For two decades after 1985, the gold-clad 63 Building on Yeouido was Seoul's emblematic skyscraper. The 2000s brought a wave of tall residential towers, especially in Busan's Haeundae beachfront district, and the 2010s added office supertalls in Seoul's Yeouido and Jamsil districts, culminating in Lotte World Tower — the fifth-tallest building in the world at its completion.
