Kishore Kumar

Kishore Kumar (born Abhas Kumar Ganguly, 4 August 1929 – 13 October 1987) was an Indian playback singer, actor, composer and director, widely regarded as one of the greatest singers in the history of Indian cinema.

Career

Starting in the late 1940s, he became the singing voice of stars such as Dev Anand, Rajesh Khanna and Amitabh Bachchan, recording thousands of songs in Hindi and other languages, famous for his yodelling and versatility across romantic, comic and melancholy numbers. He won a record eight Filmfare Awards for Best Male Playback Singer.

Acting and legacy

As an actor he starred in comedies including Chalti Ka Naam Gaadi (1958) and Padosan (1968). His songs remain staples of Indian popular culture decades after his death.
