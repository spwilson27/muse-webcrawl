List of mayors of New York City

The mayor of New York City is the chief executive of the city's government. The office traces to 1665 under English rule; the modern consolidated city dates from 1898.

MayorTerm
Thomas Willett1665, first mayor
Fernando Wood1850s–1860s
Fiorello La Guardia1934–1945
Robert F. Wagner Jr.1954–1965
John Lindsay1966–1973
Ed Koch1978–1989
David Dinkins1990–1993
Rudy Giuliani1994–2001
Michael Bloomberg2002–2013
Bill de Blasio2014–2021
Eric Adams2022–2025

Mayors serve four-year terms and, under current rules, are limited to two consecutive terms.
