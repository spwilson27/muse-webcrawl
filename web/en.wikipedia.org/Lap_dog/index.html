Lap dog

A lap dog or lapdog is a dog small enough to be held in the arms or lie comfortably on a person's lap, and temperamentally predisposed to do so. Lapdogs are not a specific breed, but a human designation, often applied to small toy breeds bred for companionship rather than work.

History

Small companion dogs have been kept for centuries in China, Europe and elsewhere. Breeds such as the Pekingese, Japanese Chin, Maltese, Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, Chihuahua, Pomeranian, Shih Tzu and Papillon were favoured by royalty and aristocracy as status symbols and companions. Portraits of ladies with lapdogs are common in Renaissance and later art.

Characteristics

Lapdogs are typically under about 10 kg, affectionate, people-oriented, and suited to indoor living, though exercise and training needs vary by breed. Historically some were also used as hand warmers, flea attractors to protect their owners, and bed warmers.
