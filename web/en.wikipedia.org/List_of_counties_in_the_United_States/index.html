List of counties in the United States

The United States has 3,143 counties and county-equivalents in its 50 states and the District of Columbia, plus 100 county-equivalents in U.S. territories. Louisiana calls its subdivisions parishes and Alaska calls them boroughs and census areas.

Extremes

  • Most counties in a state: Texas — 254
  • Fewest: Delaware — 3
  • Largest by area: San Bernardino County, California (~52,000 km²); including Alaska boroughs/census areas, Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area is larger
  • Smallest: Kalawao County, Hawaii (and independent city Falls Church, Virginia among equivalents)
  • Most populous: Los Angeles County, California — over 10 million
  • Least populous: Loving County, Texas — under 100 residents

By state

Separate lists exist for each state, e.g. counties in Alabama (67), Alaska (30 boroughs/census areas), Arizona (15), Arkansas (75), California (58), Colorado (64), Florida (67), Georgia (159), Illinois (102), New York (62), Pennsylvania (67), Virginia (95 counties plus 38 independent cities), and so on.

County functions vary widely: in New England counties have minimal government, while elsewhere they administer courts, law enforcement, roads, health and records.
