Lipid profile

Lipid profile or lipid panel is a panel of blood tests used to find abnormalities in lipids, such as cholesterol and triglycerides. This article is a redirect/duplicate-topic summary; see the main article lipid panel.

Tests included

Total cholesterol, HDL, LDL (calculated or direct) and triglycerides, sometimes with VLDL and ratios. Results help estimate risk of cardiovascular disease and guide treatment with lifestyle changes or statins. See lipid and cholesterol.

Reference ranges

Desirable total cholesterol is generally below 200 mg/dL; LDL targets vary by risk category.
