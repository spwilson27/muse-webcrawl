Kinescope

Kinescope (also a verb: to kinescope) was a method of recording television programmes by filming the image off a video monitor, the principal way of preserving live television broadcasts in the years before videotape became practical in the late 1950s.

Use

Networks used kinescopes to distribute live New York programmes to affiliates in other time zones and to archive broadcasts. The film image was of noticeably lower quality than the original live picture, and many kinescopes were later discarded — yet surviving kinescopes preserve many otherwise lost programmes of television's early "Golden Age", and the word came to mean the recording itself.
