Montreal Canadiens

The Montreal Canadiens (French: Les Canadiens de Montréal), nicknamed the Habs, are a professional ice hockey team based in Montreal, Quebec. They compete in the National Hockey League (NHL) as a member of the Atlantic Division of the Eastern Conference. Founded in 1909, the club is the longest continuously operating professional ice hockey team and the only NHL club to predate the founding of the league.

History

The Canadiens were founded on 4 December 1909 by J. Ambrose O'Brien as part of the National Hockey Association. They joined the NHL at its founding in 1917. The team won its first Stanley Cup in 1916 and went on to win a record 24 championships, including a record five consecutive titles from 1956 to 1960.

Rivalries

The Canadiens' most storied rivals are the Toronto Maple Leafs, the oldest rivalry in NHL history, and the Boston Bruins, whom they have met frequently in the playoffs.

Notable players

Legendary Canadiens include Maurice "Rocket" Richard, Jean Béliveau, Guy Lafleur, Ken Dryden, Patrick Roy and Carey Price. The club has retired more numbers than any other NHL team.
