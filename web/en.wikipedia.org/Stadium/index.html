A stadium (plural stadiums or stadia) is a place or venue for (mostly) outdoor sports, concerts, or other events, consisting of a field or stage either partly or completely surrounded by a tiered structure designed to allow spectators to stand or sit and view the event.

History

The word derives from the Greek stadion, both a foot-race and the length of its course (about 600 Greek feet), run in stadiums such as the one at Olympia, site of the ancient Olympic Games. Rome built vast venues including the Circus Maximus for chariot racing and the Colosseum for gladiatorial spectacles. Modern stadiums emerged in the 19th century with codified sports, growing from simple grounds into enclosed bowls; the first modern Olympic stadium was built in Athens in 1896.

Design

Stadiums are designed around sightlines (the "C-value" of viewing quality), bowl geometry, roof structures — including fixed, retractable and tensile roofs — crowd safety, segregation and egress, transport links and increasingly sustainability. The catastrophic disasters of the 20th century (such as Hillsborough, 1989) led to sweeping safety regulation in many countries and, in British football, all-seater requirements, though safe standing has since returned in places. Modern venues typically include hospitality suites, media facilities and multi-purpose event spaces.

Records and notable stadiums

The largest stadium in the world by capacity is the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, India (132,000, cricket), followed by North Korea's Rungrado 1st of May Stadium in Pyongyang (listed capacity 114,000). Iconic venues include Wembley Stadium in London, the Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro, Camp Nou in Barcelona and Michigan Stadium in Ann Arbor, the largest in the United States (over 107,000).

Economics

Large stadiums are frequently publicly subsidised and justified by regeneration and prestige, though economists debate their net public benefit. Naming-rights sponsorship is now a major revenue source, and mega-event stadiums (Olympics, World Cups) face recurring criticism over cost and "white elephant" legacies.
