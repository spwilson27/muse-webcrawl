List of political parties in Iceland

Iceland has a multi-party system, with numerous political parties competing for the 63 seats in the Althing, the Icelandic parliament. Since the collapse of the old four-party system after the 2008 financial crisis, Icelandic politics has been characterised by a more fragmented party landscape, with governments formed by coalitions of two or three parties. Parties are traditionally identified by a letter used on the ballot paper.

Defunct parties

  • Old Independence Party and Home Rule Party — early 20th-century parties that merged into the modern Independence Party.
  • Social Democratic Party (Alþýðuflokkurinn) — founded 1916; merged into the Social Democratic Alliance in 2000.
  • People's Alliance (Alþýðubandalagið) — socialist alliance, 1956–1999; much of it joined the Left-Green Movement and the Social Democratic Alliance.
  • Communist Party of Iceland — 1930–1938, later part of the People's Unity Party – Socialist Party and the People's Alliance.
  • National Preservation Movement — 1950s anti-NATO party.
  • Union of Liberals and Leftists — 1969–1974.
  • Women's List (Kvennalistinn) — see above.
  • Liberal Party (Frjálslyndi flokkurinn) — 1998–2012.
  • The Movement (Hreyfingin) — successor to the Citizens' Movement in the Althing.
  • Rainbow (Regnboginn) — left-wing splinter that contested the 2013 election.
  • National Awakening (Þjóðvaki) — 1990s splinter from the Social Democratic Party.
