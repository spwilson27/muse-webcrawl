Monte Ceneri

Monte Ceneri is a mountain pass in the canton of Ticino in Switzerland, connecting the Magadino plain near Lake Maggiore with the valley of the Vedeggio to the south. It separates the northern Sopraceneri region from the southern Sottoceneri around Lugano, and has long been a strategic crossing of the Lepontine Alps foothills.

Elevation554 m (1,818 ft)
LocationTicino, Switzerland
Traversed byA2 motorway, Gotthard railway

Transport

The pass is crossed by the A2 motorway and by the Gotthard railway line. In December 2020 the Ceneri Base Tunnel opened beneath the pass, a 15.4 km twin-bore rail tunnel forming the southern approach to the Gotthard Base Tunnel and completing the flat railway route of the New Railway Link through the Alps (NRLA).

Geography

The summit area is wooded, and the pass road climbs from Rivera to Bironico. A military road and several hiking trails cross the ridge, and the area hosts radio transmission facilities.
