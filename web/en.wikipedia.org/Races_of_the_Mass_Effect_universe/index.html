Races of the Mass Effect universe
Franchise: Mass Effect (BioWare)
Setting: Milky Way galaxy, 22nd century
Governing body: Citadel Council

The Mass Effect video game universe features numerous fictional alien species inhabiting the Milky Way galaxy, united (or divided) by a network of ancient mass relays and governed politically from the Citadel space station. Major species include the asari, salarians, and turians, who hold seats on the Citadel Council, alongside humans and many others.

Major species

The asari are a long-lived, mono-gendered species renowned for diplomacy and biotic ability. The salarians are short-lived amphibians with fast metabolisms and a talent for science and espionage. The turians are an avian-featured, militaristic species with a rigid hierarchy. Other prominent species include the nomadic quarians, creators of the rogue synthetic geth; the long-lived, hardy krogan; the elephantine elcor; the masked, environmentally suited volus; and the multi-eyed batarians.

Antagonists

The galaxy is threatened by the Reapers, immense sentient starships that periodically exterminate advanced organic civilization, and by their servants such as the insectoid Collectors. The extinct Protheans, once thought to have built the relays, loom large in the galaxy's archaeology and politics.
