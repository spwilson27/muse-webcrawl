Tikka (food)

Tikka is a dish consisting of pieces of meat, paneer or vegetables marinated in spiced yogurt and grilled in a tandoor or over charcoal. The word "tikka" means a small piece or a morsel in Hindi and Urdu. It is popular throughout South Asia and in South Asian restaurants worldwide.

Preparation

Cubes of chicken, lamb, fish or paneer are marinated for several hours in yogurt mixed with spices such as cumin, coriander, turmeric, chilli and garam masala, then threaded onto skewers and cooked at high heat until lightly charred at the edges. It is typically served sizzling on a hot plate with onions, lemon wedges and mint chutney.

Variants

Chicken tikka is the best-known version; when the grilled pieces are simmered in a creamy spiced tomato sauce the result is chicken tikka masala, one of the most popular curry dishes in Britain. Paneer tikka is a vegetarian alternative made with Indian cottage cheese.
