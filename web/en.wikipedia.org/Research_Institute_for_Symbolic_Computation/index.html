The Research Institute for Symbolic Computation (RISC) is an Austrian research institute in software technology with a focus on symbolic computation, computer algebra, automated theorem proving, and related areas. It is part of the Johannes Kepler University Linz and is located in the castle of Hagenberg im Mühlkreis.

History

RISC was founded in 1987 by Bruno Buchberger, known for the Gröbner basis algorithm developed in his 1965 PhD thesis. The institute played a central role in establishing the Softwarepark Hagenberg, a technology park modelled on the institute's success.

Research

Groups at RISC work on algorithms for symbolic computation, formal methods, and applications in mathematics and engineering, and contribute to systems and conferences in the computer algebra community.
