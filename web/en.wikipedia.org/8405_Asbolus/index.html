8405 Asbolus is a centaur — a class of icy small Solar System bodies orbiting between Jupiter and Neptune. It was discovered on 28 April 1995 by the Spacewatch survey at Kitt Peak National Observatory and named after Asbolus, a centaur in Greek mythology who was a soothsayer.

Orbit and characteristics

Asbolus has a highly eccentric orbit crossing those of Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune, with a period of about 76 years. Its orbit is unstable on timescales of a few million years due to gravitational perturbations by the giant planets, typical of centaurs, which are thought to originate in the Kuiper belt and may eventually become short-period comets.

Observations

Observations with the Hubble Space Telescope revealed a fresh, bright impact crater-like feature on Asbolus's otherwise dark, red surface, suggesting a collision within roughly the last ten million years exposed unweathered ice. Spectral observations indicate water ice and complex organics on its surface.
