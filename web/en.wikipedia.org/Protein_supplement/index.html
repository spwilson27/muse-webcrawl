Protein supplement

A protein supplement is a dietary supplement or nutrition product intended to provide concentrated protein, usually in powder, bar, or ready-to-drink form. Common sources include whey, casein, egg, soy, pea, and rice proteins. Whey protein, a byproduct of cheese manufacture, is the most widely sold form and is available as concentrate, isolate, and hydrolysate.

Uses

Protein supplements are marketed primarily to athletes and bodybuilders to support muscle protein synthesis and recovery, and are also used in clinical and elderly nutrition to help meet protein requirements. Research indicates supplements can aid muscle gain when total dietary protein would otherwise be insufficient, with benefits plateauing around intakes of roughly 1.6 g/kg body mass per day for resistance trainees.

Regulation and quality

In the United States, protein supplements are regulated as dietary supplements and do not require pre-market approval; quality varies, and independent testing has occasionally found discrepancies between labeled and actual contents.
