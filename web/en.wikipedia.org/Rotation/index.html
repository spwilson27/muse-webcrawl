Rotation

A rotation is a circular motion of an object around a centre (or axis) of rotation. In geometry it is a transformation turning a figure about a fixed point through a given angle; in physics it underlies angular velocity, torque and angular momentum.

Mathematics

In the plane, rotation about the origin by angle θ maps each point using a rotation matrix with cosine and sine entries, preserving distances and orientation. In three dimensions, rotations about an axis are described by Euler's rotation theorem, quaternions and rotation groups such as SO(3). See rotation (mathematics) for detail.

Physics and astronomy

A rotating rigid body has kinetic energy depending on its moment of inertia; gyroscopic effects stabilise tops, wheels and spacecraft. The Earth's rotation causes day and night and the Coriolis effect; stellar and galactic rotation are measured spectroscopically. Combined rotation and translation gives the general motion of a rigid body. See also angular momentum and Earth's rotation.
