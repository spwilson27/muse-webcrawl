Tårnby is a Danish town and municipality on the island of Amager, south of Copenhagen, in the Capital Region of Denmark. Much of Copenhagen Airport (Kastrup) lies within the municipality, making Tårnby a major transport hub.

Geography

The municipality includes the towns of Tårnby, Kastrup and Vestamager, and stretches across the southern part of Amager, bordering Copenhagen to the north and Dragør to the south. The Øresund Bridge to Sweden begins near its coast.

History and attractions

Historically a farming and fishing community serving Copenhagen, Tårnby's growth followed the airport's expansion. Attractions include the Kastrupgård Collection of modern art, the Blue Planet national aquarium (Den Blå Planet), and the protected Kalvebod Fælled wetlands.
