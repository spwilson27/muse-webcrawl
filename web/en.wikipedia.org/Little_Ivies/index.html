Little Ivies

The Little Ivies are a group of small, highly selective liberal arts colleges in the northeastern United States. The informal term plays on the Ivy League: like the Ivies, these colleges are known for academic excellence, historic campuses, and social prestige, but they are smaller, undergraduate-focused institutions.

Members

Colleges commonly counted among the Little Ivies include Amherst College, Williams College, Wesleyan University, Bowdoin College, Colby College, Bates College, Middlebury College, Hamilton College, Swarthmore College, Haverford College, Colgate University, Tufts University, and Trinity College, among others; lists vary by source.

Background

Many of these colleges are members of athletic conferences such as the New England Small College Athletic Conference (NESCAC), founded in 1971. They share features such as low student-to-faculty ratios, residential undergraduate life, and strong graduate and professional school placement.
