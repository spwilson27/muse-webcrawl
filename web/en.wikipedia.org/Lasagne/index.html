Lasagne

Lasagne (singular lasagna) is a type of wide, flat pasta and the baked dish made with stacked layers of it, originating in Italy. The classic lasagne alla bolognese from Emilia-Romagna alternates pasta sheets with ragù and béchamel sauce, topped with Parmesan and baked.

History

Layered pasta dishes date to Roman laganon and medieval cookbooks such as the Neapolitan Liber de Coquina. Regional variants include Neapolitan lasagne with ricotta, meatballs and sausage, and Ligurian lasagne with pesto. Vegetarian and other modern adaptations are common worldwide.

Etymology

The name may derive from Greek laganon (flat dough) or lasanon, a cooking pot, via Latin lasanum.
