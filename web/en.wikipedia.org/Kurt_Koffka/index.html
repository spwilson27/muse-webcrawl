Kurt Koffka

Born: March 18, 1886, Berlin, Germany
Died: November 22, 1941, Northampton, Massachusetts
Known for: Gestalt psychology

Kurt Koffka (March 18, 1886 – November 22, 1941) was a German psychologist and, together with Max Wertheimer and Wolfgang Köhler, one of the founders of Gestalt psychology.

Life and work

Born in Berlin, Koffka earned his PhD there in 1908 and joined Wertheimer in Frankfurt, where the Gestalt movement began with studies of apparent motion (the phi phenomenon). His book The Growth of the Mind (1921) applied Gestalt principles to child development and helped introduce the school to American readers.

Later career

Koffka emigrated to the United States in 1927, teaching at Smith College in Massachusetts. His systematic treatise Principles of Gestalt Psychology (1935) presented the school's concepts of perception, learning, and the "behavioural environment." He died in Northampton, Massachusetts, in 1941.
