Nevado de Toluca, also known by its Nahuatl name Xinantécatl, is a stratovolcano in central Mexico, located about 80 kilometres west of Mexico City near the city of Toluca. At 4,680 metres above sea level, it is the fourth highest peak in Mexico.

Crater and lakes

The volcano has a wide caldera, about 1.5 kilometres across, which can be reached by road. Inside the crater lie two lakes, Lake of the Sun (Lago del Sol) and Lake of the Moon (Lago de la Luna), which are popular with hikers and were sites of pre-Hispanic ritual offerings; artefacts recovered from the lakes are held in Mexican museums.

Protected area

The mountain and its surroundings were declared a national park in 1936 and reclassified as a flora and fauna protection area in 2013, covering some 53,590 hectares of forested slopes.
