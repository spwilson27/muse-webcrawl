The Tour Championship is a golf tournament that is part of the PGA Tour. It has historically been one of the final events of the PGA Tour season; prior to 2007 its predecessors were the Nabisco Championship and other events. Since 2007 it has served as the final event of the FedEx Cup playoffs, contested by the top 30 players in the FedEx Cup standings.

History

The tournament began in 1987 as the Nabisco Championship, inviting the top 30 money winners of the season. From 2004, it has been played permanently at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta, Georgia, the home course of Bobby Jones. Long-time sponsor Coca-Cola gave the event its extended title "The Tour Championship presented by Coca-Cola."

FedEx Cup formats

In its first years as the FedEx Cup finale, the Tour Championship winner did not necessarily win the FedEx Cup, which was decided on points. In 2019, the PGA Tour introduced a staggered-start ("starting strokes") format in which the FedEx Cup leader began the tournament at 10-under par and others at progressively lesser scores, so that the winner of the tournament also won the FedEx Cup. Notable champions include Tiger Woods, who won the event in 2018 in a celebrated comeback, and Rory McIlroy, a multiple winner of both the tournament and the FedEx Cup.
