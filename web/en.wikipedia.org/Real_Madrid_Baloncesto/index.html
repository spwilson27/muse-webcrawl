Real Madrid Baloncesto

Real Madrid Baloncesto is a Spanish professional basketball club based in Madrid. It is the basketball section of the Real Madrid multi-sports club and was founded on 8 March 1931. The team plays in the Liga ACB and the EuroLeague, and holds its home games at the WiZink Center (Palacio de Deportes de la Comunidad de Madrid).

History

Real Madrid is the most successful basketball club in Europe, having won a record eleven EuroLeague championships, including a run of titles in the 1960s under legendary players such as Emiliano Rodríguez and Clifford Luyk, and coach Pedro Ferrándiz. The club has also won four FIBA Intercontinental Cups and numerous domestic honours.

Honours

Domestically, Real Madrid has won a record number of Spanish League (Liga ACB) titles and Copa del Rey trophies, and it contests a fierce rivalry with FC Barcelona, known in basketball as El Clásico. Notable players across its history include Fernando Martín, Dražen Petrović, Arvydas Sabonis, Sergio Llull, Luka Dončić and Rudy Fernández.

Recent era

Under head coach Pablo Laso (2011–2022), the team enjoyed a golden decade, winning EuroLeague titles in 2015 and 2018 alongside many domestic trophies. The club claimed its eleventh European crown in 2023 under Chus Mateo.
