Badass. And cheap to run.

Unveiled in 2017 and delivered to first fleet customers from Giga Nevada, the Semi centres the driver in the cab between two touchscreens, wraps them in a drag coefficient lower than most sports cars (0.36), and cuts fuel and maintenance costs versus diesel — Tesla estimates savings of up to $200,000 over a million miles.

Megacharging

A growing Megacharger network at depots and truck stops restores most of the battery in a driver's break.

[Image: Fleet Semi trucks in PepsiCo-style livery at a distribution center]

Fleets first

Early production has gone to fleet partners running regional routes, with volume production ramping at the Nevada expansion.

[Image: Enhanced Autopilot graphic over a highway scene]

Safety built in

Enhanced Autopilot, jackknife prevention from the tri-motor torque control, and a million-mile powertrain target.

Specs in brief

Two variants — roughly 300-mile and 500-mile range — share the Model 3-derived tri-motor powertrain, energy consumption under 2 kWh per mile, and a central seat with seating for the driver's paperwork and coffee within arm's reach.
