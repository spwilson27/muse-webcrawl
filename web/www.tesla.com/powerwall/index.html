Your home, powered around the clock

Powerwall stores solar energy during the day and powers your home at night, detects outages and switches over automatically — keeping lights, Wi-Fi and appliances running through storms. Storm Watch even charges Powerwall ahead of severe weather forecasts.

Part of the Tesla energy ecosystem
