An exoskeleton, not a body on a frame

Cybertruck's shell is made of Ultra-Hard 30X cold-rolled stainless steel — the same alloy family used on SpaceX's Starship — over an armored glass canopy. The wedge silhouette, first shown as a concept in 2019, survived to production nearly unchanged.

Work-ready

A powered stainless tonneau vault, 120V/240V outlets in the bed and Powershare bidirectional charging that can back up your home.

[Image: Cybertruck minimalist interior with squared steering wheel and 18.5-inch display]

Steer-by-wire

No mechanical link between the squircle wheel and the road wheels — with rear-wheel steering, it turns tighter than most sedans.

[Image: Cyberbeast tri-motor badge detail on the tailgate]

Cyberbeast

The tri-motor flagship: 845 hp, 0–60 in 2.6 seconds and a 130 mph top speed.

Foundation Series and beyond

Early production trucks shipped as the limited Foundation Series before regular Long Range, All-Wheel Drive and Cyberbeast configurations opened up, with a lower-priced rear-wheel-drive Long Range joining the line-up later.
