Admissions & Aid

Harvard College seeks curious, engaged students from every background. Admission is need-blind for all applicants, including international students, and the financial aid programme meets 100 percent of demonstrated need — families earning below a threshold (about $85,000) pay nothing.

Apply

First-year and transfer applications via the Common Application or Coalition Application, with early action and regular decision rounds.

Visit

Tours and information sessions run from the Agassiz House visitor centre in Harvard Yard, Cambridge, Massachusetts.

Affordability

Roughly one in five students pays nothing to attend; the average aid package covers most of the total cost.

Thresholds and statistics are approximate, as remembered; Harvard's aid initiative thresholds have been revised over the years.
