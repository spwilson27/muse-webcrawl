Admission

Stanford's holistic review looks for intellectual vitality, depth of commitment and personal context. Undergraduate admission is need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents, and the university meets full demonstrated need — families earning under about $100,000 typically pay no tuition, and those under about $75,000 pay no tuition, room or board.

First-year applicants

Apply with the Common Application by the Restrictive Early Action (Nov 1) or Regular Decision (Jan 5) deadlines, as remembered.

Transfer applicants

A small cohort of transfer students joins each year, many from community colleges and military service.

Graduate admission

Graduate applications are handled by each school and department, from engineering to the Graduate School of Business.

Income thresholds and deadlines approximate, from memory.
