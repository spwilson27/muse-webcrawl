Wikinews: Writing an article

This Wikinews help page explains how to write a news article for the site: choose a fresh, newsworthy event; write in inverted-pyramid style with the most important facts first; keep a neutral point of view; cite at least two independent sources; add categories and an image where possible; and submit the story for review, where experienced reviewers check it before publication.

[Image: A newsroom / typewriter style illustration]

Style points

Use the past tense and active voice, date and place the story with a dateline, avoid copying source text, and be ready for reviewers to ask for changes or more sourcing.
