Welcome to Wikinews — a free-content news source, written by contributors around the world. Unlike most news sites, its articles are written collaboratively by volunteers, aiming for a neutral point of view, and anyone can start a story.

[Image: The Wikinews logo — a globe with a pen nib / news motif]

Lead stories

The front page leads with the day's top developed stories — typically on world politics, disasters and accidents, science and technology, sport, business and culture — each with a dateline, a summary and links onward.

Latest news

A running list shows the most recently published articles in date order, alongside developing stories still being worked on and the day's news briefs.

Write a story

New contributors are invited to start an article, follow the style and content guides, cite sources, and submit the story for review before publication.

Categories

Browse by region — Africa, Asia, Europe, North and South America, Oceania and the Middle East — or by topic such as Politics, Law and crime, Health, Weather and Sport.
