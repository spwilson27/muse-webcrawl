Welcome to Wiktionary — the free, multilingual dictionary that anyone can edit. The English Wiktionary aims to describe all words of all languages using definitions and quotations in English, and contains entries for words, phrases, pronunciations, etymologies, translations and thesaurus material.

[Image: The Wiktionary logo — a stylised book / tile design]

Word of the Day

Each day the main page features a Word of the Day — an interesting or unusual English word with its part of speech, definition and an example of its use — and a Foreign Word of the Day from another language.

Browse

Look words up through the search box, the alphabetical index, or by language. Entries typically give pronunciation (in the International Phonetic Alphabet), etymology, meanings in order, example sentences, synonyms and antonyms, derived terms and translations.

Other languages

Wiktionary exists in well over 150 languages; the English edition is the largest and also documents vocabularies from many other languages for English readers.

Community

Editors gather in the Beer Parlour (the community discussion page), the Tea Room for questions about words, and in requests for verification, deletion and attention.
