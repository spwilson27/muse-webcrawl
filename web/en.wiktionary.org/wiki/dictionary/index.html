dictionary

Pronunciation: /ˈdɪkʃən(ə)ɹi/ (UK), /ˈdɪkʃəˌnɛɹi/ (US). Etymology: from Medieval Latin dictiōnārium, from dictiō ('word, speaking'). Noun: (1) A reference work with a list of words from one or more languages, normally ordered alphabetically, explaining each word's meanings and accompanied by information such as pronunciations, usage and etymologies. (2) By extension, any reference work arranged in a similar way.

[Image: Audio speaker icons for the UK and US pronunciations of the word]

Synonyms and derived terms

Synonyms include wordbook and lexicon (in some senses). Derived terms include dictionary attack, pocket dictionary, electronic dictionary and Dictionary Day.

Translations

The word is translated in many languages — for example Wörterbuch in German, dictionnaire in French, diccionario in Spanish and 辞書 in Japanese.
