Wiktionary: Word of the Day

This Wiktionary project page organises the Word of the Day that appears on the main page: a daily English word chosen for being interesting, useful or unusual, with its definition and example, plus the Foreign Word of the Day. It holds the nomination process, the archive of past words by month, and the schedule of forthcoming entries.

[Image: A calendar-style archive grid of past Words of the Day]

Nominating a word

Editors nominate words that have complete, well-formed entries; words are normally featured only once, and themed sets are sometimes prepared for special dates.
