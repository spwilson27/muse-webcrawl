The Wikipedia portal is hosted by the Wikimedia Foundation, a non-profit organisation that also hosts a range of other projects:

  • Commons — Free media repository
  • Wikivoyage — Free travel guide
  • Wiktionary — Free dictionary
  • Wikibooks — Free textbooks
  • Wikinews — Free-content news
  • Wikidata — Free knowledge base
  • Wikiversity — Free learning resources
  • Wikisource — Free-content library
  • Wikispecies — Directory of species
  • Meta-Wiki — Community coordination
  • MediaWiki — Wiki software
  • Wikiquote — Collection of quotations
