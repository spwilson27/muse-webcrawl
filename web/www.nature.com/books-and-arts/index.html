Books & Arts — Nature | Nature

Nature's Books & Arts section reviews science books and covers the meeting points of science and culture: art exhibitions, theatre, film, and the science in and behind them.

Coverage

Weekly book reviews range across popular science, biography and history of science, with occasional fiction. Arts coverage reviews museum exhibitions and performances where science is the subject, and essays explore how science is portrayed in wider culture.

Regulars

The section historically included the Futures science-fiction short stories and the long-running Books in Brief column; reviewers are scientists and science writers commissioned by the books editors.
