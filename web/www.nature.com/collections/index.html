Collections — Nature | Nature

Collections gather Nature Portfolio content around a theme: curated sets of articles, news and commentary from across the journals, assembled by editors for special issues, conferences and sponsored topics.

What collections look like

Each collection page frames a topic — a disease area, a technology such as quantum computing, a region's research, or an anniversary — with an editorial introduction and a browsable article list, sometimes including multimedia and sponsored supplements flagged as such.
