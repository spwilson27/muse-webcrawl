Nature News | Nature

Nature's news team reports on science and science policy worldwide: funding, discoveries, misconduct and ethics, publishing, and the people and politics of research. Nature News stories are journalism, separate from the journal's peer-reviewed research.

Latest coverage

The news pages carry daily stories — major paper announcements covered independently, space missions, climate negotiations, public-health developments, and investigations — plus the daily Nature Briefing newsletter's selection and the journal's obituaries and Q&A interviews.

The Briefing

Nature Briefing is the journal's flagship daily newsletter, summarising the day's essential science news and research highlights with the team's characteristic light touch.
