Research Articles in Nature | Nature

The Articles section collects the journal's primary research papers — Articles, Letters (in the journal's older terminology) and Brief Communications — peer-reviewed original research across the life, physical and Earth sciences.

What appears here

Recent articles typically include genomics and cell biology, AI and machine-learning methods, climate and Earth-system studies, astronomy and physics results, neuroscience, and major collaborative resources from consortia. Each article carries structured abstracts, methods, references and data-availability statements.

Access

Nature is a subscription journal; many articles are available open access under the journal's hybrid model, and accepted manuscripts are often posted by authors as preprints elsewhere. Article pages include citation metrics and altmetric attention scores, as recalled.
