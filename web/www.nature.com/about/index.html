About Nature | Nature

Nature is a weekly international journal publishing the finest peer-reviewed research, founded in 1869 and published by Springer Nature. Its contents span the sciences, with news, commentary and analysis alongside primary research papers.

The journal

Nature's first issue appeared on 4 November 1869, edited by Norman Lockyer. It went on to publish landmarks including the structure of DNA (1953, Watson, Crick, Franklin and colleagues), the discovery of the neutron, and the first cloned mammal reports. The journal's masthead mission is to publish papers of the highest significance and originality across all of science.

Nature Portfolio

Alongside the flagship sit the Nature research journals — Nature Physics, Nature Medicine, Nature Climate Change and dozens more — plus Nature Communications, Scientific Reports and review journals such as Nature Reviews. Nature.com gathers them in one platform, with news from the Nature news team.
