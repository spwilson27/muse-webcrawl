Winston Churchill

Sir Winston Leonard Spencer Churchill (30 November 1874 – 24 January 1965) was a British statesman, soldier and writer who served twice as Prime Minister of the United Kingdom. His Wikiquote page collects his speeches and writings by period — including the wartime speeches of 1940 — quotations about him, and misattributed sayings.

Themes (paraphrased)

The sourced quotations range over war and resolve, democracy and freedom, history and writing, and his dry humour about politics and his own career; each is given with its speech or publication source where known.

Misattributed

As with Einstein, a section separates famous lines popularly credited to Churchill from those that can actually be sourced to him.
