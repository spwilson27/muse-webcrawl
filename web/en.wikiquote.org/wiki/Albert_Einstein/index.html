Albert Einstein

Albert Einstein (14 March 1879 – 18 April 1955) was a German-born theoretical physicist who developed the theory of relativity. His Wikiquote page collects quotations from his published works, letters and interviews, quotations about him, and a section of misattributed sayings.

[Image: Portrait photograph of Einstein in later life]

Quotations (paraphrased themes)

His sourced quotations return to imagination and curiosity, the relationship of science and religion, education, peace and war, and remarks on his own work and fame; the page gives each with its source where known.

Misattributed

A well-known section lists famous sayings popularly credited to Einstein — such as some versions of the 'insanity' and 'fish judging' aphorisms — for which no reliable source in his writings has been found.
