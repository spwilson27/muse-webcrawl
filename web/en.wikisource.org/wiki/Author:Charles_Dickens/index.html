Author: Charles Dickens

Charles Dickens (1812–1870) was an English writer and social critic, regarded as the greatest novelist of the Victorian era. His Wikisource author page lists public-domain editions of his novels and stories hosted here, together with works about him.

[Image: Portrait photograph / engraving of Charles Dickens in later life]

Works

Novels such as Oliver Twist, David Copperfield, Bleak House, A Tale of Two Cities, Great Expectations and A Christmas Carol (a novella), plus short stories, essays and travel writing.

Editions

Texts come from public-domain editions, many linked to scanned volumes for proofreading.
