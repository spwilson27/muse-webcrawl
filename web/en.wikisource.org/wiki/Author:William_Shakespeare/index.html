Author: William Shakespeare

(1564–1616)

William Shakespeare was an English playwright, poet and actor, widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language. His Wikisource author page lists the public-domain editions of his works hosted here: the plays (comedies, tragedies and histories), the sonnets and poems, and works about him.

[Image: The Chandos portrait attributed to Shakespeare, as used on his author pages]

Works

Plays such as Hamlet, Macbeth, Romeo and Juliet, A Midsummer Night's Dream, Othello and King Lear; the Sonnets (1609); Venus and Adonis and The Rape of Lucrece.
