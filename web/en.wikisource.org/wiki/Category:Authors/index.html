Category: Authors — Wikisource

This category collects Wikisource author pages: pages that list the works of an author hosted on Wikisource, the free-content library of source texts. Authors are browsable alphabetically and by era, nationality and genre subcategories.

Subcategories and pages

Author pages follow the 'Author:' namespace — for example Author:William Shakespeare and Author:Charles Dickens, both already in this recreation — giving dates, a portrait or description where the memory of the page supports it, and organised lists of the author's works on Wikisource, from plays and novels to speeches and letters.

Browsing

Category pages on Wikisource, as across Wikimedia projects, list subcategories first and then member pages alphabetically, with letter-by-letter index navigation for large categories such as this one, which holds thousands of authors (as recalled).
