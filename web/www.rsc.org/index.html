Royal Society of Chemistry

The Royal Society of Chemistry is the UK's professional body for chemists and a major scientific publisher: 'We connect the world with the chemical sciences and invest in the future of chemistry.'

Publishing

RSC journals include flagship Chemical Science (its gold open-access flagship), Chemical Communications, Chemical Society Reviews and the Journal of Materials Chemistry family; the homepage promotes new articles, books and its platform for chemistry teachers, 'RSC Education'.

Community

Membership grades from student to Fellow (FRSC), accreditation of degrees, careers support, and policy work on science education and research funding form the society's professional offer; its roots run to the Chemical Society founded in 1841.
