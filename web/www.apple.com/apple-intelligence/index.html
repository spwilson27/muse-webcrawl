Apple Intelligence

Apple Intelligence is the personal intelligence system that helps you write, express yourself, and get things done effortlessly — Apple's page for its AI features across iPhone, iPad and Mac, with privacy 'at its core' through on-device processing and Private Cloud Compute.

Features

Writing Tools, notification and mail summaries, Clean Up in Photos, Genmoji and Image Playground, a more conversational Siri with ChatGPT integration (offered from late 2024), and visual intelligence — rolled out in stages with iOS 18 releases and by language/region, as the page explains.
