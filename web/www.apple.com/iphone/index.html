iPhone

iPhone. Forged in titanium. (in the Pro era's phrasing) Apple's iPhone page walks the current lineup — Pro models first — through finishes, cameras, chips and Apple Intelligence, in long scrolling product storytelling.

The lineup

Standard and Pro tiers, in two sizes each in recent generations; Pro models carry titanium bodies, the Action button, USB-C (adopted with iPhone 15 for the whole line, as recalled) and the best cameras, while standard models bring the Dynamic Island and main-camera upgrades down the range.

Cameras and chips

Pro photography and video (including ProRes and spatial video for Vision Pro) and the latest A-series Pro chip anchor the page's claims, with battery and safety features (Crash Detection, Emergency SOS via satellite) in supporting sections.
