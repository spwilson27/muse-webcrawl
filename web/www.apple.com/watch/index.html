Apple Watch

Apple Watch. The ultimate device for a healthy life, in Apple's framing: fitness tracking, health sensors, safety features and connectivity, in Series, SE and Ultra models.

Models and health

The flagship Series models, the rugged larger Ultra for endurance sports, and the budget SE. Health features anchor the marketing: ECG, blood-oxygen (whose US availability has been affected by a patent dispute and import measures in recent years, as recalled), sleep tracking, cycle tracking and fall/crash detection.
