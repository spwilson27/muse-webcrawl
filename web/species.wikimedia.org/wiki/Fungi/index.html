Fungi

Fungi is the taxonomic kingdom of the fungi — yeasts, moulds and mushrooms. Its Wikispecies page gives the classification and main phyla, including Ascomycota, Basidiomycota, Chytridiomycota and Zygomycota in the arrangement used here, linking down to orders, families, genera and species such as the common mushroom Agaricus bisporus.
