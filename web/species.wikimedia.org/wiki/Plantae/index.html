Kingdom of plants in Wikispecies

Plantae is the taxonomic kingdom of the plants. Its Wikispecies page presents its classification and main divisions — including the green algae groups, bryophytes (mosses, liverworts, hornworts) and the vascular plants (ferns, conifers and the flowering plants, Angiospermae) — with links down to families, genera and species.
