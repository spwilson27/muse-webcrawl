Welcome to Wikispecies — a free directory of species. It aims to catalogue all forms of life: animals, plants, fungi, bacteria, archaea, protists and viruses, giving each taxon a page with its classification, scientific name, authors and references, and links to its Wikipedia articles.

[Image: The Wikispecies logo — a stylised leaf / tree of life motif]

Taxonomy navigation

Browse the tree of life from the top taxa: Archaea, Bacteria, Eukaryota — and within them Animalia, Fungi, Plantae and the other kingdoms — down through phylum, class, order, family and genus to individual species.

Species pages

A typical page gives the taxon's name and authority, its classification, type information, synonyms, distribution, references and vernacular names in many languages.

Contribute

Taxonomists and editors can add new taxa, correct classifications and link pages to Wikidata and to images on Wikimedia Commons.
