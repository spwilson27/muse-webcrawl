Lucy is a NASA Discovery Program spacecraft launched in October 2021 on a 12-year journey to explore the Jupiter Trojans — two swarms of asteroids sharing Jupiter's orbit that are thought to be primitive leftovers of the early Solar System. No mission had visited them before.

Tour

After an early encounter with the small main-belt asteroid Dinkinesh in 2023 (which revealed a surprise contact-binary moon), Lucy's trajectory threads through both Trojan swarms, visiting a series of named targets including Eurybates, Polymele, Leucus, Orus and the binary pair Patroclus–Menoetius, using Earth gravity assists between encounters.
