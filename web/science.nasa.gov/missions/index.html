NASA Science Missions A–Z

A directory of NASA's science missions — operating spacecraft, missions in development, and historic missions whose data live on in the archives. Mission pages recreated so far are linked; the full directory lists well over a hundred missions across astrophysics, planetary science, Earth science and heliophysics.

Featured, with pages in this recreation

  • James Webb Space Telescope — infrared observatory at L2 (2021– )
  • Hubble Space Telescope — orbiting observatory (1990– )
  • Europa Clipper — Jupiter ocean-moon explorer (2024– )
  • Parker Solar Probe — closest-ever mission to the Sun (2018– )
  • Psyche — mission to a metal asteroid (2023– )

More missions in the directory

  • Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope — wide-field infrared survey telescope, in development
  • Dragonfly — rotorcraft lander bound for Saturn's moon Titan
  • Lucy — tour of Jupiter's Trojan asteroids (2021– )
  • Mars 2020 / Perseverance — rover caching samples in Jezero Crater
  • Juno — polar orbiter studying Jupiter's interior
  • Voyager 1 & 2 — the twin interstellar travellers (1977– )
