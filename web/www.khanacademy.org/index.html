Khan Academy — Free Online Courses, Lessons & Practice

Our mission is to provide a free, world-class education to anyone, anywhere. Khan Academy's homepage invites learners in with course menus by subject and grade, and (in recent years) its AI tutor, Khanmigo.

Learn

  • Math — by grade from early maths through calculus, statistics and linear algebra, plus full courses
  • Science — physics, chemistry, biology, computing and AP courses
  • Humanities and more — history, grammar, economics, personal finance, and test prep (including the official SAT prep built with the College Board, as long offered)

How it works

Short videos, practice exercises with instant feedback, and mastery-based progress, free for learners and with teacher dashboards for classrooms. Sal Khan founded the nonprofit after tutoring his cousins remotely in the mid-2000s, as its story goes.
