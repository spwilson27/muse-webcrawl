ESA — Enabling & Support

Behind every ESA mission is the enabling layer: launchers that reach orbit, operations teams that fly spacecraft, and the ground stations and technology programmes that make missions possible.

Launchers

Europe's launcher family is built around Ariane 6 (heavy lift, first flown in 2024 as recalled) and the Vega-C small launcher, flying from Europe's Spaceport at Kourou.

Operations

ESOC in Darmstadt controls ESA spacecraft around the clock, supported by the ESTRACK network of ground stations in Spain, Australia, Argentina and elsewhere.

Technology

The Technology Development Element and the General Support Technology Programme mature new components — propulsion, materials, robotics, optics — from concept to flight readiness for industry across Europe.
