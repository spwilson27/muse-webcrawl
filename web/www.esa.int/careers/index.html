ESA — Careers at ESA

Working for ESA means working at the forefront of space science and technology, in a multicultural, interdisciplinary environment — engineers, scientists, and professionals in law, finance, communications and more, across Europe and French Guiana.

Who ESA hires

Recruitment is open to nationals of ESA Member, Associate and Cooperating States. The annual Young Graduate Trainee programme and the student internship programme are the classic entry routes; experienced professionals join through vacancy notices on the ESA careers portal.

Astronauts

ESA recruits astronaut candidates in rare, Europe-wide selections — the 2021–22 campaign drew more than 22,000 applications and produced a new class of career and reserve astronauts, including the first astronaut candidate with a physical disability (the Parastronaut project), as recalled.
