ESA — Space Safety

ESA's Space Safety programme protects Earth and our space infrastructure: watching for asteroids and comets that might strike our planet, tracking space debris, monitoring space weather, and developing missions to clean up orbit.

Planetary defence

ESA's Planetary Defence Office tracks near-Earth objects; the Hera mission, launched in 2024 as recalled, is travelling to the Didymos asteroid system to study the aftermath of NASA's DART impact — the first asteroid-deflection test. A future rapid-response interceptor concept, Ramses, targets asteroid Apophis's 2029 Earth flyby.

Space debris

Tens of thousands of tracked objects orbit Earth. ESA's Space Debris Office publishes environment reports, promotes mitigation guidelines, and is developing debris-removal demonstrations such as ClearSpace-1.

Space weather

The Vigil mission, planned for a vantage point at the Sun–Earth L5 Lagrange point, will watch the Sun's side to warn of solar storms heading for Earth.
