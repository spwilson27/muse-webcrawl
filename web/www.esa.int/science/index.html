ESA — Science & Exploration

ESA's science and exploration programme asks how the Universe began and evolved, how stars and planets form, and whether life exists elsewhere — then sends spacecraft to find out.

Flagships and missions

  • Juice — en route to Jupiter's icy moons (arrives 2031)
  • Euclid — mapping the geometry of the dark Universe (launched 2023)
  • Gaia — the billion-star survey (science observations ended in 2025 as recalled)
  • ExoMars — the Rosalind Franklin rover programme, re-planned with partners
  • Solar Orbiter, BepiColombo (at Mercury), and the Hubble/Webb partnerships with NASA

Human and robotic exploration

ESA astronauts fly to the ISS, ESA provides the European Service Module for NASA's Orion spacecraft, and the agency is a partner in the lunar Gateway and the Argonaut lunar lander programme, as recalled.
