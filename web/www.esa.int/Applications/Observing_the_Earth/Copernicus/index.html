Copernicus — ESA

Copernicus is the European Union's Earth observation programme, coordinated and managed by the European Commission in partnership with ESA, which develops and flies the Sentinel satellite families. It is, as ESA describes it, the most ambitious Earth observation programme to date.

The Sentinels

Each Sentinel family serves a Copernicus service: Sentinel-1 (radar imaging, with a gap after the Sentinel-1B failure and a replacement launched in 2024, as recalled), Sentinel-2 (multispectral land imaging), Sentinel-3 (ocean/land colour and temperature), Sentinel-5P and the atmospheric Sentinels, and Sentinel-6 (sea level). Services cover land, marine, atmosphere, climate change, security and emergency management.

Free and open

Copernicus data are free, full and open — a policy that made Sentinel imagery the default source for environmental monitoring worldwide.
