ESA — Applications — Space for Earth

ESA's applications programmes turn space technology into services used every day: watching the planet with Copernicus, navigating with Galileo, connecting people with satellite telecommunications, and forecasting weather with Europe's meteorological satellites.

Earth observation

The Copernicus programme — run with the European Commission — flies the Sentinel satellite families measuring land, oceans, atmosphere and climate, with all data free and open. ESA develops most Sentinels and operates several of them.

Navigation

Galileo is Europe's global satellite navigation system, providing metre-level positioning worldwide; EGNOS augments GPS accuracy for aviation across Europe.

Telecommunications

ESA's ARTES programme partners with industry on satellite communications — broadband, broadcast, and secure government connectivity — including large platforms and smallsat constellations.
