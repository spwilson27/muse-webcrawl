Quest

The Quest line of all-in-one VR/MR headsets (Quest 3 and the lower-priced Quest 3S, as recalled) headlines the store: mixed-reality gaming, fitness and entertainment, with bundles and accessories sold directly.

Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses

Camera-and-AI glasses made with EssilorLuxottica: capture photos and video, listen to music and ask Meta AI questions hands-free — heavily promoted on the homepage.

Meta AI
