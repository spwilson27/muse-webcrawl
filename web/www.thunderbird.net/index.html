📧 All your accounts, one app

Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud and any IMAP/POP account in a single unified inbox, with powerful search and filters.

📅 Calendar & tasks built in

The Lightning calendar handles events, invitations and CalDAV sync — no separate app needed.

🔒 Privacy first

Remote content is blocked by default, phishing protection is built in, and OpenPGP encryption is native — your mail stays yours.

🧩 Add-ons & themes

Customise with thousands of community add-ons, or restyle the whole app with themes.

📱 Thunderbird for Android

The mobile app (built on the K-9 Mail codebase) brings the same account to your pocket, with a desktop sync import.

❤️ Community powered

Thunderbird is funded by user donations and built by the MZLA team with a global community of volunteers.

Supernova and beyond
