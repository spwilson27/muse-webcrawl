The Wikibooks Cookbook

The Cookbook is one of Wikibooks' most popular books: a collaboratively written collection of recipes and cooking knowledge. Its table of contents organises recipes by ingredient and dish type — appetisers, soups, salads, breads, main courses by meat, fish and vegetarian dishes, desserts and drinks — alongside techniques, equipment and diets and meals sections.

[Image: A cookbook cover / a plated dish photographed for a recipe]

Recipes by category

Chicken, beef, pork, seafood, pasta, rice, vegetables and baking are among the heavily populated categories, each with individual recipe pages giving ingredients and method.

Cuisines of the world

Recipes are also grouped by cuisine — for example Indian, Italian, Mexican, Japanese, French and British cooking.
