Wikimedia Foundation

The non-profit behind Wikipedia and other free knowledge projects

The Wikimedia Foundation is the non-profit organisation that hosts Wikipedia, Wiktionary, Wikimedia Commons, Wikidata and the other Wikimedia projects. Its site presents its mission — to empower people around the world to collect and develop educational content under a free licence and to disseminate it effectively and globally — its annual plan and reports, its staff and Board of Trustees, and ways to donate and get involved.

[Image: Photograph of a Wikimedia community / staff group and the Wikimedia Foundation logo]

Donate

Most of the Foundation's funding comes from small donations from readers; the site carries prominent donate appeals and explains how funds are spent.

About and transparency

Pages cover the Foundation's finances, annual reports, policies, press materials, research and technology work, and current job openings, with its offices based in San Francisco.
