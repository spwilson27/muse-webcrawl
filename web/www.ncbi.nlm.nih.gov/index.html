NCBI — National Center for Biotechnology Information

The National Center for Biotechnology Information, part of the US National Library of Medicine at the National Institutes of Health, builds the public databases at the heart of modern biology — PubMed, GenBank, and BLAST among them.

Key resources

  • PubMed — citations and abstracts for millions of biomedical journal articles
  • GenBank — the annotated collection of all publicly available DNA sequences
  • BLAST — the Basic Local Alignment Search Tool for comparing sequences
  • Bookshelf, ClinVar, dbSNP, GEO and the Taxonomy database — among dozens of specialised resources listed from the NCBI homepage

Search all databases

The NCBI homepage centres on a cross-database search box and menus of resources by category — Literature, Genes, Proteins, Genomes, Clinical and more — in the long-familiar blue design of the NCBI/NIH sites.
