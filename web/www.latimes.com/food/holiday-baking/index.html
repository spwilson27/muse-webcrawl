Holiday Baking: 6 Cookies That Always Make Our List

By Los Angeles Times Food Staff  ·  Published for the Food section
The December cookie tray, assembled by our test kitchen. (Illustration placeholder)

Every December our test kitchen bakes through dozens of cookie recipes in search of the perfect holiday box. Six of them return every year, without debate. Here they are, with the tricks that make them work.

  1. Classic sugar cookies

    Chill the dough a full hour before rolling; add a quarter teaspoon of almond extract for the bakery-style finish. Decorate simply — icing is optional.

  2. Chewy ginger molasses cookies

    Roll the dough balls in coarse sugar before baking for a crackled top. Underbake by one minute for maximum chew.

  3. Chocolate crinkle cookies

    Oil in the batter keeps them fudgy. The powdered-sugar coating should be thick — coat twice.

  4. Brown-butter snickerdoodles

    Browning the butter turns a good cookie into a great one. Cinnamon sugar, as always.

  5. Thumbprint cookies with jam

    Use a wine cork to press the centers evenly, and fill after baking so the jam stays bright.

  6. Shortbread fingers

    The simplest recipe on the list: butter, flour, sugar, salt. Dip half in dark chocolate and finish with flaky salt.

All six freeze well — bake now, box later.
