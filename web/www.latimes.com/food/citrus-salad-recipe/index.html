Recipe: Winter Citrus Salad With Fennel, Olives and Mint

By Los Angeles Times Food Staff  ·  Published for the Food section
Blood oranges, fennel and olives — winter's brightest plate. (Illustration placeholder)

When California citrus season peaks, the smartest thing you can do with a bag of blood oranges is almost nothing: slice them, shave some fennel over the top, and let the fruit do the talking. This salad is the one we make on repeat from December through March.

Ingredients

  • 4 blood oranges or a mix of citrus (cara cara, navel, grapefruit)
  • 1 small fennel bulb, very thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup green olives, halved
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • Flaky salt and black pepper
  • A handful of torn mint leaves

Instructions

  1. Slice the tops and bottoms off the citrus, then cut away the peel and pith. Slice crosswise into ¼-inch rounds.
  2. Arrange on a platter, alternating varieties. Scatter the fennel and olives over the top.
  3. Whisk the olive oil and lemon juice with a pinch of flaky salt; drizzle over the salad.
  4. Finish with mint, more flaky salt and black pepper. Serve within 30 minutes.

Serves 4 as a side.

Tip: supreme the citrus (cut segments free of membrane) for a more refined presentation.
