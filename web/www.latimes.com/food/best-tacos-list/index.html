The 8 Best Tacos in Los Angeles, According to Our Critics

By Los Angeles Times Food Staff  ·  Published for the Food section
Street-style tacos at a night market in East Los Angeles. (Illustration placeholder)

Los Angeles has a fair claim to the title of America's taco capital. From the trompo towers of the Eastside to the Baja fish shops of the Westside, the taco is the city's defining dish. After months of eating, our critics agree on eight that stand above the rest.

  1. The carne asada taco

    Chopped grilled beef with a charred edge, tucked into two soft corn tortillas with cilantro, onion and a squeeze of lime. The standard against which all others are measured.

  2. The al pastor taco

    Marinated pork shaved from the trompo with a sliver of grilled pineapple. Sweet, smoky and structurally perfect.

  3. The Baja fish taco

    Beer-battered fish, shredded cabbage, crema and lime. The beach taco, best eaten within sight of the ocean.

  4. The carnitas taco

    Slow-cooked pork, crisped at the edges, finished with pickled onion and fresh salsa verde.

  5. The barbacoa taco

    Rich, falling-apart beef, ideally from a Sunday-morning stand, with consommé for dipping.

  6. The nopales taco

    Grilled cactus paddles with queso fresco — proof that the city's best taco need not involve meat.

  7. The shrimp taco

    Garlic-butter shrimp with chipotle crema, a surf-side classic.

  8. The breakfast taco

    Scrambled egg, potato and machaca — the morning taco that anchors the weekend.

Have a favorite that didn't make the list? Our critics return to this guide each year.
