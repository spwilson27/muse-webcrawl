Zero-Proof Cocktails That Actually Taste Like Cocktails

By Los Angeles Times Food Staff  ·  Published for the Food section
Bitter, balanced and entirely alcohol-free. (Illustration placeholder)

The trick to a great zero-proof cocktail isn't removing the alcohol — it's replacing what alcohol does: bitterness, burn, body and balance. Build with those four in mind and nobody at the table feels like they're holding the consolation drink.

The formula

Something bitter (tea, gentian-style bitters alternatives, grapefruit), something sour (citrus or verjus), something sweet (a syrup with real flavor — rosemary, honey, demerara), and something sparkling on top. Shake or stir like you mean it; dilution and temperature matter just as much as in a classic cocktail.

The Sunset Spritz

Combine 2 ounces strong-brewed hibiscus tea (chilled), 1 ounce grapefruit juice, ½ ounce honey syrup and 2 dashes non-alcoholic aromatic bitters over ice. Top with 3 ounces tonic water. Garnish with a grapefruit twist.

The Garden Gimlet

Shake 2 ounces cucumber juice, ¾ ounce lime juice, ½ ounce basil syrup and a pinch of salt with ice. Strain into a coupe; garnish with a basil leaf.

Serve in real glassware, always — presentation is half the ritual.
