A Season-by-Season Guide to Shopping the Los Angeles Farmers Markets

By Los Angeles Times Food Staff  ·  Published for the Food section
Peak stone-fruit season at a Sunday market. (Illustration placeholder)

Los Angeles farmers markets run year-round, but what you should buy changes dramatically by month. Here is what our editors look for each season — and the simple rules for shopping well.

SeasonBuy this
WinterCitrus of every kind, avocados, leafy greens, cauliflower, beets, winter squash
SpringStrawberries, asparagus, peas, fava beans, artichokes, green garlic
SummerStone fruit, tomatoes, corn, peppers, melons, figs, basil
FallApples, persimmons, pomegranates, grapes, mushrooms, pumpkins

Five rules for market shopping

  1. Go early for selection, late for deals. Vendors discount perishables in the final hour.
  2. Ask what's good this week. Growers will tell you; it's their livelihood.
  3. Bring cash and a tote. Some stands still don't take cards.
  4. Buy the ugly fruit. Cosmetic seconds taste the same and cost less.
  5. Shop the middle aisles first. The best small farms are rarely at the entrance.

Markets operate weekly across the city; check local listings for days and hours.
