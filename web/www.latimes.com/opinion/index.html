Opinion

Ideas and arguments for Los Angeles, California and the nation

The Opinion section presents editorials representing the institutional voice of The Times, op-ed essays from outside contributors, letters from readers, and columns that examine politics, housing, education, the environment and public life. Views expressed in signed pieces are those of their authors.

Recurring themes in recent years have included housing affordability, homelessness, water and climate policy, criminal justice, and the future of democracy — issues debated daily across Southern California.
