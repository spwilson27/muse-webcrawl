Google Scholar

Google Scholar provides a simple way to broadly search for scholarly literature. From one place, you can explore articles, theses, books, abstracts and court opinions, from academic publishers, professional societies, online repositories, universities and other web sites.

Features

Results ranked by relevance with citation counts and 'Cited by' links, author profiles with h-index metrics, library links to institutional subscriptions, and alerts — under the famous tagline, a play on Newton: 'Stand on the shoulders of giants.'
