YouTube Premium

Ad-free, offline, and in the background: YouTube Premium is the paid tier of YouTube, bundling ad-free videos, downloads, background play and YouTube Music Premium into one subscription.

Benefits

  • Ad-free videos across YouTube and YouTube Kids
  • Downloads — save videos and playlists to watch offline
  • Background play — keep listening with the screen off or while using other apps
  • YouTube Music Premium included — ad-free music, downloads and background play in the Music app
  • Higher-quality 1080p Premium bitrate and other members-only experiments, as recalled

Plans

Individual, Family (sharing with up to five household members) and Student plans, billed monthly after a trial where offered. The US individual price was $13.99/month as last recalled.
