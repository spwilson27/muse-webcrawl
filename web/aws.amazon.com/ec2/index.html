Why EC2?

Graviton processors

AWS-designed Arm chips deliver up to 40% better price performance for cloud workloads.

Nitro System

Offloaded virtualisation gives bare-metal performance with hardware-level security.

Flexible purchasing

On-Demand, Savings Plans, Reserved Instances and Spot — mix them to optimise cost.

Pricing snapshot

Free Tier includes 750 hours/month of eligible t2/t3.micro instances for new accounts for 12 months. Everything else is per-second billing (60-second minimum) — representative only; see the live pricing page for rates.
