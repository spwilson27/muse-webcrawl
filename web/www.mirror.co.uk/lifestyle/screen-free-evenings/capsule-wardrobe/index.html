Try this tonight: lay out tomorrow’s outfit and feel tomorrow morning change completely

A capsule wardrobe makes getting dressed effortless – and spending five screen-free minutes choosing tomorrow’s outfit tonight might be its loveliest benefit.

By Fashion Writer • 19:30, 25 Oct 2025

The magic of a capsule wardrobe isn’t minimalism for its own sake. It’s that around 35 well-chosen pieces, all in a shared palette, combine into weeks of outfits – which means choosing one takes minutes, not a wardrobe-floor crisis.

The five-minute evening ritual

After dinner, phone charging in another room, stand in front of your edited rail and pick tomorrow’s whole outfit: clothes, shoes, coat, bag. Hang it on the back of the door. Morning you – half asleep, possibly late – will be enormously grateful.

Why a capsule makes it work

In a cluttered wardrobe, this ritual fails because nothing goes with anything. In a capsule, nearly every top works with every bottom. Add weather-checking by looking out of the window (radically screen-free) and you’re set.

Building yours in an evening a week

No need for a weekend blitz. One screen-free evening a week, pull five pieces you haven’t worn, try them on, and decide: keep, tailor, donate. Within a couple of months you’ll have a rail where everything earns its place.

Photograph your favourites: When an outfit works, snap it (phones are allowed for this bit) and keep an album. On uninspired mornings, just copy a greatest hit.
