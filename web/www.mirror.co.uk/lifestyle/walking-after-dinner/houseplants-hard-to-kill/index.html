If your evening walk is the best part of your day, these unkillable houseplants bring that feeling home

Fresh air, green views, a clear head – walkers know the magic. Capture a little of it indoors with plants that thrive on neglect.

By Homes Writer • 18:30, 6 Nov 2025

There’s a reason the after-dinner walk feels so good: movement, daylight and greenery are a powerful combination. On the nights you can’t get out – or to extend the feeling when you get back – a few well-chosen houseplants do some of the same work for your mood.

Start by the front door

A tall snake plant in the hallway greets you like a deep breath when you come in from your walk. It needs almost no water and doesn’t mind the draught from the door.

Green views in every room

Trail pothos where your eye lands from the sofa. Put a spider plant in the bathroom’s humidity, which it loves. A jade plant on the kitchen sill catches the morning sun while the kettle boils.

The care routine that fits a walker

People who walk daily are creatures of routine, which is exactly what plants want: a weekly check on the same day, a drink when dry, nothing more. Add a slow lap of the houseplants to your evening and you’ve built a second, smaller stroll into your day.

Rainy-day swap: When the weather truly wins, do your “indoor lap” instead – water, wipe leaves, rotate pots. Ten minutes of pottering keeps the streak (and the plants) alive.
