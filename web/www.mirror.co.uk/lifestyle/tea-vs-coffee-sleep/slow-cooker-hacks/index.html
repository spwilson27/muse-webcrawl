Your slow cooker’s secret evening job: warm, spiced drinks that help the whole house wind down

Dinner’s done, but the slow cooker isn’t. On its keep-warm setting it’s a brilliant drinks station for caffeine-free evening brews.

By Food Writer • 18:55, 4 Nov 2025

When the tea-versus-coffee debate turns to evenings, the answer is usually “neither, after a certain hour”. That’s where the slow cooker comes in: a big, gentle batch of something warm and fragrant that everyone can help themselves to all evening.

Mulled apple and spice

Cloudy apple juice, a cinnamon stick, star anise, cloves and orange slices. Two hours on low and the house smells like a Christmas market; keep warm until bedtime.

Spiced rooibos chai

Rooibos is naturally caffeine-free with a round, tea-like body. Simmer it with ginger, cardamom and a splash of milk for a chai that won’t keep anyone up.

Golden milk

Warm milk (or oat milk) whisked with turmeric, cinnamon, a pinch of black pepper and honey. Earthy, golden and very much a lights-down drink.

Party trick: At gatherings, a slow cooker of mulled drinks keeps you out of the kitchen and your guests out of the cold – and nobody has to be the one who made coffee at 9pm.
