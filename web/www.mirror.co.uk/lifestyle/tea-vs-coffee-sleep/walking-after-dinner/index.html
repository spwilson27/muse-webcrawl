Walk after dinner, then a brew before bed: the ideal order of an evening, explained

Dinner, a stroll, a calming drink, bed. Getting the sequence right makes each part work better – here’s the sleep-friendly running order.

By Health Writer • 20:05, 5 Nov 2025

Two of our favourite healthy habits – the after-dinner walk and the calming evening drink – are even better in the right order. Think of the evening as a staircase, each step taking you closer to sleep.

Step 1: Dinner, not too late

Finish eating ideally two to three hours before bed. Your body sleeps better when it isn’t busy digesting a big meal.

Step 2: The walk

Within half an hour of dinner, take your 10–15 minute stroll. It aids digestion, steadies blood sugar, and the outdoor light (even at dusk) helps anchor your body clock. Save the brew for when you’re back – walking with a full mug of tea is nobody’s idea of relaxing.

Step 3: The drink

Home again, kettle on. This is herbal’s moment: chamomile, rooibos or a mint tea. Coffee at this hour would undo the walk’s good work for many sleepers, and even black tea is best kept to the afternoon. Sip it somewhere screen-free, and let it be the last thing before your bedtime routine.

Too cold to walk? A few minutes of gentle tidying, stretching or stair-climbing after dinner captures some of the same digestion benefits. Movement is the point, not the mileage.
