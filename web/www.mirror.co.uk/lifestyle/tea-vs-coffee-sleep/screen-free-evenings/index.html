Build the perfect screen-free evening: kettle on, phone in a drawer, and your brew chosen wisely

A screen-free evening runs on rituals, and the best one starts with a kettle. Here’s why what you brew matters as much as putting the phone away.

By Lifestyle Reporter • 19:15, 3 Nov 2025

The screen-free evening has three pillars: something to do with your hands, something to sip, and a bedtime that arrives naturally. Getting the drink right is the easiest win of the three.

Coffee drinkers, read this first

Nobody is taking your morning coffee away. But caffeine hangs around for hours – a late-afternoon cup can still be half-strength in your system at bedtime. If evenings are for winding down, coffee is simply on the wrong team after about 2pm for most people.

Tea, glorious tea

A proper black tea has about half coffee’s caffeine – a reasonable afternoon bridge. By evening, switch to the herbal shelf: chamomile for calm, rooibos for a tea-like depth without caffeine, peppermint after rich dinners, or a valerian blend if you like your rituals with extra intent.

The evening, assembled

Phone charging in the kitchen at 7pm. Kettle on, herbal tea brewing. A walk, a chapter of a book, a board game or a long bath. Lights lower at 10. It sounds simple because it is – the difficulty was never knowledge, just the scroll-shaped habit in the way.

Make it a ceremony: A favourite mug, a teapot instead of a bag-in-cup rush, and sitting down to drink it. Ritual tells your brain the day is ending far more powerfully than willpower alone.
