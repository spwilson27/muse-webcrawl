The capsule wardrobe, but for plants: five core houseplants that work in every single room

Fashion fans swear by doing more with less. The same trick works for your indoor jungle – a tight edit of hard-working plants you can move from room to room, season to season.

By Homes Writer • 10:20, 21 Oct 2025

A capsule wardrobe works because every piece earns its place. Apply the same thinking to plants and you end up with a collection that’s easy to care for, easy to move around, and never looks tired.

The statement piece: snake plant

Every capsule needs a hero. Tall, architectural snake plants anchor a corner the way a great coat anchors an outfit – and they ask for almost nothing in return.

The versatile basic: pothos

Trailing over a shelf, climbing a moss pole or cut back into a bushy pot, pothos is the white T-shirt of the plant world: it goes with everything.

The neutral knit: ZZ plant

Glossy, calm and shade-tolerant, the ZZ is the reliable jumper you reach for daily. It fills awkward low-light gaps without complaint.

The classic: spider plant

Like a Breton stripe, it never dates. Spider plants suit bathrooms, kitchens and bedrooms alike, and their babies are the plant equivalent of hand-me-downs.

The finishing touch: jade plant

A sunny windowsill succulent with sculptural charm – the equivalent of the one piece of jewellery that completes the look.

Styling tip: Keep pots in a tight palette – terracotta, white and black – and you can shuffle plants between rooms endlessly, just like mixing capsule outfits.
