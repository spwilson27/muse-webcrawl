Phones away, watering can out: the plant lover’s screen-free evening routine

Tending plants is the perfect screen-free hobby – slow, tactile and quietly satisfying. Here’s how to turn plant care into a calming evening ritual.

By Lifestyle Reporter • 18:10, 23 Oct 2025

Scrolling is passive; plant care is the opposite. It asks you to look closely, use your hands and slow down – exactly what a frazzled evening brain needs. Build a little ritual around it and your plants will thrive while you unwind.

The 20-minute plant round

Once or twice a week, do a slow lap of the house with a small watering can. Check soil with a fingertip before watering, wipe dusty leaves with a damp cloth (they photosynthesise better clean), and turn pots a quarter-turn so growth stays even.

Propagation station

Keep a little tray for cuttings: snip leggy pothos or philodendron stems, root them in water, and pot them up when roots reach a few centimetres. It’s quietly addictive – and free plants make lovely gifts.

Then, proper downtime

Plants done, make a herbal tea, light a candle and settle in with a book or a puzzle. The room literally breathes easier, and so will you.

Evening light check: Lamplight is for you, not them – plants rest at night too. If a plant looks leggy, it needs more daylight, not more evening light.
