Declutter in 30 days: the plant lover’s plan that keeps the jungle but ditches the chaos

Love your plants but drowning in stuff? This gentle month-long plan clears every room while making your greenery the star of the show.

By Homes Writer • 07:45, 22 Oct 2025

Plant people collect more than plants: spare pots, half-bags of compost, propagations in jam jars on every sill. A declutter doesn’t mean fewer plants – it means every plant gets the space to shine.

Days 1–10: Surfaces and sills

Clear every windowsill to just the plants that love it there. Group the rest on a single stand or shelf – a “plant station” that looks intentional instead of scattered. Sort pots: keep the good ones, recycle the cracked, donate the duplicates.

Days 11–20: The hidden clutter

Tackle one cupboard or drawer a day. Wardrobe next, using the try-everything-on rule. Paperwork gets a single folder system. Each finished space earns a small reward – perhaps a cutting swapped with a friend rather than a new purchase.

Days 21–30: Systems that stick

Label storage, set up a watering routine (one day a week, same day), and adopt one-in-one-out for plants and possessions alike. Finish with a full house tidy and a long look at your calm, green rooms.

Propagation amnesty: Those jars of cuttings count as clutter if you never pot them. This month, pot them, gift them, or compost them – no judgement either way.
