The winter garden jobs you need to do before Christmas

A couple of weekends of work now will save your plants from frost, your lawn from mud and your spring self from a mammoth tidy-up.

By Mirror Lifestyle TeamPublished: 09:07, Sun, Oct 18, 2026 | Updated: 10:31

The garden does not stop for winter — it just slows down. And the jobs you tick off before the festive season are the ones that decide whether spring starts with a pleasure or a disaster zone. Here is the essential checklist.

1. Protect tender plants

Wrap pots in horticultural fleece or bubble wrap and move the most vulnerable against a house wall. One hard frost can split an unprotected terracotta pot and kill the plant inside.

2. Clear the leaves — mostly

Rake leaves off lawns and paths, where they smother grass and turn lethal-slippery. But leave a pile in a quiet corner: hedgehogs and insects will thank you for the winter hotel.

3. Prune while it is dormant

Apple and pear trees, wisteria and most deciduous shrubs can be pruned now that the leaves have fallen — you can actually see the shape you are cutting.

4. Plant for spring

It is not too late for tulip bulbs, bare-root roses and hedging. The soil is still workable, and roots will quietly establish over winter.

5. Service the tools

Clean, sharpen and oil secateurs, shears and the mower before they go away. Ten minutes now beats a rusty, blunt disaster in March.

6. Look after wildlife

Keep bird feeders topped up and break the ice on ponds and bird baths on frosty mornings. Winter is when garden wildlife needs you most.

7. Drain and store

Empty water butts that might freeze and crack, lag outdoor taps, and bring hosepipes under cover.

None of it takes long, and there is a quiet pleasure in a garden put properly to bed. Do these jobs before Christmas and you can enjoy the view from the warm side of the window, mulled wine in hand, guilt-free.
