How to build a capsule wardrobe with 30 pieces you'll actually wear all season

A wardrobe bursting at the seams but "nothing to wear" is a familiar morning misery. The capsule wardrobe promises to end it - with fewer clothes, more outfits and far less decision fatigue.

By Lifestyle Reporter · 08:20, Updated 09:47

The idea is simple: strip your wardrobe back to a small collection of versatile pieces that all work together, in a colour palette that suits you. Instead of 100 items that clash, you keep around 30 that mix and match into dozens of outfits.

Step one: empty everything out

Take every item out of the wardrobe and drawers. Anything you haven't worn in a year, that doesn't fit, or that you're only keeping "just in case" goes into the charity, sell or recycle pile. Be honest - the dress you wore once in 2019 is not coming back into rotation.

Step two: pick your palette

Choose two or three neutral base colours - black, navy, grey, camel or white - then add one or two accent colours you love. If everything shares a palette, everything matches.

Step three: the core 30

  • Two pairs of trousers and one pair of jeans
  • Two skirts or dresses that layer well
  • Five tops that pair with everything
  • Three knitwear pieces
  • A blazer, a coat and a casual jacket
  • Three pairs of shoes: trainers, boots and a smarter pair
  • The basics you already own and reach for constantly

Step four: the one-in, one-out rule

Once the capsule is set, new purchases replace something rather than adding to the pile. Fans say it transforms shopping from impulse buying into considered choices - and saves a fortune.

One convert told us: "Getting dressed takes me four minutes now. I used to stand in front of a stuffed wardrobe feeling stressed before I'd even had my tea."

Another said: "I sold the clothes I cleared out and made enough to buy two really good quality pieces I'd never have stretched to otherwise."

You don't need to go full minimalist overnight. Many people start with a single rail or one drawer, live with it for a month, and find they never go back to the chaos.
