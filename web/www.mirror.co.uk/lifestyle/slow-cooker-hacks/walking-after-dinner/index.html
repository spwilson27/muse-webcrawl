The perfect evening pairing: the slow cooker minds dinner while you take your after-dinner walk

One cooks, one strolls. Together, slow cooking and a post-dinner walk build an evening that looks after your digestion, your energy bills and your sleep.

By Food Writer • 17:35, 1 Nov 2025

Here’s an evening that runs itself. Load the slow cooker at lunchtime or before work. Eat early, while everything is tender and the house smells wonderful. Then, instead of the sofa, head straight out for your 15-minute walk.

Why they work so well together

Slow cooker dinners are ready when you are, which makes eating earlier easy – and earlier eating plus a gentle walk is a proven double act for steadier blood sugar and easier digestion. There’s no frantic cooking window eating into your evening, and no heavy late meal sitting awkwardly as you try to sleep.

The routine, step by step

Morning: ten minutes of prep, cooker on low. Home time: dinner is done – serve, enjoy, no rush. Within 30 minutes of eating: shoes on, loop round the block, daylight (or dusk) in your eyes. Home again: leftovers portioned for tomorrow, herbal tea on, screens off.

Weekend version: Put a stew on before a longer afternoon walk and come home to a house that smells like a hug. Future-you will be very smug indeed.
