Declutter the kitchen in 30 days – one cupboard at a time, while the slow cooker handles dinner

The kitchen is the hardest room to declutter because it’s always in use. This plan works around mealtimes: dinner bubbles away while you sort, one small zone a day.

By Homes Writer • 09:45, 30 Oct 2025

Set the slow cooker going in the morning and you’ve bought yourself a calm evening slot. Use it for one of these daily ten-minute tasks and the kitchen transforms over the month.

Week 1: Food first

Check dates in one cupboard a day. Spices older than a year have lost their punch – out they go. Decant staples into clear, labelled jars so you can see what you have and stop buying duplicates of cumin.

Week 2: Equipment honesty

Two of everything? Keep the better one. Gadgets untouched in a year get donated. The slow cooker, a good knife and a cast-iron pan will carry almost every meal you make.

Weeks 3–4: Surfaces and systems

Clear the counters to near-empty – the room will feel twice the size. Set up zones: prep, cooking, drinks. Finish with a “use it up” week of slow cooker dinners built from the freezer and store cupboard.

The box test: Unsure about a gadget? Box it with the date on. If the box is unopened in three months, donate it unopened.
