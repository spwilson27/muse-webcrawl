The capsule kitchen: just 12 hard-working items – and the slow cooker is the star of the show

Capsule wardrobes prove you don’t need much, just the right things. Your kitchen drawers deserve the same treatment.

By Food Writer • 11:30, 29 Oct 2025

Open the average kitchen drawer and you’ll find a garlic press used twice, an avocado slicer and three corkscrews. A capsule kitchen strips it back to a dozen multi-taskers that genuinely earn their space.

The magnificent twelve

1. The slow cooker – your set-and-forget sous chef. 2. One excellent chef’s knife. 3. A large chopping board. 4. A cast-iron pan that goes stove-to-oven. 5. A big stockpot with a lid. 6. A baking tray. 7. Wooden spoons (the original multi-tool). 8. A mixing bowl that doubles as a serving bowl. 9. A colander. 10. Tongs. 11. A kettle. 12. Storage tubs that stack.

Why the slow cooker leads the list

Like the perfect capsule coat, it goes with everything: stews, soups, curries, porridge, pulled meats, even mulled drinks. It turns the cheapest ingredients into the best dinners and frees up your evening while it does it.

One-in, one-out: Borrowed a fancy gadget from a friend and loved it? Fine – but something else leaves the drawer. Capsule discipline is what keeps counters clear.
