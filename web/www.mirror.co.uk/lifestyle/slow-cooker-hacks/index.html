7 slow cooker hacks that'll change your weeknight dinners

Dust off the slow cooker — these simple tricks turn a humble pot into the hardest-working gadget in your kitchen.

By Mirror Lifestyle TeamPublished: 11:05, Sun, Oct 18, 2026 | Updated: 12:44

The slow cooker is the most underrated appliance in Britain: pennies to run, near-impossible to mess up, and it makes cheap cuts of meat taste like a Sunday roast. These seven hacks take it from good to indispensable.

1. Brown first, thank yourself later

Searing meat before it goes in takes five minutes and adds a depth of flavour that no amount of slow cooking can fake. Deglaze the pan with a splash of stock and pour it all in.

2. Layer like a lasagne

Hard veg (carrots, potatoes, swede) on the bottom where it is hottest, meat in the middle, soft veg and herbs on top. Everything cooks evenly instead of turning to mush.

3. Go easy on the liquid

Unlike the hob, a slow cooker barely loses moisture to evaporation. Halve the liquid you would use in a normal recipe — you can always add more, but you cannot take it away.

4. Thicken at the end

Stir in a teaspoon of cornflour mixed with cold water 30 minutes before serving for a glossy, restaurant-style sauce instead of a watery stew.

5. Use it as a cake tin

Yes, really — slow cookers bake brilliant sponge puddings and chocolate cakes. Grease the pot well, cook on high for two to three hours, and serve warm with custard.

6. Batch-cook breakfast

Overnight porridge with apple and cinnamon, or a full breakfast casserole, cooks itself while you sleep. Wake up to the smell of a café.

7. Freeze in the liner

Line the pot with a freezer bag before cooking, lift the whole lot out when cool, and freeze the bag. Zero washing up, and a homemade ready meal for a busy night.

One last rule: resist lifting the lid. Every peek drops the temperature and adds 15–20 minutes to the cook time. Trust the process — dinner is doing itself.
