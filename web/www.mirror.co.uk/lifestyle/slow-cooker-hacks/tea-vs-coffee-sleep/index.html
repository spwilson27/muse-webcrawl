Sleep-friendly slow cooker suppers: eat earlier, eat gentler, and swap the evening coffee for tea

Heavy, spicy, late dinners are a recipe for a restless night. The slow cooker’s gentle style is perfect for food that satisfies without keeping you up.

By Health Writer • 16:20, 31 Oct 2025

What you eat in the evening matters, but when and how you eat it matters just as much. Slow cooking helps on all fronts: dinner is ready early, flavours are mellow, and portions of rich ingredients go further.

Build a sleep-friendly plate

Base supper on vegetables, pulses and lean proteins – a lentil and root-veg stew, a gentle chicken and barley soup, or a mild bean chilli. Go easy on very spicy, very fatty or huge portions close to bedtime, and try to finish eating two to three hours before sleep.

And the drink question

Tea vs coffee at night has a clear winner. Coffee’s caffeine lingers for hours; even decaf carries a little. Regular tea has less, but herbal teas – chamomile, rooibos, peppermint – are caffeine-free and signal “wind down” to your brain. Many slow cooker fans keep a pot of mulled herbal tea or spiced warm milk on the keep-warm setting for the evening.

Timing trick: Put tomorrow’s porridge in the slow cooker on low before bed. You’ll wake to a ready breakfast – and one less morning decision.
