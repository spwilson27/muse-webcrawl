A decluttered home still needs life – these are the houseplants even serial plant-killers can keep alive

Day 18 of our 30-day declutter: you’ve cleared the clutter, now add back one beautiful, living thing. Choose from these famously forgiving plants.

By Homes Writer • 07:30, 18 Oct 2025

There’s a moment in every declutter when the house looks calm but a bit bare. A plant or two solves that instantly – as long as it doesn’t become another chore. These are the varieties our gardening experts reach for when someone says “I kill everything.”

Snake plant: the set-and-forget star

Upright, sculptural and content with monthly watering, it’s ideal for the newly tidy living room. Keep it out of soggy soil and it will outlive the sofa.

Pothos: green without the grief

Trail it from the top of a bookshelf you’ve just decluttered. It roots happily in water, so cuttings make free gifts for friends.

Spider plant: the comeback kid

Forgot it for a fortnight? It will sulk, then bounce straight back after a drink. Its dangling babies look anything but cluttered.

ZZ plant and cast iron plant

Both tolerate the dark corners where clutter used to hide. The ZZ’s glossy leaves look designer; the cast iron plant has been unbothered by neglect since Victorian times.

Declutterer’s rule: One statement plant beats ten fussy ones. Choose a single hero pot per room and your space stays serene – and alive.
