How to build a capsule wardrobe: the 33 pieces that end the “nothing to wear” panic for good

A wardrobe bursting at the seams but nothing to put on? The capsule method – a small, hard-working set of clothes that all mix and match – is the declutterer’s best friend.

By Fashion Writer • 09:00, 17 Oct 2025

Day 12 of our 30-day declutter challenge turns to the wardrobe, and for many readers it’s the toughest task of the month. The capsule wardrobe idea is simple: keep only the pieces you love, that fit, and that work together – typically around 30 to 40 items per season.

Step 1: The honest edit

Take everything out. Yes, everything. Try each piece on and sort into keep, donate, sell and recycle piles. If you haven’t worn it in a year and it isn’t occasionwear, it’s taking up space that useful clothes could fill.

Step 2: Choose your palette

Pick two or three neutral base colours (black, navy, grey, camel) and one or two accent colours you love. When everything shares a palette, almost everything goes with everything else.

Step 3: The core 33

A classic capsule includes: a tailored coat, a versatile jacket, three pairs of trousers (smart, casual, jeans), two skirts or dresses, five tops, four knitwear pieces, a white shirt, two pairs of everyday shoes, one pair of boots, workout kit, and the basics. Your list will differ – the point is intention, not the exact number.

The one-in, one-out rule: Once your capsule is built, protect it. Anything new that comes in means something else goes out. Your wardrobe stays calm and so do your mornings.

Step 4: Store the rest out of sight

Not ready to part with the maybes? Box them up for three months. If you never open the box, you have your answer.
