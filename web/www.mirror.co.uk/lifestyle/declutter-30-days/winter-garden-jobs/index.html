Winter garden jobs: 10 tasks to do now and your spring garden will thank you for every single one

The garden may look asleep, but winter is when the real work happens. Think of it as a declutter for your outdoor space – clear, sort and protect, and spring will repay you.

By Gardening Writer • 08:15, 20 Oct 2025

Just like day 24 of our declutter challenge tackles the shed, these winter garden jobs are about putting things in order before the growing season. Wrap up warm, put the kettle on, and work through the list a couple of jobs at a time.

1. Clear fallen leaves from lawns and paths

Rake leaves off grass before they smother it, but pile them in a corner – leaf mould is free garden gold, and hedgehogs love a leaf pile.

2. Mulch, mulch, mulch

A thick blanket of compost or bark over bare beds suppresses weeds, protects roots from frost and feeds the soil. It’s the garden equivalent of putting everything away in labelled boxes.

3. Prune apples, pears and roses

Winter is the time to shape fruit trees while they’re dormant. Remove crossing, dead or diseased branches and open up the centre for air and light.

4. Protect tender plants

Wrap pots in fleece or bubble wrap, move terracotta indoors before frost cracks it, and lift dahlias if your soil stays wet.

5–10. The quick list

5. Clean and oil tools before storing them. 6. Order seeds – the good varieties sell out. 7. Top up bird feeders and keep water ice-free. 8. Check fences and trellis after storms. 9. Plan next year’s beds on paper. 10. Leave some seed heads standing – they feed birds and look magical in frost.

Shed sort-out: While you’re out there, declutter the shed. Out-of-date chemicals, broken pots and the strimmer you never use can all go – you’ll find your tools in half the time.
