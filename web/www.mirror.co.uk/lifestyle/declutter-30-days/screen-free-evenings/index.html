The declutter challenge with a twist: swap one evening of scrolling for a drawer, a shelf and a calmer home

Our 30-day declutter pairs perfectly with a screen-free evening. Small daily tasks, no phone in hand – and a house that feels lighter by the end of the month.

By Homes Writer • 19:00, 19 Oct 2025

The average person checks their phone well over a hundred times a day. Our declutter challenge asks a simple question: what if just one of those evening scroll sessions became a small, satisfying tidy-up instead?

How the challenge works

Each day has one bite-sized task – never more than 30 minutes. The junk drawer. The bathroom cabinet. The “important papers” pile. By day 30 you’ve touched every hotspot in the house without a single overwhelming weekend blitz.

The first week

Day 1: The entryway drop zone. Day 2: Expired food from one kitchen cupboard. Day 3: The bathroom shelf. Day 4: Socks without partners. Day 5: Phone photos – delete duplicates and screenshots. Day 6: The car. Day 7: Rest, and enjoy it screen-free.

Weeks two to four

Wardrobes, paperwork, kids’ toys, the loft and the garage follow in the same gentle rhythm. The secret weapon is the screen-free rule: with no phone to fall into, a 20-minute tidy feels easy – even enjoyable with music on.

Keep it going: Put a charity bag by the front door for the whole month. When it’s full, it goes out. No second-guessing, no “maybe” piles.
