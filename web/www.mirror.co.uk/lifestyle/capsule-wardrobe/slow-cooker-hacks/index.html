11 slow cooker hacks that will change how you cook forever – including the teatowel trick

It’s the hardest-working gadget in the kitchen, but most of us barely scratch the surface. These simple tricks turn good slow cooker dinners into great ones.

By Food Writer • 12:15, 16 Oct 2025

Like a capsule wardrobe for your kitchen, a slow cooker does more with less: cheap cuts, a handful of store-cupboard staples and a bit of patience. But a few insider tricks make all the difference.

1. The teatowel trick

Condensation dripping from the lid can make sauces watery. Place a clean teatowel under the lid to catch the drips and keep sauces thick and rich.

2. Don’t peek

Every lift of the lid lets out heat and can add 20 minutes to the cooking time. Set it, forget it, and trust the process.

3. Brown the meat first

It’s tempting to skip it, but five minutes in a hot pan builds deep, savoury flavour you can’t get any other way.

4. Less liquid than you think

Slow cookers barely lose any moisture, so use about a third less liquid than a stovetop recipe. You can always add more at the end.

Money-saver: Tough, cheaper cuts like shin of beef, pork shoulder and chicken thighs become meltingly tender in the slow cooker – and cost a fraction of prime cuts.

5–11. The rapid-fire round

5. Add dairy and fresh herbs in the last 30 minutes so they don’t split or fade. 6. Cut veg chunky so it doesn’t turn to mush. 7. Fill it at least half full for even cooking. 8. Use liners or a light oiling for painless washing up. 9. Make porridge overnight for ready-when-you-are breakfasts. 10. Mulled drinks keep beautifully on the “keep warm” setting at parties. 11. Double up stews and freeze half – future you says thanks.
