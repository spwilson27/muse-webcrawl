Screen-free evenings are trending – here are 14 lovely ways to spend the night without scrolling

Phones in a drawer, telly off, kettle on. More of us are reclaiming our evenings, and it turns out there’s plenty to do once you put the screen down.

By Lifestyle Reporter • 18:45, 15 Oct 2025

It starts the same way for most of us: you sit down “for five minutes”, and suddenly it’s 11pm and you’ve watched forty videos about people renovating vans. A screen-free evening sounds drastic, but readers who’ve tried it say the first night is the hardest – and the most rewarding.

Start with one evening a week

Pick a night, charge your phone in the kitchen, and tell the household the plan. Having a start time (say, 7pm) and an end time (bedtime) makes it feel like an event rather than a punishment.

Things to do instead

1. Dig out a board game or a pack of cards. 2. Start a jigsaw and leave it on the table all week. 3. Cook a recipe that takes its time – a slow stew or homemade bread. 4. Read an actual paper book. 5. Write in a journal, even just three lines. 6. Do a face mask and have a proper bath. 7. Play records or an instrument, badly is fine. 8. Sort one drawer – oddly satisfying. 9. Learn five phrases in another language. 10. Stargaze from the garden with a blanket. 11. Write a letter to a friend. 12. Do a crossword together. 13. Plan a trip on a paper map. 14. Simply talk – it’s amazing what comes up.

Reader tip: “We keep a ‘phone basket’ by the front door on Wednesdays. Guests think it’s charming, and nobody misses their notifications after the first ten minutes,” says one reader from Leeds.

Why it works

Without the constant drip of notifications, your brain gets a chance to be bored – and boredom is where daydreams, ideas and proper rest come from. Many people report falling asleep faster and waking up clearer after just a few screen-free nights.
