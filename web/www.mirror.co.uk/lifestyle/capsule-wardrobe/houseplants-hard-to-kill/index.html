Eight houseplants that are almost impossible to kill – even if you forget to water them for weeks

Not everyone has green fingers, but that doesn’t mean your home has to be a plant-free zone. These tough-as-old-boots favourites thrive on neglect.

By Lifestyle Reporter • 06:30, 14 Oct 2025

We’ve all been there: you bring home a glossy new plant full of good intentions, and within a month it’s a crispy brown shadow of its former self. The good news is that some plants positively thrive on being ignored.

1. Snake plant (Sansevieria)

The undisputed champion of neglect. A snake plant can go a month without water, tolerates low light, and still stands tall. Water it sparingly – soggy soil is the only thing that really upsets it.

2. Pothos

This trailing beauty forgives missed waterings, dim corners and enthusiastic pruning. Snip a stem, pop it in a jar of water, and you’ll have a whole new plant for free.

3. Spider plant

Spider plants bounce back from almost anything and even reward you with baby plantlets you can pot up and give away. Brown leaf tips usually just mean it wants a drink.

4. ZZ plant

With its waxy, architectural leaves, the ZZ plant stores water in its roots, so it’s perfectly happy if you jet off on holiday and forget it exists.

Golden rule: More houseplants die from overwatering than underwatering. If in doubt, wait a few more days before reaching for the watering can.

5. Cast iron plant

Victorian parlours were dark and draughty, and this plant thrived in them – hence the name. It will shrug off the gloomiest hallway in your home.

6. Jade plant

A succulent with plump, coin-shaped leaves said to bring good fortune. It likes a sunny sill and very little fuss.

7. Heartleaf philodendron

Fast-growing, forgiving and happy to trail from a bookshelf. It will politely droop when thirsty and perk up within hours of a drink.

8. Chinese evergreen

Patterned leaves, low light tolerance and a calm temperament make this a brilliant first plant for nervous beginners.

Start with one or two from this list, put them where you’ll actually see them, and you might be surprised how quickly your confidence grows.
