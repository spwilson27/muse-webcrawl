Artemis

Returning Humans to the Moon

With the Artemis campaign, NASA will land the first woman and first person of colour on the lunar surface, explore more of the Moon than ever before, and prepare for human missions to Mars. Artemis II will carry astronauts around the Moon, and Artemis III aims to land near the lunar South Pole.
