Google News

Google News aggregates headlines from publishers worldwide into a personalised front page: a morning 'Your briefing', Top stories, local news, and topic sections, each story labelled with its source and time.

Top stories

Clusters of the day's biggest stories, each shown with several publishers' coverage side by side and a 'Full coverage' view gathering reporting, timelines and reactions on one topic.

Sections

  • World · U.S. · Business · Technology · Entertainment · Sports · Science · Health — standing topic tabs
  • Local — news for places you follow; Fact Check — a section surfacing fact-checkers' verdicts on circulating claims

For you

A personalised stream based on followed topics, sources and locations, with controls to see more or less of a topic — the defining feature of the post-2018 Google News design.
