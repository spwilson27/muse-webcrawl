NASA Technology & Innovation

NASA technology shows up far beyond space: memory foam, scratch-resistant lenses, water purification and countless 'spinoffs' began as solutions to spaceflight problems. The agency's technology programs mature ideas from laboratory concepts to flight-ready systems.

Spinoffs

NASA's annual Spinoff publication documents hundreds of commercialised technologies — medical devices, firefighting equipment, air-quality sensors and more — that trace their lineage to agency research.

Programs

The Space Technology Mission Directorate runs programs such as Tipping Point and the Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) awards, flight-tests new propulsion, robotics, in-space manufacturing and lunar surface technologies, and publishes the NASA TechPort catalogue of projects.

Prizes and challenges

Centennial Challenges and other competitions invite the public to solve problems from lunar excavation to CO2 conversion, with prize purses for winning teams.
