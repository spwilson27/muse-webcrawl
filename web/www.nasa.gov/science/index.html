NASA Science

NASA's Science Mission Directorate explores Earth, the Sun, the solar system and the universe beyond, answering big questions: How does the universe work? How did we get here? Are we alone?

Four divisions

  • Earth Science — studying our home planet as a system
  • Planetary Science — the Moon, Mars and the worlds of the solar system
  • Astrophysics — stars, galaxies, exoplanets and the cosmos
  • Heliophysics — the Sun and its influence through space

How science missions happen

Most NASA science missions are proposed by teams of scientists, selected competitively, and flown as principal-investigator-led or flagship missions. Programs such as Discovery, New Frontiers and Explorer run regular competitions for small, medium and large missions.

Open data

NASA science data are public by default, archived in systems such as the Planetary Data System and the Earth science archives, and used by researchers and citizens around the world. Citizen-science projects invite anyone to help classify galaxies, hunt asteroids and more.
