NASA+ — Streaming

NASA+ is the agency's free, ad-free streaming service, launched in November 2023: live coverage of launches and spacewalks, award-winning documentaries, original series and children's programming — no subscription required.

What's on

Live events include rocket launches, ISS arrivals and departures, and mission briefings. The catalogue features series on Artemis, Webb, Mars and Earth science, plus NASA's historical film archive.

How to watch

NASA+ streams through the NASA app on phones, tablets and connected TVs and directly on the web, as part of the redesigned nasa.gov experience.
