NASA Learning Resources — For Students and Educators

NASA STEM Engagement connects students and educators with the agency's missions: lesson plans, activities, internships, challenges and live events for learners from kindergarten through graduate school.

For students

  • Internships — paid NASA internships for high-school, undergraduate and graduate students, applied for through a single system as recalled
  • Challenges — student design competitions such as the Human Exploration Rover Challenge and Lunabotics
  • NASA EXPRESS — a weekly newsletter of STEM opportunities and resources

For educators

Classroom activities aligned to missions, educator professional development, downlinks and Q&A sessions with astronauts aboard the ISS, and the NASA Educator Resource Centers network.

Space Place and Climate Kids

NASA's Space Place (spaceplace.nasa.gov) and Climate Kids sites explain space and Earth science for younger audiences with games, crafts and simple explainers.
