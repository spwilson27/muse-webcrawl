NASA Aeronautics

NASA's 'A' is Aeronautics. The agency researches quieter, cleaner, safer flight — from the X-59 quiet supersonic demonstrator to electric aircraft propulsion and the air-traffic systems of the future.

X-planes

The X-59 Quesst mission aims to prove that a supersonic aircraft can fly over land with a gentle 'thump' instead of a disruptive boom, gathering data for regulators. Other experimental aircraft test electric propulsion (the X-57, concluded without flying as recalled) and advanced aerodynamics.

Airspace

NASA's Air Mobility work researches drones and advanced air mobility — electric air taxis and delivery aircraft — and how they can share the sky safely.

Sustainable flight

Partnerships with industry develop hybrid-electric engines, lightweight composite wings (the Sustainable Flight Demonstrator, based on a modified MD-90 as recalled) and sustainable aviation fuels to cut aviation's footprint.
