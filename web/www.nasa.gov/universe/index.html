The Universe — NASA Astrophysics

NASA's telescopes peer across the cosmos: Webb's infrared eye sees the first galaxies; Hubble still maps the universe after three decades; Chandra reveals the X-ray sky of black holes and exploded stars.

Big questions

How does the universe work? How did we get here? Are we alone? Astrophysics missions study dark matter and dark energy, the birth of stars and galaxies, and planets around other stars — more than five thousand exoplanets are now known, many found by Kepler and TESS.

Observatories

  • James Webb Space Telescope — infrared flagship at Sun–Earth L2, launched December 2021
  • Hubble Space Telescope — optical/ultraviolet workhorse since 1990
  • Chandra X-ray Observatory — high-energy universe since 1999
  • Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope — wide-field infrared surveyor in development
