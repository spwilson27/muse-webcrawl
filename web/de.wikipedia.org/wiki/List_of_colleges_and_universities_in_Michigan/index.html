Liste der Hochschulen in Michigan

Diese Liste enthält die Universitäten und Colleges im US-Bundesstaat Michigan. Zu den bekanntesten zählen die University of Michigan in Ann Arbor und die Michigan State University in East Lansing, die beide zu den führenden Forschungsuniversitäten der USA gehören.

  • Michigan State University (East Lansing)
  • Wayne State University (Detroit)
  • Michigan Technological University (Houghton)
  • Western Michigan University (Kalamazoo)
  • Central Michigan University (Mount Pleasant)
  • Eastern Michigan University (Ypsilanti)
  • Grand Valley State University (Allendale)
  • Ferris State University (Big Rapids)
  • Northern Michigan University (Marquette)
  • Oakland University (Rochester)
  • Saginaw Valley State University
  • Lake Superior State University (Sault Ste. Marie)
  • University of Michigan–Dearborn
  • University of Michigan–Flint

  • Calvin University (Grand Rapids)
  • Hope College (Holland)
  • Kalamazoo College
  • Albion College
  • Hillsdale College
  • Andrews University (Berrien Springs)
  • College for Creative Studies (Detroit)
  • Kettering University (Flint)
  • Lawrence Technological University (Southfield)
