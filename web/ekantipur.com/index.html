eKantipur — Nepal's Online News Portal

eKantipur is the online portal of Kantipur Publications — publisher of Kantipur and The Kathmandu Post — and one of Nepal's most-visited news sites, in Nepali and English.

Top stories

The homepage leads with Nepali politics and Kathmandu news, followed by sections for the provinces, the economy, world news, sports (cricket looms large) and entertainment from the Nepali film world. The English-language sister coverage runs through The Kathmandu Post.

About Kantipur

Kantipur daily, founded in 1993, is Nepal's largest-circulation Nepali newspaper; Kantipur Television and Radio Kantipur belong to the same group, and eKantipur aggregates them online.
