TitleComputing Machinery and Intelligence
Author(s)A. M. Turing
JournalMind 59 (236): 433–460
Year1950
DOI10.1093/mind/LIX.236.433

About this paper

'I propose to consider the question, "Can machines think?"' Turing replaces the question with the imitation game — the Turing test — and, in a tour of objections from theology to mathematics, predicts the shape of seventy years of argument about artificial intelligence, including learning machines and the idea that a machine's behaviour, not its innards, is the fair criterion.
