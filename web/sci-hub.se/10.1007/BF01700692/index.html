TitleÜber formal unentscheidbare Sätze der Principia Mathematica und verwandter Systeme I
Author(s)Kurt Gödel

Year1931
DOI10.1007/BF01700692

About this paper

Gödel's incompleteness paper, one of the most consequential results in the history of logic. By encoding statements about arithmetic as numbers within arithmetic itself, Gödel shows that any consistent formal system rich enough for arithmetic contains true statements it cannot prove, and cannot prove its own consistency. The result ended Hilbert's programme for a complete foundation of mathematics and echoes through computability theory and philosophy ever since.
