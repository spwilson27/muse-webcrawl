Über den anschaulichen Inhalt der quantentheoretischen Kinematik und Mechanik

TitleÜber den anschaulichen Inhalt der quantentheoretischen Kinematik und Mechanik
Author(s)Werner Heisenberg
JournalZeitschrift für Physik 43 (3–4): 172–198
Year1927
DOI10.1007/BF01397280

About this paper

The uncertainty principle paper. Heisenberg argues that position and momentum of a particle cannot both be sharply defined: Δx·Δp ≳ ħ/2, illustrated by his gamma-ray microscope thought experiment. What began as a remark about measurement became a structural limit of quantum mechanics itself and a cornerstone of the Copenhagen interpretation.
