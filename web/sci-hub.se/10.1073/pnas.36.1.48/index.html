TitleEquilibrium Points in n-Person Games

JournalProceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 36 (1): 48–49
Year1950
DOI10.1073/pnas.36.1.48

About this paper

Nash's one-page 1950 note proving that every finite n-person game has at least one equilibrium point in mixed strategies — the Nash equilibrium — using a fixed-point argument. A cornerstone of game theory and modern economics.
