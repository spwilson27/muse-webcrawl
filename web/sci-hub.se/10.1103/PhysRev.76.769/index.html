Space-Time Approach to Quantum Electrodynamics

TitleSpace-Time Approach to Quantum Electrodynamics
Author(s)Richard P. Feynman
JournalPhysical Review 76 (6): 769–789
Year1949
DOI10.1103/PhysRev.76.769

About this paper

Feynman reformulates quantum electrodynamics as a sum over space-time paths, introducing the diagrammatic bookkeeping — Feynman diagrams — that turned the most precise theory in physics into a calculable one. Together with the parallel work of Schwinger, Tomonaga and Dyson, it remade field theory and earned the 1965 Nobel Prize.
