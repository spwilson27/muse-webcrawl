Broken Symmetries and the Masses of Gauge Bosons

TitleBroken Symmetries and the Masses of Gauge Bosons
Author(s)Peter W. Higgs
JournalPhysical Review Letters 13 (16): 508–509
Year1964
DOI10.1103/PhysRevLett.13.508

About this paper

Higgs's second 1964 letter, the one that explicitly predicts a massive scalar boson as the leftover of spontaneous symmetry breaking in a gauge theory — the particle now called the Higgs boson. Rejected at first by one journal and published in Physical Review Letters, it became the keystone of the electroweak theory; the boson itself was found at CERN's Large Hadron Collider in 2012, and Higgs shared the 2013 Nobel Prize in Physics.
