About this paper

The first direct detection of gravitational waves: on 14 September 2015 the two LIGO interferometers recorded the chirp of two black holes of roughly 36 and 29 solar masses merging 1.3 billion light-years away, radiating three solar masses of energy as spacetime ripples. The signal matches general relativity's predictions in the strong-field regime Einstein's theory had never before been tested in — a century after he predicted the waves.
