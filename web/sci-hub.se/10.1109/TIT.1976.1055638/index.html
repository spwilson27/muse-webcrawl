New Directions in Cryptography

TitleNew Directions in Cryptography
Author(s)Whitfield Diffie, Martin E. Hellman
JournalIEEE Transactions on Information Theory 22 (6): 644–654
Year1976
DOI10.1109/TIT.1976.1055638

About this paper

The paper that split cryptography open: public-key cryptography. Diffie and Hellman propose that encryption and decryption keys can differ — one published, one secret — solving key distribution and enabling digital signatures, and they give the first concrete key-exchange protocol. Every TLS handshake on the modern web descends from this paper.
