About the project

Sci-Hub is a shadow library that gives free access to tens of millions of scientific articles and books, removing the paywalls that publishers place between readers and research. The project holds that knowledge should be available to everyone, not only to those at wealthy institutions: research paid for by the public should be readable by the public.

The collection is commonly described as holding well over 80 million documents. Founder: Alexandra Elbakyan (2011).
