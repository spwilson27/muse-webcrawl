About the project


Raven perched on a stack of scientific journals, holding a key (Sci-Hub artwork)

Sci-Hub is a shadow library founded in 2011 by Alexandra Elbakyan, a researcher and programmer from Kazakhstan who — like most scientists outside wealthy institutions — could not afford the per-article fees publishers charge, then commonly around $30 a paper. The service answers a DOI, title or article URL with the paper itself, drawn from a collection that has grown to tens of millions of documents, much of it mirrored alongside its sister project, Library Genesis.

The idea

The project's stated goal is to remove all barriers in the way of science. Its argument, as presented on the site and in Elbakyan's writings: research is overwhelmingly funded by the public; the scientists who write and referee papers are not paid by publishers; copyright over scholarly articles therefore functions as a toll on knowledge rather than an incentive to create it. Sci-Hub's motto: knowledge to all.

How it works

Paste a DOI (for example 10.1038/171737a0), a PMID or a publisher link into the box on the home page and the paper is served immediately — no registration, no fees. Requests the collection lacks are fetched on demand and added to it, so the library grows with use.

Mirrors

Because domains are periodically seized or blocked, the project rotates through mirror domains — sci-hub.se, sci-hub.st and sci-hub.ru among the best known — and is also reachable through Tor. The content is the same on every mirror.

Controversy

Publishers have sued: Elsevier won a default judgment in the United States in 2015, and the American Chemical Society won another in 2017; courts in several countries have ordered ISPs to block the domains. Universities and open-access advocates are divided — the service is plainly infringing, and also, its supporters note, the single most-used source of scientific literature in much of the world. Elbakyan has compared her work to that of Aaron Swartz and describes it as civil disobedience in defence of open knowledge.
