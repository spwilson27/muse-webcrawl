About this paper

The first of Einstein's 1905 'miracle year' papers. Arguing from the entropy of blackbody radiation, Einstein proposes that light itself consists of discrete energy quanta (later called photons) whose energy is proportional to frequency. The hypothesis explains the photoelectric effect — electrons are ejected with energies depending on the light's frequency, not its intensity — a prediction Millikan's experiments confirmed a decade later, and the work Einstein's 1921 Nobel Prize ultimately recognised.
