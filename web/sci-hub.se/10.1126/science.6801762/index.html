Novel Proteinaceous Infectious Particles Cause Scrapie

TitleNovel Proteinaceous Infectious Particles Cause Scrapie
Author(s)Stanley B. Prusiner
JournalScience 216 (4542): 136–144
Year1982
DOI10.1126/science.6801762

About this paper

Prusiner coins 'prion' and argues the scrapie agent is a protein with no nucleic acid at all — an infectious particle that propagates by corrupting the shape of its normal counterpart. Derided as heresy, the hypothesis explained kuru, CJD and later BSE ('mad cow disease') and won the 1997 Nobel Prize, adding protein-only inheritance to biology's repertoire.
