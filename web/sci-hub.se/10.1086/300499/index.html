Observational Evidence from Supernovae for an Accelerating Universe and a Cosmological Constant

TitleObservational Evidence from Supernovae for an Accelerating Universe and a Cosmological Constant
Author(s)Adam G. Riess et al. (High-z Supernova Search Team)
JournalThe Astronomical Journal 116 (3): 1009–1038
Year1998
DOI10.1086/300499

About this paper

Distant Type Ia supernovae appear fainter — hence farther — than a decelerating universe allows: cosmic expansion is accelerating, driven by something behaving like Einstein's cosmological constant, now called dark energy. Published the same year as the rival Supernova Cosmology Project's result, the discovery overturned the assumed fate of the universe and took the 2011 Nobel Prize.
