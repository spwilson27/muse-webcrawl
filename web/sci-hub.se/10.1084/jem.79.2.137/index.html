Studies on the Chemical Nature of the Substance Inducing Transformation of Pneumococcal Types

TitleStudies on the Chemical Nature of the Substance Inducing Transformation of Pneumococcal Types
Author(s)Oswald T. Avery, Colin M. MacLeod, Maclyn McCarty
JournalJournal of Experimental Medicine 79 (2): 137–158
Year1944
DOI10.1084/jem.79.2.137

About this paper

The Avery–MacLeod–McCarty experiment: the 'transforming principle' that could permanently change one strain of pneumococcus into another was purified and shown to behave, by every chemical and enzymatic test, as DNA. Published a decade before the double helix, it was the first strong evidence that genes are made of DNA — received cautiously at the time because proteins were still the favoured candidate.
