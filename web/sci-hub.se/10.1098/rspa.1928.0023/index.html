The Quantum Theory of the Electron

TitleThe Quantum Theory of the Electron
Author(s)Paul A. M. Dirac
JournalProceedings of the Royal Society A 117 (778): 610–624
Year1928
DOI10.1098/rspa.1928.0023

About this paper

Dirac's relativistic wave equation for the electron: a single equation that yields electron spin and the correct magnetic moment as consequences rather than assumptions. Its negative-energy solutions, at first an embarrassment, led Dirac to predict antimatter; Anderson found the positron in cosmic rays four years later.
