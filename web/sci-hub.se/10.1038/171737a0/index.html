Molecular Structure of Nucleic Acids: A Structure for Deoxyribose Nucleic Acid

TitleMolecular Structure of Nucleic Acids: A Structure for Deoxyribose Nucleic Acid
Author(s)James D. Watson, Francis H. C. Crick
JournalNature 171: 737–738
Year1953
DOI10.1038/171737a0

About this paper

The one-page 1953 letter in which Watson and Crick propose the double-helical structure of DNA: two antiparallel sugar-phosphate chains joined by hydrogen-bonded adenine–thymine and guanine–cytosine pairs, immediately suggesting a copying mechanism for genetic material. Built from the data of Rosalind Franklin, Maurice Wilkins and others, it founded molecular biology.
