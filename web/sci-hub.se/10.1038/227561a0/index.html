Central Dogma of Molecular Biology

TitleCentral Dogma of Molecular Biology
Author(s)Francis Crick
JournalNature 227: 561–563
Year1970
DOI10.1038/227561a0

About this paper

Written partly in response to the discovery of reverse transcriptase, Crick restates and clarifies the central dogma he had proposed in 1958: sequence information passes from nucleic acid to nucleic acid and from nucleic acid to protein, but never from protein back to sequence. A short paper of definitions that remains the standard reference for what the dogma does — and does not — claim.
