Science — CERN

What is the Universe made of? How did it start? Physicists at CERN use the world's largest and most complex scientific instruments to study the basic constituents of matter — the fundamental particles — and the laws that govern them.

Research areas

Accelerators, detectors and computing: the LHC experiments (ATLAS, CMS, ALICE, LHCb and smaller experiments) study the Higgs boson, antimatter, the quark–gluon plasma and searches for physics beyond the Standard Model, while CERN's theory division and the Worldwide LHC Computing Grid support the science.
