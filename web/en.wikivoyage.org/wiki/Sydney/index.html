Sydney is Australia's largest city, built around its harbour in New South Wales. Wikivoyage's guide covers the city centre and districts from The Rocks and Darling Harbour to Bondi and Manly, arrival at Sydney Airport, ferries and trains, and the harbour's beaches and walks.
