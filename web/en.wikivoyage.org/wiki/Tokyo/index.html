Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital of Japan, a vast metropolis of special wards and neighbouring cities. Wikivoyage divides its Tokyo guide into districts — including Asakusa, Ginza, Shinjuku, Shibuya, Ikebukuro, Akihabara and Odaiba — with arrival information for Narita and Haneda airports and the city's dense rail and subway network.

[Image: The Tokyo Skytree above the city skyline at dusk]

See

The Sensō-ji temple in Asakusa, the Meiji Shrine, the Imperial Palace East Gardens, the Tokyo Skytree and the observation decks of the Metropolitan Government Building are classic sights.

Practicalities

Suica / Pasmo cards cover transport; the guide explains etiquette, tipping (not customary), and day trips to Nikko, Kamakura and Mount Fuji.
