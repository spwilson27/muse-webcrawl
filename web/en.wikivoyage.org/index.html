Welcome to Wikivoyage — the free, worldwide travel guide built by travellers. Its guides cover destinations across the globe with practical information on getting in, getting around, seeing, doing, buying, eating, drinking and sleeping, written in a lively, traveller-to-traveller voice.

[Image: The Wikivoyage logo — a globe formed of arrows pointing outwards]

Destination of the Month

The front page features a Destination of the Month, a previous winner, and an Off the Beaten Path destination — complete guides with photographs and highlights.

Discover

A rotating Discover box offers unusual travel facts — a little-known sight, festival or custom from somewhere in the world.

Explore the world

Start from the continents: Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania, South America — or search for a country, region, city or park. Phrasebooks help travellers with local languages.

Travel topics

Guides also cover themes and practicalities: budget travel, business travel, festivals, UNESCO World Heritage sites, driving, visas and staying healthy and safe.
