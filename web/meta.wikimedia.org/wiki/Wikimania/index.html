Wikimania

This Meta-Wiki page is the hub for Wikimania, the Wikimedia movement's annual conference for contributors, researchers and staff — covering the current edition's host city, programme and scholarships, and linking to pages for past editions held in cities around the world since 2005.

[Image: Photograph of a large Wikimania conference audience]

The conference

Talks, workshops and hackathons cover community projects, technology, research, education and free-culture advocacy, with the Wikimedian of the Year award traditionally announced there.
