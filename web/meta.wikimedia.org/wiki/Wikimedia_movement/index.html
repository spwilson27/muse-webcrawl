Wikimedia movement

This Meta-Wiki page describes the Wikimedia movement: the international community of contributors to the Wikimedia projects, together with the Wikimedia Foundation, national chapters, thematic organisations and user groups that support them, and the movement's values, strategy process and governance discussions.

[Image: Group photograph of Wikimedians at a Wikimania conference]

Roles and organisations

Chapters and user groups run outreach, edit-a-thons, partnerships with libraries and museums, and conferences such as Wikimania.

Strategy

Movement strategy discussions and recommendations — including work towards the 2030 direction of becoming the essential infrastructure of the ecosystem of free knowledge — are documented here.
