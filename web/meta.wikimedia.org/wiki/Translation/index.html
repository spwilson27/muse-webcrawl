Translation

This Meta-Wiki page coordinates translation across the Wikimedia movement: how to request translations of announcements, policies and fundraiser messages, how volunteer translators work in many languages, the translation memories and tools in use, and links to the translators' mailing list and Babel pages for finding speakers of each language.

[Image: Speech bubbles / globe with many language scripts]

How it works

Requests are listed with priority and deadlines; translations are usually prepared on Meta and then copied to the projects and foundation sites that need them.
