A complete list of Wikimedia projects

This Meta-Wiki page lists all of the Wikimedia Foundation's projects: the content projects (Wikipedia, Wiktionary, Wikibooks, Wikiquote, Wikisource, Wikivoyage, Wikiversity, Wikinews, Wikispecies), the multimedia and data repositories (Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata), and the coordination and technical projects (Meta-Wiki, MediaWiki.org, Wikimedia Incubator, Wikimania and others), with links and statistics for each.

[Image: A grid of Wikimedia project logos]

Content projects

Each content project exists in many language editions; Wikipedia is by far the largest, with editions in over 300 languages.

Coordination

Outreach, grants, chapters and user groups are documented on Meta, and new language editions are incubated in the Wikimedia Incubator before launch.
