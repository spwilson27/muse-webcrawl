Grants

Meta-Wiki's grants pages document the Wikimedia Foundation's and community's grant programmes, which fund individuals, groups and affiliates to run events, content campaigns, software work and outreach aligned with the movement's mission. Pages explain current programmes, eligibility, how to apply, and reports from past grants.

[Image: Photograph of a community event funded by a Wikimedia grant]

Programmes

Programmes have included rapid grants for small, time-sensitive activities and larger project / conference and event grants, administered with community review.

Learning

Past grant reports and learning patterns are collected so other organisers can reuse what worked.
