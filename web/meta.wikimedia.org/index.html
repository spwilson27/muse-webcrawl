Welcome to Meta-Wiki — the global community site for the Wikimedia Foundation projects and the Wikimedia movement. Meta is where contributors coordinate across languages and projects: planning, documentation, discussions, events, grants, elections and movement strategy.

[Image: The Meta-Wiki / Wikimedia community logo — a circle of community figures]

What happens here

Meta hosts the movement's central discussions and pages: requests for comment, steward and global administrator coordination, spam and vandalism blacklists, cross-wiki help, translation coordination and documentation of Wikimedia conferences and chapters.

The projects

From Meta you can reach all Wikimedia projects — Wikipedia in its hundreds of languages, Wiktionary, Commons, Wikidata and their sisters — and the Wikimedia Foundation itself.

Participate

Community members can join discussions in the Babel language forum, help translate, propose grants, organise events and take part in movement-wide votes and consultations.
