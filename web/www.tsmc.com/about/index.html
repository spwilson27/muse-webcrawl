About TSMC

Founded in 1987 in Hsinchu, Taiwan, TSMC pioneered the pure-play semiconductor foundry model: it manufactures chips designed by its customers rather than selling its own branded products. That model helped ignite the fabless semiconductor industry.

Today TSMC is the world's largest dedicated chip foundry, producing the most advanced logic processes (including 3-nanometre-class technology and its successors) for customers in smartphones, high-performance computing, automotive and AI. Major fabrication sites are in Taiwan, with additional fabs in the United States (Arizona), Japan (Kumamoto) and Germany (Dresden) in various stages of construction and operation.
