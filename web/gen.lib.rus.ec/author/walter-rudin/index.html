Author: Walter Rudin

Walter Rudin (1921-2010) was an Austrian-American mathematician at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, author of the three classic analysis texts known to students as 'Baby Rudin', 'Papa Rudin' (Real and Complex Analysis) and 'Functional Analysis'.
