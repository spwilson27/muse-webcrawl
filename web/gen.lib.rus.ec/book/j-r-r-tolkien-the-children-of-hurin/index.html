Description

The first complete book by Tolkien published after The Silmarillion, assembled by Christopher Tolkien from his father's manuscripts and illustrated by Alan Lee. It tells the tragic tale of Túrin Turambar and his sister Niënor, cursed by Morgoth, in the First Age of Middle-earth.
