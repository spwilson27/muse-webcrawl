Description

The book that made Keynes famous. Having resigned from the British Treasury delegation at Versailles in disgust, he published in 1919 a blistering critique of the Treaty: the reparations demanded of Germany were unpayable, the economic disorganisation of Europe would deepen, and the settlement sowed the seeds of future catastrophe. Part reportage — with unforgettable portraits of Wilson, Clemenceau and Lloyd George — part economic warning, it was an international bestseller and remains a classic of economic polemic.
