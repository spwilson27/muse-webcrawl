Description

Charles Dickens' 1861 novel of the orphan Pip, whose encounter with the escaped convict Magwitch on the Kent marshes and a mysterious fortune that lifts him into London society entangle him with the frozen Miss Havisham and the cold Estella. A story of ambition, shame, gratitude and moral education, with one of literature's most famous revised endings.
