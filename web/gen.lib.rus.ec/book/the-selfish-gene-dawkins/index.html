Description

Richard Dawkins's 1976 synthesis of the gene-centred view of evolution: natural selection acts most clearly at the level of the replicator, and organisms are the survival machines genes build to copy themselves. The book popularised kin selection and reciprocal altruism for general readers and, in its final chapter, coined the word 'meme' for units of cultural transmission. The 30th anniversary edition adds a new introduction by the author reflecting on the book's reception.
