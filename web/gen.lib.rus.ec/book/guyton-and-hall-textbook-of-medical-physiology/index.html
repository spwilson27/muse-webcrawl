Description

Arthur C. Guyton's classic physiology textbook, continued after his death by John E. Hall, long the best-selling medical physiology text in the world. It explains the function of the human body from the cell to whole organ systems, with Guyton's characteristic emphasis on quantitative reasoning and feedback control, covering circulation, respiration, the kidneys and body fluids, the nervous system, endocrinology and metabolism. The 13th edition updated the molecular and clinical content throughout.
