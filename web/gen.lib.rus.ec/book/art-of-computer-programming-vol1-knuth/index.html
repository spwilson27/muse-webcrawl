Description

The first volume of Donald Knuth's monumental, still-unfinished treatise on algorithms, in print since 1968. Volume 1 lays the mathematical preliminaries and then treats information structures: stacks, queues, linked lists, trees and dynamic storage allocation, all illustrated in the assembly language of Knuth's idealised MIX (later MMIX) computer. Famous both for its depth and its exercises, whose answers and difficulty ratings fill the back of the book, and for Knuth's long-standing offer of a reward cheque for every error found.
