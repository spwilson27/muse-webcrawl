Description

Jane Austen's beloved novel of manners, first published in 1813. Elizabeth Bennet, second of five sisters whose family's estate is entailed away from them, spars with the proud and wealthy Mr Darcy while her family navigates the marriage market of Regency England. A comedy of misjudgement, class and self-knowledge, and one of the most popular novels in English literature. This Penguin Classics edition, edited by Vivien Jones, includes an introduction and notes on the text and its contexts.
