Description

Neurologist Oliver Sacks' 1985 collection of clinical tales: patients with visual agnosia, Tourette's, amnesia, autism and extraordinary musical or mathematical gifts, described with literary care as people rather than deficits. The book that relaunched the clinical case-history as literature; the title story concerns Dr P., a singer who could no longer recognise faces or objects.
