Description

James D. Watson's 2007 memoir of manners for a life in science, organised as chapters of remembered events — Chicago childhood, the DNA years, building Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, the Human Genome Project — each closing with 'manners' lessons for young scientists. Frank, funny and deliberately provocative in the manner of The Double Helix, it is part autobiography and part career manual, including candid accounts of the controversies that shadowed its author's later years.
