Description

Machiavelli's political treatise, written in 1513 and published posthumously in 1532: a blunt analysis of how principalities are won, held and lost, arguing that a ruler must learn how not to be good and must attend to the effectual truth of politics rather than its imagined ideals. Harvey Mansfield's translation is the standard scholarly English version.
