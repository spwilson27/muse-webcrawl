Description

Viktor Frankl's 1946 memoir and manifesto: a psychiatrist's account of survival in Nazi concentration camps, and his psychotherapeutic theory of logotherapy — that the primary human drive is the search for meaning, which can sustain a person even in suffering. One of the most influential books of the twentieth century, in print continuously since the war.
