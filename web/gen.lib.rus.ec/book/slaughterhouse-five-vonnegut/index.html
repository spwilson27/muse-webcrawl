Summary

An original short summary: Billy Pilgrim, a hapless American soldier captured at the Bulge and held in a Dresden slaughterhouse during the 1945 firebombing, comes "unstuck in time," reliving episodes of his life and an alleged abduction to the planet Tralfamadore. Vonnegut's anti-war novel blends memoir, science fiction and dark humour; its refrain "So it goes" follows every death.
