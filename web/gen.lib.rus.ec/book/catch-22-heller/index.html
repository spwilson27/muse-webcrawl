Description

Joseph Heller's 1961 satirical war novel about Captain John Yossarian, a US Army Air Forces bombardier on the island of Pianosa who is desperate to stop flying missions as the required number keeps rising. The bureaucratic paradox of Catch-22 — a flyer who asks to be grounded proves his sanity and so must fly — gave the English language a phrase. Non-linear, absurd and bleakly funny, it is a landmark of postwar American fiction.
