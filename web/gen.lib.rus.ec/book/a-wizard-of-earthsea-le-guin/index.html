Description

The first Earthsea novel (1968). The proud young wizard Ged, in a duel of pride at the school for wizards on Roke, looses a shadow creature into the world and must sail the archipelago to hunt the thing that hunts him. Le Guin's spare, mythic fantasy about power, naming and self-knowledge, written for young readers and read by all ages.
