Description

Dostoevsky's final novel (1880), a murder mystery and philosophical drama about the three Karamazov brothers — sensual Dmitri, intellectual Ivan and saintly Alyosha — and the murder of their father Fyodor Pavlovich. Through the trial and the famous Grand Inquisitor chapter it wrestles with faith, free will, morality and patricide. Widely considered one of the supreme achievements in world literature.
