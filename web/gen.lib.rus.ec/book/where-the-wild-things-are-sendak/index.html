Description

Maurice Sendak's 1963 picture book, winner of the Caldecott Medal. Sent to bed without supper, Max sails in a private boat to the land of the Wild Things, becomes their king, joins the wild rumpus — and sails home to where his supper is waiting, still hot. Forty-eight pages of cross-hatched colour illustration that changed children's picture books.
