Summary

An original short summary: Meursault, a French Algerian clerk, attends his mother's funeral with detachment, then days later shoots a man on a sun-blinding beach. His trial turns as much on his indifference as on the killing. Camus's novella is a classic exploration of the absurd, alienation and colonial Algeria.
