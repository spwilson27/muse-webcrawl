Description

Plato's dialogue on love, set at a drinking party in Athens in 416 BC, where each guest delivers a speech in praise of Eros. The speeches build from conventional compliments to Aristophanes' comic myth of humans split in half, and finally to Socrates reporting the teaching of Diotima: love as a ladder ascending from one beautiful body to Beauty itself. Part philosophy, part literary masterpiece, it is among the most read and loved of all Plato's works.
