Description

Thomas Piketty's study of wealth and income inequality built on centuries of tax records from France, Britain, the United States and other countries. Its central claim is that when the rate of return on capital (r) exceeds the growth rate of the economy (g), wealth concentrates in few hands; the twentieth century's mid-century compression of inequality appears as the exception, driven by war, inflation and policy, rather than the rule. The book revived public debate about inherited wealth and proposed remedies including a progressive global tax on capital.
