Description

Graham, Knuth and Patashnik's textbook blending continuous and discrete mathematics ('CONCRETE = CONtinuous + disCRETE') for computer science: sums, number theory, binomial coefficients, generating functions, discrete probability and asymptotics. Grown from Knuth's Stanford course, famous for its marginal graffiti written by students in the first edition.
