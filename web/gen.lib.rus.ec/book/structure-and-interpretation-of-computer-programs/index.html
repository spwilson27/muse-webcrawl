Description

The famous 'Wizard Book' of MIT's introductory computer science course 6.001, written in Scheme, a dialect of Lisp. Abelson and Sussman use programming to teach general principles: building abstractions with procedures and data, modularity, streams, metalinguistic abstraction, and finally the design of register machines, evaluators and a compiler. Its exercises are notoriously instructive, and its approach shaped a generation of programmers' thinking about the craft.
