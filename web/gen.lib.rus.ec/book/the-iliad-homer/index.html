Summary

An original short summary: The Iliad is an ancient Greek epic poem set in the final weeks of the Trojan War, focusing on the rage of Achilles after Agamemnon takes Briseis, his withdrawal from battle, the death of Patroclus at Hector's hands, and Achilles' return, vengeance and eventual meeting with Priam. It explores honour, fate, grief and the human cost of war.
