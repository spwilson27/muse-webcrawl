Description

William Golding's 1954 debut novel: a group of British schoolboys stranded on an uninhabited island after a plane crash descend from order into tribal savagery, as Ralph's conch-shell democracy loses to Jack's hunters and the imagined 'beast'. Initially a modest seller, it became a set text worldwide and helped win Golding the Nobel Prize in Literature. This edition carries an introduction by E. M. Forster's contemporary E. L. Epstein line of commentary as remembered.
