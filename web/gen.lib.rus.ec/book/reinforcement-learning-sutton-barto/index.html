Description

Sutton and Barto's standard textbook of reinforcement learning: the agent-environment interface, Markov decision processes, dynamic programming, Monte Carlo and temporal-difference methods, function approximation, policy gradients and the integration of learning with planning and search. The second edition (2018) reflects two decades of progress since the 1998 original.
