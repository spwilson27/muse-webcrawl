Description

Thomas Kuhn's 1962 landmark in the philosophy of science, arguing that science advances not by steady accumulation but through paradigm shifts — revolutions in which one framework of 'normal science' replaces another after crisis. The book popularised the word 'paradigm' and transformed debate about scientific progress. Fourth edition with an introductory essay by Ian Hacking.
