Description

The dominant university textbook in artificial intelligence, used in well over a thousand universities worldwide. Russell and Norvig organise the field around the idea of intelligent agents and cover search, constraint satisfaction, game playing, logic, probabilistic reasoning, machine learning, natural language processing, perception and robotics. The third edition expanded the treatment of probabilistic models, learning and the philosophical and ethical foundations of AI.
