Description

J. R. R. Tolkien's children's fantasy novel of 1937, the prelude to The Lord of the Rings. The comfortable hobbit Bilbo Baggins is swept from his home by the wizard Gandalf and thirteen dwarves on a quest to reclaim the Lonely Mountain and its treasure from the dragon Smaug, finding a magic ring in the goblin tunnels on the way. Written in a lively, storyteller's voice, it introduced Middle-earth to the world.
