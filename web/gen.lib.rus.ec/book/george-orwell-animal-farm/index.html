Description

Orwell's 1945 allegorical novella, subtitled 'A Fairy Story': the animals of Manor Farm overthrow their human owner, only to watch the pigs build a new tyranny. A satire of the Russian Revolution and Stalinism, famous for the commandment 'All animals are equal, but some animals are more equal than others'.
