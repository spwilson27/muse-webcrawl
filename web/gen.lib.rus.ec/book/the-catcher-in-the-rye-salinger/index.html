Summary

An original short summary: Narrated by teenager Holden Caulfield after his expulsion from prep school, the novel follows a few days wandering New York City as he rails against perceived phoniness, grieves his brother Allie, and seeks connection — notably with his sister Phoebe — while teetering toward breakdown. A landmark of postwar American fiction about adolescence and alienation.
