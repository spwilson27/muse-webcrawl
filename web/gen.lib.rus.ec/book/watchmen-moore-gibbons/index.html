Description

Alan Moore and Dave Gibbons' twelve-issue series (1986-87), collected here, set in an alternate 1985 where masked vigilantes are outlawed and the world slides toward nuclear war. The murder of the Comedian pulls the retired Rorschach, Nite Owl, Silk Spectre, Dr Manhattan and Ozymandias back into a conspiracy. The only graphic novel on Time's list of the 100 best English-language novels since 1923.
