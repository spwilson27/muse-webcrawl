Description

The definitive cell biology textbook, first published in 1983 and led for four decades by Bruce Alberts. The sixth edition covers the chemistry of the cell, DNA and chromosomes, gene expression, membrane structure and transport, intracellular compartments, signalling, the cytoskeleton, the cell cycle, and cells in tissues, illustrated with its famously clear figures and electron micrographs. A standard reference for undergraduate and graduate biology teaching worldwide.
