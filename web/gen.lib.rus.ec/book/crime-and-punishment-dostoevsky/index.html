Description

Dostoevsky's 1866 psychological novel follows Rodion Raskolnikov, a destitute ex-student in St Petersburg who murders a pawnbroker to test his theory that extraordinary men stand beyond good and evil, and is consumed by guilt and pursued by the patient detective Porfiry. It is one of the most influential novels of world literature.
