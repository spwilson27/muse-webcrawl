Description

Wittgenstein's second great work, published posthumously in 1953 from manuscripts he revised until his death. Rejecting his own Tractatus picture theory, he presents meaning as use within 'language-games' embedded in forms of life, develops the notions of family resemblance and rule-following, and mounts the private language argument. Written as numbered remarks rather than a continuous treatise, it reshaped philosophy of language, mind and the social sciences.
