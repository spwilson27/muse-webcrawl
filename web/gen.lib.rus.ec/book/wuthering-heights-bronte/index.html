Summary

An original short summary: Told largely by housekeeper Nelly Dean to the tenant Lockwood, the novel recounts the fierce bond between the foundling Heathcliff and Catherine Earnshaw on the Yorkshire moors, her marriage to Edgar Linton, Heathcliff's revenge on both families, and the gentler reconciliation of the next generation.
