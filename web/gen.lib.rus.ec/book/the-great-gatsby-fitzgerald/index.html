Description

F. Scott Fitzgerald's 1925 Jazz Age novel, narrated by Nick Carraway, who rents a house beside the Long Island mansion of the mysterious millionaire Jay Gatsby. Gatsby's glittering parties mask his obsession with the married Daisy Buchanan, and the pursuit of his version of the American Dream ends in violence and disillusion. The defining novel of the Roaring Twenties.
