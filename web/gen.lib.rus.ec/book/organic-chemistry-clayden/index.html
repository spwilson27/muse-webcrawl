Description

The leading modern undergraduate organic chemistry textbook, written by Clayden, Greeves and Warren at Oxford and Cambridge. It teaches organic chemistry mechanistically and logically rather than by functional-group memorisation, building from structure and bonding through stereochemistry, substitution and elimination, carbonyl chemistry, aromatic chemistry, synthesis strategy and biological molecules, with hundreds of worked examples and problems. The second edition reorganised several chapters and added extensive online resources.
