Description

Douglas Adams's comic science fiction novel of 1979, adapted from his BBC radio series. Seconds before Earth is demolished to make way for a hyperspace bypass, Arthur Dent is rescued by Ford Prefect, a researcher for the Guide, and hurled into a galaxy of Vogons, depressed robots and the ultimate answer to life, the universe and everything (42). The first of a 'trilogy in five parts' that became a permanent fixture of popular culture, famous for its towels, its babel fish and the words DON'T PANIC on the cover of the Guide itself.
