Description

Jared Diamond's companion volume to Guns, Germs, and Steel, asking why some past societies destroyed themselves while others solved similar problems. Case studies range from Easter Island and the Maya to Norse Greenland and the Anasazi, balanced against successes such as Tokugawa Japan and Iceland, and extended to modern Montana, Rwanda, Australia and China. Diamond's five-point framework — environmental damage, climate change, hostile neighbours, friendly trade partners and a society's response — frames environmental failure as a choice societies can still refuse.
