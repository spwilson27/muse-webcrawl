Description

Art Spiegelman's graphic memoir of his father Vladek, a Polish Jew who survived Auschwitz, drawn with Jews as mice, Germans as cats and Poles as pigs. Published in two volumes (1986, 1991) and collected here, it interweaves the wartime narrative with the difficult present-day relationship between father and son. The first graphic novel to win a Pulitzer Prize (a Special Award in Letters, 1992).
