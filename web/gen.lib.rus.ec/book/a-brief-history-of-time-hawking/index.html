Description

Stephen Hawking's landmark popular account of cosmology, first published in 1988 and one of the best-selling science books of all time. Written for the general reader with almost no equations (famously only E=mc2), it tours the big bang, black holes, the arrow of time, wormholes and the search for a unified theory of physics. The updated and expanded edition of 1996/1998 revised several chapters in light of new observational results and developments in string theory.
