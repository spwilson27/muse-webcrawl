Description

Nobel laureate Daniel Kahneman's summary of a lifetime's research, much of it with Amos Tversky, on the two modes of thought he calls System 1 (fast, intuitive) and System 2 (slow, effortful). The book tours cognitive biases, heuristics, overconfidence, framing, prospect theory and the experiencing versus remembering self, drawing on decades of elegant experiments. A bestseller on publication, it reshaped how economists, doctors, managers and judges think about human judgement.
