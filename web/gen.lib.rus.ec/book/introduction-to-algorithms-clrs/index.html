Description

The standard university textbook on algorithms, known everywhere by the initials of its authors as 'CLRS'. It covers sorting, data structures, dynamic programming, greedy algorithms, graph algorithms, string matching, NP-completeness and much more, each algorithm presented in pseudocode with rigorous analysis of correctness and running time. The third edition added chapters on van Emde Boas trees and multithreaded algorithms and revised the treatment of recurrences and dynamic programming.
