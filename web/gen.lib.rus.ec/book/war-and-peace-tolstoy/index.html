Description

Leo Tolstoy's immense novel of Russian society during the Napoleonic invasion, serialised in 1865-67 and published in book form in 1869. Following the Rostov, Bolkonsky and Bezukhov families through balls, battles and the burning of Moscow, it interleaves narrative with essays on history and free will. This edition uses the acclaimed translation by Richard Pevear and Larissa Volokhonsky, widely praised for restoring the roughness and variety of Tolstoy's Russian prose.
