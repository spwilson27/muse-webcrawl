Description

The published version of the famous two-year introductory physics course Richard Feynman taught at Caltech in 1961-63, edited by Robert Leighton and Matthew Sands. Volume I covers mechanics, radiation and heat; Volume II electromagnetism and matter; Volume III quantum mechanics. The New Millennium edition corrected the errors accumulated over decades of printings. Renowned for Feynman's fresh, insightful derivations, the lectures remain a touchstone of physics exposition, though many find them better as a second pass over the subject than as a first textbook.
