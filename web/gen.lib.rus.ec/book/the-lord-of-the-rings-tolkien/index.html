Description

J. R. R. Tolkien's epic fantasy, published in three volumes in 1954-55 (The Fellowship of the Ring, The Two Towers, The Return of the King) and here in a single volume with the appendices. Frodo Baggins and the Fellowship set out to destroy the One Ring and defeat the Dark Lord Sauron, in a narrative built on Tolkien's invented languages and the deep history of Middle-earth. The defining work of modern fantasy and one of the best-selling novels ever written.
