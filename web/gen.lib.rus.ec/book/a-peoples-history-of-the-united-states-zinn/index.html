Description

Howard Zinn's bestselling retelling of American history from the perspective of those usually left at the margins: Native Americans facing Columbus, enslaved Africans, striking workers, women, immigrants and soldiers who resisted the wars they were sent to fight. First published in 1980 and repeatedly updated, it challenged textbook narratives of uninterrupted progress and became a fixture of classrooms and reading groups, praised by admirers as a necessary corrective and criticised by others for its openly partisan stance.
