Summary

An original short summary: Set in London and Paris before and during the French Revolution, the novel follows Dr Manette, recalled to life after 18 years in the Bastille; his daughter Lucie; Charles Darnay; and Sydney Carton, whose self-sacrifice closes the story. It contrasts resurrection and darkness, and depicts both revolutionary hope and mob violence.
