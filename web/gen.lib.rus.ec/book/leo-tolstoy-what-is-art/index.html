What Is Art?

TitleWhat Is Art? (Chto takoe iskusstvo?)
Year1898 (first published)
LanguageRussian original; English translations available
SubjectAesthetics, philosophy of art, ethics
Publisher (early English ed.)Various
PagesVaries by edition, approx. 250–350

Summary

In this late philosophical essay, Tolstoy argues against defining art by beauty or pleasure. He proposes instead that art is the conscious transmission of feelings experienced by the artist, so that others are infected by them. On that basis he judges much celebrated art of his time harshly, praises art that expresses sincere religious or universal human feeling and is intelligible to ordinary people, and links artistic value to moral and spiritual purpose rather than technical sophistication for an elite audience.
