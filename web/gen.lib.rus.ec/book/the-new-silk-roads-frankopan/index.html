Description

Peter Frankopan's update to The Silk Roads, published in 2018, surveying how the world's centre of gravity is shifting eastward: China's Belt and Road Initiative, new pipelines, ports and railways across Central Asia, and the resource politics of the region between Istanbul and Beijing. Shorter and more journalistic than the original, it argues that the networks of the old Silk Roads are being rebuilt while the West is looking elsewhere.
