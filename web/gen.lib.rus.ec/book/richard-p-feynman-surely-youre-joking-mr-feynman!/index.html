Description

A bestselling collection of anecdotes from the life of Nobel physicist Richard Feynman, edited from conversations with Ralph Leighton: safecracking at Los Alamos, learning Portuguese and bongos in Brazil, investigating art and lock-picking, and his irreverent encounters with the academic establishment.
