Description

Aldous Huxley's 1932 dystopian novel of a future World State where citizens are genetically engineered, conditioned and kept docile with pleasure and the drug soma. When the outsider John 'the Savage' is brought from a reservation to London, his Shakespeare-fuelled revolt exposes the cost of a stability bought with freedom, art and religion. Includes a foreword by the author and his 1946 reflections on the book.
