Description

The biochemistry textbook founded by Albert L. Lehninger and continued by David Nelson and Michael Cox, a mainstay of advanced undergraduate courses since 1970. It moves from the foundations of biochemistry and the structure and function of proteins, through bioenergetics and the metabolism of carbohydrates, lipids, amino acids and nucleotides, to information pathways and hormonal regulation, with quantitative problems throughout. The seventh edition refreshed the structural figures and clinical examples.
