Description

Bram Stoker's 1897 epistolary Gothic novel. Solicitor Jonathan Harker travels to Transylvania to complete a property sale for Count Dracula, who then moves to England and preys on Lucy Westenra and Mina Harker; Professor Van Helsing leads the hunt to destroy him. Told through letters, diaries and newspaper cuttings, it fixed the modern image of the vampire in popular culture.
