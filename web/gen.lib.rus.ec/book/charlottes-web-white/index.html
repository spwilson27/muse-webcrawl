Description

E. B. White's 1952 children's classic, illustrated by Garth Williams. Wilbur the pig is saved from slaughter first by the girl Fern and then by Charlotte, the wise spider who weaves words — 'Some Pig', 'Terrific', 'Radiant' — into her web above his pen. A story about friendship, language and the cycle of life on a farm, consistently ranked among the greatest children's books ever written.
