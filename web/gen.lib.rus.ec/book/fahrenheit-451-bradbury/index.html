Description

Ray Bradbury's 1953 dystopia of a future America where books are outlawed and 'firemen' burn them — 451°F being the temperature at which book paper catches fire. Fireman Guy Montag, shaken by a young neighbour and a woman who chooses to die with her books, begins to steal and read them. A classic warning about censorship and mass entertainment, with an introduction by Neil Gaiman in this edition.
