Description

Hofstadter's Pulitzer Prize-winning exploration of how self-reference and formal systems connect the theorems of Kurt Gödel, the art of M. C. Escher and the music of J. S. Bach. Woven from Achilles-and-the-Tortoise dialogues and expository chapters, it ranges over logic, recursion, artificial intelligence and the nature of mind and meaning.
