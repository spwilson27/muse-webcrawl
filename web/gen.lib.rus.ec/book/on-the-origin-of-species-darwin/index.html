Description

Charles Darwin's presentation of evolution by natural selection, first published by John Murray in London on 24 November 1859 under the full title On the Origin of Species by Means of Natural Selection. Drawing on variation under domestication, the struggle for existence, geographical distribution, embryology and the fossil record, Darwin argues that species descend with modification from common ancestors, shaped by selection acting on small variations. This facsimile reproduces the first edition, whose print run sold out on the day of publication.
