Category:Animals

This Commons category collects media of animals, organised by taxonomic group and by kind: mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, insects, spiders and other invertebrates, as well as animal anatomy, behaviour, tracks and sounds.

[Image: Photographs of a lion, a bird in flight and a butterfly, representing the category]

Subcategories

Mammalia, Aves, Reptilia, Amphibia, Pisces, Insecta and many finer groups, plus Animals by country and Animals in art.

Media

Photographs dominate, alongside video of animal behaviour and recordings of birdsong and other animal sounds, most of them freely licensed.
