Commons: Picture of the Day

This Commons project page explains the Picture of the Day: each day one of the repository's Featured Pictures is displayed on the Main Page, with a caption in many languages. The page lists the current and upcoming pictures, the archive by month, and instructions for adding captions and translations.

[Image: Today's Picture of the Day with its multilingual caption]

How pictures are chosen

Only Featured Pictures — images the community has assessed as among Commons' finest — are eligible, and they are scheduled in advance by editors.

Syndication

The picture is also available as an RSS/feed and is reused by other Wikimedia projects and external sites.
