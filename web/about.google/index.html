About Google

Our mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful. About Google tells the company's story and gathers its products, commitments and impact initiatives in one place.

Products for everyone

Search, Maps, Gmail, Chrome, Android, YouTube, Google Photos, Drive, Translate and the Pixel line — presented as tools built to help people in everyday moments, including the AI features (Gemini) now woven through them.

Commitments

Standing themes of the about site: digital safety and privacy, sustainability (Google's carbon-neutral history and 24/7 carbon-free-energy goal for 2030, as long stated), crisis response, and Google.org philanthropy and AI-for-good programmes such as flood forecasting.

Company

Google was founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin at Stanford; since 2015 it has been the largest subsidiary of Alphabet Inc., whose about pages this site links alongside careers, investor and press resources.
