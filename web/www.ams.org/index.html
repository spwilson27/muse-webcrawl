American Mathematical Society

The American Mathematical Society, founded in 1888, advances mathematical research and scholarship: it publishes journals and books, runs MathSciNet, convenes meetings, and advocates for the mathematical sciences.

Publications and MathSciNet

AMS journals include the Journal of the AMS, Transactions and the widely read Notices of the AMS (its membership magazine). MathSciNet, its reviewing database, indexes the mathematical literature with reviews — a defining tool of the profession since Mathematical Reviews began in 1940.

Meetings and community

The Joint Mathematics Meetings, held each January with partner societies, is among the largest mathematics gatherings in the world; sectional meetings run through the year, and the site's news feed covers prizes (such as the Cole and Steele Prizes) and policy for mathematicians.
