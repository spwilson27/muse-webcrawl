SteamOS + Proton

Deck runs SteamOS, Valve's Arch-based Linux system, with the Proton compatibility layer bringing the Windows catalogue along. Suspend and resume instantly, cloud saves follow you to your PC, and the battery lasts from a couple of hours in heavy AAA play to much longer in indies.
